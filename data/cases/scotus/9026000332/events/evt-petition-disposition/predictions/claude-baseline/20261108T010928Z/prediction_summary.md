We predict the Supreme Court will most likely turn down this petition. We put the chance it takes the case at about one in five. Racetrack owners argue that the leaders of the horseracing safety authority should have been appointed like federal officers. The Court is expected to review the main dispute over the horseracing law through the government's own petitions. Both the government and the authority oppose adding this question, and no lower court has agreed with it. The Court will probably set this petition aside until it decides the main case, and then deny it.
