# Forecast: No. 26-332

**Relists.** The petition has been distributed once (for the 12/4/2026 conference) and never relisted. I expect it to be distributed again (0.6). The likeliest path is that the Court grants, or relists and then grants, the lead petitions 26-199/26-201, and holds this cross-petition for their merits decision. A held petition is redistributed after that decision, probably in June 2027. An outright denial at the 12/4 conference is the main alternative.

**CVSG.** None. The Solicitor General is already a respondent and has filed a memorandum opposing review.

**Disposition.** Denial is more likely than not, most likely after a hold for 26-199 (P(any grant) 0.20). If the Court takes this case, it would take QP1 as written, the Appointments Clause question, and likely consolidate it with 26-199/26-201. It would not take QP2, which duplicates the private-nondelegation issues in the lead petitions and Texas's 26-335. If it is granted, a summary route is plausible (0.35 conditional on a grant): a GVR after the merits decision in 26-199 if that opinion reshapes the private-versus-governmental analysis. Plenary consolidation is the larger share.

**Separate writing.** A dissent from denial or a statement respecting it is unlikely (0.08 conditional on denial). The Court will be resolving HISA in the lead case. If anyone writes, Justice Gorsuch (who flagged the officer question in *Consumers' Research*) or Justice Thomas is likeliest.
