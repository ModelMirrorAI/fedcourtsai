# Predicted disposition and procedure

The Court will deny this cross-petition rather than add its Appointments Clause and broader nondelegation challenges to the enforcement dispute presented by the lead petitions. It can address the conflict over private enforcement without resolving every constitutional objection to the Authority. Denial will not endorse the Fifth Circuit's reasoning and will most likely carry no explanation.

The snapshot records **one distribution**, on November 4 for the December 4, 2026 conference. The modal forecast is **zero additional distributions** before disposition. There is a substantial minority chance of further scheduling or consideration: P(at least one further distribution) is **42%**, with one or two additional distributions the central positive-increment scenario. Coordination with the lead petitions, rather than repeated scrutiny of an independently split appointments question, is the principal reason for that risk. A hold without a fresh distribution does not satisfy the increment forecast.

There will be no call for the Solicitor General's views; the probability of a new CVSG is **0.5%**. The government has already briefed the added questions as a respondent. Its opposition is not itself a CVSG.

# Conditional review route

If plenary review is granted, the Court will principally take **question 1**, concerning whether directors exercising the Authority's federal functions must be appointed consistently with Article II despite private incorporation. It will likely coordinate that issue with the lead enforcement cases rather than independently revisit every rulemaking objection under question 2. Conditional on reaching that question, the central issue will be whether the statutory functions and supervision, rather than a corporate label alone, determine officer status. This is a conditional merits forecast, not a prediction that a denial contains such a holding.

Conditional on any grant-side outcome, there is a **35%** chance of a disposition in the cert order itself. The main summary route is a GVR following a lead decision that changes the basis of the judgment below; it is not an anticipated immediate summary merits reversal. A grant for briefing and argument remains the more likely route within the grant branch.

# Separate writings

Conditional on denial, the probability of a dissent or statement is **13%**. No writing is the modal outcome. If one appears, Gorsuch is the most likely author, with Thomas and Alito the next candidates, expressing concern that the appointments issue remains unanswered. These are forecasts of writing, not disclosed or predicted cert votes.
