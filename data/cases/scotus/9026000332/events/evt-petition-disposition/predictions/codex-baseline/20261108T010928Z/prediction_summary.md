I expect the Supreme Court to decline this request for review, with an 18% chance of accepting it or sending it back for reconsideration. The challengers raise an important question about whether privately chosen horse-racing regulators need constitutionally required appointments. But the Court can address the related enforcement dispute through other petitions without taking this additional question, which also faces a standing objection. The strongest reason for review is its connection to that larger dispute; the biggest uncertainty is whether four Justices want to broaden the case.
