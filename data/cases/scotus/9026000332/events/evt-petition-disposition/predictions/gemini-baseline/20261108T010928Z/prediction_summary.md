The Supreme Court is very unlikely to grant this conditional cross-petition for certiorari (an 8% chance of a grant, meaning a petition to review a lower court decision, or a grant-vacate-remand order), and is expected to deny it. Although the Court is almost certain to hear the main appeals by the Federal Trade Commission and the Horseracing Integrity and Safety Authority over whether the Authority's enforcement powers are constitutional, Gulf Coast Racing's separate cross-petition asks whether the Authority's board members must be appointed by the President under the Constitution's Appointments Clause and whether its rulemaking powers are invalid. Because every federal appeals court has rejected those arguments and both the federal government and the Authority oppose expanding the case, the Court will likely deny this cross-petition.
