# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny the petition. My probability of any grant, including a GVR or summary reversal, is 16%.

The snapshot records two distributions: April 15 for the May 1, 2026 conference, and July 15 for the September 28, 2026 conference. A response was requested between them, on April 24, after Netflix initially waived its response. No CVSG is recorded. From this two-distribution state, I expect no additional distribution and a denial in the first order cycle following the September 28 conference, most likely in early October 2026. The probability of at least one additional distribution is 28%; if another occurs, one or two further distributions are more likely than an extended series.

I expect no call for the Solicitor General's views, assigning a 10% probability to a new CVSG. If the Court seeks those views, the likely window is the autumn conference sequence, with the ultimate petition disposition delayed beyond the ordinary early-October path.

If review is granted, the Court will most likely take the petition's single statutory question substantially as presented: whether a contract governing administration of benefit claims is a contract under which the plan is established or operated within ERISA Section 104(b)(4). The focus would be the disclosure obligation for claims-administration agreements, not the separate statutory-penalty award or every internal document originally requested. Conditional on review, the central issue would be whether a contract's role in operating the plan suffices despite its limited additional informational value to an individual beneficiary.

Plenary review is the more likely grant route. I assign 10% conditional probability to disposition in the cert order itself and 90% to proceeding beyond that order. The competing statutory readings would benefit from briefing, and the materials examined identify no intervening Supreme Court decision requiring a GVR.

Conditional on denial, I assign 6% probability to a noted dissent or statement respecting denial. I expect an unexplained denial without a separate writing and do not forecast any particular Justice's private cert vote.
