## In brief

Mark Zavislak asks the Court to hear his dispute with Netflix over whether certain claims administration contracts must be disclosed to health plan beneficiaries under federal benefits law.

## What happened

Zavislak, a beneficiary of Netflix's health plan through his spouse, asked Netflix under a federal benefits disclosure law to produce documents governing the plan, including agreements between Netflix and its outside claims administrators. Netflix gave him several plan summaries but not the administrators' agreements. Zavislak sued, and after a bench trial the district court ruled Netflix had no obligation to disclose those agreements, reasoning they governed only Netflix's relationship with the administrators, not participants' benefits. The Ninth Circuit affirmed in an unpublished decision, while also striking a small penalty award against Netflix.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked to decide whether a contract that governs how a health plan's claims get administered counts as one of the "instruments" that a benefits law requires plan administrators to disclose to beneficiaries who request it. Zavislak argues other appeals courts read this disclosure duty to cover such contracts, while the Ninth Circuit applies a narrower rule asking only whether a document gives participants information about their benefits.

## Where it stands

The petition has been distributed for two conferences, and the respondent, Netflix, has filed a brief opposing review; Zavislak has since filed a reply. The docket does not yet record any decision on the petition.

## What each outcome would mean

Zavislak, as the petitioner, is asking the Court to take up the case. If the Court grants review, it would hear full arguments and decide the disclosure question itself, without yet deciding who is right. If it denies review, the Ninth Circuit's decision stands, meaning Netflix's refusal to turn over the claims administration agreements to Zavislak remains in effect and Netflix's win in the lower courts is left undisturbed.
