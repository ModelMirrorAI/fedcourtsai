The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review (94% chance of denial; 6% chance of grant) in *del Rio v. CrowdStrike, Inc.* Airline passengers sued cybersecurity firm CrowdStrike under state law for flight delays and cancellations caused by its July 2024 global software outage, and lower courts held those claims preempted—meaning blocked by federal law—under the Airline Deregulation Act. Although the Court requested a response from CrowdStrike, it rarely reviews one-word, non-precedential appellate orders where courts are not divided on the specific issue.
