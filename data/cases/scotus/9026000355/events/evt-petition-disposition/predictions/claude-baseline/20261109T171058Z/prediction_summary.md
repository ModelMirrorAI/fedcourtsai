We predict the Supreme Court will very likely refuse to hear this case (about a 1-in-20 chance it agrees). Passengers sued CrowdStrike over the July 2024 software outage that grounded flights. The lower courts held that a federal airline-deregulation law blocks those state-law claims. The Court did ask CrowdStrike to file a response, which is a mild sign of interest. But the appeals court affirmed without writing an opinion, and the Justices have long left the underlying disagreement among the lower courts unresolved.
