# Forecast — No. 26-355, del Rio v. CrowdStrike

- **State:** distributed once (for the 11/6/2026 conference). The Court requested a response on 10/19/2026, due 11/18/2026, after the respondent waived. No relists yet and no CVSG.
- **Relists:** once the brief in opposition and any reply are in, expect the petition to be distributed again for a December 2026 or January 2027 conference, and most likely denied at that first post-response conference with no further relist. A second relist is unlikely (~10%).
- **CVSG:** not expected. A dispute between private parties, a weak vehicle, and no signal yet beyond a single response request.
- **Disposition:** denial, most likely with no separate writing. The Court has lived with the Hodges/Charas split for decades, and here CA5 affirmed without an opinion.
- **If granted:** the Court would take the question as written, perhaps narrowed to whether ADA "service" preemption reaches claims against an entity that is not an air carrier. That framing would let it avoid choosing between Hodges and Charas outright. Plenary review, not a GVR or summary reversal.
- **Dissent from denial:** not expected. If anyone writes, the likeliest are Justice Gorsuch or Justice Thomas, questioning a broad reading of preemption.
