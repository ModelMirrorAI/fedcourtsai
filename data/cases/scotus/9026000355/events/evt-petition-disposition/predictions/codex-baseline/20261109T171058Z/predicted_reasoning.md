# Forecast of the Court's handling

The Court will most likely deny the petition after obtaining the response it has requested. The probability of any grant is **12%**; the modal disposition is denial without a separate writing. The longstanding disagreement over airline-service preemption will not, on the most likely path, overcome the lack of a reasoned appellate opinion and uncertainty that the vendor dispute cleanly presents that disagreement.

## Next procedural steps

The record shows **one distribution**, for the November 6, 2026 conference, followed by an October 19 request for a response due November 18. I forecast **at least one additional distribution with 98% probability**, most likely exactly one before disposition. That redistribution will principally reflect processing the requested opposition and any reply, not necessarily a substantive relist of a fully briefed petition. The Court will most likely dispose of the petition in December 2026 or January 2027, allowing for response processing and possible extensions. If it receives repeated post-response relists, a later disposition becomes more likely.

The Court will most likely **not call for the Solicitor General's views**; the probability of a new CVSG is **4%**. If it does request those views, I expect the request after the opposition and a subsequent conference, rather than before the present response deadline, and disposition would likely be delayed by months.

## Conditional grant and denial paths

If review is granted, the Court is more likely to take the petition's single question in a focused formulation: whether the Act's preemption clause bars passengers' state-law claims against an independent cybersecurity vendor for losses from airline disruptions. It is less likely to undertake an abstract definition of every airline service. If the Court reaches the merits, the likely contest will be the limits of the relationship between vendor duties and airline services, including when that relationship is too remote, not a categorical rule that only airlines may invoke preemption.

**Conditional on any grant, summary disposition has a 12% probability; plenary review has an 88% probability.** I do not expect a grant-vacate-remand in light of a particular lead case because none is identified in the record. A summary reversal remains possible but is less likely than briefing and argument on this contested statutory issue.

**Conditional on denial, a noted dissent or statement has a 7% probability.** I expect no separate writing. If one appears, Thomas is the leading individual candidate, given the historical service-definition issue identified in the petition; a Gorsuch writing is a smaller possibility. The per-Justice probabilities in `prediction.json` are unconditional probabilities of writing on the disposing order, not forecasts of cert votes and not a merits-opinion authorship distribution.
