The Supreme Court is very likely to decline this appeal: I estimate a 12% chance it takes the case. Passengers argue that federal airline law should not protect CrowdStrike from lawsuits over its software outage. The Court has requested a response, showing some interest, and lower courts disagree about the law's reach. But the appeals court supplied no explanation, and the case may not cleanly resolve that disagreement. Expect another scheduling step before a decision, probably without a written explanation.
