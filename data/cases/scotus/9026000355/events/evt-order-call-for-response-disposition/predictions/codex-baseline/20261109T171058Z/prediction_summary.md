The Supreme Court is very likely to decline this case; I put the chance of review at about one in nine, or 11%. Travelers want to sue CrowdStrike over flight disruptions caused by faulty software. Asking the company to respond shows some interest, but the lower appeals court issued no explanation, and the petition does not clearly establish conflicting appeals-court rulings about comparable software vendors. The broader issue—when federal airline law prevents ordinary state-law claims—is important, but that alone is unlikely to secure review.
