# Forecast — No. 26-355, del Rio v. CrowdStrike

The docket shows **one distribution** (for the Nov. 6, 2026 conference), which the response request took the petition off. **No CVSG** has been called for, and there are no amici.

- **Relists:** Once CrowdStrike's response (due Nov. 18) and any reply are in, the petition will be distributed again, most likely for a December 2026 or January 2027 conference. Expect it to be denied at that first post-response conference without a further relist.
- **CVSG:** Not expected. The ADA's preemption clause implicates DOT, but the Court rarely invites the Solicitor General on a vehicle this thin.
- **Disposition:** Denied.
- **If granted:** The Court would take the question as written but would likely reformulate it more generally, asking whether "service" under § 41713(b)(1) is limited to the bargained-for provision of air transportation (Charas) or covers its operational elements (Hodges), and whether claims against non-carriers can be "related to" carrier services at all. Plenary review rather than a summary disposition.
- **Summary disposition:** Unlikely. No intervening decision supports a GVR, and a no-opinion Rule 47.6 affirmance gives nothing to summarily reverse.
- **Separate writing:** None expected on the denial.
