We expect the Supreme Court to **decline** to hear this case (roughly a 1-in-25 chance it takes it). Travelers sued CrowdStrike over the July 2024 software outage that grounded flights. The lower courts said a federal airline-deregulation law blocks those state-law claims. The Supreme Court has asked CrowdStrike to respond, which shows some interest. But the appeals court affirmed in a one-line order with no explanation, and the Court has left this disagreement among appeals courts unresolved for decades. That makes this case a weak candidate.
