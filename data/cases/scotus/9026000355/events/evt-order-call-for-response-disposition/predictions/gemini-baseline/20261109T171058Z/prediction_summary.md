We predict the Supreme Court will deny review in *Julio del Rio v. CrowdStrike, Inc.*, putting the chance that the Court takes the case at 8% (fewer than one in ten). Airline passengers sued the cybersecurity company CrowdStrike after its faulty July 2024 software update grounded Delta flights, and lower courts threw out the lawsuit partly because a federal airline-deregulation law blocks state lawsuits related to airline services. Although the Supreme Court asked CrowdStrike to respond to the passengers' appeal, the lower appellate court issued an unpublished, one-word ruling and the trial judge also dismissed the claims on independent state-law grounds, making this a poor case for the Justices to hear.
