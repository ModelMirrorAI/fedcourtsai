Distributed twice so far (the first was removed when a response was requested, and the second is for the upcoming Long Conference). I expect no further relists before a decision on the petition. The case presents severe vehicle problems, as the lower court dismissed several claims based on the Section 1500 jurisdictional bar and the statute of limitations. Furthermore, the decision below faithfully applies the Court's recent 2023 precedent in Arizona v. Navajo Nation, which the Court is unlikely to revisit so soon, particularly in an unpublished decision. 

A CVSG will not happen because the United States is already a party and its Solicitor General has filed a Brief in Opposition. 

If granted, the Court would likely take up the first question presented regarding whether the Winters doctrine and tribal land trust status establish a money-mandating fiduciary duty under the Tucker Acts. A summary disposition route (GVR or summary reversal) is highly unlikely because there is no intervening decision or obvious error. Should the Court deny the petition, as expected, there will likely be no separate writing or dissent from denial, as the case involves a straightforward application of recent precedent combined with multiple vehicle flaws.
