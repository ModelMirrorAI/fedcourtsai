Distributed once so far; expect the Court to deny the petition without further relists. The issue presented involves the application of the jurisdictional bar in 8 U.S.C. 1252(a)(2)(B)(ii) following *Patel v. Garland*. The courts of appeals have largely converged on interpreting the statute to preclude review of predicate non-discretionary determinations for discretionary immigration relief. Consequently, there is no pressing circuit split requiring the Court's intervention.

No CVSG is expected, as the federal government (the Secretary of Homeland Security) is already the respondent and has filed a brief in opposition. If granted, the Court would likely take the statutory interpretation question to clarify the reach of *Patel* under clause (ii), but a summary disposition (GVR) is unlikely given the uniform lower court precedent. Should the Court deny the petition, expect no separate writing or dissent.
