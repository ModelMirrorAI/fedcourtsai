# Forecast of the Court's action

shortly after the scheduled October 9, 2026 conference, during October 2026.
The probability of any grant, including a GVR or summary reversal, is 6%.
Denial will leave the appellate judgment in place without a merits opinion
endorsing its interpretation.

The provisioned record shows **one distribution and no CVSG**. I predict

least one additional distribution is 20%; if that occurs, the likeliest course
is one further distribution rather than a prolonged hold. I predict no new

January 9 Eighth Circuit opinion, **not a quotation or verified formulation

address that jurisdictional question rather than directly decide whether
Fofana's past conduct makes him inadmissible.

cert order itself: **85% plenary versus 15% summary route**. The competing

Conditional on denial, I predict **no dissent or statement respecting denial**
(5% probability of at least one such writing). I make no individual-Justice
vote or authorship prediction. The modal denial will offer no explanation of
the jurisdictional merits.
