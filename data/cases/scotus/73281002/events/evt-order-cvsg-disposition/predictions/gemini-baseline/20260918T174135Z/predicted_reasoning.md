Distributed twice so far, and the Court has called for the views of the Solicitor General. Expect at least one further distribution once the SG files their brief, followed by a grant of certiorari. The petition presents an acknowledged circuit split on the application of the Anderson-Burdick balancing test to mail-voting rules, making it a prime candidate for plenary review. If granted, the Court will likely take up both the general standard of review for mail-voting rules (QP 2) and the specific application of Anderson-Burdick (QP 3) to clarify election integrity safeguards. A summary disposition is unlikely given the need for a precedential opinion on the balancing framework. Should the Court deny, expect a dissent from denial by one or more conservative Justices, given the narrow 7-6 en banc split below and the partisan valence of the issue.
