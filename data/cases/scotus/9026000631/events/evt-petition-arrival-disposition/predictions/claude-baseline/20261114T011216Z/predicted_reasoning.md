# Forecast — Robison v. Collins, No. 26-631

- **Disposition:** The Court will deny certiorari. The docket shows zero distributions at this point.
- **Path:** The Solicitor General will waive the right to respond (or, less likely, file a short opposition after an extension). The petition will be distributed once for conference — most likely a January 2027 conference if a waiver is filed in December — and denied on the following order list, without a relist. If the Court calls for a response (about 5–8% likely), the denial slips to spring 2027 but remains the outcome.
- **Relists:** One distribution, no further ones.
- **CVSG:** None; the United States is the respondent.
- **Question presented:** If the Court took the case at all, it could not take the QP as written without first confronting the Federal Circuit's § 7292(d)(2) jurisdictional dismissal; the likeliest form of any grant would be a GVR directing the Federal Circuit to reach the statutory question, not plenary review of whether §§ 5109A and 5121 allow a survivor's CUE claim.
- **Summary disposition:** Not expected; no intervening decision bears on the issue. Conditional on a grant, a cert-order disposition and plenary review are roughly comparable, with plenary slightly likelier.
- **Separate writings:** None expected on the denial. If anyone wrote, it would most plausibly be Justice Gorsuch, Sotomayor or Jackson on the fairness of the survivor bar.
