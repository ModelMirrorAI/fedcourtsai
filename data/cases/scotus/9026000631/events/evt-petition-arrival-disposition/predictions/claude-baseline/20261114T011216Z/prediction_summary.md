The Supreme Court will very likely refuse to hear this case — I put the chance of review at under one in a hundred. A veteran's widow asks whether a surviving spouse may reopen an old VA benefits decision for "clear and unmistakable error". The appeals court below did not rule on that question; it dismissed her appeal for lack of jurisdiction in an unpublished decision. No other courts disagree on the issue, and the Court has already turned away the same question twice.
