# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny the petition, most likely in an unsigned order without explanation or separate writing. The statutory survivor question will not overcome the vehicle's jurisdictional complication and its reliance on arguments seeking to overturn existing Federal Circuit precedent.

The arrival snapshot shows **zero conference distributions**. Expect **one initial distribution** followed by denial at the first conference considering the petition, ordinarily in early 2027 if the response schedule proceeds without substantial extensions. A first distribution itself satisfies the recorded `relist-increment` claim, which has probability 97% from this zero baseline. A genuine relist means a second or later conference distribution; expect **zero such relists**, with roughly a 6% chance of at least one. If there is one, one extra conference is more likely than a prolonged sequence.

The Court will not call for the Solicitor General's views. The Solicitor General already represents the federal respondent and can address the petition through the ordinary response process. The probability of a separate CVSG is 0.1%; no timing for that unlikely event is forecast.

If review is granted, the likely substantive question is the petition's single question in a tighter form: whether an eligible survivor may initiate revision under § 5109A of a deceased veteran's benefits decision to establish accrued benefits under § 5121. The Court may need to include or first resolve whether § 7292 permits appellate review of that preserved statutory challenge. It would not simply decide whether the veteran's original benefits determination was erroneous.

Conditional on any grant, plenary briefing and argument are more likely than summary action: 80% versus 20%. A summary order, if issued, is more plausibly a vacatur and remand concerning the jurisdictional characterization than an immediate merits reversal establishing survivors' entitlement. No identified intervening decision makes a routine hold-and-GVR the expected path.

Conditional on denial, the probability of any dissent or statement respecting denial is 3%. Expect none. If one occurs, Gorsuch is the leading individual possibility and Thomas the next, but neither is a likely writer.
