The Supreme Court is very likely to decline Linda Kay Robison's request for review. I give it about a **2-in-100 chance of being accepted**. She asks whether a veteran's surviving spouse can challenge a serious error in an old benefits decision to recover money that should have been paid. The issue matters to survivors, but this case reaches the Court through a procedural dismissal, and the petition largely renews arguments against longstanding precedent. I expect denial without a written explanation.
