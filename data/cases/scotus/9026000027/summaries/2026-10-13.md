## What the Court is being asked

The case asks whether a state's workers' compensation rule, which can treat a company as the "employer" of a worker who technically works for someone else, can be applied to a federal inspector carrying out official government duties at a private business, so that the business is shielded from a lawsuit over her injuries there.
