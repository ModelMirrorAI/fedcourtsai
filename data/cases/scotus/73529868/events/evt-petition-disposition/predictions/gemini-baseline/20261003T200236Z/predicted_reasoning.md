Distributed once so far; expect the petition to be granted without further relists, or at most one relist. The federal government is the petitioner, and petitions filed by the Solicitor General are granted at very high rates. No CVSG is expected because the Solicitor General is already the petitioner. If granted, the Court is likely to take the case for plenary review rather than a summary GVR, as there are no obvious intervening decisions and four amicus briefs highlight the significance of the issue. Should it deny, a dissent from denial is possible given the interest of the 22 states and other amici.
