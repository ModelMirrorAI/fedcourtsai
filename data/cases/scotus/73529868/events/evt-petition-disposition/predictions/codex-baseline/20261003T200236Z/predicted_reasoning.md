# Forecast of the Court's action

The modal disposition is a grant, vacatur, and remand after the Court resolves
related immigration-detention litigation. The probability of any grant is
80%, including that GVR route; this is not a prediction that this particular
petition will necessarily receive briefing and argument.

The record shows exactly one distribution, for the September 28, 2026
conference, and no CVSG. From that state, I assign 85% to at least one further
distribution. The central path is one to three additional distributions,
with vehicle selection in autumn 2026 and a hold that could carry final
disposition into the spring or summer of 2027. A long hold need not generate
a distribution every conference. An early plenary grant remains the principal
alternative grant route.

The Court will not call for the Solicitor General's views: the federal
petitioners already appear through the Solicitor General. I assign 0.1% to
a new CVSG nevertheless, rather than treating an unobserved event as impossible.

If the Court grants plenary review here, the central statutory question will
be whether section 1225(b)(2)(A)'s mandatory-detention regime reaches
noncitizens arrested in the interior after entering without admission,
rather than section 1226(a)'s bond-eligible regime. This is a paraphrase from
the lower opinion, not a quotation of unavailable questions presented. The
constitutional question about individualized bond hearings is a distinct
issue; the Court is more likely to coordinate it with a related vehicle than
use this petition for an unrestricted constitutional ruling. The September
25 letter identifies that vehicle-selection choice but does not settle it.

Conditional on a grant, I assign 65% to disposition in the cert order itself,
predominantly a later GVR, and 35% to plenary review. A GVR would send the
case back for consideration of intervening guidance without itself resolving
every statutory and due-process issue. It would not be an unconditional
Supreme Court merits holding endorsing either side's construction.

If review is denied, I expect an order without a separate writing. The
probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial, conditional
on denial, is 15%. I do not predict a named author or a cert vote count.
