The Supreme Court will very likely decline to hear this death-penalty case, in which a Texas prisoner says his lead trial lawyer slept during testimony; the chance of any grant is about one in eleven. The petition mainly asks the Court to correct an error in one case, and federal law requires heavy deference to state courts in such challenges. A dissent in the appeals court, two supporting friend-of-the-court briefs and experienced counsel raise the odds somewhat. If the Court does act, a short order sending the case back is likelier than full argument.
