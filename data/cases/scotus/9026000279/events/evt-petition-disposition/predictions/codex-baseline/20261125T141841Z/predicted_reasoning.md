The petition has been distributed once, for the December 4 conference. I expect the Court to deny review after that conference, with zero further distributions, no call for the Solicitor General’s views, and no separate writing. If a Justice writes on denial, Justice Sotomayor is the likeliest author.

If the Court takes the case, I expect it to narrow the single question presented to whether clearly established Sixth Amendment law permits a state court to rely on the presence of co-counsel when the trial judge allowed only the allegedly sleeping lawyer to object to or cross-examine a witness. A short opinion vacating the judgment and returning the case to the Fifth Circuit is more likely than plenary review. That would let the Court address the panel’s demand for a factually matching precedent while leaving the consequences of the missed testimony to the lower court.
