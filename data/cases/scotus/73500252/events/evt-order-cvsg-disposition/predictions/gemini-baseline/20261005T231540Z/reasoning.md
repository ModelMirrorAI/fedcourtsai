P(grant) 0.85. The petition presents a direct, acknowledged circuit split (1st and 9th vs. 2nd Circuit) on a major issue of National Bank Act preemption of state interest-on-escrow laws. The Court previously addressed this in *Cantero*, remanding for a nuanced analysis, and the circuits have now split again on the application of that standard. 

The Court has called for the Solicitor General's views (CVSG). The SG previously recommended granting certiorari in this exact case (*Kivett*) prior to the Court deciding to grant *Cantero* instead. Given their past position, the OCC's interest in a uniform national banking standard, and the mature circuit split, the SG is highly likely to recommend a grant. 

The salience band is 'high', which typically has a risk-set grant rate of ~40% (Term 2024: 40.5%). However, the specific posture of a post-remand split combined with a CVSG and a highly likely affirmative SG recommendation elevates the probability well above the statistical baseline. The BIO itself concedes that if the Court grants any petition on this issue, *Kivett* is the best vehicle due to its developed evidentiary record. I predict the SG will recommend a grant and the Court will grant plenary review.
