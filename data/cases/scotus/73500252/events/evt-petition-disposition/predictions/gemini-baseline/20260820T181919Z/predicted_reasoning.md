Distributed twice so far (once before the BIO was requested, and now for the September 28 conference with full briefing). Expect at least one further relist (a true post-briefing relist) before a decision. The petition presents a clean circuit split (Second vs. First/Ninth) on the application of this Court's recent decision in *Cantero v. Bank of America* to state interest-on-escrow laws. The OCC's interest in national uniformity and the presence of significant industry amici make a grant highly likely. Given the federal preemption issues and the OCC's stated position, a CVSG is a distinct possibility if the Court wants the Solicitor General's formal view before granting, but they may simply grant outright to resolve the split quickly. If granted, the Court is likely to take the single question presented as written. A summary disposition is unlikely as the Court will need plenary review to resolve the circuit split. If denied, a dissent from denial is possible given the significant banking interests and the split, perhaps from a Justice sympathetic to national bank preemption.
