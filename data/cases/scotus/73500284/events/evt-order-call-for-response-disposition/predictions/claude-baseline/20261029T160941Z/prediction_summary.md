The insurance companies are asking the Supreme Court to hear their appeal (to "grant certiorari"). The question is whether federal law or Louisiana law decides when an insurer can enforce an arbitration clause in a contract it did not sign, under the international arbitration treaty. I think the Court will probably turn the case down, but the chance it takes the case is real, about one in four (27%). Appeals courts disagree on the question, and the Court left it open in 2020. The other side says the disagreement is overstated and that this case is a poor one for settling it.
