The Supreme Court is more likely to turn away this insurers' appeal than hear it, though I give review about a one-in-three chance (32%). The insurers argue that federal appeals courts disagree about which law governs enforcement of international arbitration agreements by companies that did not sign them. That issue has wider commercial importance, and the Court requested a response. But separate insurance contracts and Louisiana's special insurance rules complicate this case, making it a less straightforward way to resolve the disagreement.
