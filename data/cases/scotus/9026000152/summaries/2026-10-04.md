## What happened

Dora Adkins sued Rosslyn Syndicate, LC, the operator of a hotel, after she says she came into contact with chemical poisoning in two guest rooms she had paid to stay in. The district court denied her requests for emergency relief, including proposed emergency complaints and an injunction, and canceled a scheduled hearing.
