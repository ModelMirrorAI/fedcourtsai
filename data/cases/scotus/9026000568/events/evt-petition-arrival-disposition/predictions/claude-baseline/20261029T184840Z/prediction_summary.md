The government asks the Supreme Court not to decide this immigration-detention case on its own, but to set it aside until the Court decides a nearly identical case it has already agreed to hear (Rhoney v. Barbosa da Cunha). If the government wins that case, the Court will most likely send this one back to the lower court to reconsider — which counts as a grant here. If the government loses, the Court will turn this petition away. We think that is slightly better than a coin flip (about 56%) in the government's favor.
