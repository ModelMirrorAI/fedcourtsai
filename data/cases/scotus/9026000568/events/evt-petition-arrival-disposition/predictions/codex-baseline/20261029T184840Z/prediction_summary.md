The Court is likely to keep this petition waiting for another case already
accepted on the same immigration-detention question, then send it back to
the lower court for another look. I put the chance of some grant of review
at about four in five, chiefly through that brief send-back order rather
than a separate hearing. The dispute concerns whether immigrants arrested
inside the country can ask for release on bond while their removal cases
proceed. Denial remains possible if the earlier case supports the existing
lower-court ruling.
