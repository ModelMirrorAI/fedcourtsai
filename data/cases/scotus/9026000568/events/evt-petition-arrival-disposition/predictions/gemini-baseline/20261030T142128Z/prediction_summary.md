The Supreme Court is slightly more likely than not (about a 55% chance) to grant this petition, wipe out the lower court's ruling, and send the case back to the Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals for a new decision (a summary procedure known as "grant, vacate, and remand," or GVR). The federal government is appealing a ruling that noncitizens who entered the country without border inspection are eligible for bond hearings while in immigration custody. Because the Supreme Court already agreed on October 1, 2026, to decide this exact legal question in a lead case (*Rhoney v. Barbosa da Cunha*), the Court will hold this petition and then send it back for reconsideration if the government wins the lead case.
