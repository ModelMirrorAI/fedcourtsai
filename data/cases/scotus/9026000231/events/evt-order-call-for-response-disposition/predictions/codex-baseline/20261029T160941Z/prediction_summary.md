The Supreme Court is more likely to decline Mario Delaine's appeal, although I put the chance of review at about two in five (38%). His petition asks whether an intentional touching that unexpectedly causes serious injury can trigger a fifteen-year federal minimum sentence. The Court's request for a government response and disagreement among appeals courts make review plausible. But the lower court offers reasons those cases may differ, and asking for a response does not mean the Court will hear the case.
