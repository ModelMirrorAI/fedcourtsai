## Question and route if review is granted

The Court would most likely address whether intentional nonconsensual touching that causes serious injury, without a required mental state concerning that injury, satisfies ACCA's elements clause. A narrower formulation tied to Florida felony battery is more likely than reconsideration of all of Borden or of the categorical approach.

Conditional on a grant, plenary briefing and argument are substantially more likely than an immediate disposition: P(summary route | grant) is 12%. The dispute concerns the reach of a fractured precedent, not a new intervening decision plainly requiring remand. The Sixth Circuit supplied reasons for distinguishing the contrary authorities. If granted for argument, the Court would likely resolve whether the necessary intent attaches to the use of qualifying force against another person or additionally to the resulting harm; no unconditional merits holding is forecast here.
