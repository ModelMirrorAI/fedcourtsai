We predict the Supreme Court will most likely turn down Mario Delaine's request to hear his case. We put the chance it agrees to hear it at about one in five. Delaine argues that a prior battery conviction should not count toward the 15-year minimum sentence under the Armed Career Criminal Act. Federal appeals courts disagree on this question. The Court asked the government to respond, which raises the odds above the usual few percent. Still, most cases that get such a request are denied.
