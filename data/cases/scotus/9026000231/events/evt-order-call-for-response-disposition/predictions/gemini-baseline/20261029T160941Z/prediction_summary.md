The Supreme Court is unlikely to grant review in *Mario Delaine v. United States*, with roughly a one-in-seven chance (14% probability) of taking the case, so the petition will most likely be denied. Delaine challenges his 15-year mandatory minimum sentence under the Armed Career Criminal Act—a federal law imposing harsher penalties on repeat violent offenders—arguing that his prior Florida battery and assault convictions do not qualify as violent felonies. Although the Court asked the federal government to file a response, the lower court's ruling aligns with other federal appeals courts on the battery issue and rests on an independent state-law ground on the assault issue.
