# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny certiorari in Mario Delaine v. United States, without a separate writing. The probability of any grant, including a summary disposition, is 30%.

The snapshot records **one distribution**, on September 2, 2026, for the September 28 conference. It also records a September 4 request for a response after the United States waived its response, and an extension making that response due November 4. The Court will await the requested response and any reply, then distribute the petition again. I assign 97% to at least one additional distribution. This forecast primarily concerns re-distribution after completion of briefing, not an inference that the Court has already considered and relisted the petition. The modal path is one additional distribution followed by denial; a grant path is more likely to include one further actual relist. I expect disposition in late 2026 or early 2027, assuming no further response extensions or hold.

No CVSG appears in the snapshot. I assign only 0.1% to a future CVSG: the United States is already the respondent, and the requested response will provide its position without a separate invitation to the Solicitor General as a nonparty.

If the Court grants review, it will most likely take the petition's single question, focused on whether intentional nonconsensual touching that causes serious injury satisfies ACCA's elements clause without knowledge or intent as to the resulting injury. The conflict between the treatment of intentional force and the treatment of injury is the likely focus, not the separate timing question about interpretations of Florida aggravated assault discussed below. The issue would receive plenary briefing and argument rather than a summary ruling: P(summary route | grant) is 8%. No provisioned material identifies an intervening controlling decision or a lead case warranting a routine GVR. Conditional on plenary review, the Court would clarify how Borden's mens rea requirement attaches to the use of violent force; disagreement over that clarification makes summary reversal less likely than argument.

Conditional on denial, I assign 10% to a noted dissent or statement respecting denial, and predict none as the modal result. If a writing appears, Thomas is my leading individual candidate because the petition specifically invokes his Borden concurrence, with Gorsuch and Sotomayor secondary possibilities. Those are authorship possibilities, not a forecast of their undisclosed cert votes.
