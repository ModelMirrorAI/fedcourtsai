P(grant) 0.001. The arrival-moment base rate (the weakest band's bracketed reached rate over strictly prior Terms) is ~6.5% for paid petitions, but this case falls far below that baseline. The petitioners (Randy and Evgenia Quaid) are proceeding pro se, which strongly depresses the grant probability. Substantively, the petition asks the Court to correct the Ninth Circuit's application of California state law (the California litigation privilege, Cal. Civ. Code § 47(b)). The U.S. Supreme Court is highly unlikely to grant certiorari merely to correct a federal court of appeals' allegedly erroneous application of state law, especially where no circuit split on a federal question is implicated. There is also no federal interest that would prompt a CVSG. Given the lack of a split, the state-law nature of the question, and the pro se status of the petitioners, a grant is extremely improbable.
