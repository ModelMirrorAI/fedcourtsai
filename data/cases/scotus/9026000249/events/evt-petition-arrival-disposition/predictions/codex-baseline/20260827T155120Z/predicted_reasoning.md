# Forecast of the Court's disposition

The Court will deny the petition. From the arrival record's zero-distribution state, the petition will probably receive one ordinary initial conference distribution, but it will not be distributed a second time as a true relist.

The Court will not call for the views of the Solicitor General. The dispute is between private parties, and the lower-court history does not present an evident federal interest requiring the government's views.

If the Court grants any relief, it will more likely dispose of the matter in the certiorari order through a GVR or other summary route than set it for plenary briefing and argument. Any question taken would be narrowed to a procedural issue concerning the dismissal or prefiling order rather than the underlying private property dispute.

On denial, no Justice will note a dissent or issue a statement respecting the denial.
