# Evaluation of gemini-baseline

The predictor incorrectly called a grant with a probability of 0.52.

While the candidate properly grounded the initial baseline using the "elevated" salience band, the reasoning relies perhaps too heavily on heuristics (such as the presence of PLF as counsel and the number of amici) to push the probability sharply upward into "grant" territory. It mentions the vehicle concerns raised in the BIO but somewhat discounts them as "standard defensive posturing," which ultimately proved fatal to the petition. The analysis remains doctrinally sound, but the weighting of the factors was slightly miscalibrated.

The prediction was made cleanly in a forward posture with no outcome leakage.
