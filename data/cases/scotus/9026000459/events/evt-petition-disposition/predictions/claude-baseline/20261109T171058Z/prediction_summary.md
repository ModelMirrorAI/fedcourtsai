We predict the Supreme Court will most likely **turn down** Marshall Grace's appeal, giving about a one-in-five chance that it takes some action for him. The case asks whether a federal appeals court can decide for itself if a prosecutor struck a juror because of race, rather than sending that question back to the trial judge. The Court has asked the government to respond, which is a sign of interest. A recent related Supreme Court decision (Pitchford v. Cain) also makes it possible the Court sends the case back for a fresh look, but most cases like this are still denied.
