# Forecast — Grace v. United States, No. 26-459

**Posture.** Paid petition from a published Fifth Circuit decision (170 F.4th 980) affirming a federal drug conviction over a Batson challenge. The United States waived response (Oct 16); the petition was distributed once, for the Nov 6, 2026 conference; on Oct 28 the Court **requested a response**, due Nov 27, 2026. One distribution on the record, no relists, no CVSG.

**Further distribution.** Expect the petition to be distributed again once the government's brief in opposition is in (or its time runs out, or after an extension) — most likely for a conference in December 2026 or January 2027. A relist after that is plausible if the Court is weighing a GVR or plenary grant; I expect one to two further distributions in total.

**CVSG.** None. The United States is the respondent and is already filing at the Court's request, so there is nothing to invite.

**Disposition.** Denial is the most likely outcome. Expect the Solicitor General to argue that the Fifth Circuit reviewed the district court's step-three ruling for clear error and affirmed the result, so the claimed 4–2 split is not cleanly presented, and that the evidence of guilt was strong. The main alternative to denial is a **GVR in light of *Pitchford v. Cain*** (decided May 28, 2026, after the Fifth Circuit's judgment and its denial of rehearing), which the petition asks for in the alternative. Pitchford stresses that the trial court's step-three role is essential, and the government could choose not to oppose a remand. If the Court grants anything, a GVR is roughly as likely as plenary review.

**If granted plenary review,** the Court would take the single QP as written: whether a court of appeals that finds the district court's step-three findings inadequate may make those findings itself rather than remand (Pullman-Standard v. Swint).

**Dissent from denial.** Unlikely but possible. If one comes, it would most likely be a statement by Justice Sotomayor or Justice Jackson on how appellate courts handle Batson step three.
