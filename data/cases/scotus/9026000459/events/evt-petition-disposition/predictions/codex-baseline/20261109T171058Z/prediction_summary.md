The Supreme Court will most likely decline to hear Marshall Grace's case, although I put the chance of some relief at 28%, a little better than one in four. He argues that appeals judges improperly decided whether a prosecutor discriminated when removing a prospective juror, instead of returning that question to the trial judge. Conflicting appeals-court approaches and a recent related Supreme Court ruling make this petition stronger than most. If the Court intervenes, sending it back for reconsideration is likelier than a full hearing.
