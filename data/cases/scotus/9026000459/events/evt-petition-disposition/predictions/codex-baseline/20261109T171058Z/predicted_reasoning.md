# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny the petition after receiving the requested response from the United States. The denial will most likely be unexplained and unaccompanied by a separate writing.

The baseline docket shows **one distribution**, for the November 6, 2026 conference, and **no CVSG**. The October 28 response request sets a November 27 deadline. The petition will be distributed **at least once more** after that response and the opportunity for a reply, most likely in December 2026 or January 2027. **One additional distribution** is the central forecast. That redistribution need not represent a substantive relist after completed consideration; a grant path is more likely to involve an additional conference beyond it.

The Court will not call for the Solicitor General's views. It has instead requested a response from the United States, already a party, which supplies the government's position through ordinary adversarial briefing.

**Conditional on granting review**, the more likely route is a grant, vacatur, and remand for reconsideration in light of *Pitchford v. Cain*, rather than plenary briefing and argument. That route would ask the Fifth Circuit to reassess its treatment of the missing trial-level credibility determination, not itself find purposeful discrimination or order an automatic acquittal. A summary reversal deciding the ultimate discrimination issue is not the expected alternative.

**If the Court grants plenary review**, it will take the petition's single question substantially as written: whether a federal appellate court may make the necessary Batson step-three findings initially when the district court has failed to do so. It will not take the separately litigated sufficiency-of-drug-conspiracy-evidence issue. Any merits ruling would concern the proper factfinder and remedy, not a categorical ban on peremptory challenges.

**If review is denied**, no dissent or statement respecting denial is expected. If one appears, Sotomayor or Jackson is the likeliest author, focusing on the failure to obtain a proper trial-level determination before affirmance. That is a minority branch of the forecast, not an expectation of a written explanation for denial.
