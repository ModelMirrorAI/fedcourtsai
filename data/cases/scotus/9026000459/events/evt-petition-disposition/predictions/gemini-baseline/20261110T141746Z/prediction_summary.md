We predict that the Supreme Court is very likely to deny Marshall Grace's petition for review, giving it only about a 3.5 percent chance of being granted. Grace challenges his federal drug convictions by arguing that the appeals court should have sent his case back to the trial judge after finding a procedural mistake in how the judge handled a *Batson* challenge—an objection that prosecutors improperly excluded a Black prospective juror because of race. Because the federal government waived its right to respond and the trial record shows no evidence of racial discrimination, the Court is overwhelmingly likely to turn the appeal away.
