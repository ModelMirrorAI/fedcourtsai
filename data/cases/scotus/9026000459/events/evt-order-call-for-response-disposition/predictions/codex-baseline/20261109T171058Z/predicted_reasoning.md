# Forecast of the Court's handling

The Court will deny the petition after receiving the requested response and returning it to conference. The denial will leave the Fifth Circuit's judgment standing without an explanation of the merits; it will not endorse that court's Batson analysis.

The snapshot shows **one distribution**, on October 21, for the November 6 conference. I predict **one further distribution** after response briefing and then disposition, rather than a prolonged series of genuine relists. The probability of at least one additional distribution is 94%. Because the response deadline is November 27, that additional distribution is principally a briefing-and-scheduling forecast, not a prediction that the Court will consider the fully briefed petition at multiple conferences. Disposition is most likely during winter 2026–27.

The Court will not call for the Solicitor General's views. I assign a 0.5% probability to a new CVSG: the United States is already responding through the Solicitor General.

If the Court grants plenary review, it will take the single question presented, substantially as written: whether a federal appellate court may make the initial Batson step-three discriminatory-intent determination after finding the district court's necessary findings deficient. It will not take the unrelated sufficiency-of-the-drug-conspiracy-evidence issue discussed in the decision below. Conditional on plenary review, the likely ground is that credibility-dependent intent determinations ordinarily belong to the trial court, with remand necessary unless the record permits only one resolution; that is not a forecast that Grace necessarily wins a new trial.

Conditional on any grant, I assign **40%** to a disposition accompanying the cert order and **60%** to plenary briefing and argument. A GVR for reconsideration under Pitchford is the principal summary route; a summary merits reversal is much less likely. Pitchford's different procedural issue prevents the intervening decision from making summary relief the dominant conditional route.

I expect no dissent or statement respecting denial. The conditional probability of such a writing is **9%**. If a denial writing appears, Sotomayor is the leading author candidate, followed by Jackson; no individual writing is my modal forecast. The structured order-writer probabilities also allow separate writings accompanying a grant or summary order.
