The Supreme Court is likely to decline Marshall Grace's appeal, with about a one-in-four chance of accepting it or sending it back for another look. Grace argues that appeals judges improperly decided whether a prosecutor excluded a juror because of race, instead of asking the trial judge to decide. The Court's request for a government response and conflicting appeals-court approaches improve his chances, but the case may not cleanly present that disagreement. Expect another conference listing after the response, followed most likely by denial without explanation.
