# Forecast — Grace v. United States, No. 26-459

- **State:** distributed once (Conference of 11/6/2026, overtaken by the Oct 28 call for a response, due Nov 27, 2026). No relists. No CVSG.
- **Further distribution:** near-certain. After the SG's response and any reply come in, the petition will be redistributed, likely for a January 2027 conference. Expect one or two distributions before disposition. A relist or two is plausible if a GVR or summary disposition is under consideration.
- **CVSG:** none. The United States is the respondent and is already responding.
- **Disposition:** most likely denied (about 80%). Expect the SG to argue that the Fifth Circuit reviewed the district court's own step-three ruling for clear error and made no first-instance findings, and that Grace had and used an opportunity to argue pretext, so Pitchford does not apply.
- **Grant routes:** if the Court acts, a GVR in light of *Pitchford v. Cain* (decided after the judgment below) is about as likely as plenary review. That is especially true if the SG does not oppose remand.
- **If plenary review is granted:** the Court would take the QP as written. The question is whether a federal court of appeals may make Batson step-three findings in the first instance, or must remand under *Pullman-Standard*/*Icicle Seafoods*. The likely ground is remand-required.
- **Writing on a denial:** a separate writing is unlikely. If there is one, it would be a statement or dissent from Justice Sotomayor or Justice Jackson on the district court's sua sponte questioning of the juror.
