## What moves me up
- **The Court called for a response** (Oct 28, 2026) after the SG waived (Oct 16), with the petition distributed once (for the 11/6 conference). This means at least one chambers found the petition worth a closer look. Called-for-response paid petitions are granted far more often than the baseline-band population, but most are still denied once the response comes in.
- **Intervening decision.** The petition alternatively asks for a GVR in light of *Pitchford v. Cain*, 608 U.S. 391. A web search (see retrieval.md) puts that decision on May 28, 2026, after the Fifth Circuit's judgment (Mar 27) and its denial of rehearing (May 5). Pitchford was a 5-4 Kavanaugh opinion holding that Batson step three requires a chance for the defense to rebut the prosecutor's reasons. GVRs for a Batson decision issued after the judgment below are a standard route. That makes the GVR arm real and not just a remote possibility. The Court asked for the SG's response, and the SG's position on a GVR will probably decide this arm.
- **Claimed split and counsel.** The petition claims a 4-2 split (CA2/4/6/7 vs. CA5/9) on whether an appellate court may make Batson step-three findings in the first instance, with *Pullman-Standard* as the doctrinal hook. Counsel of record is Paul Hughes (McDermott), an experienced Supreme Court practitioner. The Fifth Circuit opinion is published (170 F.4th 980).

## What holds me down
- **Vehicle.** Reading the opinion below (decision-below.txt, which matches App. 1a), the Fifth Circuit says it reviewed for clear error and that the district court "correctly denied" the challenge. The district court did rule on the challenge, although on a reason it elicited from the juror itself. The SG will likely say no appellate factfinding happened, so the split is not implicated. It will also say the defense did argue pretext at trial, so Pitchford's rebuttal-opportunity holding does not apply. The evidence of guilt is overwhelming (confession, 441 g of meth), but a Batson error is structural, so that cuts less here.
- **The split may be thinner than claimed.** Several of the cited cases are habeas or unpublished decisions.
- The fifth-circuit row in the statpack (gvr 2.3%, granted 1.6%) shows the court below gives no special lift.
