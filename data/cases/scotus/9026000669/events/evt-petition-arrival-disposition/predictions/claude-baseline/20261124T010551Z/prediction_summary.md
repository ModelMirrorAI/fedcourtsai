The government asked the Supreme Court to put this immigration case on hold until it decides a related case, D.V.D., that raises the same question about whether courts can hear challenges to how a deportation order is carried out. The Court will likely wait and then, about three chances in four, grant the petition, most probably by sending the case back to the lower court to reconsider (a "GVR", meaning grant, vacate and remand) rather than hearing argument. If the government loses in D.V.D., the petition will be denied.
