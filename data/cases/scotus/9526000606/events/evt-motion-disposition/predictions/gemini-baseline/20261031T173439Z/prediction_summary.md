Gina Silva and her family are very likely to have their emergency request for a stay of removal (a temporary court order halting deportation) denied, with only a 1% chance (one in one hundred) that the Supreme Court grants relief. Representing themselves without a lawyer, the applicants ask Justice Alito to block their deportation to Colombia after the Third Circuit Court of Appeals upheld the denial of their asylum and related protection claims. Because the lower court's unpublished ruling rested on routine factual findings and arguments that were not properly raised before the immigration board, rather than a broader legal disagreement among federal courts, the Supreme Court is almost certain to deny the stay without requesting a response from the government.
