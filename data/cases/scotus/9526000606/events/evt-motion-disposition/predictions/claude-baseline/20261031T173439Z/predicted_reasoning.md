# Forecast — 26A606, Silva v. Blanche (stay of removal)

The record shows one entry: on Oct 16, 2026 a self-represented application for a
stay of removal (Third Circuit No. 25-2933) was submitted to Justice Alito as Circuit
Justice. No response has been requested, the application has not been referred, and no
amicus filings appear.

- **Disposition:** denied. I expect Justice Alito to deny it alone, without calling for
  a response, likely within one to three weeks. An unqualified grant is very unlikely
  (~1%).
- **Response requested:** unlikely (~4%). Pro se removal stays almost never get a call
  to the Solicitor General.
- **Referral to the full Court:** somewhat possible (~12%). This mainly happens if the
  applicants renew the application to a second Justice after Alito denies it, and that
  Justice refers it to the Court. That is a common shape for pro se renewals, and if it
  happens the full Court denies.
- **Amicus:** none expected (~1%).
- **Separate writing:** none expected. A noted dissent from the denial (most plausibly
  Sotomayor or Jackson) would only come with a referral, and even then it is rare.

Conditionally, if a stay were granted, it would most likely be a temporary
administrative stay pending a called-for response, and not relief on the merits.
