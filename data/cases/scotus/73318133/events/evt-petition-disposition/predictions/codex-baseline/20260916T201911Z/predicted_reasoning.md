# Forecast

The Court will deny the petition, most likely on an orders list following the September 28, 2026 conference. The provisioned docket records exactly one distribution, entered June 24 for that conference, and no CVSG. I expect zero additional distributions and no invitation to the Solicitor General. The probability of another distribution is 2%; the probability of a new CVSG is 0.1%.

The petition will not attract review as a vehicle for nationwide marijuana regulation, antitrust restructuring, or aviation oversight. The underlying dispute, as described in the petition, concerns summary judgment in an individual wrongful-termination action and the evidentiary basis for the employee's reported suspicions. The Court will leave that judgment undisturbed without explaining its denial; this forecasts the procedure, not a merits endorsement of Boeing's position or a finding about the petitioner's allegations.

If review is granted, the most plausible question is a narrowed reformulation of the first and eighth questions: whether the reasonable-cause analysis governing the employee's retaliation claim properly accounts for his reports of suspected conduct prohibited by federal law. I do not expect the Court to take the nine questions as an omnibus request to impose corporate penalties or invalidate state laws. This conditional question selection does not assume that the issue was preserved or actually decided in the required form below.

Conditional on any grant, I assign a 25% probability to disposition in the cert order itself and 75% to further merits proceedings. No intervening decision or companion case in the consulted material supplies a concrete GVR predicate, so plenary review is the more likely route within that very small grant branch.

Conditional on denial, I expect no noted dissent or statement respecting denial; the probability of any such separate expression is 0.2%. I forecast no individual Justice's cert vote or writing.
