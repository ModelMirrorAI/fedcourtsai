# Forecast: what the Court will do with No. 25-1270, Veto v. The Boeing Company

**Disposition.** The petition will be denied, almost certainly at or shortly
after the September 28, 2026 conference for which it has been distributed. It
will appear on the order list as a one-line denial with no separate writing.

**Relists.** The docket shows **one** distribution (entered June 24, 2026, for
the conference of September 28, 2026) and no response or waiver from Boeing. I
forecast **no further distribution**: this is a pro se paid petition presenting
no legal question the Court could take, and such petitions are disposed of at
their first conference. The residual chance of a second distribution entry is
administrative (a reschedule of the long conference's overflow, or the Court
requesting a response it has not yet requested) rather than any Justice's
interest.

**CVSG.** The Court will not call for the views of the Solicitor General. The
petition names federal statutes (21 U.S.C. § 802, FAA delegation rules,
Export-Import Bank lending) but no federal party, no federal program whose
administration is in question, and no question the Government could usefully
brief.

**Which question presented, if any were taken.** None is takeable as written.
The nine numbered "questions" ask what penalty Boeing should suffer, whether
Boeing is a monopoly, whether the Export-Import Bank confers unfair advantage,
and whether the Ninth Circuit may characterize the case as a "diversity"
termination. The only question that touches the judgment below is the eighth
(the Ninth Circuit's treatment of the appeal), and that is a fact-bound
complaint about an unpublished memorandum affirming summary judgment on
California Labor Code retaliation claims. A reformulation into a certworthy
question does not exist in this record.

**Summary disposition.** In the remote event of any grant, it would not be
plenary review of these questions; it would have to be a GVR-type order, and no
intervening decision of this Court bears on a California-law whistleblower
retaliation claim. So the summary route and plenary route are both
vanishingly unlikely and I place them roughly even conditional on a grant.

**Dissent from denial.** None. No Justice has a stake in the questions raised,
and the petition's framing (marijuana use at a Boeing facility as a Supremacy
Clause and Commerce Clause violation, Boeing's patents as a monopoly) will not
draw a statement respecting the denial.
