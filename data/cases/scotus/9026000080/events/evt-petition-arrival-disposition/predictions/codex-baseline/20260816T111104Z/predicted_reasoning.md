# Forecast

The Court will not call for the views of the Solicitor General. The dispute is between an individual and a municipality over the application of settled summary-judgment and employment-discrimination rules, with no identified federal interest requiring the Solicitor General's participation.

If the Court takes any question, it will narrow the first question to whether the Fifth Circuit impermissibly resolved genuine factual disputes against the nonmovant under *Tolan v. Cotton*. It will not take the broader second and third questions as written. Conditional on a grant, a summary disposition is substantially more likely than plenary review: the plausible route is a per curiam summary reversal for failure to view the record in the nonmovant's favor, rather than a GVR based on an intervening authority.

If the petition is denied, no Justice will note a dissent from denial or issue a statement respecting the denial.
