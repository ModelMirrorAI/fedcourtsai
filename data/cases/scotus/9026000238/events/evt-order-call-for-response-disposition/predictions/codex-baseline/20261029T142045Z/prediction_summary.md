The Supreme Court will probably decline to hear this dispute over music-performance royalties. I put the chance of any grant of review at about one in four, or 24%. Asking the concert promoters to respond shows interest, but does not promise a hearing. The central dispute is how closely appeals judges should revisit trial judges' valuation decisions. The Court could instead wait for a related trademark case and then send this case back for reconsideration; that possibility accounts for much of the chance of intervention.
