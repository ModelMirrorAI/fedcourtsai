We expect the Supreme Court to turn down BMI's request to review this case, but the chance it steps in is real: about one in five. The case asks how closely an appeals court should second-guess a trial judge's finding of a "fair market" royalty rate for music played at concerts. The Court asked the other side to respond, and music groups have filed supporting briefs. A similar pending case, RiseandShine v. PepsiCo, could lead the Court to send this one back for another look instead.
