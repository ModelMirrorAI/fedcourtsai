The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Broadcast Music, Inc.’s petition for review, with an estimated 14% chance (about one in seven) of granting the case. Although the Court asked the concert promoters' association to file a response—an initial sign of interest—and BMI has top-tier appellate counsel challenging how intensely appeals courts may second-guess a trial judge's fair-market-value calculations, several factors weigh heavily against review. Most importantly, the Second Circuit sent the case back to the trial court to recalculate the music-licensing rate, meaning the dispute is not yet final, and the legal rules governing music-licensing consent decrees apply only in New York federal courts rather than dividing federal appeals courts nationwide.
