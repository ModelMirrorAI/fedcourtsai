The federal consent-decree setting gives the government a potential interest, but the question is principally about appellate review of valuation findings.

If review is granted for plenary consideration, the Court will most likely address whether selection and weighing of valuation benchmarks are predominantly factual determinations reviewed for clear error, while preserving de novo review of genuinely legal constraints. That is a narrower framing of BMI's question than treating every component of every fair-market valuation identically. Conditionally on review, the likely doctrinal focus is the distinction between factual application and legal methodology, not the correct royalty percentage itself.

Conditional on any grant, I assign **35%** to disposition in the cert order and **65%** to plenary review. A hold followed by a GVR connected to the standard-of-review issue identified in the petition's RiseandShine discussion is the principal summary-route possibility; I do not assume that case's status or result. Absent such intervening guidance, plenary review is more likely than summary correction. A hold could extend resolution beyond the modal winter window.

Conditional on denial, I expect **no separate writing**. The probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial is **3.5%**; I do not predict an individual Justice's vote or authorship.
