## In brief

Broadcast Music, Inc. asks the Court to review how closely appeals courts should examine a judge's ruling setting music royalty rates.

## What happened

Broadcast Music, Inc. (BMI), which licenses songwriters' music to users including concert promoters, asked a federal district court to set a reasonable royalty rate for live concerts, after rate negotiations with the North American Concert Promoters Association (NACPA) broke down. Following a five-week trial, the district court set a rate of 0.5% of a broadened revenue base. NACPA appealed, and the Second Circuit vacated that decision, reviewing the rate-setting components independently rather than deferring to the trial court's findings, and sent the case back. The Second Circuit then denied rehearing.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether an appeals court should independently re-examine, rather than defer to, a trial judge's determination of a fair market royalty rate and the underlying choices that go into it, such as which comparison agreements to rely on. BMI's petition says nine other appeals courts defer to trial judges on these fact-heavy questions, while the Second Circuit alone reviews them independently.

## Where it stands

The petition was distributed for the Court's September 28, 2026 conference. NACPA waived its right to respond, but the Court then requested a response, which was due October 13, 2026; NACPA has since received an extension to file it by November 2, 2026.

## What each outcome would mean

BMI, which lost in the Second Circuit, is asking the Court to take up the case. If the Court grants review, it would hear the case fully and decide which standard of review applies, without yet deciding who wins. If it denies review, the Second Circuit's ruling vacating the rate and sending the case back to the district court stays in place, a result favoring NACPA's position in that lower court proceeding. BMI's petition also suggests, as an alternative, holding the case pending a related decision in another appeal.
