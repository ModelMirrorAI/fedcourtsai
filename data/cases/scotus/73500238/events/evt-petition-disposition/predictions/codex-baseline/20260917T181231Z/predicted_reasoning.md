# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny the petition. The modal path is an ordinary orders-list denial following the September 28, 2026 conference, in late September or early October, without an opinion or separate statement. This is a forecast of discretionary review, not approval of the conviction or the reasoning below.

The baseline contains **one distribution**, entered June 17 for that conference, and no CVSG. I expect **zero further distributions**. The probability of at least one additional distribution is **15%**; if that occurs, one additional distribution is more likely than a prolonged series. The summer interval before the scheduled first conference is not itself a relist or a hold.

I expect **no CVSG**; its incremental probability is **0.1%**. The United States is already the respondent and waived its response. If the Court seeks the government's position, an ordinary request for a response is the more plausible mechanism, not an invitation to participate as a nonparty. I expect the waiver to remain undisturbed on the modal denial path.

If review is granted, the most likely question is a narrowed version of QP 1: whether forfeiture permits ordinary plain-error review when prosecutors knowingly exploit materially false testimony in summation. That question is more likely to draw review than both questions together. Conditional on reaching it, the Court is likely to distinguish the prosecution's continuing correction duty from the defendant's obligation to preserve an appellate claim, rather than announce that every uncorrected falsehood automatically requires a new trial. QP 2 would instead focus on whether an appellate harmlessness analysis can substitute for the missing reliability inquiry, not whether every lay/expert classification error requires reversal.

Conditional on any grant, I assign **25%** to disposition in the cert order itself and **75%** to plenary proceedings. A Glossip-based GVR is the principal summary possibility, but Glossip already preceded the amended decision below; it is not a newly intervening decision after that judgment. Plenary review is the likelier grant route if the Court wants to resolve the preservation issue or the asserted evidentiary conflict.

Conditional on denial, the probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial is **3.5%**. The forecast is no separate writing; I do not forecast individual cert votes.
