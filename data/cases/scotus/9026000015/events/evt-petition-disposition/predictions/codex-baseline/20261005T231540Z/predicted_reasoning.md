# Forecast

The Court will most likely deny this petition after receiving the requested response and considering the completed briefing. P(any grant), including a summary reversal or GVR, is **24%**. I expect disposition in late 2026 or early 2027, rather than treating the originally scheduled September conference as dispositive.

The provisioned record shows **one distribution**, on July 29 for the September 28, 2026 conference, followed by a July 30 response request. The response deadline was subsequently extended to October 30. I assign **93%** to at least one additional distribution entry. My modal forecast is one additional distribution after briefing, with a second possible if the Court examines the petition more closely. The first additional entry would likely represent rescheduling after the requested response, not substantive reconsideration after an actual conference. The scored claim counts the entry either way.

There is no CVSG on the record. I expect none, assigning **4%** to a future invitation. This is principally a dispute between private religious claimants and a municipal government; the petition does not establish a need for the federal government's participation before disposition.

If review is granted, the central issue will be QP1's boundary between accepting a claimant's sincere account of religious practice and adjudicating a legally substantial burden. Because the decision below also rests on strict scrutiny, I expect review to address the linked least-restrictive-means issue in QP2 rather than resolve the judgment through QP1 alone. A narrowed formulation separating deference to religious beliefs from the legal sufficiency of a burden is more likely than wholesale acceptance of the petition's characterization of the lower courts.

Conditional on a grant, I assign **25%** to disposition in the cert order itself and **75%** to plenary review. The Court could summarily correct an apparent failure to respect sincere religious beliefs, but the alternative strict-scrutiny holding and disputed treatment of accommodation evidence favor briefing and argument if it intervenes. I identify no verified intervening decision requiring a GVR.

Conditional on denial, I assign **22%** to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. The modal forecast is an unexplained denial with no separate writing. I do not forecast a named Justice or infer a cert vote lineup from the absence of a public writing.
