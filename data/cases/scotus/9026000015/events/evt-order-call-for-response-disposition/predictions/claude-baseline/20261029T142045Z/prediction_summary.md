The Supreme Court will most likely turn this case down; the chance it agrees to hear it is about one in seven. Two leaders of a Native American church want to stop San Antonio from cutting trees and driving away birds at a riverside site in a city park where they hold ceremonies. The Court has asked the city to respond, and many outside groups support the leaders. That raises the odds. But the lower court ruled at an early stage of the lawsuit and mostly applied Texas law, so the case is a weaker candidate.
