# Forecast — Perez v. City of San Antonio, No. 26-15 (response-requested moment)

**Call: denied** (P(grant) ≈ 0.14).

- **Docket state forecast from.** One distribution (for the 9/28/2026 conference),
  then the Court called for a response on 7/30/2026 after the City waived. No
  relist and no CVSG on the record at this moment.
- **Further distributions.** Near-certain: the petition is redistributed once the
  City's brief in opposition is in (its time now runs to 10/30/2026), most likely
  for a December 2026 or January 2027 conference. Expect one or two
  conferences before a decision; a relist or two is plausible given amicus
  volume and a probable written dissent being prepared.
- **CVSG.** Not expected. The respondent is a city, the claims are Free Exercise
  and Texas RFRA, and no federal program is implicated.
- **If granted, which QP.** The Court would more likely take QP 1 (the
  substantial-burden question — whether a court may second-guess a claimant's
  account of what the religious exercise requires), possibly reformulated to
  the federal Free Exercise burden inquiry; QP 2 (least-restrictive means after
  pre-litigation notice) is the more fact-bound and likelier to be left aside.
- **Summary disposition.** Unlikely. No intervening decision obviously bears on
  the Fifth Circuit's ruling, and a per curiam reversal on a contested
  preliminary-injunction record with a state-law overlay is improbable.
- **Should it deny.** A written dissent from denial is a real possibility,
  most likely from Justice Gorsuch (who has written on Native American
  sacred-site claims before), possibly joined by Justice Thomas; Justice Alito
  is a possible joiner or writer.
