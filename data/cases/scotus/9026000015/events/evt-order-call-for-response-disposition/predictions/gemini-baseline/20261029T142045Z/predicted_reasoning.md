## Conditional Merits Scope (If Granted)

If the Court grants certiorari, it would likely grant on both questions presented—or consolidate them to address (1) whether courts assessing a religious burden may override a claimant's sincere theological understanding of sacred-site religious exercise based on alternative park access, and (2) whether a government defendant can satisfy strict scrutiny's least-restrictive-means requirement through post-litigation rationales after admitting it refused to consider accommodations beforehand. On the merits, if granted, the Court would likely vacate or reverse the Fifth Circuit's judgment, holding that under *Fulton* and *Ramirez* the City failed to carry its strict-scrutiny burden under TRFRA and the Free Exercise Clause when it obtained variances from its own tree-preservation ordinances while refusing to evaluate less-restrictive engineering designs prior to litigation.
