We predict the Supreme Court will likely deny review in *Perez v. City of San Antonio*, though the petition has a substantial, roughly two-in-five (42%) chance of being granted. Ceremonial leaders of the Lipan Native American Church challenge San Antonio's plan to remove trees and deter nesting cormorants at a sacred riverbend in Brackenridge Park. Although a six-judge Fifth Circuit dissent and twelve friend-of-the-court briefs strongly favor review, the Court recently declined a similar Indigenous sacred-site case (*Apache Stronghold*) and may hesitate to intervene in municipal park management.
