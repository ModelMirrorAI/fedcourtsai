Distributed once so far (prior to the request for response), expect the petition to be distributed and then relisted once or twice as the Court considers the deep circuit split on class-action incentive awards. No CVSG is expected, as the federal government's interests are not directly implicated. If granted, the Court would take the single question presented as written to resolve the 5-1 split with the Eleventh Circuit. A summary disposition is unlikely, as the issue requires plenary review. Should the Court deny, a dissent from denial by conservative Justices interested in curbing class-action abuses is possible.
