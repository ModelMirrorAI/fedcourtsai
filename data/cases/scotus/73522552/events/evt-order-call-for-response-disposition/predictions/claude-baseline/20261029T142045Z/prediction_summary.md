We expect the Supreme Court to turn down Eric Isaacson's appeal, with only about a one-in-seventeen chance (6%) that it agrees to hear it. Isaacson objected to a $5,000 "service award" paid to the person who led a class action against The New York Times. He argues that century-old Supreme Court decisions forbid such payments. One appeals court agrees with him, but five others do not. The Court asked the other side to respond, which is a mild sign of interest. Still, it has passed on this question before, and this small-dollar case is a weak vehicle for deciding it.
