The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Isaacson v. Moses*, with only about a 1 in 17 chance (6%) of taking the case. Petitioner Eric Alan Isaacson, a class member objecting to a consumer class-action settlement with The New York Times, asks the Court to ban "incentive awards"—modest bonus payments (here, $5,000) given to the lead plaintiff who stepped forward to bring the lawsuit—arguing that 1880s Supreme Court precedents forbid them. Although the Court asked the respondents to file a written response and one federal appeals court agrees with Isaacson, ten other federal appeals courts allow these payments, and the Supreme Court has repeatedly turned down nearly identical appeals in recent years.
