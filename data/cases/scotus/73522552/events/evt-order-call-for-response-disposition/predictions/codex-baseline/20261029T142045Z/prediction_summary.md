The Supreme Court is likely to decline this case; I estimate about a one-in-five chance, or 22%, that it agrees to review it. The dispute concerns whether people who represent a group in a class-action settlement may receive extra payments for their work. Federal appeals courts disagree, and the Court's request for a response shows some interest. But similar disputes have previously been turned away, and a response request does not mean the Court has decided to take the case.
