# Predicted course — Stevens-Reynolds v. Wilshire Insurance Co., No. 26-87

**Disposition: cert denied at the conference of November 6, 2026, with no relist.**

- **Where the docket stands.** One distribution so far (DISTRIBUTED for Conference of
  11/6/2026, entered Oct 7, 2026), never relisted, no CVSG. The respondents filed a brief in
  opposition on Sep 21, 2026, after an extension. Nobody had to call for it.
- **Relists.** I expect none. The petition will go on the November 6 order list as a denial
  without drawing a second conference. A relist would need a Justice to take an interest
  in an unpublished summary-calendar affirmance, and nothing in this record would draw one.
- **CVSG.** I expect none. This is a private insurance dispute governed by Louisiana law and the
  Federal Rules' ordinary sanction provisions, and no federal interest is involved.
- **Question the Court would take, if it took one.** At most, QP 1 narrowed to whether a court
  must make willfulness/bad-faith or prejudice findings before a Rule 37(c)(1) exclusion that is
  effectively case-dispositive. QPs 2 and 3 would fall away. QP 2 restates QP 1 as a
  sequencing complaint, and QP 3 asks for a duty the Federal Rules do not impose.
- **Summary route.** A GVR or summary reversal is not a realistic path. No intervening decision
  bears on the issue. If the Court did grant, the most plausible form would still be a
  summary disposition rather than plenary review, because the vehicle is poor for argument.
- **Dissent from denial.** I expect no separate writing.
