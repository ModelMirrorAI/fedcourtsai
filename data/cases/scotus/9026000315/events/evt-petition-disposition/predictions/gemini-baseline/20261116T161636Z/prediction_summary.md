The Supreme Court will likely deny review in *Goldman v. Phillips*, with about a one-in-seven chance (14%) of taking the case. Although the federal appeals courts are genuinely divided over when a Chapter 7 bankruptcy trustee—the court-appointed official who winds down a debtor's assets—can be sued personally for mistakes, and the Court has asked the opposing side to respond, this dispute arrives too early. Because the lower court merely sent the case back for further proceedings where the trustee can still win on other threshold defenses, the Justices will likely wait for a final judgment before resolving the split.
