The Supreme Court will probably decline to hear this case, with roughly a one-in-six chance it agrees to. A bankruptcy trustee asks the Court to decide when trustees can be personally sued for mistakes in handling an estate, a question on which federal appeals courts disagree. The Court has asked the other side to respond, which shows some interest, and the petition is well presented. But the lower-court ruling sends the case back for more proceedings, which often leads the Court to pass.
