# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny certiorari after receiving the requested response. The probability of any grant is **36%**. The central reason to take the case is the disagreement over the degree of fault necessary to impose personal liability on a Chapter 7 trustee. The central reason to pass on this vehicle is that the decision only permits litigation to continue: the bankruptcy court still must address other defenses, and immunity may be established on a fuller record.

## Distributions and the response

The baseline contains **one distribution**, entered October 28 for the November 13, 2026 conference, and **no recorded redistributions**. A response was then requested on November 4, due December 4. The Court will ordinarily await that response and an opportunity for a reply before considering disposition; the November 13 listing will not itself yield the predicted terminal action.

I assign **98.5%** to at least one additional distribution. The modal path is **one further distribution**, followed by denial without a writing. A second additional distribution is a meaningful alternative if the Justices consider plenary review. The first new distribution will mainly reflect completion of requested briefing, not necessarily a genuine relist after completed conference consideration. Absent extensions or a CVSG, disposition is most likely in early 2027.

## Solicitor General

No CVSG has been issued. I forecast **no future CVSG**, with a **28%** chance that the Court instead calls for federal views after the requested response, most plausibly during the first several conferences considering the completed papers in early 2027. On that alternative, resolution would be delayed pending the government's submission. The U.S. Trustee Program's interest in trustee standards makes consultation plausible even though the United States is not a party. No present government recommendation is assumed.

## Scope and route if review is granted

If granted, the Court will most likely focus the question on **whether a Chapter 7 trustee may be held personally liable for negligent performance of non-adjudicative estate-administration duties, absent court-authorized-action immunity, or whether a heightened fault standard is required**. That focuses the petition's single question on the conflict actually driving the judgment, rather than eliminating personal liability for all official acts.

Conditional on a grant, **96.5%** of the probability is on plenary consideration rather than disposition in the cert order. Only **3.5%** is on a GVR or another summary route. The mature disagreement and the opinion's distinct immunity doctrines require a reasoned choice among standards; no supplied intervening decision supplies a straightforward remand instruction.

## Writings on disposition

The denial will most likely be silent. Conditional on denial, I assign **4%** to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. If someone writes, Thomas is the leading individual possibility, followed by Alito and Gorsuch; no particular Justice is predicted to write as the modal outcome. The structured `order-writers` probabilities are unconditional and count authorship, not simply joining another Justice's writing. No majority opinion, merits author, or merits vote split is forecast for a denial.
