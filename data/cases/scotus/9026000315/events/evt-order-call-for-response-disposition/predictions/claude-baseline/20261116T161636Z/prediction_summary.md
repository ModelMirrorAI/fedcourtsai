The Supreme Court will probably decline to hear this case, though it has a real chance: about one in five. A bankruptcy trustee asks the Court to decide when trustees can be personally sued for mistakes made on the job, and federal appeals courts have answered differently for decades. The Court has asked the other side to respond, a sign of some interest, and retired bankruptcy judges support review. But most petitions that reach this step are still turned down, and the disagreement is old.
