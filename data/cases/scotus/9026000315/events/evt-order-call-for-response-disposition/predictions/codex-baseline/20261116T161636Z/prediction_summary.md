The Supreme Court will probably decline to hear Amy Goldman's case, although I give review a 38% chance—about two in five. The dispute asks when a bankruptcy trustee, who manages assets for creditors, must personally pay for mistakes. Federal appeals courts use conflicting standards, making this a stronger candidate than most appeals. But the case still has unresolved proceedings, and the requested response has not yet been filed in the supplied record. If accepted, it would likely receive full briefing and argument.
