## In brief

The federal government asks the Court to revive the prosecution role of an acting U.S. Attorney whom lower courts found was unlawfully installed under a federal vacancy-filling law.

## What happened

When Nevada's U.S. Attorney resigned, a first assistant briefly served as acting head under a federal vacancy law. The Attorney General later appointed Sigal Chattah as interim U.S. Attorney, and when her term neared its limit, she resigned and was immediately redesignated "First Assistant," continuing to lead the office. Several defendants indicted during her tenure argued she was serving unlawfully and moved to disqualify her. The district court agreed and disqualified her, but declined to dismiss the indictments. The Ninth Circuit affirmed the disqualification and dismissed the defendants' cross-appeal of the refusal to dismiss indictments.

## What the Court is being asked

The case asks whether a person named "first assistant" only after a leadership vacancy already began can automatically become the acting head of that office under the vacancy law's default rule, and separately, whether that same law bars an agency head from delegating essentially all of a vacant office's duties to one person as a workaround to the law's limits on acting officials.

## Where it stands

The petition was distributed after full briefing, including an opposition brief filed in November 2026; the docket does not yet show a ruling on whether the Court will hear the case.

## What each outcome would mean

The federal government, as petitioner, asks the Court to overturn the Ninth Circuit's ruling against it. If the Court grants review and eventually rules for the government, Chattah's authority to lead the office could be restored and similar designations elsewhere could continue. If it denies review or rules for the respondents (Jackson and the other defendants), the Ninth Circuit's decision disqualifying Chattah stands, limiting how the government can fill such vacancies. A vacate-and-remand would leave the question unresolved and send the case back.
