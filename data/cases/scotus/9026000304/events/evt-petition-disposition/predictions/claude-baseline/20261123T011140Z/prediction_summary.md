The Supreme Court will likely agree to hear this case, with a probability of about seven in ten. The federal government is asking the Court to review a ruling that a prosecutor was not lawfully serving as acting U.S. Attorney for Nevada under the law on filling vacant Senate-confirmed posts. The Court usually takes cases the government's top lawyer says are important, and three appeals courts have now rejected a long-standing government practice. The main doubts are that those courts all agree and that a parallel case may be chosen instead.
