The Court will grant the United States’ petition for briefing and oral argument on both questions as presented. The docket shows one distribution, for the December 4 conference, and no relist yet. I expect one further distribution before the grant. The Solicitor General filed the petition and reply, so there is no occasion to ask for the government’s views.

The two questions address independent grounds for Sigal Chattah to supervise the Nevada prosecutions. The first asks whether someone named first assistant after a vacancy arises can serve as acting officer under 5 U.S.C. § 3345(a)(1). The second asks whether § 3347(a) permits an agency head to give that person all the duties of the vacant office that may lawfully be delegated. Both grounds were decided below. I expect the Court to hear them in Jackson and hold No. 26-348. The disputed reach of the Federal Circuit’s patent-office precedent calls for argument, and no intervening decision supplies an answer that the Ninth Circuit could simply apply on remand. If the Court denies review, I expect no Justice to note a dissent or issue a statement.
