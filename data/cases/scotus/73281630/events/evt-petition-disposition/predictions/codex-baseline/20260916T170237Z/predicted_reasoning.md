# Forecast

The Court will most likely deny certiorari in D. A. v. Tri County Area Schools. The probability of any grant, including a summary route, is 32%.

The snapshot records **two distributions**: May 12 for the May 28, 2026 conference, and July 1 for the September 28, 2026 conference. A response was requested between them, on May 18. From this two-distribution vantage, I expect **no additional distribution** as the modal result, with a **42% probability of at least one more**. If the Court continues considering the petition, one or two additional distributions are the likeliest continuation. The modal timing is denial in the first order list following the September 28 conference; a continued case is more likely to resolve during October or November 2026 than to remain under consideration for many months.

No CVSG appears in the snapshot. The Court will probably decide without seeking the Solicitor General's views; the probability of a new CVSG is **1%**. If one issues, the likely window is the early fall conferences, and the petition would then remain pending substantially longer.

If review is granted, the Court will most likely take the petition's single question in substance: whether Fraser permits suppression of nondisruptive political speech that is not plainly profane or lewd. A useful narrowing would focus on passive political apparel conveying a profane message by euphemism, and on the deference due school officials' classification of that message. Conditional on review, the likely doctrinal dispute is whether political expression in sanitized words remains protected under Tinker or falls within Fraser because of its understood meaning; this is not a prediction that a denial will decide that dispute.

**Plenary review is substantially more likely than a summary disposition if the Court grants**: P(summary disposition in the cert order | grant) is **8%**. No intervening controlling decision supporting a GVR has been identified in the material consulted. Resolving the disputed boundary between political euphemism and vulgarity would more naturally call for briefing and argument than a summary reversal.

On denial, I expect no separate writing, but assign **25% conditional probability** to a noted dissent or statement respecting denial. Such a writing would likely focus on the risk of viewpoint discrimination through school officials' characterization of political speech. I make no individual-Justice prediction.
