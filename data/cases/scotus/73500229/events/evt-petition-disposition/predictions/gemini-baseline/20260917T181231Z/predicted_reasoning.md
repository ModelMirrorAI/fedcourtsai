Distributed once so far (for the 9/28/2026 conference); expect one further relist before a decision on the petition. The Court requested a response after the state waived its right to respond, indicating some interest in the FDCA preemption question, but the lack of an overt circuit split and the fading salience of COVID-era mandates suggest the Court will ultimately decline to hear it. No CVSG is expected, as the federal government's interest is unlikely to be sought at this late stage for a state mandate issue, though the PREP Act is a federal statute.

If granted, the Court would likely focus on QP 1 regarding whether the FDCA's EUA voluntary consent provisions preempt state mandates. A summary GVR is unlikely because there is no recent intervening decision from this Court on the PREP Act's preemptive scope. Should the Court deny review, a dissent from denial by Justice Thomas or Justice Alito is a distinct possibility, given their past writings on COVID-19 mandates and religious liberties, though this petition focuses more on statutory preemption.
