# Forecast

The petition has been distributed zero times and no CVSG appears on the docket. It will probably receive an initial conference distribution, most likely with only one recorded distribution and no conventional relist. A later distribution is highly likely from this zero-distribution arrival state; repeated relists are not.

The Court is unlikely to invite the Solicitor General's views on this follower docket. The federal-common-law and treaty question gives the United States an institutional interest, but any invitation is more likely to issue in the pending *Town of Vinton* lead petition, or jointly, than uniquely here.

Denial is the most likely disposition. This petition expressly presents the same question as *Indian Harbor v. Town of Vinton*, No. 25-1383, and calls that case the better vehicle. If the Court takes up the issue, it will likely do so through *Town of Vinton*, hold this petition, and later dispose of this case consistently. Conditional on this petition receiving a grant-family disposition, a cert-order disposition is much more likely than plenary review here: the expected route is a GVR after a favorable ruling in the lead case.

If the Court unexpectedly grants plenary review here, it would take the single question presented substantially as written: whether federal common law or state law governs equitable-estoppel enforcement by a nonsignatory under the New York Convention. If review is denied, no dissent or statement respecting denial is expected.
