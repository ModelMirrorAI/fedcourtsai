# Rationale

I assign a 0.23 probability to any grant-family disposition and predict denial as the modal label. The cell is forward-mode, in the sal-v3 `baseline` band, with zero distributions and no CVSG. Pooling the baseline band's bracketed `reached` rate over the displayed strictly prior Terms, OT2017 through OT2025, gives 863 grant-family dispositions over a weighted 13,163 resolved paid petitions, or 6.56%. That is the arrival-population anchor; I did not use the terminal relist-zero rate.

I adjust substantially upward from 6.56% because the petition and questions presented identify the same issue in pending lead petition No. 25-1383. The petition describes an outcome-determinative 4–1 circuit conflict over whether federal or state law supplies equitable-estoppel rules in New York Convention cases, and *GE Energy* expressly reserved the governing-law question. A CourtListener search also confirmed that the Fifth Circuit's *Town of Vinton* opinion is published. Those features make a hold-and-GVR path materially more plausible than for an ordinary arrival petition.

I stop at 0.23 because this petition concedes that *Town of Vinton* is the better plenary vehicle and the decision here is only a one-paragraph application of that case. The brief in opposition identifies substantial vehicle and antecedent-contract issues: separate domestic insurance contracts, dismissal of the foreign insurers, Louisiana's insurance-arbitration rule, and service-of-suit endorsements. It also disputes whether the asserted circuit cases address materially comparable contracts. These complications make a direct grant here unlikely and could also deter review of the lead case.

The 0.88 conditional summary-route probability reflects the case-specific posture. The mechanically observable prior-Term paid-petition proxy is 34.8% explicit GVR labels (300 of 863 grant-family dispositions), not a complete estimate of cert-order routes because summary reversals and some historical route labels are not recoverable from that split. Nearly all plausible grant-side scenarios for this follower involve holding it for *Town of Vinton* and later issuing a GVR, so a standalone plenary grant is the minority grant scenario.

The 0.98 relist-increment probability means at least one distribution after the frozen count of zero, not a 98% chance of repeated relists. The petition is paid and fully briefed, making an initial distribution very likely. I put the later-CVSG probability at 0.12 because the issue implicates federal treaty and arbitration interests, discounted because the lead docket is the natural place for any invitation. The 0.02 denial-writing probability reflects the technical commercial setting and the absence of any pre-distribution signal of unusual public salience.

The snapshot and filed documents were complete and readable. The snapshot postdates docketing and includes the August 12 brief in opposition, but it still shows the arrival event's required zero-distribution state. Exact CourtListener searches did not surface the Supreme Court companion docket, so the companion's current procedural posture remains a meaningful uncertainty; I relied on the provisioned filings for its pending status and did not seek this case's outcome.
