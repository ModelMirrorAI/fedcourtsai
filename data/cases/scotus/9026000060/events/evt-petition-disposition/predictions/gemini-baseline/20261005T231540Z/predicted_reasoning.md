# Predicted Reasoning of the Court

If certiorari is granted, it will likely be to address the deepening circuit split regarding Article III traceability in third-party harm cases, specifically concerning liability for the marketing of firearms under the Protection of Lawful Commerce in Arms Act (PLCAA) and general Article III principles as outlined in *California v. Texas*. The Court would likely take the question presented as written.

The case has been distributed once so far. Given the high profile of the case, it is very likely to be relisted at least once before a final disposition is reached. 

A CVSG is unlikely because the states are already represented and the federal government's view is not strictly necessary for resolving the Article III standing issue.

A summary disposition route (GVR) is highly unlikely because there is no obvious intervening Supreme Court decision that bears directly on this specific traceability holding to warrant a simple remand without argument. 

If the Court denies certiorari, expect a dissent from denial (likely from Justices Thomas or Alito) given the strong amicus interest from 22 states and the implications for Second Amendment-adjacent commercial liability.
