# Forecast

The Court will deny the petition after obtaining the requested response and redistributing it for consideration. The August 27, 2026 snapshot records one distribution, on August 19 for the September 28 conference, followed by the August 26 response request. That request is not an actual post-conference relist. Expect one further distribution after the response process, rather than a prolonged sequence of substantive relists. A second additional distribution is possible, but is not the modal path. The probability of at least one additional distribution is 0.98; this increment is masked for this response-requested moment.

No CVSG is recorded. The Court will probably resolve the petition without asking for the Solicitor General's views; the chance of a new invitation before disposition is 0.025. The dispute is a private damages action with a general jurisdictional question, rather than a federal program whose administration particularly requires the government's advice.

If review is granted, the Court will focus on Article III traceability when the immediate injury was inflicted by an independent criminal actor. Expect a narrowed formulation concerning the adequacy of pleaded exposure to the defendants' advertising and the alleged connection between that advertising and the shooting, rather than a decision on firearms immunity or gun ownership rights. The petition's literal premise that exposure was not alleged will require care: the bound-in appellate opinion describes generalized exposure allegations, whose sufficiency is disputed.

Conditional on a grant, plenary review is more likely than disposition in the cert order: 0.65 versus 0.35. Summary reversal is the principal immediate-disposition possibility because petitioners expressly seek it and invoke existing standing precedents. A GVR is less likely on this record, which identifies no new controlling decision requiring reconsideration. If the Court grants plenary review and rules for petitioners, the likely ground is that the pleaded causal connection is too speculative, not a categorical rule insulating all product advertising from liability.

A denial will most likely be unaccompanied by a separate writing. Conditional on denial, the chance of a dissent or statement respecting denial is 0.14. Thomas, followed by Alito and Gorsuch, is the most plausible author of such a writing; none is individually likely to write. The per-Justice probabilities in prediction.json are unconditional probabilities of authorship on the disposing order, not probabilities of joining someone else's opinion.
