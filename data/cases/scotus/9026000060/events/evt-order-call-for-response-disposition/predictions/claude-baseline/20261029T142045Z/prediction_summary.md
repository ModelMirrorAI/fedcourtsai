Gun and accessory makers ask the Supreme Court to throw out a lawsuit by victims of a school shooting, arguing the victims cannot link their injuries to the companies' advertising because they do not even claim the shooter saw it. The Court asked the victims to respond, a sign of interest, and many states backed the companies. We still expect the Court to decline the case — about a one-in-six chance it takes it — mainly because the lawsuit is still at an early stage and the companies have other defences left in the lower courts.
