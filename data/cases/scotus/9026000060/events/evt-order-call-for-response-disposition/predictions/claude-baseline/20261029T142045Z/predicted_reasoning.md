# Predicted course — Daniel Defense, LLC v. Lowy (No. 26-60)

**Call: denied (P(grant family) ≈ 0.17).**

**Posture at this moment.** One distribution on the record (for the 9/28/2026
conference), then a response called for on Aug 26, 2026 after the respondents
waived. No CVSG, no relist. The response clock has since run (BIO filed
Oct 26, 2026 per the live docket), so the petition will be **distributed
again** — almost certainly for a November or early-December 2026 conference.

**Further relists.** I expect the petition to be decided at the first
conference after the BIO is in, or with one relist at most. A plenary grant
would most likely follow a single relist; a denial most likely comes without
one. Expect at most one relist beyond the redistribution the call for a
response makes certain.

**CVSG.** None expected. This is a private-party tort suit; the United States
is not a party and the question is Article III traceability, which the Court
does not need the Solicitor General to brief at the cert stage.

**Question the Court would take, if it takes one.** The QP as written —
whether third-party-harm traceability is satisfied by speech the third party
is not alleged to have seen — perhaps reframed as the general standard
("predictable effect" vs. "determinative or coercive effect") for
third-party causation after *FDA v. Alliance for Hippocratic Medicine* and
*Murthy v. Missouri*.

**Summary disposition.** Conditional on any grant, roughly one chance in five
of a summary reversal (the petition asks for one, and the Fourth Circuit's
"predictable effect" gloss sits awkwardly with *Alliance for Hippocratic
Medicine*'s "distant (even if predictable) ripple effects"). No GVR: no
intervening decision is in view. Plenary review is the likelier grant route.

**Why denial remains likelier.** The judgment is interlocutory — the Fourth
Circuit remanded with the PLCAA and proximate-cause defences open, and both
could end the case on remand without the Court's help; the claimed split is
over phrasing of a standard that every circuit says is fact-dependent; and
the case arrives on pleadings about a specific shooting.

**Separate writing on a denial.** Possible but not expected (≈0.17): the
likeliest writer is Justice Thomas or Justice Alito, perhaps joined by
Justice Gorsuch, noting the interlocutory posture and inviting a renewed
petition after final judgment. If the Court instead summarily reverses,
expect a dissent from Justice Jackson or Justice Sotomayor.
