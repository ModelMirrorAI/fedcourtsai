The Supreme Court will more likely than not decline to hear this case, but it is a real contender: about a one-in-three chance of review. Families of 9/11 victims want to collect court judgments against the Taliban from frozen Afghan central-bank money held in New York, and an appeals court said no. The Court has asked the federal government's top appellate lawyer for an opinion, a step that signals genuine interest. The Court usually follows that advice, and the government may well urge leaving the ruling alone.
