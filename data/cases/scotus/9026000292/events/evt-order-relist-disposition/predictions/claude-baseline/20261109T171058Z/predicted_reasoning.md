# Forecast — Havlish v. Taliban, No. 26-292

The docket shows two distributions (11/6 and 11/13 conferences), so this petition has been relisted once. I expect it to be **distributed at least once more** (about 60%). The most likely route is a **CVSG** at or shortly after the 11/13 conference (about 40%). No respondent has appeared, and the dispute turns on the Executive's blocking of DAB assets and its non-recognition of the Taliban, so the Court will want the United States' view before granting. If a CVSG issues, the petition comes back months later after the SG's brief. Without a CVSG, a further relist is possible while a Justice writes.

The companions 26-293 (Ashton) and 26-294 (Owens) will be disposed of with this petition.

If the Court grants, it will take the QP as written: whether TRIA §201(a)'s agency-or-instrumentality status is measured at blocking or at execution. It would likely appoint an amicus to defend the judgment if the Taliban and DAB still do not appear. A summary disposition is unlikely (about 5% conditional on a grant). No intervening decision supports a GVR, and the fact-bound blocking-date application makes a summary reversal implausible.

If the Court denies, a separate writing is plausible (about 40%), since a relist without a response often precedes one. The likeliest authors are Justice Gorsuch, Justice Thomas or Justice Alito, writing on TRIA's text and the plight of the 9/11 families.

Overall: denial is somewhat more likely than a grant, with a grant at about 36%.
