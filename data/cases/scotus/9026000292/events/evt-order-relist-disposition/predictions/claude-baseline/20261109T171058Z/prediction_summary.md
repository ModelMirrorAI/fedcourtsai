Families of 9/11 victims and other terrorism victims are asking the Supreme Court to let them collect court judgments from billions of dollars of frozen Afghan central-bank money. The lower court said the money was off-limits because the Taliban took over the bank only after the U.S. froze it. We think the Court will more likely than not decline to hear the case. Still, there is a substantial chance it agrees, about one in three. The judges below were sharply divided, many States support the families, and the Court has already held the case over for a second conference. A likely next step is asking the federal government for its views.
