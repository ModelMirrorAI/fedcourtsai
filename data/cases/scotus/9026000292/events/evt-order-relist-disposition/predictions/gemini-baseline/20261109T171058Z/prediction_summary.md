We predict the Supreme Court will deny the petition for review, with about a one-in-eight chance (13%) of granting the case. Victims of the September 11 attacks seek $3.5 billion in frozen Afghan central-bank funds to satisfy judgments against the Taliban, challenging a lower-court ruling that the Terrorism Risk Insurance Act applies only if the Taliban controlled the bank on the exact day the funds were frozen. Despite strong lower-court dissents, no conflicting appellate rulings exist, and the recent one-week delay likely reflects a late procedural motion rather than an upcoming grant.
