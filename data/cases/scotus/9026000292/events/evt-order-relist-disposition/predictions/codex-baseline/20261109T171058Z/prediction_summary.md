The Supreme Court is more likely to decline this case than to hear it: I estimate a 34% chance of review, about one in three. Families of terrorism victims seek access to billions in frozen Afghan bank funds. Their petition raises a serious disagreement about when those funds become available to satisfy judgments, and the Court has already postponed its decision once. But disputes about sovereign immunity and Afghanistan's legal status complicate the case. The Court may first ask the federal government for its views.
