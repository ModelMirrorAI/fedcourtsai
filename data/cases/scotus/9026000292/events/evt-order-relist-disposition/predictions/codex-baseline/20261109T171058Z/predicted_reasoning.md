# Forecast of the Court's handling

**Most likely eventual disposition: certiorari denied.** Probability of any grant is 34%; denial remains more likely than review. A denial would ordinarily explain no doctrinal ground, so I do not forecast a majority opinion endorsing the lower court's reasoning.

The record already contains two distributions and one relist. I expect at least one additional distribution more often than not (65%), ordinarily one or two additional distributions if consideration proceeds without an invitation for federal views. A denial following the November 13 conference is a substantial alternative. If the Court invites federal views, consideration could instead extend for months and further distribution would follow that process.

I put a new call for the Solicitor General's views at 45%, plausibly in the next several order cycles or early 2027 rather than after merits briefing. The government's sanctions and foreign-relations interests make consultation natural, but the Court can deny without it. No CVSG is currently on this record.

If granted, I expect plenary review of the single timing question substantially as presented: whether TRIA requires the owner to be a terrorist party or its instrumentality when assets are blocked. The Court would not necessarily take every broader sovereign-immunity question implicated by the companion petitions. Conditional on a grant, a disposition in the cert order itself is only 8% likely. There is no identified intervening authority making a routine grant-vacate-remand the natural route; disputed statutory issues and possible alternative grounds favor argument.

Conditional on denial, I assign 24% to a noted dissent or statement respecting denial. Silence is the leading forecast. If a Justice does write, Alito or Thomas is my leading speculative candidate, with Gorsuch next; that prediction concerns authorship, not a count of Justices favoring review. A possible statement would criticize the added timing restriction and leave the recognition and immunity questions unresolved. I do not predict any Justice's unreported cert vote.
