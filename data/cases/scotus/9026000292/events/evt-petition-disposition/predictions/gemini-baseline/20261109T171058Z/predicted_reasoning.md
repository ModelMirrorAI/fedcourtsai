## 2. Question Presented and Contingent Merits Reasoning If Granted

- **Question Taken If Granted:** If the Court grants certiorari in No. `26-292`, it will take the petition's single Question Presented as written—whether under TRIA § 201(a), 28 U.S.C. § 1610 note, a plaintiff's right to execute on blocked assets depends on whether the assets' owner was a terrorist party or its agency or instrumentality at the time the assets were blocked—either alone or consolidated with the FSIA/recognition questions in Vide companions Nos. `26-293` and `26-294`, and will appoint an amicus curiae to defend the judgment below given the default of the Taliban and DAB.
- **Contingent Ground of Decision:** If granted, a majority of the Court would likely reject the Second Circuit panel's categorical rule freezing agency-or-instrumentality status as of the date of the blocking order (agreeing with Judges Sullivan and Menashi that TRIA § 201(a)'s text speaks in the present tense as of execution), but would either vacate and remand for the Second Circuit to resolve the antecedent questions it bypassed—whether the $3.5 billion in FRBNY central-bank reserves are actually assets "**of**" a Taliban instrumentality (see Judge Nardini's concurrence, 170 F.4th at 101–04) and whether treating the Taliban as controlling Afghanistan's central bank infringes the President's exclusive recognition power under *Zivotofsky v. Kerry*, 576 U.S. 1 (2015)—or affirm the denial of turnover on those alternative grounds if consolidated with Nos. `26-293` and `26-294`.
