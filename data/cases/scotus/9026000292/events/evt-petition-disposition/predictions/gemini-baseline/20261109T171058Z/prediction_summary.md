We predict that the Supreme Court will deny the petition for a writ of certiorari (a request that the Court review the lower court's ruling), with about a one-in-five chance (22% probability) of a grant. Although the case involves $3.5 billion in frozen Afghan central-bank reserves sought by September 11 terrorism victims and divided the appeals court, reviewing it would force the Court into sensitive presidential foreign-recognition questions where the United States opposes turning over the funds and no opposing party has appeared to defend the judgment.
