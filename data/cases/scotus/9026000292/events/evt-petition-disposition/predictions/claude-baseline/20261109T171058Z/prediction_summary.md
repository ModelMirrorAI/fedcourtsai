Families of 9/11 victims ask the Supreme Court to let them collect court judgments against the Taliban out of about $3.5 billion in frozen Afghan central-bank money. A federal appeals court said no, over strong dissents. I think the Court will most likely refuse to hear the case, giving it about a one-in-five chance of agreeing. The question matters and has strong backing from 31 states. But nobody has appeared to argue the other side, and the Court may first ask the federal government for its views.
