# Forecast — No. 26-292

- **Relists:** the docket shows two distributions (11/6 and 11/13), and no respondent has filed anything. I expect the Court not to finally dispose of the petition at the 11/13 conference. More likely it will **call for the views of the Solicitor General** (about 38%) or call for a response. Either way the petition would be distributed at least once more (about 58%). If the Court instead denies at 11/13, that ends it.
- **CVSG:** likely enough to be the modal non-denial step. If it comes, expect it on an order list in November or December 2026, with the SG's brief late in OT2026 and a decision on the petition in spring or summer 2027.
- **Question, if granted:** QP 1 as written, which asks whether TRIA §201(a) execution turns on the owner's terrorist-party or instrumentality status when the assets were blocked, as opposed to when execution is sought. The Court would leave the FSIA/pre-judgment attachment holding alone. If granted, the likely ground is TRIA's text ("blocked assets of that terrorist party"), assessed at the time of execution, consistent with Dole Food's present-tense reasoning.
- **Summary disposition:** unlikely. No intervening decision supports a GVR, and no respondent briefing exists for a summary reversal.
- **Ultimate disposition:** denial is more likely than not; I put a grant at about 22%.
- **Dissent from denial:** if the Court denies, a written dissent or statement is possible (about 25%). It is most likely from Justice Gorsuch, Thomas or Alito, stressing TRIA's text and the 9/11 victims.
