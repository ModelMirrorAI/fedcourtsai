Families of September 11 victims want the Supreme Court to let them collect court judgments against the Taliban from about $3.5 billion in frozen Afghan central bank money. The Court has asked the federal government's top appellate lawyer, the Solicitor General, for an opinion, a step that signals real interest. We predict the Court will narrowly more likely than not decline the case, with roughly a four-in-ten chance it agrees to hear it. Much depends on what the government recommends, which is hard to foresee.
