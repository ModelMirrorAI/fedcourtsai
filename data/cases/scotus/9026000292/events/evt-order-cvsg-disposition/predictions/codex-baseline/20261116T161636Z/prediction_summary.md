The Supreme Court is slightly more likely to turn down this appeal than to hear it; I put the chance of review or another favorable reopening order at 46%, just under one in two. Terrorism victims challenge a rule preventing them from collecting judgments from frozen Afghan central-bank assets. The stakes and disagreement among lower-court judges favor review, but foreign-policy concerns could weigh against it. The Court has asked the federal government for advice and will probably wait several months before deciding.
