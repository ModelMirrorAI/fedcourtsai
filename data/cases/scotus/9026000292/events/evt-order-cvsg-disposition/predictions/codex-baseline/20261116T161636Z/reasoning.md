## Why the case is stronger than an average high-band petition

The petition asks whether TRIA execution eligibility depends on the asset owner's terrorist-party or agency/instrumentality status **when the assets were blocked**. It separates that question from the other plaintiffs' prejudgment-attachment and recognition issues. The panel expressly made the blocking-date rule dispositive for these judgment creditors while holding that TRIA can overcome both jurisdictional and execution immunity. That makes the timing question consequential rather than merely advisory. Sources: `questions-presented.txt`; `petition.txt`, pp. 12–20; Second Circuit opinion, August 26, 2025, CourtListener opinion 11127439, pp. 42–55.

The textual challenge has force: TRIA's language identifies blocked assets belonging to a terrorist party or its instrumentality without expressly requiring that relationship to exist on the original blocking date. The majority's contrary reasoning connects eligibility to when the Executive froze the property and whether the property could have furthered the terrorist party's objectives. That is a real interpretive dispute, not just disagreement over damages. Judge Sullivan dissented at the panel stage; the March 19, 2026 en banc order identifies his dissent joined by Livingston, Bianco, and Park and a separate Menashi dissent. Five judges' objections make an isolated, unexamined error a less satisfactory description. Sources: panel opinion 11127439, pp. 49–55 and dissent; en banc opinion 11277807, order p. 4 and accompanying opinions. The provisioned lower-court summary's failure to find a dissent in its limited entry sample is not affirmative evidence of unanimity.

The alleged circuit conflict deserves weight, but not full acceptance in the petition's terms. The Eleventh Circuit's August 23, 2022 opinion in *Keith Stansell v. UBS Financial Services, Inc.*, No. 20-11736, pp. 14–17, rejects restricting instrumentality status to the time execution is sought and permits reliance on past terrorist ties. I verified that discussion in CourtListener opinion 7802935. It demonstrates genuine disagreement about the temporal test. However, former private facilitators of a Colombian terrorist group are not the same situation as a central bank allegedly becoming an instrumentality after a freeze. The petition's Ninth Circuit comparison similarly concerns ownership at execution, not necessarily the precise sovereign-bank sequence here. The conflict is substantial but less square than identical facts with opposite judgments. Source for the Ninth Circuit contention: `petition.txt`, pp. 18–20; I did not independently retrieve that opinion.

## Why denial still leads

The CVSG can reflect the need for foreign-affairs advice rather than four Justices' willingness to hear the case. The competing interests are not reducible to sympathy for victims: the panel treats DAB as the central bank of the recognized state of Afghanistan, distinct from the Taliban, and the Executive froze reserves to prevent Taliban access. The majority's separation of asset ownership, blocking, and government recognition gives the SG grounds to urge caution even if the timing rule is debatable. Sources: panel opinion 11127439, background and pp. 49–55; en banc opinion 11277807, Nardini concurrence. I do not assume the current administration's recommendation or carry forward a former administration's litigation position as if it were today's invited brief.

The petition reports billions of dollars at issue and judgments held by families, injured civilians, and insurers. That supports the significance score of 0.82, but financial magnitude does not itself establish a recurring conflict warranting review. Nor do five circuit dissenters establish four Supreme Court votes. Kagan's recorded recusal narrows the participating bench, although I make no mechanical probability adjustment from eight Justices to seven or nine. No Supreme Court vote lineup is inferred from the lower court's ideological divisions.

The official Vide companion, 26-293, and shared-amicus docket 26-294 concern the same lower judgment and each received a November 16 CVSG. They reinforce coordinated treatment, not independent confirmations of grant likelihood. A grant in another vehicle could yield a later GVR here, so I retain a meaningful summary-route probability. `same-question.json` has no matches; that is only an empty similarity search, not proof that no other litigation exists.

## Other claims and limitations

The **0.98 redistribution** probability primarily prices the petition's return to conference after the invited brief, not a 98% chance of prolonged merits deliberation. The **0.0 CVSG increment** is an explicit placeholder for the already-fired, harness-masked claim; it does not deny today's invitation. Conditional on any grant, I assign **0.16 to disposition in the cert order**, leaving 0.84 for plenary proceedings. No provisioned intervening Supreme Court decision clearly dictates reconsideration of this timing rule, so a GVR is not the leading route.

Conditional on denial, I give **0.22 to a noted dissent or statement**. Most denials remain unsigned. The independent order-writer probabilities are unconditional across outcomes and concentrate modestly on Thomas and Alito, with Gorsuch next, reflecting my assessment of who might object to the extra-textual limitation; they are not recorded votes or a claim that any Justice has already expressed a view. Kagan's probability is zero in light of the docketed recusal.

Corpus retrieval supplied broad recent grants, not close substantive analogues. A targeted Bank Markazi citation query returned no row and explicitly warned about sparse citation coverage; I did not treat that as absence of the precedent. Web lookups returned no usable tool output, so no web snippet or web claim informs the probability. The initial CLI path command hit a read-only uv cache; setting `UV_CACHE_DIR=/tmp/uv-cache` restored the provisioned CLI without installing anything. No case outcome or another person's forecast surfaced, and I sought neither. The principal remaining limitation is the unknown future SG recommendation.
