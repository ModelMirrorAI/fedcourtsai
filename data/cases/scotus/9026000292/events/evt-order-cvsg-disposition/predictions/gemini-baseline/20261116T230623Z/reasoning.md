- **Extraordinary financial stakes ($3.5B) and Second Circuit venue centrality:** Because the Federal Reserve Bank of New York (FRBNY) and Manhattan clearing banks hold the vast bulk of blocked sovereign and terrorist assets in the United States, a published Second Circuit restriction on TRIA § 201(a) functions as near-national law for major terrorism-judgment turnovers.
