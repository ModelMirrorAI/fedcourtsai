# Forecast

The Court will most likely deny the petition. The docket shows zero distributions at this arrival moment. I expect the petition to be distributed once in the ordinary course, but not relisted after its first conference, and then denied.

A call for the views of the Solicitor General is unlikely. The petition invokes Guam's Organic Act and the structure of a territorial attorney general's office, which gives the federal government some institutional interest, but the dispute principally concerns Guam ethical rules, a case-specific conflict, and the petitioner's failure to obtain substitute counsel.

If the Court grants review, it would more likely reformulate the question narrowly to ask whether federal law limits a territorial court's office-wide disqualification of an elected attorney general, rather than accept the petition's abstract question as written. Plenary briefing would be more likely than a summary disposition because the petition asks the Court to extend lower-federal-court principles and identifies no intervening decision that would support a GVR.

On denial, no Justice is likely to note a dissent or issue a statement respecting the denial.
