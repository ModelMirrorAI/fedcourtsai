# Forecast

The Court will deny Guam's petition. The record shows one distribution, entered October 14, 2026, for the November 6, 2026 conference, and no CVSG. The modal path is denial following that conference, during November 2026, without a further distribution or separate writing. This timing is a forecast, not a scored claim.

- **Further distributions:** 18% probability of at least one additional distribution beyond the recorded one. Zero is the modal increment; if further consideration occurs, one additional distribution is the likeliest increment. A request for an opposition or coordination with the related petition could extend consideration.
- **CVSG:** 3.5% probability of a new invitation. The modal path has none. If the Court seeks the federal government's views on the Organic Act and territorial prosecutorial structure, expect an invitation during its next few conference cycles rather than a prompt denial.
- **Disposition:** 8% probability of any grant, including summary relief. The broad institutional issue will not overcome this vehicle's fact-specific conflict findings, preservation concerns, and dismissal for failure to obtain substitute appellate counsel.
- **Route conditional on grant:** 20% probability of disposition in the cert order itself; 80% probability of plenary treatment. There is no identified intervening decision calling for a GVR. A grant would more likely require briefing on the relationship between territorial ethics authority and the Organic Act than immediate application of a directly controlling holding.
- **Writing conditional on denial:** 4% probability of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial. Expect no separate writing; no individual Justice's vote is forecast.

If review is granted, the most plausible question is narrower than the petition's general invitation to extend federal prosecutor-disqualification doctrine to all states and territories: whether the Organic Act permits conditioning review of an office-wide disqualification on the appearance of conflict-free substitute counsel, with dismissal when none appears. That conditional question is context, not a prediction of a merits opinion accompanying denial.
