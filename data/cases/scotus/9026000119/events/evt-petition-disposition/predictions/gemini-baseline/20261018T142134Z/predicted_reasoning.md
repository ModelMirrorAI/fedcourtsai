# Predicted Disposition

The Court will deny the petition for certiorari.

## Procedural Forecast
The docket currently records a single distribution (distributed for the November 6, 2026 conference). The Court is highly unlikely to relist the petition further or call for a response from the respondent. Given the local nature of the dispute and the weakness of the federal claim, a CVSG (Call for the Views of the Solicitor General) will not be issued. The petition will be denied outright at its first conference.

## Question Presented and Doctrinal Ground (If Granted)
If the Court were to grant the petition, it would likely take the sole question presented as formulated: whether federal constitutional rules disfavoring the summary disqualification of an entire public prosecution office should be extended to state and territorial courts. However, the Court would likely rule that the Fourteenth Amendment does not confer such structural protections upon state or territorial subdivisions against their own judiciaries, or it would dismiss the writ as improvidently granted (DIG) for lack of a substantial federal question.
