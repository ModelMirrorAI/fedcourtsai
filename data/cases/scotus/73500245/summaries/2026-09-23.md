## What happened

Jerry M. Blevins, an Alabama lawyer, had a fee dispute with a client in 2017. He filed an action in circuit court under an Alabama statute that lets attorneys ask a court to resolve disputes over fees they are holding. The Alabama State Bar later brought disciplinary charges against him over the same dispute.
