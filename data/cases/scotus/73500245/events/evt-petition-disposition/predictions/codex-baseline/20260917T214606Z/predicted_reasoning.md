# Forecast

The Court will deny the petition. The snapshot records one distribution, on July 8, 2026, for the September 28, 2026 conference; it records no subsequent distribution and no CVSG. I expect no further distribution and disposition in the first order release following that conference, around late September or early October 2026. P(at least one additional distribution) is 0.06. If additional consideration occurs, one further distribution is more likely than a long sequence. The elapsed summer interval does not itself constitute a relist.

I expect no request for the Solicitor General's views: P(a new CVSG) is 0.002. This state attorney-discipline dispute presents no identified federal-government institutional interest warranting that step. Should a CVSG unexpectedly issue, it would most plausibly follow initial conference consideration during fall 2026.

The petition's single question asks whether the Alabama Supreme Court's interpretation and application of Alabama Code section 34-3-62 violated procedural due process. If the Court grants review, I expect it to address the narrower federal fair-notice question: whether applying the statute's limited protection to an attorney who claims reliance on broader immunity is an unforeseeable judicial construction permitting constitutionally impermissible discipline. I do not expect a freestanding determination of the correct state-law construction.

P(any grant) is 0.01. Conditional on a grant, plenary review is more likely than disposition in the cert order: P(summary-disposition route | grant) is 0.20. The available petition identifies no intervening decision calling for a GVR; a summary intervention remains possible if the Court sees a sufficiently clear fair-notice error. These conditional possibilities do not change the primary denial forecast.

Conditional on denial, P(a noted dissent or statement respecting denial) is 0.01. I expect a routine denial without a separate writing and forecast no particular Justice as an author. The likely denial leaves the disciplinary judgment in place without announcing a merits rationale.
