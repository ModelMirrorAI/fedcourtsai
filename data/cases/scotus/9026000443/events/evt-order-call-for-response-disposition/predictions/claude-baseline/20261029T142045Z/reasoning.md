≈ 0.35 (no circuit split — both circuits agree — is the main drag; a

Court reverses, which I put at ≈ 0.55–0.6 given the textualist framing and
this Court's ERISA pleading-stage cases. If the companions are denied, a
small chance (~3%) this one is granted as the alternative vehicle. That gives
≈ 0.35 × (0.25 + 0.75 × 0.58) + 0.65 × 0.03 ≈ 0.26; I write 0.25.
