Southern Company asks the Supreme Court to overturn an appeals-court ruling
that pension plans must use reasonable, up-to-date assumptions (like life
expectancy tables) when converting a retiree's pension into a joint pension
that also covers a surviving spouse. Two nearly identical petitions are
pending, and the Court asked for responses in all three. We predict this
petition will most likely be turned down, but give about a one-in-four
chance that the Court takes the issue up in a companion case and then sends
this one back for reconsideration.
