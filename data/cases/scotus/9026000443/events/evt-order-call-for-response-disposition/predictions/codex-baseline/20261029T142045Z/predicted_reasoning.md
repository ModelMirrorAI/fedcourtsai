# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition after receiving a response and redistributing it. The probability of any grant, including a grant followed by vacatur and remand, is 13%.

The baseline contains one distribution, on October 7 for the November 6, 2026 conference, followed by the October 19 response request setting a November 18 deadline. The response deadline falls after that scheduled conference. Expect at least one further distribution after the response process, with one or two additional distribution entries the most likely ordinary-denial path. The 98% increment probability concerns another distribution, not a substantive relist after the Court has actually discussed the petition; this response-requested moment masks that claim in scoring. A longer hold for the related petitions remains possible. An ordinary denial is most likely in late 2026 or early 2027; a hold through a related merits decision would extend that horizon substantially.

No invitation for the Solicitor General's views appears in the baseline. The Court probably will not issue one; the probability of a new invitation is 12%. If it does, expect it after the response and renewed conference consideration, rather than before the response deadline.

If the Court takes up the question, it will address whether ERISA's actuarial-equivalence requirement for joint-and-survivor annuities requires reasonable actuarial assumptions. It will not need to decide the correct mortality table or interest rate for these particular retirees. The most plausible favorable route for this petition is a hold followed by a grant, vacatur, and remand if the Court grants one of the related FedEx or Bakery petitions and later rejects the shared lower-court interpretation. Conditional on some grant here, assign 75% to disposition in the cert order itself, predominantly that later companion-case remand, and 25% to plenary review. This does not predict that either related petition has already been granted, or that an intervening decision already exists.

If review is denied, expect no noted dissent or statement respecting denial: the probability of such a writing conditional on denial is 3%. The order-writer probabilities are unconditional and small for every Justice. If a denial does attract a writing, Thomas, Alito, or Gorsuch are the leading individual possibilities, not a predicted vote lineup. No majority opinion is forecast on the denial path.
