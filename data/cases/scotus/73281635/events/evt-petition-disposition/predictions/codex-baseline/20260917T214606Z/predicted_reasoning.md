# Forecast

The Court will deny the petition, most likely in an order following the September 28, 2026 conference, in early October. The probability of any grant, including a GVR or summary reversal, is 10%.

The baseline records two distributions: May 12 for the May 28 conference and August 5 for the September 28 conference. Between them, the Court requested a response following a waiver. From that two-distribution state, I expect no further distribution before disposition. The probability of at least one additional distribution is 25%; if that occurs, one additional distribution is more likely than a prolonged sequence. This is not a forecast that the two recorded distributions disappear or that the second has already resulted in substantive conference consideration.

No CVSG is recorded. I expect none before disposition, with a 1.5% probability of a new invitation. On that unlikely path, the invitation would most plausibly follow the September conference or an early-autumn redistribution and would postpone disposition while the Solicitor General responds.

Conditional on review, the Court would most plausibly address whether prejudgment interest is categorically unavailable on past noneconomic damages in a Section 1983 action, narrowing the petition's formulation that spans noneconomic damages generally. Plenary briefing and argument are more likely than disposition in the cert order: the latter has a 6% probability conditional on a grant. The record supplies no identified intervening decision that would make a GVR the natural route.

If denied, I expect no recorded dissent or statement respecting denial; the probability of any such writing or notation is 4%, conditional on denial. I do not forecast a public individual-Justice lineup.

The likely denial leaves the lower court's treatment of prejudgment interest, including its remand for separating past from future damages, undisturbed by this petition. It would not announce agreement with the lower court's merits reasoning. The competing authorities are more readily distinguished by discretionary assessment, statutory context, and the treatment of delay and future injury than understood as a clean categorical conflict requiring immediate review.
