The Supreme Court is very likely to deny this petition for review, with only a 6% chance (about one in seventeen) of granting it. Although the Court asked the respondents to file a response after three outside groups supported the petitioners, the case has severe procedural flaws: the state court's ruling is an interim decision that sends the case back for trial rather than a final judgment, the petitioners are third-party defendants trying to assert the rights of an absent English company that refused to appear in court, and the Supreme Court already turned down a nearly identical challenge to South Carolina's asbestos receivership law earlier in 2026.
