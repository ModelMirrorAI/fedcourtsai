The Supreme Court is likely to turn down this appeal, with about an 18% chance it agrees to review it. The dispute asks whether a South Carolina court can put someone in charge of litigation decisions for an English company. The request for a response and support from outside groups suggest genuine interest. But the challenged order is narrower than the challengers suggest, and unresolved procedural questions could prevent review. A decision is most likely after further briefing, rather than at the originally scheduled November conference.
