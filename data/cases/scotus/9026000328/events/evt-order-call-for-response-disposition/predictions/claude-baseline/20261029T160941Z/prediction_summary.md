We predict the Supreme Court will probably turn down this case, though the chance it
takes it up is real: about one in four. The case asks whether a South Carolina court
can appoint a "receiver" (a court-chosen manager) to run lawsuits for an English
company. A federal appeals court rejected that same practice in a separate case. The
Court asked the other side to respond, and parties in the conflicting federal case are
also asking for review. Those are signs of interest. But this case is still mid-trial,
which makes it a less attractive one to take.
