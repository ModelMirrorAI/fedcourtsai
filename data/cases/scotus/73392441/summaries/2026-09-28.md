## What happened

A bank sued in Washington state court to fix an error in the legal description of a deed of trust on Alvin White's property, later substituting U.S. Bank as plaintiff. Both sides moved for summary judgment, a procedure for deciding a case without trial when there is no real factual dispute. ## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether a judge can decide contested factual questions affecting someone's property rights by weighing evidence and making findings while ruling on a request for judgment without trial, rather than sending those questions to a trial. The petition also asks whether doing so fits with fair-process guarantees, the historical understanding of judges' role, and the right to a jury trial in suits involving legal claims.
