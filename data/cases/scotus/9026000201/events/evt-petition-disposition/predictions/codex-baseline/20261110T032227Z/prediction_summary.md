The Supreme Court is very likely to hear this case: I estimate a 96% chance of review. A federal appeals court struck down the national horse-racing law's enforcement provisions, while another appeals court upheld them. The federal government and the challengers agree the Supreme Court should resolve that disagreement. I expect full briefing and argument, probably after one additional conference consideration. The main uncertainty is which related petition the Court chooses to lead the review, not whether the constitutional issue deserves attention.
