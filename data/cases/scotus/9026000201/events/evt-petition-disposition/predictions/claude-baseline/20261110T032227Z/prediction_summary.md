The Supreme Court will very likely (about 93%) agree to hear this case. The federal government is asking the Court to review a Fifth Circuit ruling that struck down how the Horseracing Integrity and Safety Act is enforced, finding that it gives a private body too much government power. Another appeals court has upheld the law, and every party, including the law's challengers, agrees the Supreme Court should take the case. The main risk is that the Court picks a companion case as its vehicle instead.
