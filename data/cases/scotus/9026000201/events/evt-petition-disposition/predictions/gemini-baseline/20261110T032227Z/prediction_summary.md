The Supreme Court is very likely to grant review (94% probability). The Fifth Circuit struck down the enforcement provisions of a federal statute—the Horseracing Integrity and Safety Act—on the ground that Congress unconstitutionally gave federal law-enforcement power to a private organization without enough oversight by the Federal Trade Commission. Because that ruling invalidates an Act of Congress, directly conflicts with a Sixth Circuit decision upholding the same law, and all sides agree the Supreme Court should step in, the Court will almost certainly hear the case.
