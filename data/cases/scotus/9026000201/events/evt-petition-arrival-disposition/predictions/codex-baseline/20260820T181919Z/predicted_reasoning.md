# Forecast of the Court's disposition

The petition has been distributed zero times. It will be distributed for conference and is unlikely to be disposed of at its first conference; expect one or two further distributions while the Court coordinates this petition with the Authority's related petition in No. 26-199. No CVSG will issue because the Solicitor General and the Federal Trade Commission are already petitioners and have supplied the federal government's position.

The Court will grant certiorari, most likely in this petition and the Authority's parallel petition together, and set the cases for plenary briefing and argument. It will take the single question presented substantially as written: whether HISA's enforcement provisions facially violate the private nondelegation doctrine. Reformulation, if any, will clarify that the dispute concerns the Authority's investigative, charging, sanctioning, and litigation powers under FTC supervision rather than HISA's rulemaking provisions, which the Fifth Circuit upheld.

A summary disposition is possible but not the likely route. The Court already gave the Fifth Circuit an opportunity to reconsider after *FCC v. Consumers' Research*, and that court adhered to its invalidation while expressly disagreeing with the Sixth Circuit. That entrenched conflict and the distinction between front-end enforcement authority and back-end agency review are better suited to plenary treatment than another GVR. If review is nevertheless denied in this docket—most plausibly because the Court uses the companion petition—no Justice is likely to note a dissent or statement respecting denial.

If the case reaches the merits, the likely ground will be that a private entity may assist federal enforcement when the agency retains controlling authority and meaningful review, and that a facial challenge cannot prevail where the Authority's initial decisions function as recommendations subject to de novo FTC review and Commission rules constrain independent coercive steps. That merits forecast is conditional on a grant.
