# Forecast

The Court will deny the petition, most likely in the orders following the September 28, 2026 conference, in late September or early October. The snapshot shows **one distribution**, dated June 17 for that conference, and no CVSG. I expect **zero additional distributions** before disposition. The probability of at least one further distribution is **8%**; if one occurs, one additional conference is more likely than a lengthy sequence. The summer interval is not itself a relist.

I assign **1.5% to any grant**. The likely denial will leave the military appellate judgment undisturbed without deciding whether the firearms restriction is constitutional. The statutory-review question, rather than the ultimate validity of firearm disarmament, is the petition's immediate obstacle. The analogous denials described in the filed briefs and the removal of the indorsement requirement make another denial the expected procedural result.

No CVSG is expected: **0.1%** probability of a new call. The United States is already the respondent and its Solicitor General has filed an opposition addressing the question.

**If review is granted**, the Court will most likely take the single question in a neutral statutory formulation: whether Article 66(d)(2) permits a military court of criminal appeals to review a firearms-indexing indorsement signed following the military judge's entry of judgment. It would not need to accept the petition's premise that the firearms ban is unconstitutional. The likely initial issue would be whether the relevant act occurred after entry of judgment and falls within the statute's post-trial-processing authority; the underlying Second Amendment claim could remain for a lower court.

Conditional on any grant, I assign **25% to disposition in the cert order itself**, and 75% to review requiring further briefing and argument. No identified intervening authority in the record directly requires a GVR on this military-review question. A summary disposition is possible, but not the leading grant route.

Conditional on denial, I assign **2.5% to a noted dissent or statement respecting denial**. The forecast is an ordinary denial without a separate writing; I do not forecast an individual Justice's vote or authorship. Timing and the conditional question formulation are context, not separately scored claims.
