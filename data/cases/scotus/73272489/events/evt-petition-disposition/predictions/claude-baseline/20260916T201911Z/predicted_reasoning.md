# Predicted reasoning — Sanders v. City of Long Beach, No. 25-1235

**Disposition.** The Court will deny the petition without comment. Denial will appear on the order list following the September 28, 2026 long conference (the October 5, 2026 order list is the expected slot).

**Relists.** The docket shows one distribution (June 17, 2026, for the September 28, 2026 conference); the petition has never been relisted. I expect no further distribution: it will be denied at its first conference. The residual chance of one more entry is mostly a clerical reschedule of a long-conference item, not a Justice holding the case.

**CVSG.** None. No federal party, statute, or program is implicated; the dispute is a California Government Code dangerous-condition tort claim against a municipality, resolved on state evidentiary and credibility rulings. The Court will not invite the Solicitor General.

**Which question, if any.** None of the petition's question would be taken as written. The single question presented is a narrative of grievances against the trial judge (exclusion of a 2005 stormwater report and deposition transcripts, refusal to permit impeachment with prior inconsistent statements, alleged bias) framed as Fifth, Sixth, and Fourteenth Amendment due-process violations, with the Federal Rules of Evidence and Civil Procedure invoked against a state-court trial where they do not apply. There is no circuit or state-court split alleged, the Court of Appeal opinion is unpublished, and the federal question was not presented to the state courts in a form the Court could review; any reformulation would still confront an adequate and independent state-law ground.

**Summary disposition.** No intervening decision of this Court bears on the case, so a GVR is not in prospect. Conditionally, were the Court to grant at all, the only plausible form would be a summary order rather than plenary review, since nothing here could be set down for argument; that is why the summary-route conditional sits above one half despite the near-zero grant probability.

**Dissent or statement respecting denial.** None expected. No Justice has a signaled interest in the fact-bound evidentiary complaints of a pro se civil litigant against a city, and no separate writing will accompany the denial.

**Conditional merits.** If, contrary to this forecast, review were granted, the ground would be limited to whether the state courts' refusal to permit impeachment with deposition testimony deprived the petitioner of a fundamentally fair trial under the Fourteenth Amendment; the Sixth Amendment confrontation theory has no application to a civil trial and would be left behind.
