# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely grant certiorari for briefing and argument. The probability of some grant, including vacatur and remand or summary reversal, is **90%**. The central reason for review will be the national importance of deciding whether the President and implementing agencies may foreclose access to asylum under the challenged proclamation, rather than the existence of an established circuit conflict.

The docket already contains **two distributions and one relist**. No further distribution is narrowly more likely than another one: **46%** probability of at least one additional distribution. In the ordinary prompt-grant path, expect zero or one additional relist, occasionally two, with action in November or December 2026. A hold for D.V.D. could instead delay disposition until that case is decided in 2027. The Court will not call for the Solicitor General's views; the small probability of a new CVSG is **0.1%**, with the government already petitioning through that office.

If the Court grants plenary review, it will most likely take the asylum component of Question 1: whether the proclamation and implementing guidance lawfully bar covered individuals from applying for asylum despite Section 1158. A narrowed grant need not reopen mandatory withholding or torture-protection questions that the government is not materially defending in the same way. The Court may also take the class-definition issue in Question 3. Question 2's remedial issue is especially susceptible to coordination with D.V.D., described in the staged reply as presenting a Section 1252(f)(1) question involving a broad class.

**Conditional on a grant**, the probability of disposition in the cert order itself is **30%**. A hold followed by a GVR in light of D.V.D. is the leading summary alternative, not immediate summary reversal. Plenary review is still more likely because the legality of the categorical asylum restriction remains a substantial independent question. No unconditional prediction of a merits holding or majority author accompanies this cert forecast.

If review is denied, an unsigned order without a separate writing is most likely. The conditional probability of a dissent from denial or statement respecting denial is **32%**, with Thomas or Alito the leading candidates for a denial-related writing. Separate liberal writings are more plausible on a summary disposition granting the government relief than on an ordinary grant for argument. Most likely, however, no Justice writes on the disposition order.
