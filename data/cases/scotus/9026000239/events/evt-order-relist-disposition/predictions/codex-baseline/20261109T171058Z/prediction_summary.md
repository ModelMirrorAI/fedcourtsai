The Supreme Court is very likely to take this case, with about a nine-in-ten chance of some form of review. The dispute concerns whether the President can block access to asylum at the southern border and how broadly lower courts may stop that policy. The Court has already considered the petition once and scheduled it again. Full briefing and argument are the most likely next major step, although the justices could wait for a related immigration case and then send this one back for reconsideration.
