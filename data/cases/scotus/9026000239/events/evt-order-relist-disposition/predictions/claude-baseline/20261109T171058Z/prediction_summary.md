The Supreme Court will very likely (about 9 in 10) agree to hear this case. The federal government is asking the Court to review an appeals-court ruling that blocked a presidential proclamation barring asylum claims from migrants who cross the southern border between ports of entry. The Court almost always takes cases where a lower court has struck down a major federal policy. Holding the petition over for one more conference (a "relist") is the Court's usual step right before granting review.
