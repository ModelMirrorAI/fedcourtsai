# Forecast: Mullin v. RAICES, No. 26-239 (first-relist moment)

**State forecast from:** the docket shows two distributions. The first was for the 11/6/2026 conference. On 11/9 the petition was distributed again for 11/13/2026, which is the first relist and the event that opened this cell. There is no CVSG. Four amicus filings support the petition: Iowa + 21 States, FAIR, America First Legal and America's Future et al.

**Disposition: certiorari granted, most likely from the 11/13 conference (orders around 11/14–11/17).** The modern Court routinely relists a petition once before granting it. This petition is a Solicitor General filing that challenges a D.C. Circuit ruling which largely affirmed classwide relief against a presidential proclamation. That is the kind of petition the one-relist-then-grant practice exists for.

**Further relists:** probably none. The likeliest path is a grant at the 11/13 conference. A second relist would mostly mean a Justice is writing something, or the Court is weighing whether to limit the grant to particular questions. I put that at about one in five.

**CVSG:** none. The United States is the petitioner, so a CVSG is effectively impossible.

**Questions taken:** I expect a grant on QP 1, the lawfulness of Proclamation 10888 and its guidance under 8 U.S.C. 1182(f) and 1185(a) against 1158(a)(1). QP 2, whether 8 U.S.C. 1252(f)(1) bars the classwide injunction, is likely included. The respondents frame it as fact-bound, but it recurs in immigration litigation. QP 3, the class definition under Article III and Rule 23, could be taken as written or folded into QP 2. A limited grant that drops QP 3 is plausible.

**Route:** plenary review, not a summary disposition. No intervening decision calls for a GVR: Mullin v. Al Otro Lado (2026) predates the denial of rehearing below and is already cited by both sides. A summary reversal of a nationwide policy ruling this significant is very unlikely.

**If denied (unlikely):** I expect a written dissent from denial, most likely by Justice Alito or Justice Thomas, possibly joined by Justice Gorsuch. If the Court grants, I expect no separate writings on the order.
