# Forecast of the Court's disposition

The petition has been distributed zero times at this arrival moment. It will be distributed at least once and will probably be granted after its initial conference or one relist. The Court will not call for the Solicitor General's views because the United States, represented by the Solicitor General, is the petitioner.

The Court will most likely grant the first question, concerning the legality of Proclamation No. 10,888 and its implementing guidance, and may add the Section 1252(f)(1) question. The Article III and Rule 23 question is less likely to be granted independently. Plenary briefing and argument are more likely than a summary disposition, although a GVR or narrow summary ruling based on the Court's recent immigration and classwide-relief precedents remains a material alternative.

If the Court reaches the merits after a plenary grant, it is likely to reverse on at least one ground: either by accepting broader executive authority to implement the entry suspension or by holding that the lower courts could not issue the classwide relief entered here. If certiorari is denied instead, the likeliest denial will carry no separate writing.
