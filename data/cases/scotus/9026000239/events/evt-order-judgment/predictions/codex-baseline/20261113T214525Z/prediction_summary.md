I expect the Supreme Court to change part of the lower court's ruling, with about a three-in-four chance of some change. My best guess is a 6–3 decision affirming some parts and reversing others. The majority will likely allow broader government discretion to deny asylum and narrow the court order protecting the class, while preserving mandatory protections against persecution or torture. The key uncertainty is whether Congress's express right to apply for asylum prevents the government's categorical approach.
