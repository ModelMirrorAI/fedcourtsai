The Supreme Court will very likely (about five chances in six) overturn the appeals court ruling that blocked the President's January 2025 proclamation closing off asylum for people who cross the southern border illegally. The predicted judgment is a reversal by a 6–3 vote. The main reason: asylum is something the government may grant or refuse at its discretion, and the Court's majority is expected to hold that the government may refuse it to a whole category at once. The law's "may apply" wording is the main risk to this forecast.
