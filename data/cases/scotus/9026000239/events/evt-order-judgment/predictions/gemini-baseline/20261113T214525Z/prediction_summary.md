The Supreme Court is very likely (84% chance) to overturn or narrow the lower court's ruling, most likely reversing it by a 6–3 vote. The Court took up two questions: whether the President can bar border crossers on U.S. soil from seeking asylum (discretionary protection allowing noncitizens fearing persecution to stay permanently), and whether a federal statute bars classwide injunctions (court orders blocking enforcement for a whole group rather than just named plaintiffs) in immigration removal cases. Because asylum is discretionary under federal law and a 6–3 majority previously strictly enforced that statutory ban on classwide removal injunctions, the Government has two strong paths to prevail.
