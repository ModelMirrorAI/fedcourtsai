Distributed once so far; expect the Court to grant certiorari, potentially after one relist. The Court is highly likely to take up this case because it involves a lower court injunction of a major Presidential Proclamation on immigration and asylum, implicating the Executive's statutory authority and the limits of classwide relief under 8 U.S.C. 1252(f)(1). 

No CVSG is expected, as the Solicitor General is already the petitioner. Plenary review is the expected route rather than summary disposition, given the complexity and national importance of the multiple questions presented. If granted, the Court will likely address all three questions: the lawfulness of the proclamation, the 1252(f)(1) jurisdictional bar, and the class certification. Should the Court deny the petition, expect a dissent from denial by conservative Justices.
