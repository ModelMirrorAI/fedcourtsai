The Supreme Court will very likely refuse to hear this case, at roughly a 1-in-125 chance of review.
Mr. Sessanga, who is representing himself, sued police officers and a prosecutor after he was
acquitted. The lower courts dismissed his complaint before any evidence-gathering. The appeals
court's ruling was unpublished and turned on the specific facts. The other side has not filed a
response, and the Court almost never takes a case without first asking for one. Expect a routine
denial after the December 4 conference.
