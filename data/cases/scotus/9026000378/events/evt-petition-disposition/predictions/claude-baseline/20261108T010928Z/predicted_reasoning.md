# Forecast — scotus/26-378

Distributed once so far (for the Dec. 4, 2026 conference). It has never been relisted, and no response is on file. Expect
the petition to be **denied at that first conference** without being relisted and without a call
for a response. No CVSG: no federal party or federal interest is involved.

If the Court took the case at all, it would most likely take QP2 in a narrowed form: whether the
independent-intermediary doctrine bars a Fourth Amendment malicious-prosecution claim (*Thompson v.
Clark*, *Chiaverini*) where officers allegedly withheld exculpatory facts from the prosecutor. QP1
(pre-service screening of a pro se complaint) and QP3 (plea offer as evidence of no probable cause)
would be left behind. A summary disposition is not the likely route. No intervening decision bears
on the questions, so a GVR is improbable. Even if the case were granted, a GVR or summary action
would be only somewhat less likely than plenary review.

Should the Court deny, expect no separate writing. A short statement on the independent-intermediary
doctrine is the only conceivable exception, and it is remote.
