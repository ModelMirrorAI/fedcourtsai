The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Sessanga v. City of DeSoto Police Department* (about a 0.5% chance of a grant). Representing himself without a lawyer, the petitioner asks the Court to revive his civil-rights lawsuit alleging malicious prosecution after a Texas jury acquitted him of child-abuse charges. Both the federal trial court and the Fifth Circuit dismissed his suit before the defendants were even served because a grand jury indictment creates a legal presumption of probable cause (sufficient reason to bring charges), and his complaint pleaded no concrete facts showing that police misled the grand jury. Because the lower court's ruling is unpublished, applies settled pleading standards, and raises no genuine conflict among federal appeals courts, the Court is almost certain to deny the petition without further briefing.
