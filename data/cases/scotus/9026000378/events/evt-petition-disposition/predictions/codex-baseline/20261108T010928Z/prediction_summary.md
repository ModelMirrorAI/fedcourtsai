The Supreme Court is very likely to decline this case, with roughly a one-in-a-hundred chance of agreeing to review it. The petitioner says he was unfairly denied a chance to gather evidence for his police-misconduct lawsuit. But the appeals court's actual opinion allows such cases when supported by enough factual allegations; it found this complaint insufficient. That makes this look more like a dispute about this particular complaint than a clear disagreement between courts. Expect no further conference consideration or separate statement from a Justice.
