# Forecast — Emberton v. Superior Court of California (No. 26-290)

**Denied, at the November 6, 2026 conference, by a plain order-list entry.**

- **Relists.** The docket shows one distribution (for the 11/6/2026 conference)
  and no relist. I expect no further distribution: the petition is decided at
  its first conference.
- **Response / CVSG.** No brief in opposition is on the docket and none will be
  called for. No CVSG: no federal interest, and nothing about the case would
  lead the Court to ask for the Solicitor General's views.
- **Question presented.** If, improbably, review were granted, the Court would
  have to reformulate the QPs. As written they ask the Court to choose between
  two lines of its own precedent (*Ciraolo*/*Dow*/*Barlow's* against
  *Katz*/*Kyllo*). That is not a conflict the Court recognizes. The live issue
  underneath is whether a municipal code-enforcement inspection of a fence
  visible from the street was a Fourth Amendment search.
- **Summary disposition.** Unlikely in absolute terms. Conditional on any grant,
  a summary route (GVR) is roughly a 30% chance. No intervening decision bears
  on the case.
- **Separate writing.** I expect no dissent from denial and no statement
  respecting it.
