# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny certiorari, most likely without a separate writing and without another distribution. The snapshot records exactly one distribution, on October 21, 2026, for the November 6, 2026 conference; it records no prior relist and no CVSG. I expect disposition in the order release following that conference, approximately early to mid-November 2026.

From that one-distribution state, the probability of at least one further distribution is 3.5%. Zero additional distributions is the modal forecast; if there is a further distribution, one additional distribution is more likely than a sustained series. The probability of a new CVSG is 0.1%; I forecast none. The petition concerns local code enforcement and does not present an evident reason to obtain the federal government's institutional views.

If review is granted, the likely question would narrow the petition's two overlapping questions to whether intentional observation of residential exterior property from a lawful public vantage constitutes a Fourth Amendment search merely because it obtains information about property subject to an owner's right to exclude. I do not forecast acceptance of the petition's entire account of search, seizure, due process, and code-enforcement authority. A grant would require the Court to find a reviewable federal issue in the state writ-denial posture rather than simply accept the petitioner's characterization of the underlying dispute.

Conditional on any grant, I assign 35% to disposition in the cert order itself and 65% to plenary proceedings. I therefore favor plenary treatment within that unlikely grant branch. No identified intervening decision supplies a concrete GVR rationale; summary correction remains a minority alternative rather than the principal forecast.

Conditional on denial, the probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial is 0.8%. I expect an ordinary unexplained denial, not a merits opinion, and identify no particular Justice as a likely separate writer.
