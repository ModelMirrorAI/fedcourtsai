# Why P(grant) = 0.07

**Anchor.** `context.json` gives band `elevated` (sal-v4), Term 2025, distribution_count 2, forward mode. For the strictly-prior Terms 2017–2024, the statpack's elevated `reached` rates are 17.5, 15.9, 13.8, 16.1, 20.5, 19.0, 17.5 and 17.9%. That pools to roughly **17%**. As a cross-check, the relist-count cut (paid scored segment) gives a grant-family rate of about 20% when every bucket from 1 up is pooled (1: 13.4% of n≈2521; 2: 40.6% of n≈487; 3+: 36.8% of n≈481). The capital-marked cut is 17.1% grant family. The CA5 circuit row is about 3.7%.

**Adjustment down, by a lot.** The band's lift comes entirely from the second distribution. I believe that distribution reflects a deferral, not interest in granting:
- Petitioner moved on 8/14 to defer consideration pending a companion petition from the related Fifth Circuit COA proceeding. That proceeding was decided 8/28. The companion was filed as No. 26-353 on 9/11, and his 9/17 letter asks the Court to consider the two together. The Court then moved 25-1246 from the 9/28 conference to 11/6 without ruling. The plain reading is that the Court is holding it for 26-353, where the State's BIO was filed on 10/16 (the supremecourt.gov docket for 26-353 shows only the petition and BIO). A rescheduled petition like this one inflates both the band and the relist cut. The prompt warns about exactly this.
- On the merits of the petition, review of a Fifth Circuit successive-petition AEDPA ruling is rarely granted. The BIO argues the circuit split is illusory because the Fifth Circuit's test is fact-dependent, like other circuits'. QP3 has jurisdictional and vehicle problems, which the motion to defer concedes the State raised. QP4, deference to a DA who supports relief over the AG, is novel but unlikely to attract four votes.
- On the other side: the record was requested on 8/11, which means at least one chambers looked closely. The recantations and the DA's support for relief make the case sympathetic. These facts make a separate writing more likely than a grant, so I keep some weight above the CA5 floor.

I land at 0.07: well below the 17% band anchor, which is inflated by the deferral, and above the ~4% CA5 and baseline-like floors, because of the record request and the capital posture.

**Other claims.** relist-increment 0.70: 25-1246 likely has to be distributed again to sit at the same conference as 26-353, and a capital denial with a written dissent often adds relists. cvsg 0.01. Summary route given a grant, 0.20: if the Court acts, a GVR or summary disposition tied to the companion is conceivable. Dissent from denial given a denial, 0.30.

**Uncertainty.** I could not see whether the Court has ruled on the motion to defer; the docket shows no order. If 26-353 is denied quickly, 25-1246 may be decided on 11/6 with no further relist.
