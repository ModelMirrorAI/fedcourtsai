# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition. From the two distributions already recorded, I expect one additional distribution before disposition, with a further-distribution probability of 0.55. The snapshot shows the original September 28, 2026 conference and redistribution for November 6, 2026. I expect resolution during November or December 2026, rather than a prolonged invitation for federal participation. No CVSG is recorded, and I assign only 0.01 to a future CVSG.

The strongest route to review is the first question: whether section 2244(b)(2)(B)(i)'s diligence inquiry can conclusively impute a reasonable lawyer's discoveries to a prisoner despite alleged egregious misconduct by his actual lawyers. If the Court grants plenary review, I expect a narrowed version of that question, not all four questions together. The independent innocence analysis and the dispute over whether the asserted circuit conflict is real will probably keep the Court from using this case to announce a broad constitutional entitlement to merits review of successive ineffective-assistance claims.

Conditional on any grant, a disposition in the cert order is more likely than plenary briefing and argument: probability 0.60. A limited vacatur and remand addressing the treatment of local prosecutors' support for relief is the leading summary possibility. The reply's discussion of Newberry supplies a potential analogy, not a prediction that an intervening ruling compels relief here. An outright summary reversal is less likely than a remand because the separate diligence, innocence, preservation, and state-representation issues resist a simple merits correction.

Conditional on denial, I expect no separate writing, but assign 0.35 to at least one noted dissent or statement respecting denial. The capital stakes, alleged misconduct by prior counsel, and prosecutor support make a statement a substantial minority possibility. This is a forecast of the existence of a writing, not of a public vote count or an author.
