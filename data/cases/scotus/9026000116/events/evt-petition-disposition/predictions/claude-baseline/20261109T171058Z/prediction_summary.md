Alabama asks the Supreme Court to wipe out a lower-court order that blocked its congressional map for the 2026 elections. The Supreme Court had already paused that order in June, and those elections have now taken place. We think it is fairly likely (about 72%) that the Court takes the case and, without full argument, cancels the lower-court order. The probable reason is that the dispute is now "moot", meaning there is no longer a live controversy. A dissent from the Court's liberal justices is likely.
