The Supreme Court is likely to take Alabama's challenge, with about an 82% chance of some form of review. The most likely result is a short ruling overturning the lower court's temporary restriction on Alabama's congressional map, without another full hearing. The Court has already intervened in this dispute, and two closely connected cases are moving alongside it. The main uncertainty is whether the completed election makes the temporary restriction legally irrelevant, which could change how the Court ends the case.
