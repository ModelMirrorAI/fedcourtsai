The Supreme Court is likely (about a two-in-three chance, or 65%) to grant Alabama's request to review this congressional redistricting case before the federal appeals court rules, acting alongside two companion appeals that go directly to the Supreme Court. However, because the lower court's temporary order blocking Alabama's 2023 congressional map was already paused by the Supreme Court in June 2026 and the November 2026 election has now passed, the Court is most likely to resolve the petition summarily in its order—by vacating or reversing the temporary injunction and returning the case to the trial court for a final decision under the Court's recent *Louisiana v. Callais* voting-rights ruling—rather than holding a full round of oral arguments.
