Alabama asks the Supreme Court to review a lower-court order that blocked its congressional map under the Voting Rights Act. The same dispute is pending in two companion appeals that the Court must decide. We think it is very likely, about four in five, that the Court takes action in Alabama's favor rather than simply declining the case. Most likely it will summarily reverse, meaning it overturns the order without full briefing and argument. The alternative is wiping the order out because the 2026 election it governed is over. The Court already paused this order in June.
