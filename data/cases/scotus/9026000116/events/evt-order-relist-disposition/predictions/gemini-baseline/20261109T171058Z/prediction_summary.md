The Supreme Court is likely—about a 72% chance—to grant Alabama's request for immediate review and wipe out (`gvr`, meaning grant, vacate, and remand) the lower court's preliminary injunction blocking Alabama's 2023 congressional map. In June 2026, the Supreme Court already paused this exact injunction and stated that the lower court failed to follow the Supreme Court's recent Voting Rights Act decision in *Louisiana v. Callais*. Because the November 2026 election has now taken place and companion direct appeals (*Milligan* and *Singleton*) are being considered alongside this case, the Court is likely to vacate or summarily reverse the preliminary injunction without holding full oral arguments.
