The Supreme Court is more likely than not—about seven chances in ten—to accept Alabama's request for review. The likeliest single outcome is summary reversal: overturning the lower court's order without another full hearing. The Court previously intervened in this redistricting dispute, and closely related appeals give it a reason to handle these cases together. But the election has already occurred, raising a serious question about whether further relief is still needed. Expect another conference or two, not a request for the federal government's advice.
