Justice Amy Coney Barrett is almost certain to deny William L. Armstrong III's emergency application, with less than a one-in-a-hundred chance (0.5%) that the Supreme Court grants any relief. Representing himself without a lawyer, Armstrong asks the Court to pause his ongoing federal appeal, appoint a criminal-defense lawyer for his civil lawsuit, and excuse a $605 appeal fee after a trial judge dismissed his sprawling stolen-car conspiracy complaint and fined him $500 for abusive language. Because Armstrong has already paid the $605 fee so his appeal can be decided normally, and because the Supreme Court virtually never steps into the middle of routine lower-court fee and sanction disputes, the request will be turned down quickly without full-Court review.
