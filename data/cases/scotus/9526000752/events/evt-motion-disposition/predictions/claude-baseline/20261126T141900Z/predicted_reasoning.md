# Forecast — 26A752, Armstrong v. Alter Trading Corp. (application for a stay)

The record shows no response requested, no referral, and no amicus filing.

- **Disposition:** Justice Barrett will deny the application herself, without calling for a response and without opinion. The stay of the Seventh Circuit's fee order, the stay of appellate proceedings, and the request for appointed counsel will all be refused. No partial grant.
- **Response:** No response will be requested from the respondents.
- **Referral:** The application will not be referred to the full Court in the first instance. The only route to a referral is the applicant renewing the denied application to a second Justice, who would refer it to the Conference, where it would be denied in an order list; I put that at about one in twenty.
- **Amicus:** None will be filed.
- **Timing (unscored):** A denial by the Circuit Justice within about one to three weeks of submission — most likely by early-to-mid December 2026. If renewed, a Conference denial in January 2027.
- **Writings:** No Justice will write.
- **Standard, conditionally:** Were anyone to engage the merits, the application fails at each step — no reasonable probability of certiorari on a fact-bound denial of in forma pauperis status, no fair prospect of reversal, and a filing fee is not irreparable harm; the failure to seek the stay below first (Rule 23.3) is an independent ground.
