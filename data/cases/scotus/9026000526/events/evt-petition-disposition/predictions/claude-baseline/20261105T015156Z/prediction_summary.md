We expect the Supreme Court to turn down this case (a "denial" of certiorari), with
only about a 2–3% chance it agrees to hear it. Lane Mitchell argues that the trial
judge ended his attempted-murder trial with an acquittal, so Mississippi cannot try
him again. But the judge ended the trial because a key witness failed to appear, not
because the evidence fell short, and the Court's past cases generally allow a retrial
in that situation. Mississippi did not even bother to file a response, and no outside
groups have weighed in.
