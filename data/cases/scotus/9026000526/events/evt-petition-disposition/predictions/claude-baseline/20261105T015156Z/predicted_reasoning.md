# Forecast — Mitchell v. Mississippi, No. 26-526

Distributed once so far (for the conference of November 13, 2026), never relisted,
with the State having waived a response. Expect the Court to **deny** at or shortly
after that conference without calling for a response; the chance of any further
distribution is about one in eight, and that mostly via a Justice calling for a
response from Mississippi (which would push the petition to a later conference). No
CVSG: no federal interest is implicated in a state attempted-murder prosecution.

If the Court took the case, it would take the single QP essentially as written,
likely reframed as whether a post-close-of-evidence "judgment of acquittal" resting on
a compulsory-process violation is an acquittal under Evans v. Michigan or a Scott-type
dismissal unrelated to guilt. A summary disposition is the less likely route even
on a grant: there is no intervening decision for a GVR, though a per curiam summary
reversal is conceivable if a majority thought Evans controlled. Plenary review is
the likelier form of any grant.

Should it deny, expect no separate writing; if any Justice writes, a statement
from Justice Gorsuch or Justice Sotomayor on acquittal finality is the most plausible.
