The Supreme Court is very likely to decline Lane Mitchell's request for review; I estimate about a one-in-forty chance it takes the case. He argues that another trial would violate the constitutional protection against being tried twice after an acquittal. But the first judge dismissed the charge because of a witness-related constitutional problem, rather than expressly finding the prosecution's evidence inadequate. Existing precedent may already resolve that distinction. I expect a short denial without further conference consideration or a separate written opinion.
