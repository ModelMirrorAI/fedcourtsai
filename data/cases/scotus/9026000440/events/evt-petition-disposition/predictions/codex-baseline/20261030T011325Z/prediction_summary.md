The Supreme Court is very likely to decline this case; I estimate a 3.5% chance it will grant review. Jabateh argues that two convictions added twenty years to his sentence for conduct the appeals court said the charged law did not cover. That is a serious concern, but his request faces an unusual procedural barrier because the issue was already considered on appeal. The latest ruling creates no clearly identified disagreement among appeals courts, and the government has waived a response.
