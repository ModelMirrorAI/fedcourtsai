The Supreme Court will very likely turn down this petition: about a 2.5% (one
in forty) chance it takes the case. Mohammed Jabateh argues that an appeals
court wrongly refused to reopen his immigration-fraud convictions, even though
that court had earlier agreed his conduct did not fit the statute's words. The
government did not even file a response, which usually signals the Court will
deny. The petition also points to no disagreement among lower courts, which is
what the Court normally looks for.
