The Court is asked whether a temporary order should block the district judge from issuing a ruling on the merits of Schultz's bankruptcy appeal while his request that she be removed from the case remains unresolved in the lower appellate court. Schultz argues that letting her rule first would make it impossible to meaningfully review whether she should have been handling the case at all.

## Where it stands

## What each outcome would mean

Schultz, the applicant, is asking for the stay. If granted, the district judge would be temporarily barred from issuing a merits ruling in his bankruptcy appeal while higher courts consider whether she should be removed from the case; other proceedings could continue. If denied, no such bar would apply, and the district judge could proceed to rule on the appeal while the recusal and rehearing issues continue elsewhere. Either way, this order would not decide the recusal question or the underlying bankruptcy dispute itself.
