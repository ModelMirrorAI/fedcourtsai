# Forecast

The Court will deny certiorari in F.E.B. Corp. v. United States. The most likely disposition is an unexplained denial following the September 28, 2026 conference, on an ensuing order list in late September or early October. This is a forecast of timing, not a known disposition.

The supplied docket shows **one distribution**, entered June 24 for that conference, and **no CVSG**. I expect **zero additional distributions**. The probability of at least one further distribution is **6%**; if that occurs, one additional distribution is more likely than a prolonged series. I expect no invitation for the Solicitor General's views: **0.1%** for a new CVSG. The United States is already the respondent and has waived its response; a request for its response as a party would be different from a CVSG.

The petition will not persuade the Court to reconsider the treatment of documentary factfinding through this quiet-title dispute. The decision below applies a deferential factual standard while reserving legal questions for de novo review, and the underlying trial included expert testimony. Those features make this an unattractive vehicle for the proposed purely documentary-evidence exception.

If review is granted, the most likely question is a narrowed version of QP 1: whether factual inferences concerning historical governmental intent receive clear-error review when drawn from documentary evidence. QP 2's statutory setting would supply the application rather than a separate invitation to retry ownership of the island. Conditional on any grant, I assign **12%** to disposition in the cert order itself and **88%** to plenary proceedings. No intervening decision requiring a GVR is identified in the supplied material. A change to the standard of review would more naturally require briefing and argument.

Conditional on denial, I assign **1.5%** to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. The modal forecast is no separate writing; I do not predict individual Justices' unobservable cert votes or a merits opinion accompanying denial.
