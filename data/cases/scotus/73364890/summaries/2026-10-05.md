## In brief

The Supreme Court declined to review a company's bid to overturn a ruling that the United States, not the company, owns Wisteria Island near Key West.

## What happened

F.E.B. Corp. and the United States have fought for years over who owns Wisteria Island, land created from dredged material dumped off Key West in the 1920s and 1940s. A federal law gives states title to such coastal lands unless the United States built them up for its own use. After an earlier appeal sent the case back for trial, a district court found the United States intentionally created the island for naval purposes, including storing dredge spoils, protecting the harbor, and future military development. The Eleventh Circuit Court of Appeals affirmed that decision, applying a deferential standard of review to the trial court's factual findings. F.E.B. then asked the Supreme Court to take the case.

## What the Court is being asked

F.E.B. asked the Court to decide how closely appeals courts should scrutinize a trial court's factual findings when those findings rest entirely on historical documents rather than witness testimony or credibility judgments. F.E.B. argued that appeals courts should examine such findings more critically than the usual deferential review, and that the lower court wrongly accepted a merely "plausible" reading of the evidence instead of applying tougher scrutiny.

## Where it stands

The United States waived its right to respond to the petition. The Court distributed the case for its September 28, 2026 conference and denied the petition on October 5, 2026.

## What each outcome would mean

F.E.B., which lost in the lower courts, asked the Court to take up its case. The denial leaves the Eleventh Circuit's ruling in place, meaning the United States keeps title to Wisteria Island and the deferential standard of review the appeals court applied to the trial court's findings remains unchanged. The denial does not mean the Supreme Court agreed with the lower court's reasoning; it simply leaves that decision standing without further review.
