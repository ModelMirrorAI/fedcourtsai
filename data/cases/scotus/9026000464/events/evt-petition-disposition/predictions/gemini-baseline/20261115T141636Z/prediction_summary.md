Hamp's Construction asks the Justices to revive a nearly $4 million federal contract claim after canal banks collapsed during an Army flood-control project in Louisiana. However, the federal appeals court unanimously rejected the claim based on project-specific soil evidence, several key arguments were forfeited—meaning raised too late—in the earlier proceedings, no federal appeals courts disagree on the legal rule, and the government waived filing a response.
