This petition is at the arrival moment, meaning it has not yet been distributed for conference. I expect the petition to be distributed once and then denied without any further relists. The Court will not call for the views of the Solicitor General (a CVSG), as the federal government (the Secretary of the Army) is already the respondent in this matter, making a CVSG procedurally impossible or unnecessary.

The questions presented raise fact-bound issues of contract interpretation regarding a Type I Differing Site Conditions claim. The dispute centers heavily on the specific construction contract, drawings, and subsurface boring logs for a particular project, rather than a broad question of federal law that divides the circuits. Consequently, if the Court were to grant review, it would likely be to correct a fact-specific error, but a grant is highly unlikely. Should it deny review, I expect no separate writing (no dissent from denial) from any Justice given the mundane, factual nature of the dispute.
