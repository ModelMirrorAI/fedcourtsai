I reduce that approximately 5.02% anchor to **P(any grant) = 1.5%**, for three principal reasons:

- **An application dispute rather than a demonstrated conflict.** The petition argues that the Federal Circuit effectively imposed a categorical exact-location-boring requirement. But its own account says the court recognized that contractual indications need not be explicit or site-specific in every detail (petition pp. 9–10). The disagreement therefore appears substantially to concern how that accepted framework applies to these particular boring logs and project geography. The petition identifies no concrete inter-circuit conflict or conflicting Supreme Court holding. I give that absence some weight, while recognizing that a specialized federal-contract appeal need not produce an ordinary regional-circuit split to merit review.
- **A material preservation problem.** The petition acknowledges that the Federal Circuit found the traffic-access-drawing arguments forfeited (pp. 8–9). Those drawings are central to its proposed whole-contract theory. That creates a substantial obstacle to clean resolution of the question as framed, even accepting that a contract can indicate conditions implicitly. This is a vehicle concern, not an independent conclusion that the forfeiture ruling was correct.
- **The solicitation amendment complicates the claimed assurance.** According to the petition, the original barge prohibition was removed before bidding, and the amendment warned that canal work might require a floating platform or barge (pp. 7, 9). That supplies a record-specific alternative to the petition's broad allegation that the government promised safe bank-based operations. The Court could see a contest over construction assumptions and contract interpretation rather than an appropriate occasion to announce a new national rule.

There are counterweights. The petition describes a recurring federal procurement clause, a reported appellate decision, and a substantial request for adjustment of approximately $3.95 million plus 237 days. A genuinely categorical limitation on project-wide contractual indications could affect bidders well beyond this project. Those considerations keep my grant probability meaningfully above zero. But the petition alone does not establish that the challenged categorical rule is actually the holding, and it does not remove the preservation problem. I do not infer a lack of attention from the absent conferences or CVSG: their absence defines the arrival moment.
