This is not evidence of two completed substantive conferences: the first distribution preceded the response request, and the second conference remains in the future at prediction time.

I expect **zero additional distributions**, followed by denial in the first order cycle after the September 28 conference, most likely in early October 2026. P(at least one further distribution beyond the recorded two) is **0.30**. No CVSG appears in the record. I expect none: P(a new CVSG) is **0.01**. The petition presents detainee-rights and pleading questions arising from local custody, not a dispute for which the federal government's institutional position appears essential.

Question 3's pleading issue would support that inquiry rather than become a freestanding categorical question. Question 1's broad characterization of the mental-health holding is a less likely vehicle.
