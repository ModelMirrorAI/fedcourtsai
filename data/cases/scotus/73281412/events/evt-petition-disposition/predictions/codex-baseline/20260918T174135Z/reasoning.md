- **The asserted conflict may be error correction rather than a clean split.** The petition, especially printed pages 10–25, argues that the Fifth Circuit departed from established mental-health, pretrial-punishment, and pleading principles. These are respondents' arguments, not my findings that all claims necessarily fail. In particular, defenses the Fifth Circuit did not decide are not established independent appellate holdings. Nonetheless, the need to separate defendants and grounds makes a comprehensive grant less attractive.

There are meaningful counterweights. The petition describes severe confinement lasting more than five days, a published decision, and a substantial dissent by Judge Dennis. The requested response distinguishes this from a wholly unnoticed petition.
