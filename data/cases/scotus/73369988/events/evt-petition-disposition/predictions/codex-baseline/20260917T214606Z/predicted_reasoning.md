# Forecast

The Court will deny the petition, most likely in the ordinary orders following the September 28, 2026 conference identified in the snapshot. This forecasts a discretionary refusal of review, not agreement with the arbitration award or a merits holding on the petition's legal theories.

The record shows one distribution, entered July 1 for that future conference, and therefore no completed relist sequence. I expect zero additional distributions before denial. The probability of at least one additional distribution is 7%; if that occurs, one additional distribution is more likely than a prolonged sequence. I expect no call for the Solicitor General's views; the probability of a new CVSG is 0.2%.

The petition is unlikely to secure review because its challenges to the governing review framework and neutrality analysis turn on the handling of this arbitration and this appeal rather than a developed conflict between courts on a necessary rule of decision. Its own account says the appellate court tested the federal manifest-disregard theory on an assumption favorable to petitioner and found it unsatisfied. The asserted institutional affiliations are unlikely to persuade the Court that this is a clear departure from its impartiality precedents requiring intervention.

If review is granted, the most plausible question is a narrowed version of QP 2: what relationship or undisclosed interest requires vacatur for evident partiality when an employment arbitration agreement invokes the FAA alongside nonpreempted state law. QP 1's claim that a court must invariably select between review frameworks, and QP 3's broader constitutional challenge, are less likely to be taken together as written. This question-selection forecast is conditional, not a prediction that an opinion will issue.

Conditional on any grant, I assign 30% to disposition in the cert order and 70% to plenary consideration. A narrow summary intervention addressing the neutrality standard is possible, but the provided material identifies no intervening decision furnishing a concrete GVR basis and does not establish an obvious factual match to the cited precedents. Thus summary disposition is not the leading grant route.

Conditional on denial, I assign 1.5% to a noted dissent or statement respecting denial. I expect an unexplained denial with no separate writing and do not predict an individual Justice's cert vote.
