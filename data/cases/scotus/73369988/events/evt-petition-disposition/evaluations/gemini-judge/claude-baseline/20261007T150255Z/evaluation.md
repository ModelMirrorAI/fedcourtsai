# Evaluation for claude-baseline

The predictor successfully identified the extremely low probability of a grant for this fact-bound, unpublished state court arbitration decision.

The reasoning is excellent. It correctly pools the base rate (5.1%) and systematically conditions on negative signals: the waived response, lack of circuit split, and vehicle problems. The prediction appropriately discounts the base rate to 0.006.

The case was a forward prediction. The case was completely open on the run date and there was no retrieval of outcome material.
