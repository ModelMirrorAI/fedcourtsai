## What happened

After the verdict, the trial court discovered it had mistakenly given jurors evidence that had never been admitted at trial, including a recorded statement suggesting a co-defendant lacked knowledge of the crime, and had withheld some evidence that was admitted. ## What the Court is being asked

The case asks how courts should decide whether a jury's exposure to information it should never have seen requires a new trial. One approach asks only whether that outside information likely swayed the jury against the defendant. The other approach goes further, asking whether the defendant would have been convicted anyway even without the improper information. Maund's petition argues that federal appeals courts and state courts disagree on which approach applies, and that the Court's own past decisions point toward the first approach rather than the second.
