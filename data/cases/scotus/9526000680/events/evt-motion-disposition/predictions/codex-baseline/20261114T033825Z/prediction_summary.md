The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Nancy Roncati Dowden's request to pause her Connecticut foreclosure proceedings. I estimate about a three-in-one-hundred chance of relief, assuming the request does not become moot. Her allegations that disability prevented meaningful participation, and the threat to her home, are serious. But the unfinished state case and fact-specific disputes make Supreme Court intervention unlikely. I expect a brief order, probably without explanation, around the November 16 hearing rather than a ruling on the underlying mortgage dispute.
