The Supreme Court is overwhelmingly likely to **deny** Nancy Roncati Dowden's emergency stay application, with only a half-percent (about one in two hundred) chance of granting any relief. Dowden, representing herself without a lawyer, asks Justice Sonia Sotomayor to halt an ongoing Connecticut state-court mortgage foreclosure hearing based on disability-accommodation and fair-hearing objections. However, the Supreme Court generally may review state cases only after a final judgment—meaning a decision that finishes the state lawsuit—and here the Connecticut courts have decided only initial liability, while state law still provides an automatic pause on losing the home once a final foreclosure judgment is appealed.
