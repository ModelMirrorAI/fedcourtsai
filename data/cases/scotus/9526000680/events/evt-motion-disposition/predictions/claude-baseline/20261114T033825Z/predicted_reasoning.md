# Forecast — 26A680, Dowden v. Deutsche Bank National Trust Co.

Justice Sotomayor, as Circuit Justice, will deny the stay application herself, without calling for a response and without referring it to the full Court. The denial will be a one-line docket entry with no opinion or statement from any Justice.

- **Response:** none will be requested. The record shows none requested so far.
- **Referral:** not referred on first presentation. If the applicant renews the application to another Justice after the denial, that renewed application would be referred to the Court and denied at a later conference; I think a renewal is unlikely because the November 16, 2026 state-court hearing the application targets will already have passed.
- **Amici:** none. The record shows zero and it will stay zero.
- **Timing:** denial within about a week of submission, most likely on or shortly before November 16, 2026. If the hearing passes first, a plain denial is still more likely than a mootness dismissal.
- **Disposition:** denied. No administrative stay will issue in the interim.

If, contrary to this forecast, the Court were to act favourably, the ground would be a brief hold to permit a response rather than any view on the ADA Title II or due-process questions; the likely absence of a final state judgment under 28 U.S.C. § 1257 makes relief on the merits standard implausible.
