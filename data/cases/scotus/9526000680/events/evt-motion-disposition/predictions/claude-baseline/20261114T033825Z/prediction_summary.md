A homeowner representing herself asked Justice Sotomayor to pause a Connecticut court hearing in a dispute with a bank over her house while she prepares an appeal to the Supreme Court. The request will almost certainly be denied: about a one in a hundred chance of success. Such stays (temporary pauses of a lower court's proceedings) are rarely granted to private parties, and this dispute turns on its own facts in a state case that is still ongoing, which the Court seldom steps into.
