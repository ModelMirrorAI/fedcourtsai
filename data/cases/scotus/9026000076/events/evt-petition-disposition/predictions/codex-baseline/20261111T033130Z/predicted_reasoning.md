# Forecast of the Court's treatment

The Court will most likely **deny certiorari**. The probability of any grant is **24%**. A denial will most likely be by routine order without an explanation and will not constitute a ruling that Florida's law is constitutional.

The snapshot records **one distribution**, followed by rescheduling before its scheduled conference. Expect **at least one renewed distribution** beyond that count, with one or two additional distributions the central procedural forecast. The probability of any increment is **94%**. The first such increment can simply restore the petition to the conference calendar; it need not represent a substantive relist. Consideration alongside Hunt, No. 26-78, is the likely sequencing explanation, but there is no existing hold order in the provided record. A decision is more likely to follow that renewed conference process than a call for the Solicitor General's views. The probability of a new CVSG is **0.5%**.

**If review is granted**, the Court will most likely take the presented Sixth/Eighth Amendment unanimity question without adding Hunt's distinct ex post facto issue to Jackson's case. The focus will be whether a state's choice to require a jury's death determination subjects that determination to unanimity even though the Constitution may allow a judge to make the ultimate sentencing selection. The Sixth Amendment's historical jury function and the distinction between eligibility and selection will be the central doctrinal contest; no merits result is forecast unconditionally.

**Conditional on a grant**, a disposition in the cert order itself has probability **10%**; plenary briefing and argument have probability **90%**. There is no identified intervening controlling decision that presently makes an immediate GVR the natural route. The summary possibility chiefly concerns later action after a same-question lead case, rather than a prediction that McKinney or Ramos already makes summary reversal appropriate.

**Conditional on denial**, the probability of a dissent or statement respecting denial is **42%**. No separate writing is the most likely individual outcome. If there is a writing, Sotomayor is the most likely author; Jackson is another plausible author, with a smaller possibility of Gorsuch addressing the historical jury-right issue. Authorship does not imply a forecast of who joins. The per-Justice, unconditional authorship forecasts are recorded in `prediction.json`.
