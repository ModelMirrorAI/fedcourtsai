# Forecast — Jackson v. Florida, No. 26-76

- **Distributions so far:** one (for the 9/28/2026 conference), rescheduled on Aug 28 and not yet redistributed. I expect it to be **distributed again** together with Hunt v. Florida (26-78) once Hunt's reply is in, probably for a conference in late November or December. I expect one or two further relists of the pair before a final order.
- **CVSG:** none. No federal interest is implicated in a state capital-sentencing statute.
- **Disposition:** cert **denied** (about 84%), probably on the same day as Hunt. If the Court grants, it would most likely grant Hunt or both petitions and consolidate them.
- **If granted:** the Court would take the QP as written, on the Sixth Amendment ground: whether a jury determination that state law makes a prerequisite to a death sentence must be unanimous under Ramos and Hurst. The Eighth Amendment theory would be secondary. Plenary review is far likelier than a summary disposition, since there is no intervening decision to support a GVR.
- **Dissent from denial:** about even odds. The likeliest writer is Justice Sotomayor, possibly joined by Justice Jackson (or writing separately). Justice Gorsuch is an outside possibility, given his Ramos reasoning.
