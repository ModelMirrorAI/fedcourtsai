We predict the Supreme Court will most likely refuse to hear this case (about a one-in-six chance it agrees). The case asks whether Florida can impose a death sentence when only 8 of 12 jurors recommend it. The Court postponed its first look at the case, apparently to consider it alongside a nearly identical Florida case. That suggests real attention. But no live disagreement exists among state courts, and the Court has turned away similar challenges before. A written dissent from the refusal is quite possible.
