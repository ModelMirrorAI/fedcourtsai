The Supreme Court will almost certainly turn this case down. The chance it agrees
to hear it is well under one in a hundred. Muhammad Saeed, convicted in Arizona
but not yet sentenced, argues his trial broke the rule against being tried twice
for the same thing. Arizona did not bother to respond. The appeal comes up before
the case has finished in the state courts, and it does not point to courts
disagreeing on the issue. Those are two of the things the Justices look for
before agreeing to hear a case.
