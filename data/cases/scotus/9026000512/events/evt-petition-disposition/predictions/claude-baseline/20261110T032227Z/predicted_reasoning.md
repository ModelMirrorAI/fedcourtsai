# Forecast — Saeed v. Arizona, No. 26-512

- **State forecast from.** Distributed once (for the Dec 4, 2026 conference),
  never relisted, no CVSG. Respondent Arizona waived its response.
- **Disposition.** Denied at the December 4, 2026 conference, which is its first
  conference, most likely on the order list of the following Monday.
- **Relists.** None expected. I also expect no call for a response, because
  nothing in the petition invites a closer look.
- **CVSG.** None. This is a state prosecution with no federal-government interest.
- **Question the Court would take, if any.** Conditionally only: a reformulated
  QP1, asking whether *Abney*-style interlocutory protection reaches a
  collateral-estoppel double-jeopardy claim raised after a verdict but before
  sentencing. The § 2241 framing would be dropped, and QP2 would be left behind.
- **Summary disposition.** Unlikely. No intervening decision supports a GVR, and
  the § 1257 finality problem stands in the way of a summary reversal.
- **Separate writing.** I expect no dissent from, or statement respecting, the
  denial.
