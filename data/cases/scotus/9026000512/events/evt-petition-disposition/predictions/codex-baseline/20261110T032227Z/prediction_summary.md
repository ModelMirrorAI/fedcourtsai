The Supreme Court is very likely to decline Muhammad Saeed's request for review; I put the chance it takes the case at about one in one hundred. He argues that protections against being tried twice should allow immediate relief after a guilty verdict but before sentencing. His case, however, comes through a disputed state procedure rather than a federal challenge to detention, and he has not been sentenced because of his continued absence. Those complications make this a poor case for deciding a broader constitutional rule.
