# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny certiorari, most likely without a written explanation, around the order list following the December 4, 2026 conference shown in the snapshot. A short delay into a later December order list is more likely than prolonged consideration. The docket currently shows exactly one distribution and zero relists. I expect no additional distribution; the probability of at least one is 6%, allowing a reschedule or a request for a response as well as a genuine relist. If another distribution occurs, one additional distribution is more likely than a long series.

The Court will not call for the Solicitor General's views. No CVSG is currently recorded, and I assign a 0.1% probability to a new one. The petition presents a proposed remedy in an individual state criminal prosecution, not a developed federal institutional controversy requiring the Solicitor General's assistance.

If review is granted, the Court will most likely focus on a narrowed form of the first question: whether a defendant can obtain immediate review of a double-jeopardy issue after the second trial but before sentence, in the procedural posture actually presented. It is less likely to take the abstract proposition in QP2 that a supposedly void conviction eliminates the need for judgment or sentence. A federal section 2241 question would first require the Court to explain how this state motion-to-vacate record properly presents it.

Conditional on any grant, the probability of a disposition in the cert order itself is 12%; further merits proceedings are more likely. There is no identified intervening ruling supplying an obvious GVR instruction, and the dispute over what the first jury necessarily decided makes a summary reversal unlikely. If the Court reaches the merits, the likely analysis would distinguish a pretrial right to avoid a second trial from a proposed right to bypass sentencing and ordinary review after that trial has already occurred. This is conditional reasoning, not a forecast that a merits opinion will issue.

Conditional on denial, the probability of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial is 1.5%. I expect no separate writing. Thomas, Gorsuch, and Alito have the largest, still very small, individual order-writer probabilities in the structured forecast; I do not predict that any of them will actually write or reveal a vote. The state appellate finality ruling, the petitioner's continued absence, and the mismatch between the requested habeas rule and this direct-review vehicle give the Court several reasons to decline this petition without resolving the claimed constitutional injury.
