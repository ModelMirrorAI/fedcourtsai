# Forecast of the Court's disposition

**The Court will most likely deny certiorari after obtaining the Solicitor General's views.** The probability of any grant is **45%**. The likely reason for declining review is an insufficiently clean presentation of the indirect-purchaser issue together with a fact-dependent conspiracy-pleading question, rather than a conclusion that the dispute lacks commercial significance. A routine denial will not announce that reasoning or decide the merits.

## Procedure and timing

The frozen baseline records **two distributions** and an **October 5, 2026 CVSG**. I predict at least one additional distribution with **96% probability**, most likely one or two additional distributions after the SG brief and responsive submissions. A duplicate/rescheduled listing of the September conference is not an extra substantive relist in this forecast. The expected route is further briefing followed by renewed conference consideration, rather than immediate action before the United States responds.

No initial CVSG remains to be predicted: it has already occurred. The required incremental claim is recorded as **0.00** and is vacuous for this moment. I expect disposition most likely in **spring or summer 2027**, with a longer hold possible if a related indirect-purchaser case becomes a lead vehicle.

## If review is granted

The likeliest focus is **QP1**, narrowed to whether an indirect purchaser excluded from a distribution channel can recover lost profits without alleging a passed-on overcharge. I expect this issue to have a stronger prospect of review than the petition's broader suggestion that trade-association participation or joint lobbying alone establishes conspiracy. A limited grant leaving QP2 aside is a meaningful possibility.

Conditional on any grant, I assign **20%** to disposition in the cert order itself and **80%** to a route opening further merits proceedings. Within the summary possibility, a hold followed by GVR in light of a related Illinois Brick decision is more plausible than immediate summary reversal. This does not assume that the related United Biologics petition mentioned in the BIO will be granted.

If the Court reaches the merits, the central distinction will be between injuries transmitted through a distribution chain and an alleged direct exclusion from a market channel. I expect a ruling focused on that distinction rather than a general reconsideration of Illinois Brick. That is conditional context, not an unconditional prediction that a merits opinion will issue.

## If review is denied

I expect no separate writing. Conditional on denial, I assign **12%** to at least one dissent from, or statement respecting, denial. I do not forecast its author or any unreported cert votes.
