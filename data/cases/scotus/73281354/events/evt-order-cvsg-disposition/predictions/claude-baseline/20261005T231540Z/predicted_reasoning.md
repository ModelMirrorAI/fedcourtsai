# Forecast: AstraZeneca Pharmaceuticals LP v. Mosaic Health, Inc. (No. 25-1070), after the CVSG

**State forecast from:** the docket shows two distributions (for the May 14, 2026 and September 28, 2026 conferences, the second after one reschedule), a response requested on April 30, 2026 after the respondents' waiver, a brief in opposition and reply, and today's order (October 5, 2026) inviting the Solicitor General to express the views of the United States. No CVSG brief exists yet.

## What the Court will do with the petition

- **Disposition.** More likely denied than granted, but close: P(any grant) = 0.42. The petition will not be decided until the Solicitor General files, which for an October invitation most often means a brief in the spring of 2027 and a conference in May or June 2027. The outcome turns chiefly on the SG's recommendation. My expectation is that the SG recommends denial on the Twombly question as fact-bound and is genuinely split on the Illinois Brick question; a recommendation to grant on question 1 alone, or to hold this case for *United Biologics v. Amerigroup* (No. 25-1388), is the main route to a grant.
- **Relists.** The petition will be distributed at least once more (P = 0.96): every CVSG petition is redistributed after the SG brief arrives, and the only path to no further distribution is a settlement or withdrawal before the SG files. Expect one to three further distributions after the SG brief, with a relist or two likely if the SG recommends a grant or the Court is weighing a hold.
- **CVSG.** Already on the docket; no second invitation (P = 0.02).
- **Which question, if granted.** Question 1 (whether Illinois Brick bars an indirect purchaser's claim for lost profits on purchases that were never made) is the likelier vehicle for a grant, probably reformulated to ask whether Illinois Brick reaches damages that do not rest on an overcharge passed down a distribution chain. Question 2 (joint lobbying and trade-association participation as a plus factor) is the one the amici care about, but the Second Circuit's own framing treats it as "further support" for an inference already grounded in action against unilateral self-interest and the timing of the first two announcements, which makes it hard to isolate; if the Court takes question 2 it would be alongside question 1, not alone.
- **Summary disposition.** Conditional on a grant, a cert-order disposition rather than plenary review has P = 0.18. The only realistic summary route is a GVR after a merits decision in *Amerigroup* if that petition is granted first; absent that, a grant here means briefing and argument in October Term 2027.
- **Dissent from denial.** Conditional on denial, P(a Justice writes respecting or dissenting from the denial) = 0.10. The Illinois Brick issue has drawn statements from Sixth Circuit judges and has a long-running academic and judicial critique, so a short statement from Justice Gorsuch or Justice Kavanaugh noting the question for a future case is possible, but a CVSG'd petition the SG recommends denying usually goes quietly.

## Conditional merits shape

If granted on question 1, the likely ground is that Illinois Brick is a rule about overcharge pass-on and duplicative recovery, so a plaintiff whose injury is exclusion from a sales channel rather than an overcharge is not barred; a reversal would instead reaffirm the bright-line, plaintiff-specific reading of *Apple v. Pepper*. If granted on question 2, the likely result would be a narrow holding that shared lobbyists and trade-association membership add nothing to the plausibility calculus, with the judgment below possibly left standing on the other plus factors.
