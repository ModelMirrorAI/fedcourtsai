# Forecast: what the Court will do with No. 25-1070

**State forecast from.** The docket shows two `DISTRIBUTED` entries (for the
5/14/2026 conference, then for 9/28/2026), one `Rescheduled`, and a
`Response Requested` order of April 30, 2026 after respondents waived. The
first distribution was superseded by the call for a response, so the petition
has **never actually been considered at conference**; the September 28 long
conference is its first real look. No CVSG is on the docket. Two cert-stage
amicus briefs (Chamber of Commerce/NAM; Electric Power Supply Association)
support the petition, both on the *Twombly* question only.

**Companion petition.** United Biologics v. Amerigroup Tennessee, No. 25-1388
(from the Sixth Circuit's *Academy of Allergy & Asthma* decision), which
presents the same Illinois Brick lost-profits question from the other side of
the split, is distributed for the **same** 9/28 conference. The Court will
consider the two together.

**Disposition.** More likely denied than granted, but this is a genuine
candidate: P(any grant) 0.30. The most probable grant path is a plenary grant
on Question 1 (Illinois Brick), either consolidated with or alongside 25-1388.
A secondary path is a hold for 25-1388 followed by a GVR once *Amerigroup* is
decided, which is why the conditional summary-disposition route is priced at
0.25 rather than the paid segment's lower plenary-heavy share. If the Court
takes only one Illinois Brick vehicle, 25-1388 is the cleaner one (a single
question, a squarely presented group-boycott/lost-profits holding, and a
Sixth Circuit en banc record in which judges invited review), so the
likeliest single outcome for *this* docket remains a denial or a hold.

**Relists.** P(at least one further distribution) 0.55. A petition with a
called-for response, elite counsel, business amici, and a paired companion is
seldom disposed of at its first conference; expect one or two relists before
an order, whether that order is a grant, a hold-then-GVR, or a denial with a
separate writing. If the Court simply holds this case for 25-1388, no new
distribution entry appears, which is the main path to this claim resolving
false besides a clean denial on October 5.

**CVSG.** P(a CVSG issues) 0.20. The federal government administers 340B and
lost its own litigation over these very contract-pharmacy policies, and the
Illinois Brick question is one on which the Solicitor General has views. But
a CVSG is likelier to issue in 25-1388 (the cleaner vehicle) or in neither,
and the pair is already teed up for a decision without one.

**Which question.** If granted, the Court takes Question 1 (whether Illinois
Brick bars an indirect purchaser's lost-profit claim for purchases never made),
probably reformulated to track the Sixth Circuit split, and leaves Question 2
(the "opportunity to conspire" plus factor) behind: the BIO shows the panel
used joint lobbying and PhRMA participation only as "further support" for an
inference already drawn from parallel conduct against self-interest, so QP2 is
fact-bound as presented.

**Summary disposition.** Conditional on a grant, P(disposed of in the cert
order itself) 0.25, essentially the hold-then-GVR path if *Amerigroup* is
granted and decided first. Plenary review is the likelier grant form.

**Dissent from denial.** Conditional on denial, P(some Justice writes) 0.12.
Justice Gorsuch's *Apple v. Pepper* dissent shows appetite for revisiting
Illinois Brick's scope, and a statement respecting denial noting the split is
conceivable, but denials in paired-vehicle situations usually issue silently,
especially where the other vehicle is granted.

**If granted, the likely ground** (conditional only): a majority reaffirming
Illinois Brick as a plaintiff-status rule rather than a damages-measure rule,
reversing or vacating the Second Circuit on Question 1, with the 340B channel
facts complicating the analysis enough to invite a narrow opinion.
