We predict the Supreme Court will likely agree to hear this case (about a 72% chance). The federal government is asking the Court to overturn a ruling that it probably violated free-speech rights when it cancelled University of California research grants tied to diversity, equity and inclusion. The Court usually takes cases the government's top lawyer, the Solicitor General, brings. The issue also affects many similar lawsuits and large amounts of money. The main reason the Court might decline is that no other federal appeals court has ruled differently yet.
