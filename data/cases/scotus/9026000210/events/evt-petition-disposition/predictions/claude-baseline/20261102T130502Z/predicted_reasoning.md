# Forecast — Trump v. Thakur, No. 26-210

The petition has been distributed once, for the November 13, 2026 conference, and
has not been relisted. I expect the Court to **grant plenary review**, either at
that conference or after one relist (about even odds that it is distributed
again). There will be no CVSG, because the Solicitor General is the petitioner.

If granted, the Court takes the question as written: whether the First Amendment
bars the government from considering the viewpoint of a research project when
terminating (as well as awarding) competitive grants, with *Finley* and *Rust*
on one side and *Rosenberger* on the other. It may add a question on the Tucker
Act or remedy preclusion, but I think that is unlikely. A summary disposition
(GVR or per curiam reversal) is unlikely; the case would be briefed and argued
this Term.

If the Court instead denies, which could happen to let the 2d Cir. NEH appeals
play out first, I expect a written dissent from denial, most likely from Justice
Alito, joined by Justices Thomas and Gorsuch.
