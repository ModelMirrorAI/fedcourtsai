The Supreme Court is likely to agree to hear this case, with about an eight-in-ten chance of review. The dispute asks whether the federal government can cancel academic research grants because it disagrees with the viewpoints the research advances. The government's request, the nationwide importance of research funding, and the clear constitutional disagreement favor review. The lack of conflicting appeals-court rulings weighs against it. Expect full briefing and argument, rather than an immediate decision. This forecast does not predict who ultimately wins.
