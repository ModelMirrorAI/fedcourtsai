The Supreme Court is modestly likely—about a 56% chance—to grant the Solicitor General's petition to review this case. When the federal government asks the Supreme Court to review a lower-court ruling that blocks an executive policy under the Constitution, the Court agrees to hear the case nearly three-quarters of the time. Here, the Ninth Circuit ruled that the First Amendment's free-speech guarantee barred federal agencies from canceling competitive university research grants that promoted diversity, equity, and inclusion. That strong baseline chance of review is tempered, however, because this appeal involves only a temporary injunction while final proceedings are still underway in the trial court, and no other federal appeals court has yet issued a conflicting ruling on the same First Amendment question.
