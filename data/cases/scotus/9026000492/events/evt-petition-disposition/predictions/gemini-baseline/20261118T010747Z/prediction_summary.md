Roughly 540 out-of-state plaintiffs sued Zantac manufacturers in Illinois state court despite buying and taking the medication, and suffering their alleged injuries, outside Illinois. An Illinois appeals court dismissed the suits in an unpublished, non-binding decision that straightforwardly followed a 2017 Supreme Court precedent barring out-of-state plaintiffs from suing corporate defendants in a state unconnected to their injuries, and both drugmakers waived filing a response, signaling virtually no chance the Justices intervene.
