The Supreme Court will almost certainly decline to hear this case — the chance it agrees to review is under one in a hundred. About 180 out-of-state people who say the heartburn drug Zantac gave them cancer want to sue its makers in Illinois; Illinois courts said they lacked personal jurisdiction (the power to hear claims against those out-of-state companies). The ruling below is unpublished, follows a 2017 Supreme Court precedent closely, and the drug makers did not even file a response, which usually signals a routine denial.
