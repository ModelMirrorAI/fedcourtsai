# Forecast — No. 26-492, Coudrain v. Boehringer Ingelheim

The docket shows **one distribution** of the petition (for the December 4, 2026 conference), with both respondent groups having waived response and no response called for.

- **Disposition:** The Court will deny certiorari, most likely on the order list following the December 4 conference (December 7, 2026).
- **Relist:** No further distribution. The petition will not be relisted, and no Justice will call for a response.
- **CVSG:** None. The dispute is between private parties over a state court's personal jurisdiction and implicates no federal program.
- **Question presented:** If the Court ever took the case, it would take the single QP as written — whether a nonresident plaintiff injured elsewhere may invoke specific jurisdiction based on the defendant's in-forum packaging, labeling and distribution activity under *Ford Motor* — but it is more likely to await a published decision from a state high court or federal court of appeals presenting a developed conflict.
- **Summary disposition:** Unlikely; no intervening decision bears on the question, so a GVR has no predicate, and the unpublished decision below is a faithful-looking application of *Bristol-Myers Squibb* rather than a candidate for summary reversal.
- **Separate writing:** The denial will be unaccompanied by any noted dissent or statement.
