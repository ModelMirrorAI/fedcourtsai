## In brief

Nonresident Zantac plaintiffs ask the Court to revive their Illinois lawsuits against two drugmakers after state courts dismissed their claims for lack of jurisdiction over out-of-state injuries.

## What happened

Marilyn Coudrain and roughly 180 other out-of-state plaintiffs sued Boehringer Ingelheim and Sanofi entities in Cook County, Illinois, claiming Zantac, a heartburn drug, caused their cancer. None of these plaintiffs lived in Illinois, bought Zantac there, or were injured there, but they argued the companies used Illinois companies to repackage, label, market, and ship Zantac nationwide, including to Walgreens, an Illinois company. The circuit court dismissed their claims for lack of personal jurisdiction, the Illinois Appellate Court affirmed, and the Illinois Supreme Court denied review.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether a state court may hear claims by out-of-state plaintiffs against out-of-state companies when those companies used the state to package, label, market, and distribute a nationwide product, even though the plaintiffs were not injured in that state. The petitioners argue the lower courts wrongly required a direct, in-state injury rather than asking whether their claims merely relate to the companies' in-state activities.

## Where it stands

The petition was distributed for the Court's conference on October 9, 2026, where the Court granted a motion to file part of the petition under seal; both groups of respondents then waived their right to respond. The case was distributed again for the conference of December 4, 2026, and the docket shows no decision yet on whether the Court will hear the case.

## What each outcome would mean

Coudrain and the other plaintiffs are asking the Court to take up their case and overturn the Illinois courts' rulings. If the Court grants review, it would hear full briefing and argument on when states may exercise jurisdiction over nonresident defendants in cases like this, deciding the question itself without presuming either side correct. If the Court denies review, the Illinois Appellate Court's decision stands, leaving the plaintiffs' claims dismissed and Boehringer Ingelheim and Sanofi not subject to suit in Illinois on these claims.
