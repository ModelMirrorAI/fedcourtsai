# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny the petition, most likely in the orders following its October 16, 2026 conference. The snapshot contains **one distribution and zero relists**. My modal forecast is **zero additional distributions**, no CVSG, and no dissent from denial or statement respecting denial. The probability of at least one further distribution is 28%; on that less likely branch I expect one or two additional distributions rather than an extended series. A respondent-response request would be the most natural preliminary step if the petition attracts serious attention, and is distinct from a CVSG.

I assign a 14% probability to any grant, including summary relief. Conditional on intervention, the Court is more likely to dispose of the case in its cert order than to set plenary argument: **72% summary route, conditional on grant**. The leading such route is a GVR for reconsideration in light of Margolin's party-presentation instruction; a summary reversal on that procedural ground is a secondary possibility. This would not itself adjudicate the underlying adoption or require the Court to decide that no protective order could survive strict scrutiny.

If the Court grants plenary review, I expect it to focus the petition's single question on whether an appellate court may uphold a content-based prior restraint on a materially new compelling-interest rationale, not advanced by a party, without affording adversarial consideration. I do not expect plenary review of the entire custody controversy or every application of the protective order. On that grant branch, the likely corrective ground is the procedure used to sustain the restraint, rather than a categorical rejection of national-security interests in confidentiality.

The modal denial forecast anticipates that the Court will leave this fact-sensitive interlocutory dispute in the lower courts rather than select it to clarify the boundary between a new issue and an alternative legal ground. A denial will ordinarily supply no explanation; this sentence forecasts the likely selection consideration, not reasons that a denial order will necessarily state. The probability of a CVSG is 4%, and the probability of a recorded separate writing is 10% conditional on denial. I predict no particular Justice will write and no merits opinion will accompany the modal denial.
