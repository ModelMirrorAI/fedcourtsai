The Court will likely treat the State's account of an independent no-prejudice determination, together with the late filing, as stronger than the request to preserve time for the Sixth Circuit's threshold analysis. This is a forecast of the rationale supporting the interim result, not a prediction that the order will contain a reasoned majority opinion. A short grant-and-vacatur order is the expected form.

That route, and the possibility that intervening lower-court action makes this application unnecessary, account for the remaining probability. No individual votes or writing assignments are forecast.
