## In brief

An objector asks the Court to decide whether judges may award class-action representatives extra payments for their service, beyond sharing in the settlement.

## What happened

A class action brought by three nonprofits accused the federal government of overcharging PACER users for court-record access. The case settled for $125 million, with class members reimbursed, class counsel awarded fees of nearly $24 million, and each of the three named plaintiffs given a $10,000 "incentive award" for serving as class representatives. Eric Alan Isaacson, a class member who objected to the settlement, argued these incentive payments were unlawful. The district court approved the settlement anyway, and the Federal Circuit affirmed, holding that older Supreme Court rulings limiting such payments do not bar incentive awards in modern class actions.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether judges may approve payments to class representatives, from a common settlement fund, specifically to reward them for serving as representatives in the lawsuit—even though no statute or procedural rule authorizes such payments, and old Supreme Court precedent has been read by some courts to forbid rewarding litigants for their personal service in a case.

## Where it stands

The petition was filed in September 2026. The federal government, as a respondent, waived its right to respond. The other respondents received two extensions of time to respond, with the deadline now set for November 12, 2026. The Court has not yet acted on the petition.

## What each outcome would mean

Isaacson, the objector, is asking the Court to take up the case and strike down the incentive awards. If the Court grants review, it would hear the case fully and decide whether such awards are lawful, without yet deciding who wins. If the Court denies review, the Federal Circuit's decision stands, leaving the $10,000 incentive awards to the three named plaintiffs in place and leaving intact that court's approval of the settlement and fee award.
