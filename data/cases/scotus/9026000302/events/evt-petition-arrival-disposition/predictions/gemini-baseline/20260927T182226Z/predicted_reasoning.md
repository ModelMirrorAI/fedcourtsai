Forecast at docketing: No distributions yet. I expect the petition to be distributed, and likely relisted once or twice as the Court considers the deep circuit split on class-action incentive awards. 
The Court is unlikely to call for the views of the Solicitor General (CVSG) because the United States is already a respondent in the case (the underlying litigation is the PACER fee class action against the government). The Solicitor General will likely express the government's views in a Brief in Opposition.
If granted, the Court would take the question presented regarding whether *Greenough* and *Pettus* prohibit incentive awards to representative plaintiffs in common-fund class actions. A summary disposition route (like a GVR) is unlikely unless the Court grants a companion case (like *Isaacson v. Moses* from the Second Circuit, which raises the exact same issue) and holds this one for it. 
If denied, there is a fair chance of a dissent from denial (e.g., by Justice Thomas or Alito) given the 5-1 circuit split and the importance of the issue in class-action practice.
