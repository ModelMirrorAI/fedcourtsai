# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny certiorari, most likely without a separate writing. The petition presents an express disagreement over class-representative service awards, but this vehicle will not attract sufficient support for plenary review. P(any grant), including a GVR, is 22%.

The baseline records **zero distributions and no CVSG**. I expect an initial conference distribution and ordinarily no actual relist after that conference; one additional distribution is the next most plausible short path. P(at least one distribution after this baseline) is 98%, which includes the first conference distribution rather than requiring a second. A longer hold for another petition on the same issue is a secondary path.

The Court will not call for the Solicitor General's views separately: P(a new CVSG) is 2%. The government's participation as a respondent can supply its position through ordinary briefing.

If review is granted, the Court will take the petition's core question: whether the common-fund precedents categorically prohibit reasonable service awards to Rule 23 class representatives absent express statutory or rule authorization. It will not use that grant to reopen the PACER-fee merits or the separate attorney-fee calculation. This is a conditional question-selection forecast, not a prediction that any merits opinion will issue.

Conditional on a grant, plenary consideration is more likely than disposition in the cert order: 65% versus 35%. The principal summary path is a hold and later GVR if another case supplies controlling guidance, rather than an immediate summary reversal on this record.

Conditional on denial, I expect no noted dissent or statement respecting denial; the probability of any such writing is 10%. I make no Justice-specific forecast.
