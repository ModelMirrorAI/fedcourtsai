We predict the Supreme Court will **deny** the petition for review, with about a **one-in-five chance (22%)** that the Court agrees to hear the case. The Public Interest Legal Foundation asks the Court to decide whether the National Voter Registration Act requires Hawaii to release its entire statewide list of registered voters to the public, noting that the Ninth Circuit disagreed with the First Circuit on that question. Although the Supreme Court asked Hawaii to file a response, the Court is still likely to turn the case away because only two federal appeals courts have directly decided this specific statutory issue and other courts have questioned whether the challengers suffered enough concrete harm to sue in federal court.
