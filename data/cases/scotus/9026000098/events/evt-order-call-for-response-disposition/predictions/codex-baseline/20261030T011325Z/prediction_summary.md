The Supreme Court is more likely to decline this case than hear it, although review has about a two-in-five chance. The dispute asks whether federal law requires states to let the public inspect statewide voter-registration lists. Appeals courts disagree, and the Supreme Court asked Hawaii to respond after it initially waived a response. Those are meaningful signs of interest, but they do not guarantee review. A refusal to hear the case would leave the lower-court result in place without deciding which interpretation is right nationwide.
