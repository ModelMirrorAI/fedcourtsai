# Forecast of the Court's action

The most likely eventual disposition is denial of certiorari. The probability of any grant, including a GVR or summary reversal, is 42%. This is a close certworthiness call because the First/Ninth Circuit conflict over the NVRA's disclosure requirement is express and recurring, and the Court has requested a response after waiver.

The record contains **one distribution** and **no CVSG**. The petition will most likely receive at least one additional distribution after completion of the requested response and reply cycle: probability **97%**. Expect one new distribution, with a second a plausible alternative. The first additional distribution will principally serve to put the completed papers before a conference, rather than necessarily represent renewed consideration after a prior conference. The already listed September 28 conference is not evidence that the Court actually considered the petition before the requested response arrived.

Expect no call for the Solicitor General's views: probability of a new CVSG is **22%**. If the Court takes that route, the invitation will most likely follow the first conference with completed briefing, rather than precede Hawaii's response. A CVSG would prolong the petition into later 2027; without one, the expected disposition window is December 2026 through early 2027, assuming the current November 25 response deadline holds. Timing is a forecast, not a claim that the docket has scheduled those steps.

If review is granted, the Court will most likely take the single question presented substantially as framed: whether a statewide voter registration list falls within § 20507(i)(1)'s disclosure requirement for records concerning implementation of list-maintenance programs and activities. The Court need not decide every issue concerning permissible uses, redactions, or sensitive voter information to decide whether the list is covered at all. If it reaches the merits, the likely ground for disturbing the decision below is that the statutory coverage of records relating to list-maintenance implementation includes the resulting voter file, while leaving particular privacy limitations to more focused analysis. This merits forecast is conditional on review, not a prediction of an opinion accompanying denial.

**Plenary review is the likelier grant route.** Conditional on any grant, the chance of disposition in the cert order itself is **6%**. No intervening controlling ruling or mootness development appears in the record to supply a natural GVR basis; resolving the statutory disagreement is more suited to briefing and argument than summary reversal.

If review is denied, expect an unexplained order and no separate writing. The conditional chance of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial is **15%**. No particular Justice's writing or cert vote is forecast.
