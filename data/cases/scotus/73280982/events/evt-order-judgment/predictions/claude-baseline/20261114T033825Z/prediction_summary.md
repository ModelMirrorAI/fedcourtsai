The Supreme Court will very likely (about 84 percent) set aside the Ninth Circuit's ruling that let investors sue Robinhood over its 2021 stock offering simply because it left out important recent financial results. The predicted judgment is "vacated", meaning the lower ruling is wiped out and the case sent back, by a 9–0 vote. The main reason: the federal government told the Court the lower court misread the law, which requires showing that an omission made a specific statement misleading, and the Court took up exactly that question.
