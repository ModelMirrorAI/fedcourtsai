The Supreme Court is very likely (84% chance) to disturb the Ninth Circuit's decision, most likely by vacating it—wiping away the ruling and sending the case back to the lower courts—in a unanimous 9–0 vote. Federal securities law makes a company liable in a public stock offering for leaving out important facts only when a rule requires disclosure or when leaving the fact out makes something the company actually said misleading, and the Ninth Circuit wrongly skipped that second step; only a modest chance that the Justices dismiss the appeal without deciding it tempers the odds.
