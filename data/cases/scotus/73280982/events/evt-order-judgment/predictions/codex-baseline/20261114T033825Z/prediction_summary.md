The Supreme Court is very likely—about an 86% chance—to set aside the appeals court's decision in Robinhood's investor lawsuit. I expect all nine Justices to agree and send the case back. The likely reason is that leaving out information important to investors does not automatically make a company's actual statements misleading; courts must examine both questions. That would help Robinhood without necessarily ending the lawsuit, because investors could still pursue other disclosure requirements or show that specific statements really were misleading.
