# Forecast of the Court's decision

## Judgment and votes

The Court will **vacate the Ninth Circuit's judgment and remand for further proceedings** under a corrected misleading-omissions standard. The predicted lineup is **9–0**: Roberts, Thomas, Alito, Sotomayor, Kagan, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh, Barrett, and Jackson will join the Court's opinion. This disposition will not direct judgment for Robinhood on every theory in the complaint.

## Majority ground

The Court will hold that Section 11(a)'s misleading-omissions prong requires an independent determination that omitted material information makes an identified affirmative statement misleading. Materiality of interim financial results does not itself establish that requirement. The Ninth Circuit's merger of the inquiries conflicts with the statutory text and Omnicare's contextual understanding of misleading omissions. Macquarie's interpretation of the parallel language in Rule 10b-5(b) will reinforce that distinction.

The opinion will distinguish Section 11's separate required-statements branch. It will not read Macquarie as eliminating liability for omissions that another disclosure requirement makes actionable. Nor will it require that materiality and misleading effect be proved with entirely different evidence: the error is treating one finding as automatically establishing the other.

## Breadth and remand

The Court will announce a rule governing misleading-omissions claims involving interim financial information generally, while leaving application to Robinhood's particular disclosures and the separate Item 303 required-disclosure theory for further proceedings. It will not create a categorical exemption for financial information whose ordinary quarterly reporting deadline has not arrived.

The Court will reach only the reformulated first question granted on November 13, 2026. It will not decide the petition's second question about the scope of Item 303, mandate the First Circuit's extreme-departure test, or hold that accurate historical figures can never become misleading when placed alongside selective contemporary disclosures. The lower courts will have to examine the allegedly misleading statements, the omitted information, and the full context rather than infer misleading effect from materiality alone.

## Writings and procedural alternatives

Sotomayor is the single most likely author, given her authorship of Macquarie; Kagan, who wrote Omnicare, is a close alternative. The modal forecast includes no separate writing. A concurrence, if one appears, will most likely stress that genuine half-truth allegations remain actionable and that the Court has not resolved whether these plaintiffs adequately pleaded one.

A dismissal as improvidently granted remains the principal procedural alternative: the respondents argue that the lower court already required a misleading statement and that the unreviewed Item 303 theory keeps the suit alive. The reformulated grant makes an actual legal resolution more likely than that exit. No equally divided disposition is expected on the provisioned participation record.
