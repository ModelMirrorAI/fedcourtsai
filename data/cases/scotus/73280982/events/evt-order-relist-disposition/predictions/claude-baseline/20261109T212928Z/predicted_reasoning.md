# Forecast — Robinhood Markets v. Sodha (No. 25-944)

**Record state.** The docket shows three distributions: 5/28/2026 (before the CVSG), 11/6/2026, and a relist on 11/9 for the 11/13/2026 conference. The CVSG has been answered: the SG recommends granting QP1, reformulated, and denying QP2.

- **Disposition.** The Court grants certiorari limited to Question 1, most likely in the SG's reformulated wording ("…without an additional inquiry to determine whether the omission rendered any affirmative statement misleading"). It denies or does not reach Question 2 (Item 303).
- **Timing and relists.** A grant most likely comes from the 11/13 conference, announced on the following order list, with no further distribution. There is a meaningful chance (about 1 in 3) of one more relist before the order. That would most likely happen while the Court settles the QP wording or while a Justice writes.
- **CVSG.** The CVSG has already issued and been answered, so no further call is coming.
- **Summary disposition.** This is unlikely. No intervening decision supports a GVR, and the SG asks for plenary review rather than a summary reversal.
- **If granted.** The Court would likely hold that materiality and misleadingness are separate elements of a Section 11(a) misleading-omissions claim. The likely basis is *Omnicare* and *Macquarie*: an omission is actionable only if it makes an affirmative statement misleading. The Court would then vacate the Ninth Circuit's merger of the two inquiries.
- **If denied.** A dissent from denial is plausible given the SG's support and the circuit conflict. Thomas, Alito, Gorsuch, or Kavanaugh are the likeliest authors.
