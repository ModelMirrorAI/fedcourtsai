The Supreme Court is likely to hear Robinhood's appeal, with about a four-in-five chance of accepting at least part of it. The likeliest outcome is review of only the first issue: whether leaving out important financial information makes an investment document misleading without a separate examination of what it actually said. The federal government's recommendation and the Court's renewed consideration support review. Investors counter that there is no genuine legal disagreement. A full hearing, rather than an immediate ruling, is expected if review is granted.
