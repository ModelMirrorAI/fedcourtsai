Distributed once so far, followed by a CVSG. I expect the petition to be distributed at least once more after the Solicitor General files their brief. Given the prominent circuit split on Section 11(a) disclosure duties and the recent precedent in *Macquarie Infrastructure Corp.* (2024), the SG is highly likely to recommend that the Court take the case. 

If granted, the Court would likely take both questions presented to resolve the interplay between Section 11(a) and Item 303 regarding interim financial data. A summary GVR in light of *Macquarie* is possible, as the Ninth Circuit decision was issued after *Macquarie* and arguably failed to apply its principles to Section 11(a), but a plenary grant is the likelier route given the importance of the issue to the SEC's reporting framework. Should the Court deny the petition, there is a moderate chance (25%) that a conservative Justice notes a dissent from denial, given the perceived overreach of the Ninth Circuit's disclosure rule.
