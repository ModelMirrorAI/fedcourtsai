State being forecast from: the docket shows **one distribution** (for the

## What the Court will do

- **The SG's brief lands in roughly December 2026–February 2027.** A June
  CVSG typically draws the invited brief six to eight months later, often
  timed to a winter filing so the case can be resolved within the Term. No
  deadline binds the SG, so slippage to spring 2027 is possible.
- **The petition will be redistributed after the SG files — at least one
  further distribution is near-certain (0.96).** Redistribution following an
  invited brief is the Court's standard mechanics; only a settlement or
  withdrawal of the petition while it sits with the SG would prevent it.
  Expect one to three further distributions, with resolution one to four
  weeks after the SG brief arrives.
- **No second CVSG** — one is already on the docket; the cvsg-increment claim
  is vacuous for this cell (stated at 0.02).
- **Disposition: a close call I put slightly against grant — P(grant family)
  0.45, so the single most likely outcome remains denial.** If the SG
  recommends grant, expect a plenary grant (likely certiorari on both
  questions presented, since QP2's Item 303 theory is the required-statements
  half of the same omissions architecture); the Court follows the SG's cert
  recommendation in the large majority of CVSG'd cases. If the SG recommends
  denial — most plausibly on the interlocutory-posture and
  fact-boundedness grounds the BIO presses — denial follows with high
  probability. Resolution most likely lands March–June 2027; a grant would be
  argued in OT2027.
- **If narrowed, QP1 survives.** The misleading-omissions-prong question
  (whether Section 11(a) reaches pure omissions of material interim data
  without an inquiry into whether any affirmative statement was rendered
  misleading) is the cleaner, Macquarie-adjacent question; the Item 303
  quantification question (QP2) is the more fact-entangled one and the likelier
  casualty of a narrowing.
- **No summary disposition.** There is no intervening decision for a GVR to
  ride on — Macquarie Infrastructure v. Moab Partners predates the Ninth
  Circuit's decision, which distinguished rather than overlooked it — and the
  Court does not summarily reverse in this posture. Probability of GVR or
  summary reversal: under 0.03 combined.
- **If denied, expect no separate writing.** Denials that follow an SG
  recommendation to deny rarely draw dissents; a short statement respecting
  denial (most plausibly from a Justice sympathetic to cabining private
  securities litigation) is possible but against the odds.
