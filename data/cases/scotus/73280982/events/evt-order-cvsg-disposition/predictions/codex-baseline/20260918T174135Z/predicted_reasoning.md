# Forecast

The Court will most likely deny the petition after receiving the Solicitor General's views. The probability of any grant, including a partial grant or summary grant route, is **44%**. Denial would leave the remand in place without endorsing the Ninth Circuit's reasoning.

The baseline contains **one conference distribution**, entered May 12, 2026 for the May 28 conference, and the **June 1, 2026 CVSG**. I expect the government brief to be followed by renewed conference consideration: most likely **one or two additional distributions** beyond the one already recorded. The probability of at least one additional distribution is **96%**. This includes the first post-CVSG return to conference; it need not be a true relist following another consideration. I do not predict another invitation to the Solicitor General. The existing invitation is settled history, and the structured CVSG-increment entry is zero and vacuous for this conditioning state.

If review is granted, the stronger candidate is the petition's **first question**: whether liability under Section 11(a)'s misleading-omissions clause requires identifying an affirmative statement made misleading by the omitted interim financial information, rather than treating materiality alone as sufficient. I expect any grant to focus on that distinction, potentially reformulating the question to avoid assuming the contested characterization of the decision below. The broader Item 303 question could accompany it, but is less likely to be taken alone in the petition's categorical form.

Conditional on any grant, **plenary review is substantially more likely than a summary disposition**; the summary-route probability is **12%**. The disputed reach of the majority opinion, the separate required-disclosure theory, and the unresolved application to the allegations favor briefing and argument. Macquarie predates the decision below, so it supplies a possible conflict argument, not an intervening-decision GVR premise. If granted, I expect the Court's analysis to preserve the distinction between misleading omissions and independently required disclosures rather than announce a categorical exemption for all interim financial information.

Conditional on denial, I expect **no separate writing**; the probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial is **12%**. I make no per-Justice vote prediction.
