# Rationale

I assign a 0.42 probability to any grant and select denial as the modal disposition. The committed statpack's paid CVSG cut has a 34.9% grant-family rate among resolved petitions (29.4% plenary grants plus 5.5% GVRs). More specifically, this cell carries the `high` band under matching salience version `sal-v3`; pooling the bracketed risk-set rates across Terms 2017 through 2024, all strictly before this petition's Term 2025, gives 439 grant-equivalents over 1,074 weighted resolved petitions, or about 40.9%. I use that as the main anchor and adjust slightly upward for this petition's particulars.

The strongest positive evidence is the CVSG itself. The Court took an affirmative step after the petition's first conference, and the dispute concerns the interaction between Section 11(a), Item 303, and the SEC's periodic-reporting framework, an area in which the federal government's view is unusually informative. The petition presents two developed legal questions, a divided 100-page Ninth Circuit decision, denial of rehearing over dissent, sophisticated Supreme Court counsel, and three amicus filings from securities-market and business interests. The rule's potential reach beyond this IPO supports the 0.76 significance score.

The opposition supplies substantial reasons not to move above even odds. It argues that the Ninth Circuit merely remanded for application of ordinary materiality principles, that the purported split concerns fact-specific applications rather than competing rules, and that Robinhood does not ask the Court to adopt the First Circuit's extreme-departure test or a fixed Item 303 duration rule. The case is interlocutory, the district court has not applied the remand standard, and the BIO plausibly analogizes the petition to the pleading-stage securities cases the Court recently dismissed as improvidently granted. Those vehicle concerns make a recommendation to deny, or to let the SEC address Item 303, quite plausible. The Ninth Circuit origin has a modestly higher whole-docket grant-family rate than the overall modern docket, but it adds little after conditioning on the CVSG/high band.

The snapshot shows one distribution and a CVSG dated June 1, 2026. Because a CVSG petition ordinarily returns to conference after the Solicitor General's brief, I assign 0.98 to at least one additional distribution. The already-issued CVSG makes `cvsg-increment` vacuous for scoring; 0.01 reflects the negligible chance of another invitation after prediction time. Conditional on a grant, I assign only 0.08 to a cert-order disposition because the questions call for plenary interpretation and no intervening judgment supports a GVR. Conditional on denial, I assign 0.06 to any separate statement or dissent: the stakes are appreciable, but the vehicle objections offer an ordinary basis to deny without writing.

The provisioned `questions-presented.txt` does not contain the questions presented; it begins in the petition's importance discussion. I recovered the two questions from the intact petition and BIO, so this did not block the forecast. The petition PDF text is marked truncated in `documents.json`, but its cert-stage argument and appendix portions needed here were present.
