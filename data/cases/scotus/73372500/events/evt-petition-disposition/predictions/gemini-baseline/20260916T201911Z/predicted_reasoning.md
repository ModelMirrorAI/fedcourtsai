Distributed once so far; expect zero further relists before a decision on the petition. Although the petition attempts to frame a circuit split regarding substantive due process in land-use disputes, the vehicle is fatally flawed. As the petitioner concedes, the South Carolina Supreme Court issued an unexplained summary order that may rest on adequate and independent state grounds. Because of this defect, the Court is highly unlikely to take the case, and the respondents correctly waived their right to respond. No CVSG is expected, as no federal interest is implicated and the vehicle issues prevent review anyway.

If granted, the Court would likely only do so to issue a summary GVR (vacate and remand) for clarification of the state court's grounds, rather than granting plenary review on the substantive due process question. Should it deny, expect no separate writing.
