# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny certiorari. The snapshot records one distribution, for the September 28, 2026 conference, and no CVSG. Expect disposition following that conference with zero additional distributions, no request for the Solicitor General's views, and no dissent from or statement respecting denial. The probability of at least one additional distribution is 8%; if further distributed, one additional distribution is the likeliest extension rather than a prolonged series. The probability of a new CVSG is 0.2%.

The likely obstacle to review is the vehicle: the South Carolina Supreme Court declined to exercise original jurisdiction without deciding the competing federal substantive-due-process standards. The disputed permitting authority, other state proceedings, and uncertain presentation of the federal claim will prevent this case from becoming the vehicle for resolving the asserted circuit disagreement. A denial will not adjudicate whether the permits were lawful or whether CAGI's constitutional theory is correct, and no explanation of the denial is expected.

Conditional on any grant, a summary vacatur and remand for clarification of the state court's grounds is more likely than plenary briefing and argument: 65% conditional probability for the cert-order disposition route. The petition expressly requests that alternative. The anticipated summary route would clarify whether the refusal rested on state procedural grounds, not decide the zoning merits or summarily reverse on substantive due process. It is not a forecast of a GVR tied to an identified intervening merits decision.

If instead plenary review is granted, the Court would take the single question presented in a narrowed form: whether allegedly unauthorized executive permitting after a zoning board's contrary ruling can support a Fourteenth Amendment substantive-due-process claim, and what constitutional arbitrariness standard applies. It would not take a general question about the project's desirability or directly resolve the local zoning classification. Conditional on denial, the probability of any noted dissent or statement is 1.5%; no particular Justice is forecast to write.
