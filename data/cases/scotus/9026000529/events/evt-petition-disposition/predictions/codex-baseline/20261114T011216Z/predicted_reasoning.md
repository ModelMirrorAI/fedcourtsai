# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny certiorari, most likely without an accompanying writing. P(any grant) is 1.2%; the grant-side category includes summary relief. A denial will leave the conviction standing without deciding whether every challenged evidentiary ruling was correct.

The record already shows **one distribution**, for the December 4, 2026 conference, and **no relist**. I assign 7% to at least one additional distribution beyond that baseline. The modal path is no additional distribution and denial on an order list shortly after that conference, most likely in December 2026. Conditional on any further distribution, one more is more likely than a long string; a requested response could instead push consideration into early 2027. The government has waived a response, and the modal path includes no response call.

There has been **no CVSG**. The probability of a new call for the Solicitor General's views is 0.1%. The ordinary response mechanism, rather than an invited third-party assessment from a government already appearing as respondent, would be the natural first step if the Court wanted further briefing.

If review is granted, the Court will most likely retain the petition's core constitutional question but focus it on whether excluding these mental-health messages under Rule 403 denied a meaningful opportunity to present the suicide defense. I do not expect a broad reformulation covering all the other alleged trial errors.

Conditional on a grant, I assign **25%** to disposition in the cert order itself and **75%** to a grant opening plenary merits proceedings. The plausible immediate route would be a summary reversal for misapplication of the complete-defense precedents; no identified intervening decision makes a routine GVR the leading grant scenario. If plenary review occurs, the central issue will be whether the individualized exclusion was arbitrary or disproportionate despite the defense expert testimony and the messages' age.

Conditional on denial, the probability of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial is **1.8%**. I expect none. If a writing appears, Sotomayor or Jackson is more plausible than the other individual writers, with Gorsuch another possibility.
