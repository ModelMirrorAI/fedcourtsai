The Supreme Court is very likely to decline Craig Becker's request to review his murder conviction; I put the chance of review at about one in eighty, or 1.2%. He argues that excluding his wife's messages about her mental health undermined his defense that she died by suicide. The issue is serious, but the petition mainly challenges how established evidence rules were applied in this particular trial, rather than showing a clear disagreement among courts. The government has waived a response, and the Court has scheduled its first consideration for December 4.
