The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Lieutenant Craig Becker's request to review his Navy murder conviction, with less than a one-in-one-hundred chance (0.8%) of taking the case. Becker argues that the military trial judge violated his constitutional right to present a defense by excluding two ambiguous text messages from his late wife that he claimed suggested suicide, but the judge reasonably found from surrounding messages that she was actually expressing fear of Becker's own abuse. Because the case involves a routine, fact-specific evidence ruling rather than a disagreement among lower courts, and the federal government waived its right to file a response, the Court will almost certainly turn the appeal away without comment.
