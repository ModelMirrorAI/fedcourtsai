The Supreme Court will very likely decline to hear this case — a grant is about a one-in-two-hundred chance. A Navy officer convicted by a military court of murdering his wife argues that the trial judge wrongly kept out her messages about fearing memory loss, which he says supported his suicide defense. The dispute turns on the particular evidence in one trial rather than a legal question dividing lower courts, and the government did not even file a response, which usually precedes a routine denial.
