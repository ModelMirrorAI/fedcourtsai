# Forecast — Becker v. United States, No. 26-529

The docket shows **one distribution** (for the December 4, 2026 conference), made after the United States waived its response.

- **Disposition:** The Court will deny certiorari, most likely on the order list following the December 4 conference (December 7, 2026).
- **Relists:** No further distribution. The petition will be disposed of at its first conference. The only realistic path to another distribution is a Justice calling for a response from the Solicitor General before the conference; I expect that not to happen (about 4%).
- **CVSG:** None. The United States is the respondent, so there is no occasion to invite the Solicitor General's views.
- **Question presented:** If the Court took the case, it would take the single QP as written — whether excluding the decedent's statements about her mental health violated the right to present a complete defense — but it would more likely be treated as a request for error correction than as a vehicle for a new rule.
- **Summary disposition:** A GVR is unlikely because no intervening decision bears on the question. Conditional on any grant, plenary review is somewhat more likely than a summary vacatur (roughly 65/35).
- **Separate writings:** The denial will be unsigned and without noted dissent or statement from any Justice.
