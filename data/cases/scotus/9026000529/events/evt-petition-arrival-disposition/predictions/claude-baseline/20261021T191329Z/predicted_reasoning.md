# Forecast — Becker v. United States (No. 26-529), at docketing

**State forecast from:** docketed Oct 21 2026; zero distributions, no CVSG, response due Nov 20 2026.

- **Disposition:** the Court will **deny** certiorari.
- **Distribution:** the petition will be distributed for conference (most likely after the United States waives its response, for a conference in roughly late November–December 2026). I expect it to be denied at its **first** conference with **no relist**.
- **Response:** the Solicitor General will most likely file a waiver; a call for a response from the Court is unlikely.
- **CVSG:** none — the United States is the respondent, so a call for the Solicitor General's views cannot meaningfully arise.
- **Question the Court would take, if any:** the single QP as written — whether excluding the decedent's statements about fearing loss of her mental faculties violated the right to present a complete defense (Chambers / Holmes).
- **Summary route:** if the Court did act, a GVR or summary disposition is somewhat less likely than plenary review, since no intervening decision bears on the question; plenary grant is itself very unlikely.
- **Separate writing:** I expect no dissent from or statement respecting denial.
