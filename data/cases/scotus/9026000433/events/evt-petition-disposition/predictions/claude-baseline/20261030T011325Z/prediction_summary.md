We expect the Supreme Court to turn down this case: we put the chance it agrees to hear it at about one in twenty. A mother is suing a Philadelphia officer who photographed her dying son and shared the pictures as a joke. She asks whether outrageous official conduct alone violates the Constitution. Courts of appeals disagree on that question. But the officer won on "qualified immunity", a rule that shields officials unless the law was already clear. That makes the case a poor vehicle for settling the question.
