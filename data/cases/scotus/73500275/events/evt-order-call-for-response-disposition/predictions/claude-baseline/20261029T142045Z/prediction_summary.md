We think it is slightly more likely than not (about 52%) that the Supreme Court will act on this petition from ON24 and its IPO underwriters, most likely by holding it and then sending it back to the lower court for another look. A closely related securities case, Robinhood v. Sodha, raises the same question about when leaving information out of an IPO filing (the document a company files before first selling shares to the public) counts as misleading. The government has urged the Court to take that case. If the Court takes it and rules for the companies, this case would likely go back to the Ninth Circuit too.
