# Forecast: ON24, Inc. v. Leadersel Innotech ESG (No. 25-1376)

**State forecast from.** One distribution (for the 9/28/2026 conference, entered July 15, 2026), no relist, no CVSG. The Court called for a response on July 22, 2026, after the respondent waived. (The live docket shows the response was later filed, then the reply, and the petition went out again for the November 6, 2026 conference.)

**Disposition: the Court holds this petition for *Robinhood Markets, Inc. v. Sodha*, No. 25-944, then GVRs it.** *Robinhood* is distributed for the same November 6 conference, and the Solicitor General recommends granting it on its first question (reformulated): whether a Section 11(a) misleading-omissions claim can rest on materiality alone, without a separate inquiry into whether an affirmative statement was misleading. ON24's QP 2 is that question, and the Ninth Circuit put this appeal on hold for *Robinhood* and decided it in light of that ruling. I expect the Court to grant *Robinhood* and hold ON24 until *Robinhood* is decided, probably at the end of OT2026. If *Robinhood* reverses or vacates, ON24 gets a GVR. If *Robinhood* affirms, or is denied, ON24 is most likely denied.

**Relists.** It will almost certainly be distributed again: a held petition is relisted or rescheduled conference after conference until the lead case is decided. Expect many more distributions, not one.

**CVSG.** None. The government has already given its views in the companion case.

**Which question, if plenary.** A plenary grant here is the less likely route. If it happens, the Court would take QP 1, whether describing unmaterialized risks as hypothetical can mislead. That is the risk-factor split *Facebook v. Amalgamated Bank* left open when it was dismissed as improvidently granted. The Court would leave QP 3 (Item 303) alone, consistent with the SG's advice to deny the parallel required-statements question in *Robinhood*.

**Summary route.** If the Court grants anything, it is most likely a GVR in light of *Robinhood* rather than plenary review. The unpublished memorandum disposition below makes ON24 a weaker plenary vehicle than *Robinhood*.

**Dissent from denial.** If the Court denies, expect no separate writing.
