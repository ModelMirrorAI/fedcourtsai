The Supreme Court is likely to deny ON24’s petition for review, with about a one-in-four (25%) chance of granting it. After its 2021 initial public offering, ON24 was sued by investors claiming its registration documents described customer losses as a future risk when those losses were already happening. Although the Court asked the investors to respond to ON24’s appeal, the lower court’s ruling is an unpublished, fact-specific decision, and the Justices are already considering a better-positioned case (*Robinhood Markets v. Sodha*) that raises the same securities-law questions. Unless the Court holds ON24’s appeal waiting for a decision in *Robinhood* and later sends it back to the lower court for a fresh look, the Court will turn the appeal away without a full hearing.
