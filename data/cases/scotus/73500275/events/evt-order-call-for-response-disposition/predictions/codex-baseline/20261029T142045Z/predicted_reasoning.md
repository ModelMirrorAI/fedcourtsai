# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition without a separate writing. The
probability of any grant-family disposition is 24%; denial remains substantially
more likely than either plenary review or a grant, vacatur, and remand (GVR).

The July 22, 2026 snapshot records one distribution, for the September 28
conference, followed by the Court's request for a response after respondent
waived one. It records no completed conference consideration or actual relist.
From that snapshot's vantage, expect at least one further distribution after
the response arrives or its deadline passes: P(distribution increment) = 99%.
One or two additional distributions are the central procedural forecast if the
petition is not held for related litigation; a further distribution alone will
not establish that the Court actually reconsidered the petition at successive
conferences. No prediction is made about the unfiled opposition's contents.

A separate invitation for the Solicitor General's views in this docket is
unlikely: P(new CVSG) = 10%. The petition identifies Robinhood Markets, Inc. v.
Sodha, No. 25-944, as already carrying a CVSG and overlapping the second and
third questions. That creates a plausible hold-and-remand route without a
second invitation here. Conditional on any grant, the Court is more likely to
dispose of this case in the cert order itself than to set it for argument:
P(summary route | grant) = 65%, principally a GVR following resolution of the
overlapping issues in Robinhood, not an immediate summary reversal.

If the Court instead selects ON24 for plenary review, the leading question is
whether a registration statement's hypothetical risk warnings become misleading
when an issuer knows of precursor customer losses or nonrenewal intentions but
the warned-of material business harm has not yet occurred. Expect a narrowed
version of the first question rather than plenary review of all three broad
questions. If granted on that issue, the likely doctrinal focus would be the
relationship between actual known facts, materiality, and the misleading
impression created by the particular disclosures, not an absolute duty to
disclose every customer loss.

From the July record, ordinary denial would fit renewed consideration during
autumn 2026. A hold for the related petition could defer the terminal order into
2027; this is a forecast horizon, not a claim about what has happened since the
snapshot. A dissent from or statement respecting denial is unlikely:
P(any such writing | denial) = 3.5%. If one occurs, Thomas is the leading
individual possibility, followed by Alito and Gorsuch; no particular writer is
expected. The normal terminal order will contain no merits opinion.
