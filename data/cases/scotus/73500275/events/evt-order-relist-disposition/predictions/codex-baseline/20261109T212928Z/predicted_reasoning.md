# Forecast of the Court's disposition

The Court will most likely retain this petition while addressing Robinhood Markets v. Sodha and then grant, vacate and remand ON24 for reconsideration in light of that decision. The probability of any grant, including that summary remand, is 56%. Denial is a substantial competing outcome; a hold alone would not count as a grant.

The docket already contains **three distributions** and the first clear post-conference relist. From that state, I assign 52% to at least one further distribution entry. Expect zero or one more distribution before the petition is either held or disposed of, rather than a long visible series of relists. If held, it can remain pending through the lead case without repeated conference entries.

The Court will not request a separate Solicitor General brief in ON24. A new invitation has a 3.5% probability; government participation in Robinhood makes another invitation here unnecessary in the central forecast.

If the Court takes ON24 for plenary review rather than holding it, the likeliest focus is **QP2**, narrowed to whether Section 11 misleading-omissions liability requires an omission to make an identified affirmative statement misleading, not merely to concern material information. QP1's characterization of the risk as unmaterialized presents a factual vehicle obstacle, and QP3's Item 303 issue is less likely to receive independent review. No question need be expressly granted for briefing if the case instead receives a GVR.

Conditional on any grant, I assign **80%** to disposition through the cert order rather than plenary briefing and argument. The expected summary route is a later GVR tied to Robinhood, not a per curiam merits reversal. A GVR would require the lower court to reconsider; it would not decide that ON24's disclosures were lawful or that the investor claims must be dismissed.

If the Court denies review, it will most likely do so without any separate writing. The probability of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial, conditional on denial, is **6%**. Thomas, Alito or Gorsuch are the likelier potential denial commentators, but no individual writing is expected. If a lead decision produces a limited remand, the remaining factual and Item 303 issues will be left for the lower court rather than resolved in the cert order.
