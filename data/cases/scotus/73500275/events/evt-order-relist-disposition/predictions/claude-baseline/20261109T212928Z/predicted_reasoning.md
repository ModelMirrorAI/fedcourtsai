I expect the Court to grant Robinhood and hold ON24 for it. The held petition would then be distributed again after the Robinhood decision (about spring or summer 2027). An outright denial at the 11/13 conference is the minority path.

The main grant path is a **GVR** in light of a pro-issuer Robinhood decision. A plenary grant on QP1 (hypothetical risk factors, the *Facebook* question) is unlikely on an unpublished interlocutory memo.
- **If granted on plenary review**, the Court would take QP1 or QP2 as written. QP3 (Item 303) would follow Robinhood's second question.
- **Dissent from denial:** none expected.
