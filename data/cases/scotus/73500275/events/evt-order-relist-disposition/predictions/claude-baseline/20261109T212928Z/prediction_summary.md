We think the Supreme Court will most likely turn this case away, with about a one-in-three chance it acts on it instead. The key is a closely related pending case, Robinhood v. Sodha, on the same securities-disclosure law, which the government has urged the Court to hear. If the Court takes Robinhood and rules for the company there, it will likely send this case back to the lower court to reconsider in light of that ruling (a "GVR"). Otherwise, this petition will probably be denied.
