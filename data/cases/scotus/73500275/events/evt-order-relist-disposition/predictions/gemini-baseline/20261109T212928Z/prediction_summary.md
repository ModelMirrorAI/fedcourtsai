The Supreme Court is likely to deny review in *ON24, Inc. v. Leadersel Innotech ESG*, with a roughly three-in-ten chance (31%) of granting the petition or sending the case back to the lower court for reconsideration. Although the Court postponed its decision for another private conference alongside a related securities case, *Robinhood Markets v. Sodha*, the *ON24* appeal comes from a short, non-binding lower-court ruling with factual disputes that make it a poor standalone candidate for full Supreme Court review. If the Court acts favorably on ON24's petition at all, it will most likely hold the case until *Robinhood* is decided and then return it to the appeals court to apply that new ruling.
