Guam's Attorney General wants the Supreme Court to take up a 1990 federal court order that
blocked Guam's abortion ban under Roe v. Wade, which the Court has since overruled. The appeals
court threw out his challenge as moot (no longer a live dispute) because Guam's own top court
had ruled the ban was already repealed. We think the Court will most likely refuse the case:
about a one-in-eight chance it acts. If it does act, it would more likely send the case back
with brief instructions than hear full argument.
