The Supreme Court is very likely to decline this case; I estimate about a one-in-eight chance that it intervenes. Guam's attorney general wants an old abortion-related court order removed after the Supreme Court overturned Roe v. Wade. But Guam's highest court separately ruled that the underlying abortion law had been repealed. Removing the federal order would therefore not, by itself, let the attorney general enforce that law. The request for a response shows interest, but does not overcome this practical obstacle.
