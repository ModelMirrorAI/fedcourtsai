No CVSG is expected, because the federal government does not have a direct interest in the enforcement of Guam's territorial laws. If granted, the Court is highly likely to resolve the case via a summary disposition (GVR) instructing the lower court to vacate the injunction under *Munsingwear*. Plenary review is unlikely because the underlying statute was found to be impliedly repealed by the territory's supreme court, making the case moot, and the remaining issue is purely the procedural vehicle for vacating the *Roe*-based injunction. Should the Court deny the petition, expect a dissent from denial or statement respecting denial from conservative justices (e.g., Justice Alito or Thomas) given the Ninth Circuit's refusal to vacate an injunction resting entirely on overruled constitutional precedent.
