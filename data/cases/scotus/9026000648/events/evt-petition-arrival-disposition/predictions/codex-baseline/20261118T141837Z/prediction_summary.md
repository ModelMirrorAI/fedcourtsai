I predict that the Supreme Court will agree to hear this challenge to New York's restrictions on gas appliances, with about a seven-in-ten chance of review. Two federal appeals courts have reached opposite answers about whether federal appliance law blocks such restrictions. That direct disagreement, the nationwide effects on construction and energy use, and the federal government's involvement make review substantially more likely than for an ordinary petition. The Court could still wait for other appeals or identify a procedural obstacle.
