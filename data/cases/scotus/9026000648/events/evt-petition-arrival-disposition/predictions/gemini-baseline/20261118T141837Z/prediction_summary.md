We predict the Supreme Court will deny certiorari—meaning it will decline to take up the appeal—though the petition has nearly even odds of being heard, at 45 percent. Trade groups and labor unions ask the Court to decide whether federal appliance-efficiency law overrides New York State and New York City bans on gas stoves and furnaces in new buildings, an issue on which federal appeals courts in New York and California openly disagree. Despite that conflict, the Justices will slightly more likely wait while three other federal appeals courts finish deciding similar gas-ban cases.
