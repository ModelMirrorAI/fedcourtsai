Plumbing, building and gas-industry groups ask the Supreme Court to decide whether a federal appliance-efficiency law blocks New York City and New York State from banning gas appliances in new buildings. We predict the Court will more likely than not decline to hear the case, but it is close: about a two-in-five chance of review, far above the usual few percent. Two federal appeals courts openly disagree and the federal government sides with the challengers, yet the Court may wait for other pending appeals to be decided first.
