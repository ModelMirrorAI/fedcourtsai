The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Genesis Financial Solutions' petition for review, with less than a one-in-ten chance (8%) of granting the case. Although the Court asked the consumer plaintiff to file a response to the company's argument that the Federal Arbitration Act overrides Maryland's rule requiring separate mutual promises for arbitration clauses, the lower appellate court's ruling is a brief, unpublished two-paragraph decision entangled in contract-specific disputes over unilateral change-of-terms language.
