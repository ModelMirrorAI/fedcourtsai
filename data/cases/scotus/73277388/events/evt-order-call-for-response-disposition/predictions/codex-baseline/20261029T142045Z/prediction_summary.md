The Supreme Court is more likely to decline this case than take it up. I put
the chance of review or another grant-related action at about one in four
(24%). Genesis argues that Maryland unfairly makes arbitration clauses meet
a stricter contract test, and the Court has asked the other side to respond.
That signals interest, not a commitment to hear the case. The lower decision
is short and unpublished, and a related petition may offer a better vehicle
for addressing the issue.
