# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition after obtaining the requested
response. The probability of any grant, including a summary reversal or a grant,
vacatur and remand, is 24%.

The snapshot records **one distribution**, on October 29, 2025, for the November
14 conference, followed by the November 6 response request, with a December 8
response deadline. It records no completed relist and no CVSG. The Court will
almost certainly distribute the petition again after the response arrives or
the response period expires: P(at least one additional distribution) = 99%.
The modal path is one further distribution followed by denial, not a sustained
series of substantive relists. A second further distribution is plausible if
the Court considers the relationship to the earlier-filed Johnson petition.

The Court will probably not invite the Solicitor General's views; P(a new CVSG)
= 2%. This private contract dispute presents a federal preemption question but
does not obviously require the federal government's institutional position.
Absent extensions or a hold, expect action in early 2026 after the response and
redistribution, rather than a decision at the originally scheduled November
conference. A hold for related litigation would lengthen that timeline.

If review is granted, the Court would most likely address whether FAA Section
2 permits an arbitration clause within an otherwise supported contract to face
a separate-consideration requirement that other contract terms do not face.
That is the substance of the provisioned question presented, rather than the
underlying Maryland lending-license claims. The potential federal ground would
be equal treatment of arbitration clauses, not a general decision insulating
unilateral contract changes from ordinary contract defenses.

Conditional on a grant, I assign 40% to disposition in the cert order itself
and 60% to plenary review. The summary possibilities are reversal under existing
equal-treatment precedents or a remand following a ruling in related litigation;
the record supplies no already-issued intervening decision requiring a GVR.

If review is denied, expect an ordinary unsigned denial without a statement or
noted dissent: P(a dissent or statement respecting denial | denial) = 6%.
Thomas or Alito would be plausible writers in the less likely separate-writing
branch, but no individual writing is the modal forecast. The JSON's small
per-Justice writing probabilities are unconditional and also allow writings on
a grant-related order.
