We expect the Supreme Court to turn down this case, but the chance it agrees to hear it is about one in five, well above the usual few percent. The Court asked the other side to respond, which signals some interest. The case asks whether federal arbitration law overrides a Maryland rule requiring separate "consideration" (something of value exchanged) for an arbitration clause alone. Against review: the appeals court's decision was a brief, unpublished ruling, and the disagreement among courts may be thinner than claimed.
