The Court is highly likely to take up this case to resolve the entrenched 5-1 circuit split over the Federal Arbitration Act (FAA) and state-imposed heightened consideration requirements for arbitration clauses. 

A CVSG is unlikely because the federal government is not a party, and the issue primarily concerns private arbitration agreements governed by established federal statute, though the SG's views could theoretically be sought given the FAA's importance. If granted, the Court will take the question presented as written to resolve the circuit split. A summary GVR is unlikely because there is no recent intervening Supreme Court precedent that would simply resolve the split without plenary review. Should the Court deny, a dissent from denial is possible given the depth of the split and the clear division of authority, though not guaranteed.
