# Forecast

The Court will most likely deny the petition. I assign a 22% probability to any grant, including a GVR or summary reversal, rather than just plenary review.

The provisioned docket shows one distribution, on October 29, 2025, for the November 14, 2025 conference. It then shows a requested response and extensions through October 30, 2026, but no second distribution and no CVSG. I expect at least one additional distribution after the response process, with probability 88%; one additional distribution is the modal forecast. That would satisfy the declared distribution-increment claim even if it is simply the first consideration with complete briefing rather than a substantive relist. More than one additional conference is possible, particularly if the Court waits for related litigation.

I expect no CVSG, assigning 2.5% to a new invitation. The dispute is a private contract-enforcement controversy with a developed federal preemption question; I do not expect the Court to need an executive-branch recommendation to decide whether to hear it.

If review is granted, the Court will focus on the single question presented: whether FAA section 2 preempts an arbitration-specific requirement of independent consideration when the surrounding contract is supported by consideration. I expect the Court to address that issue rather than announce a general rule validating every unilateral change-of-terms clause. Conditional on merits review, the likely ground favors equal treatment of arbitration clauses while leaving genuinely generally applicable contract-formation defenses available. This is a conditional forecast, not a prediction that a denial will contain substantive reasoning.

Conditional on any grant, I assign 60% to disposition in the cert order itself, rather than plenary briefing and argument. The short, unpublished decision follows the Fourth Circuit's Johnson decision, and the petition identifies a separate petition from that litigation. A hold followed by a companion-case GVR is a plausible route if that other matter produces a controlling ruling; a direct summary reversal is another, less likely route. I do not assert that the companion petition has been granted or decided. If the Court instead takes this case for plenary review, I expect the FAA question substantially as presented.

Conditional on denial, I assign 5% to a noted dissent or statement respecting denial and predict an ordinary order without separate writing. I do not forecast individual cert votes.

Assuming the current response deadline holds, I expect disposition in late 2026 or early 2027, after briefing and another distribution. A companion-case hold or further extension would push the decision later. The primary forecast remains denial without a substantive opinion.
