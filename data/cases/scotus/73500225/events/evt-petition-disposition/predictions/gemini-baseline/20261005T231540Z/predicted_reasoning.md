Distributed twice so far (relisted once). Expect at least one further relist as the Court considers whether to grant this major Second Amendment challenge. The petition raises the important and unresolved question of what constitutes a "sensitive place" under *Bruen*, an issue that has deeply divided the lower courts since 2022. Because this case centers on state law rather than federal property or a federal statute, a CVSG is unlikely—no unique federal interest strictly requires the Solicitor General's input at this stage. 

If the Court grants review, it will likely take the case for plenary consideration to provide nationwide guidance on the "sensitive places" doctrine, making a summary disposition route improbable unless a companion case is decided first. Should the Court deny the petition, a dissent from denial is highly probable, likely authored by Justice Thomas or Justice Alito, given their focus on ensuring lower courts faithfully apply the *Bruen* historical-tradition test.
