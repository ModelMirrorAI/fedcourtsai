The Supreme Court is slightly more likely to turn down this challenge to Maryland's restrictions on carrying guns in public places. I estimate a 44% chance it accepts the case or sends it back for reconsideration. The petition raises important disagreements, and the Court has returned it for another conference. But several earlier delays were scheduling changes, not repeated substantive consideration. A recent gun-rights decision concerns a different restriction, and the Court may prefer a narrower case before addressing Maryland's broad collection of rules.
