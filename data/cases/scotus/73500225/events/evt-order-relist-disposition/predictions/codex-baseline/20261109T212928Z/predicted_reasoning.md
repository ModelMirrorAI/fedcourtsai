# Forecast of the Court's action

## Disposition and procedure

The Court will most likely **deny the petition**, leaving the Fourth Circuit's judgment in place without a merits opinion. The probability of any grant, including a grant-vacate-remand (GVR), is **44%**. A denial would not itself endorse the Fourth Circuit's reasoning.

The record already contains **five distributions**, no CVSG, and one joint amicus brief. Three earlier distributions were followed by pre-conference reschedules. The November 9 distribution for November 13 is the first visible redistribution after an actual conference.

I forecast **at least one further distribution with 60% probability**. My central procedural path is one additional distribution, with one or two more conferences before disposition, rather than a prolonged series extending through another Term. The central timing window is late November through December 2026. A longer wait for related lower-court developments is a secondary possibility, not an assumed fact about a pending companion.

I forecast **no call for the Solicitor General's views**; the chance of a new CVSG is **2%**. If one unexpectedly issues, the likely timing is within the next few conferences, with the eventual petition disposition substantially delayed.

## If review is granted

The likely question would be a narrowed version of the petition's single question: how the Second Amendment's sensitive-place exception applies to specified ordinary public locations, particularly public transit, medical facilities, or the demonstration restriction. I do not expect the Court to take up every enumerated place as an undifferentiated package or to require comprehensive armed government security in every constitutionally sensitive location.

**Conditional on a grant, 38%** goes to a disposition in the cert order and **62%** to setting the case for briefing and argument. The leading cert-order route is a GVR directing reconsideration in light of Wolford's already-issued June 25, 2026 opinion, not a hold awaiting that decision. Summary reversal is a much smaller possibility: resolving all the challenged locations without full briefing would go beyond Wolford's private-property consent holding.

If granted for plenary review, the likely ground would limit overbroad historical analogies and reject a blanket rule that government ownership alone ends the constitutional inquiry, while retaining genuinely analogous sensitive-place restrictions. That is a conditional merits forecast only; the central prediction remains denial.

## Writings on the order

**Conditional on denial, the probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial is 32%.** The most likely denial is nevertheless silent. The structured `order-writers` forecast gives unconditional probabilities for each Justice, with Thomas the leading candidate and Alito and Gorsuch next. It predicts authorship rather than joinders; it does not claim that any Justice's vote will be disclosed. No majority author or merits vote lineup is forecast for a cert denial.
