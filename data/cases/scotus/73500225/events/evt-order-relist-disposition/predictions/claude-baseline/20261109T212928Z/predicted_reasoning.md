# Forecast — Novotny v. Moore, No. 25-1324

- **State:** five distribution entries, three of them pre-conference reschedules. The petition was considered once (11/6) and relisted for 11/13. There is no CVSG.
- **Relists:** I expect at least one further relist after 11/13, probably several. The Court tends to hold a Second Amendment petition while a dissent from denial is drafted, or while it decides whether to grant.
- **CVSG:** none. The respondent is a State and the issue does not involve a federal interest.
- **Disposition:** a denial is the likelier outcome (about 64%). The Court is likely to wait for Koons (CA3 en banc) and for more lower courts to apply Wolford. A plenary grant is about 22% and a GVR in light of Wolford v. Lopez about 14%.
- **If granted:** the Court would likely take the QP but narrow it to particular location categories, such as mass transit, parks, or places serving alcohol / health facilities. It would ask whether "crowded place" and "vulnerable population" analogues can justify bans in places the public goes about its daily life.
- **Summary route:** if the Court acts on the grant side, a GVR is a real possibility (about 40% of grant-side outcomes), given that the CA4 decision predates Wolford.
- **On denial:** I expect a written dissent from denial (about 65%), most likely by Justice Thomas, possibly joined or accompanied by Justices Alito and Gorsuch. A statement from Justice Kavanaugh noting the issue for a future case is possible.
