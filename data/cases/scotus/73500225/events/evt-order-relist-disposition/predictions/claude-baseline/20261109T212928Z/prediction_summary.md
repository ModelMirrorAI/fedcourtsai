Gun owners ask the Supreme Court to strike down Maryland's bans on carrying guns in many public places, such as transit, parks, hospitals and stadiums. The Court discussed the case once and scheduled it for another conference, which signals real interest. We still think it more likely than not that the Court declines to hear it (roughly a one-in-three chance it takes the case or sends it back for another look under its recent Hawaii gun ruling). It will likely wait for other courts first, and a written dissent from some Justices is likely.
