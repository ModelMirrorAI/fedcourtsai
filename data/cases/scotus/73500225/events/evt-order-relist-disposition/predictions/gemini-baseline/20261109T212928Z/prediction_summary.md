The Supreme Court will likely deny this petition for review, with about a one-in-five chance (22%) of agreeing to hear the case. The petitioners ask the Court to strike down Maryland laws banning concealed-carry permit holders from bringing firearms into nine types of public locations, including parks, mass transit, bars and restaurants serving alcohol, museums, health care facilities, school grounds, and government buildings. Although the Court held the petition across several preliminary conferences and relisted it once after its first conference—often a sign that a Justice is writing a dissent from the refusal to hear the case—every federal appeals court to decide these location bans has upheld them, and the Court recently turned away similar challenges from Hawaii and Illinois.
