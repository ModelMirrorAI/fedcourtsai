# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition without a separate writing. P(any grant), including a GVR or summary reversal, is **0.13**. The likely obstacle to review is that the asserted categorical circuit conflict can be understood instead as different applications of a common, fact-sensitive antitrust-standing inquiry. A denial would not endorse the Second Circuit's reasoning or decide the antitrust merits.

The snapshot records **two distributions**, for June 25 and September 28, 2026, and **no CVSG**. The June 23 response request intervened before the first scheduled conference; the September distribution follows the requested opposition and reply. Thus two distributions do not establish that the fully briefed petition has already received two substantive conference reviews.

- **Further distribution:** P(at least one additional distribution beyond the recorded two) = **0.30**. The modal path is no further distribution, followed by denial in the early-October orders following the September 28 conference. If the Court keeps the petition, one additional conference is more likely than a prolonged series of relists.
- **Solicitor General:** P(a new CVSG) = **0.12**. The modal path is no invitation. The federal antitrust-enforcement interest and the United States' participation below make an invitation a meaningful alternative; if issued, expect it during the autumn rather than an immediate final disposition.
- **Question if granted:** Expect review focused on whether a buyer alleging a targeted price-fixing conspiracy, an established course of competitive dealing, and lost downstream profits can qualify as an efficient antitrust enforcer despite refusing the allegedly inflated price. That is a narrower, more vehicle-specific version of the petition's question, not a general ruling that all nonpurchasers can or cannot sue.
- **Route conditional on grant:** P(summary disposition in the cert order | grant) = **0.06**. Plenary briefing and argument are substantially more likely if review is granted: resolving the claimed conflict would require engagement with proximate causation, proof of injury, and the pleaded business relationship. No identified intervening decision supplies a natural GVR route.
- **Writing conditional on denial:** P(any noted dissent or statement respecting denial | denial) = **0.05**. Expect no writing and do not forecast an individual Justice's vote. The appellate dissent is a reason the petition might attract attention, not evidence that any particular Justice will dissent.

If granted, the likely doctrinal focus is the boundary between merely speculative lost purchases and a sufficiently direct, measurable loss supported by established dealings. No merits opinion is forecast on the modal denial path.
