Distributed for the September 28, 2026 Long Conference (its first conference, following a requested response). Expect one further relist before a decision on the petition. The QP presents a clean circuit split on whether a non-purchaser has antitrust standing to recover downstream losses from an alleged price-fixing conspiracy. The Court actively requested a response after the respondent initially waived, signaling interest, and the decision below includes a dissent by Judge Sullivan, making this an attractive vehicle to resolve the split. 

No CVSG is expected, as the federal government is not a party and its views, while sometimes sought in antitrust cases, are not strictly necessary to resolve this private standing circuit split. 

If granted, the Court would likely take the single QP as written to resolve the split on antitrust standing. A summary GVR is unlikely, as no intervening decision bears on the issue. Should the Court deny review, expect no separate writing.
