## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether a prisoner should receive permission to appeal when the legal issue he wants to raise has already split the appeals courts, even though the court handling his case has its own binding precedent against him. Some appeals courts deny permission in that situation, reasoning that judges in that circuit must follow their own circuit's rule. Other appeals courts grant permission, reasoning that a genuine disagreement among courts shows the issue is open to debate.
