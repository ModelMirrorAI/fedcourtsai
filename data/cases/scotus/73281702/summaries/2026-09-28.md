## What happened

John David Trice was convicted in Texas state court of continuous sexual abuse of a child and related offenses, receiving a 40-year sentence. Texas law allows a jury to convict of continuous sexual abuse without agreeing unanimously on which specific underlying acts occurred, so long as jurors agree two or more acts occurred over a 30-day period.
