The Supreme Court is likely to set aside the appeals court's ruling for PepsiCo and send the case back for another look. I put the chance of changing that judgment at 76%, with a unanimous vote expected. The central issue is whether judges or factfinders should assess how strongly consumers associate a trademark with a product. Consumer understanding points toward factfinding. But that would not mean Rise Brewing wins: PepsiCo could still prevail because the products look different or the evidence of confusion is insufficient.
