The Supreme Court is very likely—nearly nine in ten (88% probability)—to disturb the lower court's ruling by vacating it, meaning wiping out the Second Circuit's decision and sending the dispute between Rise Brewing and PepsiCo back for a fresh look, most likely by a unanimous 9–0 vote. Twelve other federal appeals courts and the U.S. Solicitor General agree that how strong a brand name seems to everyday shoppers is a factual question ordinarily left to a jury rather than a pure legal question for judges, though PepsiCo can still argue on remand that no reasonable jury would find consumers confused.
