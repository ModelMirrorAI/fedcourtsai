The Supreme Court will probably side with the small coffee company Rise Brewing against PepsiCo, about four chances in five. The dispute is over who decides how strong a trademark naturally is: a judge, as the New York-based appeals court said, or a jury weighing evidence about how shoppers see the name. Nearly every other appeals court and the federal government treat it as a factual question. I expect the Court to vacate the ruling, meaning cancel it and send the case back for another look, most likely by a unanimous 9–0 vote.
