The Supreme Court will probably decline to hear this case, with roughly a three-in-ten chance that it agrees to. The government asked the Court to let it take President Trump's place as defendant in E. Jean Carroll's defamation suit, which would erase an $83.3 million verdict. Government requests usually succeed, but this one turns on a one-off sequence of events, no lower courts disagree on the law, and the Court recently turned away the President's related appeal without comment. Expect the decision to be postponed at least once.
