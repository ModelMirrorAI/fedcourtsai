The Supreme Court is somewhat more likely than not to hear the government's appeal—about a three-in-five chance. The case asks whether the government can replace President Trump as the defendant after a trial over statements he made while President. Review would address federal officials' legal protections and could avoid broader constitutional questions. But the government's earlier withdrawal of protection and the lack of a clear disagreement among appeals courts create substantial obstacles. Expect further conference consideration before a decision on review.
