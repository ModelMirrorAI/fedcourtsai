# Forecast

The petition currently has zero distributions because this is the arrival moment. It will be distributed for an initial conference after the response period, and I expect one relist, with a second relist possible while the Justices coordinate this petition with the related presidential-immunity litigation. I expect a petition disposition during the first part of October Term 2026.

The Court will likely grant plenary review. If it grants, it will probably narrow or reformulate the question to ask whether, after an action begun in state court was timely certified and removed under 28 U.S.C. 2679(d)(2), the Attorney General may recertify and obtain substitution after a federal trial. The Court is unlikely to use this case to decide in the first instance whether the underlying statements were within the scope of presidential office.

No CVSG is expected: the United States is the petitioner and has already presented the federal government's view through the Solicitor General. A cert-order disposition is also unlikely. There is no intervening authority that naturally supports a GVR, and the interaction among the statutory timing rule, waiver, equitable finality, and judicial review of scope of employment is better suited to full briefing and argument. If the government prevails, the likely judgment would vacate and remand for the lower courts to apply the Westfall Act without the categorical post-trial timing bar, not immediately terminate the underlying suit.

If certiorari is denied, a noted dissent or statement respecting denial is materially possible given the presidential stakes and the three-judge en banc dissent below; at least one of Justices Thomas, Alito, or Gorsuch would be the likeliest source.
