The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Defense Distributed v. Davenport*, with only about a one-in-eleven chance (9%) of granting the petition. Although the Court asked New Jersey to file a response to the petition—which challenges both a refusal by the federal court in New Jersey to send the case back to Texas and a New Jersey law restricting digital files for 3D-printed firearms—the case has serious procedural flaws that make it a poor candidate for a full Supreme Court ruling. Most importantly, the challengers failed to ask for a pause before the case was moved to New Jersey and later refused two opportunities from the trial judge to add basic technical facts to their complaint explaining how their computer files work.
