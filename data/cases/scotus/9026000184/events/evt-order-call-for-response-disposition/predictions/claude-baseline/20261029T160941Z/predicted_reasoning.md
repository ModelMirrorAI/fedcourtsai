# Forecast: Defense Distributed v. Davenport (No. 26-184), after the call for a response

**State the docket shows at this moment.** One distribution (for the 10/9/2026
conference, entered 9/23 after New Jersey waived a response) and, the next day,
a Response Requested (due 10/26/2026). No relist, no CVSG, no amicus briefs.

**Disposition.** The Court will most likely **deny** the petition after New
Jersey's brief in opposition is in — about 13% chance of a grant.

**Further distribution.** Near-certain: the petition is redistributed once the
response is filed (or its time runs). Expect it to reach a December/January
conference. A grant, if it comes, will probably follow at least one relist; a
denial may come at the first post-response conference or after one relist.

**CVSG.** Not expected. The respondent is a State, no federal program is at
stake, and the questions are a First Amendment coverage rule and a
federal-court venue/comity rule the Court needs no Executive view on.

**Which question, if granted.** QP 2 — whether communicative computer code is
covered speech without additional indicia of expressiveness (the acknowledged
split with the Sixth Circuit's *Junger v. Daley*), probably reformulated more
narrowly around CAD files / digital design information. QP 1 (transferee
court's retention after the transferor circuit vacated the transfer) is the
likelier question to be left aside, though the Court might add it given the
stark Fifth/Third Circuit standoff in the same action.

**Summary route.** Unlikely (about 5% conditional on a grant): no intervening
decision supports a GVR, and a per curiam reversal on a novel First Amendment
coverage question would be out of character. Plenary review if granted.

**Separate writing on a denial.** Modestly possible (~15% conditional on
denial). If one comes, the likeliest author is Justice Thomas, then Justice
Alito or Justice Gorsuch — a statement noting the intercircuit venue anomaly or
the open question of code as speech.
