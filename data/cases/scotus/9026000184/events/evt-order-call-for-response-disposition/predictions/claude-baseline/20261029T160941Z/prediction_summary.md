We expect the Supreme Court to turn down this case, though not with certainty — roughly a one-in-eight chance it agrees to hear it. Defense Distributed, a Texas group that shares files for making 3D-printed guns, says New Jersey's law against distributing those files violates free speech, and that the case should have been sent back to Texas. The Court asked New Jersey to respond, a sign of some interest, but the case was dismissed early, before the facts were developed, which makes it a less attractive one to take.
