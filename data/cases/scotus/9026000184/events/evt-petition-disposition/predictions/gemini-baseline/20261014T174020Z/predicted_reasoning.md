The petition presents a direct circuit split on venue between the Third and Fifth Circuits in this very case, as well as an acknowledged circuit split on whether the First Amendment covers computer code. Given the high-profile nature of the First Amendment issue (3D printed firearms code) and the bizarre procedural posture, this is a strong candidate for review.

A CVSG is unlikely because no federal party's interest is directly implicated; this is a dispute between a private party and a state Attorney General. If granted, the Court would likely take both questions, as the venue issue is a threshold matter and the First Amendment issue is of national importance. A summary disposition is possible on the venue question to enforce the Fifth Circuit's vacatur of the transfer, but plenary review on the First Amendment question is the likelier route. Should the Court deny the petition, expect a dissent from denial by Justice Thomas or Justice Alito given their past interest in Second and First Amendment protections in this context.
