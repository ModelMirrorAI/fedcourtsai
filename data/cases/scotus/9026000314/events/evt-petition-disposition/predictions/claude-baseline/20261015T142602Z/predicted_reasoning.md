# Forecast — Ekladyous v. NHTSA (No. 26-314)

**Disposition: denied**, at the Conference of November 6, 2026, the first and
only conference the docket records (distributed once, October 14, 2026, after
the Solicitor General waived response on October 8).

- **Relists.** The docket shows one distribution and no relist. I expect no
  further distribution: the petition goes to the November 6 conference and is
  denied there without the Court calling for a response. The only realistic
  path to a second distribution is a call for response, which I do not expect
  for a pro se petition challenging an unpublished timeliness dismissal.
- **CVSG.** None, and none expected — the federal government is the
  respondent, so the Court would call for a response, not the SG's views.
- **Which question, if granted.** QP 1 (whether a § 706(1) "unlawfully
  withheld" claim can be recharacterized as an untimely § 706(2) challenge to
  a completed rule under 49 U.S.C. § 30161(a)). QP 2, whether IIJA
  § 24212(a)(1) compelled adoption of SAE J3069, would be left aside as a
  merits question the court of appeals never reached.
- **Summary disposition.** Not expected. If the Court did grant, plenary
  review of QP 1 is slightly likelier than a summary route; a GVR would follow
  only a call for response in which the government confessed error about the
  court of appeals' alternative holding (that no other reviewable action was
  identified). About a one-in-three chance a grant would be summary.
- **Separate writing.** None expected on denial.
