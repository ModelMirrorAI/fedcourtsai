**Inputs read.** Snapshot `record/snapshots/2026-10-15.json`; `context.json`
(forward mode, band `baseline` under sal-v4, distribution_count 1, no CVSG,
Term 2026); the petition, its QP section, and the appendix (D.C. Cir. per
curiam order of May 14, 2026 and en banc denial of July 23, 2026). No BIO
exists — the SG filed a waiver of the right to respond.

**Anchor.** The committed statpack's "Segment base rate by salience band
(sal-v4)" table: the `baseline` band's bracketed `reached` rate over Terms
2017–2025 (strictly before OT2026) runs 3.9%–5.9%, pooling to roughly 5%. That
figure is the risk-set rate for a paid petition that may still climb bands; it
is the evaluator's yardstick. The relist-0 cut (1.2% granted) and the
`baseline` terminal figure (~0.8–1.3%) sit well below it.

**Adjustments, all downward and large.**
- **Pro se petitioner** (a paid filing, but self-represented). Pro se paid
  petitions are granted at a small fraction of the counselled rate.
- **Response waived by the SG and no call for response before the first
  conference.** The Court essentially never grants a petition without first
  calling for a response; a call would also move the petition off the
  November 6 conference. Absent that, a grant is near-impossible.
- **Vehicle.** The decision below is a short unpublished Rule 36 order on
  timeliness, with an alternative ground (petitioner did not identify any
  agency action or inaction other than the 2022 final rule, nor a statute
  giving direct review). No circuit split is alleged; the petition argues
  error under *Norton v. SUWA*.
- **Stakes.** The ADB headlamp standard and the IIJA § 24212 mandate are a
  genuine regulatory question, which is the only thing keeping this above the
  floor.

Net: ~0.4%. My main uncertainty is not the direction but the floor itself —
whether the Court's few calls for response in waived pro se cases matter here.

**Increments.** Relist-increment 0.03 ≈ probability of a call for response
(which leads to redistribution) plus a reschedule. CVSG 0.002: the government
is already the respondent. Summary route | grant 0.35: a grant here would most
plausibly come as a GVR after a confession of error, though a plenary grant on
QP 1 is the slightly likelier branch. Dissent from denial 0.005.

**Retrieval.** None beyond the provisioned inputs and the committed statpack;
the pre-decision record was sufficient for a petition this clear.
