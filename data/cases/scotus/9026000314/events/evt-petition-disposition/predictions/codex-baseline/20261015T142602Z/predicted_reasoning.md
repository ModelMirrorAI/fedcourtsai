# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny the petition, most likely in an orders list shortly after the scheduled November 6, 2026 conference, during November. It will leave the unpublished jurisdictional dismissal standing without deciding whether NHTSA's lighting rule satisfies the statutory definition. A denial will not constitute an endorsement of that rule's merits.

The snapshot shows **one distribution and no CVSG**. I expect **zero additional distributions** before disposition; the probability of at least one additional distribution is 3.5%. If the Court does retain the petition, one additional conference is more likely than a prolonged series of relists. I do not expect a CVSG, assigning 0.1% to a new invitation. A request for the federal respondents to file a response, were one issued, would not itself be a CVSG.

The threshold direct-review and timeliness issues will prevent this petition from becoming the vehicle for a broad decision on agency inaction. If review is nevertheless granted, the Court will principally take the first question: whether the claimed failure to perform a discrete mandatory duty can be treated as an untimely challenge to completed rulemaking. It will frame that inquiry to address the available basis for direct appellate review. It will not initially decide the technical adequacy of the headlamp standard; the second question will supply context for identifying the alleged duty rather than a freestanding first-instance merits decision.

Conditional on any grant, plenary briefing and argument is the likelier route: **85% plenary versus 15% disposition in the cert order itself**. No intervening authority identified in the record supplies a natural GVR trigger. If the Court grants and accepts the petitioner's procedural premise, the likely relief would be a remand for consideration of the claimed statutory duty, not an order prescribing a particular headlamp standard.

If denied, the petition will attract no publicly noted dissent or statement respecting denial; I assign **0.6%** to the existence of such a writing or notation conditional on denial. I forecast neither an opinion author nor an individual cert vote.
