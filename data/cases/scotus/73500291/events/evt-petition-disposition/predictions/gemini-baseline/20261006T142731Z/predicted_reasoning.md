Distributed twice so far; expect at least one further relist before a decision on the petition. The QP presents a clean circuit split over whether the clearly-established-law standard of *Hope v. Pelzer* applies beyond the Eighth Amendment to First Amendment claims, and the Fifth Circuit's narrow reading is an outlier. No CVSG is expected, because no federal party's interest or federal statutory regime is implicated; this is a straightforward Section 1983 claim against local officials. 

If granted, the Court would likely take the sole question presented as written. A summary disposition (e.g., a GVR or summary reversal) is a real possibility given the direct conflict with *Hope v. Pelzer*, though plenary review is likelier to fully settle the circuit split. Should the Court deny review, expect a dissent from denial (likely from one or more conservative justices sympathetic to Judge Ho's dissent below or First Amendment advocates) emphasizing the importance of protecting core religious speech on public sidewalks from viewpoint discrimination.
