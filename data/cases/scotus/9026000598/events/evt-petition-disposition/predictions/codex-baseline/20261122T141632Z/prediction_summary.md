I expect the Supreme Court to decline Roy Meidinger’s case. I put the chance it takes the case at about three in a thousand. Meidinger asks whether a whistleblower can challenge the IRS’s refusal to pay an award when it collects no money. The appeals court already allowed such a challenge by another whistleblower whose tip led to an audit. It dismissed Meidinger’s claim because the IRS stopped before opening an audit. His petition identifies no other appeals court that would reach a different result.
