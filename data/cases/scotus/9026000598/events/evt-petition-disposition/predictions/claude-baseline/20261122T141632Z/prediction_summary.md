The Supreme Court will almost certainly decline to hear this case, with well under a one in a hundred chance of review. Roy Meidinger, representing himself, asks the Court to let the Tax Court review the IRS's refusal to pay him a whistleblower award. The appeals court held that no court review is available when the IRS never acted against a taxpayer. The government did not even file a response, no lower courts disagree on the issue, and the Court rarely takes such petitions.
