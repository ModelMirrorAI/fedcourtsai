We expect the Supreme Court to turn down Samsung's appeal (to deny
"certiorari", the Court's discretionary review). We put the chance it takes
the case at about 6 in 100. Samsung argues that Georgia courts cannot hear
any lawsuit against it just because it registered to do business there,
when no Georgia statute said registration carried that consequence. The
question is real. But the other side shows Samsung probably never raised it
properly before Georgia's highest court, and the Justices already declined a
similar Georgia case in 2023.
