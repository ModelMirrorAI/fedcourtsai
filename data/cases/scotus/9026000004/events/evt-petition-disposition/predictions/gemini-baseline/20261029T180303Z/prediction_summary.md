The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Samsung Electronics America's petition for review (a 3% chance of a grant, or about one in thirty-three). Samsung asks the Court to overturn a $10.86 million Georgia default judgment by ruling that registering to do business in Georgia cannot subject an out-of-state company to general "all-purpose" jurisdiction (authority to hear any lawsuit against it) when that rule comes from state court decisions rather than explicit statutory text. However, the Supreme Court recently upheld registration-based jurisdiction in 2023, has repeatedly declined similar follow-on challenges—including a challenge to this same Georgia rule—and is unlikely to intervene where a Georgia resident was injured in Georgia and Samsung renewed its registration for decades after Georgia's courts announced the rule.
