I expect the Supreme Court to decline Samsung's request for review, with about an eight-in-a-hundred chance that it takes the case or grants another form of relief. Samsung raises an important question about whether registering to do business lets a state hear almost any lawsuit against a company. But this case has serious procedural complications: Samsung may not have properly raised its constitutional argument earlier, and its admissions may independently support the state court's authority. A denial without explanation is the likeliest result.
