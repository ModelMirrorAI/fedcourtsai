The Supreme Court is very likely to decline Samsung's appeal; I put the chance it takes the case at 14%, about one in seven. Samsung argues that registering to do business in Georgia did not give fair warning that it could be sued there on unrelated claims. The Court's request for an answer shows interest, but not a promise to hear the case. Complicated rules surrounding Samsung's failure to answer the original lawsuit make this a difficult vehicle for resolving the broader issue.
