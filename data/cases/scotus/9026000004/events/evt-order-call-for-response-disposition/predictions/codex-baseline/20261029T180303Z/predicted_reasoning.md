# Forecast of the Court's actions

The Court will most likely deny Samsung's petition without a separate writing. The probability of any grant, including a grant accompanied by a summary disposition, is **14%**. This forecasts the disposition of the certiorari petition, not whether Samsung ultimately defeats the damages judgment.

The snapshot records **one distribution**, on July 22 for the September 28, 2026 conference, followed by the August 14 request for a response. There is no response or CVSG on this record. I expect the petition to be distributed at least once more after the response arrives or its time expires: **99%** for the declared distribution increment. One additional distribution is the modal forecast; another conference hold is possible but is not my central prediction. A redistribution to accommodate the requested response would not itself establish that the Court seriously considered a grant. The increment is declared even though the response-requested moment masks it from assessment.

I expect **no CVSG**, with **3%** probability of a new invitation to the Solicitor General. This private dispute presents a constitutional jurisdiction question but no identified federal party or specialized federal administrative interest requiring the government's views.

If review is granted, the Court will most likely consider the notice question presented: whether registration under a statute silent about general jurisdiction supplies constitutionally adequate consent, especially when the corporation registered before the relevant judicial interpretation. A narrower grant confined to the latter notice issue is more plausible than a wholesale reconsideration of consent-by-registration. Any merits discussion here is conditional on review; a denial would establish no answer to that question.

Conditional on a grant, I assign **12%** probability to disposition in the cert order itself and **88%** to a route opening plenary merits proceedings. The petition asks for an unresolved distinction rather than identifying a new decision that straightforwardly requires a remand. On this record, a grant would ordinarily require argument about notice and the procedural vehicle.

Conditional on denial, I assign **8%** probability to a noted dissent or statement respecting denial. No writing is the central forecast. If one appears, Alito or Barrett is the likeliest author in my order-writer forecasts; those are unconditional individual writing probabilities, not predicted votes or conditional probabilities of dissent.
