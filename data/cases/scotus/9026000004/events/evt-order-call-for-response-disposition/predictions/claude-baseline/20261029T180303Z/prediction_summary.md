We expect the Supreme Court to turn down Samsung's request to hear this case — we put the chance it agrees at about 7 in 100. Samsung argues Georgia courts cannot hear lawsuits against it just because it registered to do business there, since Georgia's law never warned that registering meant agreeing to be sued there. The Court did ask the other side to respond, a mild sign of interest, but it refused a similar Georgia case in 2023, and this one has procedural problems.
