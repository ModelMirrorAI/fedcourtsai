The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Samsung's request to hear this appeal (about an 8% chance of review). Although the Court asked the injured Texas plaintiff to respond to Samsung's argument that simply registering to do business in Georgia should not allow Georgia courts to hear lawsuits unrelated to the state, the case is still in pre-trial discovery and entangled in state-law procedural disputes over whether Samsung waited too long to challenge the trial court's orders.
