# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny the petition, most likely in an orders release following the September 28, 2026 conference, during early October. The denial will leave the judgment below intact without endorsing its Fourth Amendment reasoning.

The snapshot records **two distributions**: April 15 for May 1, and July 1 for September 28. From that two-distribution baseline, I expect **zero further distributions**. The probability of at least one additional distribution is 0.24; if the Court continues considering the petition, one or two additional distributions are more likely than a long sequence. The response request intervened before the first scheduled conference, so the existing second distribution is not strong evidence of two completed substantive conferences.

No CVSG appears in the record. I expect none to issue, assigning a 0.01 probability to a new invitation before disposition. A state criminal suppression case about the scope of established Fourth Amendment doctrine does not particularly call for the Solicitor General's institutional advice.

If the Court grants review, it will most likely focus on whether objectively search-directed police conduct exceeds the implied license to approach a home's door, rather than adopt a categorical rule that any investigative motive converts a conventional knock-and-talk into a search. That is a narrower framing of the petition's sole question presented. The disputed preservation of the purpose-based theory and the absence of a clearly opposed pair of controlling holdings are likely to prevent review in this vehicle.

Conditional on a grant, I assign 0.35 to a disposition in the cert order and 0.65 to plenary review. A summary vacatur or reversal for misapplication of the implied-license rule is possible, but the supplied record identifies no intervening decision requiring a routine GVR. The petition's alternative request for reconsideration under existing precedents does not itself supply that trigger.

Conditional on denial, I assign 0.22 to a noted dissent or statement respecting denial. The modal outcome is no separate writing. If a statement appears, Justice Gorsuch is the most plausible lead voice, potentially joined by Justices Sotomayor or Kagan, given the Bovat statement described in the petition. This is a conditional writing forecast, not a claim about a publicly observable cert vote lineup.
