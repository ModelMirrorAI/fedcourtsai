## What happened

The Court is asked whether a prisoner's claim that officials wrongly withheld earned time credits, where the prisoner is seeking faster transfer to a halfway house or home confinement rather than outright release, can be raised through a habeas corpus petition, or whether it must instead proceed through a different kind of lawsuit.

Maxwell, the prisoner, is asking the Court to reverse the lower court's decision.
