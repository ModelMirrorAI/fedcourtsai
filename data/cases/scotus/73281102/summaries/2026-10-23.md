## What happened

William Maxwell, a federal prisoner, sought transfer to a halfway house or home confinement based on time credits he says he earned under the First Step Act. The Fifth Circuit affirmed on a different ground: it held that habeas is not the right vehicle at all for this kind of claim because success would not entitle Maxwell to immediate release, and instead he should have filed a civil rights lawsuit. ## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether a claim that prison officials miscalculated an inmate's earned time credits, and wrongly denied him an earlier move to a halfway house or home confinement, can be raised through a habeas corpus petition, or whether such a claim must instead be brought as a different kind of lawsuit.
