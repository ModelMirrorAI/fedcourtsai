The Supreme Court is very likely (88% chance) to disturb the Fifth Circuit's decision in *Maxwell v. Dinis*, most likely by vacating the judgment (wiping away the lower court's ruling) by a unanimous 9-0 vote and sending the case back for further proceedings. Both the prisoner and the federal government agree that the Fifth Circuit was wrong to hold that federal inmates cannot use habeas corpus (a legal petition challenging unlawful detention) to seek earlier transfer out of prison into a halfway house or home confinement using earned First Step Act time credits, and the Justices across the ideological spectrum rejected the Court-appointed amicus lawyer's defense of that rule at oral argument while leaving procedural defenses like administrative exhaustion for the lower court to address on remand.
