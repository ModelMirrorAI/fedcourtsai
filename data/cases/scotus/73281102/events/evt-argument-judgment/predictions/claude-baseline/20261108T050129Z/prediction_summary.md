We think the Supreme Court will very likely (about 85%) throw out the Fifth Circuit's ruling and send the case back. That court said a federal prisoner cannot use habeas corpus (a court petition challenging unlawful imprisonment) to demand an earlier move to a halfway house or home confinement under the First Step Act. The government itself now agrees that ruling was wrong, and most Justices seemed to agree at argument. We expect a 7–2 vote, with Justices Thomas and Alito likely dissenting or preferring to dismiss the case.
