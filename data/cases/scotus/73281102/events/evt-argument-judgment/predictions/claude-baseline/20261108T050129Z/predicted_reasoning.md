# Predicted reasoning — Maxwell v. Dinis, No. 25-5930 (argued Nov. 2, 2026)

**Judgment: vacated (and remanded). Lineup 7–2.** Roberts, Sotomayor, Kagan, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh, Barrett and Jackson in the majority. Thomas and Alito dissent, or write separately to say they would dismiss the writ as improvidently granted.

**Question reached.** The Court reaches the limited question it granted: can a claim that First Step Act time credits were misapplied, seeking an earlier transfer to a halfway house or home confinement, be brought under §2241? It answers yes. It does not decide the §3621(b) review-bar argument, which the government raised for the first time on the merits. It also leaves exhaustion, and whether the credits still matter after release, to the Fifth Circuit on remand. Because the government agrees with the petitioner that the Fifth Circuit's Melot rule is wrong, the Court vacates rather than reverses outright: other grounds for dismissal are still open below.

**Ground.** The opinion rests on Preiser v. Rodriguez. Moving from prison into prerelease custody in the community is the modern federal counterpart of release to parole. A claim that a statute requires that move therefore attacks the fact or length of confinement in prison, and it sounds in habeas. Melot's "automatic accelerated release" test is rejected. The opinion does not adopt the petitioner's broader "distinct and lower level of custody" test. It follows the government's narrower prison-versus-community line, which Justices Barrett and Sotomayor pressed at argument. It keeps open whether a move between security levels inside the prison system could ever be a habeas claim, which was Justice Gorsuch's concern.

**Procedural exits.** A DIG (dismissal of the writ as improvidently granted) is a real possibility but not the likely result, at about 10%. Justice Alito openly invited one and the SG pressed for it. But Justices Jackson, Sotomayor, Kavanaugh and Barrett each said, in substance, that the QP can be decided and §3621(b) left for remand, and Justice Jackson pointed out that the government never raised these issues at the cert stage. Mootness is a timing risk: the government projects Maxwell's release on January 22, 2027. The Court may expedite its judgment, or it may treat the dispute over the credits as still live. If the case does become moot first, the likely result is a Munsingwear vacatur, which also disturbs the judgment. An affirmance on the court-appointed amicus's theory (prerelease custody is still BOP custody, so this is a conditions claim) is unlikely, at about 5%.

**Writers.** The November sitting's assignments are unknown. A short, near-unanimous habeas opinion fits Kagan, Barrett or Kavanaugh, with Jackson and Sotomayor also plausible. Expect a separate writing from Alito (on the DIG or the review bar), probably one from Thomas (prerelease custody is not release), and possibly a Gorsuch concurrence on line-drawing or a Jackson concurrence on the narrow rule. A short per curiam is possible if the Court rushes to decide before the January release date.
