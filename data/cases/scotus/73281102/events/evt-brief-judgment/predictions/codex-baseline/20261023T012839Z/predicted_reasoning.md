# Forecast of the Court's judgment

The Court will **vacate the Fifth Circuit's judgment and remand**, holding that a federal prisoner may use 28 U.S.C. § 2241 to challenge the withholding or application of First Step Act time credits when the requested relief is earlier transfer from prison to a halfway house or home confinement. My forecast is **7–2**, with Roberts, Sotomayor, Kagan, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh, Barrett, and Jackson in the majority and Thomas and Alito dissenting. I do not forecast an opinion author.

The majority's ground will be that habeas can remedy unlawful physical imprisonment without terminating every form of legal custody. Transfer into the community is analogous to release on parole: continuing restraints do not turn an earned-credit claim seeking earlier departure from prison into an ordinary challenge to prison conditions. The Court will reject the Fifth Circuit's treatment of continued custody as a categorical obstacle to this use of habeas. The statutory custody language and the release-to-parole precedents will carry the holding, rather than an expansion of Bivens.

The holding will be bounded to accelerated release from prison into community prerelease custody. It will not announce a right to use habeas for ordinary prison-to-prison transfers or every reduction in the restrictiveness of confinement. This is the breadth forecast recorded separately from the doctrinal ground in `semantic_claims`.

The Court will resolve only the limited question in its June 1, 2026 grant order. It will not decide how many credits Maxwell has earned, order his immediate transfer, or resolve the original petition's separate exhaustion/jury question. It will leave exhaustion and the government's asserted 18 U.S.C. § 3621(b) review bar for consideration on remand. That incomplete resolution of the underlying dispute makes vacatur more likely than a judgment conclusively reversing Maxwell's dismissal and awarding relief.

The predicted dissent will resist treating this requested change in BOP custody as the kind of release habeas supplies, or favor disposing of the case without reaching that issue because the asserted review bar makes the answer practically unhelpful. The exact lineup is less secure than the forecast that a majority rejects the circuit's categorical rule; a broader coalition or unanimity would be unsurprising.

A procedural exit is genuinely live. The respondent's merits brief reports projected halfway-house eligibility in January 2027 and proposes dismissal as improvidently granted as an alternative to vacatur. I nevertheless forecast a decision on cognizability, with a short remand rather than a ruling that Maxwell must receive the disputed credits. If an actual transfer moots the controversy first, a mootness vacatur is another possible disturbed judgment; a DIG would instead leave the judgment below undisturbed. No transfer, mootness, or future disposition is assumed to have occurred.
