The Supreme Court is very likely to decline Custodia Bank's appeal; I put the chance of review at about one in eight, or 12%. The dispute concerns whether eligible banks are entitled to direct access to the Federal Reserve's payment system. That matters for new banking businesses and state regulation, but no federal appeals courts currently disagree on the legal answer described in the filings. Problems with how constitutional arguments were raised also favor denial, most likely without an explanatory opinion.
