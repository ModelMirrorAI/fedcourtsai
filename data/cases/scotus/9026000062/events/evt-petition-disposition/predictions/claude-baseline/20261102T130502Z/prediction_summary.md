Custodia Bank, a Wyoming crypto-focused bank, asks the Supreme Court to rule that the Federal Reserve must give eligible banks a "master account," the account a bank needs to use the Fed's payment system. We predict the Court will likely **decline** to hear the case. We put the chance of review at about one in twelve (8%). No two federal appeals courts disagree on the question. The federal government opposes review. And a pending Fed rule could change or end the dispute.
