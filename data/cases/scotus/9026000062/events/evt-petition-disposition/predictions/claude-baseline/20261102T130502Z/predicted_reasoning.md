# Forecast — No. 26-62

**Expected outcome:** The petition, distributed once so far for the Nov 13, 2026 conference, will most likely be **denied**, either at that conference or after at most one relist.

**Further relists:** Probably none, about 22%. If there is one, the likeliest reason is a Justice preparing a writing respecting the denial.

**CVSG:** None. The federal government is already a respondent, and the Solicitor General has filed a brief opposing review.

**Question the Court would take:** If it grants, it would take the QP as written: whether 12 U.S.C. 248a(c)(2) removes Reserve Banks' discretion to deny master accounts to eligible nonmember depository institutions. It would not take the waived Appointments Clause theory.

**Route:** A summary disposition is unlikely. No intervening decision would support a GVR. Any grant would lead to plenary review.

**Dissent from denial:** Probably no separate writing. If one appears, Gorsuch is the likeliest author (he is Circuit Justice for the Tenth Circuit, and the issue involves textualism and agency discretion), with Thomas second.
