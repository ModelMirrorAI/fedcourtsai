The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Custodia Bank, Inc. v. Federal Reserve Board of Governors*, with only an 8% chance (less than one in ten) of granting the petition. Custodia, a state-chartered bank focused on digital assets, argues that federal law requires regional Federal Reserve Banks to grant it a "master account" (a direct bank account with the Federal Reserve used to clear payments). Although the case has drawn substantial interest, both federal appeals courts to consider the issue have agreed that Reserve Banks have legal discretion to reject high-risk applicants, leaving no split among lower courts for the Supreme Court to resolve.
