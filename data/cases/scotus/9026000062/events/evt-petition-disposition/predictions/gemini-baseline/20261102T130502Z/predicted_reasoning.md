## 2. Question Presented and Merits Scope (Conditional on Grant)

- **Question Taken:** If certiorari were granted, the Court would take the single Question Presented in the petition as written: whether the Monetary Control Act of 1980, 12 U.S.C. § 248a(c)(2), deprives regional Federal Reserve Banks of discretionary authority under 12 U.S.C. § 342 to deny master accounts to legally eligible nonmember depository institutions. The Court would not add a standalone constitutional Appointments Clause or removal question because those claims were held waived below, though Petitioner's constitutional avoidance arguments would figure into the statutory briefing.
- **Conditional Merits Ground:** If granted for plenary review, the Court would likely affirm the Tenth Circuit's judgment, holding that 12 U.S.C. § 342 ("may receive ... deposits") confers discretionary authority on Reserve Banks to evaluate institutional and systemic risk under *Farmers & Merchants Bank v. Federal Reserve Bank of Richmond*, 262 U.S. 649 (1923), and that 12 U.S.C. § 248a(c)(2)—a pricing-principle directive to the Board of Governors—forbids categorical discrimination against nonmember depository institutions as a class without creating an absolute, nondiscretionary entitlement for every state-chartered entity regardless of risk.
