# Forecast

The Court will most likely deny this petition. I assign a 38% probability to any grant, including summary reversal or a grant, vacatur, and remand. A denial will leave the litigation below to proceed; it will not announce approval of the Eighth Circuit's reasoning.

The baseline contains two conference distributions: August 26 for September 28, 2026, and September 23 for October 9, 2026. The second entry preceded the first scheduled conference, so these are not proof of two completed deliberations. From the recorded count of two, I forecast at least one additional distribution with probability 60%; my central procedural path is one more distribution, followed by disposition in October or November 2026. Further delay into December is plausible if the Court prepares a summary opinion or a separate writing. Timing is a forecast, not a scored claim.

No CVSG appears in the record. The Court will probably not seek the Solicitor General's views: probability of a new invitation is 1%. The question concerns applying existing constitutional and qualified-immunity doctrine to a municipal officer's encounter, rather than resolving a federal program's administration.

If review is granted, the Court will most likely address the petition's single question: whether clearly established law prohibited this particular use of a less-lethal projectile under the totality of the circumstances. I expect a fact-specific qualified-immunity ruling, potentially framed around whether the Eighth Circuit genuinely reconsidered the encounter under Barnes, rather than wholesale reconsideration of qualified immunity.

Conditional on a grant, I assign 85% to disposition in the cert order itself and 15% to plenary review. Within the summary route, outright summary reversal is likelier than another Barnes-based GVR: the lower court has already received that instruction, so renewed intervention would more naturally explain the remaining error. If the Court reverses, the likely rationale is insufficiently specific clearly established law in a rapidly evolving encounter, with the preceding assault and baton contact included in the analysis. This is conditional reasoning, not a prediction that an opinion will necessarily issue.

Conditional on denial, I assign 18% to a noted dissent or statement respecting denial. The central forecast is denial without a separate writing. I do not predict individual cert votes or identify an author.
