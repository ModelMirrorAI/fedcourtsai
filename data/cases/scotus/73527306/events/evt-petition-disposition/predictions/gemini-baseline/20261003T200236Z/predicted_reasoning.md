Distributed twice so far, with a record request during the recess after the first distribution. Expect at most one further relist before a decision on the petition. No CVSG is expected; this is a state/local officer excessive force case with no federal sovereign interest implicated, and the Court did not request a CVSG before its previous GVR in this exact case.

If granted, the Court will likely issue a summary reversal (a per curiam opinion without merits briefing or oral argument). The Court requested the record shortly after the initial distribution, which is a strong signal that one or more Justices are closely scrutinizing the Eighth Circuit's compliance with this Court's prior GVR in light of *Barnes v. Felix*. Summary reversals are a favored tool for correcting lower courts that are perceived to have misapplied the "clearly established" prong of qualified immunity or ignored the totality of the circumstances on remand. The Court would likely take the sole Question Presented as written.

Should the Court deny certiorari, expect a dissent from denial from at least one Justice (likely Justice Alito or Thomas), outlining why the Eighth Circuit's refusal to alter its holding post-GVR warrants correction.
