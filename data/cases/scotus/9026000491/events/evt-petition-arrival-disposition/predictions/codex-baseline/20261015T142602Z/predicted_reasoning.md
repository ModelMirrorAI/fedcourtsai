# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will **grant certiorari for plenary review** of whether APA section 706(2) authorizes universal vacatur of agency rules and, if so, under what circumstances. I expect it to take the petition's sole question substantially as written, rather than add the underlying No Surprises Act payment-calculation merits.

The supplied docket has **zero conference distributions**. The petition will receive an initial distribution after the response and reply process, and my central forecast is **one further distribution after that initial conference**, for two distribution entries from the present zero baseline. I expect action on certiorari in winter 2026–2027, with ordinary response extensions a source of timing uncertainty. The structured 0.99 increment probability means at least one distribution from this arrival state, not necessarily a true relist.

The Court will **not call for the Solicitor General's views**: the Solicitor General is already presenting the federal petitioners' position. P(a new CVSG) is 0.005.

Conditional on any grant, **plenary review is the expected route**: 0.92 plenary versus 0.08 summary disposition in the cert order. The open statutory question and the distinct treatment of APA vacatur in *Trump v. CASA*, 606 U.S. 831, 847 n.10 (2025), favor full briefing and argument rather than treating that decision as already resolving this case. A GVR or summary reversal is not the central forecast.

If review is denied, I expect **no separate statement or noted dissent**. I assign 0.20, conditional on denial, to at least one such writing or notation, without predicting any particular Justice's vote.

If granted, the case will focus on whether the APA's direction to set aside unlawful agency action supplies a remedy extending beyond the parties, and on whether universal vacatur can be the default without a party-specific necessity inquiry. The central certworthiness feature is the remedial issue's reach across federal agency litigation and its separation from the regulatory merits in this petition. This is not a prediction of a merits judgment or a majority lineup.
