Distributed three times so far (two relists); expect perhaps one further relist before a decision on the petition. The case involves qualified immunity and the application of `Barnes v. Felix (2025)` to a use-of-force incident captured on body camera, a highly salient issue which has attracted four amicus briefs. A CVSG is unlikely since no federal party's interest is directly implicated.

If granted, a summary reversal (summary disposition route) is highly probable. The Court frequently uses summary reversals to correct lower courts that deny qualified immunity, especially when video evidence is available and the standard of review is at issue (as presented in QP 2). Given that the Sixth Circuit has already considered the intervening `Barnes` decision, a GVR is less likely than a summary reversal. If the Court denies the petition, there is a substantial chance of a dissent from denial (or a statement respecting the denial) by one of the more conservative Justices, given the strong signals of interest (relists and amici) and the focus on qualified immunity.
