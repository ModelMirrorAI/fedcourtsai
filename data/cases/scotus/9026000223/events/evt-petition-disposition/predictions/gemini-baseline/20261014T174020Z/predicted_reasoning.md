Distributed once so far (for the October 9, 2026 conference). Expect the petition to be relisted at least once more after the response is filed before the Court makes a final decision. The questions presented are highly salient and center on political defamation and the First Amendment, but the Eleventh Circuit applied the fact-specific opinion/hyperbole rule under *Milkovich*.

No CVSG is expected, as the federal government has no direct interest in this private state-law defamation claim.

If granted, the Court would likely take the question as presented to reconsider the application of *New York Times v. Sullivan* or *Milkovich* to political rhetoric. A summary GVR or reversal is unlikely given the absence of an intervening decision or obvious legal error.

Should it deny, expect a dissent from denial (or a statement respecting the denial) from Justice Thomas and/or Justice Alito, who have previously expressed a strong interest in revisiting modern defamation law.
