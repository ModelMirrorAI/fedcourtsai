# Forecast of the Court's disposition

The Court will deny certiorari, most likely without a separate writing. The dispute will remain a contextual application of the fact/opinion distinction rather than become a vehicle for restructuring defamation law. A denial will not endorse either side's characterization of the underlying election-related statements.

The snapshot records **one distribution**, on September 23 for the October 9, 2026 conference, and **no CVSG**. After CNN waived a response, the Court requested one on September 30; the granted extension places its deadline on November 25, 2026. I expect another distribution after the response and any reply, most likely followed by denial in December 2026 or January 2027. My modal forecast is **one further distribution**, with a smaller possibility of additional conferences. P(at least one additional distribution) is **0.95**. This predicts the recorded distribution increment, including redistributing after a requested response, not necessarily a substantive relist after completed consideration.

I predict **no CVSG**, with P(a new invitation) **0.01**. This is a private defamation action; the petitioner's prominence does not itself create a need for the Solicitor General's views.

If review is granted, the Court will most likely address the petition's single question through a narrower formulation: whether the challenged publications, taken in context, communicate verifiable factual assertions notwithstanding their political labels and imagery. Review would focus on applying Milkovich to that distinction, not directly on overruling Sullivan's actual-malice requirement. Conditional on review, plenary briefing and argument are more likely than disposition in the cert order: **P(summary route | grant) = 0.20**. The plausible summary alternative is reversal or vacatur directing renewed contextual analysis; no identified intervening decision supplies a specific GVR rationale.

Conditional on denial, **P(a noted dissent or statement respecting denial) = 0.15**. The modal outcome remains a bare denial; no individual Justice's vote or writing is forecast.
