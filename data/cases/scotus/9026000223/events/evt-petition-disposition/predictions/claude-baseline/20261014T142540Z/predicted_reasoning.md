# Forecast — Trump v. Cable News Network, Inc., No. 26-223

**State the docket shows.** Paid petition from an unpublished CA11 affirmance of a
Rule 12 dismissal (defamation, "Big Lie" / Nazi-comparison coverage). CNN waived
its response (Sep 14); the petition was distributed once, for the 10/9/2026
conference (Sep 23); the Court then **requested a response** (Sep 30), and CNN's
extension to November 25, 2026 was granted (Oct 13). One distribution on the record;
no CVSG.

**Disposition.** Denied. Expect the Court to deny once the brief in opposition is in
and the petition is redistributed — most likely at a conference in December 2026 or
January 2027.

**Relisting.** The 10/9 distribution was overtaken by the response request, so the
petition will be **distributed again** after the BIO and any reply — that further
distribution is near-certain. Beyond that one redistribution I expect no further
relists: a denial at the first post-BIO conference is the modal path. A second
relist after that (i.e., the Court holding it for a closer look or a writing) is
possible but not likely.

**CVSG.** None. This is private litigation between a private plaintiff (suing in his
personal capacity) and a media company; no federal program or statute is at stake,
and the Court does not CVSG in common-law defamation cases.

**Question presented, if granted.** The Court would take the single QP essentially
as written — whether a pejorative label plus inflammatory imagery can carry
actionable, provably false factual implications under *Milkovich* — likely
reframed more neutrally around the *Milkovich* "provably false factual
connotation" test applied to a label in context.

**Summary disposition.** Unlikely. There is no intervening decision to GVR on, and a
per curiam reversal of a pleading-stage opinion/fact ruling is not the Court's
practice. If granted, plenary review.

**Separate writing on denial.** Possible but not expected. Justice Thomas has
repeatedly written about revisiting *New York Times v. Sullivan* (e.g., *McKee*,
*Berisha*, *Coral Ridge*), but this petition turns on the *Milkovich* opinion/fact
line rather than actual malice, which makes it a weaker hook for such a statement.
If anyone writes, Thomas (possibly joined or echoed by Gorsuch) is the likeliest.
