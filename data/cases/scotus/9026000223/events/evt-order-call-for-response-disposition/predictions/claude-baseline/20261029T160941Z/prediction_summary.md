We expect the Supreme Court to turn down Donald Trump's request to revive his defamation lawsuit against CNN over its "Big Lie" coverage — we put the chance it agrees to hear the case at about one in twenty. The Court asked CNN to file a response, which shows someone looked at the petition, but that often happens when a prominent party files. The lower court's short ruling treated the phrase as opinion rather than a provably false fact, and the case is a poor vehicle for the Court.
