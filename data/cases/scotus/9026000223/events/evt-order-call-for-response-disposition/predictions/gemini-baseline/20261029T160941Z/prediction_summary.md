The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Donald Trump's request to review the dismissal of his defamation lawsuit against CNN, with only about a 1 in 25 chance (4%) of taking the case. Although the Court asked CNN to file a written response after the network initially waived its right to do so, the lower courts applied settled free-speech law in holding that CNN's references to Trump's 2020 election challenges as "the Big Lie" were protected political opinion rather than provably false statements of fact.
