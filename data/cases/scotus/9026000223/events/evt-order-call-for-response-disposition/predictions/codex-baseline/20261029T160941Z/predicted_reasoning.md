# Forecast of the Court's disposition

The Court will most likely **deny certiorari**, leaving the judgment below in place without a majority opinion explaining its reasons. The probability of any grant-family outcome is 12%; denial is the principal alternative to that grant probability, with withdrawal or procedural dismissal residual possibilities.

## Proceedings from this moment

The baseline shows **one distribution**, dated September 23, 2026, for the October 9 conference, followed by the September 30 response request. No response, completed conference consideration, relist, or CVSG is shown. CNN will likely respond or obtain an extension, after which the petition will be distributed again. I put the probability of at least one additional distribution at **98%** and expect **one** additional distribution as the modal path. That administrative redistribution is not necessarily a genuine relist following consideration. A further conference cycle is possible, but repeated substantive relists are not the central forecast. The distribution-increment claim is masked for this response-requested moment.

I expect disposition after the response-and-reply cycle, most plausibly in late 2026 or early 2027, rather than resolution on the originally listed October 9 conference schedule. Extensions could move that horizon. Timing is a forecast, not a scored claim.

The Court will probably not call for the Solicitor General's views: **1%** for a new CVSG. The dispute concerns a private defamation damages action, notwithstanding the petitioner's office, rather than an evident federal program or governmental litigating interest.

## If review is granted

The Court would most plausibly take a narrowed version of the single QP: whether the challenged publications convey independently verifiable factual accusations that remain actionable despite surrounding political labels and imagery. It would not necessarily take a broad reconsideration of public-figure actual-malice doctrine, which the petition does not directly ask it to overrule.

Conditional on a grant, **90%** is on plenary review and **10%** on a judgment or remand in the grant order. The latter would more plausibly concern correction of the fact/opinion analysis than an identified intervening precedent; no specific GVR catalyst appears in the record.

## Writing on denial

An unexplained denial with no separate statement is the modal prediction. Conditional on denial, the probability of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial is **16%**. If there is a separate author, Thomas is the leading candidate, with Gorsuch next; this does not predict a publicly observable vote lineup.
