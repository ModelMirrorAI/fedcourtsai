The Supreme Court is very likely to decline Donald Trump's request to review the dismissal of his defamation lawsuit against CNN. I put the chance of review, including a short order sending the case back, at about one in eight (12%). Asking CNN to respond shows some interest, but the case may turn on how existing rules apply to particular political statements rather than a clear disagreement among courts. A denial would leave the dismissal in place without necessarily endorsing its reasoning.
