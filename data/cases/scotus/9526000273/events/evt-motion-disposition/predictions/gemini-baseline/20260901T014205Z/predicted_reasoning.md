This is an application for a stay of execution of a sentence of death, submitted to Justice Thomas. The applicant's execution is scheduled for September 16, 2026. The State of Georgia has already filed an uninvited response opposing the application. Given the nature of capital stay applications, Justice Thomas is highly likely to refer the application to the full Court for consideration rather than acting alone.

The Court will likely deny the application. Stays of execution are rarely granted unless the applicant demonstrates a strong likelihood of success on the merits of the underlying petition for certiorari (docketed as 26-5211) and irreparable harm. Because the State has already filed a response, the Court is very unlikely to formally request one. Further amicus activity is also unlikely given the short timeframe before the scheduled execution. The application will be disposed of shortly before the scheduled execution date.
