# Forecast — No. 26-624, Williams v. Charleston County Sheriff's Office

The docket shows no distribution yet. The Sheriff's Office will file a brief in opposition, as it responded to the first petition, or the Court will call for one if it waives. The petition will then be distributed for a conference in January or February 2027.

The Court will deny certiorari, most likely at its first conference and without a relist. I put any grant at 8%.

No call for the views of the Solicitor General will issue; no federal interest is in play.

If the Court does act, it will act summarily: a per curiam reversal holding that *Galette*'s separate-entity analysis is not confined to corporations, or a second vacatur directing the Fourth Circuit to apply that analysis to the Sheriff's Office. That path would be preceded by at least two relists, and probably by a request for the record. It would rest on question 1 (whether *Galette* is limited to corporate-form entities) and leave question 2 (whether this Sheriff's Office is an arm of South Carolina) for remand. Plenary review is the least likely outcome, because the Court decided the governing framework in 2026 and has little reason to revisit it on an unpublished opinion.

Should the Court deny, expect no separate writing. If one appears, it will come from Justice Sotomayor or Justice Jackson, objecting to the panel's one-paragraph treatment of the remand.
