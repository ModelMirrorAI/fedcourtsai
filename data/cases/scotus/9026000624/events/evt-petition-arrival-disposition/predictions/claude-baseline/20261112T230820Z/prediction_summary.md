The Supreme Court will very probably decline to hear this case; I put the chance it steps in at about one in twelve. The families of three women killed in a crash with a sheriff's deputy are asking the Court a second time to rule that the county sheriff's office can be sued in federal court. The Court earlier sent the case back for another look, and the appeals court kept its ruling. That history raises the odds slightly, but the ruling is unpublished and rests on older binding precedent.
