### Downward Factors (- ~3.4 percentage points from the 5.16% anchor)

Taslimi*, 998 F.3d 648, 654 (4th Cir. 2021)) holding that a three-judge panel cannot overrule binding circuit precedent (*Cromer*) unless intervening Supreme Court authority makes it "impossible to reconcile" (`decision-below.txt`, p. 5). Given Footnote 5's express reservation of unincorporated entities, *Cromer* was plainly not "impossible to reconcile" at the three-judge panel level.
