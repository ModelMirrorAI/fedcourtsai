The Supreme Court is more likely to decline this appeal, with about a one-in-four chance of taking action in the families' favor. The families argue that a county sheriff's office cannot claim the state's protection from lawsuits. Their strongest point is that the Court already sent this dispute back once, but the appeals court reached the same result. Still, the case turns partly on South Carolina's governmental structure. If the Court intervenes, a short corrective order is more likely than full briefing and argument.
