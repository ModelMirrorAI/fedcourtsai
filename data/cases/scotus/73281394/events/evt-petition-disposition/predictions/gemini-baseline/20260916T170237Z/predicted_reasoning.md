Distributed once so far; expect no further relists before a decision on the petition. The Court will likely deny the petition outright. The petition suffers from a significant jurisdictional defect under 28 U.S.C. § 1257(a) because the Utah Supreme Court vacated the sentence and remanded for resentencing, meaning there is no final judgment. Furthermore, the petition asks the Court to allow juvenile life without parole even where a judge finds the offender capable of reform—a direct challenge to the substantive core of *Miller v. Alabama* that the Court explicitly preserved in *Jones v. Mississippi*. Given these vehicle and merits issues, the Court has little reason to grant.

If granted, the Court would likely focus on whether *Jones* effectively leaves the substantive limits on juvenile LWOP entirely to state-court factfinding without federal Eighth Amendment review. A summary GVR is highly unlikely as there is no intervening decision. Should it deny, expect no separate writing, as the jurisdictional finality defect provides a clean reason to pass on the case.
