The U.S. Solicitor General—the federal government's top appellate lawyer, whose petitions the Court grants at a high rate—is asking the Justices to resolve a disagreement among federal appeals courts over whether private individuals can sue federal drug-enforcement agents for money damages under *Bivens* (a 1971 precedent allowing certain constitutional lawsuits against federal officers) when agents allegedly use excessive force while executing an arrest warrant in a public parking lot.
