The Supreme Court is likely (about a 72% chance) to grant this petition, most likely through a "grant, vacate, and remand" (GVR) order—a summary order that wipes out the lower court's ruling and sends the case back for reconsideration without full oral argument. The Solicitor General, representing federal drug-enforcement task-force officers sued personally for damages over an arrest in a shopping mall parking lot, asks the Court either to hear the case or to hold it behind *Nielsen v. Watanabe*, an already-granted Supreme Court case addressing when federal officers can be sued for constitutional violations under *Bivens*. Because the Court grants or remands nearly three-quarters of petitions brought by the Solicitor General and *Nielsen* involves the same legal framework, holding this case and sending it back after *Nielsen* is decided is the most likely outcome.
