The federal government's lawyers (the Solicitor General) are asking the Supreme Court to overturn an appeals-court ruling that lets a man sue federal drug agents for money over alleged rough treatment during his arrest. We think it is very likely (about four in five) that the Court acts for the government. The most probable route is to wait for a closely related case it is already deciding and then send this one back to the lower court to reconsider in light of that decision.
