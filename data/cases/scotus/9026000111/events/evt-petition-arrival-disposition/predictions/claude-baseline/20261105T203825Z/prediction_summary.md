The federal government (through the Solicitor General) asks the Supreme Court to overturn an appeals-court ruling that lets a man sue federal drug agents personally for using excessive force during his arrest. We think the Court will very likely, about four in five, act in the government's favor. Most likely it will set the case aside until it decides a similar pending case (Nielsen v. Watanabe), then send it back to the lower court to reconsider (a "GVR"). This Court has refused to expand such lawsuits for decades.
