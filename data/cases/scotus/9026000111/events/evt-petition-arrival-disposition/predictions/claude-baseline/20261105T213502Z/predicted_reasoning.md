# Forecast — No. 26-111

**Starting state:** no distributions, no CVSG, and response due Dec. 7, 2026.

- **Distribution:** the petition will be distributed after the BIO, probably for a
  January 2027 conference. I then expect the Court to **hold** it pending Nielsen v.
  Watanabe (No. 25-417). That means repeated redistributions without action until
  Nielsen is decided (expected by late June 2027). P(at least one distribution) ≈ 0.96.
- **CVSG:** none. The United States is effectively the petitioner through the SG
  (P ≈ 0.01).
- **Disposition:** the likeliest outcome is a **GVR in light of Nielsen** at the end of
  OT2026. Less likely are a plenary grant of the QP as written, or a per curiam summary
  reversal along the lines of Goldey v. Fields. P(any grant) ≈ 0.78. Conditional on a
  grant, P(summary route, either GVR or summary reversal) ≈ 0.72.
- **If granted plenary:** the Court would take the single QP as written: whether
  warrant-based arrests outside the home present a "new context" under Egbert.
- **Separate writing:** a GVR or summary reversal could draw a dissent from Justice
  Sotomayor and/or Justice Jackson. A denial (P ≈ 0.22) would draw a statement or dissent
  about 20% of the time, possibly from Justice Thomas or Justice Gorsuch urging that
  Bivens be confined or overruled.
