The Supreme Court is likely to intervene, with about a seven-in-ten chance of granting some form of review. The most likely result is to wait for a related case, then send this case back for reconsideration under the new ruling. The dispute concerns whether someone can seek money damages from federal officers accused of using excessive force while carrying out an arrest warrant outside a home. Conflicting appeals-court approaches and the federal government's support favor intervention, although review is not assured.
