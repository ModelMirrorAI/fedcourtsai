The Supreme Court will probably decline to hear this teacher's challenge to Louisiana's law requiring the Ten Commandments in every public-school classroom; the chance of any grant is about one in eleven. The case arrives with two companion cases, and the Texas one is the natural case to hear if the Court takes the issue. This petition comes from a one-paragraph order the teacher himself requested, which makes it a poor case to decide the question. Its best hope is being sent back for reconsideration after a ruling in the Texas case.
