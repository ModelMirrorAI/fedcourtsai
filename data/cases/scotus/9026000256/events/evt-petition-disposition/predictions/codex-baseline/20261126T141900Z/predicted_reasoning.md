I expect the Court to deny Dier’s petition without a separate writing. It has distributed the petition once, for December 11. I expect one further distribution as the Justices consider it alongside *Nathan* and *Roake*, and no call for the U.S. Solicitor General’s views.

If the Court grants review in Dier, the likeliest route is to hold his petition for a related ruling, then vacate and remand so the Fifth Circuit can reconsider the dismissal. If the Court instead sets Dier for argument, I expect it to take his first question as written, whether his Establishment Clause challenge is ripe. The statute fixes the text for his classroom while giving local officials discretion over the display. The Court would have to resolve that threshold before deciding his second question about the mandate’s constitutionality. *Nathan* already presents the merits on a judgment that reached them.
