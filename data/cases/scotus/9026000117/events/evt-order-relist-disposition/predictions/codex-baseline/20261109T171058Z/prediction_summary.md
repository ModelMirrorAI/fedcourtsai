The Supreme Court is very likely—about an 84% chance—to intervene in Alabama's challenge to an order blocking its congressional voting map. The likeliest result is a summary reversal, meaning the Court overturns that order without full briefing and argument. Alabama says the Court already criticized the order when temporarily blocking it in June. Recent voting-rights precedent strengthens that position, although the lower court's separate finding of intentional racial discrimination makes the result less certain. Additional consideration before a decision is likely.
