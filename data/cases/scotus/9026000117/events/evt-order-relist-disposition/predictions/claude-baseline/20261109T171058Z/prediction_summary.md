Alabama is appealing a lower court order that blocked its congressional map. We think it is very likely (about 9 in 10) that the Supreme Court rules for Alabama. The most likely form is a short unsigned decision that reverses the lower court without oral argument. The Court already put that order on hold in June 2026, saying the lower court misapplied the Court's recent Voting Rights Act decision. The Court has now put the case on its conference list three times without acting, which often means an opinion is being written. One or more liberal Justices would probably dissent.
