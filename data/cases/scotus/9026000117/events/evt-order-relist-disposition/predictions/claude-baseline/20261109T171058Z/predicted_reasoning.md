# Forecast — Allen v. Milligan, No. 26-117

- **State:** distributed three times (conferences of 10/16, 11/6, 11/13). This cell opens at the first relist.
- **Disposition:** the Court rules for Alabama. The most likely form is a **per curiam summary
  reversal** (or vacatur) of the preliminary injunction, issued together with Singleton
  (26-116) and Caster (26-118). It would follow the reasoning of the June 2026 stay: the
  district court failed to apply *Callais*'s updated Gingles framework and failed to presume
  legislative good faith. The less likely route is noting probable jurisdiction and setting the
  case for argument this Term. Summary affirmance is very unlikely.
- **Further relists:** about even odds of at least one more distribution before the order
  issues, consistent with a per curiam and dissent still being written.
- **CVSG:** none. CVSGs are not used on direct appeals of this kind, and the United States has
  already participated as amicus.
- **If plenary review:** the Court would take QP 1 (the alternative-map burden under *Callais*)
  and QP 4 (intentional discrimination and the presumption of good faith). QPs 2–3 would be
  subsumed.
- **Separate writings:** a summary reversal would likely draw a dissent from Justice Jackson
  and/or Justice Sotomayor, possibly joined or written by Justice Kagan. A concurrence from
  Justice Thomas, arguing that §2 does not reach districting, is possible. In the unlikely
  event of an ungranted disposition (summary affirmance or dismissal), expect dissents from the
  more conservative Justices.
