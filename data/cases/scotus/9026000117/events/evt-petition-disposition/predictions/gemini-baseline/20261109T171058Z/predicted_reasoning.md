- **Summary disposition vs. plenary review (`summary-disposition-route`: 0.95 conditional on grant):** Conditional on a grant-family disposition (`granted`, `gvr`, or `summary-reversal`), the Court is overwhelmingly likely (95%) to dispose of this appeal in the order itself rather than noting probable jurisdiction for full merits briefing and oral argument.

With the 2026 election cycle now concluded, and with the permanent-injunction remand from the May 11, 2026 GVR (*Allen v. Caster*, 146 S. Milligan*, 146 S. Ct. 1377 (2026).
