# Forecast — Allen v. Milligan, No. 26-117

- **State of the docket:** 3 distributions (10/16, 11/6, 11/13). The case has already been carried past at least one conference. No CVSG.
- **Further relists:** About even odds (0.45) that it is distributed again after 11/13. A separate writing from the liberal Justices, or a per curiam, could push it to a December conference.
- **CVSG:** None. This is a mandatory appeal, and the United States already participates as amicus.
- **Disposition:** Most likely the Court **vacates the May 26, 2026 preliminary injunction and remands**, most plausibly as moot under *Munsingwear*, since the injunction covered only the now-completed 2026 elections. A remand "for further proceedings consistent with" the June 2 stay order is the other likely form. The second most likely outcome is a **per curiam summary reversal** applying *Callais*. Plenary review (noting probable jurisdiction) is unlikely because the injunction has expired.
- **Which question:** If the Court reaches the merits summarily, it would rest on QP 1, the alternative-map requirement under *Callais*. It would also rest on the presumption of legislative good faith underlying QP 4, echoing the stay order. QPs 2–3 would not be separately addressed.
- **Separate writings:** Any vacatur or reversal likely draws a dissent from Justice Jackson and/or Justice Sotomayor, possibly joined by Justice Kagan. If the Court instead dismisses as moot without vacatur, expect Justice Thomas or Justice Alito to write in dissent.
- **Companions:** 26-118 (Singleton) and 26-116 (Caster, cert before judgment) will be disposed of in tandem and in the same way.
