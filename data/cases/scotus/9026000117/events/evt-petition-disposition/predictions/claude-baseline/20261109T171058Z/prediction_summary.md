Alabama is appealing a court order that blocked its congressional map for the 2026 election. The Supreme Court had already paused that order in June. We think it is very likely (about 85%) that the Court will act in Alabama's favor without full briefing. Most likely it will wipe out the lower court's order and send the case back, because the 2026 election has already happened under Alabama's map. A less likely alternative is reversing the order outright. A plain dismissal that leaves the order on the books is the main alternative.
