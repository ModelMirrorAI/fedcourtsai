Distributed twice so far; expect the petition to be denied without further relists. The QP presents a circuit split on whether district courts may consider objective video evidence at the motion-to-dismiss stage in § 1983 excessive force actions, but the fact-bound nature of the incident (where the decedent fled, fired a weapon, and ran toward a civilian vehicle) may make this a poor vehicle to resolve the broader pleading standard question. No CVSG is expected, as the federal government's interests are not directly implicated in this pleading dispute between private plaintiffs and local officers.

If granted, the Court would likely take QP 1 to resolve the circuit split regarding video evidence at Rule 12(b)(6), potentially leaving the more fact-specific qualified immunity questions (QP 2 and 3) behind. A summary GVR is unlikely as there is no clear intervening decision that would prompt a remand. Should it deny, a dissent from denial is possible but not expected, given the complex factual posture.
