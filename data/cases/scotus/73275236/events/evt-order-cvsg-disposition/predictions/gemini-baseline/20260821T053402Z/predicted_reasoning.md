Distributed once so far, followed immediately by a CVSG order on January 12, 2026. I expect at least one further distribution (relist) after the Solicitor General files the requested brief, as the Court will need to consider the SG's views before making a final disposition.

If granted, the Court would likely take the sole Question Presented as written regarding whether alleging an unwritten conspiratorial agreement sufficiently pleads an affirmative act of fraudulent concealment for tolling the Clayton Act statute of limitations. A summary GVR is unlikely here unless an intervening antitrust decision emerges or the SG flags a specific vehicle issue that warrants a remand, as the circuit split on the pleading standard itself typically requires plenary review to resolve. Should the Court deny the petition, there is a moderate chance of a dissent from denial given the high salience of the case, the prominent amici, and the depth of the circuit split alleged.
