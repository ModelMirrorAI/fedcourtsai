# Forecast — General Dynamics Corp. v. Scharpf, No. 25-293 (CVSG moment)

**Bottom line: the Court will most likely never decide this petition. Expect a
Rule 46 dismissal (or withdrawal) during OT2026, once the settlements in the
underlying action receive final approval, without the Solicitor General ever
filing the invited brief.**

The frozen record shows a petition distributed once (Conference of January 9,
2026) and a CVSG issued January 12, 2026 — ordinarily the strongest pre-grant
signal a paid petition can carry short of a grant itself. But the litigation
has moved out from under it. Public reporting after the snapshot shows the
underlying no-poach class action settling out: Faststream settled in September
2025, affiliates of Huntington Ingalls, Marinette Marine, and Serco reached
settlements by March 19, 2026, the plaintiffs dismissed General Dynamics
itself from the suit, and on May 18, 2026 the petitioners moved the Court to
hold the petition in abeyance. A petitioner who expects to prevail on a CVSG'd
petition does not ask the Court to shelve it; the abeyance motion is the tell
that the parties expect the case to end by agreement.

Specific claims:

- **No further distribution is the modal path (~75%).** A petition dismissed
  under Rule 46 after settlement needs no conference. I put roughly a 25%
  chance on at least one more distribution past the single one the record
  shows — the paths that require conference action (a denial once the case is
  moot, or the settlement unraveling and the petition resuming its ordinary
  post-CVSG course).
- **The SG will most likely never file.** The invitation is outstanding, but a
  dismissal moots it; expect it to be quietly overtaken rather than answered.
  No second CVSG can occur (one is already on the docket — the increment claim
  is vacuous here).
- **If the settlement collapses and the case resumes**, the ordinary post-CVSG
  dynamics reassert themselves and this becomes a serious grant candidate: a
  clean alleged 4th-vs-5th/6th/9th split on fraudulent-concealment tolling, a
  former Solicitor General as counsel of record, Chamber/NAM and Shipbuilders
  Council amici at the cert stage, and a CVSG issued three days after the
  first conference. On that branch I would expect a plenary grant on the QP as
  written more likely than not if the SG recommends grant. A summary
  disposition is unlikely even then (~10% conditional on a grant): there is no
  intervening decision to GVR in light of, and the question is briefing-worthy.
- **If instead the Court denies** (most plausibly a clean-up denial of a
  mooted petition), expect no dissent from or statement respecting denial
  (~4%): a mootness-driven denial of a settled commercial case draws no
  writing.
- **Timing:** dismissal or denial within OT2026, most likely between final
  settlement approval in the district court and mid-2027.
