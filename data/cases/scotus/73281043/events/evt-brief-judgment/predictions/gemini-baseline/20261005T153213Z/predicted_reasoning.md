The Eleventh and Fourth Circuits enforce a strict temporal rule.

If the Court reverses, it will likely take the question exactly as presented and establish a categorical rule that forecloses any futility exception to § 2244(b)(2)(A), holding that the date of the Supreme Court's announcement of the rule is the sole determinant of its availability.
