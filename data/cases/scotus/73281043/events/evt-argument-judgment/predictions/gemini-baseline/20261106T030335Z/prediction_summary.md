The Supreme Court is very likely (89% probability) to reverse the Fifth Circuit's ruling in *Guerrero v. Johnson* by an expected 7–2 vote. Federal law generally bars state prisoners from filing a second federal habeas corpus petition (a challenge to an unconstitutional conviction or sentence) unless it relies on a new, retroactive Supreme Court constitutional rule that was "previously unavailable." Because the Supreme Court banned executing intellectually disabled prisoners in 2002—years before Dexter Johnson's first federal petition—a clear majority of Justices signaled at oral argument that later changes in medical diagnostic manuals did not make that constitutional rule "previously unavailable."
