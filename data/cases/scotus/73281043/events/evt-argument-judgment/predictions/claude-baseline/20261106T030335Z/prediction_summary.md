We expect the Supreme Court to side with Texas and **reverse** the Fifth Circuit, very likely (about 85%), by a 6–3 vote along ideological lines. Federal law lets a prisoner file a second habeas petition (a renewed challenge to the conviction or sentence in federal court) only if it relies on a constitutional rule that was "previously unavailable." The Court will probably hold that the 2002 Atkins ruling was available to Johnson all along. Under that holding, a later change in medical diagnostic manuals does not reopen his intellectual-disability claim.
