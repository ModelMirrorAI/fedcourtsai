## What happened

Basso then sought statutory attorney's fees and sanctions. A retired judge assigned to decide the fee request denied it in a one-page order. Basso's petition says this judge may have already begun practicing law as a county attorney when he signed that order, which Basso argues made him ineligible to serve. ## What the Court is being asked

He separately asks the Court to summarily reverse and send the case back to Maryland's courts in light of a newer Maryland Supreme Court decision about how detailed fee-award explanations must be.
