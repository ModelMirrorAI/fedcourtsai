# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny certiorari in Joseph Basso v. Jose Rodriguez, et al., most likely in an early-October 2026 orders release without further distribution or an explanatory writing. The snapshot records exactly one distribution, on September 9 for the September 28 conference, and no CVSG. My modal forecast is zero additional distributions; the probability of at least one additional distribution is 7%. If the Court takes a closer look, one further distribution is more likely than an extended series.

The Court will not call for the Solicitor General's views; I assign a 0.2% probability to a new CVSG. This private state-court fee dispute does not present an evident federal operational interest that would make the government's views particularly useful. The respondent has waived a response, and the supplied docket contains no subsequent response request.

If review is granted, the Court is likelier to take a narrowed version of question one: whether federal due process requires further factual inquiry or relief when evidence discovered on appeal suggests that a recalled judge was simultaneously practicing law when entering judgment. It is less likely to take question two as a freestanding request to apply Maryland's intervening attorney-fee precedent. Conditional on plenary review, the likely focus would be the connection between the alleged disqualification and a constitutionally intolerable risk of bias, rather than a categorical equation of every state-law eligibility defect with a federal constitutional violation.

Conditional on any grant, I assign 35% to disposition in the cert order itself and 65% to plenary review. A remand for reconsideration is a more plausible summary route than an immediate merits reversal, because the record as described leaves factual and procedural questions unresolved. The petition's reliance on the Maryland Supreme Court's Sugarloaf decision does not make a summary grant my modal prediction. Unconditionally, all grant routes together have only a 1.2% probability.

Conditional on denial, I assign 2% to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. I expect an unelaborated denial, not an announced vote lineup, and do not forecast an individual Justice's participation in a separate writing. The timing estimate is a forecast, not a report of anything after the provisioned record.
