# Forecast

The Court will deny the petition. The arrival record shows zero distributions and no CVSG. The petition will probably receive its ordinary initial distribution after the response period, but it will not be relisted after that conference. No call for the views of the Solicitor General will issue, because this private Maryland dispute presents no substantial federal-government interest.

The first question will not attract review. It attempts to extend the objective due-process rule of *Caperton* to a recalled state judge allegedly ineligible to sit while practicing law, but the predicate is disputed and the petition itself places the judge's start of daily county employment after the challenged order. The case also reaches the Court through an unreported intermediate decision and a procedural dismissal by the state high court.

The Court will not take the second question independently. It asks for correction of a Maryland attorney-fee ruling in light of the Maryland Supreme Court's intervening *Sugarloaf* decision, rather than resolution of a federal conflict. If the Court nevertheless grants relief, the most plausible summary route is a vacatur and remand for the state courts to consider *Sugarloaf*, but plenary review of a narrowed version of the first question remains more likely than a summary route conditional on any grant.

The denial will carry no noted dissent or statement respecting denial.
