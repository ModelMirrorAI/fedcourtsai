Distributed once so far; expect no further relists before a decision on the petition. The questions presented attempt to raise issues regarding *Pickering* balancing and *Garcetti* academic freedom, but the petition heavily emphasizes grievance-based, fact-bound arguments about the unfairness of the petitioner's termination for his research. Given the respondents have waived their right to respond, the Court is extremely unlikely to grant review without first requesting a response, which itself is improbable for such a vehicle. If granted, the Court would likely narrow the question to whether *Garcetti* protects academic research at public universities. A summary disposition is unlikely as there is no clear intervening authority. Should the Court deny, there is a small chance of a dissent from denial by a justice concerned with academic freedom and cancel culture, though the fact-bound nature of the petition cuts against even that.
