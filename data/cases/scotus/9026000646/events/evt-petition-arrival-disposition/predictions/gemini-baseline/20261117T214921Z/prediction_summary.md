The Supreme Court will almost certainly turn down this appeal, with only about a one-in-five-hundred chance (0.2%) of agreeing to review it. The petitioners, representing themselves without a lawyer, ask the Justices to step into a twenty-two-year-old California "lemon law" lawsuit against Bentley Motors after California's appellate courts summarily rejected their latest procedural challenge. Because the Supreme Court already denied a nearly identical petition from the same litigants in the same dispute just ten months ago, and because the case involves state procedural rules rather than a national legal conflict, a routine denial of review is overwhelmingly likely.
