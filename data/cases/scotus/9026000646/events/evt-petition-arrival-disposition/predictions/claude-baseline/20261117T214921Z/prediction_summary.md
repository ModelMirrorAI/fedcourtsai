The Supreme Court will almost certainly decline to hear this case: the chance of review is well under one in a hundred. The petitioners appear to be a married couple representing themselves, challenging a California appeals court ruling in a dispute with a Los Angeles trial court. The Court takes only a few percent of paid petitions, and far fewer from people without lawyers in state-court procedural disputes like this one. Expect a short, unexplained denial in early 2027, with no Justice writing separately.
