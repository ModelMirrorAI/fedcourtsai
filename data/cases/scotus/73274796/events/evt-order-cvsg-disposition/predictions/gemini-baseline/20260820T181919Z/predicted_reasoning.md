The docket currently shows two distributions (9/29/2025 and 10/10/2025) followed by a CVSG order on 10/14/2025. Once the Solicitor General's brief is filed, the petition will be distributed again, and I expect at least one further relist beyond that initial post-CVSG distribution given the weight of the Free Exercise and preemption questions. The Solicitor General is likely to weigh in heavily on the ACA preemption issue, which directly implicates federal interests.

If granted, the Court would likely focus on QP 1 and 2, addressing whether the state's regulation of health care sharing ministries violates the Free Exercise Clause by lacking neutrality or general applicability under *Employment Division v. Smith*, and possibly revisiting *Smith* itself. The Court might leave the ACA preemption question (QP 4) behind if it decides the case on First Amendment grounds, or it could take it to resolve the statutory conflict. A summary disposition route (like a GVR) is less likely here because an outright overruling or significant narrowing of *Smith* would require plenary review.

Should the Court deny review, expect a dissent from denial (or a statement respecting denial) from Justice Alito or Justice Thomas, given their longstanding interest in revisiting *Smith* and protecting religious exemptions.
