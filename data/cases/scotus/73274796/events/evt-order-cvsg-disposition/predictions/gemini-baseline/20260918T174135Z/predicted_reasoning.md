Distributed twice so far, and the Court has called for the Solicitor General's views. Expect at least one further distribution once the Solicitor General files their brief before a decision on the petition is reached. A further CVSG is impossible as one has already issued. If granted, the Court would likely take up questions 1 and 2 to address the Tenth Circuit's application of *Employment Division v. Smith*, specifically whether secular exemptions defeat general applicability and whether subjective animus is required to prove a Free Exercise violation. A summary GVR is possible (e.g., if the SG suggests it based on an intervening case or clarification of state law), but plenary review is more likely as the Court may wish to establish a clear precedent on these prominent Free Exercise questions. Should the Court deny review, expect a dissent from denial by Justice Alito, Thomas, or Gorsuch given their strong interest in religious liberty cases.
