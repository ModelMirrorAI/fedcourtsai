# Rationale

I assign a 0.36 probability to any grant and predict denial. The leakage-safe anchor is the `sal-v3` high-band risk set pooled over the eight Terms strictly before OT2025: 40.9% on a denial-reweighted resolved denominator of 1,074. The statpack's paid scored-segment CVSG cut is somewhat lower, with 29.4% plenary grants and 5.5% GVRs, or 34.9% in the grant family. I place this case close to the CVSG-specific rate and below the pooled high-band rate.

The upward evidence is substantial. The October 15, 2025 snapshot shows a paid petition, a requested opposition, two conference distributions, a divided Tenth Circuit decision, an amicus brief, and the October 14 CVSG. The petition presents a plausible conflict between the Tenth Circuit's focus on organizational purpose or ancillary activity and the risk-based comparison described in *Tandon* and *Fulton*. Judge Carson's dissent strengthens the claim that the panel's analysis is contestable, and the United States has a genuine interest in the interaction between the ACA's HCSM provision and state insurance regulation.

The vehicle problems keep the estimate from exceeding the anchor. The case reviews denial of a preliminary injunction, and the brief in opposition persuasively identifies missing factual development about whether exempt secular organizations actually conduct comparable health-cost-sharing activity, whether applying New Mexico law substantially burdens petitioners' exercise, and what consumer risks the activities pose. The respondents also have the stronger account of preemption: the federal provision excuses qualifying individuals from the mandate but does not clearly immunize an HCSM acting as an insurer from state regulation. A CourtListener check of the lower-court docket confirmed that rehearing en banc was denied without any active judge requesting a poll, which weakens the claimed circuit-level conflict. The four questions presented also make the petition broader and less clean than its best general-applicability issue.

The 0.97 relist-increment probability reflects the frozen state of two distributions followed by a CVSG: after the Solicitor General files, a further distribution is the ordinary procedural route to disposition. The 0.01 CVSG-increment figure is nominal because the snapshot already records the CVSG and the harness masks that claim as vacuous. Conditional on a grant, I put the cert-order summary route at 0.14; the dispute lacks a clear intervening decision for a GVR and would ordinarily require plenary treatment. Conditional on denial, I put a noted dissent or statement at 0.09 because the Free Exercise issue and dissent below could attract attention, but the record defects make a silent denial much more likely.

I used the complete questions-presented text, the petition's core argument, and the complete brief in opposition. `documents.json` marks the 217-page petition text as truncated; the questions, statement, reasons for granting, and conclusion were present, while truncation affected the appended material. I did not retrieve the Supreme Court docket or any disposition of this petition. My largest uncertainty is the future Solicitor General recommendation, which could move the grant probability sharply in either direction.
