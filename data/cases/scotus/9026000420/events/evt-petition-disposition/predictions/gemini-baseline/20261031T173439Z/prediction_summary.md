The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Jack Rabbit, LLC v. Visa Inc.* (a 1% chance of a grant, or 1 in 100). The petitioner, a single gas-station business objecting to how a $5.6 billion credit-card fee class-action settlement is administered, asks the Court to decide whether merchants who processed card payments through intermediaries like Square can be included in the settlement class without first proving they had a valid antitrust claim. However, the lower court issued only an unpublished summary order that never addressed this constitutional argument, the class settlement itself became final years ago, and all opposing parties waived their right to respond without any outside groups filing friend-of-the-court briefs in support.
