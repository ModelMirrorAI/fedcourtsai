We predict the Supreme Court will very likely turn down (deny) this petition, about a 1-in-80 chance of review. A merchant that objected to how the $5.6 billion Visa/Mastercard card-fee settlement is being divided wants the Court to stop the money from also going to millions of small businesses that took card payments through payment processors. Both card companies didn't bother filing a response. The lower court treated the dispute as a question of what the settlement contract meant, in a short non-precedential ruling. And a related case the petition leaned on was already turned down in October.
