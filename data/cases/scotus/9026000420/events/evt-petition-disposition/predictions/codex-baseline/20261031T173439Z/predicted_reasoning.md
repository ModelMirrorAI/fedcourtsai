# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny certiorari, most likely in the ordinary orders following the November 13, 2026 conference, without an explanatory opinion. The order will leave the settlement-enforcement judgment in place without deciding whether the petitioner's account of federal class-action requirements is correct.

The current record contains **one distribution and zero relists**. The modal forecast is no further distribution. There is a 16% chance of at least one additional distribution; if that occurs, one additional distribution is more likely than a long sequence. A response request or a hold connected to another class-action petition is a possible minority path, not an event already shown in this record.

The Court will not call for the Solicitor General's views; the probability of a new CVSG is 0.5%. No CVSG is already on the docket.

If the Court grants review, it will most likely take a narrowed version of the first question: whether Rule 23 and Article III require a finding of injury or statutory eligibility before paying disputed members of an already approved damages settlement. The second question, concerning affirmance without deciding eligibility, will function as a consequence of that issue rather than an independently selected question. The Court will not principally use this petition to decide which participant in a payment-card transaction is a direct purchaser.

Conditional on a grant, plenary briefing and argument are more likely than disposition in the cert order: 65% versus 35%. A summary route would more likely be a hold followed by a grant, vacatur, and remand in light of a later relevant decision than an immediate summary reversal. No such intervening decision is established in the present record.

If review is denied, the order will most likely carry no noted dissent or statement respecting denial; the conditional probability of any such notation is 3%. Kavanaugh is the most plausible separate writer if one appears, given the related Labcorp position described in the petition. A writing is not the modal forecast for him or any other Justice.
