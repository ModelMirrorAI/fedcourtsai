The Supreme Court is almost certain to deny review in *Venky Venkatraman v. Bank of America, N.A.*, with a grant probability of 0.2% (about one in five hundred). After scammers persuaded Venkatraman to deposit $94,345 into third-party accounts at Bank of America, lower federal courts dismissed his lawsuit under settled Texas state law holding that banks owe no legal duty of care to protect people who deposit money into strangers' accounts. Because this case involves a routine state-law dispute decided in a non-binding opinion without any disagreement among lower courts—and Bank of America did not even file a response—the Justices have no reason to take it up.
