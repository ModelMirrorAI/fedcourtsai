The Supreme Court will almost certainly decline to hear this case, with a chance of well under one in a hundred. A Texas man who was scammed into depositing about $94,000 into fraudsters' Bank of America accounts sued the bank and lost. The appeals court held that, under Texas law, a bank owes no duty to someone who is not the customer on the account in question. The Supreme Court rarely reviews questions of state law. No other court has disagreed with the ruling, the decision was unpublished, and the bank has not even filed a response.
