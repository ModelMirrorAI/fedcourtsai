The Supreme Court is very likely to decline this appeal; I put the chance of taking action in the petitioner's favor at about one in 250, or 0.4%. The case concerns whether Bank of America owed a duty to a longtime customer who sent money to scammers' accounts. Although fraud prevention matters widely, the petition mainly challenges how Texas law was applied to an individual complaint, without showing a clear disagreement among courts. A refusal to hear it would not endorse the bank's conduct.
