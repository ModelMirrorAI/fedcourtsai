# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will **deny the petition**, most likely by an unsigned order following its scheduled December 4, 2026 conference, during December 2026. That denial will leave the judgment below in place without adopting its reasoning or deciding whether the bank acted properly.

The snapshot records **one distribution and no CVSG**. I forecast **zero further distributions**, with a 4% chance of at least one more; if the case is distributed again, one additional distribution is the most plausible increment. I forecast **no request for the Solicitor General's views**, assigning 0.1% to a new CVSG. The state-law, private-party dispute will not ordinarily require government participation.

The Court will decline to use this unpublished, pleading-stage decision to reconsider Texas negligence duties. The petition does not identify a developed conflict on a federal legal question, and the appendix ties the loss to deficiencies in the pleaded relationship and foreseeability allegations, not merely a categorical nationwide immunity for banks.

It will not establish a private damages action under banking-monitoring statutes that the petitioner expressly disclaims. Conditional on a grant, I forecast **plenary briefing and argument (90%)**, rather than a grant-order disposition (10%). No identified intervening decision or related grant makes a hold followed by GVR the expected route.

**If review is denied**, I forecast **no noted dissent or statement respecting denial** (99.5%). No Justice is expected to write. If an unusual writing does occur, Thomas is the individually most likely writer in the structured forecast, but his absolute writing probability is only 0.13%; this is not a prediction of his position on bank liability. No merits opinion or merits vote lineup is forecast on the denial path.
