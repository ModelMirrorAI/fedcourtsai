## What happened

Bell was sentenced in 2003 after pleading guilty to attempted bank robbery and a firearm charge, receiving an enhanced sentence as a "career offender." Years later, relying on Supreme Court rulings that similar enhancement language was unconstitutionally vague, he challenged his sentence through a habeas corpus petition. A district court dismissed it, and the Fourth Circuit Court of Appeals affirmed, holding that the law gave him no avenue for that constitutional challenge and that this did not violate his right to seek habeas relief. While his appeal was pending, Bell finished serving his sentence, including supervised release.

## What the Court is being asked

## Where it stands

The petition was distributed for conference twice, and the government filed a brief opposing it, arguing vacatur was unwarranted. On October 5, 2026, the Court denied the petition.
