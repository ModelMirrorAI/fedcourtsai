## What happened

Kekai Watanabe, a federal inmate, alleged that after suffering a fractured coccyx in a prison assault, nurse Francis Nielsen refused to send him to a hospital, leaving him in extreme pain for months. A district court dismissed the claim, but the Ninth Circuit reversed, holding the case was not meaningfully different from Carlson. The case also raises whether the Court should eliminate this category of lawsuits entirely.
