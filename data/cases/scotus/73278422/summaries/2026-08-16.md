Watanabe sued Nielsen for damages, arguing this fit within a category of claims the Supreme Court allowed in a 1980 case called Carlson v. Green, involving prison officials' failure to treat a medical emergency. A district court dismissed the claim, but the Ninth Circuit reversed, ruling that Watanabe's situation was not meaningfully different from that earlier case. The full Ninth Circuit declined to rehear the case, over multiple judges' dissents. Nielsen then asked the Supreme Court to take up the case.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether the appeals court was wrong to let this kind of damages claim against a federal prison employee go forward. Specifically, the parties dispute whether the existence of a prison grievance process not addressed in the earlier case, and differences in how severe or immediate the inmate's injury was, should count as meaningful differences that make this a new, unauthorized kind of claim.
