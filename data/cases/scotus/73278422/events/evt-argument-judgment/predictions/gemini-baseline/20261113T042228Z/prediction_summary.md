The Supreme Court is very likely (94% chance) to reverse the Ninth Circuit's decision in an expected 6–3 ruling and throw out federal prisoner Kekai Watanabe's damages lawsuit against prison nurse Francis Nielsen. For 46 years, the Court has refused to expand *Bivens*—a 1971 precedent allowing people to sue federal officers personally for constitutional violations without a statute from Congress. Rather than formally overruling *Bivens* altogether, a conservative majority will likely hold that non-fatal prison medical disputes where administrative grievances, court injunctions, and Federal Tort Claims Act suits against the government are available must be left to Congress.
