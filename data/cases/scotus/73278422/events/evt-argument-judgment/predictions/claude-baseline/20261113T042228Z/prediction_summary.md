The Supreme Court will very likely (about nine in ten) reverse the lower court and block this former federal prisoner's lawsuit for money damages against a prison nurse over delayed medical care. Such suits rest on a court-created right to sue federal officers that the Court has refused to expand for decades, and at argument most Justices seemed to see this case as different from the one earlier case allowing such claims. Expected vote: about 7-2, with Justices Sotomayor and Jackson dissenting, and without formally overruling the earlier precedents.
