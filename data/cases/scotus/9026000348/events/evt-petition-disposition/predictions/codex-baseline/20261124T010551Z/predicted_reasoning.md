The Court will hold this petition while it hears *United States v. Jackson*, No. 26-304. The docket shows one distribution, for December 4. Expect one or two further distributions, including a return to conference when *Jackson* is decided. The Solicitor General has already presented the United States’ views as petitioner and will receive no invitation to brief the case.

I expect the Court to grant the petition, vacate the Second Circuit’s judgment and send the case back to apply *Jackson*. That course lets the Second Circuit address what the new decision means for the expired subpoenas and for Sarcone’s ongoing disqualification. Conditional on a grant, I put the chance of disposition in the order itself at 89%. If the Court instead hears this case, I expect it to take the two statutory questions as presented, likely alongside *Jackson*. They concern whether a first assistant appointed after a vacancy can serve automatically as the acting officer and whether all delegable duties can be assigned to one person who does not qualify as acting officer. If the Court denies review, I expect no noted dissent or statement respecting denial.
