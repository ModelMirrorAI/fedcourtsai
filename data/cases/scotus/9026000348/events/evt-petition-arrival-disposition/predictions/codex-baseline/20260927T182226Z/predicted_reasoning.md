## Questions and route

The common issues are whether a first assistant appointed after a vacancy
can act under 5 U.S.C. 3345(a)(1), and whether Section 3347(a) prevents
delegation of all otherwise-delegable duties of a vacant office to someone
who is not its lawful acting officer. The Court will use the companion
vehicle rather than separately decide those questions on this petition's
grand-jury-subpoena and disqualification record.

Conditional on this petition receiving any grant, the probability of a
disposition in the cert order itself is **0.95**. A companion-based GVR is
much more likely than a summary reversal that independently decides this
case's merits. If this petition instead receives plenary review, the
delegation question is the strongest candidate for review, with the
first-assistant question a closely related alternative ground. Neither
requires a broad constitutional holding about presidential appointment power.

A remand will leave the lower court to assess the companion decision's
effect on the remaining remedial and jurisdictional issues; it will not
necessarily reinstate the expired subpoenas or settle Sarcone's continuing
disqualification. If the Court denies review instead, expect an unexplained
denial with no separate writing. P(any noted dissent or statement respecting
denial | denial) is **0.10**; no individual Justice is assigned a vote or
writing.
