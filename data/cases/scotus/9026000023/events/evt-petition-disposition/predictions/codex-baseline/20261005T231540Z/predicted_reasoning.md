# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely **deny certiorari without a separate writing**. The probability of any grant, including a GVR or summary reversal, is **16%**. The most likely reason for declining review is that the asserted interstate conflict is not sufficiently clean in this unpublished enforcement case to justify deciding the broader federalism question. A denial will not itself express approval of Pennsylvania's enforcement theory or supply a majority opinion on Younger.

The record shows **one distribution and no CVSG**. The Court requested a response after waiver, and the response deadline was extended to **November 2, 2026**, after the originally scheduled September 28 conference. I expect **one additional distribution after response/reply processing**, with a possible further distribution if the Court examines the asserted split or coordinates with the companion petition. The probability of at least one more recorded distribution is **95%**. The expected first additional distribution is administrative recirculation after the response, not necessarily a substantive relist following consideration at conference.

The Court will probably dispose of the petition in **late 2026 or early 2027**, after the response is received and circulated. A hold for related litigation is the principal route to a materially longer delay. The Court will probably **not request the Solicitor General's views**; the probability of a new CVSG is **4%**. If it does request views, the likely occasion is a post-response conference, and disposition would be delayed.

If the Court grants plenary review in this docket, it will most likely take a neutral formulation of the petition's single question: **whether, and at what level of generality, a state's interest in enforcing its laws against out-of-state transactions satisfies Younger's important-state-interest requirement**. It would distinguish that federal-forum issue from ultimate liability under Pennsylvania law and from a definitive ruling on the underlying Commerce Clause or personal-jurisdiction claims. Conditional on reaching the merits, the likely focus is the relationship between NOPSI's generic-interest instruction and the federalism interests implicated by interstate enforcement, rather than a wholesale abolition of Younger abstention.

**Conditional on any grant, summary disposition has a 40% probability**, compared with 60% for plenary review. The most plausible summary route is a hold followed by a GVR if the companion petition identified in the supplied petition becomes the lead vehicle. An immediate summary reversal is less likely: the competing formulations and the record's preservation concerns favor fuller treatment if the Court intervenes directly.

**Conditional on denial, there is a 10% probability of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial.** The modal forecast is no separate writing; no particular Justice is forecast to write. The probabilities here are the same beliefs recorded in the five structured claims.
