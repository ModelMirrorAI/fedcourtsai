The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *TMX Finance Corporate Services, Inc. v. Spicher*, with only about a one-in-twenty (4.5%) chance of granting the petition. Petitioner, a Texas back-office affiliate of auto-title lenders, asks the Court to allow a federal lawsuit blocking a Pennsylvania administrative enforcement action over high-interest loans made to Pennsylvania residents in other states, which lower federal courts dismissed under *Younger* abstention (a rule keeping federal courts from interfering with ongoing state enforcement proceedings). Although the Court requested a response from Pennsylvania, the unpublished Fifth Circuit decision below creates no binding precedent, the alleged disagreement among federal appeals courts is weak, and a companion petition involving the actual lending companies is already pending.
