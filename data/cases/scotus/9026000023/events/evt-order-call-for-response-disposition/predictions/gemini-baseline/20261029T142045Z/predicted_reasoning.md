## Expected Disposition and Order Route

- **Denial of Certiorari (`disposition` = 0.045):** After receiving Respondent's brief in opposition, the Court will deny the petition for a writ of certiorari.
- **Question Presented if Granted (Context-Only):** If the Court were to grant review, it would take the single Question Presented as framed in the petition—whether a State's "generic" interest under *NOPSI*, 491 U.S. at 365, in regulating transactions that out-of-state companies enter into with the State's residents within other States' borders is sufficiently important to trigger *Younger* abstention—limited solely to the threshold abstention question and leaving the underlying dormant Commerce Clause and Fourteenth Amendment personal-jurisdiction defenses for remand.
- **Summary Disposition vs. Plenary Review Conditional on Grant (`summary-disposition-route` = 0.25):** Conditional on the Court granting this petition, plenary review is the primary route (75%), with a 25% conditional probability of a grant, vacate, and remand (GVR). A GVR would occur primarily if the Court granted plenary review in the companion Third Circuit petition arising out of the same Pennsylvania enforcement proceeding (*TitleMax of Virginia, Inc. v. Spicher*, No. 25-1359) and held this petition for a post-decision GVR; *Suncor Energy (U.S.A.) Inc. v. County Commissioners of Boulder County*, No. 25-170, is unlikely to trigger a GVR here because *Suncor* addresses substantive preemption/extraterritoriality rather than *Younger* abstention.
- **Conditional Merits Ground if Granted:** If the Court granted plenary review, it would likely reaffirm that under *NOPSI*, 491 U.S. at 365, and *Sprint Communications, Inc. v. Jacobs*, 571 U.S. 69 (2013), the "important state interest" inquiry looks to the generic class of state civil enforcement proceedings (enforcing state consumer-lending and usury statutes with quasi-criminal penalties) rather than adjudicating the federal plaintiff's merits defenses regarding territorial reach or personal jurisdiction at the abstention threshold.
