The Supreme Court is very likely to decline this case; I estimate about a one-in-seven chance it takes action granting review. TMX argues that Pennsylvania should not be able to keep its constitutional challenge out of federal court while pursuing loans made outside Pennsylvania. The Court's request for a response shows some interest, but most such requests still end in denial. Disagreement among appeals courts helps TMX, while the unpublished ruling, possible argument-preservation problem, and related litigation make this a less straightforward case to choose.
