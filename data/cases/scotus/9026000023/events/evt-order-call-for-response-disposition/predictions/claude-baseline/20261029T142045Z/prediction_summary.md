We expect the Supreme Court to turn down this petition (about a 9% chance it takes the case, roughly one in eleven). A loan-servicing company wants a federal court to hear its challenge to a Pennsylvania enforcement proceeding. The lower court declined, under a rule (called *Younger* abstention) that tells federal courts to stay out of some ongoing state proceedings. The Court asked Pennsylvania to respond, which is a mild sign of interest. But a nearly identical companion petition is pending, and if the Court acts it will more likely take that one and hold this case.
