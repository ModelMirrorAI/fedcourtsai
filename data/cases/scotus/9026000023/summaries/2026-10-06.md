## In brief

TMX Finance asks the Court to decide when federal courts must let companies challenge a state enforcement action instead of requiring them to wait it out in state proceedings first.

## What happened

Pennsylvania regulators accused TMX Finance Corporate Services and related companies of making or facilitating loans to Pennsylvania residents at interest rates above the state's cap, even though the loans were made in stores in other states. TMX sued in federal court in Texas, arguing the proceeding violated the Constitution's limits on regulating across state lines and on suing a company with no local connections. The district court dismissed the suit under a doctrine called Younger abstention, which lets federal courts step back from cases that would interfere with ongoing state proceedings involving important state interests. The Fifth Circuit affirmed, and TMX now asks the Supreme Court to review that ruling.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether a state's broadly stated interest in enforcing its laws is enough to justify keeping a company out of federal court, even when the specific conduct being regulated happened entirely in other states. TMX argues courts should look more closely at how weighty the state's interest really is, rather than accepting a generic justification like "enforcing our laws" at face value.

## Where it stands

The petition was distributed for the Court's September 28, 2026 conference, and the Court asked for a response from Pennsylvania's Secretary of Banking and Securities, who has since received two extensions, now due November 2, 2026. The docket shows no decision yet on whether the Court will hear the case.

## What each outcome would mean

TMX Finance, which lost in the lower courts, is asking the Court to take up the case. If the Court grants review, it would hear full arguments and decide the legal question itself, without yet determining who wins. If the Court denies review, the Fifth Circuit's ruling stands, leaving TMX unable to pursue its constitutional challenge in federal court while Pennsylvania's enforcement proceeding continues. The petition also asks the Court to consider holding this case pending a related petition involving a similar dispute.
