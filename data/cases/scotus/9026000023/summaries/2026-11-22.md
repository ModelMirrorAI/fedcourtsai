## In brief

TMX Finance asks the Court to decide whether a lower court was wrong to let Pennsylvania's usury enforcement proceeding continue without federal court intervention.

## What happened

TMX Finance Corporate Services provides back-office support to affiliated companies that make vehicle title loans. Pennsylvania's banking regulator began an administrative enforcement action against TMX and related companies, alleging thousands of loans to Pennsylvania residents exceeded the state's interest-rate caps. TMX sued in federal court in Texas, arguing the proceeding violates the Constitution's limits on states regulating commerce outside their borders and on exercising authority over companies with no local connections. The district court dismissed the suit under a rule called Younger abstention, which generally bars federal courts from interfering with ongoing state enforcement proceedings that involve important state interests. The Fifth Circuit affirmed, and TMX's request for rehearing was denied.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether a state's general interest in enforcing its own laws is enough, by itself, to justify a federal court stepping back under this abstention rule, even where the regulated conduct and the companies involved are based in other states. TMX argues courts must weigh the state's interest against principles about how far one state's authority can reach into conduct occurring elsewhere; Pennsylvania argues the generalized interest is sufficient and that no court has required more.

## Where it stands

The petition has been fully briefed, with a brief opposing review, a reply brief, and distribution for a conference on December 4, 2026. The docket does not yet show any ruling from that conference.

## What each outcome would mean

TMX Finance, which lost in the lower court, is asking the Court to take up and reverse that ruling. If the Court grants review, it would hear full arguments and decide the abstention question on the merits, with no party yet favored. If it denies review, the Fifth Circuit's decision stands, leaving TMX subject to the Pennsylvania enforcement proceeding without federal court intervention at this stage. A denial would not mean the Court agrees with the lower court's reasoning, only that the case will not be heard.
