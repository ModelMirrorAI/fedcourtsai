## In brief

The Court declined to hear Frederick Allen's bid to revive his copyright lawsuit against North Carolina over Blackbeard shipwreck footage.

## What happened

Allen sued North Carolina officials, claiming they infringed his copyrighted photos and video of a shipwreck salvage project. After the Supreme Court previously ruled North Carolina was immune from the copyright claims, the case returned to the district court, which reopened the litigation and let Allen pursue a different legal theory for overcoming the state's immunity. North Carolina appealed, and the Fourth Circuit reviewed both that reopening decision and a later ruling against the state on immunity, ultimately reversing and ending the case in the state's favor. Allen asked the Supreme Court to review the Fourth Circuit's approach.

## What the Court is being asked

The case asks whether an appeals court may review a procedural ruling that is not independently appealable when it is reviewing a separate, appealable order, if the two rulings are only loosely connected rather than tightly linked under the rule the Court set out in an earlier case. Allen also raised the separate question of whether the appeals court improperly reached its decision on a legal basis neither side had argued, warranting a summary reversal without full briefing.

## Where it stands

The petition was distributed for conference in September 2026, and after the state's response and Allen's reply, the Court considered it at a conference in October 2026. The Court denied the petition.

## What each outcome would mean

Allen, who asked the Supreme Court to take the case, sought to overturn the Fourth Circuit's decision, which had ruled in North Carolina's favor and ended his lawsuit. By denying the petition, the Court leaves the Fourth Circuit's decision in place, meaning Allen's copyright claims against North Carolina remain dismissed. A grant would have meant the Court would hear full arguments on the procedural question and decide it, without that step itself determining who wins.
