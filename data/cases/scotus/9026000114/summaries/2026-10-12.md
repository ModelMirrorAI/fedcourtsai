## In brief

Photographer Frederick Allen asks the Court to review a Fourth Circuit ruling that revived and then dismissed his decade-old copyright suit against North Carolina officials over Blackbeard shipwreck footage.

## What happened

Allen sued North Carolina officials in 2015, claiming they infringed his copyrighted photos and videos of the Queen Anne's Revenge shipwreck. The case reached the Supreme Court once before, which ruled in 2020 that a federal law could not strip North Carolina of sovereign immunity (protection from certain lawsuits) for copyright claims. Back in the lower court, a judge reopened the case in 2021 and let Allen pursue a different legal theory. In 2024 that judge again refused to dismiss on immunity grounds. North Carolina appealed, and the Fourth Circuit reviewed both the 2021 and 2024 orders, ultimately ruling against Allen and ordering his claims dismissed.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether an appeals court, while reviewing an order it clearly has authority to review, may also reach back and review a separate, earlier order that normally could not be appealed yet, when that earlier order is not closely tied to the question properly before it. A second question is whether the appeals court improperly decided the case on a legal theory neither side had argued, which Allen says should lead to quick reversal without full briefing.

## Where it stands

The petition has been distributed for conference twice, most recently for October 16, 2026, after North Carolina filed a brief opposing review and Allen filed a reply; the docket shows no decision yet on whether the Court will hear the case.

## What each outcome would mean

Allen, as petitioner, asked the Court to take up the case. If the Court grants review, it would hear full arguments on whether the Fourth Circuit's approach to reviewing the earlier order was proper, deciding nothing yet about who wins. If it denies review, the Fourth Circuit's ruling against Allen stands, and his claims against North Carolina remain dismissed. The Court could also summarily reverse without full briefing, which Allen's petition requests as an alternative, or take other action on the petition.
