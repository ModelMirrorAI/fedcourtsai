# Predicted outcome — Beaird v. United States, No. 25-5343 (merits, post-argument)

**Judgment: affirmed** (judgment below undisturbed), most likely **7–2**, with
Justices Gorsuch and Thomas in dissent; Gorsuch writes the principal dissent.

**Question reached.** Certiorari was limited to whether *Stinson v. United
States* still correctly states the deference courts owe Guidelines commentary.
The Court reaches it and answers yes.

**Majority ground.** The majority keeps *Stinson*. It roots the Commission's
interpretive authority in the Sentencing Reform Act's express grant of power to
issue policy statements "regarding application of the guidelines," read
through *Williams v. United States*. That makes this an express delegation, not
the implied-delegation premise that *Kisor* and *Loper Bright* cut back. The
Court treats the *Seminole Rock* analogy in *Stinson* as secondary. It also
reasons that *Booker*'s advisory regime lowers the stakes of deference, because
a judge who disagrees with the commentary can vary, and that overruling would
unsettle a vast body of sentencing practice, so stare decisis carries extra
weight. The Court may note that commentary flatly inconsistent with a guideline
never controls, which disowns the Fifth Circuit's broadest phrasing in
*Vargas*. But the 15-round definition in 2K2.1 note 2 is plainly not
inconsistent with "large capacity magazine," so the judgment stands.

**Breadth.** The rule is categorical and covers all interpretive commentary
across the Manual. The Court does not decide on the narrow ground of this one
definition, and it rejects a *Kisor* genuine-ambiguity threshold.

**Separate writings.** Gorsuch dissents, arguing that nothing in the SRA makes
commentary binding and that "binding commentary on advisory guidelines" is
upside down. Thomas joins, or writes separately along similar lines. A
concurrence is plausible: Barrett on how "nomenclature" makes the dispute
practically small, or Kavanaugh on stability after *Booker*.

**Alternative paths.**
- **Vacatur (about 25–30%).** The Court could adopt the Solicitor General's
  middle course: *Kisor*'s limits apply, and *Vargas* erred by ignoring them. It
  would then vacate and remand for the Fifth Circuit to apply the correct
  framework. Gorsuch, Thomas and possibly Barrett would favour this, and the
  Court sometimes vacates for application of a clarified standard.
- **DIG (low single digits).** Justice Alito pressed the point that the
  district court said it would impose the same sentence regardless. That makes
  harmlessness a vehicle concern, but it is too late to DIG a case that has
  been argued with appointed amicus.
