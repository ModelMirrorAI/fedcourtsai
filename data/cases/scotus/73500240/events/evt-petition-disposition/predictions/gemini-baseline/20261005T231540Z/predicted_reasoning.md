Distributed once so far. The Court requested the record on September 22, prior to the September 28 conference, strongly suggesting that the Justices are closely examining the case. Expect one or two further relists as a per curiam opinion is drafted. 

The Fifth Circuit's denial of qualified immunity provoked a strong dissent from Judge Oldham, who argued the majority defied Supreme Court precedent on the use of deadly force. If the Court grants review, it is highly likely to be a summary reversal (or a GVR) to correct the lower court's qualified immunity analysis on the first question presented, rather than taking up the broader originalist challenge to qualified immunity in the third question. A CVSG is unlikely as no federal interests are implicated and the Court generally does not seek the SG's views on routine qualified immunity error correction. Should the Court deny the petition, expect a dissent from denial by Justice Alito or Justice Thomas.
