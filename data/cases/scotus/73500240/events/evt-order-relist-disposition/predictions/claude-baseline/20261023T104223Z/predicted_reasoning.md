# Forecast — Granado v. Ramirez, No. 25-1338 (relist moment)

**State forecast from.** The docket shows two distributions: the conference of
9/28/2026 and, after that conference passed on it, a redistribution on 10/7/2026
for the conference of 11/6/2026. Between the two the Court **requested the
record** (9/22/2026; the Fifth Circuit record is on PACER and the district court's
electronic record arrived 9/28). No CVSG.

**Disposition.** More likely denied than granted, but with a substantial chance
(≈40%) that the Court acts for the officer — most likely by a **per curiam
summary reversal** of the Fifth Circuit's denial of qualified immunity, in the
Mullenix v. Luna / City of Tahlequah v. Bond / Rivas-Villegas line. A plenary
grant is the less likely grant route (≈30% of the grant mass).

**Further relists.** Expect the petition to be distributed at least once more
after the 11/6 conference (≈60%). A record request followed by a relist is the
pattern of a Justice examining the record (and video) for a summary disposition
or writing a separate opinion; both typically take more than one further
conference. A clean denial at the 11/6 conference is the main alternative.

**CVSG.** No. This is a private § 1983 damages action against a municipal
officer; no federal interest is implicated.

**Question the Court would take.** If it acts, it would decide QP 1 in its
qualified-immunity form — that no clearly established law put the continued
firing (the six shots after the first, which the panel accepted as lawful)
beyond debate, given a fleeing suspect holding a handgun after a high-speed
chase. It would not reach QP 1(a)'s claimed split over excessive-force
"elements", QP 2 (whether circuit precedent can clearly establish law) or QP 3
(the historical basis / statutory stare decisis of qualified immunity).

**Summary route.** Conditional on a grant, summary reversal (or a summary
vacatur directing reconsideration under Barnes v. Felix's totality framing) is
the likelier route than briefing and argument.

**If denied.** A separate writing is quite plausible: a dissent from denial
from a Justice who would summarily reverse (Justice Thomas, Justice Alito or
Justice Gorsuch most likely), or a statement respecting denial noting the
interlocutory, fact-disputed posture.
