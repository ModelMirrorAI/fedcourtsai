# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely **deny certiorari**, without a statement explaining the denial. The probability of any grant is **12%**. The likely obstacle is vehicle quality: the alleged disagreement about certificates of appealability is broader and cleaner than the underlying disagreement over collateral challenges to Clark's monetary sanctions, and the government's default objection offers another reason not to use this case to settle that procedural question. This forecasts selection, not an endorsement of the financial penalties or a reasoned merits ruling accompanying denial.

The record shows **two distributions**, for September 28 and October 9, 2026, and no CVSG. I expect **no further distribution**, with a **30%** probability of at least one more beyond those two. If another occurs, one or two additional distributions is the more likely short-review path; a hold associated with the related COA question is a lower-probability, potentially much longer path. The modal timing is disposition on an October 2026 order list following the October 9 conference, with a smaller tail into later months. Timing is context, not a scored claim.

I expect **no new CVSG**; its probability is **0.2%**. The government's legal position is already before the Court through the Solicitor General's opposition.

If plenary review is granted, **QP1 is the more likely focus**: whether adverse circuit precedent categorically precludes a certificate of appealability when other circuits disagree on the relevant issue. The Court would be more likely to address that threshold rule, while retaining the separate requirement of a debatable constitutional claim, than to decide that the amount of a monetary sanction alone creates custody. QP2's proposed exception for sufficiently liberty-restricting financial punishment is a less likely independent basis for plenary review.

Conditional on a grant, I assign **40% to disposition in the cert order itself** and **60% to a route involving further briefing and argument**. The summary possibilities are a COA-focused vacatur/remand or reconsideration after a relevant intervening decision, not an immediate merits ruling cancelling Clark's financial obligations. The related petition identified in the filed briefs supplies a possible hold/GVR path, not an assumption that any intervening decision has occurred.

Conditional on denial, the probability of some noted dissent or statement respecting denial is **12%**; the modal forecast is none. No Justice-specific cert vote or authorship is forecast.
