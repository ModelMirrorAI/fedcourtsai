Distributed twice so far (one relist); expect no further relists. The petition presents a 3-3 circuit split on the standard for granting a certificate of appealability under AEDPA, an issue currently pending before the Court in a related petition (*Berry v. United States*). However, the respondent is the United States, meaning no CVSG will issue. If granted, the most likely route is a summary disposition (GVR) in light of *Berry*, as this case suffers from vehicle problems (procedural default on direct appeal) that make it a poor candidate for plenary review. If the Court denies review, expect no separate writing (dissent from denial), as the procedural hurdles and the Government's argument that the split has little practical effect weigh against a dissent.
