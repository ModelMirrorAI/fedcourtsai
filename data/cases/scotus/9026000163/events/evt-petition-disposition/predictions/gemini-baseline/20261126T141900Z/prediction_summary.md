The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review—turning down the appeal with about a one-in-twenty (5%) chance of taking the case—in Pierre Yvelt Almonor's challenge to his federal money-laundering conspiracy conviction. Although lower federal appeals courts disagree on whether a defendant can be tried in a state where an unknown co-conspirator acted unforeseeably, the Supreme Court has repeatedly declined to hear that same disagreement, and the unpublished ruling here involves disputed trial facts that make it a poor test case.
