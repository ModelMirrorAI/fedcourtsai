The Supreme Court will very likely decline to hear this case, with only about a three-in-one-hundred chance it takes any action in the petitioner's favour. Mr. Almonor argues he was wrongly tried in North Carolina for a money-laundering conspiracy because he could not have foreseen a partner's acts there. Appeals courts do disagree on that rule, but the Court has repeatedly passed on the question, the ruling below is unpublished (not binding precedent), and the government says the facts would defeat him anyway.
