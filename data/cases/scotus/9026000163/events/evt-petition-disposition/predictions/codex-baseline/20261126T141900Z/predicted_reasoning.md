The Court will deny the petition after the December 11 conference. The docket shows one distribution so far. I expect zero further distributions, no invitation to the Solicitor General, who has already filed for the United States, and no separate writing on the denial.

If the Court grants review, I expect briefing and argument on the first question alone. It asks whether venue based on a co-conspirator’s act requires that the defendant could reasonably foresee an act in the district of trial. The Fourth Circuit expressly decided that issue. The second question would require the Court to assess the trial evidence linking the participants. A summary reversal on that ground or a remand under *Abouammo* is less likely than plenary review. *Abouammo* distinguished conspiracy in deciding where a document-falsification charge could be tried.
