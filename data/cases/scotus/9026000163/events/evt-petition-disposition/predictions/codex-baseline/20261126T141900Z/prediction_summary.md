The Supreme Court is very likely to turn down Pierre Almonor’s petition. I give it a 7% chance of granting review. Almonor acted in Florida but was tried in North Carolina because of another participant’s conduct there. Federal appeals courts disagree about whether he had to be able to foresee conduct in that district. That gives the Court a reason to hear him. It has passed up the issue before, though, and the parties dispute whether changing the rule would change his case.
