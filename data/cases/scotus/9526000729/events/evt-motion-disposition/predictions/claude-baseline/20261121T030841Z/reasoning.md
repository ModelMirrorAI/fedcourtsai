- Scored baseline: the statpack's interim section, applicant-class table, bottom-right cell — the ten application-Terms before OT2026 on the `finer_*` counts: 11.7% (156/1337), well over the 50-resolved floor. That section still reads as the scored pool for an OT2026 application.
- That pool is dominated in its grants by government applicants (federal 70.8%, 46/65; state 47.4%, 37/78). The cell I lean on is `private`, `unmarked`: 7.0% (63/906). The caption rule's class (private) matches my own reading.
- I go well below that cell. Its grants are overwhelmingly counselled applications raising federal questions against government action. A self-represented application to stay a state-court judgment in a private dispute is, in my experience of this docket, almost never granted: the Circuit Justice denies it without calling for a response, and a renewed application to another Justice is referred and denied by the full Court. The stay standard (reasonable probability of certiorari, fair prospect of reversal, irreparable harm) is very unlikely to be met by a private state-law dispute from a state high court.
- I leave 1% for what I cannot see: the application text was not provisioned, and a narrow administrative stay is conceivable.
