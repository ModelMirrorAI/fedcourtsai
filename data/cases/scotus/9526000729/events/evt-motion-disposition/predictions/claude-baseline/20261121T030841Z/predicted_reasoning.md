# Forecast — 26A729, Buenaventura v. Buot

- **Disposition:** the stay will be denied. Justice Jackson will deny the application herself, without opinion, within roughly one to three weeks of the Nov 17 submission. No partial relief and no administrative stay.
- **Response:** no response will be called for from the respondent.
- **Referral:** the application will most likely not reach the full Court. If the applicant renews it to another Justice after the denial (about a one-in-five chance), it will be referred to the Court and denied in a routine order-list entry some weeks later.
- **Sealing motion:** it will be disposed of alongside the application, without separate comment.
- **Amicus:** none will be filed.
- **Writings:** no Justice will write or note a dissent on the disposing order.
- **Conditional only:** were any relief granted, it would be a short administrative stay pending a response, not a stay pending certiorari.
