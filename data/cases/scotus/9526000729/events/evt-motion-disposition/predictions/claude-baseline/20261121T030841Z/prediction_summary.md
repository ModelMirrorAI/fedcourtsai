A man representing himself has asked the Supreme Court to put a Maine state-court ruling on hold (a "stay") in his dispute with another private person. This request will almost certainly be denied — roughly a one-in-a-hundred chance of success. Such stays are rarely granted to private parties at all, and almost never to people without lawyers in private state-court disputes. Expect a single Justice to turn it down within a few weeks, without asking the other side to respond and without any written explanation.
