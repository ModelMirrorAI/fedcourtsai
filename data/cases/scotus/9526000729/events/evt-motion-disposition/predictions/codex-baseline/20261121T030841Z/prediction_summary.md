Ian Buenaventura asked Justice Jackson to pause a Maine court order in his dispute with Leslie Buot. I expect Jackson to deny the request without asking Buot to respond. If the request remains live, I give Buenaventura about a one-in-fifty chance of getting even a partial pause. The Court rarely grants such requests from private parties. Buenaventura asked to file his papers under seal, and the public entry does not say what harm a delay would cause or what federal issue the Court could resolve.
