I expect Justice Jackson to deny the stay herself within about two weeks of the November 17 submission. At that point no response had been requested, the application had not been referred to the full Court, and the docket showed no amicus filings. I expect Jackson to act without calling for Buot’s response or referring the matter, with zero further amicus filings and no signed explanation.

The likely basis is that Buenaventura has not made the combined showing needed to pause a state-court order while seeking Supreme Court review. He would need to show both a federal issue likely to warrant review and reversal, and an injury that waiting for review would make irreparable. A short denial would leave the Maine order in effect without settling the parties’ underlying dispute.
