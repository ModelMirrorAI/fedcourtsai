Justice Jackson will almost certainly deny Ian Buenaventura's request for an emergency stay—a temporary court order pausing lower-court proceedings—with less than a one-in-a-hundred chance (0.5%) of relief. Buenaventura represents himself without a lawyer in a Maine state divorce and child-custody dispute (`And-26-291`) and asked to file his papers under seal, meaning kept confidential from the public. The Supreme Court almost never steps into ongoing state family-law cases, and Justice Jackson already denied an earlier stay request from Buenaventura in the same underlying dispute without asking his opponent to respond.
