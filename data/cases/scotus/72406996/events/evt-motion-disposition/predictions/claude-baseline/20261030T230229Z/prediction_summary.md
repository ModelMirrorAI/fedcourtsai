Thomas Arthur, an Alabama death-row prisoner, asked the Supreme Court to pause (stay) his execution. His request is tied to a separate pending appeal that challenges Alabama's system letting judges impose death sentences over a jury's recommendation. We predict the Court will most likely deny the stay, with about a one-in-five chance it grants one. Last-minute execution stays are rarely granted. The linked challenge makes this one somewhat stronger than usual, but a stay needs five of the eight sitting Justices.
