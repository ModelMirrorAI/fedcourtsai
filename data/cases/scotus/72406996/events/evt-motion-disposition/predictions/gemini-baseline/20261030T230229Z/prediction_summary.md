The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Thomas Douglas Arthur's emergency application for a stay of execution (about a 14% chance of a grant, or roughly one in seven). Although Arthur faced a scheduled November 3, 2016 execution in Alabama, the Court already halted that execution in a companion federal appeal on the same night, removing the immediate need for a second stay. Moreover, this application arises from a successive state-court challenge to a conviction that became final nearly twenty years earlier, making Supreme Court review unlikely.
