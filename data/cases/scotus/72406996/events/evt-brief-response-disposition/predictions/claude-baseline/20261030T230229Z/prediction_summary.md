Thomas Arthur, an Alabama death-row prisoner, asked the Supreme Court to pause his execution on the day it was scheduled. Alabama opposed. We think the Court will probably refuse, giving about a one-in-four chance it pauses the execution. Last-minute requests like this are usually denied, and a pause needs five of the eight sitting Justices. The chance is higher than usual because his related appeal challenges Alabama's practice of letting judges override a jury's recommendation of a life sentence, which was an unsettled question at the time.
