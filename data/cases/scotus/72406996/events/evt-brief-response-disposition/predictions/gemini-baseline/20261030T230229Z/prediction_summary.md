The Supreme Court is likely to deny Thomas Douglas Arthur's emergency application for a stay of execution in this docket, with a 28% chance (about one in four) of granting a stay. Although Justice Thomas will almost certainly refer the capital request to the full eight-Justice Court now that Alabama has filed its response, Arthur's underlying appeal arises from an eighth postconviction challenge seeking to retroactively apply new jury-sentencing rules (*Hurst v. Florida*) to a murder conviction that became final nearly twenty years ago. Because new procedural sentencing rules generally do not apply retroactively to older convictions and Arthur's primary execution challenge is proceeding on a separate federal appeal, this stay application is unlikely to draw the five votes needed for relief.
