## Disposition Forecast (`Denied`, `P(granted) = 0.28`)

The application is predicted to be **denied** (`P(unqualified grant) = 0.28`). To obtain a stay of execution under *Barefoot v. Estelle* and *Hill v. McDonough*, Arthur must secure five votes (on the eight-Justice bench sitting in November 2016 following Justice Scalia's death) and demonstrate a reasonable probability that four Justices will grant certiorari in No. `16-595`, a significant possibility of reversal of the lower court's judgment (`Arthur v. State`, Ala. Crim. App. No. CR-15-1452, cert. denied, Ala. Sup. Ct. No. 1951985), and a likelihood of irreparable harm.

While irreparable harm is inherent in a capital execution, the vehicle in No. `16-595` faces formidable procedural and retroactivity hurdles that make securing five votes for a stay on this docket unlikely:

1. **Retroactivity and successive state postconviction posture:** Unlike direct-appeal Alabama capital defendants whose judgments were vacated and remanded in 2016 in light of *Hurst v. Florida*, 136 S. Ct. 616 (2016), Arthur's conviction and death sentence became final nearly two decades earlier (*Ex parte Arthur*, 711 So. 2d 1097 (Ala. 1997), *cert. denied*, 525 U.S. 963 (1998)). Under *Schriro v. Summerlin*, 542 U.S. 348 (2004), *Ring v. Arizona* (and by extension *Hurst*) is a procedural rule that does not apply retroactively to cases on collateral review under *Teague v. Lane*, 489 U.S. 288 (1989).
2. **Independent and adequate state-law procedural bars:** The Alabama Court of Criminal Appeals affirmed the dismissal of Arthur's eighth Rule 32 petition as procedurally barred and time-barred under Ala. R. Crim. P. 32.2, and held that the jury's guilt-phase verdict finding the capital offense of murder after a prior murder conviction (`Ala. Code § 13A-5-40(a)(13)`) already satisfied the unanimous jury factfinding requirement of *Ring* and *Hurst* under *Ex parte Waldrop*, 859 So. 2d 1181 (Ala. 2002), and *Ex parte Bohannon*, 222 So. 3d 525 (Ala. 2016).
3. **Eight-Justice bench dynamics:** Even if Justices Ginsburg, Breyer, Sotomayor, and Kagan were inclined to grant a stay or examine Alabama's capital sentencing scheme, four votes suffice to grant certiorari but five votes are required to grant a stay of execution (absent a "courtesy fifth" vote, which is rarely extended on a successive collateral petition raising a non-retroactive *Ring*/*Hurst* claim, particularly where a companion method-of-execution stay application is pending concurrently).

If the Court were to grant the stay application, it would do so via a standard stay order pending the disposition of the petition for a writ of certiorari in No. `16-595`. More likely, the Court will deny the stay application in No. `16A450` (either on the execution night or when disposing of the companion filings).
