# Forecast of the Court's action

The application will most likely be referred by Justice Thomas to the full Court and denied in a short, unsigned order. The probability of an unqualified substantive grant is 16%; denial is the principal alternative to that grant, not an assessment of the linked certiorari petition.

The baseline already contains Alabama's response but no recorded response request, referral, or amicus filing. From that state, the probability of a newly recorded response request is 3%, referral is 80%, and at least one additional amicus entry, including a submitted brief, is 2%. A response already filed will ordinarily suffice without another request. No conference distribution or CVSG is forecast for this application.

Disposition is expected rapidly, most likely within one to three days after the November 3 response rather than after an ordinary conference cycle; the record does not provide an execution date, so this is an emergency-posture timing forecast, not a known deadline. A temporary administrative pause, if entered, would not by itself establish the ultimate substantive disposition.

Most likely no Justice writes separately. If a separate writing appears, Breyer or Sotomayor is the most likely author; the independent probabilities for all eight sitting Justices are in prediction.json. I do not forecast a majority merits opinion, a particular vote split, or a specific substantive holding. Conditional on a denial, the application will not have persuaded the Court that the showing for emergency relief warrants intervention; the order need not explain which requirement failed.
