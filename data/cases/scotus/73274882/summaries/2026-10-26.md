## In brief

California gun owners ask the Court to decide whether the state's ban on large-capacity ammunition magazines violates the Second Amendment and the Takings Clause.

## What happened

Virginia Duncan and other California residents, along with a gun-rights group, sued California over a law banning possession of magazines holding more than 10 rounds. After years of litigation, including an earlier trip to the Supreme Court that sent the case back for reconsideration under a newer Second Amendment ruling, a divided Ninth Circuit panel upheld the law. The panel majority held that such magazines are not "arms" protected by the Second Amendment, and separately that the ban fits within a historical tradition of regulating especially dangerous weapons. Four judges dissented. The petitioners also argue the law takes their property without compensation.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether a state can ban commonly owned ammunition magazines without violating the right to keep and bear arms, and whether requiring people to give up, modify, or sell magazines they already legally owned amounts to a taking of property requiring compensation.

## Where it stands

The petition has been distributed for conference many times since November 2025 without the Court recording a grant or denial, most recently for a June 29, 2026 conference; the docket shows no action on it since. The parties have since filed supplemental letters and briefs addressing other developments, and the case remains pending.

## What each outcome would mean

Duncan and the other petitioners are asking the Court to reverse the Ninth Circuit's decision upholding California's law. If the Court grants review, it would hear the case in full and decide whether the ban is constitutional, without that grant alone indicating who wins. If the Court denies review, the Ninth Circuit's ruling upholding California's magazine ban stands, leaving the law in effect. Either outcome leaves open how similar cases in other circuits will be resolved.
