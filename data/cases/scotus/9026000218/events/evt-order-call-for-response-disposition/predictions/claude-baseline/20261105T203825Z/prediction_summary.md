We predict the Supreme Court will most likely turn down Juan Matthews's appeal, but the odds of the Court taking action for him are meaningful — about one in six. Matthews says Louisiana courts wrongly rejected his claim that prosecutors hid evidence helpful to his defense (a "Brady" claim) by asking only whether the remaining evidence could still support conviction. The Court asked Louisiana to respond after it declined to, which signals some interest, and several groups filed supporting briefs; still, most such cases are denied.
