Juan Matthews, serving life for a 1992 New Orleans murder, asks the Supreme Court to overturn his conviction. His argument is that prosecutors hid evidence that could have helped him, and that Louisiana courts used the wrong legal test to decide whether that mattered. The Court asked Louisiana to respond after the State had declined to, which is a sign of interest. Even so, we predict the Court will most likely turn the case away. We put the chance that it takes or reverses the case at about one in six.
