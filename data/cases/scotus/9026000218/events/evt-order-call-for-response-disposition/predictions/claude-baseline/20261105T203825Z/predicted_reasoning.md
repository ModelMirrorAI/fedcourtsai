# Forecast — Matthews v. Louisiana, No. 26-218

The docket shows **one distribution** (for the Oct 9, 2026 conference) and a **response requested** on Sep 29 after Louisiana waived; no CVSG.

- **Further distribution:** near-certain. Once Louisiana's brief in opposition is filed (due Oct 29, likely after at least one extension) and the reply period runs, the petition will be redistributed, most likely for a conference in December 2026 or January 2027. Expect perhaps one relist beyond that if a summary reversal or a dissent from denial is being written; a petition being prepared for a per curiam would show two or more relists.
- **CVSG:** none — a state-court criminal Brady case implicates no federal interest the Court would seek the Solicitor General's views on.
- **Disposition:** most likely **denied** (about 84%). The grant-family outcome I find likeliest is a **summary reversal** (per curiam) holding that the Louisiana courts applied a sufficiency-of-the-evidence test in place of the Kyles/Wearry materiality standard, with a remand; plenary review is the less likely grant route (roughly 45% of the grant mass).
- **Question taken, if any:** the single QP as written — whether the Louisiana courts erred by denying the Brady claim based on the sufficiency of the trial evidence — likely narrowed to this record rather than a broader pronouncement about Louisiana practice.
- **Separate writing:** if denied, about a 30% chance of a dissent from or statement respecting denial, most likely from Justice Sotomayor, possibly joined or accompanied by Justice Jackson. If summarily reversed, expect a dissent from Justice Thomas and/or Justice Alito, as in Wearry.
