## 3. Question Presented Scope (Conditional on Grant)

If the Court were to grant certiorari, it would take the single Question Presented in the petition as framed—whether the Louisiana courts erred by denying Petitioner Juan Matthews's *Brady* claim based on the sufficiency of the evidence at trial—without adding or reformulating questions.

## 4. Summary Disposition vs. Plenary Review (`summary-disposition-route`: 0.75)

Conditional on a grant of certiorari, the Court is substantially more likely (`0.75`) to dispose of the case summarily in the certiorari order itself—either via a per curiam summary reversal on the model of *Wearry v.
