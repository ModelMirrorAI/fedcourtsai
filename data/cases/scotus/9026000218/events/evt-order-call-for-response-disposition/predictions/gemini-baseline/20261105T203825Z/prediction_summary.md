The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Juan Matthews's petition for review, with only a 6% chance of granting relief. Matthews, a Louisiana prisoner serving a life sentence for a 1992 murder, argues that state courts improperly rejected his claim under *Brady v. Maryland*—a constitutional rule requiring prosecutors to turn over favorable evidence to the defense—by focusing on whether enough other evidence supported his conviction rather than whether the withheld police notes could have changed the jury's verdict. Although the Court asked Louisiana to file a response and three outside groups filed supporting briefs, the Court rarely takes fact-specific disputes where the state appellate court stated the correct legal rule, especially after rejecting a nearly identical Louisiana appeal earlier in 2026.
