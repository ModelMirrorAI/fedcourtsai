The Supreme Court is very likely to decline Juan Matthews's request to review his murder conviction; I estimate a 14% chance it intervenes. Its request for Louisiana to respond, together with support from outside groups, shows some interest. But the dispute concerns how courts applied an established rule requiring disclosure of important favorable evidence, rather than a clear disagreement over that rule. The appeals court also expressly considered the proper test. If the Court intervenes, a short ruling returning the case is more likely than full argument.
