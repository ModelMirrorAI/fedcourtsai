The Supreme Court is likely to decline Juan Matthews's request to review his Louisiana murder conviction; I put the chance of review at about one in six. The request for a response and four supporting outside briefs show meaningful interest. But the lower appeals court expressly used the required test for whether hidden evidence undermines confidence in a conviction, making this look more like a dispute over particular facts than a new legal question. If the Court intervenes, a short corrective ruling is likelier than full argument.
