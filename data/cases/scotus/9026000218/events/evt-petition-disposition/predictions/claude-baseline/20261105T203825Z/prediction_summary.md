We predict the Supreme Court will most likely **deny** Juan Matthews's petition,
giving it about a one-in-eight chance (12%) of being granted in some form.
Matthews argues that Louisiana courts wrongly rejected his claim that
prosecutors hid helpful evidence (a "Brady" claim) by asking only whether enough
other evidence supported his conviction. The Court asked Louisiana to respond,
and four outside groups support him, both signs of interest. Still, the lower
court used the correct legal wording, and the dispute is mostly about facts.
If the Court does act, it would probably reverse in a short unsigned opinion.
