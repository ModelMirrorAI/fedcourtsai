We predict the Supreme Court will probably **turn down** Juan Matthews's appeal. We put the chance it takes the case at about one in eight (12%). Matthews says Louisiana courts wrongly upheld his 1992 murder conviction even though prosecutors hid evidence that pointed away from him. Brady v. Maryland is the rule that makes prosecutors share evidence favorable to the defense. Several groups back him, and the Court asked Louisiana to respond, which is a mild sign of interest. Still, the Court rarely takes fact-specific cases like this. If it does act, it may reverse in a short unsigned order without full argument.
