The Supreme Court will very likely decline to hear Juan Matthews's challenge to his Louisiana murder conviction. I estimate about a one-in-seven chance of review. His claim that prosecutors withheld helpful evidence drew four supporting briefs and a Court-requested response, meaningful signs of interest. But the state appeals court expressly discussed the correct legal standard, making this look more like a disagreement over particular evidence than a broad legal conflict. If the Court intervenes, a short corrective ruling is likelier than full argument.
