The Supreme Court is likely to decline Juan Matthews's request to review his Louisiana murder conviction. I put the chance of review or another favorable intervention at 14%, about one in seven. The Court has asked Louisiana to respond, and several outside groups support Matthews. But the dispute concerns how courts weighed withheld evidence, and the appeals court says it used the required fairness test. If the Supreme Court intervenes, a short ruling correcting the legal standard is more likely than full briefing and argument.
