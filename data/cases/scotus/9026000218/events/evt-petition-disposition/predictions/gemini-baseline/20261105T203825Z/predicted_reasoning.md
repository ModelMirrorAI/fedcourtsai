## Scope of Review and Summary vs. Plenary Route (Conditional on Grant)

- **Question Presented if granted:** If the Court were to grant review, it would take the single Question Presented in the petition—whether the Louisiana courts erred by denying petitioner's *Brady* claim based on the sufficiency of the evidence at trial rather than evaluating the cumulative materiality of the suppressed evidence under *Kyles v. Whitley*, 514 U.S. 419 (1995).
- **Summary disposition vs. plenary review (`summary-disposition-route`: 0.55):** Conditional on the Court granting review, a summary disposition in the certiorari order itself (specifically, a per curiam summary reversal or a grant, vacate, and remand along the lines of *Wearry v. Cain*, 577 U.S. 385 (2016) (per curiam), or *Wetzel v. Lambert*, 565 U.S. 520 (2012) (per curiam)) is slightly likelier (55%) than setting a fact-intensive 1992 state trial record for full merits briefing and oral argument (45%). If granted, the Court would likely address whether the state courts properly applied *Kyles*'s cumulative-materiality inquiry rather than announcing a new doctrinal rule.
