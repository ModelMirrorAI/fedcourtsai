Watkins sued for damages, relying on a 1980 Supreme Court case, Carlson v. Green, that allows such suits against federal officials for ignoring prisoners' serious medical needs. The petitioners argue the case differs enough from the earlier ruling that courts should not extend it here, while Watkins argues his situation fits the existing rule.
