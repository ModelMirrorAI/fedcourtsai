# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely **deny certiorari**, without a separate writing.
The probability of any grant, including a GVR or other summary grant, is
**0.4%**. The petition does not demonstrate a developed appellate conflict;
its mix of state statutory interpretation and individualized discrimination
allegations is unlikely to produce plenary review.

The arrival record shows **zero distributions and no CVSG**. I expect one
initial conference distribution after the response period and then denial,
with no additional conference distribution after that first one. The
structured `relist-increment` probability is **96%** because its defined
event is any increase from zero; it does not mean a 96% chance of a true
relist after first consideration. The modal decision window is November
2026 through January 2027, allowing for the October 22 response deadline
and ordinary briefing steps. Timing is a forecast, not a scored claim.

The Court will probably not call for the Solicitor General's views:
**P(a new CVSG) = 0.2%**. Federal unemployment law is implicated, but this
record does not present a well-developed national conflict requiring that
additional review.

If review is granted, the likeliest question is a narrowed form of the
first question: whether the governing federal unemployment provisions
preclude the reasonable-assurance rule applied to this category of
public-school employee. The Court would be less likely to take the
petition's historical characterization of the state amendments as written
or separately review the fact-dependent sex-discrimination allegation.
Any merits determination would first need to distinguish public-school
employment from the higher-education employment described in the
petition's excerpts. This is conditional context, not a forecast of a
majority opinion accompanying a denial.

**Conditional on a grant**, plenary consideration is more likely than
disposition in the cert order: **P(summary route | grant) = 30%**.
A GVR is the more plausible summary possibility than a summary reversal,
but the petition's request for one supplies no intervening decision that
would make that route the expected result.

**Conditional on denial**, the probability of any noted dissent or statement
respecting denial is **0.5%**. I expect no such writing and make no
per-Justice vote prediction.
