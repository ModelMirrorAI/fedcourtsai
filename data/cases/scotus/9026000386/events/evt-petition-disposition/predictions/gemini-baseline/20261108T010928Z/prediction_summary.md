The Supreme Court is almost certain to deny this petition for review, with a grant probability of 0.2% (about one in five hundred). The petitioner, a Michigan school bus driver representing himself without a lawyer, challenges the denial of state unemployment benefits during summer school breaks. Because the state respondents waived their right to file an opposing brief and the federal appeals court below dismissed the case in a routine, unpublished order after finding that the petitioner forfeited his main constitutional arguments and raised his sex-discrimination claim too late, the Supreme Court has no clean legal question or conflict among lower courts to resolve.
