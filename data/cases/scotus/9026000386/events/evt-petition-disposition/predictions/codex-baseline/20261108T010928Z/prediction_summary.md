The Supreme Court is very likely to decline Marlin Lowery's request to review the denial of unemployment benefits connected to his school-bus work. I estimate a three-in-a-thousand chance of any grant of review. The lower court found that key arguments were not properly raised and that his complaint did not adequately state a constitutional claim. The petition shows no clear disagreement among courts requiring Supreme Court intervention. I expect a routine denial after the scheduled December conference, without additional consideration or a separate opinion.
