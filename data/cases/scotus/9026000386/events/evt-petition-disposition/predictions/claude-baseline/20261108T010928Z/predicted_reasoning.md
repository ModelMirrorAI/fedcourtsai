# Forecast — No. 26-386

The petition has been distributed once, for the December 4, 2026 conference, and has never been relisted. I expect it to be **denied at that first conference**, in an ordinary order-list denial. I do not expect:
- a call for a response, or any further distribution;
- a CVSG, since no federal party or federal funding-conformity question is genuinely contested on this record;
- a summary disposition or GVR, since no intervening decision bears on Michigan's between-terms denial provision.

If the Court granted, which I do not expect, it would rewrite QP 1 into a narrow question: whether MCL 421.27(i)(2) conflicts with federal unemployment-compensation conformity requirements. It would drop QP 2 and the *Reed* equal-protection claim.

I expect no Justice to dissent from the denial or write a statement respecting it.
