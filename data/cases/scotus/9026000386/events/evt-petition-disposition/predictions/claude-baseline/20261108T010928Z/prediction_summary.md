We predict the Supreme Court will very likely refuse to hear this case. We put the chance it agrees to hear it at about 3 in 1,000. A former school bus driver, representing himself, challenges Michigan's rule that denies summer unemployment benefits to school workers who are expected back in the fall. The appeals court rejected his claim in a short, unpublished order. The state declined to file a response, and the case raises no conflict between courts. Cases like this are almost always turned away.
