# Forecast

The Court will most likely deny the petition after receiving the requested government response. P(any grant), including summary relief, is 24%.

The supplied record shows **one distribution**, on September 30 for the October 16, 2026 conference, and **no CVSG**. It also shows an October 5 request for a response, due November 4, after the government's September 28 waiver. I expect at least one additional distribution after response briefing, with one or two further distribution entries the most likely path. P(the distribution count rises above one) is 95%. That number chiefly forecasts redistribution following the response request, not a 95% chance of a substantive post-consideration relist. A distribution entry does not establish that the Court considered the petition at that conference.

I expect disposition between December 2026 and February 2027, allowing time for the response, reply, redistribution, and possibly another conference. An extension could push that window later. This timing forecast is not a separate scored claim.

I expect no CVSG: the United States is already the respondent and its response has been requested. P(a new CVSG) is 0.1%; the existing response request is not a CVSG.

If the Court grants review, I expect it to address the petition's core question: whether an appellate court can find an erroneous instruction on a genuinely disputed element harmless by weighing the government's evidence against evidence supporting acquittal. The likely framing is the application of Neder to a Ruan subjective-intent error, rather than reopening Ruan's interpretation of the Controlled Substances Act or deciding whether this physician is guilty.

Conditional on a grant, plenary consideration is more likely than disposition in the cert order: 80% versus 20%. The substantial question is whether the circuits disagree about the legal test or only about the records to which they apply it. If the Court instead regards the error as a straightforward failure to apply Neder, summary reversal or vacatur is a plausible minority route. No intervening decision requiring a routine GVR is identified in the materials consulted.

Conditional on denial, I assign 10% to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. The modal forecast is an unexplained denial with no separate writing; I do not forecast a particular Justice's vote. Denial would leave the judgment below in place without endorsing its harmless-error analysis.
