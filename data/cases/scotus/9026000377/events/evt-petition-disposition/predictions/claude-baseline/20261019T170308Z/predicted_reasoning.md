# Forecast — Parasmo v. United States, No. 26-377

**State the docket shows.** Distributed once (for the 10/16/2026 conference)
after the United States waived response; the Court then **requested a
response** on Oct 5, 2026 (due Nov 4, 2026). No CVSG — the United States is
the respondent, so a CVSG is not a live mechanism here.

**Relisting.** The petition will be distributed again once the government's
response (and any reply) is in — most likely for a late-November or December
conference. A further relist after that first post-response conference is
possible but not my central case; I expect the petition to be disposed of
within one to two further distributions.

**CVSG.** None; the Solicitor General is already the respondent and will
answer the call for a response directly.

**Disposition.** Denial is the likelier outcome. The call for a response shows
at least one Justice wanted the government's position on the asserted 4-1
split over harmless-error review of *Ruan* instructional errors (CA4, CA5,
CA10, CA11 vs. the divided CA2 panel). Expect the government to argue the
split is factbound — each circuit applied *Neder*'s "overwhelming evidence"
inquiry to its own record — and that the evidence of subjective bad intent
here was strong. That framing usually carries the day for harmless-error
petitions.

**If granted**, the Court would take the question as written (or a slight
narrowing to *Neder*'s application where the defendant contested the
omitted element), with plenary briefing and argument. A summary route is
unlikely: no intervening decision invites a GVR, and a per curiam reversal
on a harmless-error record is rare.

**Should it deny**, a separate writing is possible but not expected; if one
comes, the likeliest author is a Justice with a record of Sixth Amendment
jury-right writings (e.g., Justice Gorsuch), possibly joined across the usual
lines.
