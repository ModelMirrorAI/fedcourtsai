The Supreme Court is likely to decline Frank Parasmo's appeal; I put the chance it agrees to review the case at about one in five, or 22%. He argues that appeals judges upheld his criminal conviction by deciding a disputed question of intent that should have been left to a properly instructed jury. The Court's request for a government response and a disagreement among the lower-court judges make review more plausible, but the Justices may see a dispute about this particular trial rather than a broader legal conflict.
