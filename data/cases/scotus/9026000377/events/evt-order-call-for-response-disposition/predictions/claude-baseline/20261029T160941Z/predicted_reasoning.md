# Predicted course: Parasmo v. United States, No. 26-377 (response-requested moment)

**State the forecast starts from.** The docket shows one distribution (for the
16 October 2026 conference), entered after the United States waived its
response on 28 September. On 5 October the Court requested a response, due
4 November 2026. No CVSG, no relist and no amicus brief appear on the record.

**Disposition: denied** (P(any grant) about 0.09). The Solicitor General will
file a brief in opposition. It will argue that there is no real split, because
every circuit applies *Neder*'s whole-record inquiry and the cited Fourth,
Fifth, Tenth and Eleventh Circuit Ruan reversals turned on weaker records. It
will also argue that the decision below is a fact-bound harmlessness call on a
record where the jury acquitted on some counts, and that the post-*Ruan* retrial
cohort is finite and shrinking. After the reply, the petition is distributed
again, probably for a conference in early-to-mid December 2026, and denied
there or after one more relist.

**Further distributions.** Close to certain: the petition goes back to
conference once the response is in. A relist after that conference is possible
but not expected.

**CVSG.** None. The United States is the respondent, so the Court will hear
its views in the requested response.

**If granted.** The Court would most likely take the question as written,
possibly narrowed to *Ruan* mens-rea instructional errors. This is plenary
review, not a summary disposition. No intervening decision supports a GVR. A
confession of error by the government, followed by a GVR, is the main summary
route, and it is unlikely.

**Separate writing on a denial.** Unlikely (about 7%). If anyone writes, the
likeliest are Justice Gorsuch (the jury-trial right and *Neder*'s reach) and
Justice Jackson or Justice Sotomayor (appellate courts reweighing a contested
mens rea).
