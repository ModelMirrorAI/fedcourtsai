We expect the Supreme Court to turn down this case. We give it about a one in
eleven chance of taking it up. Dr. Frank Parasmo, a physician convicted of
over-prescribing opioids, argues the appeals court wrongly called a faulty jury
instruction "harmless," meaning not serious enough to require a new trial,
because it judged the government's evidence strong. The Court asked the
government to respond, which raises the odds a little. But the Court seldom
takes cases that turn on how strong the evidence was in one trial.
