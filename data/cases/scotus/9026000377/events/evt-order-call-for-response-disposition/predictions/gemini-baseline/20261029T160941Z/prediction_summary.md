The Supreme Court is unlikely to grant review in *Frank Parasmo v. United States*, with roughly an 11% chance (about one in nine) that the Court takes the case. Dr. Parasmo, an 80-year-old physician convicted of unlawfully prescribing opioids, argues that after the trial judge gave a flawed jury instruction on criminal intent, the appellate court violated his Sixth Amendment right to a jury trial by weighing the disputed trial evidence itself and declaring the error "harmless" (meaning it would not have changed the jury's verdict). Although a dissenting appellate judge and the Court's request for a government response show the petition is receiving serious attention, the Supreme Court usually declines cases that turn on how strongly the trial evidence pointed toward guilt in a specific trial.
