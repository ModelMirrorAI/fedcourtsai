## In brief

Michael Salazar asks the Supreme Court to decide whether people who sign up for a company's free newsletter count as "consumers" under a 1988 video privacy law.

## What happened

Michael Salazar signed up for a free email newsletter from 247Sports, a sports news website owned by Paramount Global, and later watched free videos on the site while logged into Facebook. He sued Paramount under the Video Privacy Protection Act, claiming the company shared his Facebook ID and viewing history with Facebook without his consent. A federal district court dismissed his case, finding he was not a "consumer" under the law because he never rented, bought, or subscribed to video content itself, only a newsletter. The Sixth Circuit Court of Appeals agreed and upheld the dismissal, with one judge dissenting. Other appeals courts have read the same law differently.

## What the Court is being asked

The law defines a "consumer" as someone who rents, buys, or subscribes to "goods or services" from a company that also rents, sells, or delivers videos. The Court must decide whether that phrase covers any product or service from such a company—including things unrelated to video, like a free newsletter—or only covers products and services that are themselves video-related.

## Where it stands

The Supreme Court granted review of the case on January 26, 2026. The parties have filed their full written arguments, and the case was set for oral argument on October 14, 2026.

## What each outcome would mean

Salazar, who brought the original lawsuit, is asking the Court to reverse the lower court's ruling against him. If the Court sides with Salazar and rules that "goods or services" includes non-video offerings like a newsletter, his lawsuit against Paramount would go forward. If the Court sides with Paramount and agrees that only video-related goods or services count, the dismissal of his case would stand. The Court could also vacate the lower court's decision and send the case back without resolving the dispute for either side.
