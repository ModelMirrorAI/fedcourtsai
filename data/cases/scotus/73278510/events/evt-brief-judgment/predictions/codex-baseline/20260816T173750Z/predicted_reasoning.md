# Forecast of the Court's judgment

The Court will affirm the Sixth Circuit, most likely 6–3. The expected majority is the Chief Justice and Justices Thomas, Alito, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh, and Jackson; Justices Sotomayor, Kagan, and Barrett will dissent. No recusal is apparent, and neither a dismissal as improvidently granted nor an equally divided affirmance is a substantial route.

The majority will answer the question presented by holding that a person becomes a "consumer" under 18 U.S.C. § 2710(a)(1) only by renting, purchasing, or subscribing to audiovisual goods or services. It will read the whole phrase "renter, purchaser, or subscriber of goods or services from a video tape service provider" together with § 2710(a)(4), which makes an entity a video tape service provider through the rental, sale, or delivery of prerecorded video tapes or similar audiovisual materials. The parallel pairs—renter/rental, purchaser/sale, and subscriber/delivery—will be treated as opposite sides of the same audiovisual transaction.

That rule will be categorical within the VPPA rather than limited to the wording of Salazar's newsletter. A non-audiovisual transaction will not create indefinite consumer status that springs into a claim when the person later watches a free video. The Court will reinforce the reading with the statute's video-specific protected information, consent structure, titles, and the severe statutory and punitive remedies, but it will not announce a general rule for internet tracking or decide whether Paramount satisfies the other VPPA elements.

The dissent will read "from a video tape service provider" as identifying only the source of otherwise unmodified "goods or services." It will emphasize that Congress expressly wrote "video materials or services" in § 2710(a)(3), decline to transplant that modifier into § 2710(a)(1), and conclude that the separately video-limited definition of personally identifiable information prevents the broad consumer definition from extending liability beyond video-viewing disclosures.
