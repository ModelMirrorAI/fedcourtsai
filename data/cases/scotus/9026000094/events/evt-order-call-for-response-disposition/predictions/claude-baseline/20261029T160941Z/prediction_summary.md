A California city asks the Supreme Court to throw out an excessive-force lawsuit. It argues the plaintiff's no-contest plea to resisting arrest bars the suit under the Court's 1994 Heck v. Humphrey decision. We expect the Court to turn the case down. We put the chance it takes any action for the city at about one in seven (15%). The main reason for going above the usual rate is that the Court asked the plaintiff to file a response. That is a sign some Justice took notice, but most cases where the Court asks for a response are still turned away.
