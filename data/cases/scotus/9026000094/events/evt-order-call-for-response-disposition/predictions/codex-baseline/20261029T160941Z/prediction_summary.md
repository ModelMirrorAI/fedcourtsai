I expect the Supreme Court to decline to hear the City's appeal, although I give some form of intervention a 28% chance. The City argues that a man's conviction for resisting arrest prevents his later lawsuit alleging excessive police force. The Court's request for a response shows interest, and part of the lower court's reasoning rests on a decision now under reconsideration. But a separate, fact-specific reason could sustain the result. If the Court intervenes, sending the case back is more likely than a full hearing.
