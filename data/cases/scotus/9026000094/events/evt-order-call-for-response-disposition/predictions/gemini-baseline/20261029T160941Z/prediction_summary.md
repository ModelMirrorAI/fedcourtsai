We predict the Supreme Court will deny the petition for review, with about a two-in-five (38%) chance of granting it. Police officers and the City of Santa Maria ask the Court to send this excessive-force lawsuit back to the Ninth Circuit because the appeals court relied on a precedent about no-contest criminal pleas (*King v. Villegas*) that the Ninth Circuit has since wiped out for rehearing by a larger panel of judges. Although that intervening change makes a "grant, vacate, and remand" order (a brief order sending the case back for a fresh look) a real possibility, the Court is still slightly more likely to deny review once the respondent points out that this is a premature mid-case appeal where the trial judge found factual disputes and can apply the updated law without Supreme Court intervention.
