# Reasoning — P(grant) = 0.17

**Inputs read.** I read the snapshot `2026-10-22.json`, `record/context.json`
(forward mode, band `elevated` under sal-v4, distribution_count 2, no CVSG,
Term 2026), and the provisioned QP, petition, and BIO texts, none of them
truncated or empty. I ran one CourtListener search for King v. Villegas
en banc activity.

**Anchor.** I used the statpack's sal-v4 segment table, `elevated` column,
bracketed `reached` rate, pooled over Terms 2017–2025. Those rows run about
13.5%–20.5%, and the pooled figure is roughly **16–17%**. The context is
sal-v4 and so is the table, so the anchor applies. This band is the yardstick
for my cell.

**Adjustments up.**
- The Court **requested a response** after the first distribution, which means
  at least one Justice wanted a closer look.
- Elite Supreme Court counsel represents the petitioners (Latham), and a
  municipal-lawyers amicus supports them.
- The panel below was divided, with a concurrence (Judge Koh) accepting the
  petitioners' key factual premise.
- The Court has recently been willing to summarily correct lower courts in
  police-liability and party-presentation cases, and the petition asks
  expressly for summary reversal or, at minimum, a GVR.

**Adjustments down.**
- The decision is an **unpublished memorandum disposition** applying circuit
  precedent to a plea record. That is classic error correction, and the BIO
  frames it so persuasively.
- The Rule 410(a) conflict vanished when King was vacated en banc, and the
  petitioners themselves say it will not be reinstated. That weakens both
  plenary review and a GVR, since there is no intervening decision yet.
- Olivier v. City of Brandon (2026), the Court's latest Heck word, reads
  "necessarily imply" narrowly, which cuts against the petitioners'
  broad-Heck theme.

On balance, the factors roughly cancel against the band anchor. I put it a
touch above it at **0.17**. Any grant would most likely be summary (a per
curiam reversal or a GVR), so summary-disposition-route | grant = 0.75.

**Increments.** I treat the effective state as first real conference
upcoming. A summary reversal would almost certainly be preceded by relists,
which pushes the relist increment up to **0.35**, against the statpack's
modest first-relist hazard. CVSG at **0.02**: no federal interest. Dissent
from denial | denial at **0.12**.

**Uncertainties.** I cannot see the content of the Oct 20 reply. I also don't
know whether the King en banc court will rule before conference, which could
make a GVR or a hold more plausible. My search showed only the April 2026 en
banc grant order. Discount me on how much weight the response request
deserves: such requests are sometimes routine for a petition with an amicus.
