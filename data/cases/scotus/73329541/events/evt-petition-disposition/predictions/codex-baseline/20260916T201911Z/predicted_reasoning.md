# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny this petition, most likely without an explanatory writing following the September 28, 2026 conference, in late September or early October. This is a timing forecast, not a retrieved calendar of disposition orders.

The provisioned docket contains exactly one distribution, entered June 24 for that conference, and no CVSG. I expect zero additional distributions. The probability of at least one further distribution is 12%; if there is further consideration, one additional distribution is more likely than a sustained sequence. I assign 0.4% to a new CVSG and expect none. The disagreement over habeas limitations can be considered without seeking an institutional federal-government position on this particular state prisoner's custody arrangement.

The probability of any grant, including GVR or summary reversal, is 4%. Conditional on a grant, I assign 40% to disposition in the cert order and 60% to plenary review. A Bowe-based GVR is the principal summary alternative, rather than an immediate substantive reversal. Plenary consideration is slightly more likely within the grant branch because extending Bowe to this different subsection requires deciding a distinct statutory question.

If review is granted, the Court will most likely focus on whether section 2244(d)(1)'s one-year limit governs a state prisoner's section 2241 challenge to an administrative or executive custody decision when his underlying state judgments remain valid. That is narrower than whether all section 2241 petitions are time-limited. I do not expect review of the executive agreement's meaning as a freestanding question. If the Court reaches the limitations merits, the likely ground for sustaining the limitation is that the prisoner's continuing custody under state judgments brings him within subsection (d)'s text, irrespective of the statutory label on his petition; Bowe's federal-prisoner and cross-reference holdings would be distinguished.

Conditional on denial, I assign 2.5% to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial and expect none. I make no individual Justice vote or authorship forecast.
