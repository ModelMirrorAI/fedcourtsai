P(grant) 0.35. The prior-Term salience band for an elevated petition reaches ~18%. I adjust this significantly upward for two structural reasons. First, the respondents initially waived their response (evidenced by the initial distribution without one), and the Court actively requested a response on March 23—a strong signal of internal interest from at least one Justice. Second, the petition comes with a vigorous 6-judge dissent from denial of rehearing en banc in the Ninth Circuit, authored by Judge Bumatay, arguing the district court weaponized Rule 11 sanctions to chill politically disfavored election litigation. Given the conservative majority's likely sympathy toward curbing overly aggressive sanctions against political plaintiffs, this vehicle has a strong chance of catching four votes.

I am not higher than 0.35 because the underlying suit (Kari Lake's 2022 election challenge) was widely seen as frivolous, and the Court may prefer to avoid wading into the sanctions dispute to avoid the appearance of endorsing the underlying election claims. The questions presented also challenge a highly discretionary district court ruling (Rule 11 and § 1927 sanctions), which the Court often defers to.

I expect a high likelihood of a relist (0.75) out of the September Long Conference, either to firm up a grant or to afford time for a dissent from denial. If denied, the probability of a dissent is very high (0.50) for the same reasons that the en banc dissent was written. CVSG probability is low (0.02) due to the lack of federal equities.
