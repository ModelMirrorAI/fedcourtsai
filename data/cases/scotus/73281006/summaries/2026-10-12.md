## What happened

Daniel Grand, an Orthodox Jew living in University Heights, Ohio, invited friends to pray with him at his home. The city sent a cease-and-desist letter telling him he needed a special use permit to use his home as a "place of religious assembly." Grand applied for the permit but withdrew his application before a city hearing concluded, saying he did not want to operate a "house of worship." He later sued the city and several officials, raising First Amendment, equal protection, and religious-land-use claims, along with allegations of surveillance and harassment. The district court ruled his main claims were not ready for court because there was no final city decision, and the Sixth Circuit Court of Appeals agreed.

## Where it stands
