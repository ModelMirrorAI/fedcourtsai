# Forecast — No. 26-602, Gnanam v. Keita

The docket shows **zero distributions** and no CVSG. Respondents will most likely waive a response or let the Dec 9, 2026 deadline pass. The petition will then be distributed for a conference in December 2026 or January 2027 and **denied at its first conference, with no relist**. The Court will not call for a response and **will not call for the Solicitor General's views**: no federal party or federal programmatic interest is involved.

If the Court granted, it would most likely act summarily rather than take the case for plenary review. That would mean a GVR or a short per curiam on whether state-court acts taken in violation of the § 362 automatic stay are void. It would most likely reframe QP 2 narrowly around the stay and leave QPs 3 and 4 (contempt under Taggart, and due process) aside. No intervening decision bears on the case, so a GVR has no obvious hook.

Expect **no dissent from denial and no statement respecting denial**. The case is a fact-bound, self-represented challenge to a county-court judgment that came up through a per curiam affirmance without opinion.
