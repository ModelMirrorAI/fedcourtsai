We predict the Supreme Court will very likely turn down this case. We put the chance it agrees to hear it at about 4 in 1,000. The petitioners, a couple representing themselves, lost a county-court money dispute in Florida. They argue the judgment is void because the other side secretly filed for bankruptcy, which pauses lawsuits against the person filing. The state appeals court affirmed without writing an opinion. The case raises no broad legal conflict, so the Court is very unlikely to take it.
