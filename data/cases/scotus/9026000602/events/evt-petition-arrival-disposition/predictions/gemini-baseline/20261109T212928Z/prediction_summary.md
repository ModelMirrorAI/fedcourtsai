The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in this case, with less than a one-in-five-hundred chance (0.2%) of granting the petition. The case arises from a $24,973 Florida county-court landlord-tenant security-deposit and garnishment judgment in which the landlords represent themselves without an attorney. Although the landlords argue that one tenant's later personal bankruptcy voided the judgment against them, federal bankruptcy law protects the person who filed for bankruptcy from creditors rather than wiping out debts that other people owe to that person, and the case presents no conflict among lower courts.
