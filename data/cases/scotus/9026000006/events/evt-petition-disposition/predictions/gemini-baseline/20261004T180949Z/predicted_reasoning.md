Distributed once so far; expect no further relists before a decision on the petition. The QP asks whether an employer "employs" a worker under the FLSA for overtime worked if they permit the work but do not have knowledge of it, arguing the Fifth Circuit wrongly imposed a knowledge requirement. However, this is presented as an error-correction case over the application of facts, with no circuit split alleged. Expect a denial. No CVSG is expected, as the federal government is not a party and the issue is a fact-bound private dispute. If granted, the Court would likely take the sole QP to clarify the FLSA "suffer or permit to work" standard. A summary disposition is unlikely as no intervening decision is cited. Should it deny, expect no separate writing or dissent from denial.
