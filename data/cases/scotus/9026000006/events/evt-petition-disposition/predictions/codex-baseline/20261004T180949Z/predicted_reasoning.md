The Court would be less likely to take a free-standing challenge to this jury's factual findings or decide all employee-classification and exemption questions.

Conditional on denial, the probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial is 2.5%. I expect none and do not forecast an individual Justice's vote or writing. The principal reason to decline review is the absence of a demonstrated conflict coupled with preservation and vehicle concerns, rather than a prediction that the Court will issue a merits explanation rejecting the worker's statutory theory.
