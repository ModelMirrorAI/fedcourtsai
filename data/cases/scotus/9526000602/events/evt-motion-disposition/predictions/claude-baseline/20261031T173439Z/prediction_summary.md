The applicant, representing themselves, asked the Supreme Court to pause (stay) proceedings in a Florida federal case against U.S. Bank. We think the request is almost certain to be turned down: about a 1-in-100 chance it is granted. The Court rarely grants these requests in private disputes with no wider legal importance. It grants them even more rarely when the applicant appears not to have asked the court of appeals first, and when the applicant has no lawyer. We expect Justice Thomas to deny it alone, without asking the bank to respond.
