# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will obtain the requested response, hold this petition for the lead EAJA case identified in the petition, and most likely **grant, vacate, and remand for reconsideration in light of that decision**. The probability of any grant, including that GVR, is **66%**. The Court is unlikely to give this follower petition its own plenary argument.

The supplied docket has **one distribution** and **no CVSG**. I predict **one or two further distribution entries**, with a **96% probability of at least one**. The first should follow completion of the response/reply sequence after the November 4, 2026 response deadline, subject to extensions; a later distribution may accompany final action after the lead case. A hold can last without repeated visible weekly relists. This forecast concerns the increment in recorded distributions, not the number of actual merits discussions at conference.

I predict **no CVSG** (0.2% probability of a new invitation). The Solicitor General has already supplied the government's position as petitioning counsel. The response request already entered is a request to the respondents, not an invitation for the Solicitor General's views.

The relevant question is whether the EAJA's waiver for civil actions unambiguously covers habeas petitions challenging immigration detention. If this petition produces further merits action, the Court will keep that fee-waiver issue, rather than enlarge it into a ruling on the validity of the respondents' detention or their original bond-hearing entitlement. On the modal GVR path, the Court will not issue an independent statutory holding here; it will return the fee judgments for application of the lead decision.

**Conditional on a grant, I assign 97% to disposition in the cert order itself.** A GVR is substantially more likely than either a separate plenary proceeding or an immediate summary reversal deciding the fee question independently. The leading contrary path is denial if the lead decision leaves the Third Circuit's fee-entitlement rule undisturbed. Conditional on denial, the chance of any noted dissent or statement is **4%**; I expect no separate writing and predict no individual author.

The modal timing is after the lead case's decision, plausibly in the first half of 2027 rather than an immediate October disposition. This is a timing forecast, not a claim that I have retrieved the lead case's briefing or argument calendar. A GVR would require reconsideration of the fee awards; it would not itself establish their ultimate amount or the result on remand.
