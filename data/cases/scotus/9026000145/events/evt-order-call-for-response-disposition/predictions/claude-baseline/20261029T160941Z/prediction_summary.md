The government asked the Supreme Court to set this case aside until it decides a nearly identical case, Montoya Palacios v. Liggins, about whether immigrants who win detention challenges can make the government pay their lawyers' fees. We expect the Court to do just that. If the government wins the other case, the Court will likely send this one back to the lower court to reconsider (which counts as a grant); if the government loses, it will deny. We see that as roughly a coin flip, slightly favoring denial (47% chance of a grant).
