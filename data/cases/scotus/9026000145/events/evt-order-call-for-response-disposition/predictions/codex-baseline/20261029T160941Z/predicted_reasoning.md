# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will obtain the requested response, hold this petition for its decision
in Montoya Palacios v. Liggins, No. 25-1223, and then grant certiorari, vacate the
Third Circuit's judgment, and remand for reconsideration in light of that decision.
That grant-vacate-remand disposition, rather than independent briefing and
argument in this case, is the modal forecast. The Court will not decide the
underlying detention or bond-hearing questions through this fee-award petition.

The snapshot records one distribution, on September 16 for the October 9, 2026
conference, followed by the October 5 response request. The response is due
November 4. From that one-distribution state, expect at least one further
distribution after the response process, and roughly one or two additional
distributions overall, including any redistribution when the lead case is
decided. A hold need not produce a long sequence of substantive relists. The
probability of any additional distribution is 0.995; this mostly reflects the
ordinary response-request procedure, not a prediction of repeated merits review.

No invitation to the Solicitor General is expected: the Solicitor General already
represents the petitioners and has expressly requested the hold. The probability
of a new CVSG is 0.001.

If the Court grants relief, it will most likely do so in the certiorari order
itself, applying the lead decision to the question whether 28 U.S.C.
2412(d)(1)(A) permits fee awards in habeas proceedings challenging immigration
detention. The conditional probability of a summary route given any grant is
0.97. A GVR would leave application of the new rule to the lower court; it would
not itself dictate every aspect of the ultimate fee awards. An independent
plenary grant, if necessary, would concern that statutory coverage question,
not the constitutional validity of the detentions.

The principal alternative is denial after the lead decision if that decision
supports the Third Circuit's interpretation or leaves its judgment undisturbed.
If denied, expect no separate writing; the conditional probability of a dissent
or statement respecting denial is 0.06. Sotomayor, Jackson, Gorsuch, Thomas, or
Alito are small, individually uncertain possibilities, not a forecast of any
particular author. The modal GVR will likewise be a routine unsigned order.
