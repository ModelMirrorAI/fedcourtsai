I put the chance of Supreme Court intervention at about two in three. Most
likely, the Court will wait for another case addressing whether people who
successfully challenge immigration detention can recover lawyer fees, then
send this case back for reconsideration under that ruling. The government
expressly requests that approach, and the Court has asked the other side to
respond. If the other ruling instead supports the existing fee awards, the
Court will probably decline this petition without a separate opinion.
