# Forecast

The Court will most likely grant certiorari, vacate the Third Circuit's judgment, and remand for reconsideration in light of Jules v. Andre Balazs Properties, No. 25-83 (May 14, 2026). I assign 68% to any grant. A GVR, rather than a merits reversal directing arbitration, is the modal disposition.

The baseline shows one distribution, entered June 17 for the September 28, 2026 conference, and no CVSG. It also shows a July 15 response request and an opposition submitted September 14. I forecast at least one additional distribution with probability 70%, usually one and possibly two further distribution entries before disposition. This can be a redistribution after the response rather than a genuine second consideration by the Justices. I expect disposition during October or November 2026; timing is a forecast, not a claim of a confirmed calendar change.

No CVSG is expected: the probability of a new call is 0.5%. The central jurisdictional issue can be addressed through an existing Court precedent without obtaining the government's views on this private commercial dispute.

Conditional on any grant, I assign 97% to disposition in the cert order itself, overwhelmingly a GVR rather than summary reversal. The relevant question is whether existing federal jurisdiction supports FAA Sections 3 and 4 motions within a Section 1782 discovery proceeding. The likely order will direct reconsideration in light of Jules, not resolve every aspect of that question. If plenary review is instead granted, I expect a question narrowed to that discovery-proceeding setting rather than an unqualified rule governing all motions in any pending federal matter.

Jules supplies a reason to reconsider the jurisdictional analysis; it does not itself establish that the foreign-discovery application must be arbitrated. The Court will likely leave the distinction between jurisdiction and entitlement to arbitration, and the special character of Section 1782 proceedings, to the lower court on remand.

If review is denied, I expect an unexplained denial without a public separate writing. The probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial, conditional on denial, is 2%. I do not forecast individual cert votes.
