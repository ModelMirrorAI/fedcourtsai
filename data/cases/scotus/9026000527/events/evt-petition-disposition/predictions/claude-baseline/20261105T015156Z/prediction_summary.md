We expect the Supreme Court to turn down this case (very unlikely to be heard, about a 1-in-40 chance). An Italian winemaker argues that a federal appeals court ignored a 2015 Supreme Court ruling on when a trademark office decision binds later court cases. But the appeals court's ruling was an unpublished order that sets no precedent. The winery on the other side did not even bother to file a response. The dispute is between two private wine companies over similar brand names.
