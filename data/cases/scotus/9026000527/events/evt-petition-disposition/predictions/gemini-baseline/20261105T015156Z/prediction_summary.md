The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Cesari S.R.L. v. Peju Province Winery L.P.*, with less than a one-in-one-hundred (0.8%) chance of granting the petition. Although the Italian winery Cesari argues that the Second Circuit conflicted with Supreme Court precedent when it refused to treat a 2004 Trademark Trial and Appeal Board registration decision as binding in this trademark infringement lawsuit, the decision below is a non-precedential summary order that merely sends the case back to the trial court to evaluate customer confusion in the real-world marketplace. Furthermore, the respondents waived their right to file a response, and the full Supreme Court already denied Cesari's request to pause the lower court's ruling in October 2026.
