# Forecast of the Court's treatment

The Court will deny the petition, most likely without explanation on an order list shortly after the **December 4, 2026 conference**. Early December is the central timing forecast; a request for a response would instead push disposition into early 2027. A denial would leave further proceedings in the lower courts in place, not endorse the Second Circuit's reasoning.

**Further distributions:** The record already contains **one distribution and zero relists**. I expect **zero additional distributions**. The probability of at least one additional distribution is **18%**, including a redistribution following a called-for response, a reschedule, or a true relist. If that nonmodal branch occurs, one or two additional distributions are more likely than a prolonged series. I do not predict a response request merely because respondent waived an opposition.

**Solicitor General:** I expect **no call for the Solicitor General's views**, with a **1.5%** chance of a new CVSG. If it occurs, it would most likely come after the scheduled conference or a subsequent conference following a response, and delay the petition for several months.

**Question taken if review is granted:** The most likely focus would be **QP2**, narrowed to whether actual uses within an unrestricted wine application can be materially different for issue-preclusion purposes merely because they are narrower. QP1's attack on the continued use of pre-B&B circuit cases would supply background rather than require wholesale reconsideration of trademark preclusion. The Court would not need to settle whether the two particular wines actually confuse consumers. If the Court intervenes for Cesari, the likely legal ground is that a narrower use alone does not establish material difference, while genuine differences in marketplace usage can still prevent preclusion.

**Route conditional on a grant:** I assign **45%** to a disposition in the cert order and **55%** to a grant opening a merits proceeding. Summary reversal is the principal summary route: it would correct an overly categorical exclusion from preclusion under existing precedent. A conventional GVR based on intervening authority is not the central forecast because the record identifies no new decision for which this case should be held. The dispute over what the TTAB actually decided makes plenary treatment slightly more likely conditional on intervention.

**Denial writings:** I expect no dissent or statement, with **2.5% probability conditional on denial** that at least one Justice notes a dissent or issues a statement respecting denial. If someone writes, Thomas or Alito is a more plausible author than the other individual Justices because of their respective positions in B&B Hardware, but neither is affirmatively predicted to write. The per-Justice probabilities in `prediction.json` are unconditional probabilities of authorship, not probabilities of joining someone else's writing.
