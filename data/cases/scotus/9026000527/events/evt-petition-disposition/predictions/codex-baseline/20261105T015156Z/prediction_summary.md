The Supreme Court is very likely to decline this wine-trademark dispute; I put the chance of review or another grant of relief at about one in forty, or 2.5%. Cesari argues that an earlier trademark-registration ruling should prevent its opponent from disputing consumer confusion again. But the appeals court's decision is narrow, turns on the particular products, and does not establish a clear nationwide conflict. I expect a short, unexplained denial after the December 4 conference, without another conference or a request for the federal government's views.
