The Court will wait for *Rhoney v. Barbosa da Cunha*, No. 26-104, then grant the petition, vacate the Seventh Circuit's judgment, and remand for reconsideration in light of that decision. The petition presents the same statutory question as written. It asks whether 8 U.S.C. § 1225(b)(2)(A) requires detention pending removal proceedings for someone already present in the United States without admission. The Seventh Circuit's bond-hearing ruling rests directly on its answer. I expect the Court to leave application of the new decision to the Seventh Circuit, with no separate briefing and argument in *Padula*.

The docket shows one distribution and no relist so far. I expect one or two more distributions, the first after the respondent has had time to answer, followed by a hold until the lead decision. The Court will have the government's views from its own petition and will issue no invitation to the Solicitor General. If the lead decision sustains the Seventh Circuit's rule, I expect a denial without a noted dissent or statement.
