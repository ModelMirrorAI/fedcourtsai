The Supreme Court is moderately likely — at roughly three in five odds (62%) — to dispose of this petition through a grant, vacatur, and remand (an order sending the case back to the lower court to reconsider its ruling without full Supreme Court briefing or argument). The federal government is not asking the Justices to hear oral argument here; instead, it asks the Court to hold this case behind *Rhoney v. Barbosa da Cunha*, an already-granted case testing whether federal law requires mandatory detention without bond for noncitizens living inside the country who entered without formal admission. Because the Seventh Circuit ruled against the government below, this petition will be sent back for a fresh look if the government wins *Barbosa da Cunha*, and denied if the government loses.
