# Forecast — No. 26-572, Padula v. Cirrus Rojas

The docket shows one distribution (for the Dec 4, 2026 conference) and a response requested Nov 24, due Dec 24.

- **Hold.** After the response arrives (possibly after one extension), the petition will be redistributed for a January or February 2027 conference and then held, without any order, for *Rhoney v. Barbosa da Cunha*, No. 26-104. Expect one further distribution after the response, and one more after the lead case is decided. It will not be relisted in the pre-grant sense.
- **No CVSG.** The United States is the petitioner.
- **Disposition.** The petition will be disposed of on an order list shortly after the decision in No. 26-104, most likely in late June or early July 2027. Slightly more likely than not (about 52%), the Court will rule for the government in the lead case and will grant this petition, vacate the Seventh Circuit's judgment and remand in light of that decision. Otherwise the Court will deny the petition.
- **Route.** Any grant will be a GVR in the cert order. The Court will not set this case for briefing and argument.
- **Question presented.** If the Court acts on the question, it is the petition's single question as written: whether section 1225(b)(2)(A) mandates detention of noncitizens present without admission. The Court will answer it in the lead case, not here.
- **Separate writings.** None expected on the order, whether GVR or denial. A dissent from a GVR or a statement respecting denial is possible but unlikely (a few percent).
- **Mootness.** The respondent's response may argue the case is moot because the writ issued and he is on bond. The Court will most likely still hold the petition; a Munsingwear vacatur is a small tail.
