The government asked the Supreme Court not to hear this immigration-detention case itself but to hold it until the Court decides another case raising the same question, about whether people who entered the country without permission must be detained without a bond hearing. We predict, at slightly better than even odds (about 52%), that the government wins that other case and the Court then sends this one back to the lower court for reconsideration, an order called a GVR. Otherwise the petition will simply be denied.
