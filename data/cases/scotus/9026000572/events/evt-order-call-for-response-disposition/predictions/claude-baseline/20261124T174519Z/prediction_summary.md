The government asked the Supreme Court to put this immigration-detention case on hold, because the Court has already agreed to decide the same legal question in another case, Rhoney v. Barbosa da Cunha. I think it is somewhat more likely than not, about three in five, that the Court will eventually grant the petition by a "GVR": a short order that wipes out the lower-court ruling and sends the case back for another look. That happens if the government wins the other case; otherwise the petition will be denied, probably in mid-2027.
