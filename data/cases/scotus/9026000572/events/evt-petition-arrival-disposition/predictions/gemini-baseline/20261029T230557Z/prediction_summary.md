We predict that the Supreme Court is likely (about a 68% chance, or roughly two in three) to grant this petition through a summary "grant, vacate, and remand" (GVR) order, which sends the case back to the lower court without full briefing or oral argument. The federal government is appealing a Seventh Circuit ruling that allowed an undocumented immigrant arrested inside the country to seek release on bond rather than face mandatory detention. Because the Supreme Court already agreed on October 1, 2026, to decide that exact legal question in a lead case from the Second Circuit (*Rhoney v. Barbosa da Cunha*), it will hold this petition until *Barbosa da Cunha* is decided and will likely vacate the Seventh Circuit's ruling if the government prevails in the lead case.
