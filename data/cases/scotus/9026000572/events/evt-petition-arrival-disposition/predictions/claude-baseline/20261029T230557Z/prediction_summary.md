The federal government is asking the Supreme Court to reverse a ruling that an immigrant living in the U.S. without lawful entry is entitled to a bond hearing instead of mandatory detention. The Court already agreed on October 1 to decide this exact question in another case. So this petition will probably be set aside until that case is decided, then sent back to the lower court if the government wins or turned down if it loses. We put the chance of some form of grant at about even (52%).
