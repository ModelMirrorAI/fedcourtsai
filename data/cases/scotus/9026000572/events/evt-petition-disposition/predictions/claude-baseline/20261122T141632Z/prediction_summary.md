The government is asking the Supreme Court to put this immigration-detention case on hold until it decides a nearly identical case it has already agreed to hear. If the government wins that lead case, the Court will most likely grant this petition, wipe out the lower-court ruling and send it back for a fresh look (a "GVR"). If the government loses, the petition will be denied. A grant is somewhat more likely than not, at about three in five, with no decision expected before spring 2027.
