The Supreme Court will very likely refuse to hear this case (about a 6% chance it takes it). President Trump and his lawyers ask the Court to revive his lawsuit over the "Russia collusion" accusations and to erase nearly $1 million in court-imposed penalties. The Court asked the other side to respond, which shows it is paying attention. But the questions are narrow and tied to this case's facts, and no lower courts disagree on them, so review is still unlikely.
