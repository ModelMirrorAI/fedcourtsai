# Forecast — Trump v. Clinton, No. 26-518

The docket shows one distribution (for the Nov 6 2026 conference) and a response called for on Oct 27, due Nov 27 2026. No relist and no CVSG yet. Once the response and any reply are in, the petition will be distributed again, most likely for a conference in December 2026 or January 2027. It will then be **denied**, probably at that first post-response conference. I expect no relist, or at most one. No CVSG will issue, because the Solicitor General already appears as counsel for the federal respondent.

If the Court does act, the likelier target is QP2, the inherent-authority sanctions for advocating the tolling theory. A summary vacatur of the sanctions, with no argument, is a real share of that small grant probability. Plenary review of QP1 (presidential equitable tolling of a President's own claims) as written is less likely. No intervening decision supports a GVR.

On denial, I expect most likely no separate writing. If one appears, it is most plausibly a short statement from Justice Thomas, Gorsuch or Alito about the sanctions.
