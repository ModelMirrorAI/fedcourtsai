# Forecast of the Court's treatment

The Court will deny the petition after receiving the requested response or expiration of the response period. It will most likely issue an unexplained order, without a dissent or statement. Such an order will not announce a merits ruling on presidential tolling or approve the reasoning below.

The baseline shows one distribution and no CVSG. Expect one further distribution after response-stage briefing, followed by denial at the ensuing consideration, rather than several substantive post-conference relists. The probability of any additional recorded distribution is 99%; that includes ordinary redistribution caused by the response request and is not a prediction that the Court already considered and relisted the petition at the November 6 conference.

The Court will not call separately for the Solicitor General's views; the probability of a new CVSG is 2.5%. The United States' participation as a respondent gives its counsel an ordinary opportunity to address the petition without that procedure.

If review is granted, the likelier question is a narrowed version of Question 2: whether advocacy of the presidential-tolling theory may contribute to an inherent-power bad-faith determination and resulting fee award, and whether the remaining grounds independently sustain that award. The broader request for term-long tolling in Question 1 is less likely to be taken alone. Conditional on review of that question, the dispute would concern how Article II responsibilities enter the existing equitable-tolling test, not presidential criminal immunity.

Conditional on any grant, plenary briefing and argument are more likely than resolution in the grant order (65% versus 35%). The principal summary alternative is vacatur and remand for reconsideration of sanctions, as requested in the petition, rather than a GVR tied to an identified intervening ruling. No question is predicted to be taken unconditionally.

Conditional on denial, the chance of any dissent or statement is 8%. If one appears, Thomas or Alito is the most plausible individual writer in this forecast, addressing presidential accommodation or the treatment of constitutional advocacy. The central prediction remains a silent denial, not an identified Justice's writing.
