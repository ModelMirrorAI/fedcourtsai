The Supreme Court is very likely to turn down Donald Trump's request to review this case; I put the chance of review or another grant-based intervention at about one in ten. The case asks whether presidential duties pause deadlines for private lawsuits and whether arguing for that protection can support financial penalties. Requesting a response shows some interest, but the lower court also relied on other problems with the lawsuit. Those additional grounds make this a difficult case for resolving the broader presidential questions.
