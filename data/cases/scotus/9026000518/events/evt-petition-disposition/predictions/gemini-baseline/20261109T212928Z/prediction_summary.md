The Supreme Court is very likely to deny President Donald Trump's petition for review, with only a 4% chance (about one in twenty-five) of granting the case. Trump and his lawyers ask the Court to revive his dismissed civil racketeering lawsuit against Hillary Clinton and others over 2016 Russia-collusion allegations and to overturn nearly $1 million in court sanctions—penalties for filing an improper lawsuit—by arguing that the President's constitutional duties paused the four-year filing deadline while he served in office. Although the Court asked the respondents to file a formal response, review remains unlikely because the lower courts also dismissed the claims and upheld the sanctions on several independent grounds, including procedural forfeitures and filing baseless claims that Trump did not appeal.
