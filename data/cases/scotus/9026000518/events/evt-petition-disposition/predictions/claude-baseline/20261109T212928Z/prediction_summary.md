President Trump asks the Supreme Court to revive his lawsuit against Hillary Clinton and others and to overturn nearly $1 million in court-imposed penalties ("sanctions") against him and his lawyers. We think the Court will very likely turn the case down. We estimate about a 6% chance it agrees to hear it. The Court did ask the other side to file a response, which signals some interest. But the petition points to no disagreement among lower courts. The case also turns largely on its own particular facts, and the Court rarely takes cases like that.
