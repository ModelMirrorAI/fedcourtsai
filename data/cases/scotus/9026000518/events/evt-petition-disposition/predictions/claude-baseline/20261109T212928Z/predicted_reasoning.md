# Forecast — No. 26-518, Trump v. Clinton

- **State:** one distribution so far (conference of 11/6/2026). The respondents waived, and the Court then requested a response, due Nov 27, 2026. There have been no relists and there is no CVSG.
- **Relist:** The petition will be redistributed after the response and reply come in, probably for a December 2026 or January 2027 conference. I expect it to be decided at that first post-response conference, or after at most one further relist.
- **CVSG:** None. The United States is already a respondent, represented by the Solicitor General.
- **Disposition:** Cert denied.
- **If granted:** The Court would more likely take QP2 (sanctions for advocating a novel constitutional argument) than QP1 (presidential tolling). QP1 is entangled with an alternative merits dismissal.
- **Summary route:** A summary vacatur and remand on the sanctions is a meaningful share of the grant scenarios, roughly 30%. Plenary review is still the likelier route if the Court acts at all.
- **Separate writing on denial:** Most likely none. A short statement respecting denial, from Thomas or Gorsuch on the sanctions point or from a Justice responding to the petition's framing, is the main alternative.
