## Anchor and adjustment

The matching sal-v4 **baseline reached** rate is the appropriate anchor for this private, paid petition. Pooling every displayed prior-Term row, 2017 through 2025, gives **638 grant-family outcomes / 12,720 denial-reweighted resolved petitions = 5.016%**. I used the unrounded prefix rates and weighted denominators in metrics/statpack.json, not an unweighted average of percentages, the terminal baseline rate, or Term 2026. The segment table is the primary anchor. The broader modern-cert disposition counts imply roughly 2.8% any-grant across their pooled population, and the Ninth Circuit cut is similarly low; neither replaces the selected paid-segment anchor.

I raise the forecast to **18%** for the combination of an affirmative response request after waiver, three separately docketed amicus filings, and a developed challenge to a recurring infringement methodology. The petition identifies competing formulations across several circuits, particularly the Second and Ninth, and links the methodological dispute to summary judgment in this very case. Its vehicle section says substantial similarity was dispositive, factual copying was not disputed, and the conflict was raised in the rehearing petition. Those assertions support review if the opposition cannot undermine them. Sources: provisioned snapshot entries of July 29 and August 5–20; petition pp. 14–25 and 31–32.

The upward adjustment is deliberately limited. I checked Peter F. Gaito Architecture, LLC v. Simone Development Corp., **602 F.3d 57, 63–66 (2d Cir. 2010)** through CourtListener opinion 1331. The Second Circuit allows judicial resolution of substantial similarity, including at the pleadings stage, and filters unprotectable matter while also requiring attention to overall protected expression. Thus the petition identifies a meaningful methodological disagreement, but not a simple division between circuits that always require a jury and circuits that never do. This inference weakens an overly broad Seventh Amendment framing without disproving the narrower holistic-comparison question.

Vehicle uncertainty also matters: the source article is nonfiction, and similarity in aviation facts, practices, or general themes need not establish appropriation of protected expression. The petition itself recounts that the courts addressed selection and arrangement and found the necessary protected similarity absent. A different verbal formulation might therefore leave the result unchanged. These are risks inferred from the petition, not arguments I attribute to an unread opposition. Sources: petition pp. 7–14, 23–24, and 31–33.

Finally, the petition reports en banc rehearing in **Sedlik** and expressly requests a hold as an alternative (pp. 29–30 n.7). Further circuit consideration could make immediate Supreme Court intervention less attractive or prolong this petition. I have not independently verified Sedlik's current status and use only the procedural possibility disclosed in the petition. The absence of the opposition is the main reason not to place a much larger grant probability on petitioners' split description.

## Other claims and stakes

The paid-segment terminal relist table is heavily concentrated at zero relists, while the CVSG table records only 173 such petitions among 13,997 total cases. Those cuts describe terminal populations, not forward transition probabilities. In particular, they do not justify a low redistribution probability here: the requested response deadline postdates the initially scheduled conference. **94%** for another distribution is a procedural inference, not a claim of a 94% serious merits-relist signal. Nothing in the record establishes actual consideration at the September conference.

The **8% CVSG**, **12% summary route conditional on grant**, and **4% separate writing conditional on denial** are judgmental forecasts, not measured hazards from a matching cohort. There is no identified intervening Supreme Court decision making summary treatment the natural route. The long-lived split and lack of a clear justice-specific signal favor an ordinary unexplained denial over a separate writing.

The **0.65 significance score** reflects the reach of the proposed copyright methodology and its implications for creators, studios, and adjudication of infringement, with additional visibility from the film franchise. It is not the grant probability and does not assume petitioners' asserted economic statistics are established. Missing opposition text and unreviewed underlying works constrain both the merits assessment and confidence in vehicle quality.
