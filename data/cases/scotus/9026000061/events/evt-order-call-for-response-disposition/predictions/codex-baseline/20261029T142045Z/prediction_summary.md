The Supreme Court is likely to leave the lower court's ruling in place, with about an 18 percent chance of granting some form of review. The dispute concerns how judges compare creative works when deciding copyright claims involving Top Gun: Maverick. The Court's request for a response and three supporting briefs suggest interest, but do not promise a hearing. The lower court also addressed protection for combinations of creative elements, which may make this case a less clear example of conflicting legal rules.
