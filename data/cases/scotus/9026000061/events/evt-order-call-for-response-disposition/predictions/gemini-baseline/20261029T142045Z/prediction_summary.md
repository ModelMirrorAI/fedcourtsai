The Supreme Court is very likely to deny the petition for review in *Shosh Yonay v. Paramount Pictures Corporation*, with only about a one-in-seventeen chance (6%) of agreeing to hear the case. Although the Court asked Paramount to file a response to the petition by the heirs of the journalist whose 1983 magazine article inspired the original *Top Gun* film, the lower courts unanimously concluded that the 2022 sequel *Top Gun: Maverick* shares only unprotectable historical facts about the Navy's real fighter-pilot school and general stock themes rather than any original expression from the article. Once Paramount files its response brief highlighting those factual differences, the Court is expected to deny review without any dissenting opinions.
