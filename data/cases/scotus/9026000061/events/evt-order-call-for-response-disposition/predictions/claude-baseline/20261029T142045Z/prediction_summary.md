We predict the Supreme Court will most likely turn down (deny) this petition from the family of the author whose magazine story inspired *Top Gun*, who say *Top Gun: Maverick* copied it — we put the chance the Court agrees to hear it at about one in twelve. The case raises a real disagreement among appeals courts about how to judge whether two works are too similar, and the Court asked Paramount to respond. But the Ninth Circuit is already reconsidering its own test in another case, so the Justices will probably wait.
