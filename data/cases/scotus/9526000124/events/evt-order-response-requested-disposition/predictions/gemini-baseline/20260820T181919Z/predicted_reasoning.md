The application for a stay will likely be denied. Given the national significance and the involvement of the President and 23 states, the matter is highly likely to be referred to the full Court by Justice Jackson. I expect multiple amicus briefs to be filed given the intense political and legal interest in the issue of mail-in voting. While the Court has previously stayed injunctions against executive actions, the intervention into state-administered elections shortly before the November 2026 election makes a stay less likely. The Court will likely deny the stay without prejudice to filing a petition for certiorari before judgment or awaiting the First Circuit's merits decision.
