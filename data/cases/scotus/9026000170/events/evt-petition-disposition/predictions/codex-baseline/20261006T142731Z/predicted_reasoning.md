# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny certiorari after the requested response process concludes. The central timing forecast is November or December 2026, rather than an inference of disposition from the September 28 conference date printed in the earlier distribution entry.

The baseline contains one distribution and no CVSG. I expect one additional distribution after briefing and then denial, with an 85% probability of at least one distribution beyond the frozen count. Because responses were requested after the initial distribution and the government's response is due October 28, that additional distribution will most likely reflect completion of briefing, not a second substantive conference deliberation demonstrating grant interest.

The Court will not separately call for the Solicitor General's views; probability of a new CVSG is 1%. The United States is already a respondent with a requested response outstanding, so the expected government input arrives through ordinary party briefing.

The most likely denial leaves the patent-review ruling undisturbed without a reasoned Supreme Court merits opinion. The forecast is that the dispute will not overcome the combination of the institution-review bar asserted in the opposition, the rescinded agency policy, and the case-specific disputes over the practical significance of the omitted RPI determination. This is an explanation of the predicted selection decision, not a claim that a denial will announce agreement with Qualcomm or resolve the statutory issue.

If the Court grants review, it will most likely take the stated reviewability question: whether section 314(d) forecloses review of the Patent Office's refusal to determine compliance with section 312(a)(2)'s identification requirement. It will not take the underlying patentability questions as the principal issue. Plenary briefing and argument is more likely than disposal in the grant order; the conditional probability of a summary route is 25%. A corrective remand is the main summary possibility if the government's eventual position supports one, not an assumed outcome or an existing concession.

If review is denied, I expect no noted dissent or statement respecting denial; the conditional probability of some such writing or notation is 6%. I make no prediction of individual cert votes or authorship.
