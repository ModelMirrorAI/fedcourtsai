We expect the Supreme Court to turn down FedEx's request to hear this patent case — about a 6% (roughly one in seventeen) chance it agrees to take it. FedEx says the Patent Office wrongly refused to decide whether Qualcomm hid a partner company in its challenge to FedEx's patents, and that courts should be able to fix that. The Court asked the other side to respond, a modest sign of interest, but an earlier Supreme Court decision (Thryv) points strongly against FedEx and the government will oppose.
