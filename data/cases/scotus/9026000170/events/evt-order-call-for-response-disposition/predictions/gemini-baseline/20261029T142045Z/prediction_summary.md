The Supreme Court is unlikely to grant Federal Express Corporation's petition for review, with about a one-in-eight chance (12% probability) of taking the case, and will most likely deny it. In this patent dispute against Qualcomm, the Patent Office refused to decide whether Qualcomm's challenge named all closely related companies ("real parties in interest") as required by statute, and the appeals court held that federal law bars courts from reviewing that refusal because decisions to start patent reviews are final and nonappealable. Although the Court asked Qualcomm and the federal government to file responses—showing initial interest after the Patent Office admitted its past practice was mistaken—the agency has already changed its policy going forward, there is no disagreement among different appeals courts, and FedEx refused Qualcomm's earlier offer to add the related company to its filings, making Supreme Court intervention unlikely.
