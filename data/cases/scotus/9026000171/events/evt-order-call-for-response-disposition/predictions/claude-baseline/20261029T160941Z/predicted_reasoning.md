# Forecast — Galesburg CUSD No. 205 v. N.T., No. 26-171

**Disposition: denied.** The Court will deny certiorari after the called-for
response (due October 28, 2026) is filed, most likely at a November 2026
conference.

**Relists.** The docket shows one distribution (for the October 9, 2026
conference), followed by the September 28 response request, which took the
petition off that conference. I expect it to be distributed again once the
brief in opposition is in — near-certain — and then denied at its first
post-response conference with no further relist. A second relist after that
distribution is unlikely (roughly one in seven).

**CVSG.** Unlikely but live. The United States is not a party, though the
Department of Education administers the IDEA, and the Court has invited the
Solicitor General's views in IDEA petitions before. I put a CVSG at about one
in ten; if one issues, it would come at the first post-response conference.

**Question taken, if granted.** The Court would take the question presented as
written — whether § 1415(i)(2)(C)(ii)'s "additional evidence" clause lets a
district court rely on after-the-fact evidence and decide the placement de
novo, displacing Rowley's "due weight" to the hearing officer. It would not
take up the least-restrictive-environment test split the petition argues in
its later sections. That split is better developed but is not the question
presented.

**Route.** If granted, plenary review rather than a summary disposition: no
intervening decision calls for a GVR, and the question is a statutory one that
needs briefing.

**Separate writing.** On a denial I expect no dissent or statement respecting
denial.
