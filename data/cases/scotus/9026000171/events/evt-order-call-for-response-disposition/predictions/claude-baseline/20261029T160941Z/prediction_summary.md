A school district in Illinois asks the Supreme Court to decide how much a
federal judge must defer to a state hearing officer in special-education
disputes, and whether the judge may rely on new evidence about how the child
fared later. The Court asked the family to respond, which is a mild sign of
interest. We still think the Court will very likely decline to hear the case,
giving it about an 8% (roughly one in twelve) chance. The main reason is that
the dispute is tied closely to one child's specific facts.
