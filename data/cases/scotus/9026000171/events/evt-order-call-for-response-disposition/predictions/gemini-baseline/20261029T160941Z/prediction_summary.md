The Supreme Court is very likely to deny the school district's request to hear this case, with about a one-in-twenty (5%) chance of granting review. Although the Court asked the student's parents to file a formal response—showing initial interest in how much deference federal judges owe state hearing officers under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (the federal special-education law)—the school district failed to object in the trial court to admitting most of the new post-hearing evidence, making this a poor vehicle for setting a national rule.
