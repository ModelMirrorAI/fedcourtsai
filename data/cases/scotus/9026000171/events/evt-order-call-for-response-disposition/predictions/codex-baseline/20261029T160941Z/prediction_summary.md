The Supreme Court is very likely to decline this school district's appeal; I give it about a one-in-eight chance of accepting the case or otherwise granting relief. The dispute concerns how much respect judges must give special-education hearing decisions when parents present newer evidence about a child's needs. The Court's request for a response shows interest, but the case also turns on individual facts and possible limits on which objections were preserved. The likeliest result is a denial after further briefing, without a written explanation.
