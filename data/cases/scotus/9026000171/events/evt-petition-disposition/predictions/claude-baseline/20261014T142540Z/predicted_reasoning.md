# Forecast — Galesburg CUSD No. 205 v. N.T. (No. 26-171)

**Disposition: denied** (P(grant) ≈ 0.06).

**Where the docket stands.** One distribution so far (for the October 9, 2026
conference), followed on September 28 by a Court-requested response, due
October 28, 2026. No CVSG.

**Relists.** The petition will be distributed again once the brief in
opposition is in (or the deadline passes) — expect a single further
distribution, for a mid-to-late November or early December 2026 conference,
and a denial at that conference without further relisting. A second relist
past that (the real pre-grant signal) is unlikely (~10%).

**CVSG.** Not expected. IDEA is a federally administered program and the Court
has occasionally invited the SG in IDEA cases, but this petition is a
fact-bound placement dispute decided in an unpublished opinion; the more likely
route is to read the BIO and deny.

**Question the Court would take, if any.** The QP as written — whether
§ 1415(i)(2)(C)(ii)'s "hear additional evidence" permits a district court to
rely on post-hearing events and so decline Rowley "due weight" to the hearing
officer. The petition's second theme (the four competing circuit tests for the
least-restrictive-environment mandate) would likely be left aside or folded in
only as context.

**Summary disposition.** Unlikely: no intervening decision bears on the
question, and a per curiam reversal of an unpublished, fact-heavy affirmance is
not plausible.

**Dissent from denial.** Not expected.
