# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely **deny certiorari**, after obtaining the requested response and returning the petition to its conference calendar. The probability of any grant, including a GVR or other summary grant, is **12%**.

The record already contains **one distribution**, entered September 23 for the October 9, 2026 conference. The later response request sets an October 28 deadline. I expect **one further distribution** after briefing and then denial, most likely during November or December 2026; extensions could push disposition into early 2027. The probability of at least one additional distribution is **96%**. That expected redistribution is principally a consequence of completing briefing, not a prediction that the Court has already substantively considered and relisted the case. Multiple further substantive relists are not my central forecast.

I expect **no call for the Solicitor General's views**; the probability of a new CVSG is **5%**. If an invitation occurs, the natural point would be after the response and renewed consideration, extending the timeline beyond this ordinary denial forecast.

If the Court grants review, it will most likely take the statutory due-weight question in a narrower, preserved form: whether material evidence received in judicial review of an IDEA administrative decision permits reducing deference to that decision, particularly when the evidence concerns later developments. I do not expect a separate grant to select a comprehensive least-restrictive-environment test. The limited preservation of an admissibility challenge makes a categorical ban on receiving later evidence a less likely framing than the weight owed once evidence is in the record.

Conditional on a grant, I assign **8%** to disposition in the cert order itself and **92%** to a route requiring subsequent consideration. Plenary review is the more likely grant route because resolving this deference issue would call for explanation of the statutory review framework; the supplied record identifies no intervening ruling making a GVR the natural disposition.

Conditional on denial, I assign **4%** to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. My central forecast is a bare denial with no separate writing. That would leave the lower judgment in place without endorsing its reasoning or revealing the Justices' individual certiorari votes.
