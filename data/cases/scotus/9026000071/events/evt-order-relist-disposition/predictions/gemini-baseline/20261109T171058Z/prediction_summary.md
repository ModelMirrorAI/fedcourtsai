The Supreme Court is likely to deny review in *The GEO Group, Inc. v. Ferguson*, with about a one-in-four chance (26%) of granting the petition. Although the case raises a major constitutional dispute over whether Washington State can impose health, safety, and inspection rules on a private company operating a federal immigration detention center—an issue that split the federal appeals court and led the Supreme Court to relist the case for a second conference—the appeals court has not yet made a final ruling and instead sent the case back to the trial court for further proceedings. Because a related case involving the same facility (*GEO Group v. Nwauzor*) is already before the Supreme Court after a final judgment, the Justices are likely to turn down this premature appeal and wait for the lower courts to finish their work.
