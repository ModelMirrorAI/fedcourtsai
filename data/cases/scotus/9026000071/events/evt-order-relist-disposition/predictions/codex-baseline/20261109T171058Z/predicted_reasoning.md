# Forecast of the Court's disposition

The Court will most likely keep this petition pending while addressing the related federal-contractor immunity question in GEO Group v. Nwauzor, then grant, vacate, and remand for reconsideration in light of that decision. A GVR sends the case back without deciding this petition's merits after separate argument. It would not itself establish that every challenged Washington regulation is invalid.

The baseline shows **three distributions**, including one pre-conference reschedule and the first genuine relist after the November 6 conference. I expect at least one additional distribution beyond those three, most plausibly one or two more around companion-case consideration or a later disposition following a hold. A long hold need not produce a distribution every conference. P(at least one additional distribution) is 0.72.

The Court will probably not issue a separate call for the Solicitor General's views here: the federal government has already supplied its position on the related question in Nwauzor. P(a new CVSG here) is 0.04.

If the Court instead grants plenary review of this petition, it will most likely focus on whether a State may impose discriminatory or functionally controlling regulation on a federal immigration-detention contractor, rather than recognizing blanket immunity from ordinary state health and safety laws. The petition's question as written supplies that issue; the Court may narrow it to the intergovernmental-immunity standard and appropriate comparison facilities, leaving freestanding preemption issues for remand.

Conditional on any grant, a summary route is more likely than separate plenary review: 0.70, principally a companion-driven GVR rather than immediate summary reversal. That forecast depends on a future relevant ruling, not an assertion that an intervening controlling ruling already exists.

If review is denied, the most likely order is unexplained and without separate writing. Conditional on denial, there is a 0.17 chance of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial; Thomas or Alito is the most plausible author if one appears, with Gorsuch another possibility. No majority opinion accompanies the forecast denial alternative.
