We think it slightly more likely than not (about 55%) that the Supreme Court will take up GEO Group's challenge to Washington's health-and-safety law for its Tacoma immigration detention center. That includes a "GVR": the Court sends the case back to the lower court to reconsider after deciding a related case. That related GEO case against Washington is being handled on the same conference schedule, and the federal government has urged the Court to hear it. The main risk is that this case is still at an early, preliminary stage, so the Court could hear only the related case.
