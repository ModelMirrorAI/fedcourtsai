# Forecast — No. 26-71

- **State:** distributed three times (10/16 then rescheduled, 11/6, and 11/13). The Nov 9 distribution for 11/13 is the first true relist. No CVSG on this docket.
- **Disposition:** the Court acts on this petition in tandem with GEO Group v. Nwauzor, No. 25-828, which is relisted on the same schedule after the SG recommended a grant. The most likely single outcome is that review is granted, either now or as a GVR after Nwauzor is decided. A denial is the main alternative, most likely if the Court takes only Nwauzor and finds this interlocutory vehicle too unsettled.
- **Further relists:** probably none past 11/13 if the Court grants. Another relist is plausible (about 30%) if a Justice is writing or the Court is still sorting the pair. A hold would produce no further distribution entries until Nwauzor is decided.
- **CVSG:** none expected. The United States has already given its views in the companion case.
- **QP:** if granted, the Court takes the QP as written: whether intergovernmental immunity bars a state from regulating a federal contractor's operation of a federal detention facility while exempting the state's own facilities. The direct-regulation and discrimination prongs would be addressed together with Nwauzor, and the preemption theory would likely be left aside.
- **Summary route:** if this petition is "granted," there is a substantial chance (about 40%) it comes as a GVR in light of a Nwauzor merits decision rather than plenary review.
- **Dissent from denial:** if denied outright now, a writing from Justice Thomas or Justice Alito is possible, about 25%.
