The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Ortiz-Colón v. United States*, with only a 4% chance of granting the petition. After being convicted in Puerto Rico of both enticing minors online and producing child pornography, Francisco Ortiz-Colón argued under the Constitution's Double Jeopardy Clause—which forbids punishing a person twice for the same crime—that those two charges overlapped. Although the Court asked the federal government to file a response, every appeals court to consider the issue agrees the two statutes require proving different facts, and Ortiz-Colón failed to raise this objection at trial.
