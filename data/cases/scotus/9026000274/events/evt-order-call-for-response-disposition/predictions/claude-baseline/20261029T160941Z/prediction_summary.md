We expect the Supreme Court to turn this case down, and put the chance it agrees to hear it at about 1 in 20. Mr. Ortiz-Colón argues that being convicted and punished under two federal sex-crime laws for the same conduct violates the constitutional ban on being punished twice for one offense (double jeopardy). The Court has asked the government to answer the petition. That is a small sign of interest, but most cases where the Court does this are still rejected. Nearly every appeals court to rule on the question has decided it against his view.
