# Forecast

The Court will most likely deny certiorari after obtaining the United States' response. My probability of any grant, including a GVR or summary reversal, is **12%**. A denial will most likely be an unexplained order, not a ruling endorsing the First Circuit's substantive analysis.

The snapshot records **one distribution**, on September 23 for the October 9, 2026 conference, followed by an October 1 request for a response due November 2. It records no CVSG. I expect **one further distribution** after the response and opportunity for a reply, then denial without an extended sequence of additional conferences. The probability of at least one additional distribution is **93%**. That forecast concerns the declared count increment: redistribution after requested briefing need not represent a substantive relist after conference consideration. The elapsed October 9 conference date does not establish that the petition was considered or disposed of.

I expect disposition in late November or December 2026, with a response extension or further consideration potentially moving it into early 2027. This is a forecast, not a verified future calendar entry.

The Court will not separately call for the Solicitor General's views; the United States is already the respondent whose response was requested. The probability of a new CVSG is **0.2%**, distinct from the response request already recorded.

If the Court grants plenary review, I expect a question focused on whether section 2251(a), when supplying the chargeable offense for section 2422(b), becomes a lesser-included offense for cumulative-punishment purposes. The elements-versus-means issue would supply the necessary statutory analysis rather than occasion a broad reconsideration of all double-jeopardy doctrine. Conditional on any grant, I assign **15%** to disposition in the cert order itself and **85%** to plenary review: the disputed relationship between predicate elements and the two offenses calls for developed briefing, not merely correction under an identified post-judgment decision.

Conditional on denial, I assign **6%** to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. I expect none and do not forecast an individual Justice's private cert vote.
