The petition was distributed once, but the Court subsequently called for a response (CFR) on October 1, 2026, after the government initially waived its right to respond. Because this removes the petition from the October 9 conference, I expect it will be distributed at least once more after the response is filed. The Court will not call for the views of the Solicitor General because the United States is already a party. 

The petition presents a 4-1 circuit split on whether convictions under 18 U.S.C. § 2422(b) and 18 U.S.C. § 2251(a) based on the same conduct violate the Double Jeopardy Clause under *Blockburger*. If the Court grants review, it would likely grant plenary review on the first question presented to resolve the circuit split, rather than issuing a summary disposition. Should the Court deny review, a dissent from denial is possible given the clear circuit split and the liberty interests at stake (mandatory minimum sentences), but it is more likely the Court will simply deny without a separate writing.
