A circuit split over federal criminal law (especially involving heavy mandatory minimum sentences) is a classic reason to grant certiorari. However, the Solicitor General's initial waiver suggests the government believes the split may not be deep enough to warrant review or that there are vehicle problems, tempering the probability.
