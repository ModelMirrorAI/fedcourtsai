# Predicted course: Rubicon Real Estate Holdings v. City of Pontiac (No. 26-359)

**Disposition: denied**, most likely on the order list after the November 6, 2026 conference.

- **Relists.** The docket shows **one distribution** (Oct 14, 2026, for the 11/6/2026 conference) and no relists yet. I expect **no further distribution**: the respondents waived their response, and the petition will be denied at its first conference without a response being called for. The one path to a second distribution is the Court calling for a response, which I put at about 5%. Even if that happens, I expect a denial afterwards.
- **CVSG.** None. No federal party, statute or program is involved. This is a § 1983 suit against a Michigan city and its clerk.
- **Which question, if granted.** None of the five questions presented states a legal question. Each asks whether certiorari is "warranted" because the Sixth Circuit erred on property rights, procedural due process, substantive due process, takings, or First Amendment retaliation. If the Court took anything, it would have to reframe one of them itself, most plausibly the Monell causation/policymaker question the panel used to resolve several of the claims. I do not expect that.
- **Summary disposition.** Unlikely. No intervening decision would support a GVR, and a published, unanimous, argued panel opinion does not invite a per curiam reversal. If there were a grant, I would put a summary route at about 20%.
- **Dissent from denial.** I do not expect a separate writing. A statement respecting denial on the takings or bureaucratic-delay themes is possible but remote (about 1%).
