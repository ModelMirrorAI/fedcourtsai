# Forecast: Odom v. Oklahoma, No. 26A715 (application for a stay)

The record at prediction time shows one entry: the application for a stay, with a motion to file it under seal, submitted to Justice Gorsuch on 16 November 2026. No response has been requested, the application has not been referred to the full Court, and no amicus brief is on the docket.

- **Disposition.** The stay will be denied. The likeliest form is a one-line denial by Justice Gorsuch acting alone as Circuit Justice, with no explanation. A grant in whole or in part is very unlikely (about 2%).
- **Response.** No response will be called for (about 4% that one is). The only respondent to appear on the linked petition, the minor child's counsel, waived a response to the petition itself.
- **Referral.** Justice Gorsuch will not refer the application to the full Court (about 7% that it is referred). The main route to a referral is a renewed application to a second Justice after a denial, which a counselled applicant sometimes files; that would end in a denial by the Court.
- **Amicus briefs.** None will be filed (about 1%).
- **Timing.** Disposed of within about two weeks of submission, most likely within days. The sealing motion will be handled alongside it or on the linked petition.
- **Writing.** No Justice will write on the order.
- **The linked petition (No. 26-502).** Conditional context only: the petition will be denied in the ordinary course after its conference, so the stay would have nothing to preserve.
