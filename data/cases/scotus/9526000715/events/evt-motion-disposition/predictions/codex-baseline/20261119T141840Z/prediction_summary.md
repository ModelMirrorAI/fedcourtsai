Rachel Beth Odom is very unlikely to win a pause in her Oklahoma child-welfare case. If the request still needs a decision, I put her chance of winning even a partial pause at about one in 25. Odom says she missed the first custody hearing because she had no notice and that the judge counted her silence against her while she faced criminal charges. She later participated in hearings, and the state court gave several grounds for its ruling. Those facts make emergency intervention unlikely.
