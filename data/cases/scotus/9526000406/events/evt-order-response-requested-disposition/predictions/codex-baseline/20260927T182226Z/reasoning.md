## Why not near certainty

The final order uses declaratory relief and vacatur, and the First Circuit adds statutory and constitutional-avoidance reasoning (application, pp. 14–15, 19–22, 30–37). Those differences are not erased by the earlier stay. An interim ruling is not a final merits adjudication. Potential persecution or torture following removal is also a serious, potentially irreversible counterweight; the government's assertion that diplomatic assurances eliminate meaningful risk remains contested advocacy. These considerations create genuine room for denial or a mixed disposition rather than a full stay.

The provisioned package contains no opposition, separate lower-court opinions, or appendix despite the application's appendix citations. I therefore know respondents' position principally through the applicant's account; I did not read the cited appendix materials as if they were supplied. The absence of an opposition is consistent with the future response deadline, not evidence that respondents lack an answer. Missing adversarial and underlying-record material is the main reason to discount my confidence. I leave 16% for all outcomes other than an unqualified grant, including denial, mixed relief, withdrawal, or dismissal.
