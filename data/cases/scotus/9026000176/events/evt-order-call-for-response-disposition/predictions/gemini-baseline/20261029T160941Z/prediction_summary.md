The Supreme Court is unlikely to grant review in *Davis v. Smith* (about a one-in-four chance, or 24%, of granting full review or summarily reversing the lower court). In this case, Ohio asks the Court to decide whether a federal court retains power to order a state prisoner's unconditional release after the state has already retried and reconvicted him, based on a finding that the state violated the federal court's earlier order excluding a witness's identification. Although Justices Thomas and Alito previously dissented when the Court declined to hear an earlier appeal in the same prosecution, the Sixth Circuit's decision is unpublished, rests on case-specific findings that the state trial court never formally vacated the first conviction or complied with the suppression order, and involves no conflict among the federal appeals courts.
