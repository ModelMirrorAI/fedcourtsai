Ohio is asking the Supreme Court to overturn a federal appeals court ruling that freed David Smith after his second state conviction. Ohio argues that federal judges lost power over the case once a state court retried and resentenced him. We think the Court will probably turn the case down, putting the chance it takes the case at about one in four. The Court asked Smith to respond, a sign of some interest. Two Justices wanted to hear the case the last time it came up, but the Court declined then too.
