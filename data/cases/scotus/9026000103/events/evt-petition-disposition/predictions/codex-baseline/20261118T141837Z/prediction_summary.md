The Supreme Court is very likely to decline to hear this challenge to the government's seizure of Iranian oil, with about a one-in-forty chance of review. The petition raises an important question about Congress's power over business conducted abroad. But the lower court relied on effects on American oil prices, and the claimed disagreement among courts is less direct than it appears. The unusual way the company arranged its appeal creates another obstacle. Most likely, the Court will deny review without explanation in December.
