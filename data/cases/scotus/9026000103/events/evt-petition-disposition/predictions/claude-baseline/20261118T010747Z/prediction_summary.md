The Supreme Court will very likely decline to hear this case, with only about a one-in-forty chance of taking it. A Turkish oil trader is challenging the U.S. government's seizure of Iranian oil, arguing that Congress's power over foreign trade does not reach deals made entirely overseas. The government opposes review, says the lower courts do not truly disagree on the issue, and points to procedural flaws in the case. No outside groups filed supporting briefs. One Justice has shown past interest and could write separately.
