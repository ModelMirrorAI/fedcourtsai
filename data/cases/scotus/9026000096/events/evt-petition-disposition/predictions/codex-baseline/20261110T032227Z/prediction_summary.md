I predict the Supreme Court will take this case, with about a four-in-five chance of granting review. The federal government asks whether it can exchange land once owned by the Shoshone-Bannock Tribes despite an older law limiting disposal. The ruling could affect federal land management beyond this property. Review is not certain: other legal obstacles remain, and Congress may approve the exchange itself. If that happens, the Court might erase the lower ruling and return the case rather than hear arguments.
