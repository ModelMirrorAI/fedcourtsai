# Forecast — No. 26-96 (with companion No. 26-109)

- **Disposition:** The Court grants review, about two in three. A plenary grant is the most likely form. The alternative is a GVR (Munsingwear vacatur) if Congress ratifies the land exchange in the FY2027 NDAA.
- **Relists:** Distributed once so far (Dec 4, 2026 conference). I expect at least one further distribution or reschedule, more likely than not, because the Court is likely to wait out the NDAA conference in December before acting. A grant in December or January is the modal path.
- **CVSG:** None. The United States is the petitioner.
- **Question taken:** The QP as written: whether FLPMA's disposal authority reaches the ceded Fort Hall lands despite the 1900 Act's "only" clause. The petitions in 26-96 and 26-109 would be granted together and consolidated. The district court's alternative FLPMA-procedure and NEPA grounds would be left aside.
- **Summary route:** Unlikely unless Congress ratifies the exchange. In that case, expect a GVR vacating the Ninth Circuit judgment under Munsingwear.
- **If denied:** A dissent from denial is possible but not expected (about 30%). The most likely writers are Alito or Thomas, possibly joined by others, stressing the federal-land stakes.
- **If granted, likely ground (conditional):** FLPMA and the 1900 Act read as independent, harmonizable grants of disposal authority, with the specific-governs-general and Indian-canon reasoning below rejected.
