The Supreme Court will probably agree to hear this case, about a two-in-three chance. The federal government is asking the Court to decide whether a 1976 public-lands law lets it trade away land an Idaho tribe sold to the United States in 1900. The government wins Court review far more often than other petitioners do, and several appeals judges dissented below. The main obstacles are that no other appeals court disagrees and the lawsuit isn't finished yet. Congress may also settle the dispute through pending legislation.
