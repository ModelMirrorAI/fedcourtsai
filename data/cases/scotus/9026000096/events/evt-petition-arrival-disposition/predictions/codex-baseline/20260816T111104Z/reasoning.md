# Rationale

I assign a 0.72 probability to any grant. This is a forward, Term 2026 arrival cell with a `sal-v3` federal band, a paid docket, zero distributions, and no CVSG. The snapshot is `record/snapshots/2026-08-16.json`.

The leakage-safe unconditional arrival anchor is the weakest band's bracketed `reached` rate over the nine strictly prior Terms: 863 grant-family outcomes among 13,163 weighted resolved paid petitions, or 6.56%. This petition belongs to the separately measured federal arrival class. The matching federal segment over those Terms contains 143 grant-family outcomes among 201 resolved petitions, or 71.14%; its individual Term rates range from 43.5% to 89.5%, so the estimate is strong but noisy. I use that federal rate as the principal anchor. The Ninth Circuit's all-petitioner modern-cert grant-family rate is about 3.3%, but that marginal mixes fee and petitioner classes and should not displace the federal conditioning.

The case-specific evidence roughly balances around the federal anchor. In favor of review, the United States and Solicitor General seek certiorari; the panel was divided; seven judges dissented from denial of rehearing en banc; the question is a focused issue of statutory interaction; and the petition identifies 35 similar statutes covering more than four million federally owned acres. A circuit conflict is structurally difficult where the affected land is concentrated in the Ninth Circuit. Against review, the petition concedes there is no square split, the case arrived through an interlocutory appeal with remedy unresolved, and the district court's alternative FLPMA and NEPA holdings were not addressed by the panel majority. Those grounds may prevent a decision on the question presented from finally resolving the exchange.

The probability of a distribution-count increment is 0.995 because the arrival snapshot starts at zero and this paid federal petition is awaiting a response, not a petition already approaching dismissal. The CVSG increment is 0.001 because the Solicitor General is already counsel for the federal petitioner. Conditional on a grant, I assign 0.10 to a cert-order disposition: intervening interpretive decisions leave some GVR possibility, but the importance and substantial competing arguments favor plenary review. Conditional on denial, I assign 0.04 to any noted dissent or statement because separate writings remain unusual even in important denials.

I used the extracted questions presented and the petition's complete cert-stage argument. `documents.json` marks the 197-page petition text as truncated, but the truncation occurs in the appendix after the petition's conclusion. No brief in opposition was provisioned; the response deadline had been extended to September 21, 2026. The snapshot postdates docketing and includes only non-substantive response-extension activity, with no distribution or CVSG signal. I performed no live retrieval and did not seek the case's outcome.
