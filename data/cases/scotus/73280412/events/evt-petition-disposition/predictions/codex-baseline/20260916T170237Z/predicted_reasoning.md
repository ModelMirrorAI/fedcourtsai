# Forecast

The Court will grant the petition, vacate the Ninth Circuit's judgment, and remand for further consideration following the intervening redistricting precedent and the linked Soto Palmer litigation. P(any grant) is **0.84**; the predicted disposition is **GVR**, not a plenary grant or a summary ruling that Garcia has established an equal-protection violation.

The snapshot records **three distributions**, most recently for the September 28, 2026 conference, and **no CVSG**. The earlier distributions were interrupted by rescheduling and a request for a response. From this state, the modal forecast is **no additional distribution**, with disposition in early October 2026 following the scheduled conference. P(at least one further distribution) is **0.25**; if the Court needs another conference to coordinate related petitions, one further distribution is more likely than a prolonged sequence. P(a new CVSG) is **0.01**. The Court will use the completed party briefing rather than seek another advisory submission.

Conditional on a grant, **0.97** probability goes to disposition in the cert order itself. Washington expressly requests a GVR tied to reconsideration of Soto Palmer after Louisiana v. Callais, while Garcia continues to seek review of the mootness question. That gives the Court a route to return the jurisdictional question to the Ninth Circuit without resolving the parties' disagreement about continuing racial-classification injury. This forecast concerns reconsideration, not an instruction to find a constitutional violation. Its evidentiary basis is the State's June 2 brief, pp. 1–2, 14–16, 20, and Garcia's June 10 reply, pp. 1–2, 5–7.

If the Court instead sets the petition for plenary review, it will take the mootness question: whether replacement of the challenged legislative district in separate, still-reviewable litigation ends the plaintiff's equal-protection controversy despite an asserted continuing classification injury. It will not treat this petition as a direct vehicle for deciding the constitutionality of all Section 2 remedies. The modal summary route leaves that broader substantive dispute unaddressed.

If review is denied, the forecast is a routine denial without an accompanying statement. P(any noted dissent or statement respecting denial | denial) is **0.12**. No individual Justice's vote or writing is predicted.
