## What happened

Benancio Garcia III, a Hispanic resident of Washington's Legislative District 15, sued state officials claiming the district was drawn as an unconstitutional racial gerrymander. While his case was pending, a different judge, in a separate lawsuit over the same district, ruled that the district violated the Voting Rights Act and ordered the state to redraw it. A three-judge panel then dismissed Garcia's constitutional claim as moot, reasoning that the new district replaced the one he had challenged. The Ninth Circuit affirmed. Garcia's petition says the replacement district still sorts him by race, and in his view more than before, so his injury was never fixed.
