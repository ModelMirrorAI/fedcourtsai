Distributed once so far for the long conference; expect no further relists before a decision on the petition. The QP presents a direct challenge to the Court's precedent in *PruneYard* regarding property rights and compelled speech. While it is a significant issue backed by the Pacific Legal Foundation, the vehicle is from a state court. No CVSG is expected, as the federal government's interest is not directly implicated. If granted, the Court would take the QP to reconsider *PruneYard*. A summary GVR is highly unlikely since there is no intervening decision. Should the Court deny review, there is a reasonable chance of a dissent from denial by Justice Thomas or Justice Alito given their past skepticism of *PruneYard* and interest in Takings/First Amendment issues.
