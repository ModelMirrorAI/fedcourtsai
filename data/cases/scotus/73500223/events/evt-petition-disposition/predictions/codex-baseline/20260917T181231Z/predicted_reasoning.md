# Forecast

The Court will deny certiorari in Majestic Realty Co. v. Salazar. The modal path is an ordinary denial without a separate writing following the conference scheduled for September 28, 2026, with disposition in late September or early October. The snapshot records one distribution and no CVSG. I expect zero additional distributions; the probability of at least one additional distribution is 22%. If the Court does return the petition to conference, one or two further distributions are more likely than an extended sequence. I assign only 1% to a new CVSG and expect none.

The petition asks the Court to overturn PruneYard's protection of state-mandated expressive access to private shopping centers under both the Takings Clause and the First Amendment. The likely institutional response is to leave that question for a better vehicle: this appeal concerns preliminary injunctive relief, the parties dispute the adequacy of the factual record, and the petition presents an attack on controlling precedent rather than a demonstrated conflict over the federal rule. This forecasts the Court's screening rationale, not an explanation the denial order will necessarily state. A denial will not adjudicate either constitutional theory.

Conditional on a grant, I expect plenary briefing and argument, not disposition in the grant order: P(summary route | grant) is 4%. No intervening decision requiring reconsideration is identified in the material reviewed. Reconsidering a precedent that the principal later decisions expressly distinguish is a poor fit for summary reversal. If review is granted, I expect the Court to focus on a narrowed, shopping-center-specific takings question: whether compelling access for noncommercial expressive activity appropriates a compensable right to exclude even when the premises are open to customers. The broader First Amendment theory is less likely to be the principal ground. These are conditional forecasts; no merits opinion is expected on the modal denial path.

Conditional on denial, I assign 10% to at least one noted dissent or statement respecting denial. The modal prediction remains no writing, and I make no individual-Justice prediction. The substantial stakes of reconsidering PruneYard do not themselves establish four votes to hear this vehicle.
