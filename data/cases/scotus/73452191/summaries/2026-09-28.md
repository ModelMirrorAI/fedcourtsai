Bernard's lawsuit alleges Ignelzi directed sheriff's deputies to arrest him at home without a warrant, then again jailed him weeks later, both times while related proceedings were on appeal. ## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether a judge who personally directs and oversees police officers carrying out a warrantless arrest at someone's home can still claim absolute immunity from a civil-rights lawsuit, or whether that kind of hands-on involvement in an arrest strips away the immunity judges normally have for their courtroom decisions.

## What each outcome would mean
