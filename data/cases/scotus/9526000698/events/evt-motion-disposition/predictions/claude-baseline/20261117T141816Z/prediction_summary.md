The Supreme Court will almost certainly deny this emergency request (about a one-in-a-hundred chance of any relief). Two Tennessee homeowners, representing themselves, ask the Court to block a foreclosure sale of their house, halt a state misdemeanor case and stop a wage garnishment. The filing does not point to a lower-court ruling raising a legal question the Court would likely review, and requests like this from unrepresented parties are routinely turned down without comment. No response from the other side is expected to be requested.
