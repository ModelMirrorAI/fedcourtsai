The Supreme Court is very likely to decline Shannon Cross's appeal; I put the chance it agrees to review the case at about one in eight. Asking her former employer to respond shows interest, especially because some Justices have questioned the rules courts use to evaluate workplace-discrimination evidence. But the lower court's unpublished decision turns heavily on disputed accident facts and employee comparisons. Those complications make this a less attractive case for deciding a broad legal question. Declining review would not decide whether discrimination occurred.
