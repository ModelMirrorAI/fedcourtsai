## In brief

Cleveland asks the Supreme Court to review a ruling that let a class of Black homeowners sue over its water-lien policy despite some unproven damages.

## What happened

Cleveland Water places liens on properties of customers who fall behind on water bills. A group of Black homeowners sued, claiming the policy disproportionately burdens Black residents in violation of the Fair Housing Act. A federal district court certified a class of affected homeowners to pursue both an injunction and damages. The Sixth Circuit Court of Appeals affirmed, ruling that all class members had suffered a legally sufficient injury, including the dignitary harm of racial discrimination and the lien itself, even though Cleveland says some homeowners paid no penalties or interest. Cleveland then asked the Supreme Court to review that ruling.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether a court may certify a class seeking money damages when some members of that class may not have suffered any concrete, tangible harm beyond the general claim that a race-neutral policy affected their group disproportionately. The dispute centers on what kind of harm is enough to let someone join a lawsuit and collect damages in federal court.

## Where it stands

Cleveland filed its petition in December 2025. The homeowners waived their right to respond, but the Court later asked for a response, which was filed in April 2026. Cleveland filed a reply shortly after. The petition has been distributed for several conferences since then, most recently in May 2026, and the docket shows no decision yet on whether the Court will hear the case. The parties have since jointly asked the Court to defer considering the petition.

## What each outcome would mean

Cleveland, which lost in the lower courts, is asking the Court to take up the case. If the Court grants review, it would hear full arguments and decide the legal question itself, without yet deciding who wins. If the Court denies review, the Sixth Circuit's ruling stands, and the homeowners' class action, including their damages claims, continues in the lower courts. A grant would not by itself mean either side wins the underlying dispute over the water-lien policy.
