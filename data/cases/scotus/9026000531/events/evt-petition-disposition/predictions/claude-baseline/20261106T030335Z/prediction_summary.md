We expect the Supreme Court to turn down (deny) John Green's request to hear his case — roughly a 1-in-100 chance of it being taken. Green argues his deadline to challenge his federal conviction should have started only when the Court rejected his co-defendant's later appeal, not his own. The issue is narrow, there is no disagreement among lower courts, the government did not even bother to file a response, and the lower court's ruling was an unpublished refusal to allow an appeal.
