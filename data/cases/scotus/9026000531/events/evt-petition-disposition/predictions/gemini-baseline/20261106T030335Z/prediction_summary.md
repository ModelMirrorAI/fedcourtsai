The Supreme Court is very likely to deny John O. Green's petition for review, with less than a one-in-one-hundred chance of a grant (0.5%). After Green and a co-defendant lost a joint criminal appeal, each asked the Supreme Court to review his conviction separately, and the Court denied Green's request two months before his co-defendant's. Green then waited more than a year after his own denial to file a post-conviction challenge under a federal statute with a strict one-year deadline. Because existing law ties that one-year clock to a defendant's own case, no appeals courts disagree on the rule, and the federal government waived its right to respond, the Court will almost certainly decline the case.
