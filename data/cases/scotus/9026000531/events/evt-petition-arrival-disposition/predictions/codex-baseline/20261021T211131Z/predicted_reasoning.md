# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny certiorari without an accompanying opinion or separate statement. The narrow finality distinction and the alternative certificate-of-appealability question will not attract review absent a demonstrated conflict or a clearer departure from the governing standard.

The baseline contains **zero conference distributions**. I expect one future distribution, followed by denial at the first consideration rather than an actual relist. The probability of at least one distribution beyond the baseline is **98%**, which at this arrival moment includes the initial distribution. I expect disposition after the November 20 response deadline, most likely between December 2026 and February 2027, with ordinary scheduling or a response extension capable of moving that window.

I do not expect a call for the Solicitor General's views: **0.2%** probability of a new CVSG. The ordinary participation of the Solicitor General as counsel for the United States, including any opposition or requested response, would not constitute such a call.

The chance of any grant, including a summary grant, is **0.8%**. If the Court takes the case for plenary consideration, the core question will be whether denial of this petitioner's own direct-review certiorari petition starts the Section 2255(f)(1) clock despite his codefendant's separate petition remaining pending. The COA question is derivative; I do not expect the Court to establish that every previously unaddressed factual distinction requires a COA.

Conditional on a grant, a disposition in the cert order itself is more likely than plenary briefing and argument (**60%**). The principal favorable route is a limited corrective remand concerning the COA threshold under Slack, not a broad revision of collateral-review finality. No identified intervening decision makes a routine GVR likely on the current record. If the Court instead grants plenary review, I expect it to retain an individual-judgment approach to finality rather than let a separate codefendant's pending petition extend an already completed petitioner's clock.

Conditional on denial, the probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial is **1.5%**. The modal forecast is an unexplained denial with no separate writing; no individual Justice is assigned a dissent forecast.
