The federal government is asking the Supreme Court to decide whether immigrants held in mandatory detention for a long time are constitutionally entitled to a bond hearing. It wants the Court to hear the case now, before the appeals court rules. We think the Court is very likely (about 4 in 5) to agree to hear it. The Court already agreed to decide this exact question in an earlier case, but that case ended before argument, and this petition is its replacement. The main risk is that this case also ends early, for example if the detainee is released.
