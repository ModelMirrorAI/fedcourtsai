The Supreme Court will likely agree to hear this case (about a three-in-four chance). The federal government asks the Court to decide whether immigrants with criminal records who are held a long time while facing deportation must get a hearing on release on bond. The Court already agreed to decide this question in an earlier case, which ended when that detainee was pardoned. The main doubt is that the appeals court has not ruled yet, and the Court rarely steps in that early.
