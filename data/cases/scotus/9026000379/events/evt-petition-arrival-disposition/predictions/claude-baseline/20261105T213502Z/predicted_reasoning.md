# Forecast — 26-379, Genalo v. D. C.

- **State:** Docketed Sep 21, 2026, with zero distributions and no CVSG. This is a petition for certiorari before judgment from the Second Circuit.
- **Distribution:** After the brief in opposition (due Dec 8, 2026), the petition will be distributed for a conference in late December or January. Expect a grant at the first or second conference. A relist or two is possible while the Court weighs the before-judgment posture or vehicle mootness. Expect a decision on the petition by about mid-January 2027, in time for April argument.
- **CVSG:** None. The United States is the petitioner.
- **Question taken:** The question the Court granted in *Genalo v. Black*, essentially as written: whether a noncitizen detained under §1226(c) for a prolonged period is entitled to a bond hearing, and if so, who bears the burden of proof on flight risk or danger. The Court may add a question on mootness or the vehicle, as it did in *Black*.
- **Route:** Plenary review. A summary disposition is unlikely.
- **If denied:** A denial would most likely rest on the before-judgment posture or a vehicle defect. In that case a statement or dissent from Justice Alito or Justice Thomas is plausible.
- **If granted (conditional):** On the merits, the government's position that the Due Process Clause does not require bond hearings with the burden on the government has real traction with the current majority.
