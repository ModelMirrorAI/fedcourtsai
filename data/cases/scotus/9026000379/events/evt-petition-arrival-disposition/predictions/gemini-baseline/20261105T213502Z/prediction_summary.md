The Supreme Court is very likely (an 88% chance, or nearly nine in ten) to grant the federal government's request for review (`certiorari`, meaning the Court agrees to hear the appeal) in *Genalo v. D. C.* This case asks whether the Constitution requires bond hearings for noncitizens held in mandatory immigration detention for months while their deportation cases proceed. Just three months earlier, in June 2026, the Supreme Court agreed to decide these exact same two questions in *Genalo v. Black*, and when *Black* was dismissed after the detainee received a pardon, the Solicitor General filed this case as its direct replacement.
