## In brief

TitleMax entities ask the Court to review whether Pennsylvania's interest in regulating out-of-state loan transactions justifies keeping their constitutional challenges out of federal court.

## What happened

Pennsylvania's banking regulator accused TitleMax companies of charging illegal interest rates on loans made to Pennsylvania residents, even though the loans were made at stores in other states. TitleMax sued in federal court, arguing the state enforcement action violated the Constitution's limits on states regulating commerce in other states and on asserting authority over companies with no contacts there. The district court dismissed the suits under a doctrine called abstention, which lets federal courts step back from cases touching ongoing state proceedings that serve an important state interest. The Third Circuit affirmed, reasoning that Pennsylvania's general interest in stopping usury was enough.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether a state's broad, general interest in regulating a type of transaction is enough to let that state keep a lawsuit in its own proceedings and out of federal court, when the particular transactions actually happened in other states under those states' own laws. TitleMax argues courts must look more specifically at whether the state's interest holds up once another state's competing authority over the same transactions is considered.

## Where it stands

The petition has been distributed for conference, and the Court asked the respondent to respond after initially receiving a waiver of the right to do so. The response deadline has been extended twice, most recently to November 2, 2026, and the Court has not yet acted on the petition.

## What each outcome would mean

TitleMax, the petitioner, asks the Court to take up the case and reverse the Third Circuit's ruling, which went against TitleMax. If the Court grants the petition, it will hear the case and decide the legal question; granting does not itself decide who wins. If the Court denies the petition, the Third Circuit's decision stands, meaning TitleMax's federal lawsuits stay dismissed and its constitutional challenges must proceed, if at all, within the Pennsylvania enforcement proceeding.
