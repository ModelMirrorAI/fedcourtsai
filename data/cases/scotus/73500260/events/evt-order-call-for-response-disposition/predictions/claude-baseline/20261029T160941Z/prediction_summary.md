The Supreme Court will most likely **turn down** TitleMax's request to hear this case — I put the chance it agrees to hear it at about one in eight (12%). TitleMax wants a federal court, not Pennsylvania's own enforcement proceeding, to hear its constitutional challenge to Pennsylvania's fines over loans made in other states. The Court asking Pennsylvania to respond is a modest sign of interest, and the lender points to disagreement among appeals courts, but the Court rarely takes up this kind of question about when federal courts should step aside.
