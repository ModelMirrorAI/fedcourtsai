The Supreme Court is very likely to deny TitleMax's petition for review, with only about a one-in-twenty-five (4%) chance of granting it. Although the Court asked Pennsylvania regulators to file a response after South Carolina supported TitleMax, the Court rarely grants review where, as here, the lower federal appeals court issued an unpublished, non-binding decision. Moreover, the Third Circuit had already ruled in an earlier case that TitleMax's car-title loans had sufficient ties to Pennsylvania, and a parallel appeal is still pending in the Fourth Circuit.
