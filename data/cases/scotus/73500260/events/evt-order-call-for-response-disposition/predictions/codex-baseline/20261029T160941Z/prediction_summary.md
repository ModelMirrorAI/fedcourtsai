The Supreme Court is likely to turn down these lenders' request for review; I put the chance of review or another grant-based action at 18%, roughly one in six. The dispute concerns whether federal courts must step aside while Pennsylvania pursues lending-law proceedings involving out-of-state companies. The Court's request for a response and an asserted disagreement among appeals courts make review plausible. But the lower ruling is nonbinding precedent, the facts complicate the legal question, and related litigation may provide a better case.
