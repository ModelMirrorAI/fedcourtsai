# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition after receiving the requested response, without a separate writing. The record shows one distribution, on July 15, 2026, for the September 28 conference; the July 29 response request is the last provisioned entry. No response or CVSG is on this record.

Expect one further distribution once the response process is complete, followed by denial without an extended series of actual post-conference relists. The probability of another distribution entry is 0.98, but that includes redistribution after the response request, not necessarily a conference that considers the petition and then holds it over. Allowing for an extension of the response deadline, the modal resolution window is autumn 2026, measured from this July record, rather than a forecast based on knowledge of what subsequently happened. The Court will probably not seek the Solicitor General's views; the probability of a new CVSG is 0.04.

If the Court grants review, it will most likely take the petition's single question in a more neutral formulation: whether Younger abstention's important-state-interest inquiry requires balancing interstate federalism interests when a State brings enforcement proceedings against out-of-state businesses over transactions with its residents. It would not thereby resolve the businesses' underlying Commerce Clause or personal-jurisdiction challenges. On plenary review, the likely focus would be how to reconcile the generic-interest inquiry with limits on abstention, not a wholesale elimination of Younger.

Conditional on any grant, a disposition in the certiorari order has probability 0.30; plenary review is likelier. The strongest summary-route possibility is a hold followed by vacatur and remand if the related TMX litigation identified in the petition becomes a lead vehicle. Its Supreme Court status is not established by the provisioned record, so this is a contingent route, not an observed grant or hold. An immediate summary reversal is less likely because the alleged conflict requires distinguishing precedents and enforcement settings.

Conditional on denial, a noted dissent or statement respecting denial has probability 0.07. The modal forecast is no writing. If a writing appears, Gorsuch, Thomas, or Alito is a more plausible author than the other Justices, given the petition's federal-court-access and interstate-sovereignty themes; this is a weak, unverified author forecast rather than an inference about their actual votes.
