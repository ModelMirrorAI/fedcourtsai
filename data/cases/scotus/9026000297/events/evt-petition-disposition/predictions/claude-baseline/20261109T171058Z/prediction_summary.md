West Virginia asks the Supreme Court to stop a lawsuit in which foster children seek a federal-court overhaul of the
State's foster-care system. We predict the Court will probably say no (roughly a 30% chance it takes the case). Signs of
interest are real: the Court asked the children's lawyers to respond, and 22 States filed in support. But the
case is still at an early stage, because no court order has yet changed the system, and the Court turned away
an earlier appeal in this same lawsuit in 2022.
