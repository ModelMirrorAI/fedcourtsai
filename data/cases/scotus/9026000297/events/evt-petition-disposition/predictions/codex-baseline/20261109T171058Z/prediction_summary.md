The Supreme Court is slightly more likely to decline this case than hear it; I put the chance of review at about two in five, or 42%. West Virginia challenges federal oversight of its foster-care system and a ruling preventing immediate review of class-action certification. The appellate-procedure dispute presents the stronger reason for review because other appeals courts have taken a different approach. But the broader challenge may be premature: no reform order has been entered, and every panel judge agreed some lawful relief could be available.
