We predict the Supreme Court will deny West Virginia's petition for review, with about an one-in-five (18%) chance of a grant. West Virginia asks the Court to block a class-action lawsuit seeking broad court-ordered reforms to the state's foster-care system and to review the lower court's certification of the plaintiff class. Although one appeals judge dissented on whether the appellate court should have reviewed the class-certification order now, all three appeals judges agreed that dismissing the entire lawsuit before trial was premature because no court order managing the agency has actually been issued yet.
