West Virginia asks the Supreme Court to review an appeals-court ruling that let a class-action lawsuit seeking a court-supervised overhaul of the state's foster-care system go forward. I predict the Court will most likely turn the case down, though the chance it agrees to hear it is meaningful: about three in ten. Twenty-two states support West Virginia, and the Court asked the children's lawyers to respond. But the case is still at an early stage, since no court order has yet been entered against the state, and the Court refused an earlier appeal in the same lawsuit.
