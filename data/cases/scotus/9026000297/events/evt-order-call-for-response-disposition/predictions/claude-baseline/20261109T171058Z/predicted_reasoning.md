# Forecast — Morrisey v. Jonathan R., No. 26-297

**Disposition: denied (P(grant) ≈ 0.30).**

- **Distributions.** One so far (for the 11/6/2026 conference), and the Court's Oct 19 call for a response superseded it. The response is due Nov 18, 2026. Expect an extension request (likely granted, about 30 days), then redistribution for a conference in December 2026 or January 2027. If the Court is interested, expect one or two relists before it acts. If not, expect a denial at the first post-response conference.
- **CVSG.** None expected. No federal party is involved, and the issue is the scope of federal-court power over a state agency, which the Court can assess without the Solicitor General.
- **QP if granted.** QP1 (redressability or Article III power to restructure a state agency), possibly reformulated as a question about equitable power or the scope of structural injunctions. The Court would likely leave QP2 (Rule 23(f)/merger) behind, or take it only as a secondary question.
- **Route.** If granted, plenary review. A GVR or summary reversal is unlikely, because no intervening decision bears on the case and the posture is interlocutory.
- **Separate writing.** On denial, a meaningful chance (about 1 in 5) of a statement or dissent. The likeliest authors are Justice Alito or Justice Thomas, possibly Justice Gorsuch, flagging the 4th Circuit's "duty to administer state institutions" language and the interlocutory posture as the reason to wait.
