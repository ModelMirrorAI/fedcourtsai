# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition after receiving the requested response and returning it to a conference. The forecast assigns 43% to some form of grant, so denial is the leading call, not an overwhelming expectation.

The starting docket records **one distribution**, for the November 6, 2026 conference, and an October 19 request for a response due November 18. Expect **one or two additional distributions** before disposition, including the administrative redistribution after the response; another distribution has probability 0.99. A genuine post-consideration relist is plausible, particularly if the Court examines the certification issue, but repeated substantive relists are not the modal path. The forecast does not treat the scheduled initial conference as a completed consideration.

Expect **no CVSG**. The probability of a new invitation is 0.06; if one issues, it would most likely follow a conference after the response and reply cycle, in late 2026 or early 2027.

If the Court grants plenary review, the most likely focused grant is **Question 2**, whether a court of appeals can review a class-certification order on a conditional cross-appeal from a final judgment after reversing that judgment, notwithstanding the unused Rule 23(f) interlocutory route. The Court would likely leave the broader constitutional limits on institutional reform for a case reviewing a concrete remedy. A grant encompassing Question 1 remains a meaningful alternative; its likely focus would be whether some permissible relief is sufficient for redressability rather than whether courts may assume unlimited management of state agencies.

Conditional on any grant, **plenary review is more likely than summary disposition**, 0.87 versus 0.13. There is no identified intervening decision making a routine GVR the natural result. A summary reversal on the appellate-jurisdiction issue is an alternative, not the principal forecast. No unconditional merits opinion or majority author is forecast for a petition expected to be denied.

Conditional on denial, the probability of a dissent or statement respecting denial is **0.16**. The modal denial will have no separate writing. If a Justice writes to highlight the limits of structural injunctions, Thomas is the leading candidate, followed by Alito or Gorsuch; the structured order-writer entries express unconditional individual probabilities, not a forecast of public cert votes.
