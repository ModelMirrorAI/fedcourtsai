The Supreme Court is slightly more likely to decline this case than hear it: I estimate a 43% chance of review. West Virginia challenges federal supervision of its foster-care system and an appeals court's refusal to examine the lawsuit's class certification, which allows children to sue as a group. Disagreement among appeals courts about when that certification can be reviewed makes the petition substantial. But no final reform order has been imposed, and all three lower-court judges agreed some relief could be considered.
