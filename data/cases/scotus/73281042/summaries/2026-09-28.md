## In brief

A bar association member asks the Court to review a ruling that let the group's diversity-based leadership seats survive his discrimination lawsuit under the First Amendment.

## What happened

Rajeh Saadeh, a longtime New Jersey State Bar Association member, sued the Association in state court, claiming its practice of reserving some at-large leadership seats for members of underrepresented groups violated New Jersey's antidiscrimination law. A trial court agreed the practice was an illegal quota. New Jersey's intermediate appellate court reversed without ruling on the state-law question, holding instead that the First Amendment's protection for expressive association shielded the Association's leadership-selection process, relying on the Supreme Court's Boy Scouts of America v. Dale decision. The state supreme court denied further review.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether a private association's free-speech right to shape its own message and membership can excuse it from antidiscrimination law when the association says its selection choices express its values, such as a commitment to diversity, rather than being treated as unprotected discriminatory conduct.

## Where it stands

The petition was distributed for conference after the respondent's brief in opposition and the petitioner's reply were filed, and the Court then invited the Solicitor General to file a brief giving the federal government's views on the case; the docket shows no further action since that invitation.

## What each outcome would mean

Saadeh, as petitioner, asked the Court to take up the case. If the Court grants review and eventually rules for Saadeh, the appellate court's decision favoring the Association would be overturned, reopening his discrimination claim. A ruling for the Association would leave that decision in place. If the Court denies review, the New Jersey appellate ruling favoring the Association stands, without the Court deciding who was right. Because the Solicitor General has now been asked for views, further action awaits that filing.
