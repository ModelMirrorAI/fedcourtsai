The docket shows the petition was distributed twice before the Court called for the views of the Solicitor General (CVSG). Because a CVSG necessarily entails a supplemental distribution once the Solicitor General files their brief, expect at least one further distribution, and likely one or two additional relists thereafter as the Justices weigh the SG's recommendation. No further CVSG will be called for, as one is already on the docket.

The Court will likely grant plenary review. The petition presents a clean, high-profile clash between state antidiscrimination laws and the First Amendment right of expressive association. It functions as a direct sequel to the Court's recent decisions in *SFFA* and *303 Creative*. The lower court's decision to exempt DEI-based leadership set-asides from antidiscrimination mandates on First Amendment grounds is exactly the kind of doctrinal tension the current conservative majority is likely to want to resolve. 

If granted, the Court would likely take the question presented as written to directly address whether "expressive association" protects demographic quotas. A summary disposition is unlikely here because the precise intersection of *303 Creative* and professional organization leadership structures has not been settled by an intervening decision, calling for full briefing and argument. Should the Court deny the petition, expect a dissent from denial (likely from Justice Thomas or Justice Alito) given the high salience of the constitutional and civil rights issues raised.
