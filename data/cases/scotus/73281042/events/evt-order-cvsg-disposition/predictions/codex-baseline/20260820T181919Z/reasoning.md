# Rationale

I assign P(any grant) = 0.36 and predict denial. The committed statpack's paid scored segment gives CVSG petitions a 34.9% grant-family rate (29.4% plenary grants plus 5.5% GVRs). The leakage-safe pooled `high`-band risk-set rate over OT2017-OT2024, the Terms strictly before this OT2025 petition, is approximately 40.9% (weighted n=1,074). Those are the most relevant anchors; the whole modern-cert rate is too low for a petition that has already received a CVSG.

I adjusted upward from the CVSG-only cut for the Court's affirmative request for a response after the initial waiver, four substantial amicus filings, a second distribution, the politically and doctrinally important interaction between expressive association and antidiscrimination law, and the apparent tension with the Eleventh Circuit's published *Fearless Fund* decision. The petition and questions presented frame a clean status-versus-message issue under *Runyon*, *Roberts*, *Dale*, and *303 Creative*.

I adjusted downward from the high-band risk-set rate because the brief in opposition identifies unusually strong vehicle problems. Saadeh did not apply for challenged seats, did not apply while every seat was open during the injunction, and argued below that he need not show a desire to apply. The current policy also makes him eligible through the New Jersey Muslim Lawyers Association; the decision below is unpublished; the purported split turns partly on different facts about expressive activity; and the state-law predicate remains undecided. Even if the Court wants to resolve the broader question, this record may not let it do so cleanly. The CVSG could therefore yield a recommendation to deny on standing or vehicle grounds.

The relist-increment probability is 0.97 because the frozen record already shows the CVSG and two distributions; ordinary processing after the Solicitor General's brief requires a new distribution. The 0.01 CVSG-increment number reflects that a CVSG is already on the docket, making the claim vacuous for this moment, and a second invitation is practically implausible. Conditional on a grant, I put the summary-disposition route at 0.12 because resolving the First Amendment boundary would normally require plenary treatment; a GVR remains possible if the Solicitor General identifies a narrower intervening basis. Conditional on denial, I put a separate dissent or statement at 0.22 because the issue is salient and drew substantial attention, but separate writings from denial remain uncommon.
