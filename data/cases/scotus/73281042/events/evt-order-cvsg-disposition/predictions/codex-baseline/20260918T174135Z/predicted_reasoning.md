# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely **deny certiorari after receiving the Solicitor General's views and considering the petition again**. The probability of any grant is **46%**. A denial will most likely be an unexplained order, leaving the state judgment in place without endorsing its constitutional reasoning.

The baseline already contains **two distribution entries** and the **June 22, 2026 invitation to the Solicitor General**. I forecast at least one additional distribution with **97% probability**, with **one to three further distribution entries** the central path as the SG submission and responsive filings reach conference. This does not mean every such entry will represent a substantive relist. The invitation is already complete; I forecast no new CVSG, and the required CVSG-increment probability is **0%**, vacuous for this moment.

My central disposition window is **November 2026 through June 2027**, following the invited briefing, with the winter of 2026–27 the center of that range. This is a timing forecast from the June record, not a report of developments after it.

If the Court grants review, it will more likely set the case for **plenary briefing and argument** than decide it in the cert order. I assign **14% conditional on a grant** to a summary route, including summary reversal or a GVR. The interaction between expressive-association rights and status-based leadership exclusions requires a distinction among the precedents invoked by both sides; it is not an obvious mechanical application of an intervening decision identified in this record.

If review is granted, I expect the question to be addressed more narrowly than the petition's general formulation about all diversity, equity, or inclusion programs: **whether a private voluntary bar association's expressive-association rights protect demographic eligibility restrictions on its governing positions from an otherwise applicable state antidiscrimination law**. The Court would first need to satisfy itself that Saadeh has a concrete injury and that the changed policy and damages claim leave an appropriate controversy. On reaching the merits, the central dispute would be whether ending the challenged exclusions changes the association's protected message or merely regulates status-based access to leadership. I do not forecast an unconditional merits holding in a case whose most likely disposition is denial.

Conditional on denial, I assign **25%** to at least one dissent from denial or statement respecting denial. The central forecast is **no separate writing**. If one appears, a writing by Justice Thomas or Justice Alito criticizing the breadth of the expressive-association defense would be a plausible form; that attribution is a low-confidence contextual forecast, not a scored prediction of individual cert votes.
