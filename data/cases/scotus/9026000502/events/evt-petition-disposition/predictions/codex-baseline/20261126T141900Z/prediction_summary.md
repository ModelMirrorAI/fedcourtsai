The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Rachel Odom’s petition. I put the chance of review at just over one in a hundred. Odom says Oklahoma used her silence against her in a custody hearing while prosecuting her for the same conduct. She also says she received no notice of an earlier hearing. Those issues matter to families, but Odom identifies no clear disagreement among the highest state courts. The justices’ first conference appears to have concerned permission to file records under seal.
