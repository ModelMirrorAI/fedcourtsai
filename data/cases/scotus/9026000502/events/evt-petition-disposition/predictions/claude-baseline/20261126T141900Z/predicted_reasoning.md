# Forecast — No. 26-502, Odom v. Oklahoma

The docket shows two distribution entries, but only one is a distribution of the petition itself: the 10/9/2026 conference took up the motion to file under seal (granted Oct 13), and the petition is now set for its first real conference on 12/11/2026 with both respondents having waived a response.

- **Disposition.** The Court will deny certiorari, most likely on the order list following the December 11 conference.
- **Further distribution.** No further distribution is expected. The only realistic path to one is a Justice calling for a response from Oklahoma, which would push the petition to a later conference; I put that at roughly one in sixteen.
- **CVSG.** None. No federal interest is implicated in a state child-deprivation adjudication.
- **Question presented, if taken.** Were the Court to act, it would take QP 1 (compelled testimony, adverse inference from invoking the Fifth Amendment, and selective immunity during a parallel prosecution), not QP 2 (whether a later adversarial hearing cures the no-notice emergency custody hearing), which is fact-bound.
- **Summary route.** A grant of any kind is very unlikely; conditional on one, plenary review is somewhat likelier than a summary disposition, though a summary vacatur on the Armstrong v. Manzo notice point is conceivable given that the State filed no answer brief below.
- **Separate writing.** If denied, no noted dissent or statement is expected. If one appeared it would most plausibly come from Justice Sotomayor, Justice Gorsuch, or Justice Jackson.
