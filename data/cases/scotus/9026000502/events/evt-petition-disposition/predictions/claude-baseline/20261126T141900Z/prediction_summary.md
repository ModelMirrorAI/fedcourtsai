The Supreme Court will very likely refuse to hear this case — I put the chance of review at about one or two in a hundred. A mother challenges an Oklahoma ruling that used her silence against her in a child-custody proceeding while she faced a murder charge. Both opposing parties declined to respond, the Court has not asked them to, the lower ruling is unpublished, and the petition itself admits lower courts do not disagree on the issue, which is the usual reason the Court takes a case.
