## Case-Specific Adjustments to `P(grant) = 0.01`
From that ~3% waived first-conference reference point, several case-specific factors push further downward to **`0.01` (1.0%)**, partially offset by sympathetic facts and a serious constitutional framing:

1. **Summary Stay Denial on Companion Application `26A715` (-):** On November 16, 2026, Petitioner applied to Justice Gorsuch for an emergency stay (`26A715`). Corpus lookup (`scotus/9526000715`) and the docket snapshot show that on November 19, 2026 — just three days later — Justice Gorsuch granted sealing but **denied the stay alone**, with `response_requested: false` and `referred_to_court: false`. Although stay relief requires irreparable harm and likelihood of reversal beyond certworthiness alone, a three-day solo denial without requesting a response by the Tenth Circuit Justice most receptive to Fifth Amendment and civil-deprivation due process claims is meaningful negative signal against strong chambers appetite.
2. **Unpublished State Intermediate Decision and Non-Final Dependency Posture (-):** Review is sought of an unpublished opinion of the Court of Civil Appeals of Oklahoma, Third Division (`No. 123,076`), after the Oklahoma Supreme Court denied certiorari 8–1. In `metrics/statpack.json`, `Court of Civil Appeals of Oklahoma, Third Division` has `0` grants out of `5` resolved petitions (`100%` denied; `Supreme Court of Oklahoma` has `2 / 27`, `7.4%`). Worse, the order appealed is an initial *deprived-child adjudication* rather than a final termination of parental rights, while ongoing state proceedings continue below (as evidenced by the stay application), creating serious `28 U.S.C. § 1257(a)` finality and supervening-mootness headwinds.
3. **Vehicle Defects and Overstated Splits on Both Questions Presented (-):**
   - **QP 1 (*Baxter* vs. *Lefkowitz*):** Inspection of *In re Dependency of A.M.F.*, 526 P.3d 32 (Wash. 2023), via CourtListener confirms that state high courts overwhelmingly apply *Baxter v. Palmigiano*, 425 U.S. 308, 317–18 (1976), in dependency cases: the State cannot satisfy its burden *solely* from Fifth Amendment silence (preserving *Garrity* and *Lefkowitz*), but non-dispositive adverse inferences are permitted alongside independent probative evidence. Petitioner manufactures much of her split by conflating *in-court refusal to testify* cases (where state courts uniformly follow *Baxter*) with *compelled therapy admission* cases where parents must admit uncharged crimes out of court as a condition of reunification. Furthermore, even on Petitioner's own statement of the case, the trial court heard live testimony from a DHS supervisor and **in-camera testimony from eleven-year-old E.O.D. herself** describing the altercation and bruise, meaning the adjudication did not rest solely on silence. And on the most jarring evidentiary ruling — treating Fifth Amendment invocations as substantive foundation to admit unauthenticated text messages — COCA held the issue **waived for failure to cite authority under Oklahoma Supreme Court Rule 1.11(k)(1)**, erecting an adequate and independent state-law procedural bar.
   - **QP 2 (Post-Deprivation Cure of Unnoticed Emergency Custody Hearing):** While DHS concededly failed to notify Petitioner of the February 2025 emergency hearing despite holding her in county jail, the split Petitioner cites pits federal § 1983 *damages* cases (holding that a later hearing does not erase nominal or compensatory damages for the initial days of unlawful removal) against state direct appeals refusing to *vacate a subsequent, fully noticed deprivation adjudication* months later. Those two remedial contexts do not genuinely conflict.
4. **Non-Specialist Advocacy and Zero Amici (-), Partially Offset by Stark Facts (+):** Neither side is represented by Supreme Court repeat players (Tulsa private counsel for Petitioner; county ADA for Oklahoma, which waived response), and no amicus filed. Nevertheless, the petition itself is unusually lucid, the Oklahoma Supreme Court vote was 8–1 (Vice Chief Justice Kuehn dissenting), and the combination of conceded zero notice to a jailed mother plus 50+ Fifth Amendment invocations under a trial judge's blanket adverse-inference ruling ("It is, and I can, and I will") creates roughly a ~5% chance that a chambers calls for a response — yielding `P(grant) = 0.01`.
