The Supreme Court is very unlikely to hear *Rachel Beth Odom v. Oklahoma*, with roughly a one-in-a-hundred (1%) chance of granting review and a 99% chance of denying the petition. An Oklahoma mother asks the Court to review an unpublished state appellate decision upholding a temporary child-custody deprivation order after a judge drew negative conclusions from her refusal to answer incriminating questions and after the state failed to notify her of an initial emergency hearing while she was jailed. Although the docket lists two conference distributions, the first was merely a procedural request to seal confidential juvenile records, meaning the petition is reaching the Justices for the first time with both opposing sides having waived a response, zero outside supporting briefs, procedural obstacles in the trial record, and a recent summary denial by Justice Gorsuch of a related request to pause the state case.
