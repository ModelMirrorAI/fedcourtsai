Neither respondent filed a brief in opposition and no Call for a Response (CFR) has issued; the only plausible path to an additional distribution is the roughly 5% chance that a Justice requests a response at or just before the December 11 Conference (plus minor pre-conference reschedule friction).

## Call for the Views of the Solicitor General (CVSG)
The Court will **not** call for the views of the Solicitor General (`cvsg-increment` probability 0.002). The dispute arises entirely out of an Oklahoma Title 10A child-deprivation proceeding and implicates Fourteenth Amendment procedural due process and Fifth Amendment self-incrimination doctrine, with no federal party, federal statute, Indian Child Welfare Act claim, or federal regulatory program at stake.

## Scope of Review and Route Conditional on Grant
If granted (after first calling for a response), the Court would take **Question Presented 1** — likely narrowed to whether the Fifth and Fourteenth Amendments permit a state juvenile court to draw adverse evidentiary inferences when a parent invokes the privilege against self-incrimination during a child-deprivation adjudication while facing parallel criminal charges on the same allegations — and would leave behind **Question Presented 2** (whether a subsequent contested adjudication hearing cures a total lack of notice of an initial emergency custody hearing), which turns on remedial posture and mootness-adjacent state dependency mechanics.

Conditional on a grant, **plenary briefing and oral argument** would be substantially likelier than a summary disposition in the certiorari order (`summary-disposition-route` probability 0.22). No pending merits case on the Court's docket presents the same question (`same-question` matches none), ruling out a routine hold-and-GVR. The 22% conditional summary-disposition share reflects two narrower possibilities if the Court were to grant after a response: a summary per curiam vacatur targeting the conceded lack of emergency-hearing notice or the blanket adverse-inference ruling, or a *Munsingwear* / intervening-event vacatur and remand should ongoing state parental-termination proceedings supersede the underlying deprivation order while the petition is pending. If granted for plenary review on Question Presented 1, the Court would likely reaffirm *Baxter v. Palmigiano*, 425 U.S. 308 (1976), holding that while silence alone cannot automatically forfeit parental custody under *Lefkowitz v. Turley*, 414 U.S. 70 (1973), the Fifth Amendment permits non-dispositive adverse inferences alongside independent probative evidence such as the child's testimony.

## Separate Writings on Denial
Conditional on denial, expect **no dissent from denial or statement respecting denial** (`dissent-from-denial` probability 0.012). The Court virtually never publishes a separate writing on a waived-response petition without first calling for a brief in opposition, and Justice Gorsuch already denied the companion stay application without response or referral. In the unlikely ~1.2% event that a separate writing accompanies a later denial (after a response call), the likeliest authors would be **Justice Sotomayor** or **Justice Gorsuch** (followed by **Justice Thomas** or **Justice Jackson**), writing a brief statement respecting denial that highlights the constitutional tension facing parents prosecuted alongside civil dependency proceedings while noting the unpublished posture and state-law briefing-waiver vehicle obstacles below.
