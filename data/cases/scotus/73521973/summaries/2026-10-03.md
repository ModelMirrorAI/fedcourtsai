## What happened

Jimmy Davis, Jr. was convicted of capital murder in Alabama in 1993 and sentenced to death after a jury recommended death by an 11-1 vote. Davis later argued in state and federal courts that his trial lawyers were ineffective for not investigating and presenting evidence of childhood abuse and the nonviolent details of his only prior conviction. State courts and a federal district court rejected his claims, and a divided Eleventh Circuit panel affirmed, finding Davis had not shown the state court unreasonably applied federal law on the question of whether he was harmed by his lawyers' shortcomings. The full Eleventh Circuit declined to rehear the case, over dissents.

## What the Court is being asked

Davis also asks whether the appeals court's ruling conflicts with the Court's past decisions about when missed mitigating evidence requires a new sentencing hearing.
