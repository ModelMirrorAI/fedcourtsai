Distributed once so far; expect no further relists before a decision on the petition. The questions presented allege a circuit split on whether jury hesitation during the penalty phase must be considered in the prejudice prong of an ineffective assistance of counsel claim under *Strickland*. While it is a capital case from an en banc Eleventh Circuit, the state points to vehicle issues, including waiver below, and the strict deference required by AEDPA.

No CVSG is expected, as the case involves a state prisoner's habeas petition and does not implicate the federal government's interests.

If granted, the Court would likely take the first QP regarding the jury hesitation standard, though a summary disposition (such as a GVR or summary reversal) is unlikely given the need to resolve a circuit split on the merits.

Should the Court deny review, there is a reasonable chance of a dissent from the denial, likely from Justice Sotomayor or Justice Jackson, given the capital context and the en banc division below.
