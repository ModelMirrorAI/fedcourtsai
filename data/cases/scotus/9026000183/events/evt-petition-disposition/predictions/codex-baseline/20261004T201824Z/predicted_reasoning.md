# Forecast

The Court will deny United Airlines' petition for certiorari. The modal path is a routine denial without a separate writing, rather than intervention in the still-developing class-certification proceedings.

The provisioned record shows **one distribution**, entered September 9 for the September 28, 2026 conference, and **no CVSG**. I predict no additional distribution before disposition; the probability of at least one further distribution is **27%**. If the petition receives further consideration, one or two additional distributions are likelier than a lengthy series. My central timing forecast is an October 2026 disposition, with a longer hold as a secondary possibility. The absence of an order in the snapshot is not itself a recorded hold or relist.

I predict no call for the Solicitor General's views: **2%** probability of a new CVSG. The case presents a private-employer class-certification dispute, not a record showing a request for federal-government input.

If the Court grants review, it will most likely take the petition's single Rule 23 question substantially as framed: whether common theory and evidence suffice for commonality and predominance when rejection of that evidence would require individualized liability determinations. Review would focus on classwide proof, individual sincerity disputes, and the proposed class-rostering process, not on a general constitutional right to refuse vaccination.

Conditional on any grant, I assign **25%** to disposition in the cert order itself and **75%** to plenary review. A hold followed by a GVR connected to the religious-versus-secular-belief issue raised in the petition's discussion of Detwiler is the principal summary-route possibility. This is conditional on future developments, not a claim about that other petition's current status. An immediate summary reversal is less plausible than plenary review because the uniform-policy evidence and alternative common issues complicate the asserted Rule 23 error.

Conditional on denial, I assign **4%** to any dissent from denial or statement respecting denial. I expect none and do not forecast an individual Justice's undisclosed cert vote.
