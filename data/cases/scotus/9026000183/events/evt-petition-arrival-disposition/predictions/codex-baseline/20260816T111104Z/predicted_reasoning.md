# Forecast

The Court will most likely deny the petition. The docket shows zero distributions because this forecast is made at arrival. The petition will probably receive an initial conference distribution, making the distribution-count increment true, but it is more likely to proceed to denial after that first distribution than to accumulate multiple relists.

The Court is unlikely to call for the views of the Solicitor General. This is a private employment class action, and the Rule 23 issue can be assessed without the federal government's participation.

If the Court grants review, it will most likely take the question presented substantially as written, perhaps narrowed to whether evidence that can fail differently for different class members supplies a common answer under *Wal-Mart* and *Amgen*. Plenary review is more likely than a disposition in the certiorari order because the petition attacks the Fifth Circuit's method of assessing commonality and predominance. A summary GVR remains a meaningful minority route if the anticipated *Detwiler* proceeding produces an intervening ruling bearing on which class members hold religious rather than secular objections.

If review is denied, no Justice is expected to note a dissent or issue a statement respecting denial.
