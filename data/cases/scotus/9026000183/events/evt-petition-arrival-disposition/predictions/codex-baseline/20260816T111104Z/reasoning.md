# Rationale

I assign a 0.14 probability to any grant and predict denial. The leakage-safe arrival anchor is the `sal-v3` baseline band's bracketed reached rate pooled over OT2017 through OT2025: 863 grant-family outcomes among 13,163 denial-reweighted resolved paid petitions, or 6.56%. This is the correct arrival population for the frozen zero-distribution state; the much lower terminal relist-zero rate is not an arrival prior. I adjust materially upward from 6.56%, but not to an even chance.

The upward adjustment rests on the petition's unusually plausible certiorari features. It presents a published Fifth Circuit class-certification ruling, an adverse separate writing raising Rule 23 and jury-trial concerns, a recurring question about what counts as a classwide answer under *Wal-Mart* and *Amgen*, and claimed disagreement with several circuits. The petition also describes a novel class-rostering procedure and consequences extending beyond the COVID-19 employment setting. The class-certification issue was resolved through a Rule 23(f) appeal, and the petition focuses on a single procedural question.

The adjustment is limited because the record is one-sided. The provisioned documents contain the complete, untruncated petition and extracted question presented, but no brief in opposition and no separately provisioned appendix or lower-court opinion. Several fact-matched conflict authorities described by the petition are district-court decisions, and the appellate cases may reflect applications of settled commonality principles to different records rather than a square rule conflict. The case remains interlocutory, Judge Willett joined the judgment under an abuse-of-discretion standard, and the COVID-era religious-accommodation facts may make the vehicle appear narrower or less durable than the abstract question suggests. The requested alternative hold for *Detwiler* both supports a possible GVR route and suggests that another case could control part of the dispute.

The `relist-increment` probability is 0.97. At this arrival moment the frozen distribution count is zero, so the claim asks whether the petition will ever be distributed, not whether it will be relisted after a conference. An ordinary paid petition that is not withdrawn or dismissed will reach a conference. I expect one initial distribution, with a substantially smaller chance of one or more later relists.

The `cvsg-increment` probability is 0.03 because neither the United States nor another federal entity is a party, and the procedural Rule 23 issue does not ordinarily require the Solicitor General's views. The `summary-disposition-route` probability is 0.28 conditional on a grant. The strictly prior paid-petition statpack route anchor is about 34.8% of grant-family outcomes, but this petition's principal Rule 23 challenge would more naturally receive plenary briefing; the alternative *Detwiler* hold supplies the main GVR path. The `dissent-from-denial` probability is 0.05 conditional on denial: the separate writing and broad class-action stakes create some possibility of a statement, but silent denial remains overwhelmingly more likely.

CourtListener MCP searches intended to check the asserted companion and appellate conflict were unavailable because the service returned rate-limit errors. I therefore relied on the provisioned petition and committed statpack, and the absent adversarial filing is the largest source of uncertainty.
