Forecasts for the arrival-stage cert petition in *United Airlines v. Kincannon*:

- **Relists:** The docket currently shows no distributions (arrival moment). We expect the petition to be relisted at least twice. The petition expressly requests that the Court hold this case pending the disposition of the petition in *Detwiler v. Mid-Columbia Medical Center* (No. 25A1336). Such a hold or active consideration of the substantial Rule 23 circuit split will require multiple conferences.
- **CVSG:** The Court is unlikely to call for the Solicitor General's views. The core issue is Rule 23 procedure, which the Court typically resolves without seeking the SG's input, even when underlying claims involve Title VII.
- **Summary Disposition Route:** If granted, there is a strong probability of a summary disposition (GVR) in light of *Detwiler*, assuming the Court grants *Detwiler* to resolve the Title VII religious exemption standard. The outcome in *Detwiler* would affect the threshold merits element used in the Fifth Circuit's "class rostering" phase.
- **Dissent from Denial:** Should the Court deny the petition, expect a dissent from denial (likely from Justice Alito, Thomas, or Gorsuch) arguing that the Fifth Circuit's novel "three-stage process" eviscerates the strict predominance requirements of *Wal-Mart v. Dukes*.
- **Question Presented:** If granted for plenary review, the Court would likely take the sole Question Presented as written to address whether individualized merits determinations in a "class rostering" phase defeat Rule 23 commonality and predominance.
