The Supreme Court is extremely unlikely to hear this case—there is less than a two percent chance of review, meaning the petition is roughly ninety-eight percent likely to be turned away (`denied`). Every federal appeals court to consider the issue agrees that city laws singling out in-person requests for immediate donations restrict speech based on its message, and Daytona Beach conceded its ordinance exempts commercial sales pitches while failing to enforce existing traffic-obstruction laws, leaving no disagreement among lower courts for the Justices to resolve.
