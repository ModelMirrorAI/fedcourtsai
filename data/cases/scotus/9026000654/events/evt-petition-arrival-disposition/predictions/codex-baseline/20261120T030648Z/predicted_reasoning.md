The Court will likely deny Daytona Beach's petition at its first conference. The docket shows zero distributions. I expect one distribution, no subsequent relist, and no call for the views of the Solicitor General. I expect no Justice to note a dissent or publish a statement respecting denial.

If the Court grants review, I expect briefing and argument on the first question, narrowed to whether treating in-person requests for an immediate donation differently from requests for a later donation makes the ordinance content based under the First Amendment. That would address the relevant part of the city's second question about the definition of solicitation. I expect the Court to leave the traffic-safety question aside because the panel assumed the city's interest was compelling and still held that the ordinance swept too selectively. The panel considered both *Reed* and *City of Austin*, and the record identifies no intervening decision warranting a summary remand.
