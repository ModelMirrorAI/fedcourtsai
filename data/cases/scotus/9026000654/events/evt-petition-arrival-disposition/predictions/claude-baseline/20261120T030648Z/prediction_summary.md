The Supreme Court will very likely refuse to hear Daytona Beach's appeal — I put the chance it takes the case at about one in fifty. The city wants to revive its panhandling ordinance, which a federal appeals court unanimously held violates free-speech protections because it singles out requests for immediate donations. The city has not shown that other appeals courts disagree, and the case was sent back to the trial court for more work, which makes it an unattractive one for the Justices to review now.
