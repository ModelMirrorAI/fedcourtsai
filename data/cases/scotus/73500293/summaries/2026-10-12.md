## In brief

Music publishers ask the Court to decide whether an author who reclaims a copyright under federal termination rights regains only U.S. rights or worldwide rights.

## What happened

Cyril Vetter co-wrote the 1962 song "Double Shot (Of My Baby's Love)" and assigned worldwide rights to a publisher. Decades later he exercised a federal statutory right letting authors terminate old copyright grants and reclaim their rights. He sued the publisher's successor, Resnik, seeking a declaration that he owned the song worldwide. The district court and the Fifth Circuit agreed, holding that termination recaptured Vetter's rights globally, not just in the United States. After losing, Resnik sold its interest to BMG and other music companies, who took over as defendants and petitioned the Supreme Court.

## What the Court is being asked

The case turns on a federal copyright law letting authors cancel an old grant of rights after a set number of years and get those rights back. The question is whether that reclaimed interest covers only the copyright as recognized under U.S. law, or extends to the copyright interests recognized under other countries' laws when the original grant covered the whole world. BMG argues the reclaimed interest is U.S.-only; Vetter argues it is worldwide, matching what he originally gave away.

## Where it stands

The petition was distributed for the Court's October 16, 2026 conference, after full briefing including a brief in opposition, amicus briefs, and a reply. The docket shows no action yet on the petition since that distribution.

## What each outcome would mean

BMG and the other music companies, as petitioners, are asking the Court to take up the case and reverse. If the Court grants review, it will decide the scope of the termination right in full briefing and argument, deciding nothing yet about who wins. If the Court denies review, the Fifth Circuit's ruling stands, leaving Vetter with worldwide ownership of the reclaimed share of the song and leaving BMG without the broader rights it sought to establish.
