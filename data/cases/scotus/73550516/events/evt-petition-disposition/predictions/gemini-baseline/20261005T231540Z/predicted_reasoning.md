Distributed once so far. The Court requested a response on August 17, so expect at least one further distribution once the respondent's brief is filed. The petitioner explicitly asks the Court to hold this petition pending a similar petition, *Indian Harbor Ins. Co. v. Town of Vinton*, which challenges the same Fifth Circuit precedent creating a 4-1 split on whether federal or state law governs equitable estoppel under the New York Convention. Given the explicit hold request, the Court is highly likely to hold this petition. If the Court grants *Indian Harbor*, this case will likely be held and then GVR'd, meaning a summary disposition route is highly likely conditional on a grant. A CVSG is possible given the treaty implications, but the clear circuit split makes it less necessary. If denied, a dissent from denial is unlikely.
