Distributed once so far (on the fully briefed case for the September 28 conference); expect one further relist or a delay before a decision on the petition. The case presents a clean circuit split over the extraterritorial application of the Sherman Act under the Foreign Trade Antitrust Improvements Act (FTAIA). Because the question implicates international trade and the interests of the United States, the Court is highly likely to call for the views of the Solicitor General (CVSG) before granting or denying review. If granted, the Court will likely take the single question presented as written. A summary disposition is highly unlikely given the substantive nature of the antitrust law question. Should the Court ultimately deny the petition, a dissent from denial or a statement respecting denial is possible, perhaps from Justices particularly interested in curbing the extraterritorial reach of federal statutes.
