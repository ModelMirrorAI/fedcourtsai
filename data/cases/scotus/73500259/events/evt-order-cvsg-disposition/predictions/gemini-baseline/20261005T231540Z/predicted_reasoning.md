Distributed twice so far; the Court has called for the views of the Solicitor General (CVSG). This indicates significant interest in the circuit split between the Ninth and Seventh Circuits regarding the extraterritorial application of the Sherman Act under the FTAIA. The issue of foreign commerce and extraterritoriality is one where the Court often seeks the Executive Branch's input and takes it seriously.

Expect the petition to be distributed again once the SG files their brief. A further relist after that distribution is moderately likely (35%) as the Court digests the SG's recommendation. If the Court grants review, it will almost certainly take the question presented as written to resolve the split; a summary GVR is unlikely unless there is an intervening development, as this requires plenary review. Should the Court deny, a statement or dissent from denial is possible given the sharply articulated circuit split.
