Distributed twice so far (effectively its first consideration with full briefing at the September 2026 Long Conference, as it was pulled from its initial conference for a requested response). Expect one further relist before a decision on the petition. The questions presented highlight a clean circuit split over municipal liability for independent healthcare contractors under Section 1983, and the Fourth Circuit's explicit reliance on a "nondelegable duty" theory provides a strong vehicle. No CVSG is expected, because the federal government's interests are not directly implicated by municipal Section 1983 liability.

If granted, the Court would likely take both questions presented to address whether municipalities can be held liable under either the "nondelegable duty" theory or an automatic policymaker designation. A summary GVR is unlikely unless there is an intervening decision, but a summary reversal is possible if the Court views the Fourth Circuit's decision as a blatant violation of *Monell*'s prohibition on vicarious liability. Should the Court deny certiorari, expect a dissent from denial (likely from Justice Thomas or Justice Alito) echoing Judge Richardson's dissent below regarding the improper expansion of *Monell* liability.
