Drug and medical-device companies that supplied Iraq's Health Ministry ask the Supreme Court to throw out a lawsuit by U.S. service members and their families. The suit says the companies helped fund a militia that attacked them. The Court has asked the Solicitor General (the government's Supreme Court lawyer) for the government's view. That request makes review far more likely than usual. Still, we put the chance that the Court takes the case at a bit under even, about 44%. The main reason against is that the case is at an early stage: no trial or final judgment yet.
