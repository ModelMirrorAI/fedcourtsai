We predict the Supreme Court will likely deny review in this case, though the odds of the Court taking the case are substantial—about two in five (43%). Five global pharmaceutical and medical-device companies ask the Court to dismiss a lawsuit claiming that corrupt payments and free medical supplies they allegedly gave to Iraq's terrorist-infiltrated Ministry of Health helped fund attacks on Americans during the Iraq War. Because the Court has asked the U.S. Solicitor General—the federal government's top appellate lawyer—for the government's views a second time after an earlier remand, the Court is watching the case closely, but the early procedural stage of the lawsuit makes a denial slightly more likely than full review.
