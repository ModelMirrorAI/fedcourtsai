The Supreme Court will probably decline to hear this case, although I put the chance of review at about one in four, or 24%. The dispute asks when companies supplying an Iraqi government ministry can be held responsible for terrorist attacks allegedly financed through corrupt payments and diverted goods. The Court has examined an earlier version of the case, and the companies claim that appeals courts disagree. But the alleged conduct is unusually fact-specific, and the lawsuit has not reached a final judgment.
