# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny this petition. The case will most likely end without a separate writing and without another distribution after the one already recorded on October 21 for the November 6, 2026 conference. The Court will leave further application of the existing aiding-and-abetting framework to the lower courts rather than immediately revisit this interlocutory pleading dispute. An unexplained denial will not endorse the lower court's merits analysis or reveal that rationale.

From the snapshot's one-distribution, no-CVSG state, the probability of at least one further distribution is 44%. Zero additional distributions is the modal path; if the Court relists for substantive consideration rather than awaiting an SG brief, one or two additional distributions is the likeliest extent of that review. The probability of a new invitation to the Solicitor General is 22%; if issued, it is most likely to follow the November 6 conference or a short ensuing relist sequence, with disposition delayed while the government responds. The earlier SG invitation in No. 23-9 is not an invitation already entered on this docket.

If review is granted, the Court will most likely focus on the first question: when knowing assistance to a terrorist-controlled intermediary supplies the nexus and culpable participation required for aiding-and-abetting liability under the ATA. A narrower formulation centered on assistance allegedly provided through corrupt transactions is more likely than accepting the premise that the conduct was ordinary, innocent commerce. The separate direct-liability causation question is less likely to be taken independently.

Conditional on any grant, plenary briefing and argument is the likelier route: 88%, against 12% for disposition in the cert order itself. Another routine Taamneh GVR is unlikely because the appellate court has already reconsidered the case under that precedent. If granted, the likely merits focus is the relationship between knowledge, substantial assistance, and the connection to the attacks, not a general immunity for transactions with foreign-government agencies.

Conditional on denial, a noted dissent or statement has a 6% probability. The modal forecast is none. If a Justice writes to urge review, Thomas or Alito is the likeliest candidate, but neither is individually likely to write; no public cert vote lineup is forecast.
