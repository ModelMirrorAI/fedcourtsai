Big drug companies are asking the Supreme Court to throw out lawsuits by U.S. service members and their families. The suits say the companies' business with Iraq's Health Ministry funded a militia that attacked Americans. We think the Court will most likely refuse to hear the case: about a one-in-five chance it agrees. Two things push the odds up: another appeals court applies the law differently, and the Court already sent this case back once. Two things hold them down: the case is at an early stage, and the dispute turns heavily on its particular facts. The Court may first ask the federal government for its views.
