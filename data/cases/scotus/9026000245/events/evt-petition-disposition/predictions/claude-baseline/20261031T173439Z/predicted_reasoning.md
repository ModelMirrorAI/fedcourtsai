# Forecast — No. 26-245

- **State:** The petition has been distributed once (for the Nov. 6, 2026 conference). It has not been relisted and there is no CVSG.
- **CVSG:** About a 42% chance the Court invites the Solicitor General's views, most likely right after the Nov. 6 conference or after one relist. The Court did exactly that in No. 23-9 for this case, and federal foreign-policy and reconstruction interests are directly implicated. If it calls for the SG's views, the petition will not be decided until spring or late in OT2026, after the SG's brief.
- **Relists:** If there is no CVSG, I expect an outright denial at the Nov. 6 conference more often than a relist. A grant without a CVSG would most likely come after one or two relists.
- **Disposition:** Denial is more likely than not (about 78%). Grant about 22%.
- **Question taken if granted:** QP 1 (the nexus and culpability elements of aiding-and-abetting under *Taamneh*), possibly reformulated as whether general aid not tied to specific attacks, given without intent to aid them, can support § 2333(d)(2) liability. QP 2 (proximate cause for direct liability) might be added, but it is the secondary question.
- **Summary disposition:** Unlikely (about 6% conditional on a grant). Plenary review would follow any grant. There is no intervening decision to support a GVR, and the Court already used one on this case.
- **Dissent from denial:** Unlikely (about 8%). If one comes, the likeliest authors are Justices Thomas, Alito, Gorsuch or Kavanaugh, writing a short statement on ATA liability limits.
