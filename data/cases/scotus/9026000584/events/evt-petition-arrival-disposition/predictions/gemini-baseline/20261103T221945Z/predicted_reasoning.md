## Conditional Merits Reasoning (If Certiorari Were Granted)

If the Court were to grant plenary review, a majority would likely **affirm** the Fourth Circuit's holding that personalized alphanumeric character combinations on vanity license plates constitute private speech in a state-created forum rather than government speech. Under the three-factor holistic framework of *Walker*, 576 U.S. at 209–14, and *Shurtleff*, 596 U.S. at 252—which the Court cautioned in *Matal*, 582 U.S. at 238, marks the "outer bounds of the government-speech doctrine"—a state that invites motorists to invent over 930,000 unique, individualized alphanumeric messages is regulating private expression on a government-owned medium rather than speaking for itself, leaving the validity of Virginia's recall of `"FTP&ATF"` to be adjudicated on remand under forum doctrine.
