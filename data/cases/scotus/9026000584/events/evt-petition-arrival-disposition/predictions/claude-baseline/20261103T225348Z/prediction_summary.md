Virginia asks the Supreme Court to decide whether the letters on personalized "vanity" license plates are the state's own speech, which it may control, or the driver's private speech, which the First Amendment protects. We predict the Court will most likely turn the case down, at about a one-in-six chance of taking it. Courts genuinely disagree on the question. But the lower court sent the case back for more proceedings rather than ending it, and the Justices declined a similar Tennessee case last year.
