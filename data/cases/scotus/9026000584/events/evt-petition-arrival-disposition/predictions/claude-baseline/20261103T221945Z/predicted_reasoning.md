# Forecast — Jack v. Whateley, No. 26-584

**State at prediction:** docketed Nov. 3, 2026; zero distributions; no CVSG; response due Dec. 3, 2026.

- **Distribution / relists:** The petition will be distributed for conference once the response is in (likely mid-to-late December 2026 or January 2027 after a possible extension). Expect a decision at the first conference or after one relist; a further run of relists is unlikely unless the Court is holding for the Seventh Circuit's *M J Nichols* decision.
- **Response:** Respondent, a private plaintiff, will likely file a brief in opposition (possibly with public-interest counsel), stressing the interlocutory posture and the *Gilliam* denial. If he waives, the Court will very likely call for a response given a state petitioner and an acknowledged split.
- **CVSG:** Not expected; no federal interest is directly implicated and the petitioner is itself a government.
- **Disposition:** Denied (about 80%). The likeliest stated or unstated reason is the interlocutory posture, with the Court content to await final judgment and the Seventh Circuit.
- **If granted:** the Court would take the question as written — whether personalized plate characters are government speech under *Walker* — perhaps reformulated as "whether the alphanumeric characters on personalized license plates are government speech." Plenary review, not a summary disposition; no intervening decision supports a GVR.
- **Separate writing:** Should it deny, expect no separate writing; the most plausible exception is a statement by Justice Alito or Justice Thomas noting the split and inviting a cleaner vehicle after final judgment.
