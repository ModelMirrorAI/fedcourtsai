# Forecast — Jack v. Whateley, No. 26-584

- **Distributions:** none yet (arrival). I expect the respondent to file a brief in opposition, possibly after one extension. The petition should then be distributed for a conference in early 2027. I expect it to be decided at its first or second conference. A relist is plausible (roughly one in three) given the split and a state petitioner. Several relists would be a grant signal.
- **CVSG:** none expected. No federal interest is implicated in a state vanity-plate program.
- **Disposition:** denied. The likeliest stated or unstated reasons are the interlocutory posture (remand for forum analysis) and the pending Seventh Circuit appeal in M J Nichols Co. v. Boardman.
- **If granted:** the Court would take the question as written: whether characters on personalized plates are government speech under Walker. It might reformulate the question narrowly to the registration-number characters. Review would be plenary, not summary. No intervening decision supports a GVR.
- **Separate writing on denial:** unlikely. If any appears, it would most likely be a statement respecting denial from Alito or Thomas, noting the split and the interlocutory posture.
