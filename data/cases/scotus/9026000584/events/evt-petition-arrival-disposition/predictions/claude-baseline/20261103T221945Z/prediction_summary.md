Virginia's motor-vehicle commissioner asks the Supreme Court to review a federal appeals court ruling that drivers' chosen vanity-plate letters are the driver's own speech, protected by the First Amendment, rather than the state's speech, which the state could freely control. We predict the Court will most likely say no — about a one-in-five chance it takes the case. State courts disagree with the ruling, which helps, but the case is not finished in the lower courts, and the Court turned down a similar case last year.
