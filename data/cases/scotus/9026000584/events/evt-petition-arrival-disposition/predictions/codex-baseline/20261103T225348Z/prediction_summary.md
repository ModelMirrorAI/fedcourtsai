The Supreme Court is more likely to decline Virginia's appeal than to hear it: I estimate a 32% chance of review, roughly one in three. Courts disagree about whether personalized license-plate messages are the driver's speech or the state's, making this a serious candidate. But the lower courts have not finished deciding whether Virginia's restrictions are lawful, and Virginia could still win there. If the justices take the case, full briefing and argument are much more likely than an immediate ruling.
