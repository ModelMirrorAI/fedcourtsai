The Supreme Court is more likely to turn down Virginia's request for review, though I give review a 36% chance—slightly better than one in three. Courts disagree about whether personalized license-plate messages belong to drivers or count as the government's own speech. That makes the issue worth considering. But this case has not finished in the lower courts: Virginia might still win under other free-speech rules. I expect an ordinary decision declining review, probably without any Justice writing separately.
