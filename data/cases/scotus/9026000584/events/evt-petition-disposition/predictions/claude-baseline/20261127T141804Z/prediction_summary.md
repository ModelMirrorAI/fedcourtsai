The Supreme Court will most likely decline to hear Virginia's appeal; a grant is unlikely, about one chance in fourteen. Virginia asks whether personalized license plates are the government's own speech, which would let the state reject offensive plates freely. The appeals court ruled for the driver but sent the case back for more proceedings, so it is not finished, and the Court turned down the same question from Tennessee a year ago. Another appeals court is also about to rule, giving the Justices reason to wait.
