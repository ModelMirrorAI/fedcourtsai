The Supreme Court is likely to deny review, with about a one-in-eight chance (12%) of taking the case. Virginia asks the Justices to overturn a federal appeals court ruling that personalized messages on state vanity license plates are private speech protected by the First Amendment rather than government speech. Although Indiana and Tennessee's highest courts reached the opposite conclusion, this case has no final judgment because it was sent back to the trial court, and the Supreme Court declined the same question last year.
