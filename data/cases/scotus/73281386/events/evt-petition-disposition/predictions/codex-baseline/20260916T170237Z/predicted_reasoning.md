# Forecast

The Court will most likely grant certiorari, vacate the D.C. Circuit's order, and remand for a more explicit, request-specific consideration of executive-branch discovery under the separation-of-powers principles implicated by its earlier instructions. This is a forecast of a new disposition of petition 25-1103, not a restatement of the June 6, 2025 disposition of the earlier proceeding reproduced in the petition's appendix. I assign 72% to any grant, including a GVR or summary reversal.

The snapshot records **one distribution**, entered July 8, 2026 for the September 28, 2026 conference, and **no CVSG**. I expect one further distribution, with a second further distribution plausible if preparation of a short order or separate writing takes longer. The probability of at least one additional distribution is 65%. My timing forecast is an October–November 2026 petition disposition, rather than an inference that the summer interval already represents relisting.

I expect no call for the Solicitor General's views: the Solicitor General already represents petitioners and has supplied the government's position. The probability of a new CVSG is 0.5%.

Conditional on a grant, I assign 80% to disposition in the cert order rather than plenary briefing and argument. The likeliest route is a GVR directing further consideration, not an immediate final determination that USDS is exempt from FOIA. A summary reversal conclusively rejecting particular discovery remains a secondary possibility. If plenary review is ordered instead, the Court will most likely focus on Question 1: whether the remaining discovery and the appellate treatment of it satisfy the earlier mandate and the relevant separation-of-powers principles. It would narrow Question 2 to the permissible scope and prerequisites of agency-status discovery rather than categorically prohibit every factual inquiry into an Executive Office entity.

If the Court denies review, I expect no public separate writing; the conditional probability of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial is 12%. I do not forecast individual cert votes. Any substantive reasoning forecast here is conditional on intervention: the expected ground is insufficient tailoring and consideration of executive confidentiality, not a final adjudication of DOGE's actual independent authority or all of CREW's FOIA claims.
