Distributed once so far; expect perhaps one or two further relists before a decision on the petition, as the Court may need time to draft a summary disposition. No CVSG is expected, because the Solicitor General is already representing the petitioners. 

A summary disposition route (such as a summary reversal) is highly likely: the Court previously granted certiorari, vacated, and remanded in this exact matter for the court of appeals to narrow discovery based on separation-of-powers concerns, and the petition asserts the lower court defied this instruction. If plenary review is unexpectedly granted instead, the Court would likely take both questions presented regarding the breadth of FOIA discovery against Executive Office advisory bodies and the application of *Cheney v. United States District Court*. Should the Court deny the petition, expect a dissent from denial.
