# Forecast

The Court will deny the petition, most likely without explanation in an orders list following the September 28, 2026 conference, during the opening weeks of the October Term. This forecasts the petition's disposition, not the already-denied motion to expedite.

The snapshot records **two distributions**: March 17 for the April 2 conference and June 17 for the September 28 conference. The intervening response request and briefing distinguish this history from two completed substantive conference considerations. From that frozen count of two, I forecast **no further distribution** as the modal path, with a **35% probability of at least one additional distribution**. If another distribution occurs, one or two additional distributions are more likely than a prolonged sequence.

No CVSG appears in the record. I forecast **no new CVSG**, assigning 3% to a call after this prediction. The Court can dispose of the standing and preservation issues, or order reconsideration under intervening precedent, without another round seeking the Solicitor General's views.

The probability of **any grant is 30%**, including a GVR. Conditional on a grant, I assign **80% to a disposition in the cert order itself**, principally vacatur and remand for reconsideration in light of Bost and/or Callais rather than summary reversal. A GVR would not establish that petitioners ultimately prevail. Conditional on plenary review instead, the likelier focus is a narrowed version of question 1: whether a candidate or affected voter can establish an injury traceable to the antecedent liability ruling when district changes follow in a separate remedial order. Question 2's categorical demand for strict scrutiny whenever a remedial map is race-conscious is less likely to receive plenary review in this vehicle.

The most likely denial leaves the lower-court result undisturbed without endorsing its reasoning. The standing objections, disputed preservation of the equal-protection challenge, and disagreement over the record's showing of racial predominance make this an unattractive vehicle for a broad new rule. If review is granted summarily, the Court is more likely to ask the Ninth Circuit to reconsider the application of intervening authority than to resolve those factual and procedural disputes itself.

Conditional on denial, I assign **8% to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial**. My forecast is no separate writing; I do not forecast a particular Justice or a private cert vote lineup.
