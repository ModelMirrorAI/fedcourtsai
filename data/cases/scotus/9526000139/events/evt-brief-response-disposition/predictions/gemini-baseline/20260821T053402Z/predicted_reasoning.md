I expect this application to be denied. While it is a high-profile dispute involving multiple states (Alabama vs. California) and has attracted several amicus briefs, the extraordinary relief of a stay is rarely granted. Given the involvement of multiple states and significant public interest, I expect Justice Jackson will refer the application to the full Court rather than acting alone. The Court will likely deny the application in a brief order, perhaps with a dissent noted by one or more of the conservative Justices. A response has already been filed, and the amicus count has already risen past the context threshold.
