# Forecast

The application will be referred to the full Court and disposed of shortly after the response due August 3, 2026, most likely within about one week. The frozen record already shows that Justice Jackson requested a response, so no further response request is expected. It shows no referral and zero amicus briefs; referral is highly likely, and the amicus count is likely to rise because five prospective amici or amicus groups already appear in the snapshot's counsel metadata.

The predicted disposition is **denied** under the interim event's denial-first vocabulary. The Court may be receptive to staying the injunction as to the citizenship-list provisions: the First Circuit dissent would have granted that narrower relief, and the federal government's justiciability theory has support in *Trump v. New York*. But the application seeks broader relief, while even the dissent found immediate injury from the Postal Service provisions and noted that applicants offered no substantive defense of those provisions. A partial grant is therefore a substantial possibility, but a mixed “granted in part and denied in part” order resolves as denied for this event. An unqualified grant remains plausible but is less likely than the combined probability of a denial or mixed disposition.
