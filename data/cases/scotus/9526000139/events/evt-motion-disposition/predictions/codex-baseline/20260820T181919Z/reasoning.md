# Rationale

I assign **P(unqualified grant) = 0.41**. This is a forward-mode interim application at the arrival moment. The July 30 snapshot shows an application for a stay submitted to Justice Jackson on July 29 and a response requested the same day, with no referral and zero amicus briefs in the frozen context. No application or response text was provisioned.

The committed statpack supplies a valid strictly prior-Term baseline for this OT2026 application: OT2024 and OT2025 together contain 225 resolved substantive applications and 30 grants, or 13.3%. That pool clears the 50-case floor, though its coverage is uneven and it is not conditioned on escalation. I adjust sharply upward because a response request is the strongest recorded attention signal, the case affects election administration across many states immediately before a federal election, and the First Circuit's stay denial drew a partial dissent.

I stop below 0.5 because the scored event is an **unqualified** grant. The pre-event appellate order shows that the applicants presented only standing and ripeness grounds. Its dissent would stay the injunction as to portions of section 2 but agreed that the applicants had not shown likely success as to the Postal Service provisions in section 3. That division makes partial relief materially more likely than a full stay; the registered interim resolver treats any mixed grant-and-denial order as denied. A separate federal application could also reduce the need to grant this intervenor-state application independently.

The response-requested increment is set near zero because that rung had already fired and is vacuous for scoring. Referral is 0.97 given the requested response and national stakes. The amicus increment is 0.98 because the frozen proceedings count is zero while the snapshot's undated counsel metadata already names five prospective amicus groups. The main uncertainty is the missing Supreme Court application and response text: I could assess the lower-court theories and posture, but not whether the applicants narrowed their requested relief or how the respondents answered.
