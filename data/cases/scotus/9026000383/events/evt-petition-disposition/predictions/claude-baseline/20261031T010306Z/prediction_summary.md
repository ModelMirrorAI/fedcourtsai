We expect the Supreme Court to turn this case down. The chance it agrees to hear it is very small, under one in a hundred. Felicia Scroggins is representing herself. She argues that lower courts were wrong to throw out her disability-discrimination claim against the City of Shreveport before trial. The appeals court rejected her claim in a short, unpublished order. The City did not bother to file a response. The Court rarely takes cases that ask only whether lower courts got one person's facts wrong.
