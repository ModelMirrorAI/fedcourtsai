# Forecast — Scroggins v. City of Shreveport (No. 26-383)

The docket shows **one distribution** (for the November 13, 2026 conference) and no relist; the City **waived** its right to respond. Expect the petition to be **denied at that first conference** without a call for a response, without a relist, and with no further distribution.

- **Relist / call for response:** Not expected. With a waiver on file, the only usual path to another distribution is the Court calling for a response, which happens for a small fraction of waived paid petitions and almost never for a pro se, fact-bound summary-judgment challenge to an unpublished one-line affirmance.
- **CVSG:** None. The respondent is a city, and the questions don't touch a federal program or a live federal enforcement position that would call for the Solicitor General's views.
- **Question the Court would take, if any:** QP 1 (whether an employee actually working in an accommodated role can be held not "qualified" under 42 U.S.C. §12111(8) based on past absences), reframed more generally. QP 2 is a request to correct errors under Tolan/Reeves/Anderson, and the Court does not grant on that.
- **Summary disposition:** If the Court did act, a summary per curiam reversal under Tolan-style error correction would be about as likely as plenary review. No intervening decision suggests a GVR.
- **Separate writing:** No dissent from denial or statement respecting denial is expected.
