The Supreme Court is almost certain to deny review in *Felicia Scroggins v. City of Shreveport, Louisiana* (a 0.2% chance of a grant, or about one in five hundred). Representing herself without a lawyer, a municipal firefighter challenges a federal trial court's summary judgment—a ruling ending the case before trial because the judge found no genuine factual dispute—after she missed nearly two-thirds of her shifts over five years. Because the federal appeals court affirmed in a two-sentence unpublished order, the case presents no disagreement among appellate courts, the City waived its response, and an independent ruling that the City had a non-pretextual reason for discipline makes the case unsuitable for Supreme Court review.
