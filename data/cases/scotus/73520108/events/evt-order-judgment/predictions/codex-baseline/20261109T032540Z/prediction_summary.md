I expect the Supreme Court to reverse the lower court by a 6–3 vote. There is about an eight-in-ten chance it changes the ruling in some way. The likely reason is that consumers' claims for money after unlawful bankruptcy debt collection can go to arbitration, a private dispute process they agreed to use, without stripping bankruptcy courts of their own enforcement powers. Complications involving the consumers' revised complaint could instead produce a narrower ruling. The forecast does not decide whether Goldman actually broke the law.
