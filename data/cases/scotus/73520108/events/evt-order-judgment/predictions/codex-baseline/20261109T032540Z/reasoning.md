Reversal is thus the modal exact judgment while the scored 82% also includes vacatur and mixed relief. The probabilities are judgments, not a fitted statistical model.

## Why the bank is favored

Validity and contractual coverage are not the contested gateway here. The Fourth Circuit treats the claims as core bankruptcy matters and relies on centralization, a debtor's fresh start, uniformity, bankruptcy expertise, public deterrence, and legislative history to find an inherent conflict. Yet its own opinion acknowledges that these damages claims do not directly harmonize competing creditor interests. Maze's Chapter 7 case had closed; Brown's Chapter 13 plan had been confirmed. Those postures make it easier to distinguish recovery for past injury from the collective administration of an estate. I do not equate a confirmed plan with a closed Chapter 13 case: Brown's continuing proceeding is the stronger counterexample to Goldman.

The controlling analogy is the demanding anti-displacement analysis in **Epic Systems Corp. v. Lewis, 584 U.S. 497, 510–17 (2018)**, independently checked through CourtListener's Supreme Court opinion text. It requires harmonizing statutes and rejects displacement resting on general policy preferences. The question is not whether bankruptcy policies are important, but why awarding these private damages in arbitration would make the Code and FAA irreconcilable. The appellate opinion's doubts about arbitrator expertise, private adjudication, or inconsistent results resemble objections that would disable arbitration in many other statutory settings.

**CompuCredit Corp. v. Greenwood, 565 U.S. 95, 98–104 (2012)** supplies a consumer-credit analogue: a statutory damages action does not itself establish an unwaivable judicial forum. I checked the lead opinion and Sotomayor's concurrence, joined by Kagan, rather than assuming all pro-consumer Justices oppose enforcing arbitration. The latter accepted arbitration despite finding the statutory question close. These precedents support my prediction; neither decided the bankruptcy question here. McMahon's inherent-conflict framework remains relevant, and my forecast does not require the Court to overrule it or to demand the literal word “arbitration” in every contrary statute.

The most persuasive limiting distinction in the cert reply is between a debtor's private Section 362(k) action and the court's authority to address contempt under Section 105(a). Preserving the latter means honoring an arbitration clause need not strip the court of its ability to protect the automatic stay. The reply also points to the FAA's tolerance of piecemeal adjudication. I therefore give less weight to duplication alone than respondents do. I do not adopt Goldman's disputed state-court concurrent-jurisdiction premise as an independently established fact; the BIO squarely contests it, and the forecast need not settle that subsidiary dispute.

## Why confidence stops at 82%

Respondents' argument is stronger than an ordinary objection to arbitration: the automatic stay protects the bankruptcy process, not simply a private contractual entitlement. A majority could view Section 362(k) as part of the bankruptcy court's integrated enforcement machinery rather than an interchangeable statutory damages remedy. Keeping contempt in court while arbitrating mandatory damages can generate overlapping factual and remedial proceedings. Brown's live Chapter 13 case gives that concern a concrete footing. The appellate dissent supports reversal but does not eliminate these distinctions.

There are also genuine vehicle risks. Respondents assert that Goldman conceded discretion below, dispute whether the two debtors were separately analyzed, and report dismissal with leave to amend while proposing an independent Section 105(a) basis. The reply disputes waiver and emphasizes that these are distinct remedies. I cannot resolve the current pleading status from this record. The grant means certiorari is settled, not that these objections have vanished. I reserve meaningful weight for a narrower vacatur/remand and a smaller DIG possibility, without claiming that a without-prejudice dismissal or an announced amendment automatically moots the appeal.

## Stakes and limitations

The **0.62 significance score** reflects nationwide implications for consumer-debtor remedies and financial institutions' arbitration clauses. It is not a grant-likelihood score or an assumption that all bankruptcy disputes will become arbitrable. A rule preserving contempt while enforcing Section 362(k) arbitration is important but narrower than a restructuring of bankruptcy adjudication generally.

Corpus retrieval did not supply a useful issue-matched prior: the exact-citation filter was sparse, and the broader grant query returned docket skeletons, including this case's already-provisioned grant but no merits outcome. I did not treat that repetition as new evidence. Web attempts returned no usable content. The decisive additional material was the earlier Supreme Court opinion text retrieved through CourtListener, together with the committed statpack. Every attempted external lookup, including unsuccessful ones, is recorded in `retrieval.md`.
