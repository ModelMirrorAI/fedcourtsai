We expect the Supreme Court to rule for Goldman Sachs and reverse the appeals court, about an 80% chance.
The issue is whether bankrupt credit-card customers who say Goldman kept trying to collect after they filed
for bankruptcy can sue in bankruptcy court, or whether their card agreements force them into private
arbitration. The current Court almost always enforces arbitration agreements unless Congress clearly said
otherwise, and the bankruptcy law here says nothing clear. We expect a 7-2 vote, with Justices Sotomayor and
Jackson dissenting.
