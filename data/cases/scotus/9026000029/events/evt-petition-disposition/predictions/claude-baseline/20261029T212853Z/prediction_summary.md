Baby-formula makers Mead Johnson ask the Supreme Court to stop Illinois courts from hearing lawsuits by 51 families from other states whose infants were never fed or treated in Illinois. We predict the Court will most likely turn the case away, though there is about a one-in-six chance it takes the case or reverses outright without full argument. The Court has already asked the families to respond, and earlier Supreme Court rulings strongly favour the companies. Still, the early stage of the case makes it an awkward one for the Court to take.
