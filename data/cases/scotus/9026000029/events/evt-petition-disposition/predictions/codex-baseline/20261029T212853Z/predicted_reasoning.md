# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny the petition for certiorari. The probability of any grant,
including a grant with an immediate disposition, is **22%**. The predicted denial
will leave the jurisdictional ruling below in place without endorsing its
reasoning or deciding whether the nutrition products caused the alleged injuries.

## Procedure from this vantage

The provisioned October 29, 2026 snapshot shows **one distribution**, for the
September 28 conference, and **no CVSG**. It also shows a September 1 request for
a response and an extension of that response's deadline to October 30. No
opposition brief appears on this record.

- **Further distribution: 98%.** Expect one additional distribution after the
  requested response and the usual consideration of a reply, followed by denial.
  A second additional distribution is a plausible alternative. The first expected

- **New invitation to the Solicitor General: 4%.** Expect no invitation. The

  federal government's views; the surrounding regulatory interests do not make
  an invitation the likely next step.
- **Timing:** expect action in late 2026 or early 2027 rather than before the
  requested response is considered. This is a forecast, not a docketed date.

## If review is granted

The most likely question taken is whether the continuing Illinois contacts and
former headquarters identified below permit jurisdiction over these nonresident
plaintiffs' claims. A narrower formulation focused on the temporal and substantive
limits of general jurisdiction is more likely than a question about the safety
of infant formula. Because the decision rests on both general and specific
jurisdiction, review would need to address both independent grounds or narrow
the affected claims sufficiently to eliminate the other ground.

**Conditional on a grant, the probability of disposition in the cert order is
40%.** Full briefing and argument are slightly more likely, at 60%, because the
two jurisdictional bases and the disputed headquarters facts complicate summary
correction. Within the summary route, a per curiam reversal is more plausible
than a GVR for reconsideration in light of a new decision: no intervening lead
decision providing such a hook is identified in the provisioned record.

If granted, the likely substantive direction is to reject jurisdiction based
solely on unrelated Illinois sales, continuing business contacts, or a former
headquarters, while distinguishing any genuinely claim-linked Illinois conduct.
That is a conditional prediction, not an unconditional forecast of a merits
opinion.

## Writings

Conditional on denial, the probability of a noted dissent or statement respecting
denial is **8%**. Expect an unsigned denial with no separate writing. Thomas or
Gorsuch would be plausible writers if a jurisdiction-focused statement accompanies
the order, but neither is individually likely to write. The structured
`order-writers` probabilities are unconditional across all disposing orders;
they also allow separate writings on a summary grant and are not vote forecasts.
