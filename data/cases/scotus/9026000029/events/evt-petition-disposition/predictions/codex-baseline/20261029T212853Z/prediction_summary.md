The Supreme Court is likely to decline this appeal, with about a one-in-five chance of taking it up. Mead Johnson argues that Illinois courts cannot hear these out-of-state families' claims against the company. Its argument raises serious concerns about the limits on a state's power over businesses, and the Court has asked the families to respond. But the cases are still at an early stage, and disputed facts could make them a poor vehicle for review. Denial would not decide whether the formula caused harm.
