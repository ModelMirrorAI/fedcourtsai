We predict the Supreme Court will most likely **turn down** this appeal by baby-formula maker Mead Johnson, but it is a real contender — roughly a one-in-four chance (27%) the Court takes it or reverses outright. The companies argue Illinois courts have no power to hear suits by families from 22 other states whose infants were never fed or treated in Illinois. The Court asked the families to respond, a sign of interest, and business and pediatric groups back the companies.
