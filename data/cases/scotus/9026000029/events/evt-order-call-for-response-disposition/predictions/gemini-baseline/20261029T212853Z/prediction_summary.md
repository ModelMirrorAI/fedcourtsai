The Supreme Court is unlikely to grant review in *Mead Johnson & Company, LLC v. Greear* (about a one-in-six chance, or 16%), and will most likely deny the petition. Although the Court asked the plaintiffs to respond and four outside groups filed friend-of-the-court ("amicus") briefs supporting the formula makers, the Illinois appeals court applied existing Supreme Court rules on "personal jurisdiction" (which state courts have authority over out-of-state companies) to an unusual set of facts involving Mead Johnson's recent 2022 headquarters move from Illinois to Indiana. Because the lower court relied on both general and case-specific ties to Illinois during the years the product warnings were developed, the Supreme Court will likely view the dispute as too tied to one company's transition to serve as a clean test case.
