The Supreme Court is likely to turn down this appeal, with about a one-in-five chance of taking action. Mead Johnson argues that Illinois courts cannot hear claims brought by families from other states after the company moved its headquarters. The Court's request for a response and several outside groups' filings show interest. But the case is still underway, and the headquarters facts are disputed. Those obstacles make review less likely even though the ruling could affect where many infant-formula lawsuits proceed.
