# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition after receiving the requested response, without a separate opinion. The forecast is **22% for any grant** and **78% for no grant**; denial is the predicted disposition. The Court will leave the state litigation to continue rather than decide whether the infant-formula products are defective.

The baseline contains **one distribution**, on August 19 for the September 28 conference, followed by the September 1 call for a response due October 1. Expect **one further distribution**, and possibly a second if summary reversal receives serious consideration. A renewed distribution after the response is not necessarily a true relist after substantive conference consideration. Several additional genuine relists are not the central forecast.

Expect **no CVSG**. The dispute concerns the constitutional reach of state courts over private defendants; federal interests in infant nutrition will not ordinarily require the Solicitor General's participation in that jurisdictional question.

If the Court intervenes, it will address whether former headquarters and continuing business activities support general jurisdiction after an asserted relocation, and whether Illinois marketing can supply specific jurisdiction for these nonresident plaintiffs' out-of-state injuries. Because either independent ground below could preserve jurisdiction, a grant limited to only one question without addressing the other is less likely than consideration of both, focused on the required connection between defendant, forum, and claims. Review will not reach the safety or adequacy of warnings on the products themselves.

Conditional on a grant, **summary disposition is slightly likelier than plenary briefing and argument: 55% versus 45%**. The main summary route is a per curiam reversal applying existing personal-jurisdiction limits, not a GVR in light of an identified new decision. If the Court sets the case for argument, the likely focus is the timing of general jurisdiction and the boundaries of Ford's relatedness standard. Those merits predictions are conditional; the primary forecast remains denial, with the interlocutory posture and disputed headquarters record making this an unattractive vehicle.

Conditional on denial, there is a **4.5%** chance of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial. Expect none; if a writing appears, Thomas, Alito, or Gorsuch is a more plausible author than any one other Justice, but no individual author is likely.
