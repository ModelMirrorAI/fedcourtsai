# Predicted course of the Court — Calce v. City of New York, No. 26-46

**Where the docket stands.** At the snapshot (2026-09-09), the docket shows one
distribution (for the 9/28/2026 conference) and the Court's call for a response
on 2026-09-08, which followed the City's waiver. There is no CVSG. The live
docket (read 2026-10-29) adds one later fact: the time to respond has been
extended to **November 23, 2026**. So the petition cannot be decided before a
December 2026 or January 2027 conference.

**Most likely course: a hold for *Viramontes v. Cook County*, No. 25-238.**
The Court granted *Viramontes* (Seventh Circuit, a ban on a type of arm, argued
by the same counsel of record) on 2026-06-30, after relisting it many times.
The petition itself asks for a hold for *Viramontes* as its last alternative.
Once the response is in, I expect the Court to sit on this petition, relisting
it from conference to conference without a recorded order, until *Viramontes*
is decided, most likely in June 2027. The Court handled the post-*Bruen* and
post-*Rahimi* Second Amendment petitions the same way.

**Then:**
- If *Viramontes* holds the ban unconstitutional, or says anything to the effect
  that "common use" is a historical-step question on which the government bears
  the burden, I expect a **GVR** (grant, vacate and remand) in light of
  *Viramontes* (and *Wolford*). The Second Circuit's unpublished summary order
  rests entirely on putting the common-use burden on plaintiffs at the textual
  step.
- If *Viramontes* upholds the ban on reasoning that does not touch the textual
  step, I expect a **denial**.
- A smaller chance: the Court acts before *Viramontes*, either with a GVR in
  light of *Wolford v. Lopez* (decided 2026-06-25, after the decision below) or
  with a *Caetano*-style summary reversal. Calling for a response is
  consistent with either.
- Plenary review of this case itself is unlikely, since *Viramontes* is already
  the Court's vehicle for the framework question.

**Relists:** further distributions are near-certain (the petition goes back to
conference once the response is filed). Under a hold, expect many relists
through the spring of 2027.

**CVSG:** no. No federal law is challenged, and the Solicitor General is already
taking part in *Viramontes*.

**Question the Court would reach if it grants:** QP 1 (whether a challenger must
show common use to bring a ban within the Amendment's plain text), resolved by
remand rather than by a merits opinion in this case. QP 2 and QP 3 would be left
to the Second Circuit.

**Summary route:** if the petition is granted, the likeliest form by far is a
GVR in the cert order itself, not briefing and argument.

**Separate writing:** if the Court denies outright, without a hold, I expect
Justice Thomas to dissent, possibly joined by Justices Alito and/or Gorsuch,
invoking *Caetano*. A denial after a *Viramontes* ruling for the government is
less likely to draw a writing. A GVR could draw a short dissent or statement
from a Justice who would have summarily reversed, or from one who sees no reason
to remand.
