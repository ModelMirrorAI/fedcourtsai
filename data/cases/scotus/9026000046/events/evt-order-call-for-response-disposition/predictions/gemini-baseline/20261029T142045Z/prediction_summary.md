The Supreme Court is likely to deny the petition for review, with a little over a one-in-four chance (28%) of granting it. Petitioners challenge New York State and New York City laws that ban civilians from possessing tasers and stun guns, arguing that the lower courts wrongly required them to prove how many people own those weapons nationwide before applying the Second Amendment. Because the Court is already hearing another Second Amendment case this term (*Viramontes v. Cook County*) raising the same threshold legal question, the likeliest path to a grant is for the Court to hold this petition and later send it back to the lower court for reconsideration (known as a "grant, vacate, and remand"), though the thin factual record presented in the trial court makes an outright denial the most probable result once New York City files its response.
