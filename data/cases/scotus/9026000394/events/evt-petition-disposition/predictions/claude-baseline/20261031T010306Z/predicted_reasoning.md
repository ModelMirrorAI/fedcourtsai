# Forecast — Herlihy v. DBMP, LLC (No. 26-394)

- **State**: distributed once (conference of Nov 13, 2026). The Court requested a response on Oct 30 (due Nov 30), so the Nov 13 distribution will not decide it. Expect redistribution for a December or January conference after DBMP's response and the petitioners' reply. A further relist after that is possible but less likely than not.
- **CVSG**: unlikely (~10%). This is a private dispute, and the Court has shown it can evaluate bankruptcy questions without the SG's views.
- **Disposition**: most likely **denied** (≈82%), with DBMP arguing interlocutory posture and no true conflict with *LTL*.
- **If granted**: the Court would take the QP roughly as written but likely reframe it around whether a debtor lacking financial distress, created by divisional merger, files in good faith such that "cause" exists under § 362(d), possibly also reaching the § 1112(b) good-faith standard. Plenary review, not a summary disposition: there's no intervening decision to support a GVR (~4% of grants).
- **Dissent from denial**: low (~12%). If one comes, Gorsuch (author of *Purdue*) or Jackson/Sotomayor writing about mass-tort claimants' access to courts are the likeliest.
