We predict the Supreme Court will probably **not** take this case, giving it about a one-in-five chance of review. Asbestos victims are challenging the "Texas Two-Step," in which a profitable company splits off its injury liabilities into a new company that then files for bankruptcy. The Court has asked the company to respond and several groups, including a coalition of states, back the petition, which raises the odds. But the case comes in the middle of an ongoing bankruptcy, and the claimed disagreement between appeals courts is debatable.
