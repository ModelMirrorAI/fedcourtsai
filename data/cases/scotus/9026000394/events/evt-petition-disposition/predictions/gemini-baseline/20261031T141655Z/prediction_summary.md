The Supreme Court is likely to deny this petition for review, with about a one-in-seven (14%) chance of granting it. Although the Court asked DBMP to file a response and five outside groups filed briefs supporting the asbestos claimants on an important dispute over "Texas Two-Step" corporate bankruptcies, the Court turned down a nearly identical asbestos bankruptcy appeal from the same appellate court just five months ago. Moreover, this appeal involves only two individual claimants asking to pause the bankruptcy freeze ("automatic stay") for their own state lawsuits rather than a direct request to dismiss the entire bankruptcy case, making it a flawed procedural vehicle for a national ruling.
