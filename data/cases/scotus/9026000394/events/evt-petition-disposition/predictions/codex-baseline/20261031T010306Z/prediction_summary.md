The Supreme Court is likely to decline this case, although I estimate about a one-in-four chance it agrees to review it. The dispute concerns whether a financially healthy business can shift asbestos claims into a separate company and use bankruptcy to pause victims' lawsuits. Conflicting legal standards, five supporting outside briefs, and the Court's request for a response make review a serious possibility. But this appeal concerns lifting a lawsuit pause, not dismissing the bankruptcy itself, which may make it an imperfect case for resolving the broader dispute.
