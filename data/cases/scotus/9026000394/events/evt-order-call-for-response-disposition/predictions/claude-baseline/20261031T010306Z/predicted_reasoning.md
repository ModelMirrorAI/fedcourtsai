# Forecast — Herlihy v. DBMP, LLC (No. 26-394)

**Call: certiorari denied (P(grant) ≈ 0.07).**

**Where the docket stands.** One distribution so far (for the 11/13/2026 conference), made on the respondent's waiver. On 10/30/2026 the Court called for a response, due 11/30/2026. No CVSG, no relist yet. Five amicus briefs support the petition (bankruptcy law professors, the Asbestos PI Claimants' Committee, Prof. Rave, North Carolina and other States, Public Justice).

- **Relisting.** The petition will come off the 11/13 calendar. Once the response and any reply are in, it will be redistributed, most likely for a January 2027 conference. Expect it to be decided at that first post-response conference with no further relist. If it is relisted again after that, a grant becomes a live possibility.
- **CVSG.** Unlikely (≈4%). No federal party is involved. The U.S. Trustee Program has an interest in bankruptcy good-faith policy, but the Court did not ask the SG about this scheme in the two earlier Bestwall petitions.
- **Question, if granted.** The single QP as written: whether a Texas Two-Step filing lacks good faith, so that there is "cause" to lift the automatic stay under § 362(d). The Court might reframe it as the general good-faith standard (the Fourth Circuit's subjective-bad-faith-plus-objective-futility test against the Third Circuit's financial-distress test in *LTL*).
- **Summary disposition.** Very unlikely. No intervening decision supports a GVR, and the question needs full briefing.
- **Dissent from denial.** Probably none. No Justice publicly dissented from the two Bestwall denials, as far as the record shows. If anyone writes, the likeliest are Justice Jackson or Justice Gorsuch (Gorsuch because of the *Purdue Pharma* concerns about bankruptcy being used by non-debtors).
- **If granted, the likely ground** is that financial distress, or a valid reorganizational purpose, is part of good faith, which would set aside the Fourth Circuit's "irrelevant" holding.
