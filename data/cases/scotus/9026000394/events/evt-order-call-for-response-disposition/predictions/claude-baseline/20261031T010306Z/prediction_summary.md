We predict the Supreme Court will most likely decline to hear this case (about a 7% chance it takes it). Asbestos victims are challenging the "Texas Two-Step," where a healthy company spins its injury liabilities into a new shell company and puts only that shell into bankruptcy, which freezes the lawsuits. The Court has asked the company to respond, and several groups and States back the victims. But the Court turned down two similar challenges, most recently in June 2026.
