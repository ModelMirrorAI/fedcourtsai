The Supreme Court is likely to decline this case, although I put the chance of review at about one in four, or 24%. The case challenges a corporate maneuver that puts asbestos claims into bankruptcy while the operating business continues outside it. Conflicting legal standards, five supporting outside briefs, and the Court's request for a response make review plausible. But this appeal concerns lifting a pause on particular lawsuits, rather than directly dismissing the bankruptcy, which may make it a less suitable case for settling the broader dispute.
