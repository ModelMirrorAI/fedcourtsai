## What happened

In 1987 the state condemned part of the property to build the highway, removing a bridge that had connected the northwest corner of the land to the rest. For decades afterward, the trust used a nearby state-owned access road to reach that corner. In 2020 the state's transportation department sold that access road to a private buyer, Green Acres Storage Partners, without notifying the trust or keeping an easement for it.
