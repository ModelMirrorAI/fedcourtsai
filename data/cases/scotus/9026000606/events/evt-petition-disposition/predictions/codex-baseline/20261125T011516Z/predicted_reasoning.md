The Court will most likely deny the petition after the December 11 conference. The docket shows one distribution and no relists. I expect zero further distributions and no Justice to note a dissent or issue a statement. The likeliest reason for a second distribution would be a Justice asking the United States to file a response after its waiver. I expect no invitation for the Solicitor General’s views because the United States is already the respondent.

If the Court grants review, I expect briefing and argument on the single question as written. Does the First Amendment require that a defendant charged under 18 U.S.C. § 2251(a) be allowed to raise a reasonable, good-faith mistake-of-age defense? That question can be resolved while leaving the prosecution’s burden of proof and the separate trafficking convictions intact. The Eighth Circuit followed its own precedent, and the conflict with the Ninth Circuit calls for a choice between their rules. The record supplies no intervening ruling for a summary remand.
