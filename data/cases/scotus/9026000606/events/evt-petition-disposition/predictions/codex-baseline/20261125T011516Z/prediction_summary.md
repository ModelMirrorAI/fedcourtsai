The Supreme Court is likely to decline Richard Kucera’s case. I put the chance of review at about one in fourteen. Federal appeals courts disagree about whether someone charged with producing sexually explicit images of a minor may argue that he reasonably believed the person was an adult. Kucera asked to make that defense at trial. Even where courts allow it, defendants face a demanding burden to show they could not reasonably have learned the person’s age. Kucera also has two separate trafficking convictions.
