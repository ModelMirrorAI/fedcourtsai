The Supreme Court will very likely refuse to hear this case, with only about a one-in-fifty chance it agrees to review it. Richard Kucera argues the First Amendment entitles someone charged with producing child pornography to tell the jury he reasonably believed the person was an adult. Lower courts do disagree, but one court has stood alone against many since 1988, the government did not even bother to file a response, and no outside groups have weighed in.
