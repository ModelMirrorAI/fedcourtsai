The Supreme Court is likely to decline this case, with about a one-in-four chance of taking it up. Alaska Policy Forum challenges rules requiring donor disclosure for messages criticizing ranked-choice voting without mentioning the ballot measure. Sixteen supporting briefs and the Court's request for a response make the petition unusually substantial. But the claimed disagreements among courts may be less direct than the petition suggests. Expect another conference distribution after the response arrives, and probably no explanation if review is denied.
