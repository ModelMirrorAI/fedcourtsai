The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Alaska Policy Forum v. Alaska Public Offices Commission*, with only about a one-in-nine chance (11%) of taking the case. Although sixteen outside advocacy groups filed friend-of-the-court briefs supporting the nonprofit petitioner and the Court asked Alaska respondents to file a response by November 30, 2026, the Justices have repeatedly turned away nearly identical First Amendment challenges to state campaign-finance donor-disclosure and ad-disclaimer laws—including a New Mexico think tank's appeal denied in October 2026—and Alaska's high court interpreted the pre-2021 law narrowly so general nonprofit donors need not be disclosed.
