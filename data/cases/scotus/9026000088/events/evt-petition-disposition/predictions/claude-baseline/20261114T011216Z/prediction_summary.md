The Supreme Court will probably decline to hear this case; the chance it agrees to review it is about one in nine. A nonprofit is challenging Alaska rules that forced it to register and reveal donors after it criticized ranked-choice voting during a ballot campaign. The Court asked Alaska to respond and sixteen outside groups filed supporting briefs, which raises the odds above normal, but the Court has repeatedly turned away similar donor-disclosure challenges, and this dispute is fact-specific without a clear conflict among lower courts.
