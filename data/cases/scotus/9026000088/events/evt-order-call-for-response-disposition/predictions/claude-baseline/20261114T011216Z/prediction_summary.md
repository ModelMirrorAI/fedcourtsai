The Supreme Court will probably decline to hear this case — I put the chance of it agreeing to review at about one in six. A think tank is challenging Alaska's rule that it disclose its donors after publishing criticism of ranked-choice voting while a ballot measure was pending. The Court asked Alaska to respond, and an unusually large number of outside groups filed supporting briefs, which lifts the odds well above normal. But the Court has repeatedly turned away similar donor-disclosure challenges, and the dispute is tied to specific facts.
