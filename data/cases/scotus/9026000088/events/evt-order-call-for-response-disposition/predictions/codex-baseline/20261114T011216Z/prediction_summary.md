The Supreme Court is likely to decline this case, with about a one-in-four chance of agreeing to review it. The dispute asks when a nonprofit's discussion of public issues can trigger election rules requiring disclosure of its donors. Twelve supporting outside briefs and the Court's request for a response make review more plausible than usual. But the claimed disagreements among courts are not all clear-cut, and the nonprofit's messages included election-related language. The most likely result is a denial without explanation.
