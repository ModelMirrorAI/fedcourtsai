The Supreme Court is very likely to decline review: I give this petition a
12% chance of being taken up. It asks whether bankruptcy can erase a debt
for deliberately causing someone's confinement when the debtor believed his
actions were justified. The Court's request for a response shows interest,
but not necessarily support. Although the petition alleges disagreement
among appeals courts, the detailed findings about the debtor's conduct may
make this a poor case for settling the broader legal question.
