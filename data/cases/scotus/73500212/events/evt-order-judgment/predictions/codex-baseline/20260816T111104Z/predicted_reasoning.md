# Forecast

The Court will reverse the judgment below by a 6–3 vote. Chief Justice Roberts and Justices Thomas, Alito, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh, and Barrett will form the majority; Justices Sotomayor, Kagan, and Jackson will dissent.

The majority will hold that enforcement of the anti-steering injunction could not prohibit Apple from charging a commission on purchases initiated through external links when that no-commission obligation was not clearly imposed by the injunction's text or necessary to its remedial purpose. The decision will emphasize fair notice and the requirement that contempt and enforcement measures remain within the clear terms of the underlying order.

The Court will state that ground narrowly. It will resolve the permissible enforcement of this injunction without adopting a categorical rule about app-store commissions or deciding whether Apple's commission structure violates federal antitrust law. A DIG or an equally divided affirmance is possible but unlikely.
