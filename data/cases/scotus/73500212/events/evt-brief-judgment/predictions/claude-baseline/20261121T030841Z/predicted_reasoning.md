# Forecast: Apple Inc. v. Epic Games, Inc., No. 25-1311 (judgment after argument)

**Judgment class.** The Court will disturb the Ninth Circuit's judgment. The most likely entry is **vacated** and remanded: the Court rejects the "spirit of the injunction" formulation as a basis for civil contempt, restates the *Taggart* "no fair ground of doubt" test, and sends the case back. The next most likely entries are a plain **reversed** (the Court itself holds the 27% link-out commission cannot support contempt and frames the judgment as a reversal and remand) and a mixed **affirmed in part, reversed in part** entry tracking the Solicitor General's proposal (reverse on the commission, affirm on the "plain button" restriction, vacate the rest). A plain affirmance is the main undisturbed outcome; a DIG is conceivable but not live, since the question was preserved and squarely decided below, and no recusal is apparent on the docket.

**Vote lineup.** A lopsided result on the legal standard: eight Justices in the majority, with Justice Jackson dissenting on the view that *McComb* permits contempt for deliberate circumvention found as fact by the district court. If the Court goes beyond the standard and itself clears Apple on the commission, the split widens to 6-3 with Justices Sotomayor and Kagan joining the dissent (or concurring only in part).

**Ground.** The Court reaches only Question 1 (the grant was so limited). It holds that civil contempt requires that the injunction's terms, read objectively and in context, clearly cover the conduct; an appeal to the order's purpose or "spirit" cannot fill a gap in its text, and subjective bad faith does not substitute for coverage. *McComb* is read as a case about conduct within a decree's general terms, not as licence to enforce an order's goals. The rule is stated categorically for federal injunctions generally, with Rule 65(d)'s specificity requirement as support. The Court will note that a plaintiff facing a new evasive practice can seek modification or clarification of the injunction instead of contempt.

**Application.** The Court gives some guidance on application, most likely signalling that a commission the injunction never mentions raises at least a fair ground of doubt, but leaves most or all of the design-restriction findings and the bad-faith/sanctions questions to the Ninth Circuit on remand.

**Who writes.** No strong assignment signal exists before argument (January 2027 sitting). Justice Gorsuch or Justice Barrett is the likeliest author given their writing on the historical limits of equity (the petition tied the case to *CASA*); the Chief Justice, Justice Thomas and Justice Kavanaugh are close behind, and Justice Kagan is plausible if the opinion is a narrow, near-unanimous vacatur. Expect a dissent or partial dissent from Justice Jackson, and possibly a concurrence from Justice Sotomayor stressing that courts retain power against evasion, or from Justice Thomas or Justice Gorsuch on equity's historical limits.

**Timing.** Argued January 11, 2027; decision between April and late June 2027.
