**Adjustments up, to 0.86.**
- The Ninth Circuit said in terms that "parties may be held in contempt for violating the spirit of an injunction" even where the strict letter was not disregarded, and never applied the *Taggart* "fair ground of doubt" test. That sentence is hard to affirm as written.
- The Court granted quickly (first conference, no CVSG), limited to Question 1, which Apple framed around the "spirit" standard.
- The United States, nominally supporting neither party, calls the standard "plain reversible error" and proposes reversing on the 27% commission, affirming on the "plain button" rule, and vacating the rest. Every piece of that proposal other than a full affirmance counts as disturbed.
- Epic's own brief does not defend the "spirit" wording; it recasts the decision as an application of *McComb* and asks, in the alternative, for a remand if the Court clarifies the standard. That fallback is itself a disturbed outcome.
- Ninth Circuit origin and a bench generally sceptical of expansive equitable power.

**What holds me below 0.9.** Apple is an unsympathetic contemnor: the district court found deliberate evasion, a manufactured justification and false testimony, and Apple does not challenge those findings as clearly erroneous. *McComb* gives the Court a ready path to say the Ninth Circuit's substance was right whatever its label, and the United States itself agrees contempt was proper for at least one restriction. Some Justices may also be wary of narrowing contempt at a time when it matters for enforcing injunctions against government defendants. I put affirmance near 0.12 and a DIG or other undisturbed exit near 0.02.

**Judgment label.** Among disturbed outcomes I estimate vacated about 0.42, reversed about 0.27, affirmed-in-part-reversed-in-part about 0.17. Vacatur is the Court's usual entry when it corrects a legal standard and remands; the SG's request for worked applications and Apple's request for outright reversal pull toward the other two. This split is my judgment, not a tabulated rate, and the label is the part of this forecast a reader should discount most.
