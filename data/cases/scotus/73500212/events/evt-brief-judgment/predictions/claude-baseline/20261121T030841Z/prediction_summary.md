The Supreme Court will very likely (about 86 percent) set aside the appeals court ruling that upheld Apple being held in contempt, meaning punished for disobeying a court order, in its App Store fight with Epic Games. The appeals court said Apple could be punished for violating the order's "spirit"; the Court's own precedent requires that the order clearly forbid the conduct, and even the federal government called that an error. The predicted judgment is "vacated", sending the case back for a fresh look, by a lopsided vote of roughly 8 to 1.
