## Why `P(disturbed)` Moves from `0.704` to `0.88`

Three case-specific features push `P(disturbed)` substantially above the `70.4%` merits base rate:

1. **Direct doctrinal collision with *Taggart v. Lorenzen*, 587 U.S. 554 (2019).** In *Taggart* (also reviewing the Ninth Circuit, 9–0), the Court held that civil contempt requires an objective showing of "no fair ground of doubt" as to whether the order prohibited the conduct. Yet the Ninth Circuit opinion below reaffirmed its pre-*Taggart* precedent (*Sea Shepherd*) that parties may be held in contempt for violating an injunction's "spirit" even assuming the "strict letter" was not violated—expressly relying on that assumption for three of the five link-design restrictions—and stated that even assuming Apple's reading of the injunction was reasonable, Apple's subjective bad faith negated a good-faith defense. Granting Question 1 alone (while denying Question 2's *Trump v. CASA, Inc.* universal-injunction issue) on first distribution without a CVSG strongly indicates the Court took the case to repudiate that legal framework.
2. **The Solicitor General's merits amicus brief.** The United States filed `merits-amicus-united-states.txt` **in support of neither party** (and moved for divided argument). Substantively, however, the Solicitor General agrees with Apple that the Ninth Circuit's "spirit" rule and subjective-bad-faith override are plain reversible error under *Taggart* and Rule 65(d), and urges the Court to **reverse** civil contempt on the 27% commission (because the one-sentence 2021 anti-steering injunction spoke only to developer communications and was silent on pricing, while the 2021 liability opinion contemplated commissions), **affirm** contempt on the "plain buttons" rule (which defied the express word "buttons"), and **vacate and remand** on the remaining four link-design restrictions. Crucially, every variant of the Solicitor General's approach—full vacatur and remand, partial reversal plus vacatur, or three-way affirmance-in-part/reversal-in-part—**disturbs** the Ninth Circuit's judgment. My forecast closely tracks the Solicitor General's substantive legal framework while expecting the Court's decretal line to take the cleaner `vacated` form rather than affirming "plain buttons" outright in the first instance.
3. **Why `vacated` (`0.52`) beats `reversed` (`0.26`) and `affirmed-in-part-reversed-in-part` (`0.10`).** Apple's request for outright reversal of *every* contempt specification faces steep headwinds on the "plain buttons" and five-template restrictions, which directly implicate the injunction's express references to "buttons" and "other calls to action." Conversely, the Roberts Court almost never enters `affirmed-in-part-reversed-in-part` (`0/206` in OT2022–OT2024) when vacating a legally flawed appellate framework that underpins an integrated remedial order and an undifferentiated attorneys'-fee award. Vacating and remanding—exactly as *Taggart* (`scotus/15006766`) and *Starbucks Corp. v. McKinney* (`scotus/68462680`) did—lets a broad majority unite behind the legal standard and commission guidance while leaving restriction-by-restriction application and fee recalculation to remand. (Note: if the Court writes "reversed in part, vacated in part, and remanded" without an "affirmed in part" clause, `pipeline/judgment.py` parses the first verb as `reversed`, which accounts for a meaningful share of my `0.26` mass on `reversed`.)

## Why `P(disturbed)` Stops at `0.88` (Main Uncertainties)

Two concrete paths leave `0.12` on undisturbed outcomes and argue against pushing past `0.90` before oral argument:

- **Affirmance on alternative textual grounds (`~0.09`).** Epic's merits brief forcefully reframes the case around contextual ordinary meaning—arguing that "prohibiting" external links encompasses an engineered 27% fee and friction package backed by extensive district-court findings of pretext, zero developer uptake, and two "strict letter" violations. Justice Kagan's August 13, 2026 denial of Apple's stay application (`26A194`), while primarily reflecting lack of irreparable harm from ongoing prospective-modification proceedings on remand, is a reminder not to treat Apple's factual posture as sympathetic. If five Justices conclude at argument that Apple lacked any fair ground of doubt across the core restrictions and treat the Ninth Circuit's *Sea Shepherd* discussion as harmless given its alternative remark that Apple's reading was unreasonable, the Court could disapprove "spirit" dicta while affirming.
- **DIG risk (`~0.03`).** Because district-court remand proceedings were not stayed and two restrictions rested on "strict letter" holdings, oral argument could surface vehicle concerns about untangling prospective injunction modification from retrospective contempt, slightly above the ~2.2% historical DIG rate.
