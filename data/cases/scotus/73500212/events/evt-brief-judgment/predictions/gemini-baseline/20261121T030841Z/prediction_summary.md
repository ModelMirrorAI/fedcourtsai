The Supreme Court is very likely—nearly nine chances in ten—to disturb the Ninth Circuit's decision in *Apple Inc. v. Epic Games, Inc.*, most likely by vacating (wiping away) the ruling and sending the case back for reconsideration in a unanimous 9–0 vote. Under Supreme Court precedent, a judge may hold a party in civil contempt—imposing court sanctions for disobeying an order—only when the order's actual text leaves no reasonable doubt, not merely because the party violated the order's unwritten "spirit."
