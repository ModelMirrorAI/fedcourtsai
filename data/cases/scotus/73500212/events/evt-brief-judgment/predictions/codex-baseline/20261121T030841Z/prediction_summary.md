I give the Supreme Court about a five-in-six chance of setting aside at least part of the ruling against Apple. I expect a 7–2 vote to send the case back. The original order told Apple to let developers link customers to other ways to pay. It gave Apple little guidance about what it could charge on those sales. The lower court would have to reconsider which of Apple’s rules, including its limits on visible purchase buttons, clearly disobeyed the order.
