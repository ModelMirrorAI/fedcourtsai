We predict the Supreme Court will very likely (about 99 in 100) refuse Jesse Guardado's
request to halt his Florida execution scheduled for November 5, 2026. He argues that Florida's
clemency process (the governor's power to show mercy) is a sham because no death-row
prisoner has received clemency since 1983. Courts have consistently rejected this kind of
claim, Florida says he waited too long to raise it, and the Court almost never stops
executions at the last minute.
