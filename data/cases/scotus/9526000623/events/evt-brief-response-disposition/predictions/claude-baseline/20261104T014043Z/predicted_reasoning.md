# Forecast — Guardado v. Florida, 26A623

- **Disposition:** The Court denies the stay application, most likely on Nov 5 2026, on
  or just before the scheduled 6 p.m. execution, together with denial of the linked cert
  petition 26-6013.
- **Referral:** Justice Thomas refers the application to the full Court, and the order
  reads "presented to Justice Thomas and by him referred to the Court is denied."
- **Response requested:** No call for a response; Florida has already filed one.
- **Amicus:** No amicus briefs arrive.
- **Separate writings:** Most likely none. If anyone writes, Justice Sotomayor or Justice
  Jackson would be the likeliest, for example a short statement criticizing a clemency
  process in which no one has received mercy for four decades, but a statement on a
  Florida warrant denial on this ground is unlikely.
- **Merits context (conditional):** If the Court were to entertain the claim, it would turn on
  whether *Woodard*'s minimal-safeguards floor is violated by a clemency system that
  provides process but has a de facto policy of denial. Federal courts have uniformly
  said it is not, so the Court is unlikely to find a significant possibility of reversal.
