The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Jesse Guardado's application for a stay of his November 5, 2026 execution, with only about a 1.5% chance of granting relief. To win a stay, a prisoner must show a realistic chance that the Court will agree to hear his appeal and overturn the state court's ruling, but Guardado's claims about decades-old trial evidence are blocked by Florida's filing deadlines, and the Court has repeatedly rejected similar challenges to Florida's clemency process and 31-day execution schedule. Following standard practice in death-penalty cases, Justice Thomas will almost certainly refer the application to the full nine-Justice Court, which will deny the stay without any written dissents.
