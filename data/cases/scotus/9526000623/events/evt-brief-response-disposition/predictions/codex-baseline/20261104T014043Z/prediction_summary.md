The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Jesse Guardado's request to pause his November 5 execution. I estimate about a three-in-one-hundred chance of relief if the Court reaches the request rather than dismissing it as moot. His argument that Florida's clemency process offers no meaningful chance of mercy raises serious stakes, but the supplied papers do not show a clear legal basis requiring intervention. Florida has opposed the request. The likely next step is consideration by the full Court, followed by a short order before the scheduled execution.
