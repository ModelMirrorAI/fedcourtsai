# Forecast — 26A623, Guardado v. Florida

- **Disposition:** The Court will **deny** the stay of execution, most likely on Nov 5, 2026, in the hours before the 6:00 p.m. execution, together with a denial of cert petition 26-6013. P(stay granted) ≈ 1.5%.
- **Referral:** Justice Thomas will refer the application to the full Court (≈90%), as is routine for execution stays. The order will read "denied by the Court."
- **Response:** Florida will file an opposition, most likely without a formal docketed call for a response (P(call) ≈ 8%).
- **Amicus:** none expected (≈3%).
- **Timing:** disposed of on or just before Nov 5, 2026. It will not be held past the execution time.
- **Separate writing:** most likely none. If anyone writes, the likeliest are Justice Sotomayor and then Justice Jackson, either in a short dissent or a statement on executions on a warrant schedule or the meaninglessness of clemency. Expect no writing from the other Justices.
- **Merits context:** the claim rests on Justice O'Connor's *Woodard* concurrence, which requires only minimal clemency procedures. The current Court will not read a governor's stated policy of denying clemency as the "coin flip" that concurrence condemned. There is no fair prospect of certiorari.
