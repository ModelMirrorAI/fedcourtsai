We expect the Supreme Court to **deny** Jesse Guardado's request to halt his Florida execution, set for November 5, 2026. We put the chance of a stay at very unlikely, about 1 in 70. His argument is that Florida's clemency process (the governor's power to spare a life) is a sham, because no death-row prisoner has received clemency since 1983. The Court has turned down similar claims from Florida prisoners, and it rarely stops executions at the last minute. A short dissent from one Justice is possible but not expected.
