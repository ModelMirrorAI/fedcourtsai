The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Jesse Guardado's emergency application for a stay of his November 5, 2026 execution in Florida, with only a 2% chance of granting a stay. Guardado argues that Florida's executive clemency process—in which the governor may commute a death sentence to life imprisonment—violates constitutional due process because no death-row prisoner has received clemency since 1983. However, the Florida Supreme Court unanimously found that Guardado received clemency consideration, and the U.S. Supreme Court has repeatedly turned away identical challenges in recent Florida capital cases.
