The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Jesse Guardado's request to pause his execution, scheduled for November 5, 2026. I estimate about a three-in-one-hundred chance of any relief if the request is decided rather than becoming moot. His application argues that Florida's clemency process offers no genuine chance of mercy. But the available filing does not clearly show that he was denied the minimal procedural protections the Constitution requires. The Court will probably consider the request as a full bench and issue a brief order.
