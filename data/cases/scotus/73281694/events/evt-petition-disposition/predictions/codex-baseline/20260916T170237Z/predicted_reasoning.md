# Forecast

The Court will most likely deny certiorari. The unresolved Commerce Clause question is substantial, but this interlocutory case presents enough uncertainty about preservation, the basis of the state orders, and finality to make denial more likely than immediate review. A denial would leave those questions undecided rather than endorse registration jurisdiction under the Commerce Clause.

The snapshot records **two distributions**: April 28 for the May 14, 2026 conference, followed by July 15 for the September 28, 2026 conference. The intervening response request and briefing make this different from two completed substantive conferences. From that two-distribution baseline, my modal forecast is **no further distribution** and disposition following the September conference. A further distribution is a close alternative, at 48%; if it occurs, I expect one or two additional conferences, not a long succession of relists.

I expect **no CVSG**. The probability of a new invitation is 8%; if one issues, the likely window is the September conference or the first few fall conferences.

If review is granted, I expect the Court to take the Commerce Clause question as applied to a nonresident plaintiff's out-of-state injuries against an out-of-state corporation registered in Pennsylvania. It would leave the previously decided due-process issue alone, rather than use the question's broader reference to the Constitution to reopen that holding. Conditional on reaching the merits, the likely ground for relief would concern the interstate-commerce burden of compelling jurisdiction over claims lacking a forum connection.

Conditional on a grant, **plenary briefing and argument are substantially more likely than a cert-order disposition**: the latter has probability 10%. The record identifies no new controlling decision that supplies a straightforward GVR, and the unresolved constitutional and procedural issues favor fuller consideration.

Conditional on denial, I expect **no noted dissent or statement**, but assign a meaningful 30% probability to some separate expression. If one appears, Justice Alito is the most plausible source given the concern expressed in the earlier proceeding. This is not a forecast of otherwise unobservable individual cert votes.
