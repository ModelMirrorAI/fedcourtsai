Distributed once for the upcoming 9/28/2026 conference (the prior distribution was vacated when the Court called for a response). Expect one or two further relists before a decision on the petition, as is typical for high-stakes grants while the Court finalizes the order list. A CVSG is unlikely given that the issue is a straightforward application of the Dormant Commerce Clause and the Court already has the benefit of the SG's views from the prior iteration of this case (21-1168).

If granted, the Court will take the single question presented as written to determine whether Pennsylvania's registration-based jurisdiction violates the Dormant Commerce Clause. A summary GVR is unlikely because this is a major constitutional issue that warrants plenary review, and there is no intervening decision. Should the Court unexpectedly deny review, expect a vigorous dissent from denial authored by Justice Alito (who practically invited this challenge) or one of the four dissenters from the first *Mallory* decision.
