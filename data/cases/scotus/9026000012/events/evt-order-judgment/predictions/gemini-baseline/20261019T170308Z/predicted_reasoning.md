The Court will reverse the judgment of the Fourth Circuit. The majority will hold that the Equal Protection Clause does not require plaintiffs to satisfy an aggregate disparate-impact benchmark before a court may consider evidence that a facially neutral admissions policy was adopted with a discriminatory intent. The ground will be categorical, establishing that intentional racial discrimination is unconstitutional even if the targeted group does not suffer a net disparate impact in the aggregate. 

I expect a 6-3 split along ideological lines. Chief Justice Roberts and Justices Thomas, Alito, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh, and Barrett will form the majority, viewing the Fourth Circuit's rule as incompatible with the principle that Equal Protection protects individuals rather than groups, consistent with their approach in *Students for Fair Admissions*. Justices Sotomayor, Kagan, and Jackson will likely dissent, arguing that without a showing of disparate impact, a policy does not actually harm the plaintiffs in a legally cognizable way or that the evidence of intent was insufficient. I do not foresee a summary disposition or a DIG; the Court granted cert specifically to resolve the acknowledged circuit split and will issue a full merits opinion.
