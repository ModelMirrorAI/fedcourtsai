# Forecast: Association for Education Fairness v. Montgomery County Board of Education (No. 26-12), judgment

**Judgment class: vacated (and remanded).** Being disturbed in some form (vacated or reversed) is very likely. Vacatur is the likelier label because the Fourth Circuit never addressed the district court's alternative holding that the complaint failed to plausibly allege discriminatory intent.

**Vote: 6–3.** Roberts, Thomas, Alito, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh and Barrett form the majority. Sotomayor, Kagan and Jackson dissent. Kagan concurring only in the judgment on a narrow rationale is a live alternative, and so is a fractured majority with Thomas, Alito and Gorsuch writing more broadly.

**Question reached.** The Court granted a reformulated question: whether a plaintiff must show an *aggregate* disparate impact relative to the targeted group's share of the applicant pool. The Court will answer it as framed and say no. Disparate impact stays relevant evidence under *Arlington Heights*, but there is no fixed aggregate threshold that must be met before intent can be weighed. The ground will rest on the individual-rights character of the Equal Protection Clause, citing *SFFA*, *Miller v. Johnson* and *Parents Involved*. This tracks the Alito dissents from the denials in *Coalition for TJ* and *Boston Parent Coalition*. The Second and Third Circuit approaches are endorsed over the First and Fourth.

**Breadth.** Narrow. The Court does not decide whether this complaint plausibly alleges intent. That question goes back to the Fourth Circuit. The Court also does not hold that race-neutral proxies adopted to change racial composition automatically trigger strict scrutiny, although a concurrence (Gorsuch or Thomas, perhaps with Alito) may say so.

**Procedural exits.** A DIG is conceivable (about 4%) because the BIO stresses the independent intent ground. Still, the Court chose to reformulate and grant over that objection after three conferences, which makes a DIG unlikely. An affirmance holding that some aggregate comparison is required is about 14%. That outcome is most plausible if Roberts or Barrett join the three Justices on the left in reading *Arlington Heights* and *Feeney* as requiring group-level impact. No recusal is visible, so an equally divided Court is remote.

**Dissent.** If the case goes 6–3, expect a principal dissent by Sotomayor or Jackson. It will argue that disparate impact by definition means group-level effect, and that the majority makes race-neutral efforts to widen access presumptively suspect.
