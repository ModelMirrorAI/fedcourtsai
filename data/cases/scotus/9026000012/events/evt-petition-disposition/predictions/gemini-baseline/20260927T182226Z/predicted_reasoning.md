I expect the Court to grant the petition. If granted, the Court will take the single Question Presented as written to resolve the mature 2-2 circuit split over whether an aggregate disparate impact is a necessary prerequisite to proving discriminatory intent under *Arlington Heights*. 

A summary disposition (GVR or summary reversal) is unlikely, as this is a first-impression issue at the Supreme Court level that requires plenary review to resolve the circuit split. No CVSG is expected; the Court already has the views of the parties and numerous amici, and no unique federal program is directly implicated.

If the Court ultimately denies cert, expect a vigorous dissent from denial by Justices Thomas and Alito, who previously dissented from the denial in *Coalition for TJ* on this exact issue.
