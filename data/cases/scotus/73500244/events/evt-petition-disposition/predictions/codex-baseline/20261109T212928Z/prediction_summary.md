The Supreme Court is more likely to turn down Derick Brown's appeal than hear it, although I give review a substantial 41% chance—about two in five. The case asks whether using a car makes an otherwise local kidnapping a federal crime. Repeated consideration and disagreement among appeals courts make it a serious candidate. But the government argues that the decisions are distinguishable, and Brown's ruling simply follows existing circuit precedent. I expect one more round of consideration, then denial, probably without explanation.
