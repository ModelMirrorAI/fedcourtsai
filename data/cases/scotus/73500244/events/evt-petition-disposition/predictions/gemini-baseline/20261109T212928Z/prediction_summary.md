The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Derick Irisha Brown v. United States*, with roughly an 88% chance of denial (a 12% chance of granting review). Brown asks whether using a car within a single state automatically counts as using an "instrumentality of interstate commerce" (a tool of trade between states) under the federal kidnapping law. Although the Court has postponed deciding whether to hear her petition twice, it denied review just three weeks ago in *McMichael v. United States*, the published Eleventh Circuit lead case raising the exact same question. Moreover, the Tenth Circuit decision she cites as conflicting limited its ruling to the broader phrase "motor vehicle" rather than "automobile."
