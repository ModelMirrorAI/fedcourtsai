Derick Brown asks the Supreme Court whether using a car makes any kidnapping a federal crime, even when the car never leaves the state. We think the Court will more likely turn the case down, but there is a real chance, about one in three, that it agrees to hear it. The Court has put off deciding twice, which often signals interest. But it already rejected a nearly identical case, which points toward a refusal, perhaps with one Justice writing to disagree.
