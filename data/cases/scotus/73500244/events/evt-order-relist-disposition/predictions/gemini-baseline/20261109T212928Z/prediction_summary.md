The Supreme Court will likely deny Derick Irisha Brown's petition for review, though she has roughly a one-in-three chance (32%) of the Court agreeing to hear her case. Brown asks whether using a car strictly within one state automatically turns a local kidnapping into a federal crime because cars are tools ("instrumentalities") of interstate commerce. Federal appeals courts are divided on that question, and the Supreme Court has already held her petition over for two extra private conferences, showing serious interest. However, because the kidnappers also used cell phones—which courts uniformly treat as tools of interstate commerce—the Court will likely conclude that this case is a flawed vehicle and wait for a cleaner test case.
