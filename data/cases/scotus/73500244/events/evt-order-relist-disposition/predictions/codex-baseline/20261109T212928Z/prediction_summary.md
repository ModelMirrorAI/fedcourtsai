The Supreme Court is slightly more likely to decline this case than to hear it; I put the chance of review at 44%, a little under one in two. Brown asks whether using a car for a kidnapping entirely within one state automatically makes it a federal crime. Repeated consideration and disagreement among appeals courts make review plausible. But the government offers a meaningful distinction between those decisions. If the Court hears the case, full briefing and argument are much likelier than an immediate ruling.
