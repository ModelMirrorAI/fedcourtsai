We think it is a bit less likely than not (about 42%) that the Supreme Court agrees to hear Derick Brown's appeal. The case asks whether every car counts as an "instrumentality of interstate commerce," a tool of trade between states. If it does, federal kidnapping law covers any kidnapping that involves a car, even one that never leaves the state. The Court has postponed its decision once and is considering it again, which often comes before agreeing to hear a case. But the federal government argues the lower courts do not really disagree, and the Justices may simply deny the appeal, possibly with a written dissent.
