# Predicted course — Lugo v. Birmingham Jefferson County Transit Authority (No. 26-545)

**Disposition: denied.** The petition, filed pro se (paid) from an unpublished
Eleventh Circuit per curiam affirming summary judgment for the employer, will be
denied without noted dissent on an ordinary order list.

- **Distribution.** The docket shows zero distributions (petition filed
  Oct 21, 2026; response due Nov 25, 2026). Expect the respondent transit
  authority to waive its response, after which the petition is distributed for
  a conference in roughly December 2026 or January 2027 and denied at that first
  conference. I expect exactly one distribution and no relist; the Court will
  not call for a response.
- **CVSG.** None. The respondent is a local transit authority, the claims are a
  private plaintiff's Title VII / ADA / § 1981 claims, and no federal interest
  warrants inviting the Solicitor General.
- **Which question, if granted.** If the Court took anything it would be QP 3
  (whether advocacy on behalf of a third party is protected activity under the
  ADA's anti-retaliation provision, 42 U.S.C. § 12203(a)), likely reformulated;
  QP 1's comparator standard (Lewis v. City of Union City, en banc) and QP 2's
  record-review / Seventh Amendment complaint are fact-bound error correction.
- **Summary disposition.** Not likely; no intervening decision of this Court
  calls for a GVR. In the remote event of any grant, a GVR is a meaningful share
  of that small mass.
- **Separate writing.** None expected on the denial.
