The Supreme Court is likely to decline Sherhonda Golden's appeal, with about a one-in-five chance of granting review or sending the case back for another look. Her lawsuit asks whether federal video-privacy law protects information Facebook can decode even when an ordinary person cannot. The Court's request for a response shows interest, and lower courts disagree. But it recently declined a similar case. Another pending video-privacy case could affect the timing, though it addresses a different part of the law.
