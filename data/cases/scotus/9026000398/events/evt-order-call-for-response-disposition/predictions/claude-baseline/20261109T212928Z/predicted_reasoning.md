# Forecast — Golden v. NBCUniversal, No. 26-398

- **Distributions:** The petition has been distributed once (for Nov 6, 2026), and no relist has occurred yet. The Court called for a response on Oct 22, due Nov 23. Expect a new distribution after the brief in opposition and reply, for a December 2026 or January 2027 conference. Two outcomes are about equally likely from there. One is a denial at that first post-response conference. The other is that the petition is held for *Salazar v. Paramount Global* (No. 25-459), with repeated redistributions or no action until *Salazar* is decided, likely by spring–June 2027.
- **CVSG:** Not expected. The parties are private, and the Court is already deciding a VPPA case without the SG's views.
- **Disposition:** Most likely a **denial**, either outright or after *Salazar* comes down. A grant of plenary review is unlikely: the Court denied the same Second Circuit question in *Solomon* (Dec 2025) and *Hughes* (Mar 2026).
- **Summary route:** If there is any grant, a GVR in light of *Salazar* is about as likely as plenary review. That would happen only if *Salazar*'s reasoning reaches the meaning of "personally identifiable information."
- **If granted for plenary review:** The Court would take the QP as written: does PII turn on the recipient the provider knows about, or on an "ordinary person"?
- **Dissent from denial:** Unlikely. Prior denials on this question drew no noted writing.
