We expect the Supreme Court to turn down Sherhonda Golden's appeal against NBCUniversal. We put the chance it takes the case, or sends it back to the lower court for another look, at about 7 in 100. The case asks whether a website that shares viewing data with Facebook breaks a federal video-privacy law. The Court asked NBCUniversal to respond, which is a mild sign of interest. But it has already declined this same question twice in the past year. It may set the petition aside until it decides a related video-privacy case, Salazar v. Paramount Global, likely by mid-2027.
