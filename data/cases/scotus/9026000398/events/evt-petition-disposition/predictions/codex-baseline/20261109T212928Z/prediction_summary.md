The Supreme Court will likely decline to hear Sherhonda Golden's privacy lawsuit against NBCUniversal. I estimate about a one-in-five chance of review or an order sending the case back for reconsideration. Federal appeals courts disagree about whether coded video-viewing data counts as identifying information, but the Court recently declined similar cases. It has requested NBCUniversal's response, so further scheduling is very likely. Another pending video-privacy case could prompt a delay, but it concerns a different part of the law.
