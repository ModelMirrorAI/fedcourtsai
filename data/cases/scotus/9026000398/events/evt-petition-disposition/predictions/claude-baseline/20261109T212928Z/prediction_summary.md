We predict the Supreme Court will most likely refuse to hear this case; we put the chance it takes the case, or sends it back for another look, at about one in ten. The case asks whether NBCUniversal broke a federal video-privacy law by sharing viewing data with Facebook. The Court asked NBCUniversal to respond, which signals some interest. But the Court has already turned down this same question twice. The likeliest exception is that it holds the case until it decides a related video-privacy case, then returns it to the lower court.
