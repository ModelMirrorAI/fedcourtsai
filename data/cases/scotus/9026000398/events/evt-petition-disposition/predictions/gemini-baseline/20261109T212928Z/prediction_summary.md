The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Sherhonda Golden v. NBCUniversal Media, LLC*, with only a 4% chance of granting the petition (about one in twenty-five). Golden asks the Court to revive a lawsuit under the Video Privacy Protection Act alleging that Today.com shared her video-viewing history and Facebook ID with Meta through tracking code, or else to hold the case for *Salazar v. Paramount Global*, another pending case under the same statute. However, the Court has already declined twice in the past year to review the Second Circuit's rule that such coded data is not "personally identifiable information" because an ordinary person cannot read it, and that independent ground supports dismissal regardless of how *Salazar* is decided.
