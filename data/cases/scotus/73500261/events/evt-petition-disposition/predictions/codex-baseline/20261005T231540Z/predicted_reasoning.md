# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely **deny this conditional petition**, leaving the judgment vacating the preliminary injunction undisturbed without issuing a merits opinion. The probability of any grant, including a GVR, is **22%**.

The baseline records **one distribution**, on July 8 for the September 28 conference, and **no CVSG**. Because the subsequently requested response is due October 19, I expect **one additional distribution after briefing**, with a second additional distribution possible. The probability of at least one further recorded distribution is **92%**. The next entry would likely reflect completion of briefing and rescheduling, not necessarily renewed consideration after an actual first conference. The modal timing is a late-2026 or early-2027 order after the response and reply opportunity; a companion-case hold would extend that horizon.

I expect **no call for the Solicitor General's views**; the probability of a new CVSG is **2%**. The July response request is directed to respondents and does not satisfy that forecast.

If review is granted, the Court will most likely consider this petition alongside related Ten Commandments litigation rather than treat it as an independent invitation to reverse Louisiana's favorable judgment. The initial question would be QP 1, justiciability of the pre-enforcement challenge. Any reach of QP 2 would likely focus on compulsory classroom displays and the relevance of Louisiana's implementation discretion, rather than announce a rule governing every religious display. This is a conditional question-selection forecast, not a prediction that a denial decides either constitutional issue.

**Conditional on grant**, I assign **60%** to setting the case for plenary consideration and **40%** to disposition in the cert order itself. Within the summary alternative, a GVR tied to resolution of related litigation is more plausible than an immediate summary reversal. The petition's request to consider the two statutory schemes together favors plenary treatment if both genuinely warrant review.

**Conditional on denial**, I expect no dissent or statement respecting denial; the probability of at least one such writing is **10%**. I do not forecast its author or any private certiorari vote. A denial would supply no majority merits rationale.
