The Supreme Court is more likely to decline this Louisiana petition than hear it: I put the chance of review at about one in five, or 22%. The dispute concerns required Ten Commandments displays in public-school classrooms. But Louisiana already won removal of the lower court's temporary block and asks for review only if the Court takes a related Texas case. Asking the other side to respond shows interest, not a decision to hear the case. Declining review would not itself decide whether the displays are constitutional.
