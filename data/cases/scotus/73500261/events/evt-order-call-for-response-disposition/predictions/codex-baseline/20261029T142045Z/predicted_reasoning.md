# Predicted course of the petition

The Court will most likely deny this conditional petition without a separate writing. The forecast is denial, not a judgment approving or disapproving classroom Ten Commandments displays. Louisiana already obtained vacatur of the preliminary injunction and asks for review only if the Court takes a petition arising from the related Texas litigation, Nathan. The likely path leaves that Louisiana victory in place while the underlying litigation can develop a more concrete record.

The supplied docket contains **one distribution**, entered July 8 for the September 28, 2026 conference, followed by the July 28 response request after a waiver. It records no completed conference and no genuine post-conference relist. From that state, expect at least one additional distribution after the response process, with a possible further redistribution or hold to coordinate related litigation. P(at least one additional distribution) is **0.98**; this is not a 98% forecast of substantive repeated reconsideration. The response-requested moment masks this increment for scoring. A disposition in late 2026 or early 2027 is the central timing expectation, with a longer delay possible if another petition must be considered first. These are forecasts, not assertions that any post-July docket activity has occurred.

No Solicitor General invitation appears in the record. Expect none: P(a new CVSG) is **0.04**. The legal questions concern constitutional limits on state classroom displays and threshold jurisdiction, rather than an unresolved federal administrative position for which the Court especially needs executive-branch advice.

If review is granted, the Louisiana-specific threshold question is the first question presented: whether these respondents' challenge is nonjusticiable. Expect the Court to focus on ripeness and the appropriate disposition of the suit, potentially alongside the constitutional question in Nathan, rather than assume this is a clean standalone merits vehicle. If the second question is reached, the relevant issue is the facial validity of the classroom-display requirement under the Establishment and Free Exercise Clauses, not the validity of every government religious display. Any merits resolution of those questions remains conditional on review.

Conditional on any grant, P(disposition in the certiorari order itself) is **0.45**, versus **0.55** for a grant opening further merits proceedings. Companion-driven vacatur and remand is the principal summary possibility; an immediate substantive summary reversal is less likely. The jurisdictional differences between the two statutes and Louisiana's request for outright dismissal provide a reason to hear the Louisiana case, but also prevent an automatic assumption that a Texas merits ruling would settle it.

Conditional on denial, P(a noted dissent or statement respecting denial) is **0.06**. Expect no writing. A statement about access to judicial review or the continuing force of school-religion precedent is a secondary possibility, with Sotomayor or Jackson plausible authors, but denial of this state-filed protective petition is not itself a rejection of the parents' constitutional claims. Per-Justice probabilities in the structured forecast are unconditional chances of writing on the disposing order, not votes and not conditional denial probabilities.
