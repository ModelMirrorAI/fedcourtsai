The Supreme Court will very likely refuse to hear this case, with only about a one-in-a-hundred chance of taking it. Beckie Boddie, representing herself, says a federal court disposed of her Belize property in a Federal Trade Commission fraud case without notifying her. The appeals court ruled only that she could not appeal because she was not a party and had not taken part earlier. That short, unpublished ruling applies settled rules, and the government did not even file a response.
