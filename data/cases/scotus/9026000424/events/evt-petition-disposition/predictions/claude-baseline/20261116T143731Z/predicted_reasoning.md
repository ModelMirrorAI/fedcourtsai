Any review would be confined to that threshold question (Question 1's appellate-review half).
- **Summary route.** Conditional on a grant, a cert-order disposition (a vacatur and remand for the court of appeals to reconsider the nonparty-appeal or intervention ruling) is about as likely as plenary review. No intervening decision of the Court is pending that would drive a GVR.
- **Separate writing.** The denial will be unsigned and without noted dissent or statement.
