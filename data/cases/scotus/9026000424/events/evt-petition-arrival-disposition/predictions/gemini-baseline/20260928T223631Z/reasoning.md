The baseline grant rate for the "baseline" salience band at the arrival moment (pooled over recent terms, such as OT2017-OT2025) hovers around 3.9% to 5.9%. However, this base rate encompasses a variety of petitions filed by private parties.

In this specific case, the petitioner is proceeding pro se and is attempting to sue the United States District Court for the District of Maryland. While the petition is technically paid (and thus part of the paid segment rather than IFP), lawsuits of this nature by pro se litigants against federal courts are almost universally frivolous and stand virtually no chance of being granted certiorari. 

I have therefore heavily discounted the baseline P(grant) from ~5% down to 0.2%.
