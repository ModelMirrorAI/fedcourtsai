The Supreme Court is very likely (about nine in ten) to agree to hear this case. The case asks
whether the federal law creating a private Horseracing Integrity and Safety Authority unlawfully
hands government enforcement power to a private body. The Fifth Circuit appeals court struck the
enforcement provisions down, while the Sixth Circuit upheld them. The federal government has filed
its own appeal, and every party, including the challengers, agrees the Court should decide the
question. The Court almost always reviews a lower-court decision that strikes down an Act of
Congress.
