The Supreme Court is very likely to grant review in this case (about an 88% chance, or nearly nine in ten). A federal appeals court struck down the enforcement rules of the federal Horseracing Integrity and Safety Act as an unconstitutional transfer of government power to a private organization, directly conflicting with another federal appeals court that upheld the same law after an earlier Supreme Court remand. Because a federal law was invalidated, the U.S. government has filed a companion appeal, and all opposing parties agree the Supreme Court should hear the case, review is almost certain.
