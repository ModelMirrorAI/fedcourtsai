P(grant) = 0.15. The case falls in the `baseline` salience band (base rate ~5.0% for reached). However, I adjust significantly upward because the petition identifies a deep and acknowledged circuit split on whether the Help America Vote Act (HAVA) creates private rights of action enforceable under § 1983 (1st/6th vs. 3rd/9th/11th). Furthermore, the Seventh Circuit introduced an Article III standing holding that creates a separate axis of division, testing the boundaries of *TransUnion* and *FDA v. Alliance for Hippocratic Medicine* regarding whether a total deprivation of a statutorily mandated administrative process constitutes a concrete injury. A major upward factor is the DOJ's June 2025 warning letter to the respondent (Wisconsin Elections Commission) regarding this exact practice, which signals strong federal interest. The respondent waived the right to respond; however, given the circuit split and federal interest, the Court is highly likely to call for a response (CFR) or CVSG rather than simply denying it out of hand. If CFR'd, the grant prospects remain strong. I am uncertain if the Court will view the standing issue as a clean vehicle to reach the HAVA § 1983 question, which tempers the probability slightly from a higher mark.
