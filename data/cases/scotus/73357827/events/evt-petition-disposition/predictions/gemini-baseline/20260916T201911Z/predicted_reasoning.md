Distributed once so far (for the Long Conference on 9/28). Because the respondent waived their right to respond despite a clear circuit split and a DOJ warning letter, expect the Court to either Call for a Response (CFR) or Call for the Views of the Solicitor General (CVSG) at the first conference. Therefore, expect at least one and possibly two further distributions before a final decision on the petition. A CVSG is reasonably likely given the DOJ's prior involvement in WEC's HAVA compliance, though a CFR is the more immediate next step. If granted, the Court will likely address both the Article III standing question (whether total deprivation of a mandated administrative process is a concrete injury) and the § 1983 HAVA right of action, resolving the split on whether HAVA creates individually enforceable rights. A summary GVR is unlikely as there is no clear intervening precedent. Should the Court deny, expect a dissent from denial from conservative justices given the election integrity context and the restrictive view of standing applied to right-leaning advocacy groups by the Seventh Circuit.
