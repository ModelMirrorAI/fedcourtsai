## What happened

Wisconsin Voter Alliance and two of its members filed complaints with the Wisconsin Elections Commission (WEC), arguing WEC itself was violating a federal election law, the Help America Vote Act. WEC returned both complaints without deciding them, saying it could not rule on complaints against itself. The petitioners sued WEC, seeking a court order requiring WEC to provide the hearing and decision the law calls for. A federal district court dismissed the suit, finding the petitioners had not shown a concrete enough harm to sue in federal court. The Seventh Circuit Court of Appeals agreed and affirmed the dismissal.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether this federal election law lets private citizens and groups sue in federal court to enforce it, given that federal appeals courts disagree on this point. The Court is also asked whether a state agency's total failure to give someone the hearing and decision the law requires counts as a real, concrete harm that lets a federal court hear the case, or whether it is just a technical, procedural shortfall that keeps the courthouse doors closed.
