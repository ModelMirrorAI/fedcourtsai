This is a federal habeas case challenging a state court conviction, meaning the strict AEDPA deference standard applies. For the petitioner to win, the state court's decision must have been contrary to, or an unreasonable application of, clearly established Supreme Court precedent. Because the Supreme Court has not clearly established this rule, the state court's refusal to apply it cannot be "unreasonable" under AEDPA. The Court routinely denies even deep circuit splits when presented in a federal habeas posture for exactly this reason. Furthermore, the state (respondent) waived its right to file a Brief in Opposition, and the petition is proceeding to its first conference without one.
