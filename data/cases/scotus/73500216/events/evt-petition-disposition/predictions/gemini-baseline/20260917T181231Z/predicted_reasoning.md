The Court will not take this case to resolve the split because it could not grant relief anyway. If granted, the Court would likely take the sole question presented regarding implied juror bias for crime victims, but a summary disposition is exceedingly unlikely. I expect no separate writing or dissent from denial, as the vehicle issues make this an unattractive case even for Justices sympathetic to the petitioner's underlying Sixth Amendment claim.
