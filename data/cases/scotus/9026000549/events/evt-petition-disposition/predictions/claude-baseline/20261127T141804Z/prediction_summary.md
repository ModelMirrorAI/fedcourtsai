The Supreme Court will very likely decline to hear this case — about a one-in-twenty chance it agrees to review it. A man pushed by Border Patrol agents during a checkpoint car search argues he should be able to sue the United States, and that appeals courts disagree on the rule. But the government did not even file a response, the lower-court ruling was unpublished, and the claimed disagreement rests on one thirty-year-old decision. A well-written petition and the lower court's own unease keep the odds from being lower.
