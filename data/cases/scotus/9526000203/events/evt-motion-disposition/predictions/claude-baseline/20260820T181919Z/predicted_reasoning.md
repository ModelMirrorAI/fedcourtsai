# Forecast: 26A203 — National Park Service, et al. v. National Trust for Historic Preservation

**What the Court will do:** grant the application and stay the district court's
modified preliminary injunction pending disposition of a forthcoming petition
for certiorari (and, if granted, the case's resolution). P(unqualified grant)
= 0.70.

**Timing.** The response was due August 18 at noon and the D.C. Circuit's
fourteen-day stay of its own ruling expires around August 21, when its mandate
issues. The Court will act on that clock: expect a disposition within days of
the response — most likely between August 19 and August 24, and almost
certainly before the end of August. An interim administrative stay from the
Chief Justice or the Court is possible if deliberation runs past the mandate
date.

**Referral.** The Chief Justice, as Circuit Justice for the D.C. Circuit,
requested a response within one day of filing. On an application of this
profile — the United States as applicant, the President and the Executive
Office of the President among the enjoined parties — single-Justice resolution
is very unlikely. Expect the application to be referred to the full Court and
disposed of by the Court itself: P(referral after prediction time) = 0.78,
with the residual mostly the chance that referral never appears as a
parseable docket signal rather than that the Chief acts alone.

**Response.** Already requested on the record I was shown (August 14, due
August 18) — that rung of the escalation ladder has fired, so the
response-requested increment is vacuous for this cell; I state 0.99 to record
that a requested response is effectively certain to stand on the docket at
resolution.

**Amicus activity.** The frozen record shows zero amicus briefs, but the
application docket's attorney list already carries counsel for Members of
Congress (Constitutional Accountability Center), the Society for the Rule of
Law, Campaign Legal Center/CREW, and the State of Indiana — several of them
the same amici who filed in the D.C. Circuit. Expect multiple amicus filings
within the response window: P(count rises past zero) = 0.93.

**Shape of the disposition.** Most likely: a short order (possibly with a
brief per curiam explanation, given the Court's recent practice on
high-profile applications) granting the stay, with two to three noted
dissents from the Justices most skeptical of emergency relief for the
government (expect Sotomayor and Jackson, plausibly joined by Kagan; none of
this is scored at this stage). Less likely routes: a grant of certiorari
before judgment treated together with the stay; a partial stay tailored to
particular construction activities, which the resolver would read
denial-first — that mixed-order risk is a real part of my 0.30 complement,
alongside outright denial on equitable grounds (that construction, once
built, moots the preservation claims). Withdrawal or dismissal of the
application is very unlikely (< 2%).
