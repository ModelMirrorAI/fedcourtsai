# Forecast

The Chief Justice will refer the application to the full Court promptly after the requested response. The Court is more likely than not to enter an unqualified stay of the injunction pending further review, probably within one to three days of the response filing and before or just after the D.C. Circuit's temporary hold on its judgment expires. The application presents a direct institutional dispute over control of the White House, and Judge Rao's dissent gives the applicants a developed path through standing, statutory authority, and the equities that is likely to attract the votes needed for interim relief.

The strongest contrary consideration is the lower courts' tailoring: underground security work may continue, and the injunction permits above-ground work needed for safety and structural integrity. That makes denial a substantial possibility because the applicants' claimed irreparable security harm is partly accommodated. Still, the requested response, the federal government's application, the divided appellate panel, and the time-sensitive construction dispute make a grant the likelier disposition.
