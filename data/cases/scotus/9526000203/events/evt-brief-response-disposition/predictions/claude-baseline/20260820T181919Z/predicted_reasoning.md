# Forecast — 26A203, National Park Service, et al. v. National Trust for Historic Preservation

**The Court will grant the application and stay the modified preliminary
injunction pending appeal (and pending any petition for certiorari).**
P(unqualified grant) = 0.72.

**Disposition.** The disposing order will be an outright grant of the stay,
not a partial one. The D.C. Circuit stayed its own judgment only through
August 21 and directed its mandate to issue that day, so the Court will act
fast: expect an order within days of the August 18 response — most likely by
or shortly after August 21, possibly preceded by a short administrative stay
from the Chief Justice to bridge the mandate date. Timing is not a scored
claim; it is stated because it disciplines the rest of this forecast.

**Referral.** The application was submitted to the Chief Justice as Circuit
Justice for the D.C. Circuit and has not yet been referred. He will refer it
to the full Court — a contested, government-filed application with six amicus
filings and a published 2-1 court of appeals decision is full-Court business,
and the disposing order will carry the standard "and by him referred to the
Court" recital. P(referral after prediction time) = 0.90; the residue covers a
withdrawal or mooting before any order and the small chance of a single-Justice
disposition.

**Response request.** Already on the docket — the Chief Justice called for a
response on August 14, due August 18 at noon. Nothing remains to forecast on
this rung; the stated 0.02 prices only a second, superseding request, and the
harness will mask the claim as vacuous.

**Amicus activity.** Six amicus briefs are already on the docket (Biazzo,
Members of Congress, Society for the Rule of Law, David Boyle, Campaign Legal
Center/CREW, and the State of Indiana in support of applicants). With the
disposition expected within days, few if any further briefs will arrive. The
0.10 on the increment claim additionally reflects that the harness's amicus
counter matches the phrase "amicus curiae", which the application docket's
"Amicus brief of X submitted." entry style never uses — so the resolution-time
count will most likely still read zero against the frozen zero (see
`flags.json`).

**If the stay is granted** — the likely shape: an unsigned order staying the
injunction pending disposition of the appeal and any timely cert petition, on
the reasoning that the equities and the government's likelihood of success on
its separation-of-powers and statutory-coverage arguments (Judge Rao's dissent
is the roadmap) favor the Executive's control of the White House complex.
Expect noted dissents from two to three of Justices Sotomayor, Kagan, and
Jackson, possibly with a written dissent about irreversible construction on a
historic site. **Should it deny**, expect a brief order with noted dissents
from the other wing, and the mandate below issuing on schedule.
