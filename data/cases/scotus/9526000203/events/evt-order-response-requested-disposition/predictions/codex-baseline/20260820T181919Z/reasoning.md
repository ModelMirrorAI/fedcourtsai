I stop at 0.68 because the lower-court majority produced a detailed record against emergency relief. The majority also emphasized that the government had earlier described the underground and above-ground projects as independent, weakening its later claim that the ballroom is inseparable from security work. Without the Supreme Court application text, I cannot assess whether the applicants answer those record-specific points or seek narrower relief.
