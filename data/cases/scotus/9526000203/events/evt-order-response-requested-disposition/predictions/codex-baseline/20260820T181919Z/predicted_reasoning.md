# Forecast

The Chief Justice has already requested a response, due at noon on August 18, 2026. No further response request is expected. The application will likely be referred to the full Court after the response arrives, and at least one amicus brief will likely be filed before disposition.

The Court will probably grant the application in an unqualified form, by a short order issued before or around the D.C. Circuit mandate date of August 21. The likely basis is that the federal applicants have shown a sufficient prospect of prevailing on standing or ultra vires authority, coupled with asserted security and construction harms from leaving the injunction in force. The grant will permit the challenged above-ground work to proceed while further review is sought.

The close alternative is denial. The injunction already permits below-ground security work, protective covering, site-safety work, and measures needed for presidential security, which gives the Court a practical ground to leave the lower-court judgment undisturbed while review continues.
