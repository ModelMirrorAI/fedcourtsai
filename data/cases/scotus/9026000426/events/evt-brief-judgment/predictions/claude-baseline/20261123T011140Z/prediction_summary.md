The Supreme Court is very likely (about nine in ten) to overturn the lower-court ruling that blocked the government's policy for deporting people to "third countries" not named in their removal orders. The predicted judgment is a reversal by a 6-3 vote, most likely on the ground that federal law bars this kind of class lawsuit in district court. The main reason: the Court already paused the lower court's judgment in this case, over three dissents, when it agreed to hear it.
