The Supreme Court is very likely — about a nine-in-ten chance (91%) — to overturn the First Circuit's ruling, most likely reversing it by a 6–3 vote. The Court has already paused the lower court's order three times over the same three liberal Justices' dissents, signaling strong skepticism of blocking the government's third-country deportation policy across an entire class of noncitizens. Expect the conservative majority to rule that federal immigration law forbids classwide declaratory orders and policy-wide vacaturs — court orders wiping out an agency rule nationwide — that restrain deportation operations, while leaving room for individual lawsuits.
