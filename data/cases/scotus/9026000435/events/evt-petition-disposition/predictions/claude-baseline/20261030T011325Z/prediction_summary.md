The Supreme Court will very likely turn this case away; I put the chance it takes the case, or sends it back to the lower court, at about one in twenty-five. A TV station owner says the appeals court wrongly deferred to the labor board about whether its news producers count as supervisors, who are not covered by union law. The government did not even file a reply, and the lower ruling was unpublished. The best hope is a quick send-back for the court to reconsider without deferring to the board.
