The Supreme Court is very likely to turn down Nexstar's appeal; I give it a 7% chance of obtaining review or a short order sending the case back. Nexstar argues that an appeals court gave too much weight to the labor board's legal views when deciding whether television producers can unionize. But the appeals court also relied on specific gaps in Nexstar's evidence. If the Supreme Court intervenes, a request for reconsideration is more likely than a full hearing.
