# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely **deny certiorari without a separate writing**. The record has one distribution, for the November 13, 2026 conference, and no relists or CVSG. The modal path is denial on the order list following that conference, likely in mid-November. If the Court requests a response instead, disposition will move later, plausibly into the winter; the petition is not likely to require extended treatment.

There is a **20% chance of at least one further distribution** beyond the one already shown. Zero additional distributions is the modal count; on the further-attention branch, one or two additional distributions are more likely than a long series. This includes redistribution after a response request as well as a relist or reschedule.

A **new CVSG is very unlikely (0.3%)**. The government is the respondent, not an absent nonparty whose views must be solicited. If the Court wants the Solicitor General's position after the filed waiver, an ordinary request for a response is the expected mechanism.

There is a **7% chance of any grant**. Conditional on such a grant, there is an **80% chance of disposition in the cert order itself**, principally a grant, vacatur, and remand for further consideration of the legal-review standard under Loper Bright. A remand would allow the Second Circuit to clarify whether independent statutory interpretation leaves its factual rationale intact. It would not itself decide that Nexstar's producers are supervisors. A summary reversal deciding their status outright is less likely than a GVR because the classification depends on the evidentiary record.

If the Court instead takes the case for briefing and argument, it will most likely narrow the petition's second question to the standard for reviewing the Board's statutory interpretation under section 2(11) after Loper Bright. It is less likely to take the first question as a categorical conflict over all television producers. Any merits reasoning remains conditional on a grant; denial will announce no resolution of either question.

Conditional on denial, a dissent or statement respecting denial has a **2.5% chance**. No separate writing is expected. In the unlikely writing branch, Gorsuch or Thomas is the likeliest author to emphasize independent judicial review of agency legal conclusions. No cert vote lineup is forecast.
