The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Nexstar Media Inc. v. National Labor Relations Board*, with only a 1.5% chance (about one in sixty-five) of granting the petition. Nexstar asks the Court to overturn a federal appeals court order requiring its Rochester television station to bargain with a union that includes news and digital producers, arguing that the appeals court gave too much deference to the labor board's findings. However, the appeals court's ruling was an unpublished, non-binding summary order based on specific workplace facts, the federal government waived its right to respond, and the same appeals court has already clarified in a separate published decision that it no longer defers to the labor board on legal questions.
