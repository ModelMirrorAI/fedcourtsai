# Forecast

The Court will most likely deny certiorari. P(any grant), including a GVR or summary reversal, is **12%**.

The arrival record shows **zero distributions and no CVSG**. I expect an initial conference distribution after the response period, followed by denial without a repeat conference. The modal path therefore adds one distribution to the present zero. P(at least one additional distribution) is **97%**: here the declared `relist-increment` measures entry into conference consideration, not necessarily an actual relist after a first conference. A further repeat conference is a minority possibility. My central timing forecast is disposition between December 2026 and March 2027, subject to briefing extensions.

I expect **no CVSG**; P(a new CVSG) is **0.5%**. The NLRB is already a respondent, so the federal government's position can be supplied through ordinary opposition briefing rather than a separate invitation.

If the Court grants relief, I expect a **summary route rather than plenary review**, with **80% conditional probability**. A GVR for reconsideration of the legal standard under Loper Bright is the leading grant scenario, not a summary ruling conclusively classifying all television producers as supervisors. Conditional on plenary review, the Court would most likely narrow the second question to whether the panel independently interpreted the statutory supervisory criteria or instead deferred to the Board's interpretation. It would be less likely to take the first question as a categorical conflict about every broadcast producer's status.

The likely obstacle to review is the evidence-specific basis of the judgment: the panel found inadequate proof of independent assignment authority and accountability for others' work. A denial would leave that judgment intact without endorsing its legal-deference language. Conditional on denial, P(a noted dissent or statement respecting denial) is **3%**; the modal forecast is an unexplained denial with no separate writing. I do not predict individual cert votes or authorship.
