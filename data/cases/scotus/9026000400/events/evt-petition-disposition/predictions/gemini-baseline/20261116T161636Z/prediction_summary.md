The Supreme Court is unlikely to take up *Nestlé USA, Inc. v. Falcone*, with about a one-in-seven chance (14%) of granting review or sending the case back to the lower court, so review will most likely be denied. Although business groups support Nestlé and the Court asked the plaintiff to respond, the lower-court ruling is a short, non-binding opinion with messy factual disputes over discontinued chocolate-wrapper sustainability labels and alternative ways to calculate damages, making it a poor test case compared to cleaner class-action appeals already pending this Term.
