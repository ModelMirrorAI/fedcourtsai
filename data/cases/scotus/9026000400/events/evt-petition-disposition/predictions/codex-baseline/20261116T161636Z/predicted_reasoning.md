# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court is more likely to **deny certiorari** than to grant it. Any grant, including summary relief, has a **30%** probability. The petition has substantial procedural questions and organized business support, but the unpublished, revisable class-certification order and disputed factual premises make denial the modal outcome.

## Next procedural steps

The record shows **one distribution**, entered October 28 for November 13, and a requested response due November 27. There has been **no recorded redistribution and no CVSG**. The Court will most likely await the requested opposition and any reply, then distribute the petition again. The probability of at least one additional distribution is **96%**. This would ordinarily be a response-driven rescheduling, not necessarily a substantive relist. The modal path is one additional distribution before denial; a grant path is more likely to include another one or two distributions beyond that. Disposition is most likely in early 2027, broadly January through March, with briefing extensions capable of moving it later.

The Court is unlikely to seek the Solicitor General's views: **2.5%** for a new CVSG. If one issues, it is more likely after the response and renewed consideration than before the response arrives, and would lengthen the forecast timetable.

## If review is granted

The most plausible selected issue is **QP2**, whether a damages class may be certified when some members lack Article III injury, potentially reformulated around Rule 23's predominance requirement rather than an absolute constitutional bar. QP1, concerning an informed consumer's standing to seek prospective labeling relief, is another credible grant question, alone or with QP2. The Court is less likely to accept all three questions together; QP3's full-refund model offers a narrower, more fact-dependent alternative.

Conditional on any grant, **plenary briefing and argument are favored 90% to 10% over a summary disposition**. A summary order would more plausibly correct the damages analysis than invoke a currently unidentified intervening lead decision. If plenary review is granted, the likely focus is the evidence of injury and the relationship between certification requirements and the asserted classwide relief. No unconditional merits judgment is forecast.

## If review is denied

A denial without a separate writing is most likely. Conditional on denial, the probability of some noted dissent or statement is **8%**. Kavanaugh is the most plausible author; an occasional writing by Thomas, Alito or Gorsuch is also possible. This is a forecast of writings, not a claim to know the certiorari votes.
