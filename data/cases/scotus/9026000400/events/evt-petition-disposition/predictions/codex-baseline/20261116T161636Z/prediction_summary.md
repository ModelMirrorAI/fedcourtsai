The Supreme Court is likely to decline Nestlé's appeal, with about a three-in-ten chance it agrees to review the case or grants other relief. The case asks when consumers may sue as a group over allegedly misleading chocolate labels, including whether people who were not harmed can belong to that group. Five supporting briefs and the Court's request for a response show interest. Still, the lower court's temporary, adjustable ruling and unresolved factual questions may make this a poor case for settling national rules.
