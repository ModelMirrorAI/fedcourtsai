The Supreme Court will most likely decline to hear Nestlé's challenge to a class action over "sustainably sourced" chocolate labels; the chance it takes the case is about one in eleven. Business groups filed five supporting briefs and the Court asked the other side to respond, which shows some interest. But the lower-court ruling is a short, non-precedential decision, and it found all class members were harmed, which makes this a weak case for deciding whether a class may include uninjured people.
