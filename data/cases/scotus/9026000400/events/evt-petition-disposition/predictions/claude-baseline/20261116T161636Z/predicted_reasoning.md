# Forecast — No. 26-400, Nestlé USA, Inc. v. Falcone

The docket shows one distribution (Conference of Nov. 13, 2026), overtaken by the Court's Oct. 28 request for a response, due Nov. 27, 2026.

- **Further distribution.** The respondent will file a brief in opposition, probably after one extension, and the petition will be distributed again for a conference in January or February 2027. I expect that one further distribution to be the last or nearly so: at most one true relist after it.
- **CVSG.** The Court will not call for the views of the Solicitor General.
- **Disposition.** The Court will deny certiorari. The opposition will stress that the decision below is an unpublished memorandum, that the panel found every class member injured on an alternative ground, and that the damages model may be replaced on remand.
- **If granted.** The Court would most likely limit the grant to Question 2 (whether a damages class may be certified when some members lack Article III standing), possibly with Question 3 (the Comcast damages-model question), and would leave Question 1 behind. It would set the case for briefing and argument rather than dispose of it summarily; a GVR is unlikely because no intervening decision bears on these questions.
- **Separate writing.** A denial will most likely come without any noted dissent or statement. If a Justice writes, Justice Kavanaugh is the most likely, given his dissent in Labcorp v. Davis.
