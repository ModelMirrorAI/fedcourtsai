The Supreme Court will probably decline to hear Nestlé's appeal — the chance it takes the case is about one in eight. Nestlé is challenging a class action (a lawsuit brought for millions of chocolate buyers at once) over "sustainably sourced" labels. The Court asked the buyers' side to respond, and five business groups filed supporting briefs, which lifts the odds above normal. But the lower-court ruling is a short, non-precedential decision with fact-specific wrinkles, making it a poor case for setting national rules.
