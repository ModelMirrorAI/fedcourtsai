The Supreme Court is unlikely to take this case, with roughly a one-in-seven chance (14%) of granting review and an 86% chance of denying Nestlé's appeal. Although the Court asked the plaintiff to respond and major business groups urge the Justices to clarify when large consumer class actions (group lawsuits) may include unharmed buyers, the lower court's short, non-precedential decision contains serious procedural flaws—including an invitation for the trial judge to swap out the challenged full-refund damages formula—making this a poor test case.
