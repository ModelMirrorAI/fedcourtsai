The Supreme Court is more likely to decline Nestlé's appeal, although there is about a one-in-three chance it agrees to review it (32%). The case raises important questions about when consumers can sue together and seek changes to product labels. Five supporting briefs and the Court's request for a response show interest. But the lower court's narrow, unpublished ruling and disputes about whether purchasers were harmed could make this an awkward case for resolving broader rules. A denial would not approve the labeling practices.
