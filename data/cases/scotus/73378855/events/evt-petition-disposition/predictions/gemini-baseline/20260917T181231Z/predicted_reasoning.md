Distributed once so far; expect no further relists before a decision on the petition. The questions presented ask whether a state supreme court can affirm a punitive damages award without a written opinion. This presents a very poor vehicle for Supreme Court review, as there is no lower court reasoning to evaluate. No CVSG is expected, as the case involves private parties and no distinct federal government interests are implicated. If granted, the Court would likely focus on the limits of punitive damages under *BMW v. Gore* and *State Farm v. Campbell*. A summary GVR is unlikely, as there is no relevant intervening precedent. Should the Court deny, expect no separate writing.
