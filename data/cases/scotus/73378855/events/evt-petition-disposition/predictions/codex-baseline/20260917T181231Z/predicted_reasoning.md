# Forecast

The Court will deny the petition without a separate writing, most likely in an order following the September 28, 2026 conference, in late September or early October. The provisioned docket records one distribution, entered July 8 for that conference, and no CVSG. I expect zero further distributions; the probability of at least one more is 4%. If further consideration occurs, one additional distribution is the likeliest alternative. I assign a 0.1% probability to a new CVSG and expect none: the dispute presents no apparent distinctive federal executive interest requiring the Solicitor General's views.

The petition's assertion that punitive damages received no meaningful review will not persuade the Court to intervene. The December 10, 2024 hearing transcript supplied with the opposition records actual consideration of reprehensibility, the damages ratio, and the request for remittitur. The state appellate court's unexplained affirmance therefore does not present the clean absence-of-review vehicle the petition describes. The Court will leave this particular judgment standing rather than use the petition to impose a new written-opinion requirement.

If review is granted, the likeliest question is a narrowed form of QP 1: whether due process requires a state appellate court to explain its independent review of a punitive award where the trial court held a hearing and expressed reasons orally. QP 2's broader claim of unavailable judicial review is a weaker vehicle on this record. These are conditional forecasts, not predictions that a denial will decide either constitutional question.

Conditional on any grant, I assign 30% to disposition in the cert order itself and 70% to plenary review. There is no identified intervening decision supporting a routine GVR; the more plausible grant would address the explanation requirement after briefing and argument. A summary vacatur for further constitutional consideration remains a minority alternative. Conditional on denial, I assign 1% to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. I expect none and do not forecast an individual Justice's cert vote or authorship.
