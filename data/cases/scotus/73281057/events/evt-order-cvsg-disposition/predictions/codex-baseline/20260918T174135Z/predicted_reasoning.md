# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition after receiving the Solicitor General's views. The probability of any grant, including a limited grant or summary disposition, is 43%.

The baseline records two distributions, on March 25 and June 2, 2026, and the June 22 CVSG. I expect one or two additional distributions after the government's brief arrives; the probability of at least one additional distribution is 97%. The first distribution preceded the Court's request for a response, so the two recorded distributions should not be read as two completed, fully briefed conferences. The expected next step is consideration of the invited brief, not a second invitation. The new-CVSG probability is recorded as zero; that increment is vacuous for this already-CVSG cell.

I expect the government to emphasize the federal regulatory consequences of either constitutional rule and the limits of this facial challenge. The modal path is a recommendation against review followed by denial, rather than a finding that all corporate reporting requirements escape constitutional scrutiny. A late-2026 or first-half-2027 disposition is the forecast horizon, dependent on delivery of the invited brief; timing is not a scored claim.

If the Court grants, I expect plenary briefing and argument rather than disposition in the cert order: the conditional summary-route probability is 10%. The likeliest question for a limited grant is a narrowed version of QP 1: whether compelled public narratives explaining drug-pricing decisions qualify as commercial speech and what justification those disclosures require. QP 2, concerning investment-backed expectations in trade secrets, remains a substantial alternative, but the facial posture and possible ripeness obstacle make its inclusion less likely than QP 1's. Any merits discussion would concern those bounded issues, not an unconditional prediction that the Court will invalidate Oregon's statute.

Conditional on denial, I expect an ordinary order without a separate writing. The probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial is 14%; no individual Justice's vote or authorship is forecast.
