Plenary review is much likelier than a summary disposition (such as a GVR), as this case presents major, unresolved constitutional questions rather than an error-correction scenario.

Should the Court deny review, a dissent from denial (likely from conservative Justices such as Justice Thomas or Justice Alito) is highly probable, given the divided Ninth Circuit opinion and the significant property and speech rights at stake.
