The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Richard Stuart Ross v. United States*, with a 90% chance of denial (a one-in-ten chance of granting review). Although eight friend-of-the-court briefs were filed supporting Ross and the Court asked the federal government to file a response, every federal appeals court to consider the issue agrees that the government does not owe a property claimant attorney's fees when a civil forfeiture case is voluntarily dismissed without a final court ruling on the merits. In addition, the trial court found that a separate statutory exception for cases involving multiple competing claims independently barred Ross from recovering fees, making this case a poor vehicle for the Justices to take up.
