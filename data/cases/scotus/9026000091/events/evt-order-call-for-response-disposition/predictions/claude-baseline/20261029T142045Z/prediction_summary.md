We predict the Supreme Court will most likely turn down (deny) Richard Ross's request
to hear his case. We put the chance it agrees to hear it at about one in eight. Ross
wants the government to pay his lawyers' fees after it dropped its attempt to seize
his property under civil-forfeiture law and gave the property back. Several advocacy
groups and two senators back him, and the Court has asked the government to respond.
Those are signs of interest. But four appeals courts agree on the main question
against him, and the Court usually takes cases where lower courts disagree.
