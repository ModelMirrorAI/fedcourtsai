The Supreme Court is likely to decline Richard Ross's appeal, with about a
one-in-five chance of granting some form of review (22%). Ross wants the
government to pay his legal bills after it dropped an effort to take his
property and returned the money. Eight supporting briefs and the Court's
request for a government response show meaningful interest. But the lower
courts are not clearly divided on the main question, and another statutory
obstacle could prevent Ross from recovering fees even if his main argument wins.
