# Forecast of the Court's action

The petition has been distributed **twice**, for November 6 and November 13, 2026. The Court is more likely than not to distribute it at least once more beyond those two entries (**58%**). The modal positive-increment path is one or two further distributions, although an invitation to the Solicitor General would create a much longer interval before final action.

The Court ultimately is likelier to **deny review** than to grant it: the probability of any grant-family disposition is **32%**. The petition's recognition-versus-statutory-interpretation issue and divided lower court will attract attention, but the alleged conflict concerns materially different sovereign entities and legal settings. The Court can decline review without endorsing either side's account of Afghan statehood or ownership of the blocked assets.

There is a **35% chance of a new call for the Solicitor General's views**, none having issued yet. If the Court takes that intermediate step, it will most likely do so during the next several conferences, because the United States has an immediate interest in the treatment of Afghan central-bank reserves. The invited brief would postpone disposition into 2027. Without an invitation, disposition in November or December 2026 is the likelier timing path.

If review is granted, the Court will most likely take the petition's question in a more concrete form: whether Executive recognition of Afghanistan conclusively establishes foreign-state status, and what independent analysis courts must undertake concerning DAB under the FSIA. It is less likely to accept the question's characterization as a general choice between judging and obeying government websites. A ruling on that narrower question would not itself establish the creditors' right to the money.

Conditional on any grant, **plenary review is the likelier route**: 75%, versus **25% for a disposition in the cert order itself**. A derivative GVR following treatment of a Vide-linked petition is more plausible than an immediate summary reversal on the current record. No provisioned companion grant or intervening controlling ruling supplies a present basis for predicting a GVR outright.

Conditional on denial, the Court will most likely issue an ordinary unsigned denial, with **10% probability of a dissent or statement respecting denial**. If a Justice writes, Thomas or Alito, followed by Gorsuch, is the likeliest source. The denial will not decide whether DAB assets can lawfully be attached, and no majority merits opinion is forecast.
