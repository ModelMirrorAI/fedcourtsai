The Supreme Court is more likely to decline this appeal than to hear it; I put the chance of review at about one in three. Terrorism victims seek access to billions of dollars held for Afghanistan's central bank. Their petition asks whether judges must independently decide the bank's legal protection from seizure. A divided lower court and the Supreme Court's renewed consideration make review plausible, but the claimed conflict with other courts is imperfect. The Court may first ask the federal government for its views.
