# Forecast — No. 26-294, Owens v. Taliban

**State:** distributed twice: the 11/6/2026 conference, then redistributed on Nov 9 for 11/13/2026, so relisted once. No CVSG. No response from the Taliban, who have not appeared. Companions 26-292 (Havlish) and 26-293 (Ashton) are moving with it.

- **Relists:** I expect at least one more distribution (≈60%). Most likely the Court does not decide the petition on 11/13. It either invites the Solicitor General's views, which brings a later redistribution once the SG files, or relists again. All three petitions move together.
- **CVSG:** about 35% that the Court calls for the SG's views. If it does, likely at or right after the 11/13 conference (mid-November 2026). With no adverse party briefing and the executive's own statements at the center of the question, the SG's views are the natural next step.
- **Outcome:** denial is more likely than not. P(grant) ≈ 30%, and most of that runs through a CVSG followed by a grant later in OT2026.
- **If granted:** the Court takes the QP as written, or as a reformulation: whether a court decides "foreign state" / "agency or instrumentality" status under FSIA §§ 1603–1609 independently, or defers to executive statements. It would probably consolidate all three petitions and appoint an amicus to defend the judgment unless the United States does. Plenary review, not a GVR or summary reversal (≈4% summary).
- **If denied:** a real chance (≈30%) of a dissent from, or a statement respecting, the denial. Most likely from Justice Gorsuch or Justice Thomas, possibly joined by Justice Alito, about courts handing statutory interpretation to executive litigation positions.
