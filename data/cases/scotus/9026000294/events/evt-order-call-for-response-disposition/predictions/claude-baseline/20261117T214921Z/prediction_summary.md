The Supreme Court will probably decline to hear this case, though it is a real contender: I put the chance of review at a little over one in three. Victims of the 1998 embassy bombings want to collect a judgment against the Taliban from frozen Afghan central-bank funds, and the lower court said it must defer to the government's view that the bank belongs to a foreign state. The Court has asked the Solicitor General, the federal government's Supreme Court lawyer, for advice, and I expect that advice to favor leaving the ruling alone.
