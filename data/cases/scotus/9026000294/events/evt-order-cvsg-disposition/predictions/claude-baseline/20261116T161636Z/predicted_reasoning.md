# Forecast — Owens v. Taliban, No. 26-294

- **State now:** two distributions (conferences of Nov 6 and Nov 13, 2026) and a CVSG issued Nov 16, 2026. No response from the Taliban; none will come.
- **Solicitor General:** files the invited brief in spring 2027, most likely May, covering this petition together with Havlish (26-292) and Ashton (26-293). The brief most likely recommends denial, defending judicial deference to the Executive on whether Afghanistan is a "foreign state" and on which government speaks for it.
- **Further distribution:** the petition is distributed at least once more after the SG's brief, most likely for a June 2027 conference; one further distribution is the base case, two if the Court relists before acting.
- **No further CVSG** (already issued).
- **Disposition:** denied, about 73% likely, by the end of June 2027 or, if the brief slips, at the start of October Term 2027. The three petitions are resolved together.
- **If the Court grants review of the issue:** it takes Havlish as the lead case and either grants this petition and consolidates, or holds it and later grants, vacates and remands. The question taken would be the petition's deference question as written, likely alongside Havlish's own questions. A summary reversal is unlikely; a cert-order disposition of this docket (GVR after a hold) is about one in five given a grant.
- **If granted, likely ground:** the meaning of "foreign state" in the FSIA is for courts, with formal Executive recognition conclusive but informal litigation statements not binding — a reversal or vacatur on a narrow ground.
- **Separate writing on a denial:** not expected (under 10%). If one appears, it would come from Justice Thomas, Alito or Gorsuch.
