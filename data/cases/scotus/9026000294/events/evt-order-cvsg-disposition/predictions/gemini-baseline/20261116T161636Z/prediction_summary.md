We predict the Supreme Court will deny review in *Owens v. The Taliban*, with about a one-in-four (25%) chance of granting the petition. Victims of the 1998 U.S. Embassy bombings seek $3.5 billion in frozen Afghan central-bank funds, and the Court recently asked the Solicitor General—the federal government's top appellate lawyer—for the government's views. Although five appeals-court judges dissented below and all nine Justices can hear this petition, the Justice Department previously opposed seizing these reserves to protect presidential foreign-policy authority, and companion 9/11 cases offer a narrower statutory path.
