# Forecast — Owens v. Taliban, No. 26-294

- **State:** distributed twice (11/6, then 11/13). This is the first relist. No response, no waiver and no CVSG are on the docket.
- **Next step:** the Court does not deny outright at 11/13. It either calls for the Solicitor General's views (about a 35% chance, most likely at or right after the 11/13 conference) or relists again. Overall, I expect at least one further distribution (62%), with the three Taliban petitions (26-292, 26-293, 26-294) moving in lockstep.
- **Disposition:** denial is somewhat more likely than a grant (73/27). The likeliest grant path runs through a CVSG and an SG brief, with a grant late in OT2026 or in OT2027.
- **If granted:** the Court takes the QP essentially as written: whether FSIA "agency or instrumentality" status (and so immunity from attachment) is for the courts to decide independently, rather than by deferring to Executive statements. It may add the Menashi question of whether a Taliban-controlled DAB is an instrumentality of "Afghanistan" at all. The Court would appoint amicus to defend the judgment unless the United States does. The case gets plenary review, not a summary disposition (6%).
- **If denied:** a written dissent from denial or a statement respecting denial is a real possibility (about 35%). Gorsuch is the likeliest writer, then Thomas and Alito.
