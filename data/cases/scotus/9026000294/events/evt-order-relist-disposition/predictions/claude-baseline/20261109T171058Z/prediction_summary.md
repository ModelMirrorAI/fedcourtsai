Terrorism victims holding court judgments against the Taliban want to collect from frozen Afghan central-bank money in New York. The appeals court said the money is protected by foreign sovereign immunity, mainly because it deferred to Executive Branch statements. The Supreme Court has now held the case over for a second conference. I predict it will probably turn the case down, but the chance it agrees to hear it is about one in four (27%). A likely next step is asking the federal government for its views.
