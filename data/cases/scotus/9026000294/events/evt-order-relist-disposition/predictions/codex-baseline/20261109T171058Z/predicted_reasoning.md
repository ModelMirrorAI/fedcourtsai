# Forecast of the Court's handling

The petition will most likely be denied. The baseline already contains two distributions and the first completed conference; the forecast is for what happens after that first relist, not whether it has already occurred.

At least one further distribution is slightly more likely than none, with probability 56%. Expect one additional conference if the Court continues considering the matter without outside briefing, rather than a long series of routine relists. The Court will probably not seek the Solicitor General's views, but that is a substantial alternative, assigned 32%. If it does, expect the invitation in the next several conferences and final action after the government's brief, potentially in spring or summer 2027. Without that detour, expect action in November 2026 through January 2027. These timing ranges are forecasts, not current docket facts.

The 26-292, 26-293 and 26-294 petitions will likely be considered together, though coordinated consideration need not mean each receives the same procedural treatment. The judicial-interpretation challenge is serious, but differences between recognition and statutory immunity, plus independent barriers to recovery, will probably prevent four votes to hear this particular petition.

If review is granted, expect briefing and argument rather than an immediate merits ruling or remand: the summary-disposition route has 14% probability conditional on a grant. The most likely question would be a narrower version of the sole question presented: whether executive recognition conclusively establishes foreign-state status under the FSIA and how that bears on DAB's status. The Court would not grant to decide the abstract proposition that courts interpret statutes. Conditional on reaching the merits, expect a distinction between binding recognition decisions and independent interpretation of immunity provisions, not a categorical entitlement to collect these reserves. No specific intervening decision presently supplies a compelling summary-remand route.

Conditional on denial, the probability of a noted dissent or statement is 24%; a denial without separate explanation is more likely. If a Justice writes, Thomas is the leading individual possibility, followed by Alito and Gorsuch. These are forecasts of writing, not asserted cert votes or predictions of a merits majority.
