The Supreme Court will probably decline to hear this appeal, with about a one-in-four chance of taking it up (27%). Victims of embassy bombings seek access to billions of dollars in frozen Afghan central-bank funds. They argue that judges, not government officials, must decide whether those funds are protected from seizure. The Court has already considered the petition once and scheduled it again, showing real interest. But separate obstacles to recovery make this a difficult case for resolving the broader legal question.
