## Anchor and adjustments

I use that number, not the current Term's small sample, the terminal elevated-band rate, or an unselected petition average. For scale only, the pack's all-Term modern discretionary-cert population has 1,276 grant-family resolutions among 45,537 resolved petitions, about 2.8%.

Their printed grant-plus-GVR shares are 13.5%, 41.2% and 37.3%, implying an approximately 20.7% pooled grant-family rate using rounded percentages. These are terminal buckets, not the hazard from today's first relist. CVSG petitions show 34.8% grant-family outcomes, n=164, versus 6.4%, n=13,550, without a CVSG; neither supplies a probability that a CVSG will now occur. The originating Second Circuit cut is about 5.0% grant-family, n=2,081, but mixes procedural states. The one-amicus bucket is 14.9%, n=1,159; its terminal count and inclusion of opposing briefs make it a weak marginal comparison. I do not multiply these overlapping signals.

I move only modestly above 24.8%. Upward factors are a genuine relist, a sharply framed judicial-versus-executive statutory question, a divided panel and five en banc dissenters as described in the petition, substantial financial and foreign-relations stakes, and experienced counsel. Downward factors are a contestable rather than clearly square circuit conflict, a difficult recognition/state-continuity setting, and obstacles to obtaining relief even after winning the abstract interpretation question. The aggregate correction is judgmental, not a fitted model.

## Petition and opposing evidence

The provisioned `questions-presented.txt` and `petition.txt`, printed pp. i, 2–3, 9–17 and 23–25, seek independent judicial interpretation of the FSIA rather than dispositive reliance on executive websites and litigation positions. The petition describes attachment of DAB reserves and a roughly $5.2 billion judgment arising from the embassy bombings. Its alleged conflict invokes OI European Group, Ungar and O'Bryan. Consequently, disagreement over legal method does not itself establish opposite answers to this case's precise question. CourtListener's citation search and recognition snippets for OI European Group, 73 F.4th 157 (3d Cir. 2023), confirm its Venezuela/PDVSA setting; I did not retrieve a complete lower-court opinion in this case.

These are adversarial submissions, not independently established holdings here. Nonetheless, they materially weaken the petition's clean-vehicle characterization and favor plenary consideration over summary reversal if review is granted. No respondent BIO is shown or staged; I do not mistake the amicus for a BIO. The petition's description of absent defendants does not make the legal objections uncontested.

## Other forecasts and limitations

P(new CVSG)=0.32 reflects the government's direct interests and the absence of a current Supreme Court government submission; it is not inferred mechanically from the pack's terminal CVSG outcomes. The increment forecasts overlap: a CVSG followed by resumed consideration can produce another distribution. The 0.14 summary-route probability is conditional on a grant, not an additional unconditional grant probability. There is no identified intervening decision compelling a GVR on the question presented.

P(a dissent or statement | denial)=0.24 allows for the strong objections voiced below without treating lower-court dissenters as Supreme Court votes. The independent order-writer probabilities are unconditional; Thomas, Alito and Gorsuch receive the largest speculative weights, and no writing remains the modal outcome. Their sum need not equal the denial-conditioned aggregate. The big-case score measures stakes, not grant likelihood.

The lower-court docket summary reports no dissent noted, whereas the petition describes five en banc dissenters and cites separate writings. I treat the summary's negative as an extraction limitation, not a rebuttal; the original rehearing order was not staged. A citation-specific corpus lookup returned no match with an explicit sparse-citation coverage warning, and I did not treat that as absence of precedent. The broad corpus query mixed a substantive application into granted rows, so its small sample was not used to estimate cert rates. The first CLI attempt failed because uv's default cache was read-only; UV_CACHE_DIR=/tmp/uv-cache restored the fixed environment without installing packages.

An external web search result incidentally exposed snippets of forecasts for this case from another prediction website. I did not open those forecast pages or use their probabilities or analysis.
