Distributed once so far. Expect zero further relists before a decision on the petition. The questions presented allege a conflict over whether the Federal Credit Union Act strips district courts of jurisdiction for structural constitutional challenges, but there is no true inter-circuit split. The Fifth Circuit applied the explicit text of the statute, and other circuits have treated explicit preclusion provisions similarly post-*Axon*. 

No CVSG is expected, as the federal government (through the Solicitor General) is already a party and has filed its Brief in Opposition. 

If granted, the Court would likely take the first question presented to address the application of *Axon* to explicitly preclusive statutory schemes. However, a summary GVR or reversal is highly unlikely given the lack of an intervening decision or obvious error. Should it deny, expect no separate writing or dissent from denial.
