# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition after receiving the Solicitor General's views and returning the matter to conference. P(any grant), including a GVR or summary reversal, is **0.43**. The scrutiny question attracts serious attention, but this case's party-presentation and pleading-stage difficulties make denial more likely than intervention.

The baseline records **two distributions**, on November 25, 2025, and June 29, 2026. I forecast at least one further distribution with probability **0.95**, ordinarily one or two additional distributions before disposition. This is a forecast of additional docketed distributions, including the return following the government's brief, not a claim that every distribution is a substantive relist. The Court already called for the Solicitor General's views on **June 30, 2026**. I predict no additional CVSG; the required `cvsg-increment` probability is **0.0**, and that claim is vacuous at this moment.

The most likely sequence is an invited government brief, responsive party submissions, renewed distribution, and a decision during October Term 2026, plausibly by June 2027. This timing is a forecast, not a report of post-snapshot activity. I do not predict that the Court will immediately dispose of the petition without the requested input.

If the Court grants plenary review, I expect it to focus principally on **Question 1**: the relationship between heightened equal-protection scrutiny and deference to prison administrators for challenged sex-based housing and commissary classifications. I expect a formulation tied to those policies, rather than a categorical ruling about all transgender-status classifications. Question 2, concerning the cross-gender strip search, is a less likely independent basis for plenary review because the actual search circumstances and procedural posture complicate the asserted categorical rule.

**Conditional on any grant, the probability of disposition in the cert order is 0.55.** A GVR for reconsideration of the equal-protection analysis is the principal summary route, given the petition's reliance on the vacatur of Fowler in light of Skrmetti. A GVR would leave further adjudication below; it is not a summary merits reversal. The alternative grant route is plenary briefing and argument on Question 1. If the Court takes that route, I expect a decision emphasizing the weight of concrete security and privacy justifications rather than treating scrutiny alone as dispositive of the constitutionality of every challenged policy.

**Conditional on denial, the probability of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial is 0.18.** The modal denial has no separate writing. I make no individual Justice forecast and predict no majority merits opinion accompanying a denial.
