# Predicted reasoning — Roybal v. Griffith, No. 25-442 (CVSG-moment cert forecast)

## Where the docket stands

The petition has been distributed twice (the 12/12/2025 conference, then the
6/29/2026 conference after a six-month hold), and on 2026-06-30 — the same day
the Court decided *West Virginia v. B.P.J.* and *Little v. Hecox* — the Court
invited the Solicitor General to file a brief expressing the views of the
United States. That is the state I am forecasting from.

## What the Court will do

- **The SG's brief will arrive around December 2026.** A term-end CVSG is
  typically answered before the January conferences; I expect the filing in the
  December 2026–January 2027 window, and I expect the brief to recommend a
  grant limited to the first question presented. The federal interest is
  direct — the Bureau of Prisons runs the largest carceral system in the
  country and its own housing and search policies turn on the same
  standard — and the current administration's litigating position aligns with
  petitioners on Turner deference.
- **The petition will be redistributed after the SG files** — at least one
  further distribution is close to certain (my relist-increment claim is
  0.97), and I expect two to three conferences between the SG's filing and a
  disposition, putting the disposition in roughly January–March 2027.
- **No further CVSG** — one is already on the docket; the cvsg-increment claim
  is vacuous for this cell.
- **Grant, on QP1 only, likely reformulated.** If the Court takes the case
  (my central forecast at 0.55 for the grant family), it takes the
  level-of-scrutiny question — whether *Turner v. Safley* deference or *United
  States v. Virginia* heightened scrutiny governs sex-based prison policies
  challenged by transgender inmates — and I expect it to rephrase the QP
  rather than take petitioners' framing verbatim, possibly folding in the
  transgender-classification scrutiny question it has now reserved three times
  (*Skrmetti*, *B.P.J.*, *Hecox*). QP2, the strip-search question, is
  factbound and split-free; I expect it left behind or subsumed.
- **A summary disposition is unlikely (~0.08).** The natural GVR-in-light-of-
  *B.P.J.* moment was 2026-06-30, and the Court chose a CVSG instead; a GVR
  now would only follow an SG brief recommending one.
- **If denied, expect separate writing.** A denial over the SG's contrary
  recommendation, or on vehicle grounds, would likely draw a dissent from
  denial or a statement respecting denial from Justice Thomas and/or Justice
  Alito, echoing Judge Tymkovich's dissent from the Tenth Circuit's en banc
  denial.
