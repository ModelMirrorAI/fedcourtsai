# Forecast — Roybal v. Griffith, No. 25-442 (petition disposition)

**Disposition: the Court grants, vacates, and remands to the Tenth Circuit for
further consideration in light of *West Virginia v. B.P.J.* (2026) and *United
States v. Skrmetti* (2025).** P(any grant) = 0.84.

- **State of the docket.** Three distributions so far (12/12/2025; 6/29/2026,
  after a long hold that tracks the pendency of *B.P.J.*; 11/6/2026, after the
  SG's invited brief). A CVSG already issued (6/30/2026) and was answered
  (9/25/2026).
- **Relists.** The likeliest path is disposition on the order list following
  the 11/6/2026 conference with no further distribution. A GVR in line with
  the SG's recommendation and the petitioners' own alternative ask needs no
  extended consideration. Roughly a 30% chance it is relisted at least once
  more, mainly if a Justice wants a GVR statement, as the SG suggested, or
  a dissent from the GVR.
- **CVSG.** Already called and answered. No further CVSG.
- **Route.** Conditional on a grant, about 85% it is a summary GVR rather than
  plenary review. The Court already GVR'd an analogous post-*B.P.J.* sports
  case (*Petersen v. Doe*, June 30, 2026), and it GVR'd the Tenth Circuit's
  *Fowler v. Stitt* in light of *Skrmetti*. Plenary review is the minority
  path: the Court might want to settle the Turner-vs-*Virginia* question in
  prisons on its own terms.
- **Question taken, if plenary.** QP 1 (Turner deference vs. heightened
  scrutiny for sex-based prison policies) as written. QP 2 (the cross-gender
  strip-search rule under *Bell*/*Florence*) is concededly not a split and
  would be left behind or folded in.
- **If denied.** A dissent from denial is more likely than not. Justices Alito
  and Thomas, possibly joined by Justice Gorsuch, would object to leaving the
  Tenth Circuit's heightened-scrutiny holding in place. A GVR itself might
  draw a short statement or a dissent (e.g., Justice Sotomayor or Justice
  Jackson), but that is not the scored dissent-from-denial claim.
