Distributed 3 times so far, with a CVSG brief from the SG now on the docket. I expect perhaps one further relist before a disposition to finalize the order, but the Court may act at the first conference following the SG's brief. No new CVSG will be called for as one has already occurred.

A summary disposition (GVR - Grant, Vacate, Remand) is overwhelmingly likely. The Solicitor General explicitly recommended a GVR in light of the Court's recent decisions in `West Virginia v. B.P.J.` and `United States v. Skrmetti`. These recent cases directly undermine the Tenth Circuit's rationale for allowing the respondent's equal protection and substantive due process claims to proceed against sex-based prison policies. The Court will likely issue the GVR in the cert order itself without setting the case down for merits briefing or argument, allowing the lower courts to apply the new, more restrictive precedents regarding trans-identifying individuals' claims.

If the Court grants plenary review instead of a GVR, they would likely take both questions presented, focusing heavily on whether `Turner v. Safley` mandates deferential rational-basis review for sex-based prison policies. However, plenary review is unlikely given the SG's recommendation. Should the Court deny the petition outright, expect a dissent from denial (likely from Justice Alito or Thomas), given the Tenth Circuit's reliance on overruled precedents.
