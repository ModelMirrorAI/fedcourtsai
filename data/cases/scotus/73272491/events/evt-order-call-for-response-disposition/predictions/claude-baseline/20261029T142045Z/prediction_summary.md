We expect the Supreme Court to turn down Martin Mizrahi's request to hear his case. We put the chance it takes the case at about 7%, or roughly one in fourteen. He asks the Court to overrule a 1995 decision that lets a judge, rather than a jury, decide how much money a convicted defendant must forfeit (give up to the government). The Court did ask the government to respond, which shows some interest. But it has refused this same request about a dozen times, and every appeals court follows the 1995 rule. One Justice, most likely Justice Gorsuch, may write separately objecting to the refusal.
