We predict that the Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Martin Mizrahi v. United States*, with only about a 4.5% (roughly one in twenty-two) chance of granting the petition. Mizrahi asks the Court to overturn its 1995 precedent in *Libretti v. United States* and hold that the Sixth Amendment right to a jury trial requires a jury, rather than a judge, to find the facts supporting a $4.5 million criminal asset forfeiture order. Although the Court asked the federal government to file a response after the government initially waived its right to respond, there is no disagreement among the federal appeals courts on this issue, the Court has repeatedly turned down the same question in prior terms, and the fact-specific summary order from the Second Circuit makes this case an unattractive vehicle for reconsidering a major sentencing precedent.
