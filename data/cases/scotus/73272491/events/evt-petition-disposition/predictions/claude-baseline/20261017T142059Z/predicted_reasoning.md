# Forecast — Mizrahi v. United States, No. 25-1238

**Disposition: certiorari denied** (P(grant) = 0.08).

- **State of the docket.** Distributed once (for the June 18, 2026 conference,
  on the government's waiver). The Court then requested a response; after
  three extensions it was due October 13, 2026. No CVSG — the United States
  is the respondent.
- **Further distribution.** Expect the petition to be redistributed once the
  brief in opposition is in, for a late-October or November conference. That
  is the ordinary post-response distribution. Beyond that, expect at most one
  relist. Two or more relists would point toward a grant or a dissent from
  denial being written.
- **CVSG.** None. The Solicitor General is already the respondent.
- **Question the Court would take, if granted.** The single QP as written:
  whether *Libretti v. United States* (1995) survives *Apprendi* and
  *Southern Union*. If it is granted, the likely ground is that criminal
  forfeiture is a "fine" under *Bajakajian*, so *Southern Union* applies.
  The government will answer that forfeiture has no statutory maximum, so
  there is no *Apprendi* ceiling.
- **Summary disposition.** Unlikely. No intervening decision calls for a GVR,
  and overruling a precedent cannot be done summarily.
- **Separate writing on denial.** Possible but not likely. Justice Gorsuch
  dissented from denial in the restitution analogues (*Hester*, joined by
  Justice Sotomayor; *Rimlawi*; *Burnett*). A statement or dissent from him
  is the most likely separate writing, with Justice Thomas or Justice
  Sotomayor less likely.
