# Forecast

The Court will affirm the Third Circuit's judgment. A six-Justice majority—Chief Justice Roberts and Justices Thomas, Alito, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh, and Barrett—will conclude that 8 U.S.C. § 1188(g)(2) does not authorize the Department of Labor to conclusively adjudicate contested civil penalties and back wages in its own tribunals. Justices Sotomayor, Kagan, and Jackson will dissent.

The majority will treat the statutory question added by the grant order as the narrower ground. The authority to “take such actions, including imposing appropriate penalties” identifies enforcement authority and available remedies, but it does not clearly assign adjudication of disputed monetary liability to an agency tribunal. The Court will therefore avoid deciding whether Article III independently prohibits the proceeding. One or more of Justices Thomas and Gorsuch are likely to write separately that DOL's contract-like claims for penalties and back wages concern private rights and must be adjudicated in an Article III court.

The holding will be confined to the authority supplied by § 1188(g)(2). It will leave DOL able to administer the H-2A certification program, investigate violations, and pursue authorized monetary remedies in federal court, without resolving the validity of agency adjudication under statutes that expressly establish an administrative forum.

No recusal, equally divided affirmance, or dismissal as improvidently granted is expected.
