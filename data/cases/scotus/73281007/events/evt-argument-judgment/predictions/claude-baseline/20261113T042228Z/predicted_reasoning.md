# Forecast — Department of Labor v. Sun Valley Orchards, No. 25-966

**Judgment: affirmed.** The Court will leave standing the Third Circuit's judgment that the Department of Labor could not itself adjudicate civil penalties and back wages against this H-2A employer.

**Lineup: 6–3.** Roberts, Thomas, Alito, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh and Barrett in the majority; Sotomayor, Kagan and Jackson in dissent. Alito is the least secure majority vote; a 5–4 with him in dissent is the main alternative.

**Author.** The Chief Justice writes for the Court, as he did in *Jarkesy*. Gorsuch is the next most likely, then Barrett.

**Ground.** The Court reaches question 1 and decides it under Article III. It applies *Jarkesy*'s two steps: the claim is legal in nature because it seeks civil penalties and back pay for breach of work-contract terms (*Tull*), and the public-rights exception does not cover it. *Oceanic Steam* is read as an exception about who may enter or remain and about exactions tied to that decision, not about the conditions of employment on a domestic farm months after entry. The government's broader theory — that conditions attached to a government-granted privilege may be enforced by the agency — is rejected as having no stopping point. The statutory question is noted and left undecided, or addressed only in a concurrence.

**Breadth.** Narrow within the immigration exception. The opinion will say it is not holding that all civil penalties belong in Article III courts, will not overrule *Atlas Roofing* or *Oceanic Steam*, and will reserve employer-sanctions penalties under 8 U.S.C. 1324a, and the tax, customs and benefits settings.

**Separate writings.** A principal dissent by Sotomayor or Kagan, joined by the other two, stressing that the H-2A conditions are conditions of admission and warning of disruption; Jackson may add her own. Gorsuch is likely to concur, possibly on the statute or on the original scope of public rights, and Thomas may write separately as well.

**Exits.** No DIG and no equally divided Court: nine Justices sat and the vehicle is clean. If the judgment is disturbed, the likeliest form is a partial reversal separating back wages from penalties, or a vacatur directing de novo judicial review rather than judgment for the employer.

**Timing.** A decision in the spring, most likely between March and June 2027.
