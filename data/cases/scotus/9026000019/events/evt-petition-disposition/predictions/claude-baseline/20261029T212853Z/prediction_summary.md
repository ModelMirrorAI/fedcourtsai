We expect the Supreme Court to turn this case down — about a one-in-twenty chance it agrees to hear it. Allison Nguyen was convicted of misdemeanor domestic battery in Louisiana by a judge alone and argues she deserved a jury because the conviction also takes away her gun rights for good. The Court asked Louisiana to respond, a sign of some interest, but the petition is brief, the case is still partly open in the state courts, and the Court usually declines these requests.
