The Supreme Court is very likely to turn down Allison Nguyen's request for review; I put the chance it takes any action granting review at about nine in a hundred. Her argument is that a domestic-abuse conviction's firearm restrictions make the charge serious enough to require a jury, despite a maximum six-month jail term. The Court has asked Louisiana to respond, which shows interest, but questions about how the issue was presented and the unfinished sentencing proceedings make this a less attractive case to decide.
