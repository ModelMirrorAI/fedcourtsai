We predict the Supreme Court will deny Allison Nguyen's petition for review (a writ of certiorari, the formal request for the Court to hear an appeal), with about a one-in-twenty-two (4.5%) chance of a grant. Nguyen argues the Constitution guarantees a jury trial for a Louisiana misdemeanor domestic violence charge carrying up to six months in jail because conviction also triggers federal and state gun bans and cannot be erased from her record. Although the Court asked Louisiana to respond, lower courts are not genuinely divided over temporary state firearm restrictions or collateral federal consequences, making review unlikely.
