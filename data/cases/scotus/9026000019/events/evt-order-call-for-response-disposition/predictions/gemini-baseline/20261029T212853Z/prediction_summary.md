The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Allison Nguyen's petition for review, with only about a 1-in-17 (6%) chance of agreeing to hear the case. Nguyen was convicted by a Louisiana judge, without a jury, of misdemeanor domestic abuse battery, which carries up to six months in jail plus a ten-year state ban and a lifetime federal ban on owning firearms. Although the Supreme Court asked the State of Louisiana to file a response to her argument that losing gun rights makes the charge serious enough to require a jury trial under the Sixth Amendment, the case has serious procedural flaws because the state appeals court declined to revisit the jury issue after trial and the brief lacks outside support.
