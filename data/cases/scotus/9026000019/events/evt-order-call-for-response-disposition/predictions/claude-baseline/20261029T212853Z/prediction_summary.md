We predict the Supreme Court will very likely refuse to hear this case (about a 4% chance it takes it). Allison Nguyen was convicted of misdemeanor domestic abuse battery in Louisiana without a jury. She argues that losing her gun rights made the offense "serious" enough to require one. The Court asked Louisiana to file a response, which shows some interest. But a long-standing Supreme Court rule says that crimes punishable by six months or less generally do not require a jury.
