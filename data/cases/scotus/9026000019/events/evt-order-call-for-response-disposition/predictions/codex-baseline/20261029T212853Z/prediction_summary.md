The Supreme Court is very likely to turn down Allison Nguyen's request to review her conviction; I put the chance of accepting the case at 12%, about one in eight. Her argument is that losing firearm rights makes a domestic-violence misdemeanor serious enough to require a jury, even when jail time cannot exceed six months. The Court's request for Louisiana to respond shows interest, and Nevada has recognized a similar jury right. But procedural complications and limited evidence of a clear nationwide disagreement make rejection more likely.
