# Forecast: what the Court will do with application 26A434

**Disposition.** Justice Alito will deny the application for an injunction, acting alone, without calling for a response. P(unqualified grant) = 0.01.

**Response requested.** The record shows no response called for. I forecast none will be (P(increment) = 0.04). The Solicitor General is counsel of record for the respondents, so a response could be obtained quickly, but a pro se application for an injunction against federal officers, arising from a suit dismissed for lack of subject-matter jurisdiction, is the shape the Circuit Justice disposes of on the papers.

**Referral to the full Court.** Not yet referred. P(referral) = 0.12. Justice Alito ordinarily denies pro se applications of this kind in chambers; a referral is the minority path and happens mainly where the applicant has renewed after a denial, or where the application travels with a cert petition the Court wants to handle together. The link to docket 26-153 is the one feature that gives referral a non-trivial chance, since the Court sometimes resolves an application alongside the companion petition.

**Amicus briefs.** The count stands at zero and will stay there (P(rise) = 0.01). No organized interest is implicated.

**Timing.** Disposition within roughly one to three weeks of the September 25 submission, so by mid-October 2026, most likely as a one-line "Application (26A434) denied by Justice Alito." If instead the Justice holds it to be considered with petition 26-153, the denial would come with that petition's disposition, which would push it to a later conference.

**Conditional merits content.** If, against the forecast, the application were referred and granted in any part, the relief would be narrow and tied to the pending petition rather than the injunction sought. I do not expect any written dissent or statement accompanying the denial.
