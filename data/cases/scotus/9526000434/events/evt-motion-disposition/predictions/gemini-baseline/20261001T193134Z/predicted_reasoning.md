This is an interim application for an injunction. The applicant is a serial pro se litigant who has filed multiple civil rights lawsuits against the Department of Justice and the Attorney General. The current application stems from his Third Circuit appeal. 

Pro se applications for extraordinary relief, such as an injunction against the government, are overwhelmingly denied. The legal standard requires a fair prospect of certiorari and irreparable harm, neither of which is likely to be met in this routine civil rights context.

Given the nature of the applicant's litigation history, Justice Alito or the full Court will almost certainly deny the application summarily. 
- **Response Request**: I do not expect the Court or Circuit Justice to call for a response from the Solicitor General.
- **Referral to the Full Court**: Justice Alito may deny the application in chambers, though occasionally these are referred to the full Court to be summarily denied. Referral is relatively unlikely but possible.
- **Amicus Briefs**: I expect zero additional amicus briefs to arrive, as third parties do not typically weigh in on pro se injunction requests of this nature.
- **Timing and Disposition**: The application will likely be denied shortly, within a few days to a few weeks, without any further substantial proceedings or opinions.
