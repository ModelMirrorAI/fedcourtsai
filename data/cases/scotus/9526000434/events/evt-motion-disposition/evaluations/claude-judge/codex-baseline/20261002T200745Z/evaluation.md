Outcome: denied by Justice Alito on 2026-10-01, no response requested, no referral, no amicus. Because the cell is interim, the baseline and skill are the harness's: `stamp-cell` pools the statpack's substantive slice over application-Terms strictly before 2026. **Quantitative.** Predicted `denied` at P(grant) 0.06 against an actual denial: `correct` 1, Brier 0.0036. Against the expected stamped baseline the skill would be about 0.67.

**What it got right.** The anchor is pooled correctly and the caveats attached to it (uneven parse coverage, denial-first reading of mixed orders, selection of the scored population on the escalation ladder) are the pack's own. It correctly identified the cell as arrival-position cut and did not read the absence of later entries as evidence. The injunction standard it drew from Brown v. Gilmore (an affirmative injunction demands more than a stay) is the right doctrinal frame for an application of this kind, and the direction of adjustment was right.

**What it missed.** This is the only candidate that did no case-specific investigation. The applicant's pro se status, the lower-court posture (a Section 1983 suit against the Attorney General dismissed under Rule 12(b)(1), the application's own related-proceedings list confirms it), and the applicant's substantive application 26A397 denied in chambers eight days earlier were all reachable with the same CourtListener and corpus tools the other candidates used, and the corpus confirms the 26A397 denial. The candidate treated the empty application text as the end of the inquiry and stayed within a factor of two of the pooled rate; the 30 percent referral forecast likewise reflects no read on who the applicant was. The prose is careful about what it does not know, which I credit, but a forecast for an individual's injunction application against federal officers that never asks who the individual is has left most of the available signal on the table.

**Reasoning quality 0.62.** Methodologically clean, honest about its information set, doctrinally correct, but under-investigated where investigation was cheap and would have moved the number.
