We predict the Supreme Court will deny review in *Spartan Securities Group, Ltd. v. SEC*, giving the petition about a one-in-six chance (16%) of being granted, either for full briefing and argument or for an immediate remand—sending the case back to the lower court—in light of the Supreme Court's June 2026 decision in *Sripetch v. SEC*. Although the Court asked the government to respond to the petition's challenge to disgorgement—a court order requiring a wrongdoer to give up ill-gotten profits—paid to the U.S. Treasury, the lower court upheld that $114,520 award on the independent ground that the challengers themselves had agreed it was impractical to distribute the money to individual investors.
