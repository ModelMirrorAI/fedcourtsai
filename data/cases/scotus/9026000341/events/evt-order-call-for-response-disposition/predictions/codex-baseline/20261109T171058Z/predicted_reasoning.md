# Expected petition trajectory

The most likely outcome is an unexplained denial of certiorari after the requested federal response and the petitioners' opportunity to reply. Certiorari means Supreme Court review; a denial would leave the lower judgment standing without endorsing its reasoning.

The snapshot shows **one distribution**, for the November 6, 2026 conference, and a subsequent October 23 response request with a November 23 deadline. I expect another distribution after briefing, plausibly in December 2026 or January 2027 if no extension intervenes. I assign 0.97 to at least one additional distribution. The second distribution would likely replace the initial conference setting rather than demonstrate a true relist after deliberation. My modal sequence is that redistribution followed by denial, without repeated substantive relists; a further relist remains a plausible minority path if the remedies question attracts sustained interest.

I do not expect a separate invitation for the Solicitor General's views: the SEC is already the respondent, and its requested response should provide the government's position. P(a new CVSG) is 0.005. I forecast no content for the response before it exists in this information set.

# Conditional review and reasoning

If review is granted, the likelier question is **Question 2**, narrowed to whether Section 78u(d)(7) permits disgorgement paid to the Treasury where no injured investors are identified and payment to investors is said to be infeasible. I expect the Court to leave the broader Rule 10b-5 communication issue in Question 1 aside. That issue is entangled with the evidence of public availability and the communications' role in enabling securities transactions.

Conditional on any grant, I assign **0.20** to disposition in the cert order itself and **0.80** to plenary review. A grant-vacate-remand would require the recent authority described in the petition, or the government's response, to furnish a concrete reason for reconsideration; the mere existence of a reserved question is insufficient. A summary merits reversal is less likely than a remand. If plenary review occurs, I expect a statutory distinction between the disgorgement authorization and the separate authorization of investor-benefiting equitable relief to be central, rather than a broad resolution of all SEC enforcement powers.

Conditional on denial, P(a noted dissent or statement respecting denial) is **0.08**. I expect no separate writing. If one appears, Thomas or Gorsuch is my leading possibility, focused on the unsettled character and limits of the monetary remedy. That is an uncertain authorship forecast, not a claim that either Justice will vote to grant.
