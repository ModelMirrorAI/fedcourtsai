We expect the Supreme Court to turn down this case (about a one-in-six chance it takes it up, counting an order sending the case back for another look). A securities firm and its affiliates are challenging a fraud verdict and an order to pay over profits to the U.S. Treasury rather than to harmed investors. The Court asked the government to respond after it had declined to, which signals some interest. But most petitions are still rejected at that stage, and the Court just decided a related disgorgement case (Sripetch v. SEC) in June 2026.
