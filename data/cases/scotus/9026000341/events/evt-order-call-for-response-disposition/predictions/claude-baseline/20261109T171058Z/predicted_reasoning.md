# Forecast — No. 26-341

- **State:** the docket shows one distribution (for the 11/6/2026 conference) and no CVSG. On Oct 23 the Court called for the SEC's response after the SG waived. The response is due Nov 23, 2026, and an extension is likely.
- **Relists:** once the response and reply are in, the petition will be redistributed (masked claim). I expect a decision at the first conference after the brief in opposition. One further relist is possible if a Justice is considering a GVR or a separate writing.
- **CVSG:** none. The United States is the respondent.
- **Most likely outcome: denial (about 84%).** The SG's opposition will likely argue that Sripetch narrows any practical disagreement over § 78u(d)(7), that QP1 is fact-bound with no acknowledged split, and that the stipulation on infeasibility makes this a poor vehicle.
- **If granted,** the Court would take QP2, reformulated to ask whether disgorgement under § 78u(d)(7) may be paid to the Treasury when distribution to investors is infeasible. It would leave QP1's "in connection with" question behind.
- **Summary route:** if the Court acts favorably, roughly a one-in-three chance it is a GVR in light of Sripetch v. SEC (June 2026), which postdates the judgment below, rather than plenary review.
- **Separate writing on denial:** unlikely (about 7%). If one comes, the likeliest author is Justice Thomas (given his Sripetch concurrence that (d)(7) disgorgement may be a legal remedy), or Justice Gorsuch.
