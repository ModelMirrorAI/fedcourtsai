The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in this case, with about an 11% chance (roughly one in nine) of granting the petition. A defunct brokerage firm, a transfer agent, and two executives challenge a federal appeals court ruling holding them liable for making false statements to financial regulators about "shell" companies (inactive companies used in fraud schemes) and ordering the transfer agent to forfeit $114,520 in ill-gotten fees ("disgorgement") to the U.S. Treasury rather than to harmed investors. Although the Court asked the Securities and Exchange Commission to file a response, the Court already decided a major disgorgement case five months ago, and the challengers previously agreed in the trial court that returning the $114,520 directly to individual investors was impractical, making this a poor test case for supreme court review.
