The Supreme Court is very likely to decline this appeal; I estimate about an 18% chance it accepts the case or sends it back for another look. The Court has asked the government to respond, showing some interest. The strongest issue concerns whether the securities regulator can recover profits for the Treasury rather than investors. But disputed facts and an agreement that payments to investors were impractical make this a difficult case for resolving that issue. Another scheduling round is very likely before a decision.
