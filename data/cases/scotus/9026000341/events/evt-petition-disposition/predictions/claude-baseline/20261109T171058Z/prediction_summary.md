The Supreme Court will probably refuse to hear this case (about a one-in-ten chance it takes it in some form). Brokers and a transfer agent challenge an SEC fraud finding and an order to give up about $150,000 in fees. The Court asked the government to respond, a mild sign of interest. The disgorgement question follows directly from the Court's 2026 *Sripetch* decision. Still, the small award and fact-heavy record make it a weak vehicle. If the Court acts, the most likely step is sending the case back to the lower court for another look in light of *Sripetch*.
