# Forecast — No. 26-341

- **State:** distributed once (for the Nov 6, 2026 conference), then overtaken by the Court's
  Oct 23 request for a response (due Nov 23). No relists in the true sense and no CVSG.
- **Relisting:** the petition will be distributed again after the SG's response, likely for a
  December 2026 or January 2027 conference. I expect it to be decided at that first post-response
  conference, with perhaps a 20% chance of one further relist.
- **CVSG:** none. The SEC is a party, and the SG's response is the government's view.
- **Disposition:** denial is the most likely outcome (~90%).
- **If the Court acts:** the likelier route is a GVR in light of *Sripetch v. SEC*. The Eleventh
  Circuit's disgorgement analysis predates that decision, so the Court could send it back.
  Plenary review is less likely. If plenary review is granted, the Court would take QP2, limited
  to whether § 78u(d)(7) disgorgement requires identified victims and permits payment to the
  Treasury, and leave QP1 behind.
- **Separate writing:** most likely none. If someone writes, Justice Thomas is most likely,
  revisiting whether § 78u(d)(7) is a legal remedy that triggers a jury right, with Justice Gorsuch
  next.
