# Forecast — No. 26-219, Johnson v. United States

The docket shows one distribution (for the Sept. 28, 2026 conference), overtaken by the Court's Sept. 14 request for a response from the United States.

- **Disposition:** The Court will deny certiorari. The Solicitor General will file a brief in opposition (now due Dec. 7, 2026, possibly after one further extension), the petitioner will reply, and the petition will be redistributed for a conference in January or February 2027 and denied on the following order list.
- **Relists:** The petition will be distributed once more after the response arrives. It will not be relisted beyond that redistribution; expect denial at the first conference after the briefing closes.
- **CVSG:** None. The United States is the respondent.
- **Question presented:** If the Court were to grant, it would take the petition's single question as written — whether the Leon good-faith exception applies to reliance on a legally untrained commander's search authorization issued on the advice of the same prosecutor who advised investigators — and would likely decide it narrowly, on whether reliance was objectively reasonable on this affidavit, rather than disturbing commander authorizations generally.
- **Summary disposition:** Unlikely. No intervening decision supports a GVR, and a summary reversal of the CAAF is improbable; a grant, if any, would more likely be plenary.
- **Separate writing:** Expect no noted dissent or statement on denial. If one appears, the likeliest author is Justice Sotomayor, then Justice Gorsuch or Justice Jackson.
