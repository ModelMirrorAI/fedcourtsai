The Supreme Court will very likely decline to hear this case — about a one-in-twenty-five chance it agrees to review it. An airman argues that evidence from a sweeping search of his phones should have been thrown out because the commander who approved the search had no legal training and was advised by the prosecutor helping investigators. The Court asked the government to respond, a mild sign of interest, but the issue is unique to the military and involves no disagreement among lower courts.
