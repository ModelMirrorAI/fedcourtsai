The Supreme Court is very unlikely to hear this appeal, with a 2.5% chance—one in forty—that it grants certiorari, meaning formal review of the lower court's decision, so the petition will be denied. An airman challenges evidence from a cell-phone search approved by a military commander without legal training who relied on advice from the same military prosecutor advising investigators. Although the Court asked the government to respond, the airman never raised this broad objection at his trial—where he actually agreed the commander was qualified—and no lower courts disagree on the rule.
