The Supreme Court will very probably decline to hear this case; I put the chance it takes it at about one in twenty-five. An airman argues that evidence from a sweeping search of his phones should have been thrown out because the search was approved by his commander, who had no legal training and was advised by the prosecution's own lawyer. The Court asked the government to respond, a sign of some interest, but the petition shows no disagreement among courts and raises a broader question than the military appeals court decided.
