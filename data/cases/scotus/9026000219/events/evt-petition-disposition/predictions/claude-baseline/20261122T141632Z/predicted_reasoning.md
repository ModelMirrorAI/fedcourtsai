# Forecast — No. 26-219, Johnson v. United States

The docket shows **one distribution** (Conference of Sept 28, 2026), followed by a called-for response now due Dec 7, 2026.

- **Further distribution:** Once the Solicitor General's brief in opposition is filed (on or near Dec 7, absent a further extension), the petition will be distributed once more, most likely for a January 2027 conference. Expect exactly one further distribution and no true relist after it.
- **CVSG:** None. The United States is already the respondent.
- **Disposition:** Certiorari will be denied at the first conference after redistribution, most likely in January 2027 (February if the response slips again).
- **Question presented, if granted:** The Court would not take the question as written. It would more likely confine it to whether the good-faith exception applies on this record, where the authorizing commander relied on the advice of the same judge advocate who helped investigators prepare the request, rather than a categorical rule about commanders without legal training.
- **Summary route:** Unlikely overall. If there is a grant at all, plenary review is more likely than a GVR; a GVR would require a concession by the Solicitor General or an intervening decision, and neither is in view.
- **Separate writing:** A denial will most likely be unaccompanied by any opinion. If a Justice writes, it will be a short statement or dissent from Justice Sotomayor, or Justice Gorsuch, on the breadth of the phone search and the neutrality of the authorizing official.
- **If granted (conditional):** the likely ground would be a narrow application of *Leon*'s limits (abandonment of the neutral role, or an affidavit so lacking in nexus that reliance was unreasonable), leaving the commander-authorization system itself intact.
