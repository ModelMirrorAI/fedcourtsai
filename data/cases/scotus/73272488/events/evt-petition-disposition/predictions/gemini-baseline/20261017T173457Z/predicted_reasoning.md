The petition presents a clear procedural split on whether a general Rule 29 motion preserves sufficiency-of-the-evidence challenges for de novo review. The Court requested a response after the government initially waived, indicating interest. Two amicus briefs were filed at the petition stage. Expect one or two relists as the Court considers the vehicle. No CVSG is possible as the United States is already the respondent. A summary disposition is unlikely for this type of procedural split. If denied, a dissent from denial is possible but not highly likely given the technical nature of the issue. If granted, the Court will likely address the split directly.
