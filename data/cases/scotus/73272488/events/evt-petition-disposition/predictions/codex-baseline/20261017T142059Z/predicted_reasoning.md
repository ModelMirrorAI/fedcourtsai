# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny certiorari, most likely without a separate writing. The question presents a genuine disagreement about preservation of sufficiency arguments, but the strength of the evidence supporting the convictions will make this a less attractive vehicle for resolving it. Probability of any grant is **24%**.

The provided docket contains **one distribution** and no CVSG. After the response requested on June 9 and the opposition submitted October 9, I expect **one further distribution**, followed by denial in late 2026 or early 2027. The probability of at least one additional distribution is **96%**. That next distribution will most likely resume consideration after requested briefing; it need not signify that the Court considered the fully briefed petition and then substantively relisted it. Further distributions beyond that are possible but not my modal forecast.

The Court will not issue a CVSG; the probability of a new invitation is **0.2%**. The government is already the respondent and has supplied an opposition. The earlier response request is not a request for the Solicitor General's views as an outside participant.

If the Court grants, it will most likely take the existing question substantially as written: whether a general Rule 29 motion preserves de novo appellate review of evidentiary sufficiency, without separately articulating each deficiency. It will set the case for briefing and argument rather than decide the fraud or identity-theft facts in a summary order. Conditional on a grant, I assign **12%** to disposition in the cert order itself. A summary remand tied to another case is a secondary possibility, not the predicted route.

If plenary review occurs, the likely focus is the relationship between Rule 29's acquittal mechanism and the general requirement to state a motion's grounds, rather than a broad revision of the underlying criminal offenses. Any decision allowing general motions would leave the sufficiency analysis for further appellate consideration rather than necessarily require acquittal.

Conditional on denial, the probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial is **8%**. The modal forecast is no separate writing, and no particular Justice is forecast to write.
