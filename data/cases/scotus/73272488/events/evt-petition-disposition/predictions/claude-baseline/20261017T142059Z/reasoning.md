# Why P(grant) = 0.30

**Anchor.** `record/context.json` freezes `band: baseline` under `sal-v4`
(matching the statpack table's version). The bracketed `reached` rate for
`baseline` over Terms strictly before this case's Term (2025), pooled across
the shown rows 2017–2024, is roughly 5% (row figures 4.5–5.9%). That is the
yardstick I adjust from.

**Adjustments up (large).**
- *Response requested* after the SG's waiver (June 9, 2026). A call for
  response on a paid petition is a strong signal a chambers took interest; it
  is apparently not folded into the `baseline` band, so the anchor understates
  this petition's situation.
- *Acknowledged, entrenched split* on a recurring federal criminal-procedure
  question (petition: 1st/2d/6th/7th/9th/10th/D.C. vs. 3d/8th/11th, with 5th
  leaning minority). The Third Circuit expressly declined to follow sister
  circuits in a published opinion (165 F.4th 784).
- *Government invited the decision below* — it told the Third Circuit the case
  was a vehicle to resolve the open question, which weakens any SG argument
  that the question is unimportant or poorly presented.
- *Elite counsel* (Debevoise with the FPD) and two amicus briefs, including
  former judges.
- *Clean colloquy*: the motion was "I move for judgment of acquittal on Rule 29.
  I waive argument." — no factual dispute about what was said.

**Adjustments down.**
- *Outcome-irrelevance risk.* On the fraud counts the panel held the record
  "amply supports" the convictions and found no error at Olano's first prong;
  the SG will argue the standard of review would not change the result. The
  § 1028A harm-element argument, by contrast, was rejected as not "plain",
  which helps petitioner (the standard did matter there) — but the Court may
  still see a weak vehicle.
- *Rules Committee channel.* The Court sometimes declines rule-interpretation
  splits as fit for the Advisory Committee.
- I have **not read the BIO** (submitted Oct 9; not provisioned, and I did not
  retrieve it). Its content — especially whether the SG disputes the split or
  presses harmlessness — is my largest uncertainty.
- The SG opposed the companion Tovar petition on vehicle grounds (per
  petitioner's footnote); I could not find Tovar's disposition via MCP.

Net: a clean acknowledged split with a CFR and amici is in the 20–40% range in
my experience; I land at 0.30.
