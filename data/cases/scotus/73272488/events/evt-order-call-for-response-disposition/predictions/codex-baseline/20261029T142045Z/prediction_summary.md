The Supreme Court is more likely to turn down James Abrams's appeal, although I give it about a three-in-ten chance of agreeing to hear it. Federal appeals courts disagree about how specifically a defendant must challenge the prosecution's evidence at trial to preserve an appeal. The Court's request for an answer and support from outside legal groups make this petition stronger than most. But the appeals court's conclusion that the evidence strongly supported the convictions could make this a less suitable case to settle that disagreement.
