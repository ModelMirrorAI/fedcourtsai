We expect the Supreme Court to turn down James Abrams's appeal. We put the chance it agrees to hear the case at about one in eight. The case asks whether a defendant who simply asks the trial judge to acquit, without explaining why the evidence fell short, keeps the right to a full fresh look on appeal. Appeals courts disagree on this. But the government points out that Abrams never repeated his request at the end of the trial, and the Court turned away a similar case in May 2026.
