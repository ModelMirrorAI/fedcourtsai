Distributed twice so far (the first distribution for the June 4 conference resulted in a request for a response; the second is for the upcoming September 28 Long Conference). Given the high level of amicus interest, I expect it will likely be granted, or otherwise relisted once or twice more before a final decision is reached. A CVSG is unlikely because this concerns a state (New Mexico) disclosure law, not a federal statute, though the US could have an interest, it is not strictly necessary or typical here.

If granted, the Court will likely focus on Questions 1 and 2: whether exacting scrutiny applies and whether the disclosure regime can apply to issue advocacy lacking express advocacy (touching on *AFPF v. Bonta* and *Buckley v. Valeo*). A summary disposition (GVR) is unlikely unless there is a very recent intervening decision I am unaware of, so plenary review is the expected route for a grant. If the Court denies review, a dissent from denial by Justices Thomas or Alito is plausible given their strong views on donor privacy and campaign finance disclosure.
