# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny the petition, most likely in the first orders following the September 28, 2026 conference identified in the snapshot, around early October. It will not issue a merits opinion explaining the denial.

The provisioned record shows **one distribution and no CVSG**. I expect no further distribution before disposition. The probability of at least one additional distribution is **12%**; if that occurs, one additional conference is more likely than a sustained sequence of relists. I assign **0.2%** to a new CVSG. The Solicitor General has already supplied the federal respondent's position, making a separate invitation for the government's views particularly unlikely.

I assign **3.5%** to any grant, including a GVR or summary reversal. Conditional on a grant, **25%** goes to disposition in the cert order and **75%** to plenary review. A Loper Bright-related summary correction is conceivable, but the record does not supply a compelling intervening-decision remand route. The exhaustion problem on the remedies question further obstructs summary relief.

If the Court grants plenary review, the most plausible focus is a narrowed version of question one: when the combined substance of an employer's bargaining proposals can establish bad faith under the NLRA, rather than a categorical requirement of misconduct away from the bargaining table. Question two could accompany it as a question about independent review of the governing legal rule, not renewed review of every factual inference. I expect the Court to leave question three's consequential-damages challenge aside because of the preservation ruling. Any merits correction would most plausibly distinguish lawful hard bargaining from an inference that negotiations were intended to frustrate agreement; that is conditional on review, not a predicted holding accompanying denial.

Conditional on denial, I assign **2%** to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. The modal outcome is an unelaborated denial with no separate writing; I do not forecast an individual Justice's unpublished vote.
