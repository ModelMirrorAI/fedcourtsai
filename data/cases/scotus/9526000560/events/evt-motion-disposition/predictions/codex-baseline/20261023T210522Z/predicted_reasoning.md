# Forecast

The Court will grant a stay of the Western District of Texas injunction pending the timely filing and disposition of a certiorari petition, permitting construction under the challenged Big Bend waivers to resume. This is a forecast of an unqualified interim grant, not merely some relief or a grant of certiorari.

The arrival record contains one submission to Justice Alito on October 21, 2026, no response request, no referral to the full Court, and zero amicus entries. From that state, I forecast a response request (97%), referral to the full Court (96%), and at least one additional qualifying amicus entry or distinct submitted amicus brief (68%). I expect expedited opposition and a full-Court disposition approximately October 28 through November 11, 2026, rather than the Circuit Justice resolving the contested stay alone. The timing is a forecast, not a scored claim.

The likely basis for relief is that the injunction rests on a statutory-authority objection that cannot be converted into a constitutional claim merely by invoking the major-questions doctrine. The express waiver provision and its restriction of review to constitutional challenges make the government's likelihood-of-success argument unusually strong. Even if the Court does not resolve jurisdiction, the express authorization to waive legal requirements for border-barrier construction weakens the assertion that Congress failed clearly to authorize the relevant kind of action.

The Court is likely to give substantial weight to the government's asserted construction disruption and border-security injuries. Respondents' environmental, livelihood, and historic-resource injuries provide a serious counterweight, but I expect the Court to stay the injunction rather than preserve it while the district court adjudicates summary judgment. A concise stay order is more likely than immediate final merits correction. The government's additional request to treat the application as a certiorari petition and summarily reverse is not the interim disposition I am forecasting; any discussion of ultimate merits correction is conditional on the Court taking that further step.
