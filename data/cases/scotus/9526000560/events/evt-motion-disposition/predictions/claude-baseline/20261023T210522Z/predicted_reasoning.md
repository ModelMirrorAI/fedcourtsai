# Forecast — 26A560, Mullin v. Friends of the Ruidosa Church (stay application)

**Disposition.** The Court will grant the Solicitor General's application and stay
the W.D. Tex. preliminary injunction (Oct. 2, 2026; opinion Oct. 14) that halts all
border-barrier construction in the Big Bend Sector notwithstanding the three 2026
IIRIRA § 102(c) waivers. The likeliest form is an unqualified order of the full
Court ("application granted"), probably with a noted dissent from two or three
Justices. I expect it to stay the injunction in full rather than carve out Big Bend
National Park. A stay limited to part of the injunction would read as a denial on
this event's resolver, and it is the main alternative to a clean grant. I put the
chance that the Court instead treats the application as a petition, grants it and
summarily reverses in the same order at low. If it did, the entry would still
probably dispose of the application favourably.

**Why.** The district court got around § 102(c)(2)(A), which allows only claims
"alleging a violation of the Constitution", by calling the major-questions doctrine
a constitutional clear-statement rule. That reading is hard to square with *Dalton v.
Specter* and with how the Court itself has described the doctrine (*West Virginia v.
EPA*). The statutory text that authorizes "all legal requirements" waivers is express.
Review lies only by certiorari, so this Court is the sole forum and would very likely
grant review. The district court itself acknowledged irreparable harm to the
government, and *Trump v. Sierra Club* (2019) already struck the equities in the
government's favour on border-wall construction.

**Escalation ladder (from a record showing only the submission entry).**
- **Response requested:** yes, and almost certainly within days. Justice Alito, as
  Circuit Justice for the Fifth Circuit, routinely calls for a response to an SG
  stay application.
- **Referral to the full Court:** likely. An SG application of this significance is
  decided by the full Court, and I expect the docket to record a referral entry
  before disposition.
- **Amicus:** more likely than not at least one amicus entry, for example from Texas
  or other border states or coalitions supporting the government, or from
  environmental, tribal or historic-preservation groups supporting respondents.
- **Timing:** a response due in about a week, then disposition roughly 2–5 weeks
  after filing (mid-November to early December 2026). An administrative stay is
  possible in the meantime but not expected, because construction is already
  suspended.
