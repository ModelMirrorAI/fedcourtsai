The federal government has asked the Supreme Court to pause a Texas federal judge's order that stopped border-wall construction along roughly 300 miles of the Big Bend region. We think the Court is likely to grant the pause, about a three-in-four chance. The law Congress passed lets lower courts hear only constitutional challenges to these construction waivers. The judge's ruling rested on a theory that most of the Justices are unlikely to treat as constitutional. A partial pause, or an order that is part yes and part no, is the main way this forecast could miss.
