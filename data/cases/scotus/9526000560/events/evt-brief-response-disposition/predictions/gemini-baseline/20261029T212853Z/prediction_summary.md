The Supreme Court is very likely (an 85% chance, or about five in six) to grant the federal government's emergency request to pause a Texas federal judge's order that blocked border-wall and road construction across 517 miles of the Big Bend region. In 1996 and 2005, Congress gave the Secretary of Homeland Security broad power to waive environmental and other laws to speed up border barriers and barred lower courts from hearing challenges unless they involve a violation of the U.S. Constitution, and a majority of the Justices will likely conclude that the challengers' arguments about the scale of the project are disagreements over statutory interpretation rather than true constitutional claims.
