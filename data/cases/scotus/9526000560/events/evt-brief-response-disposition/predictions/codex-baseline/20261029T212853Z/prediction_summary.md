I expect the Supreme Court to pause the lower court's order blocking border-wall construction in the Big Bend region, with about an **80% chance** of an unqualified grant. The government's strongest argument is that Congress expressly allowed legal waivers for border construction and sharply limited court review. The opposing groups present serious evidence of lasting environmental and property damage, which keeps denial plausible. A pause would allow construction while legal challenges continue; it would not finally settle every constitutional question.
