## What happened

Jane Doe, a disabled veteran, sought compensation through the National Vaccine Injury Compensation Program, arguing that a hepatitis B vaccine caused her multiple sclerosis and a later dose worsened it. A special master at the Court of Federal Claims awarded damages, but Doe says the amount was wrong and that the proceedings were tainted: she alleges her original attorney missed a deadline to include her first vaccine claim, a later attorney improperly withdrew evidence supporting it, and court officials wrongly obtained and disclosed her protected military and veterans records.
