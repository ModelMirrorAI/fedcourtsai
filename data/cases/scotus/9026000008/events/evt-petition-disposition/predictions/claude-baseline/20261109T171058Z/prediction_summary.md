A substitute teacher asks the Supreme Court to hear her challenge to a Maryland school district's former rules on student pronouns and on keeping students' gender transitions from parents. We predict the Court will most likely decline to hear it, but we give about a one-in-three chance of some favourable action. The likeliest favourable action is sending the case back to the lower court to reconsider in light of a 2026 Supreme Court ruling on a similar policy. The main obstacle is that the district has since changed its policy and let her return to teaching.
