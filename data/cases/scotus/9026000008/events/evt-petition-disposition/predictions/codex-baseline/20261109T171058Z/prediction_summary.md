The Supreme Court is likely to decline this case, with about a seven-in-ten chance of denial and a 28% chance of some form of review. The dispute raises important questions about teachers' religious objections to school pronoun and parent-communication rules. But the school district has changed its policy and offered this teacher an accommodation, making this a weaker case for a nationwide ruling. If the Court intervenes, it could send the case back for reconsideration rather than decide the constitutional issues itself.
