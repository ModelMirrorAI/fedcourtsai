We predict the Supreme Court will deny review in this case, with about a one-in-five chance (22%) of granting it. Kimberly Polk, a substitute teacher in Maryland, argues that her school district violated her constitutional rights to free speech and religion by requiring her to agree to use students' preferred gender pronouns and keep transitions private from parents. Although a dissenting appeals judge and eight supporting briefs urge the Court to step in, the school district has already revised its parent-privacy rule after a March 2026 Supreme Court ruling (*Mirabelli v. Bonta*) and reinstated Polk while offering a name-only accommodation, making this case a poor vehicle for a major ruling.
