The case presents a massive constitutional question regarding state enforcement of immigration laws and preemption under *Arizona v. United States*, drawing a 24-state amicus brief. The Court is highly likely to grant review to address the circuit split or to revisit *Arizona*. A CVSG is possible given the federal preemption question, though the Court may simply grant it outright since the federal government's opposition is well known. If granted, the Court would likely take both QPs to address standing and preemption. A summary disposition is very unlikely given the need for full briefing and argument on such a major constitutional issue. Should the Court deny, expect a vigorous dissent from denial by Justices Thomas and Alito, who have expressed skepticism of broad preemption in this area.
