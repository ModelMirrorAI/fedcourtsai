Distributed three times so far (with two reschedules) and now the Court has invited the Solicitor General to file a brief expressing the views of the United States. Expect at least one further distribution after the SG files their brief, followed by a decision on the petition.

The Court is highly likely to grant the petition for plenary review. The first question presented addresses Article III standing for pre-enforcement challenges based on disavowed enforcement, and the second addresses obstacle preemption of state immigration laws that overlap with federal laws. If granted, the Court will likely focus heavily on the second question regarding obstacle preemption and the scope of state authority to enforce immigration-related offenses. A summary disposition is very unlikely given the national importance and the split over state-level immigration enforcement. Should the Court unexpectedly deny the petition, expect a dissent from denial from conservative justices emphasizing the state's sovereign right to enforce its laws.
