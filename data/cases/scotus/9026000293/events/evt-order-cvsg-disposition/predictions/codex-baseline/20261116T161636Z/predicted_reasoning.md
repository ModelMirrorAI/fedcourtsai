# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely **deny the petition after receiving the Solicitor General's invited brief**, leaving the Second Circuit judgment in place. A denial will most likely be an unexplained order, not a merits opinion resolving the recognition or TRIA questions. The substantive reason to expect no review is that revisiting the recognition framework alone need not eliminate the separate blocking-date obstacle to these judgment creditors' recovery.

## Procedural path from this snapshot

- **Starting state:** two conference distributions, one relist, and a CVSG already entered November 16, 2026.
- **Further distribution:** 98% probability of at least one more distribution. Expect one or two additional conference distributions after the invited brief and any response papers arrive, rather than an immediate sequence of weekly relists while the government prepares its views. The decision is likely months rather than days away; no particular SG filing date is assumed.
- **New CVSG:** none expected. The existing invitation already resolves that procedural step; the recorded zero for an additional-CVSG claim does not deny its existence.

## If the Court grants relief

The probability of any grant is **32%**. Conditional on a grant, a **cert-order disposition is more likely than plenary briefing and argument: 70%**. The leading such route is a GVR directing reconsideration in light of CIMEX or later resolution of the linked litigation, rather than a summary reversal adjudicating creditors' entitlement to these assets.

If this petition instead receives plenary consideration, expect it to travel with Havlish, No. 26-292, through consolidation or coordinated treatment. The question would concern whether recognition of Afghanistan fixes DAB's foreign-state status under the FSIA and how that determination interacts with TRIA; meaningful relief for these creditors would also require dealing with the companion's blocking-date question. The Court is more likely to frame a concrete statutory question than to adopt the petition's premise that CIMEX already dictates the answer. No unconditional prediction of a merits holding is made.

## Writings

Conditional on denial, there is a **15%** chance of a dissent or statement respecting denial. The modal forecast is no separate writing. If a Justice writes, Thomas or Alito is the likeliest individual author in this forecast, with Gorsuch next; this is a low-confidence authorship allocation, not a prediction that any one of them will dissent. Kagan is expected to remain recused and write nothing on the disposition.
