# Forecast — No. 26-293, Ashton v. The Taliban

The docket shows two distributions (conferences of 6 and 13 November 2026) and a CVSG issued 16 November 2026 in this case and in its companions, Nos. 26-292 (Havlish) and 26-294 (Owens). Justice Kagan is recused.

- **Further distribution.** The Solicitor General will file the invited brief in the first half of 2027, most likely by late May, and the three petitions will be distributed together again for a June 2027 conference (or early in October Term 2027 if the brief comes later). Expect at least one more distribution, and likely only one or two before the Court acts on the lead petition.
- **CVSG.** Already issued; none further.
- **Disposition.** The Court will most likely deny this petition together with its companions, following an SG recommendation against review. The main alternative is that the Court grants plenary review in Havlish on the TRIA timing question and/or the FSIA "foreign state" question, and either consolidates this petition or holds it. A held petition would then be vacated and remanded if the Court disturbs the Second Circuit's judgment, and denied if it affirms.
- **Question taken, if any.** The Court would not take this petition's GVR question as written for argument. If it grants plenary review it will do so on the Havlish questions; this petition would follow that case.
- **Summary route.** If this petition ends in a grant, a GVR is more likely than argument on this docket: either a GVR in light of the decision in the lead case or, less likely, a direct GVR in light of *Cimex*.
- **Separate writing.** If the petitions are denied, most likely no noted dissent. If one appears, it would come from Justice Thomas, Alito or Gorsuch and would be attached to the lead petition's order.
