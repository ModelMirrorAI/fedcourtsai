The Supreme Court will probably turn this petition down, but it is far from certain: there is a bit more than a one-in-three chance it ends in the petitioners' favour. Families of 9/11 victims want the Court to send their case about frozen Afghan central bank money back to the appeals court. The Court has asked the federal government's top appellate lawyer, the Solicitor General, for advice, which usually signals real interest. This petition's fate is tied to a companion case raising the main questions.
