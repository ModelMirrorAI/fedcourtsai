The Supreme Court is likely to decline this appeal, with about a one-in-five chance of granting some form of review. Families of terrorism victims want access to Afghan central-bank money. Their request has received additional consideration, but a separate legal obstacle may remain even if the Court accepts their argument about government immunity. If the Court intervenes, it would more likely send the case back for another look than immediately decide who gets the money. No written explanation is expected if review is denied.
