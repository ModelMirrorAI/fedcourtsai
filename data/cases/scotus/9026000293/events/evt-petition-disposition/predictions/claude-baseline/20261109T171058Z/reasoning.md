## Anchoring and adjustment
- Anchor: the elevated band's reached rate, pooled over the prior 10 Terms = **0.248** (from the frozen `cert_anchor`). Cross-check: the statpack relist-count cut, bucket 2, which reads 28.3% granted + 12.9% gvr ≈ 41% grant family.
- **Up**: the whole cluster was relisted together; there is an apparent circuit split on TRIA timing; the stakes are high (DAB assets); States filed an amicus brief on the lead petition; and there is no adverse party brief.
- **Down**: this petition's own QP is weak. From the decision summaries I retrieved, *CIMEX* held that Helms-Burton abrogates Cuban instrumentalities' immunity, and it does not appear to address who decides "foreign state" status. A standalone GVR in light of it is unlikely. The unrecognized-government recognition question is entangled with Executive foreign-affairs prerogatives, and Faiq raises vehicle concerns (pending remand issues). A relist with no respondent may also just reflect the late-distributed supplemental-brief motion, or a CVSG under consideration, rather than grant interest.
- **How this petition's outcome rides on Havlish**: if Havlish is granted, this petition is most likely granted and consolidated, or held and later GVR'd. Either counts as a grant. A denial of Havlish almost certainly means denial here. I estimate P(Havlish eventual grant) ≈ 0.32 and P(this petition gets any grant | Havlish granted) ≈ 0.8, and add ≈0.02 for a standalone CIMEX GVR. That gives ≈ 0.27, close to the anchor. I'm not moving far off it because I can't tell grant interest from CVSG interest from scheduling noise.

## Uncertainties
- I did not read the Faiq brief or the full *CIMEX* opinion, and my read of *CIMEX* comes from a law-firm summary. If *CIMEX* does discuss Executive recognition and "foreign state" status, the GVR route strengthens.
- A CVSG (the US has a deep interest in the DAB assets and the Afghan Fund) would postpone everything for months. That is reflected in the relist and CVSG increments, not in the disposition number.
- The corpus `query` returned generic GVR priors and nothing comparable. The statpack and the companion dockets carried the analysis.
