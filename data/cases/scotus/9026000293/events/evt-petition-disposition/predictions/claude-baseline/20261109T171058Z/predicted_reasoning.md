# Forecast — No. 26-293, Ashton v. The Taliban

**State forecast from:** two distributions (conferences of 11/6 and 11/13/2026), meaning one relist so far, with no CVSG. Companions 26-292 (Havlish) and 26-294 (Owens) were distributed for 11/6, and Havlish was redistributed for 11/13 too. The Court is moving the cluster together.

- **Further relists**: about even odds that the petition is distributed again after 11/13. The likeliest paths to that are another relist while a Justice studies the lead petition or writes, a hold pending Havlish, or a CVSG followed by redistribution after the SG's brief. A grant or denial at the 11/13 conference ends it without further distributions.
- **CVSG**: about 20%. No respondent is defending, and the United States has a strong interest in the frozen Da Afghanistan Bank assets and in recognition questions. If one comes, it would most likely issue on the order list after the 11/13 or a following conference, across the whole cluster.
- **Disposition**: most likely **denied** (≈73%), together with Havlish and Owens. If the Court takes the cluster, it would take Havlish's TRIA "blocking-date" question as written, and possibly the FSIA/recognition question. This petition's GVR question in light of *CIMEX* would not be taken on its own.
- **Summary route if granted**: substantial (≈45%). On this petition specifically, the most likely grant is a hold followed by a GVR after the Court decides Havlish, or a GVR in light of *CIMEX*, rather than plenary consolidation.
- **Dissent from denial**: unlikely (≈8%). If anyone writes, it is most plausibly Justice Thomas or Justice Gorsuch on the terrorism-victim TRIA question, and that writing would more likely sit on the lead Havlish order.
