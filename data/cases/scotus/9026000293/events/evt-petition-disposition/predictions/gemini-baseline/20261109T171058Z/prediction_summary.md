The Supreme Court is very likely to deny this petition for review, with only about a one-in-eight chance (13%) of taking the case or sending it back to the lower court. The petitioners, families of September 11 victims holding terrorism judgments against the Taliban, seek to collect $3.5 billion in frozen Afghan central bank funds and ask the Court to send the case back in light of a recent sovereign-immunity ruling (*Cimex*). However, the appeals court already ruled that the federal terrorism-judgment statute overrides foreign sovereign immunity, and it blocked recovery for an independent reason that *Cimex* does not affect: the Afghan central bank was not controlled by the Taliban on the day the United States froze the funds.
