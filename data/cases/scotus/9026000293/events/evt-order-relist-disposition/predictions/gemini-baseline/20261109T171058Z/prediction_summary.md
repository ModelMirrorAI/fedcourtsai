The Supreme Court is unlikely to grant this petition, with about a one-in-five chance (22%) of review, and will most likely deny it. Families of September 11 victims seek $3.5 billion in frozen Afghan central-bank funds to satisfy terrorism judgments against the Taliban, and five Second Circuit judges dissented when the appeals court blocked recovery. Even after an initial relist—delaying consideration to another conference—the Court typically denies review where, as here, no other federal appeals court has disagreed on this unique post-2021 Afghanistan dispute and the Executive Branch opposes turning over the central-bank reserves.
