# Forecast — Ashton v. The Taliban, No. 26-293

**Where the docket stands:** 2 distributions (one relist, 11/6 → 11/13). No response, no waiver, and no CVSG. The petition is moving in tandem with Havlish (26-292) and Owens (26-294).

**Disposition:** Denial is the most likely single outcome (P(any grant) ≈ 0.30). The Court will treat the three Taliban/DAB petitions together.

**Further relists (≈0.55):** I expect at least one more distribution. The likeliest triggers are:
- a CVSG followed by redistribution after the Solicitor General files;
- a call for a response;
- holding Ashton while Havlish is resolved.

A clean denial or GVR on 11/13 is the main alternative.

**CVSG (≈0.30):** The United States has strong interests here: Executive Order 14064 over the DAB reserves, Afghanistan recognition policy, and FSIA/TRIA construction. With no participating respondent, inviting the Solicitor General is a natural step, likely within the next few conferences.

**Question presented:** If the Court acts on this petition, it would take the QP as written: whether to vacate and remand in light of *Cimex* for CA2 to decide FSIA "foreign state" status without deferring to Executive recognition. If it takes plenary review, that would be in Havlish on the TRIA blocking-date question, and Ashton would be consolidated or held.

**Summary route (≈0.55 given a grant):** A grant in this petition is more likely a GVR in light of *Cimex* than plenary review, because that is exactly what the petition asks for.

**Dissent from denial (≈0.12 given denial):** A separate writing is unlikely. If one comes, the likeliest authors are Gorsuch or Thomas, writing on the FSIA/recognition point or on terrorism victims' access to TRIA.
