Prediction: the Supreme Court will most likely turn this petition away, but there is about a 30% chance it grants some relief. Victims of 9/11 and other terrorism are trying to collect court judgments from Afghan central-bank money frozen in New York. This petition asks the Court to send the case back to the appeals court for a fresh look after a recent Supreme Court ruling on when foreign governments are immune from suit. The Court has already put it on a second conference, which signals interest. It may ask the federal government for its views before deciding.
