I expect the Supreme Court to decline review, with about a seven-in-ten chance of no grant and a 28% chance of taking action that favors further review. The petition concerns terrorism victims' access to frozen Afghan central-bank funds. The Court's decision to consider it at another meeting makes review more plausible than usual. But the new ruling cited by the victims does not directly resolve the lower court's main obstacle: when the bank must have come under Taliban control. Related petitions remain another possible path to review.
