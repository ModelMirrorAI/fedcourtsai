The Supreme Court will probably decline to act on this petition, though it has a real chance: about one in three. Families of terrorism victims want a lower-court ruling, which shields frozen Afghan central-bank money from their judgments against the Taliban, sent back for a fresh look. The Court has asked the federal government's top appellate lawyer for an opinion, a sign of genuine interest, but most such petitions are still turned away, and this one mainly rides on a companion case.
