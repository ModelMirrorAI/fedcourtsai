## In brief

The Supreme Court declined to hear a Wisconsin man's bid to overturn his homicide conviction through federal habeas review.

## What happened

Henry Watson was convicted in Wisconsin state court of homicide by negligent handling of a dangerous weapon after a bullet he fired in an altercation with one man killed a bystander. The jury had been instructed to consider self-defense but convicted him on that count while acquitting him of a related charge against the man he was defending against. Wisconsin courts held that state law categorically barred a self-defense claim for the bystander's death. After the Wisconsin Supreme Court denied review, Watson sought federal habeas relief, arguing the trial violated his constitutional rights. A federal district court denied relief, and the Seventh Circuit denied him permission to appeal and denied rehearing.
