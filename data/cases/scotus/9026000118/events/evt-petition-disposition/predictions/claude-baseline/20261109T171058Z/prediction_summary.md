Alabama is appealing a lower court order that blocked its congressional map, and the Supreme Court must rule on this kind of appeal. We think it is very likely (about 4 in 5) that the Court sides with Alabama. The most likely route is a short "summary reversal," meaning the Court overturns the order without full briefing or argument. The alternative is wiping the order out because the 2026 election it covered is over. The main reason is that the Court already paused this same order in June, saying the lower court misapplied its recent voting-rights decision.
