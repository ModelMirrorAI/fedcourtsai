**Anchor.** The frozen `cert_anchor` reached rate for band `high` is 34.8%. The relist-count cut for 2 relists reads granted 28.3% + gvr 12.9% ≈ 41% grant family (n≈495), and for 3+ reads ≈37%. I depart far from that anchor for two reasons:
1. **This is a mandatory appeal, not cert.** The Court must dispose of the merits. "Denial" is not an option. The non-grant outcomes are summary affirmance or dismissal (for mootness or want of jurisdiction). Discretionary-cert base rates understate the grant family here.
2. **The Court already stayed this very injunction** on June 2, 2026, in a written opinion faulting the panel for departing from Callais and failing to presume legislative good faith. The State's prior appeal on the same map (25-273) ended in a vacate-and-remand after Callais; a corpus row shows `gvr`. The Court also summarily reversed in Abbott v. LULAC (April 2026). A Court with this posture is very unlikely to summarily affirm.

**Main risk: mootness.** The Singleton appellees argue that the injunction covered only the 2026 elections and that the appeal is moot after November 3. They cite Christian Civic League v. FEC, 549 U.S. 801 (2006), where an appeal was dismissed as moot. Alabama answers that the special-master-plan portion lasts until the Legislature enacts a new plan, that the injunction has continuing effect, and, alternatively, that the Court should grant Munsingwear vacatur. Even if the Court agrees the case is moot, its usual practice for an appellant who lost the chance to appeal through happenstance is vacatur, which counts in the grant family. A bare dismissal would leave standing liability findings that a majority has already called erroneous. So I put dismissal at roughly 10–12%.

**Distribution of outcomes:** summary reversal ~40%, vacate/remand (Munsingwear or in light of the stay opinion) ~25%, note probable jurisdiction ~17%, dismissed as moot ~11%, other/affirm ~7%. Grant family = 0.82. The relist pattern (relisted after the election-eve conference, and again for 11/13) fits either a per curiam being written or the Court waiting until after the election to address mootness.

**Uncertainty.** I may underweight a plain dismissal. The Court sometimes dismisses moot PI appeals without vacatur when the appellant can obtain review after trial, and a trial is planned for early 2027.
