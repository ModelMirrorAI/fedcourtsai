The Supreme Court is very likely—about nine chances in ten—to take action overturning Alabama's lower-court redistricting order or sending it back. The most likely result is reversal without another full hearing because the justices previously found serious errors in the same order. A different possibility is that the election's completion leads the Court to cancel the order without deciding who is right. I expect a short further delay and, if the Court reverses, a dissent led by Justice Sotomayor.
