Alabama is appealing a court order that blocked its congressional map for the November 2026 election. The Supreme Court had already paused that order, and the election has now taken place under Alabama's map, so the dispute over that one election is probably over ("moot"). We think it is very likely, about 85%, that the Court will wipe out the lower court's order and send the case back, rather than simply dismissing the appeal and leaving the order on the books. Several Justices may write separately.
