The Court is likely to set aside the lower court's temporary redistricting ruling and send the case back, rather than hear a full new round of arguments. I put the chance of some review or judgment-setting-aside action at about three in four. Alabama's November 3 election has passed, creating a serious question about whether this temporary order still matters. An earlier Supreme Court stay also favors intervention. Simply dismissing the appeal remains a meaningful alternative, and setting aside the ruling would not necessarily decide the underlying voting-rights dispute.
