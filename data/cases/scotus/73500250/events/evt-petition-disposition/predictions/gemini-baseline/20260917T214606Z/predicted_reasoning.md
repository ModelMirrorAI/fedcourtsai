Distributed once so far. I expect the petition to be denied without further relists. The United States is already a party (as respondent), so a CVSG is essentially out of the question as the Solicitor General will present the government's views directly. If granted, the Court would likely take the first two QPs to resolve the circuit split on the applicability of the totality-of-the-circumstances framework to 18 U.S.C. § 242 prosecutions and clarify the mens rea requirements under *Screws v. United States*, while bypassing the fact-bound questions regarding de minimis injury and falsification of reports. A summary disposition route is unlikely, given that the petition challenges the analytic framework used by the Fifth Circuit on an issue of excessive force. Should it deny, expect no separate writing.
