A former border officer convicted of using excessive force and falsifying a report asks the Supreme Court to review his conviction. The Court asked the government to respond, which shows someone took a look, but most such petitions are still turned down. We predict the Court will **deny** review, with about a one-in-twenty chance it agrees to hear the case. The main reason: the arguments mostly dispute how the lower court weighed the evidence in this one case, which the Court rarely takes up.
