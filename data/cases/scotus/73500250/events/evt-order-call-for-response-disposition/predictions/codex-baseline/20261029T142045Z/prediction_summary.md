The Supreme Court is very likely to decline review of Miguel Angel Delgado's convictions, with about a nine-in-one-hundred chance of taking the case. Asking the government to respond shows interest, but does not promise a hearing. Delgado argues that the courts used the wrong rules for excessive force and criminal intent. The lower-court opinion, however, discusses the wider encounters and separately addresses intent. Those features make this look more like a dispute about evidence than a clear legal conflict needing Supreme Court review.
