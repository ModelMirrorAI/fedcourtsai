The Supreme Court is very likely to deny former Customs and Border Protection officer Miguel Angel Delgado, Jr.'s petition to review his convictions for using excessive force against two detainees and falsifying an official report, with only about a 1-in-17 chance (6%) that the Court grants review or sends the case back to the lower court. Although the Court asked the federal government to file a response—likely because the Fifth Circuit appeals court did not mention a recent 2025 Supreme Court ruling (*Barnes v. Felix*) requiring courts to look at all surrounding circumstances in excessive-force cases—the appeals court's opinion actually examined the full video and witness record from Delgado's three-day trial before a judge, making the case a poor candidate for Supreme Court intervention.
