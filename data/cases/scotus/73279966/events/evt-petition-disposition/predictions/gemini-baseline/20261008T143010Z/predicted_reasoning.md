# Predicted Reasoning of the Court

**Disposition:**
The Court is highly likely to grant the petition for certiorari. The grant will likely be a standard order setting the case for merits briefing and oral argument, rather than a summary disposition (GVR), because the SG recommends a full grant to resolve a recognized circuit split on the Supremacy Clause issue.

**Relists:**
The petition is scheduled for the November 6, 2026 conference. Given the SG's recommendation and the clear circuit split, the Court may grant it at the first conference after the SG's brief without needing further relists.

**Question Presented:**
The Court is expected to grant the single question presented regarding whether the Supremacy Clause prohibits a state from applying its minimum-wage law to federal immigration detainees at a privately operated federal facility.

**Dissent from Denial:**
In the unlikely event the Court denies certiorari, there is a strong likelihood of a dissent from denial (e.g., by Justices Thomas, Alito, or Kavanaugh), given the circuit split and the SG's position that the Ninth Circuit's decision is incorrect and disrupts federal operations.
