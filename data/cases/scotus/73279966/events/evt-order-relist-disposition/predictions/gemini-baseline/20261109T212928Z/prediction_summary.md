We predict that the Supreme Court will grant review in *The GEO Group, Inc. v. Nwauzor*, making it likely—at a 72% chance—that the Justices will hear the appeal. The case asks whether the Constitution's Supremacy Clause, which makes federal law supreme over conflicting state laws, bars Washington from forcing a private contractor running a federal immigration detention center to pay detained workers the state minimum wage of over $17 per hour instead of the $1-per-day federal program allowance. A grant is likely because the Court asked the U.S. Solicitor General—the federal government's top appellate lawyer—for the government's views, the Solicitor General strongly urged the Court to take the case, seven appellate judges dissented below, and the Court has relisted the petition for a second look at conference.
