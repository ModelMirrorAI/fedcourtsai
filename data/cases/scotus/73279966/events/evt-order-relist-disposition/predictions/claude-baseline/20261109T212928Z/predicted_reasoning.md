# Forecast — The GEO Group v. Nwauzor, No. 25-828

Three distributions so far: 5/14/2026, then 11/6/2026 after the SG's brief, then relisted on Nov 9 for the 11/13 conference. A CVSG is already on the docket (May 18 2026), and the SG has filed a brief recommending a grant.

- **Disposition:** I expect the Court to **grant certiorari**, most likely at the 11/13 conference or the one after. There is roughly a 40% chance of at least one further distribution before the petition is decided. That would most plausibly happen because the Court is coordinating this case with *GEO Group v. Ferguson* (No. 26-71), the related Washington case on the same conference list. Granting this case and holding 26-71 is the likeliest pairing.
- **CVSG:** a CVSG has already issued, so none is expected.
- **Question presented:** if granted, I expect the Court to take GEO's question as written, or as the SG reformulates it: whether the Supremacy Clause (intergovernmental immunity and/or preemption) allows Washington to apply its minimum-wage law to immigration detainees in a work program at a federally contracted detention facility, when the State exempts its own detainees. The discrimination half of intergovernmental immunity is the most likely focus.
- **Route:** I expect plenary briefing and argument, not a summary disposition. A GVR in light of *Hencely v. Fluor* is unlikely, because that decision supports the judgment below rather than requiring a fresh look.
- **If denied:** I expect a written dissent from denial, most likely by Justice Alito or Justice Thomas, possibly joined by Justice Gorsuch. A statement by Justice Kavanaugh is also possible.
