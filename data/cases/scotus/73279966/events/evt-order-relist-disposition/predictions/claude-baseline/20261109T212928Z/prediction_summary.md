We think the Supreme Court will probably agree to hear this case (about a 72% chance). GEO Group runs a federal immigration detention center in Washington State under contract with the federal government. The question is whether Washington can make GEO pay detainees in a voluntary work program the state minimum wage. The Court asked the federal government's top lawyer, the Solicitor General, for advice, and the government recommended taking the case. It also pointed out that another federal appeals court decided a similar question the opposite way.
