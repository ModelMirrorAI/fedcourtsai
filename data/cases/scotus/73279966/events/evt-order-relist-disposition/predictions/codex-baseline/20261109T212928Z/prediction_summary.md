The Supreme Court is more likely than not to hear GEO Group's challenge, with about a seven-in-ten chance of review. The case asks whether Washington can require minimum wages for immigration detainees working at a privately operated federal detention center. The federal government's top Supreme Court lawyer recommends review, and the justices have scheduled another discussion. But opponents dispute the claimed disagreement among courts and say changed detention standards reduce the case's future importance. A grant would not itself decide who wins.
