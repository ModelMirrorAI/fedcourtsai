# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely grant the petition for full briefing and argument. I assign any grant a 70% probability; the most likely individual disposition is an ordinary grant, not a summary merits ruling. This forecasts review only, not that GEO will ultimately win.

The docket already contains three distributions, including the November 9 distribution for the November 13, 2026 conference. I slightly favor resolution without a fourth distribution. There is a 42% chance of at least one additional distribution; if that happens, I expect one or two more rather than a long string of relists. The most likely timing is an order after the November 13 conference or within the following several conference cycles, subject to the Court's calendar.

The Court already requested and received the Solicitor General's views. I expect no renewed request. The zero `cvsg-increment` probability does not negate the May 18 invitation; that claim is vacuous for this cell.

If review is granted, I expect the Court to take the petition's single Supremacy Clause question, possibly restated to emphasize whether a State may apply minimum-wage requirements to federal immigration detainee work at private contractor facilities while exempting its own detainees. That is close to the SG's formulation. Intergovernmental immunity and preemption would be possible grounds within the question, rather than an independent general ruling on detainees' employment status under every wage law.

Conditional on a grant, I put **90%** on a proceeding for full merits consideration and **10%** on disposition in the cert order itself. A GVR or summary reversal is not the leading route: the parties dispute the relevance of recent contractor decisions, the alleged circuit conflict, and the state-law comparator. Those disputes more naturally invite briefing than an immediate correction.

If the Court instead denies review, an unexplained denial remains more likely than a written explanation. I assign **28% conditional on denial** to a dissent or statement respecting denial. Thomas or Alito would be my leading candidates for an authored objection, with Gorsuch another possibility. These are possibilities, not predictions that any particular Justice will write. The separate per-Justice probabilities in `prediction.json` are unconditional and include any writing accompanying either a grant or a denial.
