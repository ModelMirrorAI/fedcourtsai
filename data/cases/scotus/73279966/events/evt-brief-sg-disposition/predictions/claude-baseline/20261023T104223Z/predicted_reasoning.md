# Forecast — GEO Group v. Nwauzor, No. 25-828 (SG-brief moment)

**The Court grants plenary review (P ≈ 0.80).**

- **Distributions so far:** one (conference of 5/14/2026), which produced the
  CVSG on 5/18/2026. The Solicitor General's invited brief was filed 9/15/2026
  and recommends a **grant**. Expect the petition to be **distributed again**
  once the parties have had their chance to file supplemental briefs after the
  SG brief, most likely for an October or early-November 2026 conference. A
  grant at that first post-CVSG conference is the modal path; one or two
  relists before a grant are also plausible, and a relist is especially likely
  if the Court wants to take up this case together with the related
  Washington-contractor petition the SG brief describes as pending
  (GEO Group v. Inslee, No. 26-71).
- **CVSG:** already on the docket; no further call.
- **Question taken:** the petition's single QP — whether the Supremacy Clause
  (intergovernmental immunity and/or preemption) bars Washington from applying
  its minimum-wage law to detainees in a federal voluntary work program at a
  contractor-run ICE facility, when the State exempts its own detainees. The
  Court may adopt the SG's reformulation, which foregrounds the
  discrimination point (the State's exemption of its own detainees).
- **Route:** plenary briefing and argument, not a summary disposition. There is
  no intervening decision to GVR on, and a judgment of this size (~$37M) over a
  contested constitutional question with dissents below is not summary-reversal
  material.
- **If granted, the likely ground** is intergovernmental immunity under the
  anti-discrimination prong (the State treats the federal contractor worse than
  itself by exempting state detention facilities), with preemption as the
  alternative the Court could reach or leave.
- **Should it deny** (the minority branch), expect a written dissent from
  denial — most plausibly from Justices Alito, Thomas, and/or Gorsuch — given
  the SG's recommendation, the three-administration federal position, and the
  multiple dissents below.
