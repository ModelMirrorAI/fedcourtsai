# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny certiorari in Daisey Trust v. Federal Housing Finance Agency. The modal path is denial following the scheduled September 28, 2026 conference, in late September or early October, without another distribution and without a separate writing. The denial will not decide the constitutional questions on their merits.

The snapshot records **two distributions**, April 22 and July 22, and **no CVSG**. The first distribution preceded a request for a response on May 5, before the scheduled May 14 conference; the second followed the opposition and reply. From that recorded count of two, the probability of at least one additional distribution is **22%**. If there is another distribution, one additional conference is more likely than a prolonged series.

A new CVSG is very unlikely (**0.5%**): the Solicitor General already represents the federal respondents and has submitted their opposition. A request for that party's response is not a CVSG.

Conditional on a grant, plenary consideration is more likely than disposition in the cert order: **12%** for the summary route and **88%** for briefing and argument. No intervening precedent requiring a GVR is identified in the materials reviewed; the principal decisions discussed by both sides preceded the judgment below.

If the Court takes a question, the strongest candidate is QP 1, narrowed to whether the Appropriations Clause requires an ascertainable funding ceiling beyond an identified source and purpose. QP 3 could accompany it as a challenge to the adequacy of FHFA's qualitative spending constraints. The standalone statutory-label challenge in QP 2 is less likely to be taken because preservation is contested. These are conditional forecasts, not predictions that a merits opinion will issue.

Conditional on denial, the probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial is **13%**. The modal forecast remains no separate writing; no individual Justice's vote is predicted.
