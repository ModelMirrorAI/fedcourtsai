The Supreme Court is very likely to keep the government's decision ending temporary protection for Venezuelans in effect while the appeal continues. I give the government a 95% chance of getting all or part of the pause it seeks if the Court rules on the request. The justices have already paused this same lower court judgment once. A later Supreme Court ruling limits judges' power to review the decision to end protection, though families still dispute whether the government could cancel an extension it had already granted.
