# Forecast: Mullin v. National TPS Alliance, No. 26A714 (stay application)

**State forecast from:** the docket shows one entry, the application submitted to Justice Kagan on Nov. 18, 2026. No response has been called for, no referral, no amicus briefs.

- **Disposition.** The Court will grant the stay of the N.D. Cal. summary-judgment order pending the Ninth Circuit appeal and any certiorari petition. Probability 0.84 that relief is granted in whole or in part, conditional on the application not going moot. The most likely form is a short unsigned order by the full Court, in the pattern of the October 2025 order in No. 25A326, possibly citing *Mullin v. Doe*.
- **Administrative stay.** Justice Kagan (or the Court) will enter an administrative stay on or before Nov. 20, unless the Ninth Circuit extends its own administrative stay first.
- **Response.** Justice Kagan will call for a response on a short fuse, within a day or two of filing and due within about a week (0.88).
- **Referral.** The application will be referred to the full Court and decided by it (0.82).
- **Amicus.** Roughly even odds that at least one amicus brief is docketed (0.50). The two earlier applications each drew four, but this round is faster and the legal question narrower.
- **Timing.** Disposition within about one to three weeks of filing, most likely by early December 2026.
- **Separate writings.** If granted, Justice Jackson is the likeliest to write a dissent (about 0.3); Justices Sotomayor and Kagan are likelier to note a dissent without opinion, or to acquiesce given *Doe*. A concurrence addressing lower-court adherence to the Court's interim orders is possible but unlikely.
- **Main path to a non-grant.** The Ninth Circuit itself grants a stay or decides the appeal for the government before the Court acts, leading the Solicitor General to withdraw the application or the Court to dismiss or deny it as unnecessary. An outright merits-based denial is unlikely; a partial grant confined to the termination decision (leaving the vacatur ruling) is a small possibility and would still count as a grant.
