The Supreme Court is very likely (about 84 percent) to grant the federal government's request for a stay, meaning a temporary pause, of a California district court ruling that would restore Temporary Protected Status for Venezuelans. The Court has already paused rulings in this same case twice, and the government says a Supreme Court decision from June 2026 bars this kind of lawsuit. The main way the request fails is if the appeals court pauses the ruling itself first, making the request unnecessary.
