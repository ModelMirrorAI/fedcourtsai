## 3. Case-Specific Adjustments and Explicit Scenario Decomposition

Starting from the **68.4%** (`39 / 57`) `federal` `unmarked` reference rate, two strong forces pull in opposite directions:

### A. Upward substantive pull (to ~0.91 absent `CA9 DE 109`)
If the Supreme Court reaches the merits of staying Judge Chen's September 5, 2025 summary judgment order, the Government's case is extraordinarily strong:
- **Two prior stays in this exact case:** This Court already stayed the district court's preliminary § 705 postponement order (`24A1059`, May 19, 2025, 8–1) and then stayed **this exact September 5, 2025 summary judgment order** as to both the vacatur and termination (`25A326`, Oct. 3, 2025, 6–3).
- **Intervening merits precedent and GVR:** In *Mullin v. Doe*, 609 U.S. 324 (June 25, 2026) (6–3, Alito, J.), the Court held that 8 U.S.C. § 1254a(b)(5)(A) bars judicial review of *all* non-constitutional claims—substantive, procedural, and subsidiary—challenging TPS determinations, rejecting the *McNary* and reviewability-presumption premises of the Ninth Circuit's prior affirmance, and on October 5, 2026 (`No. 26-48`) GVR'd the Ninth Circuit's judgment in light of *Doe*.
- **Weakness of Respondents' remaining remand theories (`CA9 DE 106`):** On remand, Respondents concede their statutory challenge to the February 2025 *termination* is foreclosed as such, arguing instead that *Doe* (`609 U.S. at 344–45`) left open ultra vires review of the February 2025 *vacatur* of the January 2025 18-month extension (and that an invalid vacatur taints the termination's effective date or supports collateral declaratory relief in consolidated No. `26-187`). Yet (1) `25A326` already stayed the vacatur set-aside, (2) the January 2025 extension expired by its own terms on **October 2, 2026**, at which point the unreviewable termination took effect by operation of 8 U.S.C. § 1254a(b)(3)(B), and (3) ten days ago in *Mullin v. Friends of the Ruidosa Church*, No. `26A560` (Nov. 9, 2026, 8–1), the Court reiterated (*Dalton v. Specter*) that claims alleging an official exceeded statutory authority remain statutory claims subject to non-constitutional review bars.
- **Equities:** After 19 months with Venezuela's 2023 TPS termination in effect under this Court's stays, DHS represents that reviving the vacated status would compel release of ~1,800 detainees and halt up to 332 final removal orders.

### B. Downward procedural pull from `CA9 DE 109` (from ~0.91 down to **0.57**)
Why did the Solicitor General wait until November 18 to file `26A714`? Because on November 6 (`CA9 DE 102` at 3, 21), DOJ itself asked the Ninth Circuit either to rule by November 13 or to extend its administrative stay until **"seven days after any decision in these appeals."** Every immediate-harm argument in `application.txt` rested on the premise that the Ninth Circuit's 14-day administrative stay would expire tomorrow, Friday, November 20. With `CA9 DE 109` entering that exact seven-day post-judgment stay last night (`7:01 PM ET`), I decompose the **non-moot** resolution space into five concrete paths:

1. **Path G1 — Grant after Ninth Circuit affirms in whole or in part while `26A714` remains pending (`0.25`):** If the Solicitor General updates Justice Kagan on `CA9 DE 109` without withdrawing `26A714`, Justice Kagan has no need to rush before the Ninth Circuit issues its imminent published opinion (supplemental briefing closed Nov. 10). Notably, the panel (`Wardlaw`, `Mendoza`, `Johnstone`) twice previously denied stays, initially denied any post-GVR stay on October 30, ordered 5,600-word supplemental briefs, and just entered a stay expiring *"seven days after the publication of our judgment"* rather than summarily reversing (contrast *Nat'l TPS Alliance v. Mullin*, No. 26-199, 9th Cir. July 14, 2026). That strongly signals a substantial (~45–50%) chance the panel publishes an opinion preserving at least vacatur/declaratory relief. Because `26A714` explicitly prays for a stay both pending the Ninth Circuit appeal **and, if the Ninth Circuit affirms, pending certiorari** (`application.txt` p. 1), `26A714` remains live upon affirmance and is ~92%+ to be granted within the seven-day window.
2. **Path G2 — Grant pending appeal and certiorari before Ninth Circuit publishes its judgment (`0.32`):** Even before the Ninth Circuit rules (with Thanksgiving week approaching), if the Solicitor General presses `26A714` to avoid a fourth emergency fire drill on a seven-day fuse, five or more Justices of the `25A326` / *Doe* majority have strong institutional reason to simply reinstate the `25A326` stay that governed for 13 months before the November 6 GVR mandate.
3. **Path U1 — Near-term voluntary withdrawal by the Solicitor General (`withdrawn` -> ungranted, `0.20`):** Because `CA9 DE 109` granted the exact seven-day post-judgment administrative bridge DOJ requested in `DE 102` and eliminated all risk of Judge Chen's order taking effect during remand deliberations, the Solicitor General may withdraw `26A714` without prejudice over the next few days, reserving renewal for if and when the Ninth Circuit affirms.
4. **Path U2 — Subsequent voluntary withdrawal after Ninth Circuit reverses on remand (`withdrawn` -> ungranted, `0.10`):** Corpus priors (`25A227` *Trump v. Global Health Council*; `26A297` *USPS v. California*; `25A539` *Rollins*; `24A1106` *AFGE*) show that when lower-court action eliminates the challenged order while an SG stay application is pending, DOJ routinely files a withdrawal letter (`interim_reading: "withdrawn"`, scored as ungranted) before the Court enters a mootness order.
5. **Path U3 — Denial without prejudice by the Court (`denied` -> ungranted, `0.13`):** If the Solicitor General presses `26A714` while `CA9 DE 109` is in force and before the Ninth Circuit rules, zero irreparable harm can occur prior to appellate judgment plus seven days. At least two of Chief Justice Roberts, Justice Kavanaugh, and Justice Barrett could join Justices Sotomayor, Kagan, and Jackson in denying `26A714` without prejudice to renewal after the Ninth Circuit completes the GVR remand this Court ordered six weeks ago.

Summing the non-moot paths gives **P(grant) = 0.25 + 0.32 = 0.57** and **P(ungranted) = 0.20 + 0.10 + 0.13 = 0.43**.
