## Disposition Forecast (`granted`, P = 0.57 conditional on non-mootness)

Conditional on the application not being dismissed or denied as moot, the Court will **grant** a stay of the Northern District of California's September 5, 2025 summary judgment order pending disposition of the Ninth Circuit appeal and any timely petition for a writ of certiorari (`probability = 0.57`, `predicted_disposition = "granted"`).

- **Substantive and equitable posture if reached.** If the full Court reaches the stay request—either directly before the Ninth Circuit rules, or after the Ninth Circuit publishes a remand opinion affirming relief in whole or in part on Respondents' extension-vacatur theory—a six-Justice majority (`Roberts`, `Thomas`, `Alito`, `Gorsuch`, `Kavanaugh`, `Barrett`) will grant the stay. The Court already stayed this exact summary judgment order in *Noem v. National TPS Alliance*, 146 S. Ct. 23 (2025) (`25A326`), after previously staying the preliminary postponement order in `24A1059`, 145 S. Ct. 2728 (2025). Since then, *Mullin v. Doe*, 609 U.S. 324 (2026), held that 8 U.S.C. § 1254a(b)(5)(A) bars all non-constitutional challenges to TPS determinations, and the vacated January 2025 extension expired on October 2, 2026 by its own terms, leaving the unreviewable February 2025 termination independently operative under 8 U.S.C. § 1254a(b)(3)(B). Moreover, because the application expressly seeks a stay both pending the Ninth Circuit appeal and, if the Ninth Circuit affirms, pending certiorari, a Ninth Circuit remand decision preserving vacatur or declaratory relief will leave `26A714` squarely live and trigger a Supreme Court stay within the seven-day post-judgment window.
- **Substantial ungranted paths (`0.43` non-moot complement).** Two procedural paths created by the Ninth Circuit's November 18 administrative stay carry substantial weight as ungranted outcomes: (1) **voluntary withdrawal** by the Solicitor General—either immediately because the Ninth Circuit granted the exact seven-day post-judgment administrative bridge the Government requested below, or subsequently if the Ninth Circuit reverses the district court on remand before the Supreme Court acts; and (2) **denial without prejudice** by the Court if the Solicitor General presses the application while the Ninth Circuit's administrative stay prevents any interim irreparable harm during completion of the Court's October 5, 2026 GVR remand.
