The Supreme Court will very likely decline to hear this case — I put the chance it agrees to review at about seven percent. A Chicago firefighter fired over offensive Facebook posts argues courts disagree about how much proof a government employer needs before punishing off-duty speech. The Court asked the city to respond, a sign of some interest, and advocacy groups are filing supporting briefs. But the posts make this an unappealing test case, and the Court recently turned away similar disputes.
