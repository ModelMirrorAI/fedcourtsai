# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely **deny certiorari**. The probability of any grant is 20%.

The docket currently records **one distribution**, for the November 13, 2026 conference. Respondents must supply the response requested on November 2, subject to the pending extension request. The petition will very likely be distributed again after that process: **98% probability of at least one additional distribution**. The central path is one new distribution after the response and reply opportunity, followed by denial. A grant path is more likely to include an additional substantive relist. The anticipated administrative redistribution is not evidence that the petition has already survived a merits conference.

The Court is unlikely to invite the Solicitor General's views: **2% probability of a new CVSG**. No invitation is currently recorded. The question concerns the general constitutional limits on a municipal employer, rather than an evident issue requiring specialized federal advice.

**If review is granted**, the Court will most likely take the question whether a public employer may rely on projected injury to public trust to outweigh off-duty speech on public concerns without substantiated operational disruption. A narrower formulation could focus on public-facing emergency-service employees and the evidentiary showing needed to support predicted harm. The Court need not decide that employers must always wait for actual harm.

Plenary briefing and argument are more likely than a disposition accompanying the grant: **10% conditional probability of a summary route**. No identified intervening controlling decision makes a GVR the natural course, and the alleged circuit disagreement and competing readings of the record favor full consideration over summary reversal.

If the Court denies review, the most likely order contains no explanation or separate writing. The conditional probability of some dissent from denial or statement respecting denial is **20%**. Thomas is the most plausible author of such a writing because of the MacRae concerns quoted in the petition; Alito or Gorsuch are less likely possibilities. That is a forecast about possible writings, not a prediction of undisclosed cert votes.
