The Supreme Court is very likely to decline this firefighter's appeal; I put the chance of review at about one in five. He argues that Chicago fired him for offensive off-duty Facebook posts without evidence that they disrupted its work. Different appeals courts appear to demand different proof of harm. But his public-facing emergency duties and identification with the department complicate the comparison. The Court has requested a response, so another round of consideration is likely before it decides whether to hear the case.
