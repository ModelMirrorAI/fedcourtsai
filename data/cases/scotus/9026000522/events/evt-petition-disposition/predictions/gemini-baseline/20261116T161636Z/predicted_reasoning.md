## Conditional Scope and Route if Granted

- **Question presented if granted:** If the Court were to grant review, it would take the single question presented substantially as framed—or modestly narrowed to public-safety employees—asking whether a public employer can carry its burden under *Pickering v. Board of Education*, 391 U.S. 563 (1968), by relying on predicted harm to community trust from off-duty social-media speech without evidence of actual or concrete workplace disruption. If granted, the likely merits ground would clarify that while public-safety employers need not wait for an actual workplace incident before acting, predictions of eroded public trust must rest on specific job-duty nexus and objective contextual facts rather than bare speculation, remanding any unreached *Connick* step-one, *Mt. Healthy*, and qualified-immunity defenses.
- **Summary disposition vs. plenary review (`summary-disposition-route`: 0.12 conditional on grant):** Conditional on review being granted, **plenary briefing and oral argument** would be the heavily favored route (~88%). No pending Supreme Court merits case presents the same question to trigger a hold-and-GVR, and the fact-bound record plus unreached threshold and immunity defenses below make summary per curiam reversal highly unlikely.

## Separate Writings on Denial

- **Dissent or statement respecting denial (`dissent-from-denial`: 0.09 conditional on denial):** Most likely the Court will deny certiorari **without recorded dissent or separate statement**. Once respondents' brief in opposition highlights that petitioner waived his *Monell* claim against the City of Chicago, that the remaining individual-capacity officials preserved qualified immunity, that the Seventh Circuit only assumed arguendo that all thirteen inflammatory Facebook posts touched on matters of public concern, and that the First Circuit is actively considering a cleaner off-duty social-media *Pickering* case en banc (*Hussey v. City of Cambridge*), chambers interested in the underlying doctrinal issue will treat this petition as an unsuitable vehicle. If any separate writing accompanies the denial order, **Justice Thomas** (who authored a solo statement respecting denial in *MacRae v. Mattos*, 606 U.S. 937 (2025), and is the likeliest source of the November 2 call for a response) is the overwhelmingly most probable author, potentially joined by or alongside **Justice Alito** or **Justice Gorsuch**.
