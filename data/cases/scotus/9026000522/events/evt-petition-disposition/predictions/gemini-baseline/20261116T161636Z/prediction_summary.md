The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Sam Inendino v. Annette Nance-Holt*, with only a very small chance—about one in thirty (3.5%)—of taking the case. Although the Court asked the City of Chicago to respond to the fired firefighter's free-speech appeal, severe procedural obstacles stand in the way once that response arrives: Inendino forfeited his legal claim against the City itself in the lower court, the individual officials he sued can claim legal immunity from personal financial liability, and the Court turned away much cleaner public-employee social-media speech appeals earlier this year.
