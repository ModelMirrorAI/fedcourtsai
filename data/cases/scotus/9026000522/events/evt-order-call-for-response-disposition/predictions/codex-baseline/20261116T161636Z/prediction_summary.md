The Supreme Court is very likely to decline this case; I estimate a 16% chance that it agrees to intervene. The Court's request for a response shows some interest in the firefighter's claim that Chicago fired him for personal speech without evidence of harm to its work. But the lower court tied its decision to his public-safety duties and community trust, which may make this a fact-specific dispute rather than the best case for a nationwide rule. Another procedural scheduling step is likely before a decision.
