The Supreme Court will most likely decline to hear this case: the chance it agrees to review is under one in ten. A Chicago firefighter fired over offensive Facebook posts asks the Court to decide how much proof of workplace disruption a government employer needs before punishing off-duty speech. The Court asked the city to respond, a mild sign of interest, and one Justice has invited such a case. But the Court recently turned away two similar petitions, and the facts here are unsympathetic.
