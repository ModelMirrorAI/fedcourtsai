The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Sam Inendino v. Annette Nance-Holt*, with only about a one-in-seventeen (6%) chance of agreeing to hear the case. Although the Court asked the City of Chicago to respond to the fired firefighter's First Amendment appeal and outside groups are filing briefs supporting him, serious legal and factual obstacles stand in the way: Inendino forfeited his legal claim against the City itself on appeal, individual officials can claim legal immunity from money damages, and his public Facebook posts in firefighter uniform celebrated violence against protesters and used racial slurs toward the community he served.
