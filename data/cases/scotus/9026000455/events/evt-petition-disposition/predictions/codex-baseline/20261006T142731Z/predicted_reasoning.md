The baseline contains **zero petition distributions**, no petition relist, and no CVSG. Its July 22 distribution and October 5 grant concern motion 26M8. Thus the most likely path adds **one** petition distribution to the frozen count of zero, but no true relist after that first distribution. The structured `relist-increment` probability of 0.97 prices any increase over zero as the contract defines it; it does not predict a 97% chance of reconsideration at a second conference.

I forecast no CVSG (probability of a new call: 0.004). The petition concerns state child-welfare officials and federal jurisdiction over an individual civil-rights suit; it does not present an identified federal administrative interest warranting the Solicitor General's participation.

I do not forecast a direct Supreme Court adjudication of whether the particular child removal violated the Constitution.

Conditional on any grant, I assign 0.40 to disposition in the cert order and 0.60 to plenary proceedings. Summary correction or vacatur is plausible if the lower opinion unmistakably forecloses independent damages claims, but no intervening decision supplying a specific GVR trigger is identified in the materials consulted. Plenary consideration is slightly likelier on the grant branch because clarifying the jurisdictional distinction may require attention to the relief sought and the sequence of proceedings.

Conditional on denial, I assign 0.02 to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. The point forecast is no separate writing, and no individual Justice's vote is predicted.
