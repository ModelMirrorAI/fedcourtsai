Distributed once so far. I expect zero further relists before a decision on the petition. Pro se family law petitions generally do not attract the level of interest from the Court required to warrant a relist.

I expect no CVSG will be called for. The case is a domestic relations matter between private parties and implicates no federal interest.

If granted, the Court would likely take Question 1 to clarify the boundaries of the adequate and independent state grounds doctrine as applied to unexpectedly strict state procedural rules, leaving behind the specific factual disputes regarding notice and record completion in Questions 2 and 3.

A summary disposition (e.g., GVR) is unlikely. If the Court were to address the procedural due process standards applied by the Arizona courts, it would likely require plenary review rather than resting on a clear intervening precedent.

Should the Court deny the petition, I expect no separate writing or dissent from denial. The case lacks the national importance or mature circuit split that typically draws a Justice's separate statement.
