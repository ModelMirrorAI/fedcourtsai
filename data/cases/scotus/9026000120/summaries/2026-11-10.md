## In brief

An advocacy group and individuals ask the Court to revive their lawsuit against Colorado legislators over public-hearing speech rules the legislators say are protected by legislative immunity.

## What happened

Gays Against Groomers, Rocky Mountain Women's Network, and two individuals testified against a bill at Colorado legislative committee hearings. Committee rules barred "misgendering" or "deadnaming" witnesses, and petitioners say they were cut off, removed, or had testimony erased from the record for violating these rules. They sued the legislators, individually and in their official roles, claiming First Amendment violations. The district court dismissed the case, holding the legislators had legislative immunity. The Tenth Circuit affirmed, ruling that immunity covers both individual- and official-capacity suits and covers both adopting and enforcing the rules.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether legislative immunity, a protection historically meant to shield lawmakers personally from liability, can also be used as a defense when a lawsuit is aimed at the governing body itself through claims against officials in their official roles. It is also asked whether immunity covers not just writing rules but also enforcing them against individual speakers.

## Where it stands

The petition has been fully briefed, including a reply, and was distributed for conference twice, most recently for November 13, 2026. No order granting or denying the petition is recorded yet.

## What each outcome would mean

Gays Against Groomers and the other petitioners are asking the Court to reverse the Tenth Circuit's ruling in favor of Garcia and the other Colorado legislators. If the Court grants cert, it would hear full arguments on whether legislative immunity applies here and decide the question itself; a grant would not by itself indicate who wins. If the Court denies cert, the Tenth Circuit's decision stands, leaving the case dismissed and the legislators' immunity from this lawsuit intact.
