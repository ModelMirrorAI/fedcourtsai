The Supreme Court is likely to decline this case, with about a one-in-five chance of taking it up. The dispute asks whether lawmakers can be sued to stop restrictions on public testimony, or whether protections for legislative work prevent that lawsuit. Four outside groups support review, and the free-speech concerns are significant. But the disagreement among lower courts is contested, and the latest scheduling change does not show that the Justices have already debated the case and asked to consider it again.
