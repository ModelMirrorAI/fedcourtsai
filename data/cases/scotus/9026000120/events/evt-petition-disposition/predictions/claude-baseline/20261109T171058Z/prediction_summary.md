We predict the Supreme Court will most likely **decline** to hear this case. We put the chance it takes the case at about one in eight. Activists say Colorado legislators cut off their public testimony for "misgendering" a bill's namesake. The lower court held that the legislators are shielded from suit by "legislative immunity," a protection for lawmakers' official work. An earlier Supreme Court ruling already applied that shield to court orders as well as money damages. The disagreement between lower courts is also shallow. Some Justices may still write separately criticizing a refusal.
