# Forecast — No. 26-120, Gays Against Groomers v. Garcia

- **Current state:** distributed twice. The first distribution (11/6 conference) was rescheduled before it was considered, and the petition is now set for the 11/13/2026 conference. It has not been relisted after an actual conference.
- **Further relists:** about a one-in-three chance of at least one more distribution after 11/13. The likeliest reason would be a Justice preparing a separate writing. Otherwise the petition is denied on the 11/16 order list.
- **CVSG:** none expected. The respondents are Colorado legislators and no federal interest is implicated.
- **Disposition:** denied. *Consumers Union* already extends legislative immunity to equitable relief, the split is shallow, and a mootness holding below is a vehicle problem.
- **If granted:** the Court would more likely take QP2 (whether enforcing a viewpoint-based public-comment rule is a legislative act) or a narrowed version of it than QP1's broad rule that official-capacity equitable claims escape legislative immunity.
- **Summary disposition:** unlikely. No intervening decision supports a GVR.
- **Dissent from denial:** roughly a 30% chance, conditional on denial, of a writing respecting or dissenting from the denial. Most likely authors are Justice Thomas or Justice Alito, possibly joined or written by Justice Gorsuch, on the viewpoint-discrimination point.
