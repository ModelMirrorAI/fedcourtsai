## What happened

Loop separately sued the State of Washington, its attorney general, JAMS, and the arbitrator in federal court, arguing the process violated his constitutional rights as a parent. Second, whether the Constitution limits a state's ability to let a private arbitrator decide an issue that substantially affects a parent's rights toward a child, without the procedural protections normally required when the government interferes with that relationship.
