John Loop wants to revive a federal lawsuit challenging a restraining order that a private arbitrator issued in his divorce. The lower courts dismissed it under the "Rooker-Feldman" rule, which bars federal courts from acting as appeals courts for state-court judgments. The appeals court's ruling was short and unpublished, Washington did not even file a response, and the case is a poor vehicle for deciding the broader legal questions.
