# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny certiorari without a separate writing. The
petition presents a case-specific challenge to an unpublished jurisdictional
disposition rather than a clearly demonstrated disagreement among circuits.
Denial will not resolve the truth of the alleged parental-rights violations
or approve the challenged procedures.

The baseline contains zero conference distributions and no CVSG. I expect one
initial distribution after the response deadline or a waiver, followed by denial
without a further conference relist. The structured distribution-increment
probability is 97% because the first distribution already exceeds zero. It is
not a forecast of repeated consideration. I expect disposition in November or
December 2026, with briefing extensions potentially moving it into early 2027;
this timing is a forecast rather than a known conference assignment.

I do not expect a call for the Solicitor General's views (0.2% probability of a
new CVSG). If one issues, it would most likely follow initial consideration,
not precede completion of the ordinary cert briefing sequence.

If review is granted, the Court will more likely focus on the first question:
whether the complaint asserts injuries independent of the state judgment or
seeks its review and rejection. I expect it would reserve the broad second
question about mandatory safeguards in parental-rights arbitration rather than
decide that question before establishing federal jurisdiction. This is a
conditional question-selection forecast, not a predicted merits holding.

Conditional on any grant, plenary review is more likely than a disposition in
the cert order itself (70% versus 30%). A summary route would more plausibly
involve vacatur and reconsideration of the jurisdictional distinction than a
summary merits ruling establishing nationwide family-arbitration safeguards.
I do not expect a GVR merely because T. M. was decided: its timing holding does
not itself establish the independent-injury premise advanced here.

Conditional on denial, I assign only 1% probability to any recorded dissent
from denial or statement respecting it. I predict no such writing and do not
assign individual Justices unobservable cert votes.
