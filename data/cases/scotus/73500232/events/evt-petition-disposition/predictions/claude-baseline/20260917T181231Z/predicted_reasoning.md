# Forecast: what the Court will do with No. 25-1331, Mazza v. Bank of New York Mellon

**Disposition.** The petition will be denied at or shortly after the September 28, 2026 conference for which it is distributed, in an order-list line with no separate writing. P(any grant) = 0.006.

**Relists.** The docket shows exactly one distribution (July 15, 2026, for the September 28 "long" conference). I forecast from that state: the petition is most likely disposed of at that first conference and not distributed again. A further distribution is unlikely (about 8%), and if one occurs it will be a single reschedule or a one-conference hold rather than a genuine relist run signalling interest.

**CVSG.** No call for the views of the Solicitor General. The dispute is between a private homeowner couple and a private mortgage trustee over an excusable-neglect ruling; no federal statute, agency, or federal-party interest is implicated. P(CVSG after prediction time) = 0.002.

**Which question, if any.** If the Court were to take anything, it would be a reformulated version of QP 1/2/3 — whether a court of appeals abuses its discretion by denying a Rule 4(a)(5) extension without weighing all four *Pioneer* factors — not the petitioners' four fact-bound questions as written. QP 4 (the Rule 58 separate-document argument) would be left behind: it is presented as an alternative timeliness theory and the petition does not show it was preserved or passed on below. Registered context only.

**Summary disposition vs. plenary review.** Conditional on a grant, I put roughly even odds (0.5) on a cert-order disposition (a GVR or per curiam) versus plenary review. There is no intervening decision to GVR in light of, which cuts against the GVR route, but a pro se, fact-bound abuse-of-discretion petition is not a plenary-review vehicle, so any grant would more plausibly be a corrective summary order than a set-down for argument.

**Dissent from denial.** None expected. No Justice will note a dissent or file a statement respecting denial; the case has none of the features (a recurring constitutional question, a Justice's known project, a sympathetic record with a clean legal issue) that draw one. P(any separate writing on denial) = 0.01.

**Timing.** Denial on the October 5, 2026 order list following the long conference, or on the first order list after any single reschedule.
