## What happened

David and Jennifer Foley, representing themselves, sued Orange County, Florida and various officials over a long-running dispute involving their toucan-breeding business and a permit fight tied to an aviary on their property. A federal district court dismissed their complaint as barred by an earlier state lawsuit, and the Eleventh Circuit affirmed. The Foleys then asked the district court to vacate that judgment, arguing it decided a claim different from what they had actually pleaded. The district court denied that request, and the Eleventh Circuit affirmed again, relying on its earlier ruling as already settling the matter. The Foleys also lost money to sanctions for pursuing that argument on appeal.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether a rule letting judges leave undisturbed a judgment that resolved something the plaintiff never actually raised in the lawsuit still applies when a court decided the matter on an unrelated ground, without ever checking whether the judgment matched what was pleaded. Put simply: can a court rely on its earlier decision to avoid asking whether that decision addressed the right claim in the first place, when a rule about voiding judgments requires making that check.
