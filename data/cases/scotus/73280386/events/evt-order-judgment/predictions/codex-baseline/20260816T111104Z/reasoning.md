# Rationale

I assign a 0.94 probability that the judgment below is disturbed and select `reversed` as the modal judgment. A merits reversal and a mootness vacatur both count as disturbed; affirmance, a DIG, or an equally divided affirmance make up the 0.06 complement.

The registered merits baseline is a raw, unweighted 69.7% disturbed rate: 359 disturbed judgments among 515 parsed judgments in grant Terms 2017–2024, the populated portion of the ten strictly prior Terms for this OT2025 grant. The pool clears the 30-case floor. Its coverage is 515 parsed judgments among 557 grants (92.5%), with 57 cert-order dispositions excluded by the pool guard. My increase above that baseline is qualitative and case-specific: the United States obtained review of a ruling invalidating the operation of a federal detention mandate, the Eighth Circuit squarely rejected duration-based bond hearings in *Banyee v. Garland*, and *Demore v. Kim* supplies a strong doctrinal route for the Court's six-Justice conservative majority.

The petition and brief in opposition define the central dispute. The government characterizes the requested dangerousness and flight-risk inquiry as a new substantive criterion that Section 1226(c) deliberately omits; respondents characterize physical freedom as a core constitutional liberty interest requiring procedural protection after unusually long detention. The government has the stronger forecast position once certiorari has been granted, particularly because G.M.'s removal proceedings remained active and neither the petition nor the opposition identified evidence that they were a sham. The grant was limited to Second Circuit No. 22-70, so the clear-and-convincing-evidence ruling made only in Black's companion case is unlikely to be reached.

Mootness is the main uncertainty and the reason the exact judgment call is less certain than the disturbed binary. The Court itself added whether G.M.'s case is now moot. He has been out of custody since 2022, never received a bond hearing under the judgment, and the government took inconsistent positions below about whether a live controversy remained. A mootness holding would ordinarily produce vacatur, which preserves the high disturbed probability even though it competes closely with reversal as the six-way judgment label.
