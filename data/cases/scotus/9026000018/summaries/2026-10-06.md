## In brief

Louisiana prison officials ask the Court to revive their appeals of expired injunctions over heat conditions for incarcerated workers, after lower courts called the appeals moot.

## What happened

Incarcerated men at the Louisiana State Penitentiary, along with an advocacy group called Voice of the Experienced, sued prison officials over heat conditions for men doing outdoor farm labor. A district court issued three separate preliminary orders requiring heat-safety measures. Each time, officials appealed, but a federal law governing prison litigation makes such preliminary orders automatically expire after 90 days unless the court takes specific steps. All three expired before the appeals court ruled, and the Fifth Circuit dismissed each appeal as moot, relying on its own prior ruling that 90 days can be long enough for meaningful review. After a trial, the district court ultimately entered final judgment for the officials.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether a 90-day window is too short to let a legal dispute be fully fought out on appeal before it expires, and whether the relevant measure of "enough time" is the time needed to reach this Court itself, rather than just a federal appeals court. These questions affect whether the appeals should be treated as moot or allowed to proceed despite the expiration of the underlying orders.

## Where it stands

The petition was distributed for conference, and the Court then asked for a response, which the opposing side filed in October 2026 after receiving an extension. The case has not yet been scheduled for a conference to decide whether to hear it.

## What each outcome would mean

Louisiana prison officials are asking the Court to step in. If the Court grants the petition, it would hear the case and decide whether the 90-day expiration period defeats appellate review; granting alone would not decide who wins. If the Court summarily reverses, as the officials request, the lower court's mootness rulings would be undone without full briefing. If the Court denies the petition, the Fifth Circuit's dismissals stand, leaving the appeals of the expired orders unreviewed and the final judgment for the officials in place.
