Louisiana asks the Supreme Court to review a federal appeals court's rule. Under that rule, court orders about prison conditions, which by law expire after 90 days, become "moot" (no longer reviewable) before an appeal can be heard. We think the Court will more likely than not decline the case, but a grant is a real possibility: about a 37% chance. The Court asked the other side to respond even after it waived its reply, and six appeals judges dissented below. Both are signs of serious interest.
