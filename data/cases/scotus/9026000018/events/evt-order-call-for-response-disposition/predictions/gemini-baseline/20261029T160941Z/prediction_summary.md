The Supreme Court will likely deny Louisiana's petition for review, with about a one-in-four (28%) chance of granting it or summarily overturning the lower court. Louisiana challenges a Fifth Circuit rule that dismissed three state appeals of 90-day prison heat-safety injunctions as moot—meaning no longer a live legal dispute—after the orders expired before the appeals finished. Although the Fifth Circuit's rule conflicts with Supreme Court precedent recognizing that three months is too short to complete appellate review, the trial court has since held a full trial and issued a final remedial order, making these earlier temporary-injunction appeals a poor vehicle for Supreme Court intervention.
