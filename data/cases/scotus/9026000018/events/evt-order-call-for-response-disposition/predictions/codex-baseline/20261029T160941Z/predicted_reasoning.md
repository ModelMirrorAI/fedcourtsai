# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition. The grant-family probability is 30%, including summary relief; the complementary 70% is principally a denial, with only a small allowance for another non-grant termination.

The baseline docket records one distribution, on July 29, 2026, for the September 28 conference, followed by the August 3 response request. From that state, expect one additional distribution after the response arrives or its deadline passes, with disposition at that conference or after one further conference. The 98% relist-increment probability means another distribution beyond the recorded one, not a 98% probability of substantive reconsideration after a conference. The response-requested moment masks this mechanical increment for scoring. Expect a decision within roughly one to four months of the response deadline, absent additional briefing or an extended hold; this is a forecast from the provisioned state, not a report of what happened after it.

No CVSG is on the baseline docket. Expect none to issue: P(a future invitation to the Solicitor General) is 4%. This is principally a dispute over established mootness doctrine and Louisiana's ability to obtain review, not a petition whose resolution obviously requires the federal executive's position.

If the Court grants relief, summary disposition in the cert order is more likely than full briefing and argument: 65% conditional on a grant. The leading grant route is a summary reversal of the mootness dismissals and a return of the appeals for application of the correct jurisdictional standard. This would not decide whether the prison injunctions themselves violated the Prison Litigation Reform Act. A GVR merely awaiting an unidentified intervening decision is not the central forecast.

If the Court takes the questions for plenary review instead, it will most likely address them together: whether the statutory 90-day duration permits full judicial review, and whether that review includes an opportunity for plenary Supreme Court review. It is less likely to announce that every 90-day order in every setting automatically satisfies the entire mootness exception. If it reaches the timing issue, the likely ground is that realistic access to complete review, rather than theoretical expedition in the court of appeals alone, governs. The separate requirement of reasonably expected recurrence remains important.

The main denial path is vehicle selection, not an endorsement of the Fifth Circuit's entire timing rationale. Louisiana's final merits victory, the expired and vacated preliminary orders, and the differing preservation records of the two appeals will make this an unattractive vehicle for a broad jurisdictional ruling despite the recurring problem described in the petition and the rehearing dissent.

Expect no separate writing on denial: P(a noted dissent or statement respecting denial | denial) is 16%. If one appears on Louisiana's side, Thomas or Alito is my leading speculative candidate, followed by Gorsuch; a writing defending denial or objecting to summary relief is also possible. The JSON gives unconditional per-Justice writing probabilities, including grant-order writings, rather than conditional denial probabilities or predicted votes. No individual writing is the modal forecast.
