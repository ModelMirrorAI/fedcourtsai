The Supreme Court will probably decline to hear Louisiana's appeal; I give some form of review or corrective relief about a three-in-ten chance. Louisiana argues that prison injunctions lasting only 90 days keep expiring before appeals can be completed, and the Court has asked the other side to respond. But Louisiana later won the underlying trial, making this an awkward case for resolving the broader legal question. If the Court intervenes, a short corrective order is more likely than full briefing and argument.
