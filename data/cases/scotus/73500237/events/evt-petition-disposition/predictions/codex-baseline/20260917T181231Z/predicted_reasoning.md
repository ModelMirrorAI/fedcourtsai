# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny the petition without a separate writing, most likely in the orders following its scheduled September 28, 2026 conference, in late September or early October. This is a timing forecast, not a verified future order date. The record shows **one distribution**, entered June 24, and **no CVSG**. I expect **zero additional distributions**; the probability of at least one further distribution is **9%**. If further consideration occurs, one or two additional distributions are more likely than a prolonged sequence.

The probability of any grant, including GVR or summary reversal, is **1.5%**. The Court will most likely regard the petition as contesting the application of accommodation principles to a particular employee's pleaded qualifications and reassignment, rather than presenting a clean conflict requiring its intervention. The lower court's separate conclusion that Redding had not pleaded qualification for the position she sought complicates review of her proposed process-only theory.

I forecast no CVSG: **0.1%** probability of a new invitation. The federal respondent already appears through the Solicitor General and has waived a response. If the Court wants a fuller adversarial presentation, a request for a response from that respondent is the natural route, distinct from a CVSG.

Conditional on a grant, plenary review is more likely than disposition in the cert order: **20%** probability of a summary route. The strongest candidate for review is a narrowed version of QP I, informed by QP II: whether an otherwise reasonable reassignment defeats an accommodation claim alleging material nondisclosure and premature termination of the interactive process. I do not expect the Court to take the search-consent analogy in QP III as a separate constitutional question. If review is granted, the process-versus-effective-accommodation issue would be the likely focus; this is not an unconditional forecast of a merits opinion.

A GVR based on A.J.T. is possible but not the leading grant route: that decision predates the appellate judgment, and its school-specific standard-of-liability holding does not directly answer this employment-reassignment question. Conditional on denial, I assign **1.5%** to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. I predict none and do not assign individual cert votes or an opinion author.
