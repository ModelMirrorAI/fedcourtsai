Distributed twice so far (the first prior to the Court requesting a response, the second for the September Long Conference). Expect one or two further relists before a decision, given the magnitude of the question presented and the number of amicus briefs. A CVSG is unlikely but possible if the Court strongly considers taking the case, as the federal government has a massive interest in the rules governing search warrants. If granted, the Court would likely take the sole question presented to reconsider *Jones v. United States*. A summary disposition is highly unlikely for a case asking to overrule a foundational 1960 precedent; plenary review would be required. Should the Court deny review, a dissent from denial by an originalist Justice (e.g., Justice Thomas or Justice Gorsuch) is a distinct possibility, given the petition's focus on the original meaning of the Warrant Clause.
