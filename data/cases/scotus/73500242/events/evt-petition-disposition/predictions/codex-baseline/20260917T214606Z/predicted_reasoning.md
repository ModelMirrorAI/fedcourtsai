# Forecast of the Court's disposition

The Court will deny the petition for certiorari. The most likely route is an unelaborated denial following the September 28, 2026 conference, without further distribution or separate writing. This predicts the procedural result, not judicial approval of the alleged discriminatory treatment or a merits holding construing Section 1981.

The snapshot records one distribution, entered July 15 for the September 28 conference, and no CVSG. I forecast zero additional distributions as the modal path. The probability of at least one further distribution is 12%; if the petition receives additional consideration, one or two additional distributions are more likely than a prolonged sequence. I forecast no invitation for the Solicitor General's views, with a 0.8% probability of a new CVSG before disposition.

If review is granted, the Court will most likely reformulate the sole question presented around whether racially discriminatory conditions imposed during restaurant service can impair the benefits or privileges of a contractual relationship under 42 U.S.C. Section 1981(b), notwithstanding the customer's remaining to complete her meal. It will not take the question's advocacy-laden factual formulation as a finding that all disputed facts have been established.

Conditional on any grant, plenary review is more likely than disposition in the cert order itself: 70% versus 30%. The principal summary route would be a per curiam reversal addressing the handling of disputed facts at summary judgment, as the petition requests. A GVR in light of an intervening decision is not the expected route; the materials consulted identify no such intervening decision. If plenary review occurs, the statutory protection for benefits and conditions of restaurant service, rather than the subsidiary state-law claims, will be the central issue.

Conditional on denial, the probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial is 4.5%. The modal forecast is no separate writing, and I do not predict an individual Justice's vote or authorship.
