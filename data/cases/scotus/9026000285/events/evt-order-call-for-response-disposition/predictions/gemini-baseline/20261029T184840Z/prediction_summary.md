The Supreme Court is unlikely to grant Christopher Ponzo's petition for review, with about a one-in-seven (14%) chance of taking the case. Ponzo challenges a $13.2 million criminal forfeiture order—a court order requiring him to hand over profits from a bribery scheme—arguing that the trial judge violated federal sentencing rules by entering the order after his sentencing hearing without giving him a chance to respond first. Although the Court asked the federal government to file a response and the appeal raises a procedural question the Justices left open in a 2024 ruling, Ponzo failed to object to the delayed schedule during his sentencing hearing, making this case a flawed vehicle for resolving the issue.
