The Supreme Court is very likely to decline Christopher Ponzo's appeal; I put the chance of review at about one in seven. He challenges a $13.2 million forfeiture, an order requiring him to surrender money tied to his crimes. The Court's request for the government's response signals interest, and his petition alleges conflicting rules about when forfeiture must be announced. But questions about whether he objected properly, and whether the procedural errors changed anything, make this a less attractive case for resolving that disagreement.
