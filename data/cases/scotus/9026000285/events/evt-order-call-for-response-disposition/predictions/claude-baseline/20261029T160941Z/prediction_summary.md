We predict the Supreme Court will most likely turn down Christopher Ponzo's appeal. We put
the chance it agrees to take the case, or sends it back to the lower court for another
look, at about one in ten. Ponzo argues a judge wrongly took $13.2 million of his
property without letting him be heard at sentencing. The Court asked the government to
respond, which is a modest sign of interest. But most cases that draw such a request are
still rejected, and the government will likely argue any mistakes did not change the
result.
