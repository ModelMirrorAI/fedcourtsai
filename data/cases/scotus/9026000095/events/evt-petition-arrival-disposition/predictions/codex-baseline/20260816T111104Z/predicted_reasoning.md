# Forecast

The petition will be denied. It is at zero distributions and no CVSG at this arrival moment. It will probably receive one initial conference distribution, which is the `relist-increment` from the frozen zero-distribution state, but it is unlikely to be held for an additional conference after that initial distribution.

The Court will not call for the views of the Solicitor General. The dispute concerns private parties, a state civil-enforcement action, and a state judge's alleged bias; it does not present a federal program or federal-party interest that would ordinarily warrant a CVSG.

The petition identifies no conflict among lower courts and asks the Court to convert its exceptional judicial-bias precedents into a general requirement that state courts give reasoned explanations when rejecting disqualification claims. The unexplained state-court denials, sparse account of the alleged bias, extraordinary-writ posture, and still-pending underlying action make this a poor vehicle.

If review were granted, the Court would most likely narrow the question to whether the particular alleged circumstances establish an objectively intolerable probability of bias under *Caperton*, rather than accept a categorical duty to issue reasons. Plenary review would be more likely than a cert-order disposition because there is no identified intervening authority for a GVR and the record described in the petition is too thin for a confident summary reversal.

On denial, no Justice is expected to issue a dissent or statement respecting denial.
