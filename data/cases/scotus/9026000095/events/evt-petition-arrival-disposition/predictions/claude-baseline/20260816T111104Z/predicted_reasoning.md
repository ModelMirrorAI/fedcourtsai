# Forecast: what the Court will do with petition 26-95

**Disposition.** The Court will deny the petition, without comment. Because the
respondents are the Superior Court itself, the assigned judge, and the City of
Diamond Bar, I expect a waiver of the right to respond rather than a brief in
opposition, and no call for a response — the petition will go to conference on
the waiver.

**Distribution and relists.** The docket shows **zero distributions** — this is
an arrival-moment forecast, made before any conference exists. The response
date is August 20, 2026, so I expect the petition to be distributed once for an
early-OT2026 conference (late September or October 2026) and denied on the
order list that follows it. It will almost certainly be distributed at least
once (the relist-increment claim, 0.96 — the residual is dismissal or
withdrawal before conference), but I do not expect a relist: nothing here
invites a second look.

**CVSG.** No. There is no federal-government interest of any kind in a state
judicial-disqualification procedure dispute between pro se taxpayers and a
California city; a CVSG is not a realistic possibility (0.002).

**Which question presented, if taken.** If the Court took anything it would be
a narrowed, procedural version of the QP — whether due process requires a
state court to give reasoned consideration to a facially substantial
Caperton-type disqualification claim — not the QP as written, which embeds the
contested premise that the alleged facts establish an intolerable risk of bias.
This is context-only: I do not expect any version to be taken.

**Summary disposition.** Conditional on the grant family firing at all, the
route would very likely be summary — most plausibly a GVR if an intervening
decision on judicial-bias procedure landed this Term — rather than plenary
review of a 9-page pro se petition (hence 0.7 conditional). Unconditionally,
no summary disposition is expected because no grant is expected, and I know of
no pending case whose decision would trigger a GVR here.

**Dissent from denial.** None expected (0.008). Recusal-procedure denials
occasionally draw a statement, but this petition's thin, fact-free presentation
gives no Justice material to write from.

**Timing.** Denial on an order list in October or early November 2026, roughly
two to three weeks after the response deadline passes or a waiver is filed.
