Distributed once so far; expect no further relists before a decision on the petition. The government filed this petition requesting a hold for United States v. Hemani, No. 24-1234, and a subsequent GVR. However, the Court decided Hemani in June 2026, affirming the Fifth Circuit's approach and rejecting the government's categorical dangerousness arguments. Consequently, the premise for a GVR no longer exists. Furthermore, the Court has recently denied several similar petitions by the government seeking review of Fifth Circuit decisions striking down 18 U.S.C. § 922(g)(1) as applied to nonviolent offenders (e.g., Mitchell, Doucet, Cockerham). The Court is therefore highly likely to deny this petition at the upcoming conference. If granted, the Court would take up the question of whether § 922(g)(1) is constitutional as applied to a felon with a single nonviolent drug possession conviction. A summary disposition is very unlikely given that the government's position was rejected in Hemani. Should the Court deny, expect no separate writing, consistent with the Court's recent denials in similar cases.
