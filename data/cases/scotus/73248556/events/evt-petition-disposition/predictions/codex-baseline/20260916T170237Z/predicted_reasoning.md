# Forecast

The Court will deny the petition, most likely in the orders following the September 28, 2026 conference, without a separate statement or noted dissent. The denial will leave the Fifth Circuit's judgment standing without announcing a Supreme Court merits holding on firearm possession by felons.

The baseline records one distribution, entered July 15 for September 28, and no CVSG. I expect zero additional distributions. The probability of at least one additional distribution is 0.22; if additional consideration occurs, one further distribution is more likely than a long sequence. I expect no CVSG (probability 0.001), because the Solicitor General already represents the petitioner and has supplied the government's position.

The likely reason for declining intervention is the mismatch between the government's contingent request for a Hemani-based remand and the opposition's account of that intervening decision: it does not supply the broad categorical-dangerousness rationale on which the petition's proposed reconsideration depends. The Court will not use this derivative petition to decide the entire constitutionality of Section 922(g)(1).

If the Court grants, it will most likely grant, vacate, and remand for reconsideration in light of Hemani rather than set the case for argument. My probability of disposition in the cert order, conditional on any grant, is 0.92. Such a remand need not dictate a different result below; it would ask the Fifth Circuit to reassess the relationship between its Connelly-based reasoning and the intervening Supreme Court decision. An outright summary reversal is less likely than a GVR.

In the less likely plenary-review branch, the question would remain whether Section 922(g)(1) violates the Second Amendment as applied to Hembree, focusing on permanent disarmament based on his sole predicate felony of simple methamphetamine possession. I do not expect the Court to add the enumerated-powers issue discussed in the lower-court concurrence or decide a facial challenge.

Conditional on denial, the probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial is 0.06. The modal forecast is no separate writing; no individual cert votes or authorship are forecast.
