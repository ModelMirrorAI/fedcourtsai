# Forecast — Casarez v. Irigoyen Farms, No. 25-1421

**Posture forecast from:** two distributions on the docket (conference of 9/28/2026, at
which the Court called for a response from Walmart, which had waived; and conference of
11/13/2026, after Walmart's response and the petitioner's reply). No CVSG.

**Disposition:** The Court grants, vacates and remands in light of *Montgomery v. Caribe
Transport II, LLC*, 608 U.S. 219 (2026). The California Court of Appeal expressly
declined to follow *Miller*, adopted the Eleventh Circuit's *Aspen* "direct connection"
reading of the safety exception, and required the claim to allege the tractor-trailer
itself was unsafe — the reading *Montgomery* rejected while resolving that split. The
call for a response from Walmart after the first conference is the Court's typical step
before a GVR when a respondent has waived, and Walmart's response affirmatively agrees a
GVR is warranted. Only Irigoyen opposes on QP1/QP3, and its distinction (shipper vs.
broker) is exactly the kind of first-instance question a GVR sends back.

**Relist:** Most likely decided at the 11/13 conference with no further distribution
(~80%); a single relist is the main alternative path.

**CVSG:** None — no federal interest that needs the SG's views, and the GVR route makes
one pointless.

**Summary-disposition route:** If the Court acts favorably it will almost certainly be a
GVR in the cert order itself, not plenary review. Summary reversal (QP3) is unlikely:
the facts (a shipper and a receiver, not a broker) differ from *Montgomery*, and
Walmart preserved independent state-law no-duty grounds.

**Which QP, if plenary:** Only conditionally — if the Court granted plenary review it
would take QP2 (whether non-enumerated shippers/receivers may invoke §14501(c)(1)),
which Irigoyen supports; but no split is shown and the question may be academic after
remand, so I expect no plenary grant.

**Separate writing:** None expected on a GVR order. Should the Court deny instead, a
short statement is possible but unlikely.
