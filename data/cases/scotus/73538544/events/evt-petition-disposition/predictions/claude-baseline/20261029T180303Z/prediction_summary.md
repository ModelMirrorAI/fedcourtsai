We expect the Supreme Court to send this case back to the California courts — very likely, about four chances in five. The lower court ruled that federal trucking law blocked a mother's wrongful-death suit over a fatal truck crash, but it relied on reasoning the Supreme Court rejected this year in a similar case, *Montgomery*. One of the companies being sued, Walmart, agrees the case should go back. The likely order is a "GVR": the Court grants review, vacates (erases) the ruling, and remands it for the state court to reconsider.
