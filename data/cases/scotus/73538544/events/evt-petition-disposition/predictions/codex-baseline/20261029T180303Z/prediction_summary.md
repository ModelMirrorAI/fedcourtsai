The Supreme Court is very likely to send this fatal truck-crash case back to California for another look. I put the chance of some grant of relief at 88%, mainly through an order setting aside the lower judgment without deciding who ultimately wins. A recent Supreme Court decision may undermine the lower court's reasoning about federal trucking law, and Walmart itself supports reconsideration. The Court is unlikely to hear full arguments or resolve the broader question about liability throughout freight supply chains.
