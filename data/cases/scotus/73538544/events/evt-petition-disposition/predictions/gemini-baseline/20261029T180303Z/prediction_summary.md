The Supreme Court is very likely (about a 92% chance) to grant the petition, vacate (cancel) the lower court's ruling, and send the case back to the California Court of Appeal for a fresh look—a routine procedure known as a "GVR" (grant, vacate, and remand). The California court threw out truck driver Christina Casarez's personal-injury lawsuit against Walmart and Irigoyen Farms by relying on lower federal court rulings that the U.S. Supreme Court unanimously overturned just four months later in *Montgomery v. Caribe Transport II, LLC* (2026), and Respondent Walmart itself has now filed a brief agreeing that the Supreme Court should send the case back under *Montgomery*.
