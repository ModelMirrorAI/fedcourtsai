We predict the Supreme Court will deny review in *Christina Casarez v. Irigoyen Farms, Inc., et al.*, though there is a substantial chance—about one in three (32%)—that the Court grants the petition to send the case back to the California appellate court for a fresh look ("grant, vacate, and remand"). The case arises from a fatal highway crash and asks whether a federal trucking deregulation statute blocks state-law negligence lawsuits against a produce shipper and a retail buyer (Walmart). Because the California court relied on a narrow reading of the statute's safety exception that the Supreme Court unanimously rejected a few months later in *Montgomery v. Caribe Transport II, LLC* (2026), and because the Court specifically asked Walmart to file a response after Walmart initially waived one, a remand order is a live possibility; however, denial remains more likely because *Montgomery* involved a freight broker that hired the trucking company rather than a shipper or retailer several steps removed from the driver.
