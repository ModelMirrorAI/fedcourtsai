We think it is slightly more likely than not (about 55%) that the Supreme Court will take some action for the petitioner here. Most likely, it will send the case back to the California court to reconsider ("grant, vacate and remand") in light of a 2026 Supreme Court ruling, *Montgomery v. Caribe Transport II*. That ruling rejected the reasoning the California court used to throw out a wrongful-death claim against a produce shipper. The Court asking a second defendant to respond also suggests it is taking the petition seriously.
