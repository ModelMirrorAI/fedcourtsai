# Forecast: Sethi v. U.S. Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit, No. 26-296

**Disposition: denied** at the first conference (November 6, 2026). I expect the order list that follows to show a plain denial.

- **Relists:** the docket shows one distribution, for the 11/6/2026 conference, and no relist. I don't expect a further distribution: the federal respondents waived their response, and nothing about this petition is likely to make a Justice ask for one or hold it over. A call for a response, which would trigger redistribution, is unlikely (about 5%).
- **CVSG:** none, and none expected. The United States is already a respondent through the Solicitor General, so a CVSG makes little sense here.
- **Question presented, if granted:** QP 1 as written, the *In re Ruffalo* notice question: whether a court may suspend an attorney based materially on misconduct (here, the defense and the later corrective filings) that was never charged. QP 2, which asks whether FRAP 47(b) allows a suspension for breaching a source-disclosure requirement first announced in the final order, would probably be left aside.
- **Summary route:** if the Court acts at all, a summary vacatur or per curiam reversal on *Ruffalo* grounds is a real share of the grant mass, since a narrow notice defect could be fixed without plenary review. Conditional on a grant I put it near 30%. Plenary review remains the likelier route.
- **Separate writing on denial:** not expected. The petition concedes the fabricated citations, and no Justice is likely to write to defend an attorney who filed them.
