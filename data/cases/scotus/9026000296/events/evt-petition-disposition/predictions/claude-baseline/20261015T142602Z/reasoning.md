# Rationale: P(grant) = 0.012

**Inputs read:** the snapshot `2026-10-15.json`, `context.json` (forward mode, band `baseline` under sal-v4, distribution_count 1, no CVSG, Term 2026), the QPs, the petition (40 pp.) and the start of the appendix (the Ninth Circuit's published order, 177 F.4th 1014). No brief in opposition exists, because the federal respondents waived.

**Anchor.** The statpack's sal-v4 "Segment base rate by salience band" table gives `baseline` bracketed `reached` rates. I pooled Terms 2017–2025, strictly before this case's 2026 Term: about 644 weighted grants over a reached n of about 12,877, so **≈5.0%**. A paid petition at its first distribution sits inside that population.

**Adjustments down, which are large:**
- **Waiver of response with no call for one.** Petitions decided on a waiver are denied at very high rates. A grant almost always comes after the Court calls for a response, and none has been called for.
- **No split claimed.** The petition argues a conflict with *In re Ruffalo* (Rule 10(c)) and "importance". It does not allege a circuit conflict over FRAP 46/47.
- **Fact-bound, unsympathetic vehicle.** The petition concedes that the brief contained fabricated authorities and misattributed quotations. The Ninth Circuit found repeated denials that AI was involved, and similar problems in other cases. Attorney discipline of this kind is a field where the Court rarely intervenes.
- **Posture.** A six-month suspension from one circuit's bar, with collateral BIA reciprocal proceedings pending.

**What keeps this above zero:** the *Ruffalo* argument (discipline based on uncharged conduct, including the attorney's defense itself) is a recognised due-process principle. A Justice could find a published order that leans on uncharged findings worth a call for a response. That is the main path to a relist or to a summary vacatur.

**Increments.** Relist about 5%, which is roughly the chance of a call for a response. CVSG about 0.3%, since the SG is already a party. Summary route about 0.3 conditional on a grant (as explained above). Dissent from denial about 1.5%.

**Uncertainty and discounts:** I read the appendix only in part, and I did not independently check the petition's account of what the show-cause order charged. I used no MCP or corpus retrieval; this rests on the provisioned record plus the statpack.
