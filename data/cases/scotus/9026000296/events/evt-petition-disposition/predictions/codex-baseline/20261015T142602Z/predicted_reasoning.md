# Forecast

The Court will deny the petition for certiorari, most likely without a separate writing after its first scheduled conference on November 6, 2026. The record contains **one distribution**, entered October 14, and no CVSG. I expect **zero further distributions**; the probability of at least one additional distribution is 12%. If the petition receives further consideration, one additional distribution is more likely than a lengthy sequence.

The Court will not call for the Solicitor General's views (0.1% probability of a new CVSG). The Solicitor General already represents the federal respondents and has waived a response. A request that those respondents answer the petition would be different from a CVSG.

The petition will not attract four votes because the alleged notice violations are entangled with the particular disciplinary record: the original show-cause order already identified fabricated authorities, questionable corrections, and candor, and offered an opportunity to respond and request a hearing. The later findings create a substantial procedural objection, but do not present a clean, developed conflict on the questions actually presented. Denial will not establish that the disciplinary order was correct on the merits.

**If review is granted**, the likelier question is a narrowed version of QP 1: whether an appellate court must give supplemental notice before materially basing suspension on allegedly dishonest disciplinary responses or later corrective filings. The Court would be less likely to take QP 2 as a freestanding rule governing every disclosure of AI-assisted research. Any favorable ruling for Sethi would concern notice and an opportunity to defend, not immunity for inaccurate filings.

Conditional on any grant, I assign **45%** to disposition in the cert order itself and **55%** to plenary briefing and argument. Thus plenary review is marginally likelier on the grant branch. A summary reversal enforcing the existing notice principle is a plausible alternative; a GVR based on an intervening decision is not the leading route because no such decision is identified in the record.

Conditional on denial, I assign **3.5%** to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. The modal forecast is a routine unexplained denial, with no Justice singled out for a separate writing.
