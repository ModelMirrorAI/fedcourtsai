# Predicted outcome — Blanche v. Ozurumba, No. 25-1343 (judgment on the merits)

**Judgment: reversed** (the Fourth Circuit's judgment is disturbed, with a remand
to the Board of Immigration Appeals for further proceedings). I put
P(disturbed) at 0.79. If the judgment is disturbed, `vacated` is the
likeliest alternative label to `reversed`: the Court may vacate and remand
for the agency to apply the correct standard. A DIG is unlikely. The
respondent's removal to Nigeria in April 2025 does not moot a petition for
review, and the case reaches the Court cleanly on one statutory question.

**Vote: 6–3.** Roberts, Thomas, Alito, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh and Barrett in the
majority; Sotomayor, Kagan and Jackson in dissent. Kagan joining the
majority (7–2) is the likeliest deviation, since the question is textual. A
Gorsuch defection is less likely: this is a civil admissibility bar, so the
lenity and vagueness concerns that have drawn him to the noncitizen's side
before carry less weight.

**Question reached.** The Court reaches the question the government is pressing:
whether "material support ... to a terrorist organization" under
8 U.S.C. § 1182(a)(3)(B)(iv)(VI)(dd) requires that the support be usable or
readily divertible to the group's terrorist *activities*, which was the panel
majority's test, adopted over Judge Richardson's dissent. It leaves the
implied-duress-exception question alone. The Fourth Circuit expressly did not
decide that question, and it carries a Loper Bright / statutory-stare-decisis
wrinkle the Court will not want to take up on this record.

**Majority ground.** Text and structure. Subclause (dd), support "to a
terrorist organization", is set against subclause (aa), support "for the
commission of a terrorist activity". Reading (dd) to require a nexus to
terrorist activities would collapse it into (aa). The majority also relies
on Holder v. Humanitarian Law Project's reasoning that support given to an
organization's members or operations frees resources and sustains the
organization as a whole. Months of thrice-daily cooking for the group's
leadership is support to the organization. "Material" filters out trivial or
incidental contact; it does not impose an activity nexus.

**Breadth.** A categorical statutory rule: no activity-nexus requirement. It is
not a fact-bound reversal on substantial-evidence review. The Court does not
adopt the Board's "even de minimis" gloss from Matter of A-C-M- wholesale. It
reserves both the duress question and the outer boundary of "material", and
it notes that the statute's discretionary exemption authority is the
mechanism Congress provided for coerced or sympathetic cases.

**Dissent.** Likely written by Sotomayor or Jackson. It would emphasize the word
"material", the coerced, bait-and-switch facts, and the soup-kitchen
hypothetical.

**Timing.** Argued in the winter or early spring of 2027; decided by the end of
June 2027.
