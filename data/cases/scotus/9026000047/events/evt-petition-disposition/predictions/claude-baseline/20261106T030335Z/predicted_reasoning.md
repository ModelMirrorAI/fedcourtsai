# Forecast — No. 26-47, Amazon Logistics, Inc. v. Clinton

- **State now:** one distribution (for 9/28/2026) and no relist. The Court called for a response on Aug. 18, and it is due Nov. 16, 2026.
- **Relisting:** the petition will be redistributed once the response is in, most likely for a December 2026 or January 2027 conference. If the Court is weighing a GVR or a grant, expect one or two relists after that before it disposes of the petition.
- **CVSG:** none. This is a private arbitration dispute with no federal interest that would draw one.
- **Disposition:** denial is the single most likely outcome, about 70%. A **GVR in light of *Flowers Foods v. Brock*** is the main alternative, about 21%. The Court of Appeal ruled while Brock was pending and on a near-categorical "goods originated out of state" rule. Plenary review is about 9%.
- **If plenary review is granted:** the Court would take the QP as written, which asks whether a local driver is exempt solely because goods previously crossed state lines, regardless of a break in transit or the goods' local character. The likely ground would build on Brock's continuous-journey framing. The Court would likely hold that the original interstate origin of goods alone is not enough and that a break in transit and locally stocked goods are relevant factors.
- **Summary route:** if the Court acts favorably for petitioner at all, a GVR is likelier than plenary review, at roughly 70/30.
- **Separate writing:** a denial most likely comes with no noted dissent or statement. If anyone writes, Thomas or Alito is the likeliest.
