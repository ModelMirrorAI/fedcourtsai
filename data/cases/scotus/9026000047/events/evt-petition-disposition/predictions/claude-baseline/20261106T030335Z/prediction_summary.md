We forecast that the Supreme Court will most likely turn down Amazon's appeal, but the chance it does something is about three in ten. Amazon argues that a California court wrongly ruled its local delivery drivers exempt from arbitration. The California court ruled before the Justices decided a closely related case, *Flowers Foods v. Brock*, earlier this year. Because the Court asked the drivers to respond, its likeliest favorable move is to send the case back for the California court to reconsider in light of that new decision, rather than hear full argument.
