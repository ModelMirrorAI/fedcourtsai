The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Amazon's petition for review (a 94% chance of denial, and a 6% chance of a grant or remand). Amazon asks the Court to overturn a California appellate decision holding that local Amazon Flex delivery drivers are interstate transportation workers exempt from mandatory arbitration under the Federal Arbitration Act. Although the Court requested a response from the drivers, the Court just unanimously ruled in May 2026 in *Flowers Foods v. Brock* that intrastate delivery drivers can qualify for this exemption, and every federal appeals court to consider Amazon Flex package drivers has agreed they are exempt.
