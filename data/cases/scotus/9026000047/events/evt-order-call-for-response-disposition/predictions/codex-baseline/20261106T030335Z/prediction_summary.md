The Supreme Court is more likely than not to decline Amazon's appeal, but I give it about a 38% chance of granting some relief. Amazon argues that local delivery drivers should not be exempt from mandatory arbitration simply because their goods once crossed state lines. The Court's request for a response shows interest. Still, its recent ruling does not clearly undermine the California decision. If it intervenes, it will probably send the case back for reconsideration rather than hear full arguments.
