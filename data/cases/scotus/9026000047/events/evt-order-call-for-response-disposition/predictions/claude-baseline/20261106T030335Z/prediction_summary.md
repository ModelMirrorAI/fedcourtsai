Amazon asks the Supreme Court to review a California ruling that its Amazon Flex delivery drivers are exempt from the federal law that enforces arbitration agreements. We think the Court will most likely turn the case away. There is roughly a one-in-four chance of some form of grant, most likely an order sending the case back to the California court to reconsider in light of the Supreme Court's 2026 *Flowers Foods v. Brock* decision. The Court's request that the drivers respond to the petition shows real interest. But *Brock* largely sided with the drivers' position, so the lower court's ruling may already fit it.
