The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Amazon's request to hear this case, with only about a one-in-seventeen (6%) chance of granting review. Amazon asks the Court to decide whether California local delivery drivers in its Amazon Flex program are exempt from the Federal Arbitration Act—a federal law that generally requires workers to resolve disputes through private arbitration rather than lawsuits—because the packages and groceries they deliver sit in California warehouses or stores before customers order them. Although the Court asked the drivers to file a response to Amazon's petition, the Court unanimously ruled in *Flowers Foods v. Brock* in May 2026 that local delivery drivers can qualify for this exemption without crossing state lines, and it has repeatedly declined to review similar Ninth Circuit rulings, making this unpublished state-court decision an unlikely candidate for review.
