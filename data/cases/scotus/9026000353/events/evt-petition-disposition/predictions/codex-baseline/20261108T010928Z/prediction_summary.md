The Supreme Court is very likely to decline Richard Vasquez's appeal; I estimate about a one-in-forty chance it takes the case. His death sentence and allegations that prosecutors concealed evidence raise serious concerns. But other appeals courts largely agree on the disputed legal rule, and his earlier attempts to obtain review create procedural obstacles. The most likely result is a denial without explanation after the scheduled December conference, leaving the lower court's decision in place without endorsing its reasoning or deciding whether prosecutors violated his rights.
