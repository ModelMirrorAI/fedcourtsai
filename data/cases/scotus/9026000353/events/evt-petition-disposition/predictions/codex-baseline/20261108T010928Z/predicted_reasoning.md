# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny the petition, most likely in an ordinary order following its scheduled December 4, 2026 conference. The snapshot records one distribution and no completed relist. The modal forecast is **zero further distributions**. A further distribution is a 20% possibility; if that occurs, one additional distribution is more likely than a prolonged series.

The Court will not call for the views of the Solicitor General. The forecast probability of a new CVSG is 0.3%.

Denial will most likely carry no explanatory writing and will not decide whether the evidence was suppressed or the conviction is constitutionally sound. The broad successive-petition question will remain unresolved by this disposition. The expected obstacles to review are the absence of a conflict between circuit holdings and the complicated procedural route through an earlier authorization denial and the present unexplained certificate-of-appealability denial. These are predicted reasons for declining to take this vehicle, not a forecast that the Court will publish them as holdings.

If the Court grants, it will most likely address the petition's single statutory question: whether a later habeas application presenting previously unknown, State-concealed Brady and Napue/Giglio claims is second or successive under section 2244(b). A narrower formulation could address that question through the COA standard and the district court's authorization-based dismissal, without deciding the underlying constitutional claims. Plenary review is the likelier grant route: 75% conditional on grant, versus 25% for disposition in the cert order. No particular intervening precedent is forecast to trigger a GVR.

Conditional on denial, the probability of a dissent or statement respecting denial is 12%. No separate writing is expected; if one is issued, Sotomayor is the leading potential author, given the issue-specific writings cited in the petition. A writing would more likely call attention to barriers to review of concealed-evidence claims than establish any merits holding. No certiorari vote count is forecast.
