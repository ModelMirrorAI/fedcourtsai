We predict the Supreme Court will most likely turn down Richard Vasquez's request to hear his
death-penalty case (about an 8% chance it agrees). He argues that evidence-suppression claims he
could not have known about earlier should not count as a barred "second" habeas petition. The
Court has shown some interest: it pulled the lower-court record in his related earlier petition.
Still, every federal appeals court that has ruled agrees with the Fifth Circuit, and Texas
points to procedural obstacles, so a denial is likely, possibly with a written dissent.
