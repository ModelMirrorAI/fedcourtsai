# Forecast — No. 26-353

The petition has been distributed once (for the 12/4/2026 conference) and never relisted. Its
companion, No. 25-1246, is set for the same conference after a record request and a reschedule.
I expect the Court to take up the two together and deny both, either at the 12/4 conference or
after one more relist (about a one-in-three chance of at least one further distribution). I
expect no CVSG, since no federal party or federal interest is involved.

If the Court grants, it would take the question as written: whether Brady and Napue/Giglio
claims that ripened only after the first federal application are "second or successive" under
§ 2244(b), with Panetti's ripeness logic as the frame. It would probably reformulate the
question to separate it from the COA posture. Plenary review is likelier than a summary
disposition. No intervening decision supports a GVR.

On denial, a separate writing is plausible, about one in three on this docket. The likeliest
author is Justice Sotomayor, building on Storey and Bernard. Justice Jackson might join or write
too. The writing may attach to the companion 25-1246's order instead.
