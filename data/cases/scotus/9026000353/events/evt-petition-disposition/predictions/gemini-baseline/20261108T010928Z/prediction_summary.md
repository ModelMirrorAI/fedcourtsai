The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in this Texas death-penalty case, with only a 1.5% chance of granting the petition. Richard Vasquez asks the Court to allow a new federal challenge claiming prosecutors withheld evidence and used misleading testimony about toxicology tests at his 1999 murder trial, arguing that federal limits on repeat petitions ("second or successive" habeas applications) should not apply when the state hid the evidence earlier. However, the federal appeals court already rejected his request to bring these exact claims in 2023, and both state and federal courts found that the toxicology report was actually given to his trial lawyers and that the other evidence of fatal physical abuse was overwhelming, making this case a poor candidate for Supreme Court review.
