# Forecast

The Court will deny certiorari, most likely in the orders following the September 28, 2026 conference or shortly thereafter. The snapshot records **one distribution**, entered June 17 for that future conference; it records no completed conference or relist. I expect **zero further distributions** before disposition. The probability of at least one additional distribution is **8%**; if additional consideration occurs, one further distribution is more likely than a prolonged sequence.

The Court will not call for the Solicitor General's views: **0.1%** probability of a new CVSG. The United States is already the respondent and has waived its response. A request that it respond as a party would be different from a CVSG and is not what this probability forecasts.

The probability of **any grant is 1.5%**. If granted, the Court would most likely narrow the petition's broad collateral-consequences question to whether plea counsel must advise about the preclusive effect of a health-care-fraud plea on related False Claims Act liability. Resolving that question in these petitioners' favor would also require confronting the separate retroactivity ground supporting the judgment. The Court is unlikely to use this collateral-review vehicle to establish a general duty covering all severe collateral consequences.

Conditional on a grant, **plenary review is more likely than disposition in the cert order**: the latter has **10%** probability. No intervening controlling decision warranting a GVR is identified in the material consulted, and the alternative holdings make a summary reversal an especially unlikely route.

Conditional on denial, I assign **2%** probability to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. I expect a bare denial without a separate writing and make no individual-Justice vote or authorship forecast. The denial would leave the judgment below intact, not announce the Court's endorsement of its constitutional reasoning.
