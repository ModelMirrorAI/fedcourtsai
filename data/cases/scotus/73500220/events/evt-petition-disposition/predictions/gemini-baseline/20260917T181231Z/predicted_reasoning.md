# Predicted Outcomes

- **Relists**: The docket currently shows a single distribution for the September 28, 2026 conference. The petition will almost certainly not be relisted further. 
- **CVSG**: The United States is the respondent and has waived its right to respond. A CVSG is inapplicable here, and the Court will not call for the views of any outside party.
- **Summary Disposition Route**: If granted, a GVR is the most plausible route since the US waived its response. However, a grant is highly unlikely.
- **Dissent from Denial**: I do not expect any dissent or statement respecting the denial of certiorari. The waiver and routine nature of the collateral consequence issue suggest unanimous denial.
- **Merits/QP**: If the Court were to grant, it would likely take up the sole question presented regarding the Sixth Amendment right to counsel for severe collateral consequences of a guilty plea.
