# Reasoning

This is a paid petition for certiorari regarding whether the Sixth Amendment protects the right to assistance of counsel regarding a collateral consequence of a guilty plea. 

The docket indicates that on June 11, 2026, the United States filed a waiver of its right to respond. A waiver by the Solicitor General is an extremely strong indicator that the petition does not warrant the Court's review and has a very low probability of being granted. Without a response, the Court will virtually never grant certiorari; if a Justice finds the petition potentially worthy of review, the Court will request a response. 

Given the waiver and the routine nature of the collateral consequence question presented, the likelihood of a response being requested (let alone a grant) is very small. I predict the petition will be denied.

The probability of a grant is placed at 0.1%, reflecting the baseline rarity of grants in the absence of a response. The chance of a relist to request a response is estimated at 3%, and a CVSG is estimated at 0.5% (since the US is already a party, a CVSG is inapplicable anyway, though the Court might seek views of others, this is negligible). If denied, a dissent from denial is unlikely given the waiver (1%).
