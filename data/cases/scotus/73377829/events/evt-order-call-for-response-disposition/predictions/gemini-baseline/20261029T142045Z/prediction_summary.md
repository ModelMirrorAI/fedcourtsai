The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in this case, with only a 4% chance (about one in twenty-five) of granting the petition. Although the Court asked the federal government to file a response to the former federal public defender employee's challenge to the judiciary's internal workplace harassment process, the lower courts rejected her claims based on detailed factual findings after a six-day trial. Crucially, there is no disagreement among federal appeals courts on this issue, the 2013 workplace plan she challenges has since been replaced, and the appeals court ruled that she waited too long—five years into the lawsuit—to raise her main argument about being excluded from Title VII (the primary federal workplace anti-discrimination law).
