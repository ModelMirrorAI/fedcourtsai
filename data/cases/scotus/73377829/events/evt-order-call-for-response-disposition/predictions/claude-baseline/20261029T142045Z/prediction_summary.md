We expect the Supreme Court to turn down this case, putting the chance it agrees to hear it at about one in twenty. Caryn Strickland, a former federal public defender, argues that the courts' internal complaint system for their own employees' harassment and discrimination claims is unconstitutionally unfair. The Court did ask the government to respond, which shows some interest. But no appeals courts disagree on the question, and the case turns on facts settled at trial, so the Justices will probably decline it.
