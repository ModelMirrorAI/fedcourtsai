The Supreme Court is very likely to decline review, with about an eight-in-a-hundred chance of taking the case or otherwise granting relief. Strickland challenges how the federal judiciary handles employees' discrimination and sexual-harassment complaints. The Court's request for a response shows some interest, and the workplace-accountability issues matter beyond her case. But the petition does not demonstrate a clear disagreement among appeals courts, and parts of its broader challenge were not properly raised below. I expect another conference listing followed by denial, probably without an explanation.
