## In brief

Nicole Pileggi asks the Court to send her online-privacy lawsuit against a news publisher back for another look once it decides a similar case already pending before it.

## What happened

Nicole Pileggi sued Washington Newspaper Publishing Company, which operates the Washington Examiner, claiming it violated a federal video privacy law by sharing her website video-watching history with Facebook without her consent. She argued that subscribing to the Examiner's free email newsletter made her a protected "consumer" under the law. The district court dismissed her claim, finding no connection between her newsletter subscription and the videos she watched on the website. The D.C. Circuit affirmed, joining the Sixth Circuit in holding that a person must subscribe to video content itself, not just any good or service, to qualify as a consumer under the statute.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked to decide what kind of subscription qualifies someone for the privacy law's protection: whether signing up for any product or service from a company that also provides videos is enough, or whether the person must specifically subscribe to video content itself. The Court has already agreed to decide this same question in another case.

## Where it stands

The petition was distributed for the Court's conference on May 14, 2026, after full briefing, including a response from Washington Newspaper. The docket shows no action since that conference.

## What each outcome would mean

Pileggi is asking the Court to act. Washington Newspaper, which won below, agrees the petition should be held until the Court decides the related case, and says the petition should then be denied if that case favors the defendant, or granted and sent back to the D.C. Circuit if it favors the plaintiff. If the Court denies review, the D.C. Circuit's ruling against Pileggi stands. If the Court grants, vacates, and remands, it would set that ruling aside without deciding who is right, sending the case back for further proceedings.
