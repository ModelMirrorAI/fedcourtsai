The Supreme Court is more likely to turn down this appeal than hear it: I give the families about a three-in-ten chance of obtaining review. They challenge Massachusetts rules requiring children in private schools to travel elsewhere for publicly funded special-education services. Several supporting briefs and the Court's request for a response make the case stand out. But the lower court treated the rule as a permissible choice about delivering services, and the claimed disagreement with other courts is not an exact match.
