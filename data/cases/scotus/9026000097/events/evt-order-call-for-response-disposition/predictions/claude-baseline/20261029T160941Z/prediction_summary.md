Massachusetts parents of children with disabilities ask the Supreme Court to hear their challenge to a state rule that cuts off special-education services when parents choose private school. We predict the Court will most likely turn the case down, putting the chance it agrees to hear it at about one in five. That is well above the usual odds: the Court asked the State to respond after it had declined to, and fourteen states and several groups filed supporting briefs. But the legal theory is novel and the case may look like an imperfect test of it.
