# Predicted disposition and procedural path

The Court will most likely deny the petition without explanation after receiving the requested response and completing a later conference distribution. The record shows only one distribution so far, for a conference that preceded the extended response deadline. I expect at least one more distribution, most likely the redistribution needed to put the completed briefing before the Court, not necessarily a substantive relist after actual consideration. My modal path is that redistribution followed by denial; if the Court does genuinely relist, one or two additional conferences are more likely than a prolonged series.

I do not expect a call for the Solicitor General's views. If an invitation occurs, it will likely follow the opposition and renewed conference consideration, rather than precede the currently pending response.

# If the Court grants review

The Court would most likely take the petition's single question substantially as presented: whether the unconstitutional conditions doctrine applies to the Pierce right to send one's child to private school. The concrete dispute would remain the distinction between an impermissible condition on a generally available benefit and a permissible limit on the location and administration of publicly funded special-education services. I do not predict that the Court would transform this into an unpleaded Free Exercise case or a general right to tuition subsidies.

Conditional on any grant, I assign 0.86 to plenary review and 0.14 to a summary cert-order disposition. A grant for briefing and argument is the expected grant route, not an immediate summary reversal or GVR. If granted and ultimately decided for petitioners, the more plausible ground would be that parental educational choice is not categorically excluded from unconstitutional-conditions scrutiny, leaving the application to the particular state benefit for further analysis. That is a conditional merits hypothesis, not a prediction that an opinion will accompany denial.

# Writings on denial

I expect no separate writing on the modal denial path. Conditional on denial, the probability of a noted dissent or statement is 0.18. If one occurs, Thomas or Alito is the leading author possibility, with Gorsuch a secondary possibility, arguing that educational-choice rights warrant review of the asserted conflict. I do not forecast an observable cert-vote lineup.
