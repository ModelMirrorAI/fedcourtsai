The remaining 25% of grant probability is for a route opening plenary proceedings in this docket. This is a conditional route forecast, not a 75% overall chance of relief.

Conditional on denial, I expect no separate writing: **4%** probability that any Justice notes a dissent or issues a statement respecting denial. I do not forecast an individual Justice's cert vote.

Absent a lead-case hold, I expect disposition during the next few months after completion of the requested briefing. A hold followed by a GVR could extend through the end of the 2026 Term or later. Timing is a contextual forecast, not a scored claim, and the supplied record does not establish what occurred at the already-past September 28 conference.
