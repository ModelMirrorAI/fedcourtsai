# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition without a separate writing. The forecast assigns 2.5% to any grant, including a GVR or summary reversal, rather than only a grant for argument.

The provisioned docket shows exactly one distribution: the October 14, 2026 entry distributing the petition for the November 6, 2026 conference. No relist and no CVSG are shown. I predict zero additional distributions and disposition on an orders list shortly after that conference, most likely during November 2026. The probability of at least one additional distribution is 12%; conditional on a continuation, one additional distribution is more likely than a long sequence. A request for a response following the respondent's waiver is a possible alternative path, not something already present in the record.

I predict no call for the Solicitor General's views: the probability of a new CVSG is 0.5%. The dispute concerns a private employer's application of Title VII to an individual accommodation request, not a demonstrated conflict over a federal program requiring the government's participation.

If the Court grants review, its most plausible question is a narrowed version of question 1: what evidence of health-and-safety burdens establishes substantial undue hardship under Title VII after Groff when a healthcare employee seeks a religious exemption from vaccination? The Court is less likely to take the separate Rule 56(d) scheduling dispute, and the questions directed separately at the district court largely duplicate the appellate questions. This is a conditional forecast of the question, not a prediction that review will occur.

Conditional on any grant, I assign 25% to disposition in the cert order itself and 75% to a proceeding for plenary review. No intervening controlling decision is identified in the materials used. Groff predates the lower decisions and was expressly considered, so a routine GVR simply to apply a newly announced Groff standard is not the leading route. Summary correction nevertheless remains possible if the Court concludes the lower court diluted that standard.

Conditional on denial, I assign 4% to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. I expect neither and do not forecast an individual Justice's undisclosed cert vote. A denial would leave the judgment intact without supplying a merits opinion endorsing the lower court's analysis.
