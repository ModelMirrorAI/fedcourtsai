## In brief

A former nursing-facility employee asks the Court to revive her lawsuit claiming her employer wrongly denied a religious exemption from its COVID-19 vaccine mandate.

## What happened

Cara Elizabeth Liberto Dodson, an occupational therapist, worked for The Lutheran Village at Millers Grant, a skilled nursing facility. The facility granted her a religious exemption from its vaccine mandate in early 2021 but denied a renewed exemption request later that year, citing changed circumstances including state and federal guidance. Dodson was terminated and sued under Title VII, the federal law barring religious discrimination in employment. The district court granted the employer summary judgment, finding the accommodation would have posed an undue hardship, and separately denied Dodson's request to submit a late-disclosed medical expert's affidavit opposing that finding. The Fourth Circuit affirmed both rulings without a published opinion.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether the lower courts were wrong to rule, without a full trial, that accommodating Dodson's religious objection would have placed too heavy a burden on the employer's operation of its business. The Court is also asked whether the lower courts should have let Dodson introduce a late-disclosed medical expert's opinion to challenge the employer's claim that unvaccinated staff posed a risk, before deciding the case on the paperwork alone.

## Where it stands

The petition was filed and the respondent, Lutheran Village, waived its right to file a response. The docket shows no further action since that waiver was filed.

## What each outcome would mean

Dodson, as petitioner, is asking the Court to take up her case. If the Court grants review, it would hear full arguments and decide the legal questions about undue hardship and the expert-evidence ruling; a grant alone would not decide who wins. If the Court denies review, the Fourth Circuit's decision affirming summary judgment for Lutheran Village stands, and Dodson's lawsuit remains over.
