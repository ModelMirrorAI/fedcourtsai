## 3. Question Presented and Merits Scope (Conditional on a Grant)

If the Court were to grant certiorari on this petition (or consolidate its question with Nos. 26-199 and 26-201), it would take the single Question Presented as written—whether HISA's rulemaking structure unconstitutionally delegates legislative power to a private entity—and consolidate it with the enforcement nondelegation question presented in Nos. 26-199 and 26-201. If reached on the merits, a majority of the Court would likely affirm the Fifth, Sixth, and Eighth Circuits' unanimous conclusion that Congress's December 2022 amendment to 15 U.S.C. § 3053(e)—giving the FTC plenary rulemaking authority to "abrogate, add to, and modify" the Authority's rules—renders the Authority subordinate to the FTC under *Sunshine Anthracite Coal Co. v. Adkins*, 310 U.S. 381 (1940), and *FCC v. Consumers' Research*, 606 U.S. 656 (2025).
