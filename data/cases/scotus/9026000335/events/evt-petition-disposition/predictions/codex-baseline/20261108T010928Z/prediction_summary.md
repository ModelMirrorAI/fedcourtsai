The Supreme Court is very likely to refuse Texas's request to review who may write national horseracing rules. I estimate an 18% chance of any grant of review, including sending the case back for another look. Courts have agreed that federal oversight makes this rulemaking arrangement lawful, while a separate dispute concerns who may enforce the rules. The Court may delay Texas's petition while considering that related dispute, but I expect an eventual denial, probably without any Justice publishing a separate explanation.
