Texas asks the Supreme Court to rule that the federal horseracing law unconstitutionally lets a private corporation write binding rules for the industry. The Court will very likely hear the government's companion appeal about the law's enforcement provisions, which the lower court struck down. Whether it also takes Texas's question is close to a coin flip. I put the chance of any grant at a bit under one in two (42%), so I narrowly predict denial: the federal government says no appeals courts disagree on this question, and the Court could choose a parallel Oklahoma case instead.
