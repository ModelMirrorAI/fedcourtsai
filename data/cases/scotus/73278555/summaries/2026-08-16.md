## In brief

Retirement plan participants ask the Court to decide what employees must allege to plausibly claim their retirement fund managers imprudently invested retirement savings in underperforming investments.

## What happened

Winston Anderson and other former Intel employees sued the committees and individuals who managed two Intel retirement plans, claiming they violated a federal pension law by investing heavily in hedge funds and private equity starting in 2009, producing lower returns and higher fees than more traditional, equity-heavy funds during a long bull market. The fiduciaries countered that this was a deliberate, disclosed risk-reduction strategy adopted after the 2008 crash. The district court dismissed the claims, and the Ninth Circuit affirmed, holding that claims based on an investment's underperformance must identify a comparably similar "benchmark" investment that did better; the plaintiffs' comparisons, the court found, were not similar enough.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether a retirement plan participant who claims a plan manager mismanaged investments by choosing funds that performed poorly must point to a sufficiently similar alternative investment that performed better, or whether other kinds of allegations, considered together, can be enough to let the claim proceed.

## Where it stands

The Court granted review, and the case was argued on October 6, 2026; it currently awaits decision.

## What each outcome would mean

Anderson and the other plan participants, as petitioners, asked the Court to take up the case after losing in the lower courts. If the Court sides with Anderson and the other participants, it would reject the requirement that a similar comparison investment must be identified, and send the case back to the lower courts to reconsider the claims under a broader test. If the Court sides with the Intel fiduciaries, the requirement would stand, and the participants' claims would likely remain dismissed. The Court could also vacate the Ninth Circuit's decision and send the case back without deciding the legal question for either side.
