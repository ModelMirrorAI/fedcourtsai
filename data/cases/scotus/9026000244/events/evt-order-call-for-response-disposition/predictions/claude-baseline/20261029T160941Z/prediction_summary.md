We expect the Supreme Court to turn down Kroger's request to hear this case, though the chance it agrees is real: about one in six. Kroger argues that a class action (one lawsuit brought on behalf of many customers) shouldn't include shoppers who already got their 10-cent bottle deposits back and so suffered no harm. The Court tried to decide this question in 2025 but dismissed that case on a procedural problem, and it asked the customers to respond here. Still, most cases that draw such a request are ultimately denied, and the Court recently passed on a similar case.
