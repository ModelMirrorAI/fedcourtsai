The Supreme Court will likely deny review in *The Kroger Co. v. Solano*, with a one-in-four (25%) chance of granting the petition. Kroger asks the Court to decide whether a federal court may certify a class-action lawsuit seeking money damages when some members of the group suffered no actual financial harm—here, customers who already received refunds for a 10-cent orange juice bottle deposit. Although the Justices agreed to hear this exact question in 2025 before dismissing that earlier case on a procedural technicality, disputes in this case over whether customers were still harmed at the moment of purchase under Oregon law make it an imperfect test case.
