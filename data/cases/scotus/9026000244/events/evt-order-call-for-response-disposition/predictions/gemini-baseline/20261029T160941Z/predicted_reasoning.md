### Question Presented and Conditional Merits Scope
If the Court grants certiorari, it will take the single Question Presented as framed in the petition—whether a federal court may certify a class action pursuant to Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 23(b)(3) when some members of the proposed class lack any Article III injury—which matches verbatim the question granted in *Laboratory Corp.* If granted, the Court is most likely to resolve the case on Rule 23(b)(3) predominance grounds rather than announcing a standalone Article III bar at the certification stage, holding (consistent with Justice Kavanaugh's dissent in *Laboratory Corp.*) that a district court cannot certify a Rule 23(b)(3) damages class when a non-*de minimis* share of the class is uninjured and the court lacks an administrable, common mechanism to winnow out uninjured members without individualized mini-trials.

### Summary Disposition vs. Plenary Review
Conditional on a grant, plenary review with full merits briefing and oral argument is overwhelmingly the likelier route (`summary-disposition-route` = 0.08). The circuit split over uninjured class members under Article III and Rule 23(b)(3) is deep, three-way, and openly acknowledged by the courts of appeals, making a summary reversal without oral argument unlikely. A grant, vacate, and remand (GVR) would occur only if the Court grants another pending OT2026 vehicle presenting the same *Laboratory Corp.* question and holds this petition for disposition in light of that decision.
