The Supreme Court is slightly more likely to decline this case than hear it: I give review a 43% chance, a little better than two in five. The lawsuit concerns improper bottle deposits, but the larger issue is whether a group damages lawsuit may include people who suffered no legal injury. The Court has shown interest in that question before and requested a response here. Still, uncertainty about whether refunded customers were actually uninjured could make this a poor case for deciding the broader rule.
