## Questions Presented and Conditional Merits Scope

If the Court were to grant certiorari, it would confine review to **Question Presented 2**—whether an agency's repeated post-disclosure refusals to reinstate a discontinued hazardous duty pay differential or to modify an updated position description constitute independent "failures to take" a personnel action under 5 U.S.C. §§ 2302(a)(2)(A)(ix) and (b)(8), or instead are non-actionable continuing effects of a pre-disclosure discrete act under *Bosley v. Merit Systems Protection Board*, 162 F.3d 665 (Fed. Cir. 1998), and *National Railroad Passenger Corp. v. Morgan*, 536 U.S. 101 (2002). The Court would decline Question Presented 1 (which turns on fact-specific assertions regarding a duplicated electronic signature on an OF-8 form and 18 U.S.C. § 1001(a)(3)) and Question Presented 3 (which turns on fact-bound disputes over CBRN and explosives duties in the Pentagon Force Protection Agency position descriptions).

If granted and decided on the merits, the Court would likely affirm the Federal Circuit on the ground that under the Whistleblower Protection Enhancement Act, a disclosure that postdates a completed discrete decision to terminate a pay supplement cannot be a "contributing factor" in that decision, and routine subsequent paychecks implementing the prior decision are continuing consequences rather than fresh exercises of personnel authority.
