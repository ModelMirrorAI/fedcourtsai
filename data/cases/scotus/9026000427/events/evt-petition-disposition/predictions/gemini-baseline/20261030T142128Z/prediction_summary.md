The Supreme Court is virtually certain to deny review in *Coleman v. Merit Systems Protection Board*, with a 0.3% chance of a grant (about one in three hundred). Petitioner Richard Coleman, representing himself without a lawyer, asks the Court to revive his whistleblower appeal challenging the Pentagon Force Protection Agency's 2015 decision to stop paying him a 25% hazardous-duty pay supplement. The lower tribunal and the federal appeals court dismissed his case in an unpublished decision because the agency stopped the extra pay before he made his whistleblower complaints, meaning the complaints could not have caused the pay cut, and the government has already waived its right to file a response.
