The Supreme Court is very likely to decline Richard Coleman's request to hear his federal employment dispute; I put the chance of review at about one in 200. He argues that continued denial of hazardous-duty pay after he reported wrongdoing should count as a new act of retaliation. But the case turns heavily on his particular employment history and does not show a clear disagreement between appeals courts. I expect a brief denial after the November conference, without a separate statement from any Justice.
