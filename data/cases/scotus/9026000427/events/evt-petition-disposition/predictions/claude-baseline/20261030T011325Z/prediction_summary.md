The Supreme Court will very likely turn down this case — we put the chance it
agrees to hear it at well under one in a hundred. Richard Coleman, a federal
employee representing himself, says the Merit Systems Protection Board wrongly
refused to hear his whistleblower complaint about an inaccurate job description and
lost hazardous-duty pay. The government declined even to file a response, the lower
court's ruling was unpublished, and the questions turn on the facts of one dispute
rather than a broad legal conflict the Justices usually look for.
