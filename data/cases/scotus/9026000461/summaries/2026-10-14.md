## In brief

Journalist Catherine Herridge asks the Court to review an order requiring her to name a confidential source in Yanping Chen's privacy lawsuit against the government.

## What happened

Yanping Chen sued federal agencies, claiming officials unlawfully disclosed her private records to Fox News, where Catherine Herridge reported on Chen's alleged ties to the Chinese military. Seeking to prove her claim, Chen sought to depose Herridge about her confidential sources. Herridge refused, citing a reporter's privilege under the First Amendment. The district court ordered her to answer and held her in contempt when she refused. The D.C. Circuit affirmed, applying its own two-factor test asking whether the source's identity was central to Chen's case and whether Chen had exhausted other ways to find it. Herridge's request for rehearing was denied.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether, and under what circumstances, a person suing in civil court can force a journalist to reveal a confidential source, and whether courts should weigh broader free-press interests against the need for the information rather than relying only on the narrower test the D.C. Circuit used.
