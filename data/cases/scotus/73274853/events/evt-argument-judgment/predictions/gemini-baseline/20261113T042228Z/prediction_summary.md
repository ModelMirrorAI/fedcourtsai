The Supreme Court is very likely—about a nine-in-ten chance—to affirm the Eighth Circuit's ruling for WBI Energy Transmission, with less than a one-in-ten chance of overturning it, most likely by a unanimous 9–0 vote (or 8–1). When Congress lets an interstate natural-gas pipeline use federal eminent domain—the government's power to take private land for public use—the Court will hold that federal constitutional "just compensation" rules (fair market value, without attorney fees) apply nationwide rather than state fee-shifting laws.
