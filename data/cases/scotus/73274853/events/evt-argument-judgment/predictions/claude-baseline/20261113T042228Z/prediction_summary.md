The Supreme Court will very likely affirm, meaning it leaves the lower court's ruling in place: there is only about a one-in-eight chance it overturns it. The case asks whether landowners whose property is taken by a private pipeline company under a federal law are paid under state rules, which can add attorney's fees, or under the federal constitutional standard. At argument, at least six Justices appeared to favor the federal standard. The expected vote is about 8-1, with Justice Gorsuch the likeliest dissenter.
