# Forecast

The Court will most likely deny the injunction pending appeal. The probability of an unqualified grant is **30%**. A mixed order granting some relief and denying some is on the ungranted side of this forecast, consistent with the interim resolver; this is not a forecast of whether the applicants eventually prevail on the merits.

The arrival record contains only the October 2, 2026 submission to Justice Kagan. It shows no response request, no referral to the full Court, and zero amicus entries. From that state, I forecast:

- A response will be requested: **85%**.
- The application will be referred to the full Court: **80%**.
- At least one additional qualifying amicus entry or distinct submitted amicus brief will appear: **70%**. One to three additional entries is my central forecast, counting submissions rather than only accepted briefs.

I expect a response schedule followed by disposition during October or early November 2026, before the mid-November wrestling season identified in the application. I do not expect an immediate merits decision or certiorari before judgment. Timing and the alternative certiorari route are context, not additional declared claims.

The likely denial will leave the preliminary-injunction appeal in the Ninth Circuit rather than resolve the underlying Title IX or parental-rights questions. The impediment will be the showing needed for affirmative emergency relief on a disputed factual record, including the fit between the requested accommodation and the asserted rights. The Court need not endorse the Ninth Circuit's mandatory-injunction standard to decline an injunction of its own.

If relief is granted instead, I expect an applicant-specific protection concerning K.M.K.'s participation or ability to decline a match, rather than a statewide adjudication of sports eligibility. The strongest route to intervention is the applicants' contention that the lower courts failed to give effect to the sports and parental-rights precedents described in their application. That conditional route does not imply that the Court will accept the application's characterizations or resolve its factual allegations as established facts.
