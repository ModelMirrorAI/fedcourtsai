The application for an injunction pending appeal will likely be denied. The applicants seek a mandatory injunction to prevent a transgender female athlete from competing in girls' wrestling or to mandate an opt-out procedure. Mandatory injunctions require an applicant's right to relief to be indisputably clear, a standard the Court is unlikely to find met here given the ongoing litigation in the lower courts.

Expect the Circuit Justice (Kagan) to request a response from the respondents, given the high-profile nature of the Title IX and transgender sports participation issues. After responses are filed, Justice Kagan will almost certainly refer the application to the full Court. Expect the full Court to deny the application without noted dissent, though a brief statement respecting the denial or a dissent from conservative Justices emphasizing the need to resolve the Title IX question is possible. A few amicus briefs may arrive given the intense public interest, but the application will ultimately be resolved as a denial, leaving the Ninth Circuit to handle the preliminary injunction appeal on the merits.
