# Forecast

The Court will deny the petition. P(any grant, including a GVR or summary reversal) is 1.2%.

The baseline shows exactly one distribution, on June 24 for the September 28, 2026 conference, and no CVSG. I forecast zero further distributions and a denial in an orders release following that conference, most likely in early October. The probability of at least one further distribution is 6%; if the petition receives additional consideration, one additional distribution is the most likely increment. The elapsed summer interval is not itself a relist.

I forecast no call for the Solicitor General's views, with a 0.2% probability of a new CVSG. If one occurs, it would most plausibly follow further consideration this fall rather than precede the already scheduled conference.

If review is granted, I expect the Court to take the petition's single question in a narrower form: whether the prior state adjudication counts as a decision on the merits for purposes of Oklahoma's savings statute when the takings claimant characterizes it as exhaustion and cessation-based dismissal. I do not forecast a grant simply to determine the amount of compensation or to decide whether all temporary municipal restrictions are takings.

Conditional on a grant, plenary consideration is more likely than a disposition in the cert order: P(summary route | grant) = 25%, versus 75% for briefing and argument. No identified intervening decision supplies a specific GVR rationale. If a summary disposition occurs, a narrow correction and remand concerning the treatment of the earlier adjudication is more plausible than an immediate award of compensation. Any merits analysis is conditional on review being granted.

Conditional on denial, I forecast no noted dissent or statement respecting denial; the probability of any such writing is 2.5%. I do not forecast a particular Justice as a writer or assign individual cert votes. Denial will leave the lower judgment intact without resolving the petitioner's substantive compensation theory.
