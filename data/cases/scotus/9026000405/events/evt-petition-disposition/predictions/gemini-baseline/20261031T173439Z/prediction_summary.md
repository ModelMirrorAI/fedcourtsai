The Supreme Court is very likely to deny this petition for review, with less than a one-percent chance (0.8%) of granting it. Former State Department employee Gengshu He asks the Court to decide whether federal trial courts must let the Department of Labor determine first if the Federal Employees' Compensation Act (a workers' compensation law for federal employees) covers his tort lawsuit against the government over a confrontation with agency security officers at his home. Although He argues that federal appeals courts disagree on how much deference courts owe the Department of Labor on that coverage question, the federal government waived its right to respond, the lower court resolved He's claim in a brief unpublished order after He declined to submit a claim to the Department of Labor, and his family members' related claims are still moving forward in the trial court.
