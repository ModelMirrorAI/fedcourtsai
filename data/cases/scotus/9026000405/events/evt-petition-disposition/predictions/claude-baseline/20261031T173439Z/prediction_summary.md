We expect the Supreme Court to turn this case down. We put the chance it agrees to hear it at about 1 in 80. A former State Department employee argues that courts, not the Labor Department, should decide whether the federal workers' compensation law blocks his tort lawsuit. Appeals courts disagree on this, but the disagreement is decades old. The government did not even bother to file a response. His own claim was rejected in a short unpublished order, which makes the case a weak candidate for review.
