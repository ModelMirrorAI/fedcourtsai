# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny certiorari, most likely in an orders list following the September 28, 2026 conference, in late September or early October. It will not issue a merits opinion. This timing is a forecast, not a scored claim or a separately verified orders-list schedule.

The September 16 snapshot shows **one distribution**, entered July 8 for that September conference, and **no CVSG**. I forecast **zero additional distributions** and no call for the Solicitor General's views. The probabilities of at least one additional distribution and of a new CVSG are 2% and 0.1%, respectively. If another distribution occurs, one additional distribution is my modal conditional forecast; a prolonged sequence is less likely. The federal parties' response waiver is not a CVSG.

The probability of any grant, including a GVR or summary reversal, is **0.1%**. Conditional on that unlikely grant, plenary review is more likely than disposition in the cert order: the summary-route probability is **25%**, not 25% of all outcomes. The provisioned petition identifies no intervening controlling decision that supplies a concrete GVR route. If review is granted, the most plausible question is a narrowed version of Question 2 concerning the sufficiency of the civil-rights conspiracy allegations at dismissal, possibly with Question 4's jurisdictional issue. I do not forecast review of all eight questions or unconditional acceptance of their factual premises.

Conditional on denial, I assign **0.2%** to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. The forecast is a routine denial without a separate writing. I make no per-Justice vote or authorship prediction.
