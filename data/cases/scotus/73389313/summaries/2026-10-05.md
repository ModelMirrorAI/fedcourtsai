## In brief

The Court declined to hear Joan Farr's bid to revive her lawsuit alleging a decades-long conspiracy by private individuals and federal agencies to violate her civil rights.

## What happened

Joan Farr, representing herself, sued a group of private parties—including Alexandra Grant, grantLOVE LLC, attorney Scott Sims, his firm Ziffren Brittenham, and WME IMG—along with several federal government defendants, alleging a long-running conspiracy to deny her constitutional rights, including her right to an attorney, to a jury trial, and to hold public office. The United States District Court for the Western District of Missouri dismissed the case entirely. The United States Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit affirmed that dismissal and later denied Farr's request for rehearing by the full court.

## What the Court is being asked

She asked the Court to send the case back for trial.

## Where it stands

## What each outcome would mean

By denying the petition, the Court left the Eighth Circuit's decision in place, meaning the dismissal of Farr's lawsuit against Grant, grantLOVE, Sims, Ziffren Brittenham, WME IMG, and the federal government defendants stands.
