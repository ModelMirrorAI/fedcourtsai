Distributed once so far; expect one or two further relists before a decision on the petition, given the strong circuit split on the *Central Hudson* standard and the high amicus interest. No CVSG is expected, as this involves a state statute and state interests rather than federal agency action. If granted, the Court would likely take both questions presented to clarify whether the government must provide empirical evidence to satisfy intermediate scrutiny for commercial speech, rather than relying on legislative deference. A summary GVR is unlikely as there is no recent intervening SCOTUS decision on this specific commercial speech issue. Should it deny, there is a reasonable chance of a dissent from denial (e.g., by Justice Thomas or Alito) emphasizing the need to protect commercial speech from state legislative overreach.
