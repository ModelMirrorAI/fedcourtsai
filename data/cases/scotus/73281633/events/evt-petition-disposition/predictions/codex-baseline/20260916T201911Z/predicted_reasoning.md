# Forecast

The Court will deny certiorari in Council for Responsible Nutrition v. James, No. 25-1145. The likely disposition is an unexplained order rather than an opinion resolving the commercial-speech questions. My probability of any grant, including a GVR or summary reversal, is 9%.

The provisioned docket shows **one distribution**, entered July 1 for the September 28, 2026 conference, and **no CVSG**. I expect no additional distribution and a denial in late September or early October 2026. The probability of at least one additional distribution is 28%; if that happens, one or two additional distributions are more likely than a prolonged series. The summer interval before the scheduled conference is not itself a relist. I assign 2% to a new CVSG: the questions principally concern constitutional scrutiny of a state restriction, rather than a request to resolve federal regulatory administration. If a CVSG does issue, it would most plausibly follow consideration this autumn and extend the petition's life substantially.

The Court will most likely leave these questions for a case with a final judgment and a sharper conflict over the governing legal standard. This preliminary-injunction record presents disputes about the sufficiency of studies and the fit of a marketing-based sales restriction; the judgment below also rests on a public-interest assessment that complicates obtaining relief through the questions presented.

If review is granted, I expect plenary briefing and argument, not a disposition in the certiorari order: the conditional probability of the summary route is 12%. No particular intervening decision justifying a GVR is identified in the material consulted. The likeliest grant would take the first question in substance, focused on what evidence connects a marketing-defined restriction to material reduction of the asserted harms, with the second question about less speech-restrictive alternatives potentially taken alongside it. A wholesale replacement of Central Hudson is less likely than clarification of its third and fourth prongs. Any merits discussion here is conditional on a grant, not a forecast that a denial will announce a constitutional holding.

Conditional on denial, I assign 10% to an aggregate dissent from denial or statement respecting denial. The modal forecast is no separate writing; I do not forecast individual certiorari votes or authorship.
