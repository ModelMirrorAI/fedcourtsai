We predict the Supreme Court will **deny** James Pavatt's request to halt his November 12, 2026 execution in Oklahoma, with only about a 2% (one-in-fifty) chance it grants the stay. His argument asks the Court to stretch a 2017 ruling — which let courts hear jurors' testimony about racial bias in deliberations — to cover a juror who says prosecutors' false claims swayed the death vote. The Court rarely blocks executions this late and has kept that ruling narrow, so a stay is unlikely.
