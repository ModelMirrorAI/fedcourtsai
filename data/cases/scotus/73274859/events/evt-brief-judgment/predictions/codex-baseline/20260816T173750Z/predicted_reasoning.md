# Forecast

The Court will hold that it has statutory and Article III jurisdiction, reverse the Colorado Supreme Court, and rule 6-3 that federal law precludes the claims as pleaded. Chief Justice Roberts and Justices Thomas, Alito, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh, and Barrett will form the majority; Justices Sotomayor, Kagan, and Jackson will dissent.

The majority will treat the Colorado Rule 21 proceeding as a final, self-contained proceeding under *Atlantic Richfield* and the denial of petitioners' federal defense as a sufficient appellate injury. On the merits, it will avoid relying solely on free-floating constitutional preemption. It will instead read the Clean Air Act's allocation of interstate-pollution authority to EPA and source States, reinforced by equal-sovereignty and foreign-affairs principles, to bar a downwind State from imposing tort damages for injuries that necessarily run through aggregate interstate and international greenhouse-gas emissions. Displacement of the former federal common law will not be treated as transferring regulatory authority over those emissions to every affected State.

The Court's rule will cover the nuisance, trespass, unjust-enrichment, and conspiracy claims as pleaded because their causal chain depends on worldwide production and sales, third-party emissions, global climate change, and local injury. It will not announce general immunity for fossil-fuel companies. The opinion will leave open claims genuinely confined to in-state conduct, source-state law, or locally deceptive marketing whose injury does not depend on using Colorado law to assign responsibility for global emissions.

The dissent will conclude that the interlocutory ruling is not reviewable and, alternatively, that *AEP*, *Ouellette*, and *Hencely* require ordinary statutory preemption rather than preemption by constitutional structure. It will emphasize that the Clean Air Act regulates emissions rather than upstream marketing and sales and that choice of law, causation, and the remaining defenses were not decided below.
