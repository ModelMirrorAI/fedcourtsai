The Supreme Court is very likely to take action on the government's appeal over the White House East Wing project: I estimate a 94% chance of review or another grant of relief. The most likely result is a summary reversal, meaning the Court overturns the lower court without a full argument cycle. Five Justices have already temporarily blocked the construction injunction and explained their objections. The final order will probably rely on those same objections, with a separate opinion opposing the result.
