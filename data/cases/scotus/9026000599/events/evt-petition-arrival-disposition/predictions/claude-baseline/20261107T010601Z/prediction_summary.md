The government asks the Supreme Court to throw out a court order that halted construction of the new White House East Wing. We think the Court will very likely agree to take up the case (about 88%). In August the Court already paused that order by a 5–4 vote, saying the government will probably win because the challenger, who objects to how the building will look, likely has no legal right to sue. The Court may either hear full arguments or simply reverse the lower court without them.
