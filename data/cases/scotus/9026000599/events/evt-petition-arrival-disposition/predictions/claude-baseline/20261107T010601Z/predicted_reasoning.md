# Forecast

- **Disposition:** the Court grants. It either sets the case for argument or disposes of it summarily in light of its Aug 31, 2026 per curiam staying the injunction (26A203, 5–4). Denial is unlikely (about 12%). If it happens, the likeliest cause is a changed posture below or mootness, not a contested vote.
- **Distributions:** none so far, since this is the arrival moment. Expect a first distribution after the BIO (due Dec 7, 2026, possibly extended), likely for a January 2027 conference. Expect zero to two relists if the Court is preparing a summary disposition with a dissent.
- **CVSG:** none. The SG is the petitioner.
- **Question taken:** the single QP as written. In practice that means Article III standing for an organization asserting a member's aesthetic offense, with the equities as a secondary ground. The Organic Act and 3 U.S.C. 105(d) authorization questions would likely go unreached if standing resolves the case.
- **Route:** roughly even between plenary review and a summary disposition (summary reversal "for the reasons in" the stay opinion, or a GVR in light of it).
- **Separate writing:** if the Court disposes summarily, expect a dissent from the Chief Justice, Sotomayor, Kagan and Jackson, likely authored by Roberts or Jackson. On a plain grant of plenary review, expect no writing. On a denial, a dissent from Alito, Thomas or Gorsuch is somewhat more likely than not.
- **If granted on the merits:** reversal on standing. Mere aesthetic offense is not a concrete, particularized injury for this plaintiff.
