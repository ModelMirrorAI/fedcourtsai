The Supreme Court is almost certain to deny this petition (about a 99.8% chance of denial or dismissal, and only a 0.2% chance of review). The petitioners, representing themselves without a lawyer in a South Carolina landlord-tenant dispute, asked the state supreme court for an extraordinary order (a writ of mandamus) to force the trial court to rule on their motions and allow them to file documents electronically in color rather than on paper. Because the state supreme court declined that request on routine state procedural grounds—noting a trial hearing was already scheduled—and the trial court has since dismissed the entire lawsuit for litigation misconduct, the U.S. Supreme Court has no reason or clear legal basis to step in.
