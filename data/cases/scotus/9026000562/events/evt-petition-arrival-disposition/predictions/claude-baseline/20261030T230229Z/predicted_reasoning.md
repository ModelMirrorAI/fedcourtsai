# Forecast — McNeil v. Court of Common Pleas (S.C.), No. 26-562

**Denied.** This petition was docketed 2026-10-28 and has no distributions yet. The respondent is a state trial court sued only as the nominal mandamus respondent. It will most likely waive its response or let the deadline (Nov. 27, 2026) pass. The petition will then go to a conference, probably in December 2026 or January 2027, and be denied at its first conference with no relist. Expect exactly one distribution, so the increment from zero is near-certain. Any further relist is very unlikely (<3%).

**No CVSG.** No federal interest calls for the Solicitor General's views. The ADA Title II regulations are cited only as background, and the respondent is a state court.

**Question, if taken.** The single QP asks whether due process and equal protection allow a court to reserve electronic filing for licensed attorneys and send self-represented litigants to paper intake. The Court would take it only as written, and it will not take it. The judgment below is a South Carolina Supreme Court order declining original-jurisdiction mandamus, because the trial court had set a hearing. That order is not a final judgment on the federal question under 28 U.S.C. § 1257, and it does not pass on the constitutional claim.

**Summary disposition.** None is expected. No intervening decision supports a GVR.

**Separate writing.** None is expected on the denial.
