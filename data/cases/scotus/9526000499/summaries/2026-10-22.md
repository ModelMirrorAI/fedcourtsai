## In brief

Louisiana and Cameron Parish ask the Court to lift a Fifth Circuit order pausing their coastal-damage lawsuit while Devon Energy appeals a remand to state court.

## What happened

Cameron Parish sued Devon Energy and other companies in state court, claiming decades of oil and gas operations damaged Louisiana's coastline. Devon moved the case to federal court, arguing it had acted under federal oversight. The federal district court disagreed and ordered the case back to state court, then had the order sent to the state court the same day. Devon immediately appealed that remand order to the Fifth Circuit and asked for a pause (a "stay") of the remand while the appeal proceeded. The district court refused, but the Fifth Circuit granted the stay, ruling that Devon's appeal automatically froze the case's return to state court. Louisiana and the Parish then asked the Supreme Court to step in.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether an appeal of an order sending a case back to state court automatically pauses that transfer and blocks state-court proceedings from resuming, even after the transfer paperwork has already been sent to the state court. Louisiana also asks the Court, as a fallback, to treat this request as a full petition for review and decide the underlying legal question now rather than later.

## Where it stands

Justice Sotomayor asked Devon to respond to Louisiana's application, and Devon's opposition has been filed. The application is awaiting a ruling; the docket shows no decision yet.

## What each outcome would mean

Louisiana and Cameron Parish, as applicants, are asking the Court to lift the pause. If the Court grants that request, the case would return to state court and litigation could proceed there while Devon's appeal continues. If the Court denies the request, the Fifth Circuit's pause stays in effect, keeping the case out of state court until Devon's appeal is resolved. Either ruling would be temporary and would not decide the underlying legal question; if the Court also grants Louisiana's fallback request to treat this as a full appeal, the case would proceed to further briefing and a decision on the merits later.
