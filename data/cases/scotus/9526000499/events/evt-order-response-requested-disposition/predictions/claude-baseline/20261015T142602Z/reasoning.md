**Adjustments.**
- *Up:* A response request is a real act of attention. It roughly separates applications the Court
  takes seriously from ones it denies outright. The applicant is a State, represented by its
  Solicitor General's office. The split is genuine and the Fifth Circuit acknowledged it. And the
  Fifth Circuit's rule effectively halts state-court proceedings, which raises the Court's
  federalism and comity concerns.
- *Down:* Vacating a circuit's stay is rare and needs a strong showing. The same question sits in
  *City of Brunswick* at the Oct 16 conference, so the Court has a cert vehicle and little reason to
  act on the interim docket. A merits grant there would most naturally leave the Fifth Circuit's
  stay in place pending decision. The majority of circuits (Fourth, Fifth, Eleventh) are on the
  respondent's side, and *Coinbase* was 5–4 for the automatic-stay principle. The harm to the
  applicants is delay of state litigation, which the Court usually does not treat as irreparable.
  The applicants also concede the Fifth Circuit left the discretionary Nken grounds unaddressed.
- Net: modestly above the anchor, at **0.14**.
