The application to vacate the Fifth Circuit's stay will likely be referred to the full Court by Justice Sotomayor, given the clear circuit split regarding the application of the *Griggs* automatic stay rule to post-transmittal remand orders in federal-officer removal cases. A response has already been requested on the docket. Expect 1-2 amicus briefs to be submitted due to the significant state-sovereignty and energy-sector interests involved. 

Ultimately, the Court is likelier to deny the application to vacate the stay (an extraordinary remedy), perhaps opting instead to grant certiorari in the companion case *City of Brunswick* to resolve the circuit split on the merits without granting extraordinary interim relief here, or alternatively treating this application as a petition for certiorari but denying the interim vacatur. The disposition is expected within a week or two of the response being filed.
