Expect the Court to call for a response from the respondent and for Justice Sotomayor to refer the application to the full Court, given the clear 3-1 circuit split and the sensitive federalism issues implicated by a federal court enjoining state-court proceedings. Amicus briefs are also likely to be filed by other states and industry groups given the underlying climate litigation context.

Ultimately, the Court is likely to deny the application to vacate the stay in the short term, but may simultaneously grant the alternative request to treat the application as a petition for certiorari before judgment, or simply note that the denial is without prejudice to a prompt petition for certiorari. While the applicant (State of Louisiana) presents a strong argument that the Fifth Circuit improperly extended *Coinbase* to enjoin already-revested state court proceedings, the Court may prefer to resolve the circuit split on the merits docket rather than via an emergency vacatur. If the Court does grant the vacatur, it would likely be a summary order emphasizing the limits of *Coinbase* and the strictures of the Anti-Injunction Act.
