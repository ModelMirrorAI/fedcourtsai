The Supreme Court is extremely unlikely to hear this appeal — less than a one-in-a-hundred chance of review — and will deny the petition. A New York Manhattan townhouse owner asks the Justices to revive a constitutional takings lawsuit (a claim that rent regulations effectively seize private property without compensation) without first applying for a rent-hardship increase, but the Court just rejected a cleaner companion appeal ten days ago, key arguments were never raised in the New York courts, and the state appeals court separately ruled that the lawsuit failed on the merits anyway.
