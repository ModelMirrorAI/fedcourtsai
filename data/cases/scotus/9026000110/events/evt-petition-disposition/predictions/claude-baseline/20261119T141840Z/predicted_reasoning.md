# Forecast — No. 26-110, Tedford's Tenancy, LLC v. City of New York

The docket shows **one distribution** so far (conference of 28 September 2026), after which the Court requested a response; both briefs in opposition were filed 10 November 2026.

- **Further distribution:** The petition will be distributed once more, after the reply period, for a conference in December 2026 or early January 2027. It will be decided at that conference; no true relist beyond it.
- **CVSG:** None. No federal interest is implicated in a state-court as-applied challenge to New York's rent stabilization law.
- **Disposition:** Certiorari denied, most likely on the order list following that conference (mid-December 2026 to mid-January 2027). The companion-like petition from the same counsel, No. 26-107 (Walls), was denied on 9 November 2026 after the same call for a response.
- **Question presented:** If the Court took anything, it would be QP2 (whether a takings claim may be dismissed as unripe where administrative relief is unavailable or futile), not QP1 as written — QP1's Article III framing does not fit a state-court judgment.
- **Summary disposition:** Unlikely. No intervening decision supports a GVR, and the alternative merits holding below makes a summary reversal on ripeness improbable.
- **Separate writing:** The denial will come without a noted dissent or statement. If any Justice wrote, it would be Justice Thomas or Justice Gorsuch, on takings ripeness or rent regulation.
