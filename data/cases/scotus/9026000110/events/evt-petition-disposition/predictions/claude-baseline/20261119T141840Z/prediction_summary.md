The Supreme Court will very likely decline to hear this case — about a one-in-fifty chance of review. A Manhattan landlord argues New York's rent rules took its property and that courts wrongly dismissed the claim as "unripe" (brought too early, before asking the agency for relief). But the case comes from state court, where the federal rule the landlord invokes does not apply, the lower court also rejected the claim on a separate ground, and a similar petition from the same lawyers was just denied.
