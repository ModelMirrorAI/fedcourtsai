The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Tedford's Tenancy, LLC v. City of New York*, with less than a one-in-ten chance (9%) of taking the case. A Manhattan apartment-building owner argues that New York courts wrongly threw out its constitutional property-rights challenge to city rent-stabilization limits as premature because the owner never applied for a financial-hardship rent increase it claims state rules barred it from getting. Although the Court asked New York officials to respond and property-rights groups filed supporting briefs, this state-court lawsuit has serious procedural flaws—including four vacant units and no prior agency application—making review unlikely.
