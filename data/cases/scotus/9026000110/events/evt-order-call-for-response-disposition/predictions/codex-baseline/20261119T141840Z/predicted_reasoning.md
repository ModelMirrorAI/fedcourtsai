I expect the Court to deny Tedford’s petition without a noted dissent or statement. The docket shows one distribution, for the September 28 conference. The requested response is due October 9, so I expect one more distribution after the response arrives or the deadline passes, followed by denial at that conference. I expect no invitation to the Solicitor General. New York’s agencies administer the hardship relief at issue, and the Court has already asked the respondents to address the petition.

If the Court grants review, I expect it to focus on the second question, narrowed to whether an owner must apply for a rent increase when the complaint plausibly alleges that the agency cannot offer meaningful relief. The Court could answer that question under *Pakdel* without settling every dispute about the jurisdictional status of ripeness. Full briefing and argument are likelier than a summary order because the state court also rejected the underlying takings claim.
