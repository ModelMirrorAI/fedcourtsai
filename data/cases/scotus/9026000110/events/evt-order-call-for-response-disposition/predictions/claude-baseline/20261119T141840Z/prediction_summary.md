The Supreme Court will very likely decline to hear this case — I put the chance of review at about three in a hundred. A Manhattan landlord asks the Court to decide when a property owner may sue over rent regulation without first seeking an administrative exemption. The Court asked New York to respond, a mild sign of interest, but the state court also ruled against the landlord on a separate ground, so answering the question would not change the result, and a closely allied petition was just turned away.
