# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely **deny certiorari after receiving the Solicitor General's invited brief**. I assign **32% probability to any grant**, including summary dispositions. The existing October 5, 2026 CVSG demonstrates interest but does not settle whether this interlocutory case is the right vehicle for review.

The docket currently contains **one distribution**, for the September 28, 2026 conference. I expect **one further distribution after the SG brief**, with a possible additional relist before disposition. The probability of at least one distribution beyond the recorded one is **97%**. My central timing forecast is action during the remainder of October Term 2026, most plausibly in **spring or early summer 2027**; the invitation itself supplies no filing deadline. This timing is a forecast, not a measured historical interval.

**A CVSG has already issued**, so I forecast no new first invitation. The CVSG-increment probability is **0%**, a vacuous claim at this information state, not a prediction that the United States will decline to file.

The likely reason to decline review is the gap between the petition's objection to subjective cultural-loss compensation and the narrower appellate ruling allowing culturally connected uses of injured natural resources to be considered, with proof and valuation still unresolved. The asserted circuit conflict is not cleanly established by the competing accounts. The Court can allow trial and any later appeal to clarify whether an actual award presents the proposed statutory limit. This predicts a procedural choice; an unexplained denial would not announce or endorse that rationale.

**If review is granted**, the Court will most likely frame the issue as whether CERCLA permits natural-resource damages for interim lost uses with a cultural component, and what nexus to injured natural resources that recovery requires. That is narrower and more neutral than deciding whether every felt loss of cultural connection is compensable. I expect the Court to leave particular expert valuations and damages amounts to subsequent proceedings rather than resolve them on this record. This conditional question forecast is context, not a prediction of an unconditional merits opinion.

**Plenary review is the likely grant route.** I assign **7% probability to disposition in the cert order, conditional on a grant**, versus 93% to the nonsummary route. The record identifies no intervening decision calling for a routine GVR, and the disputed statutory boundary is not presented as a straightforward summary correction.

**If denied, expect no separate writing.** The probability of any noted dissent from denial or statement respecting denial is **8%, conditional on denial**. I do not predict a particular Justice as author or infer individual cert votes.
