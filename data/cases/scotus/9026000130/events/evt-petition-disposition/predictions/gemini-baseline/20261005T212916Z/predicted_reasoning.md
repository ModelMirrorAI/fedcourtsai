Distributed once so far and a CVSG was issued on October 5, 2026. Expect the petition to be distributed again after the Solicitor General files their brief. Since the question presented is a significant statutory interpretation issue without a true circuit split, the Court's grant will likely hinge on the SG's recommendation. 

If granted, the Court will likely address the single question presented directly: whether CERCLA authorizes damages for the felt loss of a cultural connection to natural resources. A summary disposition is unlikely given the absence of an intervening decision and the complexity of the statutory interpretation involved. If the Court denies the petition, there is a moderate chance of a statement respecting the denial or a dissent from denial from conservative justices concerned about expanding CERCLA liability beyond easily quantifiable property damages.
