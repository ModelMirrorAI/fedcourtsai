Distributed once so far, and the Court just called for the views of the Solicitor General. Expect no further relists before a decision once the SG files. The petition asks whether CERCLA authorizes damages for the felt loss of a cultural connection to natural resources. The SG already filed a brief below supporting the Tribes, and is highly likely to recommend denying cert here given the absence of a clean circuit split. If the Court grants despite the SG's likely recommendation to deny, it would take the single QP as written to address the novel question of CERCLA damages for cultural losses. A summary disposition is unlikely as there is no intervening decision. Should the Court deny, expect a possible dissent from denial by one of the more conservative justices (e.g., Justice Alito or Thomas) given the substantial $500M potential liability and the expansion of CERCLA damages.
