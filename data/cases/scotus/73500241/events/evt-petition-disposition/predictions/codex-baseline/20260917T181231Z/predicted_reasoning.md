# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will **deny certiorari**, most likely in the orders following the September 28, 2026 conference, in late September or early October. The timing is a forecast, not a claim that an orders date has been verified.

The baseline contains **one distribution and no CVSG**. I expect **zero further distributions** before disposition. I assign a 7% probability to at least one additional distribution; if that occurs, one additional distribution is the most plausible alternative trajectory. I do not expect an extended hold or repeated relisting.

The Court will not call for the Solicitor General's views; the probability of a new CVSG is 0.2%. This expectation concerns an invitation to the Solicitor General, not a request that the state respondent answer its waived opposition.

The petition will not be selected as the vehicle for resolving the constitutional treatment of continued property retention. Its Fifth Amendment formulation and the additional barriers identified in the lower opinion make review less attractive than the underlying issue might suggest. Sources for the forecast's premises: provisioned QP and petition, pp. 3–13; filed appendix, pp. 12a–14a. A denial will not itself articulate or endorse those grounds.

**If review is granted**, the most plausible question is a narrowed version of the QP: whether retention of identified property after the tax assessment authorizing possession has been invalidated supports the constitutional claim actually preserved against a proper defendant. I do not expect the Court to announce that every retention automatically violates the Fifth Amendment, irrespective of the applicable clause, available process, or pleaded remedy. Review would first have to address whether that question can change this judgment.

Conditional on any grant, I assign **12% to disposition in the cert order itself** and **88% to the non-summary route**. Plenary treatment is likelier because resolving the asserted conflict would require distinguishing the constitutional theories and the vehicle's barriers; no identified intervening decision supplies an obvious GVR instruction.

Conditional on denial, I assign **3.5% to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial**. My point forecast is a routine denial with no separate writing. I make no individual Justice prediction and no unconditional forecast of a merits opinion.
