# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny certiorari in Brandi Greer v. Benton School District, without an explanatory opinion or a noted separate writing. The probability of any grant is **36%**. The principal obstacle to review will be the fit between the independent-contractor discrimination question and this particular nonemployee retaliation action, rather than the absence of a genuine disagreement over section 504's incorporation of ADA employment standards.

The baseline records **two distributions**: June 2 for the June 18, 2026 conference, and August 26 for the September 28, 2026 conference. Between them, the Court requested a response on June 11 and received the opposition and reply over the summer. The second distribution therefore does not necessarily establish a second round of substantive conference deliberation.

- **Further distribution:** 43% probability of at least one additional distribution beyond the two already recorded. The modal path has no further distribution and a denial following the September 28 conference. If the Court continues considering the petition, one or two additional autumn distributions are more likely than a long sequence.
- **CVSG:** 10% probability of a new invitation to the Solicitor General. None is in the baseline. Expect no invitation; the alternative path would be an invitation during autumn 2026 concerning the federal statutory and regulatory framework, followed by a substantially longer wait for disposition.
- **Timing:** Expect disposition around the opening of the October 2026 Term, most likely in early October and ordinarily by November if the Court briefly relists the case. A CVSG would push the decision beyond this primary horizon. This is a forecast, not a statement that a later order has been consulted.
- **Question if granted:** Expect review of whether section 504's adoption of ADA employment standards requires an employment relationship with the defendant, framed to encompass a nonemployee's retaliation claim. The Court would not need to revive the separate First Amendment or Arkansas civil-rights claims. The petition's broad discrimination formulation may be narrowed to fit the actual claim.
- **Route conditional on grant:** 6% probability of disposition in the cert order itself and 94% probability of plenary briefing and argument. No intervening controlling decision identified in the consulted record supplies a natural GVR instruction, and resolving the competing statutory readings together with the retaliation question favors plenary consideration over summary reversal.
- **Separate writing conditional on denial:** 10% probability of an aggregate dissent from, or statement respecting, denial. Expect none; no individual Justice's cert vote or writing is predicted.

If plenary review is granted, the likely doctrinal focus will be whether section 504(d) imports substantive discrimination standards alone or also the ADA's employee-status limitation. The Court would also have to address how that answer applies to retaliation for advocating for a disabled child. These are conditional merits expectations, not predictions of an opinion accompanying a denial.
