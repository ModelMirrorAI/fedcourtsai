The application for a stay will be granted. The Court is highly skeptical of structural injunctions that place state institutions under the control of a federal receiver, viewing them as violating federalism principles and the Prison Litigation Reform Act's "least intrusive means" requirement. The application will be referred to the full Court, as the relief sought directly challenges a major structural injunction over a state prison system. Expect the stay to be granted by the conservative majority, likely with dissents from the liberal justices. The amicus count is likely to increase slightly as other states may file in support of Arizona prior to the full Court's disposition.
