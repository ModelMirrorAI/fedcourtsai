Distributed twice before the CVSG. Expect at least one further distribution after the Solicitor General files its brief, likely more if the SG recommends a grant or if the Court closely deliberates on the recommendation. The SG's views were called for, which indicates the Court sees the ERISA preemption and remedial questions as significant and potentially implicating federal interests. If granted, the Court would likely take both questions to resolve the circuit split on equitable surcharge and the preemption of state law claims when ERISA leaves a remedial gap. A summary disposition is unlikely here, as this is a matter of statutory interpretation requiring plenary review. Should the Court deny, a dissent from denial is possible given the recognized remedial gap, but typically the Court follows the SG's recommendation.
