# Forecast

No. 25-590. The probability of any grant, including a GVR or summary reversal,
is **16%**. The likely route is an unexplained denial rather than an opinion
resolving the ERISA surcharge dispute. The vehicle's unresolved fiduciary-status
and liability questions will outweigh the attraction of addressing the circuit
conflict. This forecasts the selection decision, not an endorsement of the
Sixth Circuit's categorical treatment of surcharge.

## If review is granted

The likely issue is the first question: whether ERISA Section 502(a)(3)
categorically excludes surcharge, with attention to the fiduciary relationship
necessary for that remedy. The Court is more likely to leave the contingent
state-law preemption question outside a grant than to take both questions.
If it reaches the merits, I forecast rejection of a categorical prohibition
on fiduciary surcharge, while leaving unresolved liability and status issues
for further proceedings. This merits forecast is conditional only.

Plenary briefing and argument are more likely than disposing of the merits in
the cert order. Conditional on any grant, I assign **10%** to a summary route
and **90%** to plenary review. The government's disagreement with the lower
court's reasoning is not itself an intervening judgment requiring a GVR.

Conditional on denial, I assign **8%** to some noted dissent or statement
respecting denial. The modal forecast is no separate writing; I do not predict
an individual Justice's vote or identify an expected author.
