The Supreme Court is more likely to turn this case away than hear it, but the chance of review is substantial: about one in three. The dispute asks whether a company with both American and foreign owners can bring certain lawsuits in federal court. Different appeals courts appear to answer that question differently, and the Supreme Court has requested a response. Still, that request does not promise a hearing, and the precise scope of the disagreement remains contested. Expect another conference distribution before a decision.
