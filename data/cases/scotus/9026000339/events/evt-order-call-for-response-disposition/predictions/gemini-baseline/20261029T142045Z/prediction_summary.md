The Supreme Court is likely to deny review in *FS Medical Supplies, LLC v. Tanner Pharma UK Limited*, with about a one-in-seven (14%) chance of granting the petition. The case asks whether a business organized as a limited liability company (LLC) with both American and foreign owners can sue both American and foreign defendants in federal court under the federal diversity-jurisdiction statute, which allows federal courts to hear disputes between citizens of different states where foreign citizens are additional parties. Although the Court asked the respondents to file a brief after they initially waived their right to respond—and the petitioner is represented by experienced Supreme Court counsel with outside support—the Fourth Circuit's dismissal relies on established precedent treating an LLC as having the citizenship of every one of its members, making review unlikely once the opposition brief is filed.
