We expect the Supreme Court to turn this case down, but the chance it agrees to hear it is real, about one in five. The case asks whether federal courts can hear a business lawsuit when a company with both American and Chinese owners sues American and British defendants. One federal appeals court said no and another said yes. That kind of disagreement interests the Justices, and they have already asked the other side to respond. Still, most petitions are denied even after that step.
