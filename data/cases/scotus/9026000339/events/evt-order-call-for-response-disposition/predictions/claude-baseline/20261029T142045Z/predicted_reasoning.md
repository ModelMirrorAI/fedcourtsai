# Forecast — FS Medical Supplies, LLC v. Tanner Pharma UK Ltd., No. 26-339

**Disposition: denied (P(grant) ≈ 0.20).** The Court called for a response on
October 6, 2026, after every respondent waived, with the response due
November 5. The Court is most likely to deny once the brief in opposition is in.
A plenary grant is a real possibility, though, roughly one in five.

**Distributions.** The record shows one distribution (for the October 16, 2026
conference), and the call for a response in effect pulls the petition from that
conference. Expect it to be distributed again after the response and reply,
for a conference in late November or December 2026. The most likely path is a
denial at that first post-response conference. If the Court is interested,
expect one or two relists before a grant, possibly in December or January.

**CVSG.** Not expected. This is a private commercial dispute about a statute's
reach, with no federal agency interest. Call-for-response petitions on purely
private diversity questions are rarely sent to the SG.

**Question, if granted.** The Court would take the single QP essentially as
written: whether § 1332(a)(3) reaches a suit in which an LLC with both state and
foreign citizenship is the sole plaintiff, the defendants include state and
foreign citizens, and the state citizenships on opposite sides are completely
diverse. The Court might reframe it more generally, as whether a party with
both U.S.-state and foreign citizenship counts as a "citizen of a different
State" for (a)(3).

**Route.** If the Court grants, it will almost certainly set the case for
briefing and argument. The split is acknowledged and textual, and no
intervening decision supports a GVR. A summary reversal of a published,
reasoned court-of-appeals jurisdictional holding is unlikely.

**Separate writing on denial.** None expected. A denial would most likely rest
on the split being shallow and old (one published Seventh Circuit decision from
2003, an unpublished Fifth Circuit table decision, and Second Circuit dicta),
which draws no dissent.
