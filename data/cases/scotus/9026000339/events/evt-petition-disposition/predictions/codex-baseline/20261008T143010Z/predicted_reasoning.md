# Forecast

The Court will most likely deny certiorari, but this is a substantial candidate for plenary review: P(any grant) = 0.42. The expected denial will leave the jurisdictional ruling below standing without a Supreme Court opinion on its correctness.

## Further proceedings

The snapshot records **one distribution**, on September 30, 2026, for the October 16 conference, and **no CVSG**. It also records an October 6 request for a response due November 5, after the scheduled conference. I therefore expect the petition to await that response and return on a later distribution rather than receive its final disposition at the October 16 conference. P(at least one additional distribution) = **0.98**. My central forecast is one further distribution after briefing; a second further distribution is a substantial possibility if the Court gives the petition extended consideration. A replacement distribution after a response request satisfies this mechanical claim even if it is not a substantive relist after conference consideration.

I expect no call for the Solicitor General's views: P(a new CVSG) = **0.03**. The presented dispute concerns private parties' access to diversity jurisdiction, and I do not expect the Court to seek executive-branch advice merely because foreign citizenship is involved. The low-probability CVSG route would most naturally arise after the opposition and reply have clarified the issue.

Absent an extension or a CVSG, I expect disposition in December 2026 or January 2027, following completion of briefing and renewed conference consideration. This is a timing forecast, not an assertion of a scheduled decision date.

## Scope and route if granted

If the Court grants review, I expect it to take the single question substantially as presented: whether section 1332(a)(3) permits jurisdiction when the sole plaintiff is an LLC with both state and foreign citizenship, the defendants include state and foreign citizens, and the state citizenships are completely diverse across the litigation. I expect the question to remain focused on that configuration, not on all possible corporation, permanent-resident, or alienage-jurisdiction problems.

Conditional on a grant, I expect plenary briefing and argument, not disposition in the certiorari order: P(summary disposition | grant) = **0.06**. The dispute calls for resolving competing readings of entity citizenship and the statutory requirement of additional foreign parties. No intervening controlling decision warranting a GVR has been identified. If granted, my tentative merits expectation is a decision permitting jurisdiction in this configuration, distinguishing Grupo Dataflux's time-of-filing problem; that merits expectation is conditional and is not a forecast that an opinion will necessarily issue.

If review is denied, I expect no noted dissent or statement respecting denial: P(any such writing | denial) = **0.04**. I do not forecast a particular Justice's participation or a public vote lineup.
