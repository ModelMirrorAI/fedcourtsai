## In brief

FS Medical Supplies asks the Court to decide whether federal courts may hear its lawsuit despite one of its members being a foreign citizen.

## What happened

FS Medical Supplies, an LLC with members from Texas, California, and China, sued Tanner Pharma UK Limited, TannerGAP, and two individuals, all citizens of North Carolina and the United Kingdom, for breach of contract and fraudulent conveyance. The case was filed in federal court in North Carolina based on the parties' differing state and national citizenships. After the parties' citizenships were clarified, the defendants argued that having foreign citizens connected to both sides of the case defeated federal jurisdiction. A magistrate judge and the district court agreed and dismissed the case, and the Fourth Circuit affirmed, holding that because one of FS Medical's members is Chinese, the company counts as having no U.S. citizenship for jurisdiction purposes.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether a federal diversity law, which lets federal courts hear certain lawsuits between citizens of different states when foreign citizens are also involved, applies when the single plaintiff is a company whose members include both a U.S. state citizen and a foreign citizen, suing defendants who likewise include both state and foreign citizens, where the state citizenships on each side do not overlap.

## Where it stands

The petition was distributed for the Court's October 16, 2026 conference. On October 6, 2026, after that distribution, the Court requested a response from the respondents, due November 5, 2026; an amicus brief supporting the petition has also been filed.

## What each outcome would mean

FS Medical Supplies, the company that sued, is asking the Court to take up its case. If the Court grants the petition, it will hear full arguments and decide whether the jurisdictional law covers this situation, without yet deciding who wins. If the Court denies the petition, the Fourth Circuit's ruling stands, meaning FS Medical's lawsuit against Tanner Pharma UK, TannerGAP, and the individual defendants stays dismissed from federal court.
