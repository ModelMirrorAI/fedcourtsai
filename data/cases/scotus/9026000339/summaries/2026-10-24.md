## In brief

FS Medical Supplies asks the Court to decide whether a business with both American and foreign owners can use diversity jurisdiction to sue in federal court.

## What happened

FS Medical Supplies, a company with members from Texas, California, and China, sued Tanner Pharma UK and related North Carolina defendants over a contract dispute involving COVID-era medical supplies. FS Medical claims the defendants secretly cut it out of a profit-sharing deal worth hundreds of millions of dollars. The district court dismissed the case, ruling it lacked jurisdiction because FS Medical's Chinese member meant there was no American citizen on the plaintiff's side once foreign citizenship was factored in. The Fourth Circuit Court of Appeals affirmed that dismissal.

## What the Court is being asked

The law lets federal courts hear cases between citizens of different states even when foreign citizens are also involved as additional parties. The Court is asked whether a company that counts both American and foreign citizens among its members can still be treated as an American citizen for this purpose, when its American owners are from different states than the opposing side. FS Medical argues yes, pointing to a conflict among federal appeals courts. The lower court held no, reasoning that the company's foreign ownership disqualified it entirely.

## Where it stands

The petition was distributed for the Court's October 16, 2026 conference. Rather than granting or denying it then, the Court asked the respondents to file a response, due December 2, 2026 after the Court granted a request to extend that deadline. No decision on whether to hear the case has been made yet.

## What each outcome would mean

FS Medical, as petitioner, is asking the Court to take up the case and reverse the Fourth Circuit's decision. If the Court grants review and eventually rules for FS Medical, its lawsuit against Tanner Pharma UK and the other defendants could proceed in federal court. If the Court denies review, or grants review and rules for Tanner Pharma UK, the dismissal stands and FS Medical would need another path, such as state court, to pursue its claims. The Court could also send the case back without deciding the underlying question.
