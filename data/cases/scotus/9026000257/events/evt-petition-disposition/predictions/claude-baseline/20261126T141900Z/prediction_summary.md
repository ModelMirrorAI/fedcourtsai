The Supreme Court is somewhat more likely than not to turn down this request to review Texas's law requiring the Ten Commandments in every public-school classroom, but it is close: about a two-in-five chance the Court takes the case. The lower court said a 1980 Supreme Court ruling against such displays no longer applies, which invites review. But no other appeals court has disagreed yet, and Texas's incoming attorney general says he will not defend the law. Expect the Court to consider the petition at more than one private conference before acting.
