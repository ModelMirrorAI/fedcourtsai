I give the Supreme Court about a three-in-five chance of taking the challenge to Texas’s law requiring the Ten Commandments in public-school classrooms. The Court struck down a similar Kentucky law in 1980. A closely divided federal appeals court concluded that later rulings had stripped that decision of its force. The justices have a reason to settle which rule schools must follow. They could also wait for another appeals court to rule on Arkansas’s law. If they take the case, I expect oral argument.
