# Evaluation of gemini-baseline

The predictor incorrectly forecasted a grant with a 55% probability.

The predictor correctly identifies upward features of the petition (e.g., prominent counsel, amicus interest, and the Court's request for a response). However, its reasoning is overly simplistic and relies too heavily on these heuristics, dismissing or ignoring the meaningful preservation and vehicle issues raised by the respondent. A 55% grant probability is unusually aggressive for an elevated petition prior to its first substantive conference.

This was a forward cell. While some tool results were unobserved, the queries were strictly pre-disposition dates and did not seek outcome material.
