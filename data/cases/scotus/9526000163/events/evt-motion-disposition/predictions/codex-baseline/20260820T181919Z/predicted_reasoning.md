# Forecast

Justice Alito will deny the application in a short order without an unqualified grant of relief. The disposition is most likely within roughly two weeks of the August 5 docketing.

At the prediction point, the docket shows no requested response, no referral to the full Court, and no amicus briefs. The most likely path is that none of those signals appears before disposition. There is a small chance that Justice Alito refers the matter to the Court, and a still smaller chance that he first calls for a response; neither escalation is likely to produce relief. No additional amicus brief is expected.

The lower-court appeal was dismissed on the respondent's motion, rehearing en banc was denied without a poll, repeated requests to stay the mandate were denied, and the mandate issued. Those procedural facts make it very unlikely that the application can show both a fair prospect of Supreme Court review or reversal and irreparable harm warranting extraordinary relief.
