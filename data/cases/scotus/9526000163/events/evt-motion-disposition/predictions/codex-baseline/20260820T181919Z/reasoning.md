# Rationale

I assign a 0.003 probability to an unqualified grant and predict denial. The committed interim statpack supplies a valid general baseline for this OT2026 cell: OT2024 and OT2025 together contain 30 grants among 225 resolved substantive applications, or 13.3%, clearing the 50-case floor. That pool is only an anchor. Its machine-parsed coverage is uneven, its escalation signals are terminal rather than as-at-prediction, and the scored reserve selects applications higher on the escalation ladder than the raw cohort.

The case-specific adjustment is sharply downward. The provisioned snapshot identifies a private, pro se stay application submitted to Justice Alito. CourtListener's lower-court docket shows that the Fifth Circuit dismissed the appeal on Walmart's opposed motion, denied rehearing en banc without a poll, repeatedly denied requests to stay the mandate, and issued the mandate on May 29. The underlying district matter is a private employment-discrimination action. This posture offers no affirmative evidence of a reasonable probability of review, a fair prospect of reversal, or irreparable harm, and the already-issued mandate makes the requested relief procedurally difficult.

The frozen context shows zero amici and that neither a response request nor a full-Court referral had occurred as of August 6. I therefore assign 0.01 to a later response request, 0.03 to a later referral, and 0.002 to any later amicus filing. Referral is slightly more likely than a response request because a Circuit Justice can send an application to the full Court without first requesting a response, but a single-Justice denial remains overwhelmingly more likely.

The largest uncertainty is the missing application text: no filed-document text was provisioned, so I could not assess the applicant's precise requested stay, claimed emergency, legal question, or irreparable-injury showing. Broad corpus retrieval found recent pro se private-civil application denials consistent with the downward adjustment, but it did not supply a conditioned baseline. I did not retrieve the Supreme Court application's disposition or subsequent Supreme Court docket history.
