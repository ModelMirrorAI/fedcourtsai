# Forecast: what the Court will do with application 26A163

**Disposition.** Justice Alito will deny the application, acting alone as
Circuit Justice, without opinion and without referral. The docketed entry will
be a one-line denial. The chance of an unqualified grant is about 1%.

**Response requested.** No. The Court will not call for a response before
disposing of the application (~3%). A response is called for when an
application presents a fair prospect of relief; this one — a pro se stay
application in a private civil dispute whose appeal was dismissed below, whose
en banc petition was denied without a poll, and whose mandate has already
issued — will be denied on the papers.

**Referral to the full Court.** Not on the initial submission. The realistic
path to a referral is a *renewal*: this applicant filed at least four
successive stay motions in the Fifth Circuit after losing, so if Justice Alito
denies alone she may well refile the application to another Justice, and a
renewed application is customarily referred to the full Court. I put the
probability of an eventual referral at ~12% — modest, because most denied pro
se applicants stop at one submission or start a fresh application number
instead.

**Amicus briefs.** None will be filed (the count rises past zero with
probability ~2%). There is no institutional constituency for this dispute.

**Timing.** A single-Justice denial of an application in this posture usually
lands within one to three weeks of docketing. Docketed August 5, this one is
likely disposed of by late August 2026, and very probably before the end of
September. (Timing resolves against nothing; it is stated to discipline the
rest.)

**Conditional merits shape.** If — against the forecast — the application were
referred and granted, it would be as a stay pending certiorari, and the
underlying petition would still face a near-certain denial: the Fifth Circuit
dismissed the appeal on the appellee's motion rather than deciding any
question a certworthy split could ride on. There is no scenario here in which
the application becomes a vehicle for anything broader.
