We predict the Supreme Court will **deny** this petition for review, with **about a one-in-six chance (16%)** of a grant. Although the case raises a recurring First Amendment question about when civil courts may apply secular corporate law ("neutral principles of law") to internal disputes within a religious organization—and has drawn three friend-of-the-court briefs and a request from the Court for a response—serious procedural obstacles make review unlikely. In particular, the Nevada Supreme Court only allowed the lawsuit to move past an initial motion to dismiss "at this juncture" without deciding any religious question, and the dispute centers on control of temple property and access to corporate records rather than religious doctrine.
