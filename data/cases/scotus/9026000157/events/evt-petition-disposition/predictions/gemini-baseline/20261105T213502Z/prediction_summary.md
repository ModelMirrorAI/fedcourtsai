We predict the Supreme Court will deny review in this case, with about a one-in-nine chance (11%) that the Court agrees to hear it. The dispute involves rival factions of a Sikh temple in Reno, Nevada, and raises an important First Amendment question that has divided lower courts: whether secular courts can apply ordinary corporate-bylaw rules ("neutral principles of law") to resolve internal church leadership disputes. Although three outside religious groups filed briefs supporting review and the Court asked the challengers to respond, the case is still in its earliest stage in Nevada state court and mixes church-leadership claims with a dispute over transferring temple real estate into a trust, making it an unattractive vehicle for a landmark ruling.
