# Forecast — No. 26-157, Singh v. Second Judicial District Court of Nevada

- **Distributions:** The docket shows one distribution (for the 10/9/2026 conference), overtaken by the Court's
  Sep 22 request for a response. Expect the petition to be redistributed after the BIO (due Nov 23, 2026) and the
  reply, for a December 2026 or January 2027 conference. Expect at least one further relist beyond that
  if any Justice is weighing a grant or a writing. Most likely, total distributions end at 2–3.
- **CVSG:** None expected. No federal party or federal-statute interest is implicated.
- **Disposition:** Denied, most likely. A grant is a real but minority possibility (about one in six).
- **If granted:** The Court would take the QP as written: whether the neutral-principles approach of Jones v. Wolf
  extends beyond church-property ownership to internal governance and membership disputes. The likely
  merits ground would confine neutral principles to property disputes, or at least bar civil courts from
  enforcing bylaws that define membership and authority in religious terms. Before reaching the merits, the
  Court would have to accept § 1257 finality under Atlantic Richfield v. Christian.
- **Summary route:** Unlikely. Plenary review is the route if the Court acts. No intervening decision supports a GVR.
- **Dissent from denial:** A meaningful chance (about 30%) of a statement respecting or dissent from denial
  if denied, most likely from Justice Alito, possibly joined by or alongside Justices Thomas and Gorsuch.
