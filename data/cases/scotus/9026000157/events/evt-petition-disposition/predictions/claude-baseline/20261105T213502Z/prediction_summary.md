We predict the Supreme Court will most likely turn down this case, giving it about a one-in-six chance of
agreeing to hear it. The case asks whether courts may settle a leadership dispute inside a Sikh religious
society by applying the society's bylaws. Some signs point toward review: the Court asked the other side to
respond, three outside groups filed supporting briefs, and lower courts disagree on the question. But the Court
recently declined a similar case, and this one is still at an early stage in the state courts.
