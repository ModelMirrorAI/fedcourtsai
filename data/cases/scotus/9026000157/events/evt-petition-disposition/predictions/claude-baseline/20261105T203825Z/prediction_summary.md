We predict the Supreme Court will most likely decline to hear this case, giving it about a one-in-five chance of agreeing to hear it. The petition comes from leaders of a Reno Sikh temple society. They argue that the First Amendment bars state courts from deciding disputes over who leads a religious group, and lower courts disagree on that question. The Court has asked the other side to respond, and religious groups filed supporting briefs. Both are good signs for the petition. But the Court turned down a similar case in May 2026, and this case comes up at an early stage of the lawsuit, so we still expect a denial.
