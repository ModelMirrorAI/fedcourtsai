The Supreme Court is likely to turn down this appeal, with about a one-in-four chance of taking it up. The dispute asks how far civil judges may go in enforcing a religious group's membership and leadership rules. Different courts have taken different approaches, and the Supreme Court has asked the opposing side to respond. But Nevada's court viewed this particular lawsuit as involving ordinary organizational procedures, not religious beliefs. That distinction and the case's early stage make refusal more likely than review.
