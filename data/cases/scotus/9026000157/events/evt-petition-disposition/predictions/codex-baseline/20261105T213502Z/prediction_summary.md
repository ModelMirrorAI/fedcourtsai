The Supreme Court is likely to decline this appeal, with about a one-in-four chance of taking the case. The dispute asks how far civil courts can go in enforcing a religious group's rules for membership and leadership. Lower courts disagree, three outside groups support review, and the Justices have requested a response. Those are meaningful signs of interest, but the lawsuit is still at an early stage and may involve ordinary organizational rules rather than religious decisions. Another conference distribution is likely before a decision.
