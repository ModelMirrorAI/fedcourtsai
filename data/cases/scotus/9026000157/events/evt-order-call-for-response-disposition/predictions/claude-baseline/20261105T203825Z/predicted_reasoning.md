# Forecast — No. 26-157

Distributed once so far (for the 10/9/2026 conference), then pulled when the
Court requested a response due Oct 22, 2026. Expect redistribution after the
brief in opposition and reply, for a conference in November or December 2026.
I expect **denial**, most likely after one or two further distributions; a
grant would most likely come after at least one relist.

No CVSG: the dispute is between private parties in a state-court case.

If granted, the Court would take the single QP essentially as written —
whether "neutral principles" may be applied beyond church-property ownership
to a religious body's internal governance/membership dispute — and could add
or address the § 1257 finality question. Plenary review, not a summary
disposition, is the likelier route on a grant; no intervening decision calls
for a GVR.

On denial, I expect no separate writing more likely than not; if any, a
statement respecting denial from Justice Alito (possibly joined by Thomas or
Gorsuch) noting the importance of the question and the interlocutory posture.
