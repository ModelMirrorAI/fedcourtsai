We expect the Supreme Court to turn down this case, with roughly a one-in-six
chance it agrees to hear it. The case asks whether courts can settle internal
leadership fights inside a religious group (here a Nevada Sikh society) by
applying its bylaws like ordinary rules. Lower courts disagree, and the Court
asked the other side to respond, a sign of some interest. But the case is at an
early stage, and the Court recently declined a similar, better-supported case.
