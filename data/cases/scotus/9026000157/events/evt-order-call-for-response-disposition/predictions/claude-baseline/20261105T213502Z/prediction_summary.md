We predict the Supreme Court will most likely **turn down** this case, with about a one-in-seven
chance (14%) that it agrees to hear it. The case asks whether courts may settle a leadership fight inside
a Sikh temple society by reading its bylaws, or whether the First Amendment keeps courts out of a
religious group's internal affairs. The Court asked the other side to respond, and lower courts disagree
on the question, both of which help the petition. But the case comes up before trial, which raises a
question of whether it is ready for review, and the Court recently passed on a similar case.
