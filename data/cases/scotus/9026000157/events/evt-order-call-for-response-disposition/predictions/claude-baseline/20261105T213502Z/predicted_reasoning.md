# Forecast: No. 26-157

The docket shows **one distribution** (for the Conference of 10/9/2026) and then the Court's call for a
response (Sep 22, due Oct 22). Once the response and any reply are in, the petition will be
distributed again, most likely for a November or December 2026 conference. Expect **one further
distribution, and a relist or two only if the Court is seriously considering a grant**.

**No CVSG.** The dispute is between private parties under state law, and no federal program or interest
is at stake.

**Likeliest outcome: denial**, probably on the finality/vehicle objection that the response will press,
and because the Court recently declined the related O'Connell petition. Some chance it is held or
denied while the Court watches Christian v. Lutheran Church–Missouri Synod (No. 26-466).

**If granted**, the Court would take the QP as written (does the "neutral principles" approach reach
beyond church-property ownership to internal-governance disputes?). It might add, or have to address, a
§ 1257 finality question. The route would be plenary briefing and argument, not a summary disposition.

**On denial**, most likely no separate writing. If anyone writes, the likeliest are a statement or dissent
by Justice Alito, possibly joined by Justices Thomas and Gorsuch, urging the Court to take up the split in
a better vehicle.
