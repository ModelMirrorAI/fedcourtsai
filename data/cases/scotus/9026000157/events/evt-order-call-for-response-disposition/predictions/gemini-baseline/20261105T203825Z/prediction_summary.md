The Supreme Court is unlikely to hear this appeal, with about a one in six (16%) chance of granting review and a four in five (84%) chance of denying it. The case asks whether the First Amendment's protection for church self-government bars Nevada state courts from using neutral corporate-law rules to resolve a dispute over a Sikh temple's bylaws, committees, and property trust. Although the Court asked the respondents to file a brief and lower courts disagree on how far civil courts may go in internal religious disputes, the Court will likely pass on this case because it is still at an early stage in state court and centers on control of the temple's real estate.
