The Supreme Court is likely to decline this appeal, with about a one-in-four chance of agreeing to review it (23%). The dispute asks how far civil judges may go in enforcing a Sikh religious organization's rules about leadership and management. Several religious groups support review, and the Court has requested an answer from the other side. But the Nevada court described the disputed procedures as secular and religious membership qualifications as uncontested, making this a less straightforward test case than the challengers suggest.
