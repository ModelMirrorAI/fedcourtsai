# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely **deny the petition**, with a **23% probability of any grant**. A denial will ordinarily be unexplained and will not decide that Nevada's constitutional analysis is correct.

The snapshot records **one distribution and no true post-conference relist**. The Court will redistribute the petition after the requested response is filed or its deadline expires. I assign **99%** to at least one additional distribution; the central procedural path is one redistribution followed by denial, rather than a prolonged series of substantive relists. If the Court considers granting, one further conference relist beyond that redistribution is a plausible deliberative path. The administrative redistribution is not itself a forecast of growing merits support.

The Court will **not call for the Solicitor General's views**; I assign **2.5%** to a new CVSG. The parties' private governance dispute does not present an evident need for a federal executive assessment.

**If review is granted**, the Court will most likely take the petition's single question with a narrower emphasis on whether civil adjudication of membership and leadership authority intrudes on religious governance, rather than announce that every non-property civil claim against a religious entity is barred. The likely issue will be the boundary between enforceable secular corporate procedures and constitutionally protected decisions about who governs a religious body.

**Conditional on a grant**, full briefing and argument are substantially more likely than disposition in the cert order: **10%** for the summary route. No identified intervening precedent supplies a natural basis for a grant, vacatur, and remand. If the Court reaches the merits, the distinction between uncontested religious eligibility and challenged governance authority will matter more than whether the litigants attach a secular label to their claims.

**Conditional on denial**, I assign **12%** to a noted dissent or statement respecting denial. The modal forecast is no separate writing. If a Justice writes, Thomas or Alito is the leading possibility, followed by Gorsuch; that is context about possible authors, not a forecast of disclosed cert votes.
