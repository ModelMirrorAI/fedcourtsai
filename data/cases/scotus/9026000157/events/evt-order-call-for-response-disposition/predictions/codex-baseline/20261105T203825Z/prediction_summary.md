The Supreme Court is likely to decline this case, with about a one-in-four chance of taking it up (24%). The dispute asks when civil courts may enforce an organization's ordinary rules without interfering in its religious leadership and membership. A request for a response, three supporting briefs, and disagreement among lower courts make review more plausible than usual. Still, procedural complications and the dispute's overlap with property management favor denial. A denial would probably come without an explanation and would not approve the lower court's reasoning.
