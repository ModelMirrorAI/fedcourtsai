# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny certiorari without a separate writing. My probability of any grant, including a summary disposition, is 16%.

The frozen context records one distribution and no CVSG. The snapshot contains two notices, entered June 24 and September 9, but both designate the same September 28, 2026 conference. They do not demonstrate two completed conferences or a post-conference relist. From the harness's one-distribution baseline, I assign 38% to at least one further distribution. The modal path is no additional conference and a denial in late September or October 2026. In the additional-distribution branch, one or two more conferences are more likely than a long series; a CVSG would extend the horizon substantially.

I expect no call for the Solicitor General's views, assigning 12% to a new CVSG. Federal Indian-law interests make such a call plausible, but the intervention and custody-procedure obstacles offer a reason to decline this vehicle before examining the federal statute's constitutionality. The June 25 request for a respondent's opposition is not a CVSG.

If review is granted, the likelier route is briefing and argument, not disposition in the cert order: my conditional summary-route probability is 30%. A summary reversal or vacatur addressing the First Amendment/intervention ruling is more plausible than an immediate categorical invalidation of ICWA and MIFPA. I do not identify an intervening controlling decision that supplies a routine GVR rationale.

Conditional on a grant, I expect the Court to focus first on the second question presented: whether a court may treat a good-faith constitutional challenge as a reason to exclude prospective intervenors. The first question would more likely be narrowed to placement preferences as applied to these prospective custodians than accepted as a challenge to every protection in both statutes. A decision requiring reconsideration of intervention without reliance on the constitutional challenge could leave the broader equal-protection issue unanswered.

Conditional on denial, I assign 20% to some noted dissent or statement respecting denial. No separate writing is the point forecast. Kavanaugh is a plausible source of a statement stressing the unresolved equal-protection question, given his Brackeen concurrence; that is contextual speculation, not a predicted observable cert vote. I do not forecast individual votes on the petition.
