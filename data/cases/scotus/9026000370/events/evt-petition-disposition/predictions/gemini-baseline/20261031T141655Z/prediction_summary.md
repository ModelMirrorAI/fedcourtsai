The Supreme Court is very likely to deny The Nielsen Company's request to review this case, with only a 4% chance (about one in twenty-five) of taking it up. Nielsen asks the Court to overturn a preliminary lower-court order that faulted Nielsen for charging Cumulus Media a high standalone price for national radio-ratings data unless Cumulus also bought local data in a discounted bundle. Although the case raises an important antitrust question about bundled discounts, the dispute is still at a preliminary stage in the lower courts and Cumulus initially waived its right to respond, making the Supreme Court unlikely to step in now.
