# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition in an unsigned order without a statement or noted dissent. The modal timing is an order following the scheduled November 13, 2026 conference, during November or December 2026. A denial would not approve the Second Circuit's antitrust analysis; it would leave the preliminary injunction ruling in place without Supreme Court merits review.

The snapshot shows **one distribution and zero relists**. I expect **zero additional distributions** on the modal path and assign **26%** to at least one additional distribution. If the Court pursues the petition, a request for a response to replace the existing waiver is a plausible intermediate step, with one or two later distributions before a decision. A response request alone would not imply a grant.

No CVSG is recorded. I forecast **no new CVSG**, with a **6%** chance of one. In that minority path, the invitation would likely follow additional consideration in late 2026 or early 2027 and postpone disposition while the government's views are obtained.

If review is granted, the Court would most likely take the single question substantially as presented, potentially narrowing it to whether a constructive tie enforced solely through price differentials requires a below-cost-equivalence or discount-attribution showing. It would not need to hold that every above-cost bundle is lawful regardless of other exclusionary conduct. Conditional on review, the key distinction would be between ordinary bundle discounting and continuation of an unlawful express tie through a nominal standalone offer.

Plenary briefing and argument are much likelier than a summary route if the Court grants: **P(disposition in the cert order | grant) = 7%**. There is no identified intervening Supreme Court decision or matched granted petition pointing to a routine GVR or hold. Reconciling the competing tests and the panel's reliance on older tying cases is not an obvious summary-correction task.

Conditional on denial, the probability of some noted dissent or statement respecting denial is **6%**. The modal denial has none. If there is a separate writing, Thomas or Gorsuch is the tentative leading possibility, but neither is individually likely to write. No merits opinion or merits vote lineup is forecast on the modal denial path.
