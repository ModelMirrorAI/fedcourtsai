Nielsen, the ratings company, asks the Supreme Court to review an appeals-court ruling that its pricing likely broke federal antitrust law. The court found that Nielsen charged so much for a product sold alone that customers were effectively forced to buy a bundle. We think the Court will probably turn the case down, about a one-in-eight chance of taking it. The legal question has split the lower courts and drawn outside support. But the case is at an early stage: the ruling under review is only a preliminary injunction, a temporary order issued before trial. The Court will probably ask the other side to respond first.
