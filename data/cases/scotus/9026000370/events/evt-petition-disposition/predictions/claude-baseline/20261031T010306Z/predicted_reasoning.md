# Forecast — No. 26-370

The docket shows **one distribution** (for the Nov 13, 2026 conference), no relist, no CVSG, and a respondent waiver.

- **Call for response / relist:** At or before the Nov 13 conference, the Court (or a Justice) will likely **call for a response** from Cumulus. The petition will then be redistributed once the BIO and reply are in, likely for a January–February 2027 conference. Expect at least one further distribution (about 62%).
- **CVSG:** Possible but less likely than not (about 15%). If one comes, expect it after the BIO is filed, in early 2027, because the federal antitrust agencies have a direct interest in the price-cost standard for bundled discounts.
- **Disposition:** Most likely **denied**, with the interlocutory preliminary-injunction posture as the vehicle problem. Grant probability is about 12%.
- **If granted:** The Court would take the QP essentially as written: whether above-cost standalone/bundled pricing can support Section 2 "constructive tie" liability. It might reformulate the question as whether a price-cost test (in the line of Brooke Group, Weyerhaeuser and linkLine) governs bundled-discount claims. The likely direction is to reject the LePage's approach.
- **Summary disposition:** Unlikely. No intervening decision supports a GVR, and a contested split is a poor fit for a summary reversal.
- **Separate writing on denial:** Not expected. If any, a short statement noting the issue's importance but the interlocutory posture, most plausibly from Thomas, Gorsuch or Alito.
