The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *The Nielsen Company v. Cumulus Media New Holdings*, with an 8% chance (roughly one in twelve) of granting the petition. Although Nielsen has strong business support and asks an important antitrust question—whether charging a high standalone price alongside a discounted bundle of radio ratings data violates federal monopoly law—the case arrives at an preliminary stage before trial, involves an ongoing bankruptcy stay, and turns on case-specific pricing negotiations rather than a clean legal disagreement among lower courts.
