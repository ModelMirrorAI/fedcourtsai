Prediction: the Supreme Court will most likely turn down Nielsen's request to hear its case (we put the chance it agrees to hear it at about one in eight, 13%). Nielsen argues a court wrongly treated its high standalone price for a ratings product as illegally forcing a customer to buy a bundle. The Court asked the other side to respond, and two outside groups backed Nielsen, which raises the odds above normal. But the ruling only upheld a temporary order while the lawsuit continues, which usually makes the Court wait.
