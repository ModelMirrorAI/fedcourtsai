# Forecast — Nielsen v. Cumulus Media, No. 26-370

The docket shows **one distribution** (for the November 13, 2026 conference), never relisted, and a **response requested on November 5, 2026**, due December 7. The petition will come off the 11/13 conference and be redistributed once the brief in opposition (and any reply) is in — expect redistribution in mid-to-late December for a January 2027 conference.

**Most likely outcome: certiorari denied**, probably at the first conference after the response, without further relists. Respondent will argue the case is an interlocutory preliminary-injunction appeal, that the Second Circuit applied *Loew's* to a fact-bound coercion finding growing out of an express tie (the Network Policy), and that the bundled-discount split (LePage's vs. PeaceHealth/Collins Inkjet) is not implicated.

**CVSG:** possible but less likely than not (~20%); if the Court is interested in the antitrust question it may ask the Solicitor General's views at the first post-response conference (January 2027) rather than grant outright, given its 2004 CVSG in 3M v. LePage's.

**If granted**, the Court would take the single question presented largely as written — whether above-cost standalone and bundled pricing can constitute a "constructive tie" under Section 2 — possibly reformulated to whether a below-cost (or discount-attribution) showing is required. Review would be plenary; a summary disposition or GVR is very unlikely, as no intervening decision bears on it.

**If denied**, expect no separate writing; a statement respecting denial (most plausibly from Justice Thomas or Justice Gorsuch) is a small tail.
