The Supreme Court is likely to decline Nielsen's appeal, with about a one-in-five chance of taking it. The dispute asks when discounted product bundles become illegal pressure to buy unwanted products. A requested response and two supporting outside briefs suggest interest, but the case involves a temporary court order and an unfinished factual record. The Court will probably reconsider the petition after the response arrives. If it accepts the case, full briefing and argument are much likelier than an immediate ruling.
