## In brief

Nielsen asks the Court to review a Second Circuit ruling that let Cumulus pursue an antitrust claim over Nielsen's data pricing.

## What happened

Nielsen sells radio ratings data, including a national report only it produces. Cumulus, a broadcaster, wanted to buy that national report plus only some local data, using a competitor for the rest. Nielsen's policy linked full access to the national report to buying local data everywhere Cumulus operates. Cumulus sued, claiming this was an illegal tying arrangement, and a district court issued a preliminary injunction against Nielsen. After Nielsen offered the national report separately at a high price, the district court found that price itself was an illegal "constructive tie," and the Second Circuit affirmed, while also ruling on a related bankruptcy-stay question.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether a company that sells its products both separately and as a discounted bundle, at prices nobody claims are below its costs, can still be held to have illegally forced a customer to buy the bundle, simply because a court decides the separate price was unreasonably high compared to the bundle price.

## Where it stands

The petition was distributed for conference, and the Court then requested a response from Cumulus, due December 7, 2026. Cumulus has since asked for more time to file that response, which Nielsen opposes; the docket does not show the Court has acted on either request.

## What each outcome would mean

Nielsen, which lost in the lower courts, is asking the Court to take up and reverse the Second Circuit's decision. If the Court grants review and later rules for Nielsen, the lower courts' finding that Nielsen's pricing could count as an illegal tie would be overturned, and Cumulus's case would proceed, if at all, under a stricter legal standard. If the Court rules for Cumulus, or denies the petition, the Second Circuit's decision and the injunction against Nielsen would stand, and the case would continue in the lower courts.
