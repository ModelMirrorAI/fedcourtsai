# Evaluation of gemini-baseline

The prediction accurately forecasted a denial of certiorari. The reasoning correctly identified the legal issues and the relevant docket signals (the call for a response and the presence of amici). The predictor appropriately utilized the base rate for the elevated band pooled over the preceding 10 Terms.

While the legal analysis correctly identified the general profile of the case, it was somewhat generic compared to a deeper dive into the specific vehicle problems and factual disputes of this particular petition.

The prediction was forward. Although many log entries were unobserved, there is no evidence of leakage in the reasoning.
