# Prediction rationale

I assign a 0.71 probability to a grant-family disposition and predict a plenary grant as the most likely exact disposition. This is an arrival-moment forecast: the snapshot shows zero distributions and no CVSG, as expected before the petition has reached a conference. Those absences are not negative signals.

The principal anchor is the committed `sal-v3` federal-band risk set. Pooling the bracketed `reached` rates for the nine Terms strictly before OT2026 gives 143 grant-family outcomes among 201 resolved federal-band petitions, or 71.1%. The same pool's exact dispositions are 98 straight grants (48.8%), 45 GVRs (22.4%), 52 denials (25.9%), and 6 dismissals (3.0%). The caption identifies the petitioner as an acting ICE field-office director, and the Solicitor General represents the federal parties, so the frozen `federal` band is consistent with the visible record. I do not use the terminal relist-zero or no-CVSG cuts as arrival anchors.

I make essentially no merits adjustment from 0.711 to 0.71. The provisioned record contains no petition text, questions presented, brief in opposition, or lower-court opinion. It identifies Second Circuit No. 25-3141 and the April 28, 2026 decision but does not disclose the holding, asserted conflict, preservation, finality, or vehicle issues. A CourtListener MCP opinion search for that lower-court docket returned HTTP 429, and the contract permits no direct REST fallback. The confidence value is therefore modest: the federal-petitioner prior is strong, while the case-specific legal basis is unavailable.

The 0.96 distribution-increment probability reflects that this paid federal petition should ordinarily proceed from its current zero-distribution state to at least one conference; dismissal or another pre-distribution termination supplies most of the residual. A later CVSG is assigned 0.002 because the Solicitor General is already representing the petitioner, making an invitation for the government's views functionally unnecessary. Conditional on a grant, I assign 0.33 to a cert-order disposition: GVRs alone were 45 of the 143 grant-family outcomes in the federal-band pool, and a small additional allowance covers another judgment riding the grant order. Conditional on denial, I assign 0.05 to any noted dissent or statement because separate denial writings are unusual and the missing QP supplies no basis for a larger adjustment.

The main uncertainty is not ordinary outcome noise but missing substantive case material. The probability should be discounted as a largely class-conditional forecast rather than a vehicle-specific assessment.
