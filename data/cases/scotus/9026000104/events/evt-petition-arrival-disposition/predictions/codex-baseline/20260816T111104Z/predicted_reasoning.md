# Forecast of the Court's disposition

The Court is more likely than not to grant review, with a plenary grant the most likely exact disposition. From the present zero-distribution state, the petition will likely be distributed for at least one conference. The likeliest path is an initial distribution followed by disposition without multiple later relists, although a further relist remains plausible for a federal petition receiving serious consideration.

The Court is not expected to call for the views of the Solicitor General because the federal government, represented by the Solicitor General, is already the petitioner. If the Court grants plenary review, it will most likely take the petition's principal federal question substantially as framed, narrowing it only as needed to review the Second Circuit judgment; the available record does not supply the question's text for a more specific forecast.

Conditional on a grant, plenary review is about twice as likely as disposition in the cert order. A GVR or other summary route remains a substantial minority possibility. Conditional on denial, no Justice is likely to issue a dissent from denial or a statement respecting the denial.
