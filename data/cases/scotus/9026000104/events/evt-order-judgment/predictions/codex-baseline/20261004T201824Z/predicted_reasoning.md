# Forecast of the Court's judgment

The Court will **reverse** the Second Circuit's judgment. The predicted vote is **6–3**, with Roberts, Thomas, Alito, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh, and Barrett in the majority and Sotomayor, Kagan, and Jackson dissenting. I expect a decision following ordinary merits briefing and argument, most likely by the end of June 2027 rather than a summary disposition. Timing and authorship are not scored claims; no author is predicted.

The majority will hold that section 1225(a)(1)'s treatment of an unadmitted noncitizen as an applicant for admission brings an interior arrestee such as respondent within section 1225(b)(2)(A)'s mandatory-detention rule. It will reject the proposition that 'seeking admission' independently requires a contemporaneous effort to cross the border. On that reading, the specific detention command governs notwithstanding section 1226(a)'s general arrest-and-bond framework.

The majority's statutory rule will reach noncitizens apprehended in the interior after entry without admission regardless of how long they have resided here. It will not confine the holding to recent entrants, this respondent's asylum application, or the wording of his arrest warrant. The rule will determine the applicable statutory detention authority, not declare all conceivable detention unconstitutional or constitutional, and not decide whether respondent should ultimately be removed. I expect individualized constitutional questions about detention to remain outside the categorical statutory holding.

The dissent will distinguish the statutory fiction of being an applicant for admission from actually seeking admission. It will read the latter as a separate requirement, emphasize section 1226(a)'s unqualified reference to removal proceedings, and point to the disruptive consequences of treating long-resident interior arrestees as border applicants.

A DIG is a small alternative rather than the expected disposition. No disclosed recusal makes an equally divided affirmance a concrete alternative on the available record. Neither a relist nor a new CVSG is part of this merits forecast: certiorari has already been granted.
