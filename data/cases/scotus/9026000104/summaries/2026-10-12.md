## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked to decide which of two immigration detention laws applies to someone who entered the country without authorization years ago and has been living here since. One law allows release on bond while removal proceedings continue; the other requires detention without bond for people seeking to enter the country. The question is whether the no-bond law extends to someone already living in the country, not just people at the border.

## What each outcome would mean

The federal government, through Rhoney, asked the Court to take up this case. The grant means the Court will decide the statutory question in full, with briefing and argument, rather than leaving the issue to the circuit courts, which have split on it. If the Court ultimately affirms, Barbosa da Cunha and others like him would remain eligible for bond hearings under the lower-court ruling.
