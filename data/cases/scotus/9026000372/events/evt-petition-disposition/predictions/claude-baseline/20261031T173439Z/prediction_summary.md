The Supreme Court will very likely turn down this petition (about a 4% chance it takes the case). The PLO and Palestinian Authority want the Court to throw out a $655 million terrorism-victims judgment. A lower court revived that judgment after Congress passed a law giving U.S. courts power over them. The petitioners point to no disagreement among lower courts. The Court has already upheld that law unanimously, and the U.S. government did not even bother to file a response.
