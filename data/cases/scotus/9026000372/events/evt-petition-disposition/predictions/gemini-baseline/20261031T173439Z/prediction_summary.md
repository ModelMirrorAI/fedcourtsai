The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Palestine Liberation Organization v. Sokolow*, with only a 2% chance (one in fifty) of granting the petition. In 2025, the Court unanimously upheld the federal law establishing personal jurisdiction (a court's authority over the parties) over the Palestine Liberation Organization and Palestinian Authority, and the Second Circuit then reinstated a $655.5 million jury verdict for American terrorism victims by recalling its earlier mandate (an appellate court's formal order ending an appeal). Because both the victims and the United States waived their right to file a response, the Court already denied an emergency stay in August 2026, and no circuit split exists, the Court is unlikely to revisit the case.
