The Supreme Court is very likely to decline this appeal: I estimate a 4% chance that it agrees to review the case. The PLO and Palestinian Authority challenge the revival of a $655.5 million terrorism judgment after an earlier dismissal for lack of authority over them. That is a serious legal issue, but this unusually complex case shows no clear disagreement among federal appeals courts. Both groups of respondents waived a response. I expect a denial without an explanation or another conference discussion.
