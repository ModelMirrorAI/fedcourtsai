## In brief

The Court declined to revive the Palestine Liberation Organization's challenge to a reinstated $655.5 million terrorism judgment against it and the Palestinian Authority.

## What happened

American victims of attacks in Israel sued the Palestine Liberation Organization and the Palestinian Authority under a federal anti-terrorism law. A jury awarded them $655.5 million in 2015, but the Second Circuit later vacated that judgment for lack of personal jurisdiction over the defendants, and the Supreme Court denied review. The Palestine Liberation Organization and the Palestinian Authority then asked the Supreme Court to take the case.

## What the Court is being asked

The petitioners asked the Court to decide whether an appeals court can bring back a final judgment that was thrown out for lack of jurisdiction years earlier, when the only new jurisdiction available comes from a law Congress passed, and conduct the defendants engaged in, after that judgment had already become final.

## Where it stands

## What each outcome would mean
