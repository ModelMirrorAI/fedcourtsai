A driver convicted of operating a vehicle with THC in his blood asks the Court to hear his challenge to Michigan's impairment standard.

## What happened

Ricky Patterson was charged after a motorcycle collided with his car in Jackson, Michigan. A state trooper testified Patterson passed field sobriety tests, but the trial court found probable cause that he had operated his vehicle while under the influence of marijuana, based on THC found in his blood. Patterson argued Michigan law gives drivers no clear standard for how much THC is legally too much, and that his blood level was too low to show impairment. The Michigan Court of Appeals and the Michigan Supreme Court both declined to hear further appeal, leaving the trial court's finding in place.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether Patterson lacked fair notice of what THC level the law treats as impairment, making the finding against him unconstitutional, and whether this kind of dispute is one courts should resolve even though it might otherwise become moot before appeals finish.
