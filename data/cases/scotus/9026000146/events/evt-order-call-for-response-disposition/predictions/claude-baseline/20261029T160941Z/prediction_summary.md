The federal government asks the Supreme Court to decide whether people who win
court challenges to their immigration detention can make the government pay
their lawyers' fees under a federal fee-shifting law. A federal appeals court in
Denver said yes. We think it is fairly likely — about two chances in three —
that the Court agrees to hear the case, because the government is asking,
appeals courts disagree on the question, and it comes up in a great many
detention cases.
