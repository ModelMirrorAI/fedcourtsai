The Supreme Court is likely to hear this case: I put the chance at about
four in five, or 78%. It concerns whether people who successfully challenge
immigration detention can recover their lawyers' fees from the government.
The federal government is asking for review, and lower appeals courts disagree
about the governing law. Those features make review more likely than in a
typical petition. I expect full briefing and argument, rather than an immediate
ruling, although the petition itself was not available in the supplied record.
