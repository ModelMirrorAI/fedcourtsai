# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will grant plenary review after receiving a response. The snapshot
shows one distribution and no completed relist. Expect one rescheduling or
redistribution to a conference after the response, with one additional relist
the most plausible grant path: roughly two further distributions in total.
The next distribution is highly likely even if the Court ultimately denies.

The Court will not call for the Solicitor General's views separately; that
office already represents the petitioners. Review will focus on whether
EAJA's authorization for fees in a civil action encompasses habeas challenges
to immigration detention. This is a predicted framing inferred from the
opinion below, not a quotation of the unavailable petition's QP.

A grant followed by briefing and argument is more likely than a GVR or summary
reversal. If granted, the central interpretive dispute will concern the
statutory coverage of habeas and the clarity of the waiver of government
immunity from fees, not a ruling on detention's underlying legality.

No separate writing is the modal prediction on the cert order. If review is
denied, a dissent or statement remains unlikely; Thomas, Alito, and Gorsuch
are the leading low-probability author possibilities, with no confident
individual attribution. These are authorship forecasts, not disclosed votes.
