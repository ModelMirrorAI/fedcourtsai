# Predicted course — Baltazar v. Daley (No. 26-146)

**State forecast from:** one distribution (for the October 9, 2026 conference). No relist so far.
No CVSG. The Court requested a response on September 22, 2026, and the response is now due
November 23, 2026 after one extension.

- **Further distribution: yes, almost certainly.** The response is due November 23. Once
  the reply period runs, the petition will be redistributed, most likely for a December 2026
  or early-January 2027 conference. Expect one to two more distributions before the Court acts.
  The Court can grant on the first post-response conference, or it can relist once to check
  the record.
- **CVSG: no.** The Solicitor General is the petitioner, so there is no federal interest
  left to invite.
- **Disposition: granted (plenary review is the likelier outcome).** This is a Solicitor
  General petition seeking review of a published Tenth Circuit decision against a federal
  warden, after rehearing was denied. The Court asked for a response even though the petition
  had already gone to conference. That is the usual step before the Court acts on an SG
  petition it is taking seriously.
- **Which QP:** I expect the Court to take the government's question as written. I could not
  read the QP text (no documents were provisioned), so this is context only.
- **Summary route:** if the Court grants, I expect it to set the case for briefing and
  argument. The main alternative is a hold followed by a GVR in light of a related merits
  case this Term, if one is pending. I put that at about 1 in 5.
- **If denied:** a separate writing is unlikely but possible. A statement or dissent from a
  Justice sympathetic to the government's position is the most likely form.
