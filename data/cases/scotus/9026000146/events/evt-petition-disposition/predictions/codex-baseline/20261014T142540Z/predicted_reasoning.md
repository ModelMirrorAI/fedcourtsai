# Forecast

The Court will grant plenary review of the petition. P(any grant) is 0.80; the modal disposition is an ordinary grant for briefing and argument, not a GVR or summary reversal.

The October 14 snapshot records one distribution, entered September 16 for the October 9 conference, followed by a September 22 request for a response. The response deadline has been extended to November 23. From that one-distribution baseline, I expect two additional distribution entries: redistribution after the response and reply opportunity, then one further conference before a grant. My probability of at least one additional distribution is 0.96. Redistribution following the requested response is an increment under the declared claim, even if it is not a substantive relist after conference consideration. I expect a decision on the petition in winter 2026–27 rather than before the extended response deadline.

I expect no CVSG: the Solicitor General is already counsel for petitioners and can present the federal government's position directly. P(a new CVSG) is 0.005.

If review is granted, I expect the question to focus on whether 28 U.S.C. § 2412(d)(1)(A) permits EAJA attorney-fee awards in habeas actions challenging immigration detention, including whether its reference to civil actions unambiguously waives sovereign immunity for those proceedings. This is a forecast of the issue's substance reconstructed from the decision below, not a quotation of an unavailable petition. I expect the Court to leave the underlying entitlement to a bond hearing and the particular amount of fees outside the grant.

The grant will address the conflicting treatment of immigration habeas proceedings as civil actions eligible for EAJA fees or as hybrid proceedings outside the waiver. I expect plenary review rather than a summary resolution because that statutory disagreement requires choosing between competing approaches; the consulted record supplies no intervening decision requiring a GVR. Conditional on any grant, P(summary disposition in the cert order) is 0.08.

If the Court instead denies review, I expect no separate writing. Conditional on denial, P(any noted dissent or statement respecting denial) is 0.18. I do not predict an individual Justice's vote or authorship.
