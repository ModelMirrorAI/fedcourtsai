# Forecast

The application will be denied, most likely without a response request and without an unqualified grant of interim relief. The record currently shows zero requested responses, no referral to the full Court, and zero amicus briefs. I expect those counts to remain unchanged; there is a smaller possibility that Justice Thomas refers the application to the Court before denial, but referral is not my modal forecast.

Disposition is likely in September 2026, by a short order that does not reach the merits of any linked petition. No further amicus brief is likely to arrive. Because the application text is unavailable, this forecast does not identify a substantive ground for denial; procedurally, the expected result is failure to obtain extraordinary injunctive relief rather than partial relief or withdrawal.
