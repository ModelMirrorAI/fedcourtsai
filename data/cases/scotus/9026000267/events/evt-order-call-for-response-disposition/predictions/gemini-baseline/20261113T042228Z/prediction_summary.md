The Supreme Court is likely to turn away this appeal, with about a one-in-four chance of agreeing to hear it. Capital Group asks the Justices to enforce a workplace retirement-plan rule requiring individual arbitration—private dispute resolution outside court—and barring an employee from recovering investment losses for coworkers' 401(k) accounts. Although federal appeals courts recently split seven-to-one over that federal retirement-law question and the Justices asked the employee to respond, unusual contract wording and procedural differences make this case an imperfect test case.
