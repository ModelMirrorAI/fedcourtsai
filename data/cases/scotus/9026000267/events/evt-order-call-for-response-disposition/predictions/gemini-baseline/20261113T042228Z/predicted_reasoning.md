- **CVSG (`~14%`):** At a January 2027 Conference, the Court invites the Solicitor General to file a brief expressing the views of the United States, given the Department of Labor's administration of ERISA Title I, its prior appellate amicus support for plan participants, and the new March 2026 Fourth Circuit split.
- **Scope of grant, if granted (`~25%` overall grant probability):** If the Court grants review (either directly in early 2027 or after a CVSG), it will grant **plenary briefing and oral argument** (`~94%` conditional on grant) on the petition's single Question Presented as written—whether "appropriate relief" for a defined-contribution-plan participant under ERISA § 502(a)(2) includes monetary recovery to other participants' individual retirement accounts—without adding threshold contract or delegation questions.
