The Supreme Court is more likely to turn this case away than hear it; I give review about a three-in-ten chance. The case asks whether one retirement-plan participant can recover investment losses affecting other participants' accounts. Federal appeals courts disagree, and the Court's request for a response shows interest. But this case also involves the wording of an arbitration agreement, which could complicate resolving that broader question. I expect another scheduling step after the response, followed most likely by denial without an explanation.
