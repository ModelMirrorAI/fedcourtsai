# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition after obtaining the requested response and allowing a reply. The probability of any grant is 30%; denial is the point prediction. The Court will ordinarily issue an unexplained order, without deciding the scope of ERISA remedies on the merits.

The frozen docket shows one distribution and no CVSG. I forecast at least one additional distribution with 95% probability. The central path is one redistribution after the response-and-reply process, followed by denial at the next conference considering the completed papers. If the petition instead proceeds toward a grant, one or two further distributions beyond that redistribution are plausible. The first additional distribution need not represent a substantive relist: the existing conference was scheduled before the Court requested a response due after that conference.

A CVSG is unlikely: I assign 12% to a future invitation. If issued, it will most plausibly follow the completed cert briefing at a winter 2026–27 conference, and will extend consideration while the federal government's views are obtained.

If review is granted, the Court will most likely take the single question presented, possibly narrowed to monetary recovery under sections 502(a)(2) and 409 for losses in other participants' defined-contribution accounts. It will distinguish that statutory issue from who decides arbitrability and what this particular representative-action waiver means. No separate question about delegation is forecast to be added.

Conditional on a grant, plenary briefing and argument is the expected route: 94%, versus 6% for a GVR or other disposition in the cert order. No identified intervening decision provides the central path to a GVR. If the Court reaches the merits, the dispute will center on whether LaRue's authorization of individual-account recovery limits, or merely supplements, plan-wide recovery; that is a conditional merits issue, not a prediction that a merits opinion will issue.

Conditional on denial, there is a 6% probability of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial. No separate writing is the point forecast. Thomas or Alito are the leading low-probability candidates for a writing, with Kavanaugh and Gorsuch additional possibilities; these authorship possibilities do not assert any Justice's undisclosed cert vote.
