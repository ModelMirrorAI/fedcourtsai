The Supreme Court will probably decline to hear this case, with roughly a one-in-eight chance it agrees to take it. An investment company wants the Court to decide whether one employee suing over a retirement plan can recover money for other employees' accounts. The Court asked the employee to respond after she first declined, which shows some interest, and appeals courts disagree on the issue. But that disagreement is lopsided and new, and the case arrives at an early stage of an arbitration dispute, which makes it a less attractive candidate.
