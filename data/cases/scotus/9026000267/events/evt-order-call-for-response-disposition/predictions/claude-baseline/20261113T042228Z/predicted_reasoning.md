# Forecast — No. 26-267, Capital Group Cos. v. Pover

The docket shows one distribution (conference of Oct 9, 2026) followed by a call for a response. The brief in opposition will be filed in early December 2026, the petition will be redistributed for a January 2027 conference, and the Court will most likely deny certiorari at or shortly after that conference.

- **Further distributions:** one more is near-certain (redistribution after the response). Expect one, possibly two; a true relist after the January conference is unlikely (about one in five) and would signal real interest.
- **CVSG:** probably not (about 15%). If one issues, it will come at the first conference after the reply, in January 2027, and push the disposition to late spring 2027 or the following Term.
- **Question presented, if granted:** the petition's QP as written — whether "appropriate relief" under ERISA § 502(a)(2) for a defined-contribution participant includes monetary recovery to other participants' accounts. The Court would not add an arbitration or effective-vindication question.
- **Route, if granted:** plenary briefing and argument. A summary disposition is unlikely; the only realistic version is a hold and GVR if the Court first takes the same question in another vehicle.
- **If granted, likely ground (conditional):** a contest between reading *LaRue* as confining monetary relief to the plaintiff's own account (with Article III and Rule 23 concerns in support) and the majority-circuit reading that the plan is the real party and recovers all its losses.
- **Separate writing on a denial:** none expected.
