The Supreme Court is more likely to decline this case than hear it: I put the chance of review at about one in four, or 24%. The dispute asks whether one retirement-plan participant can recover money lost from other participants' accounts. Federal appeals courts disagree, and the Supreme Court has requested a response, both reasons to take the petition seriously. But complications involving arbitration and other available remedies could make this a poor test case. I expect another conference distribution, then a denial without explanation.
