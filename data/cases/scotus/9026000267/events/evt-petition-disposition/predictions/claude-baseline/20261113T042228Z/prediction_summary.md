The Supreme Court will probably decline to hear this case, with roughly a one-in-eight chance it agrees to. The retirement-plan company asks whether one 401(k) participant can sue to recover losses for other participants' accounts. The Court asked the other side to respond, an industry group filed a supporting brief, and one appeals court disagrees with seven others. But that disagreement is new and arose in a different procedural setting, and the Court has passed on similar disputes before. It may first ask the federal government's view.
