# Forecast: No. 26-267, The Capital Group Companies, Inc. v. Pover

**Disposition.** The Court denies certiorari (P(any grant) = 0.13).

**Further distribution.** The docket shows one distribution (for the 10/9/2026 conference), overtaken by the Court's request for a response on 10/1/2026. The response is now due 12/2/2026. The petition will be distributed again once the brief in opposition and reply are in, most likely for a January 2027 conference (P = 0.97). After that redistribution I expect it to be decided at its first real conference; a further relist beyond that is the minority path (roughly one in five), and would be the main sign of a grant or a CVSG.

**CVSG.** A call for the Solicitor General's views is possible but not expected (P = 0.13). It is the likeliest non-denial path: the question turns on ERISA's remedial provisions, the Department of Labor has a standing interest, and the Court invited the SG on a closely related ERISA-arbitration petition a few Terms ago. If it comes, it comes at the first conference after redistribution (January–February 2027).

**Question presented.** If the Court grants, it takes the single QP as written: whether "appropriate relief" under § 502(a)(2) for a defined-contribution participant includes monetary recovery to other participants' accounts.

**Route.** If granted, plenary briefing and argument, not a summary disposition (P(cert-order disposition | grant) = 0.04). No pending merits case would support a GVR.

**Separate writing.** Should the Court deny, expect no noted dissent or statement (P = 0.03).

**If granted (conditional).** The likely ground would be a reading of LaRue and Russell on whether § 409 losses "to the plan" in a defined-contribution plan are plan-wide or account-specific, with the arbitration and effective-vindication overlay left to remand.
