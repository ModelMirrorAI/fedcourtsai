The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Sprint and T-Mobile's petition for review (about a one-in-seven chance, or 14%, of a grant). Sprint and T-Mobile ask the Court to overturn or send back over $92 million in Federal Communications Commission fines for failing to safeguard customer cell-phone location data. However, the Supreme Court already upheld the constitutionality of the Commission's fine process in a nearly identical case in June 2026 (*FCC v. AT&T, Inc.*), and all three federal appeals courts to review these fines agree that cell-phone location data is protected by federal privacy law.
