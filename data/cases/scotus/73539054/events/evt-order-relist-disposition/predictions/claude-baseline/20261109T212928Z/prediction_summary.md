T-Mobile and Sprint are asking the Supreme Court to throw out about $92 million in FCC privacy fines, or at least to send the case back to the lower court in light of the Court's June 2026 decision in a similar AT&T/Verizon case. We think the Court will most likely refuse to hear the case (about a 15% chance it takes any action in the carriers' favor). The Court already refused Verizon the same kind of do-over, and the government is strongly opposed. One Justice, probably Justice Thomas, may write separately to object.
