The Chief Justice requested a response, which has now been filed along with two amicus briefs. Given the high-profile nature of the case and the institutional interests involved, I expect the application will be referred to the full Court rather than being decided by the Circuit Justice alone, and that additional amicus briefs will be filed.

I predict the Court will grant the application for a stay in full. The Court is likely to find that the government has made a strong showing of likely success on the merits, particularly regarding the district court's application of the APA and its failure to defer to the BOP's expertise in prison administration. The Court will also likely agree that the district court's order violates the PLRA by extending further than necessary and issuing relief without the requisite particularized findings. The stay will remain in effect pending the disposition of the government's appeal in the D.C. Circuit and any subsequent petition for a writ of certiorari.
