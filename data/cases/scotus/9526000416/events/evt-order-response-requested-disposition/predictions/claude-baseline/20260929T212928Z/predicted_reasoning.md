# Forecast: Trump v. Kingdom, No. 26A416 (application for a stay)

**Moment.** `response-requested`. The Solicitor General filed the application on
September 28, 2026, asking the Court to stay the District of Columbia district
court's August 26, 2026 order (a 90-day PLRA preliminary injunction plus a
§ 705 stay) that blocks the Bureau of Prisons' 2026 gender-dysphoria policy
class-wide. The same day the Chief Justice called for a response, due October
8, 2026 at 4 p.m. The record shows no referral to the full Court and no amicus
entries yet.

## What the Court will do

**Disposition: the stay will be granted in full (P = 0.72).** The Court will
stay the district court's order pending the D.C. Circuit appeal and any
petition for certiorari. The order will most likely be unsigned, with a short
per curiam or a bare order, and the three-Justice bloc of Sotomayor, Kagan and
Jackson will note a dissent, probably in a written dissent by Justice
Sotomayor or Justice Jackson. A concurrence from Justice Kavanaugh or Justice
Alito addressing the PLRA or the deference owed to prison administrators is
plausible but not the likelier shape.

The remaining mass splits roughly as follows: a **denial** (about 0.16), most
likely on the reasoning that the district court's fact-bound arbitrary-and-
capricious finding and the inmates' irreparable-harm showing (forced tapering
of hormone therapy) tip the equities, or that the 90-day injunction's own
expiry in late November makes Supreme Court intervention premature; a
**grant in part** (about 0.10) that stays the order as to sex-trait-
modification surgery and social accommodations but leaves the injunction in
place for inmates currently receiving hormones, which the interim resolver
records as ungranted; and a withdrawal or dismissal (about 0.02) if the
district court or the D.C. Circuit changes the posture first.

**Referral to the full Court: yes (P = 0.93).** An application by the
Solicitor General on a nationwide class-wide order against a headline federal
policy, with a response already requested, will be referred by the Chief
Justice and decided by the Court as a whole. The only realistic path to no
referral is a withdrawal before the response is in.

**Amicus filings: the count will rise past zero (P = 0.88).** Expect at least
two and more likely four to ten amicus entries: a coalition of Republican-led
states and a medical-policy group in support of the application, and civil-
rights, medical-association and former-corrections-official amici in support
of respondents. Every substantive application by the federal government with
a requested response in the corpus's recent window drew amici.

**Timing.** The response is due October 8; the Solicitor General will reply
within two or three days. The disposition will come between about October 14
and October 31, 2026, most likely in the week of October 20. A ruling after
the Court's early-November sitting begins would be unusual for this shape of
application but is possible if a Justice writes at length.

## Conditional merits shape (context only)

If the Court grants, the short explanation, where it gives one, will rest on
the government's likelihood of success on the APA claim under the deferential
standard of Prometheus Radio and Wages & White Lion, combined with the
deference owed to prison administrators under Bell v. Wolfish and the
irreparable harm the government suffers when a nationwide policy is enjoined
(Trump v. CASA; Trump v. Orr). It will not reach the PLRA findings question
unless it needs a narrower ground. If the Court denies, it will do so without
opinion, or with a brief statement that the fact-bound findings below and the
non-expedited appeal counsel against intervening.
