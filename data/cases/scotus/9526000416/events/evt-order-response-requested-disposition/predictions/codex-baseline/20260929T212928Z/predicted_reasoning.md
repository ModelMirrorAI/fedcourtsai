## Expected basis and principal alternative

The expected grant will emphasize the government's prospect of overturning the injunction under deferential administrative-law review and the PLRA's limits on prospective prison relief. The Court is more likely to stay enforcement while those issues are litigated than to finally resolve the constitutional adequacy of particular treatments on this application. A short order without a full merits opinion is the expected form; no per-Justice lineup is asserted.

The principal narrower alternative is relief limited to portions of the injunction, especially its coverage of surgeries, while protection for existing hormone treatment remains. A mixed grant-and-denial entry belongs to the **ungranted** side of the scored binary. The 0.68 forecast excludes that alternative rather than treating any government success as a full grant.
