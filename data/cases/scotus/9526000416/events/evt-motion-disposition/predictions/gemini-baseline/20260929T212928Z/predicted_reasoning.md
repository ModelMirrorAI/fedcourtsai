The application for a stay was submitted to the Chief Justice and the record currently shows no response requested, no referral to the full Court, and no amicus briefs filed. Given the high stakes of the case—involving a nationwide injunction against a federal prison policy regarding gender dysphoria treatment—the Chief Justice will almost certainly call for a response, and the application will be referred to the full Court for consideration. We also expect the amicus count to rise as states and civil liberties organizations weigh in. Ultimately, the Court is likely to grant the requested stay. The Court has shown a strong willingness to stay nationwide injunctions that halt executive branch policies, particularly in the context of prison administration where the executive is owed substantial deference and where the government raises significant Prison Litigation Reform Act (PLRA) concerns.
