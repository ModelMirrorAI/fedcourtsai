Two Missouri tax lawyers convicted in North Carolina ask the Supreme Court to send their case back to the appeals court. Their argument is that a June 2026 Supreme Court decision on venue (the rule about where a crime may be tried) undercuts the reasoning that kept their trial in North Carolina. We think the Court will most likely refuse: about a one-in-ten chance it sends the case back. The appeals court already saw that decision when it refused to rehear the case, and the government did not even file a response.
