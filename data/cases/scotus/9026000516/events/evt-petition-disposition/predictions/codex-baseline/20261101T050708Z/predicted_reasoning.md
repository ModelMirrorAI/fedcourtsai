# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely **deny the petition without a separate writing**. My probability of any grant is 12%. A denial would not supply a majority rationale or endorse the lower court's venue analysis.

The docket currently contains **one distribution**, for the November 13, 2026 conference, and no relist. I expect **zero additional distributions**, with a 20% chance of at least one more. If additional proceedings occur, one or two further distributions are the most plausible extension, particularly after an ordinary request that the United States respond. The modal disposition window is November 16 through early December 2026; a requested response would likely move resolution into winter 2026–2027. This timing forecast is not a separate scored claim.

No CVSG is recorded. I expect none to be called, assigning only 0.1% to that increment. An order requesting an ordinary opposition from the United States, already the respondent, would not be a CVSG.

**If the Court grants relief**, it will most likely grant, vacate, and remand for the Fourth Circuit to reconsider venue under **Abouammo**. Conditional on any grant, I assign 96% to disposition in the cert order itself rather than a new round of merits briefing and argument. The requested GVR is substantially more likely than a summary reversal resolving the tax-statute venue issue outright.

If review is granted, the Court will most likely take the single question presented in its existing, limited form: whether the Fourth Circuit should reconsider the § 7206(2) convictions in light of Abouammo. A remand would leave the lower court to distinguish the defendants' own prohibited assistance from third-party acts, conspiracy activity, and effects, including on the counts involving Simmons's returns. It would not itself establish that every challenged conviction must be overturned or disturb the conspiracy count's venue.

Conditional on denial, I assign 3% to a noted dissent or statement respecting denial. The forecast is no writing by any Justice; if one appears, Thomas or Gorsuch is a somewhat more plausible author than most colleagues. The structured `order-writers` probabilities express unconditional, individual chances of writing, not votes or probabilities that someone merely joins another Justice's opinion.
