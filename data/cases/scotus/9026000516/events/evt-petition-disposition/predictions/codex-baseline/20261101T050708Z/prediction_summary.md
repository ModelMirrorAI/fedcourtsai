The Supreme Court is very likely to decline this appeal; I put the chance of any favorable review at about one in eight. Two tax lawyers argue that a new Supreme Court decision requires another look at where they could lawfully be tried. But their tax offenses involve different conduct, the appeals court already had a chance to reconsider, and the government waived its response. If the Court intervenes, it will probably send the case back for reconsideration rather than hear full arguments.
