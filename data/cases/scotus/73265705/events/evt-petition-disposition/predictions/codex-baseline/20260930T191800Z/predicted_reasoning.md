# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny Google's petition for certiorari. The most likely course is a routine denial following the October 16, 2026 conference, without another distribution and without a separate writing. This forecasts a decision not to review the case, not a merits endorsement of the PTO's settled-expectations policy.

The snapshot records two distributions: June 9 for the June 25 conference and September 30 for the October 16 conference. The intervening response request and briefing extensions make this look like redistribution after completion of requested briefing, not two completed substantive conference considerations. From the recorded count of two, the probability of at least one additional distribution is 24%. Zero more is the modal forecast; one more is the principal alternative, rather than a long series of relists.

No CVSG appears in the snapshot, and none is expected. The Solicitor General has already supplied the federal respondent's opposition; a request for that party's response is not a CVSG. The probability of a new CVSG is 0.5%.

If review is granted, plenary consideration is more likely than disposition in the cert order: the conditional summary-route probability is 12%. The likely focus would be the second question, narrowed to whether mandamus can review a denial of inter partes review alleged to rest on a statutorily impermissible factor. The patent-age question would supply the concrete setting, rather than an occasion to announce an unrestricted entitlement to institution. If the Court reaches that issue, the likely inquiry is whether patent age may be considered at all or instead operates as an unlawful categorical bar. No identified intervening decision makes a GVR the leading grant route.

Conditional on denial, the probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial is 16%; no separate writing is the forecast. A statement, if one occurs, would most likely address the availability of judicial review of allegedly unlawful agency action. No particular Justice or individual cert vote is forecast.
