Distributed twice so far (one relist); expect it will not be relisted further and will be denied. The petition presents a clear APA challenge to the PTO's "settled expectations" basis for denying inter partes review, raising a conflict over whether 35 U.S.C. 314(d) bars judicial review of such discretionary rules. However, the Court has repeatedly interpreted 314(d) broadly to preclude review of institution decisions (e.g., Cuozzo, Thryv), making a grant unlikely despite the tech industry's interest. A CVSG is possible given the challenge to PTO authority, but less likely after a relist has already occurred. If granted, the Court would likely take the first question on statutory authority and could issue a summary GVR if a related challenge succeeds elsewhere, though plenary review is the standard route for such jurisdictional and administrative law questions. Should it deny, a dissent from denial is possible from Justices concerned with unreviewable agency discretion, such as Justice Gorsuch.
