The Supreme Court is virtually certain to deny review in *Don Hou v. Commissioner of Internal Revenue*, with well under a one-percent chance (about one in three hundred) of taking the case. Both lower courts dismissed this self-represented tax-whistleblower lawsuit solely because the Tax Court lacked jurisdiction—legal authority to hear the dispute—after the Internal Revenue Service rejected the tip at the outset without auditing any taxpayer, yet Hou's petition argues only unreached tax-collection issues, and the government waived a response.
