The Supreme Court will almost certainly decline to hear this case, with well under a one-in-a-hundred chance of review. A man representing himself wants a reward for reporting alleged tax violations to the IRS. The appeals court ruled in a short, unpublished order that the Tax Court had no power to hear his claim because the IRS never opened a case based on his tip. The government did not even file a response, and the petition shows no disagreement among lower courts for the Justices to resolve.
