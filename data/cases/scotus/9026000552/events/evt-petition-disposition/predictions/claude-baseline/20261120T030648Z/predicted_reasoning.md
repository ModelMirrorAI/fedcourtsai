# Forecast: Hou v. Commissioner of Internal Revenue, No. 26-552

The docket shows one distribution (for the conference of December 4, 2026), made on the Solicitor General's waiver of a response. The Court will deny the petition on the order list following that conference, most likely December 7, 2026.

- **Relist:** none. The petition is decided at its first conference. The only route to another distribution is a Justice calling for a response from the Commissioner, which will not happen here.
- **CVSG:** none. The United States is already the respondent, so there is nothing to invite.
- **Question presented:** the Court takes none. The three questions argue the meaning of "collected proceeds" under 26 U.S.C. § 7623(b)–(c) and agency deference, but the judgment below rests on a different ground: the Tax Court lacked jurisdiction because the IRS never commenced an administrative or judicial action on the claim. If the Court were ever to take the case, it would have to reformulate the question to that jurisdictional holding (the rule of *Li v. Commissioner*, D.C. Cir. 2022).
- **Summary disposition:** no intervening decision points to a GVR. In the remote event of a grant, a summary remand would be about as likely as plenary review.
- **Separate writing:** none. A plain, unsigned denial with no noted dissent or statement.
