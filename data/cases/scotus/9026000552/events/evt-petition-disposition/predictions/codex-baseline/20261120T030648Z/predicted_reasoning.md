The Court will likely deny Hou’s petition after the December 4 conference. The November 17 distribution is the first. I expect zero further distributions, no request for the Commissioner to respond, and no separate writing or noted dissent on denial. The Solicitor General already represents the Commissioner, so I expect no invitation to file as a friend of the Court.

If the Court grants review, I expect it to narrow Hou’s third question to whether the Tax Court may review the IRS’s rejection of a whistleblower claim when the IRS commenced no enforcement action against the identified taxpayers. Either answer would address the ground of dismissal. The Court could leave open which corporate payments qualify as collected proceeds under the first two questions. Full briefing and argument are more likely than a summary order on that jurisdictional issue.
