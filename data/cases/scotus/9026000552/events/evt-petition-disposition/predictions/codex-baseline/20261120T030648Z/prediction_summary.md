The Supreme Court is very likely to turn down Don Hou’s petition. I put the chance that it grants review at about one in 500. Hou says a company’s tax payments show that his tip earned him a whistleblower award. The appeals court found that the IRS had taken no enforcement action against the taxpayers he identified. Even if the justices agreed with Hou about which payments count toward an award, that finding would still stand in his way.
