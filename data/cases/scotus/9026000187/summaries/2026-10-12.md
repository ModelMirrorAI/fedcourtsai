## What happened

Chasity Congious was a pretrial detainee at the Tarrant County Jail when she gave birth alone in her cell; the baby, stuck in her clothing, died ten days later. ## What the Court is being asked

Federal appeals courts disagree about how to judge claims like this one, brought by people jailed but not yet convicted. Some courts ask only whether the official actually realized the person faced a serious risk and ignored it. Others ask instead whether the official's actions were unreasonable judged from the outside, regardless of what was in the official's head. The Court is asked to decide which of these two approaches governs claims that jail medical staff failed to provide needed care to a pretrial detainee.
