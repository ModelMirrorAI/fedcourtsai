We predict the Supreme Court will most likely turn this case down: about a one-in-twenty chance it agrees to hear it. The case asks whether jail officials who deny a pretrial detainee medical care are judged by what they actually knew or by what they should have known, a question on which the federal appeals courts disagree. The Court asked the doctor to respond, which shows some interest. But the doctor also won on a separate legal shield for officials (qualified immunity), which makes this a poor case for settling the disagreement.
