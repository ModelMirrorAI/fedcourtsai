# Forecast: what the Court will do with No. 26-187, Congious v. Shaw

**Disposition.** The petition will be denied, most likely on the order list following a January 2027 conference. P(any grant) = 0.08.

**Distributions.** The docket shows one distribution (for the October 16, 2026 conference), and the Court then called for a response on October 5. The petition will not be considered on October 16. Expect: a brief in opposition on or about November 4, 2026, or 30 days later if respondent seeks the routine extension both prior petitions on this question obtained; a short reply; then redistribution for a conference in December 2026 or January 2027. That redistribution is near-certain (0.96), and is a mechanical consequence of the response request rather than a relist. Beyond it, expect at most one further relist: the two prior petitions on this question that drew a response request (23-210 and 23-259) were each decided at the first conference after redistribution.

**CVSG.** None (0.02). No federal party, no federal statute, and the Court has never invited the Solicitor General's views on this split across the four prior petitions presenting it.

**Which question, if granted.** The petition's single QP as written, since it is already the clean form of the split: whether a pretrial detainee's Fourteenth Amendment medical-care claim is governed by the subjective deliberate-indifference standard (CA5, CA8, CA10, CA11) or by Kingsley's objective standard (CA2, CA4, CA6, CA7, CA9). The Court would not reformulate it; it might limit the grant to that question and leave the summary-judgment record alone. If granted, the likely ground is a holding on the Kingsley question rather than on these facts, with qualified immunity on remand remaining open; the Court would not reach whether Dr. Shaw's conduct was objectively unreasonable.

**Summary disposition.** Unlikely conditional on a grant (0.15). No intervening decision exists to GVR against, and the Court would not resolve a 4–5 split by summary reversal. The residual probability covers the Court granting a cleaner vehicle on the same question this Term, holding this petition, and GVR-ing it afterward; no such vehicle surfaced in retrieval.

**Dissent from denial.** Expect none noted (0.10). The three prior denials and the dismissal on this question drew no noted dissent or statement. The facts here are unusually sympathetic, so a short statement respecting denial from Justice Sotomayor about jail medical care is the live possibility, not a dissent urging a grant.

**Timing.** Final order most likely between late January and February 2027.
