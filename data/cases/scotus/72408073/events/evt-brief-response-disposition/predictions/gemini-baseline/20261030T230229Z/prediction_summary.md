We predict that the Supreme Court will **deny** Steven Lynn Long's application for a stay of execution (a court order pausing his scheduled execution while the justices consider his appeal), with about a **one in eight (12%)** chance of an unqualified grant. Although the Supreme Court recently struck down parts of Texas's legal standard for assessing intellectual disability in death-penalty cases (*Moore v. Texas*), the lower federal appeals court denied Long's appeal primarily because multiple experts concluded he intentionally underperformed ("malingered") on his IQ tests. Because federal law requires strong deference to state-court factual findings like malingering, the Court is unlikely to halt the execution, though Justice Alito is very likely (78%) to refer the application to all nine justices before a final order issues.
