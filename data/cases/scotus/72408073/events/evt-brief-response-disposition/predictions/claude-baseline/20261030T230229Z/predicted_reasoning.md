# Forecast — 17A145, Long v. Davis (stay of execution)

**Call: denied.** P(an unqualified grant of the stay) = 0.04.

- **The record so far.** A capital applicant in Texas asked on 31 July 2017 for a stay of execution of his death sentence, and the application went to Justice Alito as Circuit Justice for the Fifth Circuit. It is linked to his cert petition No. 16-8909, which seeks review of the CA5 judgment in No. 16-70010 (federal habeas). On 10 August 2017 Texas filed a response in opposition. The docket shows no call for a response before that filing, no referral to the full Court, and no amicus briefs.
- **Disposition.** I expect the Court to deny the stay. The likeliest timing is together with its action on the linked cert petition. If an execution date is close, it may come sooner, by order of the full Court after referral. A stay of execution pending cert needs a reasonable probability that four Justices grant review, a fair prospect of reversal, and irreparable harm. Texas habeas petitioners in the CA5 posture almost never clear that bar.
- **Response requested (increment).** Very unlikely (0.02), because Texas has already filed its opposition and there is nothing left to call for.
- **Referral to the full Court (increment).** Likely (0.6). Circuit Justices routinely refer capital stay applications to the Court. The main way this does not happen is the stay being denied as moot, or disposed of along with the cert denial without a separate referral entry.
- **Amicus (increment).** Very unlikely (0.03).
- **Separate writing.** Most likely no Justice writes. If one does, the likeliest are Justice Sotomayor or Justice Breyer (Breyer's recurring statements about the death penalty), with Justice Ginsburg next.
