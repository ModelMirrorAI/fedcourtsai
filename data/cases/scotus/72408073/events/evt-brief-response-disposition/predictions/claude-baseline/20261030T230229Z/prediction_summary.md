A Texas death-row prisoner asked the Supreme Court to pause (stay) his execution while it considers his separate request to review his case. Texas opposed. We predict the Court will **deny** the stay. We put the chance of a full grant at about 4 in 100. The Supreme Court very rarely grants stays of execution, especially in Texas federal habeas cases from the Fifth Circuit. Nothing on the docket so far shows the Justices paying special attention to this one.
