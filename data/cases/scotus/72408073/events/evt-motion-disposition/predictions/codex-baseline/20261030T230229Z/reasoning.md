The 2017 roster, rather than today's roster, governs the writing distribution.

This clears the 50-resolution floor but is not ten years of uniformly observed applications. I use neither the all-Term 11.6% rate nor the 2017 row, and do not use cert bands or caption-class floors.

My 8% probability stays close to that prior-Term anchor. There is too little application-specific evidence to make a large merits adjustment. ## Legal and procedural judgments

Barefoot v. Estelle, 463 U.S. 880, 895–96 (1983), explains that an execution stay pending Supreme Court review is not automatic: the applicant must establish sufficient prospects of review and reversal as well as irreparable harm. I verified that passage through CourtListener opinion 111017, searching for “reasonable probability.” It is general context, not evidence of the grounds or procedural posture of this particular application. The snapshot's linked petition suggests related review proceedings, but I cannot determine their state and do not infer a cert denial or a procedural bar.

The 55% response-request forecast reflects a plausible request for the state's position before acting on irreversible relief, balanced against the possibility of immediate action without an express request. The 80% referral forecast reflects my judgment that a capital application is likely to reach the full Court; submission to Alito is not already a referral. The 8% amicus-increment forecast reflects the compressed likely schedule and absence of identified broader issues, not any assertion about support outside this record. These probabilities are judgmental because the pack publishes no conditioned increment baselines. Referral can occur without a new response request, so its probability can properly be higher.

The writing probabilities are independent, not a distribution summing to one. I put the greatest, still modest, chances on Breyer and Sotomayor based on general pre-boundary capital-case writing tendencies; no application-specific issue supports a confident authorship forecast. I omit a vote block rather than fabricate a lineup. The stakes score of 0.45 separates the gravity of a potential execution from unestablished national doctrinal significance.
