# Forecast — 17A145, Long v. Davis (stay of execution)

The record shows one docket entry: on Jul 31 2017 the application for a stay of execution of a death sentence was submitted to Justice Alito, Circuit Justice for the Fifth Circuit. At this point no response has been called for, the application has not been referred, and no amicus brief has been filed. The application is linked with cert petition 16-8909, which comes from the Fifth Circuit's capital habeas case No. 16-70010.

- **Disposition:** I expect the stay to be **denied**, most likely in the same order that disposes of the linked petition 16-8909 or shortly before it. P(unqualified grant) ≈ 3%.
- **Response:** I expect Texas to file a response, because it routinely does in capital stay matters. Whether a response is formally *called for* is less likely than not (≈35%), since the State usually answers without being asked.
- **Referral:** Justice Alito, like other Circuit Justices, usually refers capital stay applications to the full Court instead of acting alone. I put referral at ≈75%.
- **Amicus:** I expect no amicus brief (≈3%).
- **Timing:** Disposition should come within a few weeks, tied to the conference that takes up 16-8909 or to any execution date, whichever is sooner.
- **Separate writing:** Most likely none. If a Justice writes, the likeliest is a short statement or dissent from Justice Breyer (on his long-running concerns about the death penalty) or Justice Sotomayor.
