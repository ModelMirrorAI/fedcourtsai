Steven Long, a Texas death-row prisoner, asked the Supreme Court in July 2017 to pause (stay) his execution while it considers his separate petition for review of a federal appeals court ruling. We predict the Court will **deny** the stay; we put the chance of it being granted at about 3 in 100. The Court rarely stops a Texas execution unless it plans to take up the case, and nothing on the docket yet suggests it will take this one.
