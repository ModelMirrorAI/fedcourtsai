## In brief

The Court declined to hear a policyholder's challenge to Ohio courts applying a shortened filing deadline to his insurance lawsuit.

## What happened

Naren Chaganti sued Cincinnati Insurance Company in 2024 for breach of a business insurance contract covering damage that occurred in 2010. At the time, Ohio's statute of limitations for such suits was 15 years, but a 2012 law shortened it to 8 years. An uncodified part of that law, not printed alongside the main statute, said the shorter period applied even to claims that arose before 2012. Ohio trial and appellate courts sided with Cincinnati and dismissed Chaganti's suit as filed too late. The Ohio Supreme Court declined to review the case.

## What the Court is being asked

Chaganti asks whether it violates constitutional fair-notice requirements for a state to rely on a provision that was passed as part of a bill but never printed in the official law books, to apply a shortened filing deadline retroactively to claims that already existed before the new deadline took effect.

## Where it stands
