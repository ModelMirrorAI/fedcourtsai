## What happened

Chaganti held a business insurance policy with Cincinnati Insurance Company covering property damage from 2010. He sued Cincinnati in 2024 for breach of contract. Cincinnati argued the suit was too late, relying on a 2012 law that shortened Ohio's contract statute of limitations from fifteen to eight years, with a separate, uncodified provision in the enacting Senate Bill applying that shorter period retroactively to older claims. The trial court agreed and dismissed the case. The Ohio Court of Appeals affirmed, and the Ohio Supreme Court declined to take the case further.

## What the Court is being asked

Chaganti asks the Court to decide whether a state can shorten the time allowed to sue, and apply that shorter deadline to claims that arose years earlier, based on a provision that appears only in the legislature's session bill rather than in the published, codified statute itself. He argues that ordinary people and even lawyers would only check the official code, not old legislative bills, so relying on the uncodified provision denies the fair notice that due process requires.
