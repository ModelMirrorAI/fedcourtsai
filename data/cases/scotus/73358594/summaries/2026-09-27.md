## In brief

A former USDA employee asks the Court to review whether the whistleblower board and appeals court had to rule on both legal bases for her retaliation claim.

## What happened

Tomasa Gabriella Bolanos-Reynoso, a former Department of Agriculture employee, says she reported financial mismanagement and possible legal violations in her office. She claims she was later demoted and given poor performance ratings in retaliation. She pursued a claim under the Whistleblower Protection Act before the Merit Systems Protection Board, arguing her disclosures reasonably showed both a violation of law and gross mismanagement. The Board addressed only the legal-violation theory, ruled against her, and the Federal Circuit affirmed without a written opinion and denied rehearing.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether an employee who reports wrongdoing under two different protected categories is entitled to have a decision addressing both categories, or whether reviewers may rule against the claim by analyzing only one of them.

## Where it stands
