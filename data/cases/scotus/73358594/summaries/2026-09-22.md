## What happened

She says she reported financial mismanagement and possible legal violations there, and that she was then demoted and given poor performance ratings in retaliation. She pursued relief under the federal whistleblower protection law before the Merit Systems Protection Board, which handles federal employee disputes. ## What the Court is being asked

Bolanos-Reynoso raised concerns fitting both categories, but the Board only evaluated whether her claim counted as a legal violation and never addressed whether it separately qualified as gross mismanagement. The Court is asked whether an employee who raises both types of protected concerns is entitled to have the Board actually decide both, rather than skip one entirely.
