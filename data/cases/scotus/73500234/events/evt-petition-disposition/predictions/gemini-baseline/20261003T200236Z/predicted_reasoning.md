# Predicted Reasoning

Distributed once so far; expect no further relists before a decision on the petition. The Court will likely deny the petition because the questions presented involve fact-bound applications of the law of the case doctrine and the Quiet Title Act's discovery rule on remand. There is no CVSG expected, as the United States is already a party and has filed its opposition. 

If granted, the Court would likely focus on the second question presented regarding the QTA discovery rule and whether actions of private citizens can trigger the statute of limitations. A summary disposition is highly unlikely given the lack of any clear intervening Supreme Court precedent. Should the Court deny the petition, expect no separate writing or dissent from denial, as the case does not present a prominent doctrinal dispute.
