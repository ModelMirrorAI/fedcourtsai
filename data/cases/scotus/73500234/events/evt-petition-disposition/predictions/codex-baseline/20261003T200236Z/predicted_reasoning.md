# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny the petition, most likely without a separate writing, on an order list following the scheduled October 9, 2026 conference. My central timing forecast is October 2026, not a prolonged hold.

The snapshot contains one distribution, entered September 16 for that conference, and no CVSG. I expect no further distribution. The probability of at least one additional distribution is 20%; if additional consideration occurs, one or two further distributions are the most plausible path. I assign a 0.5% probability to a new CVSG: the United States is already a respondent and its Solicitor General has supplied a brief in opposition.

The principal obstacle to review will be vehicle quality. The government's independent-ground argument makes the first question, about law of the case after this Court's reversal, unlikely to justify review by itself. The second question will more likely be treated as a dispute over what longstanding public use of this particular easement conveyed to a reasonable landowner, rather than a clean conflict over Quiet Title Act accrual.

If the Court grants plenary review, I expect it to focus on the second question, narrowed to whether public use of a federal easement over private land gives constructive notice of the United States' adverse claim without an affirmative governmental assertion. I do not expect a categorical question about all unlawful acts of private citizens to be the final formulation. A grant addressing the first question would need to confront the Ninth Circuit's asserted alternative de novo ground as well.

Conditional on any grant, I assign 45% to disposition in the cert order itself and 55% to further briefing and argument. The summary path would principally be a corrective vacatur or reversal addressing the handling of this Court's earlier remand, not a routine GVR based on a newly identified intervening decision. Plenary review is slightly more likely within the small grant branch because resolving the alternative accrual ground would require more than correcting a label attached to the earlier findings.

Conditional on denial, I assign 5% to a noted dissent or statement respecting denial. My forecast is that none appears; I make no individual-Justice vote or authorship forecast. A denial will not itself explain the Court's view of either question or decide the easement's lawful scope.
