Justice Kavanaugh has already requested a response, indicating that the application is receiving serious consideration. Given the acknowledged circuit split on the standard of review for pretrial detention orders under the Bail Reform Act and the dissenting opinion in the Sixth Circuit, it is highly likely that Justice Kavanaugh will refer the application to the full Court.

However, the full Court will likely deny the application for a stay. While the circuit split on the standard of review (de novo vs. clear error vs. intermediate deference) might warrant certiorari in the ordinary course, a stay of a pretrial detention order requires both a likelihood of reversal and a showing of irreparable harm that outweighs the government's interest. Given the allegations of violent threats against government supporters and the government's strong position on the defendant's dangerousness, the Court is unlikely to find that the equities favor a stay, or that the district court's release order would necessarily be reinstated even under a more deferential standard of review. Therefore, expect the Court to deny the stay application.
