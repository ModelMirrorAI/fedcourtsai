I adjust upward from that baseline because the United States is the petitioner and the Ninth Circuit displaced a district-court dismissal through a novel application of APA finality. The government's strongest argument is narrow: a permit application submitted to a different decisionmaker ordinarily begins, rather than completes, the operative process, and the ultimate legal consequences depend on Guam EPA's later grant, conditioning, or denial. Franklin, Dalton, Bennett, and the text of the permitting scheme give the Court a route to reverse without deciding the broader and harder NEPA/RCRA question. Avoidance also matters because the case concerns pre-2023 NEPA and the statutory interaction question divides the parties over different actors, different timing, and no square conflict. I do not use the petition's pre-grant distribution history or salience band; those cert-stage signals are spent and say nothing about the merits judgment.

I stop at 0.88 because respondent has a serious second-prong argument. Timely filing kept the 2018 permit in force and allowed operations to continue, and respondent plausibly characterizes the application as the consummation of the Air Force's own choice of location and disposal method. Hawkes and Sackett also support a pragmatic approach to legal consequences. Those points leave meaningful undisturbed risk through affirmance, a DIG, or an equally divided Court. Separately, a vacatur-and-remand remains possible within the 0.88 disturbed probability and is the main uncertainty about the exact `reversed` label.

The 7-2 lineup is less certain than the judgment. The conservative bloc is likeliest to accept the government's finality framing, and Justice Kagan is likely to join an administrable, narrow application of Bennett, Franklin, and Dalton that does not decide the NEPA question. Justices Sotomayor and Jackson are likeliest to credit the immediate permit-extension effect and preserve APA review. Justice Gorsuch's solicitude for APA access could move him toward dissent, while either predicted dissenter could accept the separate-regulator distinction. I therefore place more confidence in reversal than in the precise split.
