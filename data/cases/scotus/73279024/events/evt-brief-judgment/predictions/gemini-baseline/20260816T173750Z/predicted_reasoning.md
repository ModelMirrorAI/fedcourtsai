The Court will reverse the Ninth Circuit's decision. The majority will hold that the mere submission of a RCRA permit-renewal application by the Air Force to a territorial regulator does not constitute "final agency action" under the APA (5 U.S.C. 704). Applying *Bennett v. Spear*, the Court will likely reason that an application is a preliminary step that does not mark the consummation of the agency's decisionmaking process, and from which no legal rights or obligations directly flow. Because there is no final agency action, the Court will find the APA challenge unreviewable, meaning the Air Force was not required to comply with NEPA prior to submitting the application. We expect a 6-3 decision along ideological lines. Justices Sotomayor, Kagan, and Jackson are likely to dissent, arguing that the Air Force's decision to pursue the permit renewal without conducting a NEPA review has immediate, practical environmental consequences that warrant judicial review.
