The Supreme Court is almost certain to deny this petition for review, with a grant probability of half of one percent (about 1 in 200). After a federal judge ordered the petitioner, who is representing himself without a lawyer, to resolve his dispute with Citigroup in arbitration, the petitioner refused to start arbitration for more than seven months, leading the judge to dismiss the lawsuit for failure to prosecute (failing to move the case forward) and the appeals court to dismiss his appeal as frivolous (lacking any arguable legal basis). Because Citigroup waived its right to file a response, no outside groups filed supporting briefs, and the case involves a fact-specific procedural dispute rather than a disagreement among federal appeals courts, the Supreme Court will almost certainly decline to hear it.
