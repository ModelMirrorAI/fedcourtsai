# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition without an explanation or separate writing, leaving the Second Circuit's order in place without endorsing its reasoning.

The record already shows **one distribution**, for the December 4, 2026 conference. I expect no additional distribution and a denial on an order list shortly after that conference, most plausibly in December 2026. The probability of at least one further distribution is 9%; if the matter is redistributed, one additional distribution is more likely than a prolonged series. A request for a response would be the principal reason for a longer schedule, but is not my modal forecast.

No CVSG appears on the record. I predict none will be issued; the probability of a new invitation is 0.1%. This private dispute over appellate screening offers little reason to solicit an institutional federal position.

If review is granted, the Court will most likely address only whether the court of appeals could dismiss the final Rule 41(b) appeal as frivolous without prior notice while deciding a motion seeking partial dismissal on another ground. It will not take up the validity of the arbitration agreement or undo the separate nonmerger ruling. The grant probability, including summary relief, is 1.8%.

Conditional on a grant, the probability of resolving the matter in the cert order is 80%, rather than ordering full merits briefing and argument. The likely grant-side action is a short summary procedural correction vacating or reversing the final-appeal portion of the order and returning that portion for further proceedings. That would not predetermine the eventual result of the appeal. No identified intervening decision makes a hold followed by an intervening-precedent GVR the expected route.

Conditional on denial, the probability of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial is 2%. I expect none. If a Justice does write, Thomas or Gorsuch are the comparatively likelier authors in my low-probability distribution; these are not predictions of a majority opinion or of recorded individual cert votes.
