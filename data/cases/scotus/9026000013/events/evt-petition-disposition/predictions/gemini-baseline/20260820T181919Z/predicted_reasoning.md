Distributed once so far; expect one or two relists before a decision, given the immense stakes of the 9/11 military commission and the likelihood that some Justices will closely scrutinize the D.C. Circuit's aggressive use of mandamus in a criminal posture. No CVSG is possible, as the United States is already the respondent. If granted, the Court would likely take both QPs to address the scope of mandamus under the All Writs Act when the government lacks an interlocutory appeal right. A summary disposition is highly unlikely given the lack of intervening precedent and the magnitude of the case; plenary review would be required. Should the Court deny review—allowing the government to proceed with a capital trial by leaving the D.C. Circuit's writ intact—expect a dissent from denial by one or more of the liberal Justices highlighting the circumvention of ordinary appellate jurisdiction.
