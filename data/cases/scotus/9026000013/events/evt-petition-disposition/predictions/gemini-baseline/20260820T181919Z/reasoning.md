P(grant) 0.08. The petition is in the 'baseline' salience band (with a prior-Term reached rate of ~6-7% for paid petitions). I adjust this up slightly due to the monumental stakes—this is the 9/11 military commission—and the presence of a strong jurisdictional question regarding the government's use of mandamus to bypass the lack of an interlocutory appeal, accompanied by a well-reasoned dissent from Judge Wilkins in the D.C. Circuit. However, I heavily discount the chance of a grant because the conservative majority on the Court is highly unlikely to intervene to reinstate plea deals for KSM and his co-defendants that eliminate the death penalty. Denying certiorari allows the D.C. Circuit's writ to stand and the capital trial to proceed, which aligns with likely majority preferences without requiring a merits ruling on a thorny jurisdictional issue. The U.S. is already the respondent, so CVSG is impossible. Relists are plausible (0.25) simply for the Court to process the high-profile denial or for dissents to be drafted. If denied, a dissent from denial is very likely (0.60) given the procedural irregularities and capital context.
