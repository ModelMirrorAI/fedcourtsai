Distributed once so far; expect no further relists before a decision on the petition. The case is in an interlocutory posture, as petitioners seek to challenge the D.C. Circuit's writ of mandamus that reinstated their capital trial by validating the Secretary of Defense's withdrawal from their plea agreements. The Court typically waits for final judgment in military commission proceedings before granting review, as petitioners can raise this claim on post-conviction appeal. No CVSG will occur, as the United States is already a party and the Solicitor General filed the brief in opposition. If granted, the Court would take QP 1 regarding the scope of mandamus jurisdiction under the All Writs Act. A summary disposition is highly unlikely as there is no intervening authority. Should it deny, there is a fair chance of a dissent from denial by Justices Sotomayor or Jackson regarding the D.C. Circuit's aggressive use of mandamus.
