# Forecast: what the Court will do with Application 25A622

**Disposition.** The application for a stay will be **denied**, in full and without
opinion. The disposing entry will read either "Application (25A622) denied by Justice
Kagan" or, if referred, "Application (25A622) denied by the Court." No Justice will
note a dissent. P(unqualified grant) = 0.01.

**Timing.** Disposition within roughly one to three weeks of the November 18, 2025
submission — a single-Justice denial typically lands inside a week; a referral adds
one or two weeks while the full Court considers it on the papers.

**Response requested.** No. The record shows no response called for, and the
applicant is a pro se litigant seeking relief against a federal district court, the
kind of application a Circuit Justice disposes of on the applicant's papers alone.
P(a response is called for after prediction time) = 0.04.

**Referral to the full Court.** Uncertain but less likely than not. Justice Kagan
regularly denies pro se stay applications in chambers; the two routes to a referral
are her choosing to refer this one on receipt, or a single-Justice denial followed by
the applicant renewing the application to another Justice, which by the Court's
practice is referred to the full Court under the same docket number. Together I put
that at 0.35. (The record shows `referred_to_court: false`.)

**Amicus filings.** None. The record shows zero amicus entries; a pro se application
naming a district court as respondent draws no outside filers. P(the count rises
above 0) = 0.01.

**Grounds, conditionally.** A stay would require a reasonable probability that four
Justices would grant certiorari (or, given the mandamus posture, grant an
extraordinary writ), a fair prospect of reversal, and irreparable harm. None of those
is remotely satisfied on the visible record: the respondent is the district court
itself, so the underlying Ninth Circuit matter (No. 25-2374) is a mandamus petition,
a class of relief the Court grants essentially never, and there is no indication of
any question of general importance. If, against expectation, a stay were granted, it
would be a narrow administrative stay pending a certiorari petition, not a merits
signal.
