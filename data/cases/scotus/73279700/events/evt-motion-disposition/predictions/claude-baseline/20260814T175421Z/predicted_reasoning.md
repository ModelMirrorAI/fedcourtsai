# Forecast — Watkins v. United States District Court for the District of Arizona, No. 25A622

**Disposition: the application will be denied, without an unqualified grant of any relief.**

The resolvable procedural claims for this interim cell:

- **No response will be called for.** The docket shows none requested in the
  roughly nine months since the application was submitted to Justice Kagan on
  November 18, 2025 (docketed November 25). A request for a response is an
  affirmative act of attention the Court takes early when it takes it at all;
  its continued absence at this age is close to dispositive. P(response
  requested before disposition) ≈ 0.05.
- **No referral to the full Court.** A pro se stay application naming the
  district court itself as respondent is the classic single-Justice denial.
  Some Circuit Justices refer even weak applications as a courtesy, so this is
  not certain, but the nine months of silence cut against any sign the
  application is being treated as substantial. P(referred) ≈ 0.15.
- **No amicus participation.** None is on the docket and none is plausible for
  a dispute of this shape.
- **Form and timing of disposition.** Expect a one-line denial by Justice
  Kagan (or a routine denial after referral, if referred), with no noted
  dissent and no opinion. Timing is the genuinely uncertain part: the
  application has already sat unusually long, which is consistent with a
  defective or low-priority filing awaiting a batch disposition. A denial
  whenever the Court next acts on it is the modal path; a small residual goes
  to the application being dismissed or dying on the docket without a recorded
  disposition.

Merits-shaped content, conditionally: were the Court somehow to engage, the
question would be whether a stay of district-court proceedings pending review
of a Ninth Circuit mandamus denial (CA9 No. 25-2374) meets the fair-prospect
and irreparable-harm standard. Nothing in the record suggests it could — the
underlying matters are the applicant's own routine civil suits — so any grant,
even in part, would be a severe surprise.
