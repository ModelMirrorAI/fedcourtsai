# Forecast — Watkins v. United States District Court for the District of Arizona (25A622)

The application will be **denied**, and denied without ceremony: a one-line
docket entry reading "Application (25A622) denied by Justice Kagan," with no
opinion and no noted dissent.

The four procedural claims, from the state the record shows:

- **Response.** No response has been requested as of the snapshot, and none
  will be. The Court calls for responses when an application might plausibly
  be granted or presents a question worth airing; a pro se stay application
  naming the district court itself as respondent — a mandamus-adjacent attempt
  to halt the applicant's own civil litigation — presents neither. P ≈ 0.02.
- **Referral.** The application sits with Justice Kagan as Circuit Justice and
  will most likely be resolved in chambers rather than referred to the full
  Court. Referral is the pattern for capital applications and contested
  emergency matters, not for this shape. A courtesy referral before denial is
  possible but unlikely. P ≈ 0.04.
- **Amici.** Zero amicus briefs are on the docket and zero will arrive; no
  organized interest attaches to a pro se applicant's attempt to stay his own
  district-court proceedings. P ≈ 0.01.
- **Disposition.** An unqualified grant is a practical impossibility: the
  applicant is unrepresented, the respondent is the tribunal itself, the relief
  would interrupt ongoing district-court proceedings that can be reviewed in
  the ordinary course, and nothing on the docket suggests any Justice has asked
  for more. P(grant) ≈ 0.01.

**Timing.** Applications of this kind are ordinarily denied within days to a
couple of weeks of submission. This one was submitted November 18, 2025 and the
snapshot shows no action through August 16, 2026 — nine months of silence that
is far outside the normal disposition window, which suggests the docket record
is lagging rather than that the application is under active consideration. If
it is genuinely still pending, expect denial whenever the docket next moves;
timing is unscored, so this disciplines the forecast rather than resolving
anything.
