The application for a stay will be denied. It is highly unlikely that the Court or Circuit Justice will call for a response, as the application—filed pro se—is expected to fall far short of the stringent standard required for interim relief (a fair prospect of certiorari, a likelihood of reversal, and irreparable harm). Justice Kagan may deny the application alone, though a referral to the full Court for denial is also possible. Given the nature of the parties and the lack of broader legal significance, no amicus briefs will be filed.
