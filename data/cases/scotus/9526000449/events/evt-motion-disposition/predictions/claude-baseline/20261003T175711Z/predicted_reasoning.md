# Forecast — 26A449, Holloway v. Polk (application for a stay)

**Disposition: denied, by Justice Alito acting alone, without a response.**
The application was submitted to Justice Alito on September 27, 2026 and
docketed October 2, 2026. It seeks a stay of a matter from the Supreme Court of
Texas (No. 26-0569); the applicant is self-represented and the respondent is
an individual. A stay from a state-court judgment requires a reasonable
probability that four Justices would grant certiorari, a fair prospect of
reversal, and irreparable harm, and the Court almost never finds that showing
made on a pro se application from a state civil matter. I expect a one-line
denial.

Procedural claims that resolve against this docket:

- **No response will be called for.** A request for a response is an
  affirmative act of attention; nothing visible here — no institutional party,
  no identified federal question, no lower-court dissent — invites one.
  P ≈ 0.03.
- **No referral to the full Court.** The commonest route for an application of
  this shape is a single-Justice denial. Referral stays possible because some
  applicants renew after a denial and the renewed filing is sometimes docketed
  on the same number, and because a Circuit Justice occasionally refers so the
  denial issues from the full Court. P ≈ 0.10.
- **No amicus filings.** The docket shows zero and I expect it to end at zero,
  counting submissions as well as accepted briefs. P(the count rises) ≈ 0.01.
- **Timing.** Justice Alito typically disposes of unopposed pro se stay
  applications within one to three weeks of docketing; expect a denial entry
  between roughly October 6 and October 23, 2026. (Timing is unscored and is
  stated to discipline the rest.)

Conditional merits-shaped content: an unqualified grant would require a
requested response and, almost certainly, a referral first, so the disposition
claim and the ladder claims move together. I put almost no weight on the first
rung firing, and the small residual on the disposition claim is order-form and
classification noise rather than a route to relief I can describe.
