I expect **plenary briefing and argument**, not summary correction: the conditional probability of a cert-order summary disposition is **4%**. No particular intervening decision in the record supplies a likely GVR route.

The most likely denial is **without a recorded separate writing**. Conditional on denial, I assign **15%** to at least one dissent or statement respecting denial. A denial would leave the lower-court judgment in place without announcing a Supreme Court rule on the merits; I do not forecast a majority opinion or an individual cert-vote lineup.
