The Supreme Court is likely to turn down George Anibowei's challenge to warrantless searches of phones at the border. I give it about a one-in-four chance of taking the case. The privacy issue is important, ten outside briefs have been filed, and the Court requested a government response. Still, courts agree on some search rules, and the Supreme Court has previously declined related disputes. A refusal to hear this case would leave the lower court's ruling standing without approving its reasoning.
