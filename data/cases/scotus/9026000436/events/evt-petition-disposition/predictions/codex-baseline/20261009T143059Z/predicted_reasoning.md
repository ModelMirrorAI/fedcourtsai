# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny the petition. The substantial question about the causation standard for FMLA retaliation will not overcome this case's preservation obstacles: the court below relied on the employer's proposed jury instruction, its failure to challenge the standard in post-trial motions, and the absence of plain error. Denial will leave the judgment standing without expressing agreement with the substantive causation rule.

The snapshot records **one distribution**, on October 7, 2026, for the November 6, 2026 conference, and **no CVSG**. I expect **zero additional distributions** and a denial on an orders list shortly after that conference, most likely during November 2026. The probability of at least one additional distribution is **20%**; conditional on additional distributions, one or two is the likeliest course. A request for a response following the respondent's waiver is a possible intermediate step on the minority path toward further consideration, rather than an event already observed.

I expect **no CVSG**; the probability of a new invitation is **2%**. If one issues, I expect it during late 2026 or early 2027, with disposition delayed while the Solicitor General considers the federal labor regulation.

**If review is granted**, the Court will most likely take the first question in a neutral formulation: whether FMLA retaliation requires but-for causation rather than a motivating-factor showing. Resolving that question in this vehicle would also require confronting the preservation issue framed by the second question; the conditional grant path likely includes that threshold issue rather than silently treating it as resolved. I do not expect wholesale acceptance of the petition's premise that a challenge first made on appeal was necessarily made at the first meaningful opportunity.

Conditional on any grant, I assign **25%** to disposition in the cert order itself and **75%** to plenary review. On the summary branch, a remand for reconsideration of the preservation issue is more plausible than an immediate merits reversal. A routine GVR solely because Chevron was overruled is not the leading route: Loper Bright predates the appellate decision, was presented to the panel, and preserved the force of prior statutory holdings against challenges based only on their use of Chevron.

Conditional on denial, I assign **4%** to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. The modal forecast is an ordinary denial without separate writing. I make no individual-Justice vote or authorship forecast.
