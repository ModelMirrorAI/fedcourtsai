# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will grant a substantive stay of the Fourth Circuit's October 7 mandamus order pending further review. **Probability of an unqualified grant: 0.82.** An administrative stay, if issued, will be a temporary bridge rather than the substantive relief this number forecasts.

The likely rationale will be narrow: preserve the effect of the Court's earlier intervention and avoid forcing an agency decision on an exceptionally compressed schedule while campaign advertising arrangements remain time-sensitive. The Court will likely regard the demonstrated entitlement to this particular mandamus deadline as insufficiently clear, without deciding the ultimate meaning of the lowest-unit-charge statute. It need not hold that courts can never compel delayed FCC action.

The principal alternative is denial on the ground that an order requiring a decision neither dictates the FCC's answer nor itself changes advertising rates, and that further agency delay would frustrate effective review. I nevertheless expect the prior stay and the election-period equities to carry more weight.
