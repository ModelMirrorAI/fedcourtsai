# Forecast — NRCC v. Brown, No. 26A477 (stay of Fourth Circuit mandamus)

**State at this moment (snapshot 2026-10-11).** Application for a stay submitted to the
Chief Justice (Circuit Justice for CA4) on Oct 8; a response was requested the same day
(due Oct 10); one amicus brief (RNC et al.) submitted; the candidates' response was
filed Oct 10. The response is captioned for Nos. 26A476 & 26A477 — the United States /
FCC filed a parallel stay application — and asks the Court to "lift the administrative
stay," so an administrative stay of the Fourth Circuit's writ is already in place. No
referral to the full Court appears on the record I was shown.

**Disposition: granted.** I expect the full Court to grant the stay of the Fourth
Circuit's mandamus judgment pending certiorari, most likely in a short unsigned order
disposing of 26A476 and 26A477 together, within days of the response (before the
FCC's Oct 19 reply deadline and well before the Nov 3 election). The Court stayed the
same panel's merits judgment in this very dispute on Sept 4 (No. 26A274), reasoning
that CA4 likely lacked jurisdiction while the application for review was pending and
that the party committees face irreparable harm in the weeks before the midterms. A
writ compelling the FCC to decide by Oct 9, mid-comment-period, is the same
disruption a month later, and *Purcell*-type timing concerns cut harder now. The
Solicitor General's own application adds weight. Expect a noted dissent from the
three more liberal Justices (votes are not scored here).

Main routes to a non-grant: the FCC itself resolves the application for review (the
response says the Chairman circulated a proposed order in August), mooting the writ
and producing a dismissal; or the Court acts only on 26A476 and dismisses 26A477;
or a partial grant (e.g. staying only the deadline), which resolves as denied.

**Response requested:** already fired; no further increment expected.

**Referral to the full Court:** likely (~75%) — Brown I was decided by the full Court,
and a contested election-season stay with the SG as co-applicant will be referred,
though the docket may record it only inside the disposing order.

**Further amicus filings:** unlikely (~25%); the briefing window is effectively closed
and the case may be disposed of within days.
