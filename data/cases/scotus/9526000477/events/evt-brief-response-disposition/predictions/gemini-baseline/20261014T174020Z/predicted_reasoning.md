The application will be referred to the full Court by the Chief Justice. A response has already been requested and filed. Expect the amicus count to rise past the one brief currently on the docket (from the RNC) as other interested political organizations or campaign finance groups weigh in given the proximity to the midterm elections.

The Court will grant the application for a stay pending certiorari within a few days. The Court previously stayed the Fourth Circuit's initial ruling in this same underlying dispute to avoid altering election rules shortly before an election (the *Purcell* principle). The Fourth Circuit's subsequent mandamus order effectively circumvents that stay by forcing the FCC to act on a tightened timeline. The Court will likely view this as inconsistent with its prior order and grant the stay to maintain the status quo through the election.
