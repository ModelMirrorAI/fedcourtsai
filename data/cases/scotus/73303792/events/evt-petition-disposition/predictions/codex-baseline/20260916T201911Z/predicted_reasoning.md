# Forecast

The Court will deny the petition. The snapshot shows **one distribution**, entered June 17, 2026, for the September 28, 2026 conference, and no CVSG. I expect **zero additional distributions** and a routine denial in the orders following that conference, most likely in early October 2026. Probability of at least one additional distribution is **4%**; if that less likely branch occurs, one additional distribution is more likely than an extended sequence.

I expect no call for the Solicitor General's views (**0.1%** probability of a new CVSG). The record presents an individual challenge to state and local officials' conduct and federal pleading rulings, not a developed federal-government interest demanding the Solicitor General's participation.

If review is granted, the likelier question is a narrowed version of **QP 1**: whether the alleged judicial-assignment and recusal misconduct supplies an injury independent of the state judgment for Rooker-Feldman purposes. I do not expect the Court to take QP 2's combined amendment-and-prefiling-restrictions formulation wholesale. Any favorable merits ruling would most plausibly address the jurisdictional boundary while leaving immunity and pleading issues for further proceedings; this is conditional, not a forecast that an opinion will issue.

Conditional on any grant, I assign **35%** to disposition in the cert order itself and **65%** to plenary review. Summary correction remains possible, but no intervening controlling decision supporting a GVR has been identified in the consulted material, and the overlapping grounds below complicate a simple summary resolution.

Conditional on denial, I assign **1%** to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. I expect no separate writing and make no individual-Justice vote or authorship forecast. The likely denial would not endorse the lower courts' reasoning.
