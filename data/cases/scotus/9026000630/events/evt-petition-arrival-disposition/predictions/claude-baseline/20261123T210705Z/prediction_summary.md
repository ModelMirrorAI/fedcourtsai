The government is asking the Supreme Court to review a ruling that immigration detainees in New England who entered the country without inspection are entitled to bond hearings. The Court already agreed in October 2026 to decide the same legal question in another case. This petition will most likely be held until that case is decided. I think it is somewhat more likely than not, about three in five, that the Court then grants it, most likely by sending the case back to the lower court to reconsider (a "GVR").
