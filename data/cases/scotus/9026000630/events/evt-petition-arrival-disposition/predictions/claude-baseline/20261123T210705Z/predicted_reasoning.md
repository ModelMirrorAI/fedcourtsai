# Forecast: Mullin v. Guerrero Orellana, No. 26-630 (arrival)

The docket shows zero distributions. The petition will be distributed once for a conference (most likely in January 2027, after the respondent's December 14 response date or an extension of it) and will then be **held** without a published order for the decision in *Rhoney v. Barbosa da Cunha*, No. 26-104, which the Court granted on October 1, 2026 and which presents the same core question: whether 8 U.S.C. 1225(b)(2)(A) mandates no-bond detention of noncitizens who entered without inspection and were arrested in the interior.

- **Relists.** At least one distribution is near certain. After the first conference the petition will sit as a hold rather than being visibly relisted week to week; it will be redistributed once after *Rhoney* is decided (expected by the end of June 2027).
- **CVSG.** None. The United States is the petitioner.
- **Disposition.** The outcome follows *Rhoney*. If the government prevails there in whole or in substantial part, the Court will grant, vacate and remand this First Circuit judgment in light of that decision. If the government loses outright, the Court will deny. I rate the GVR path slightly more likely than not. A separate plenary grant on a class-specific question (class-wide relief, 8 U.S.C. 1252(f)(1)) is possible but unlikely; consolidation with *Rhoney* is unlikely because merits briefing there is already under way.
- **Question presented.** If the Court took this case on its own, it would be for a class-relief question distinct from *Rhoney*'s statutory question, not the statutory question again.
- **Summary route.** If the Court grants, it will almost certainly be a GVR in the cert order, not briefing and argument.
- **Separate writings.** If the petition is denied, expect no noted dissent or statement. If it is GVR'd, a noted dissent from the GVR by Justices Sotomayor or Jackson is conceivable but unlikely.
- **Timing.** Final disposition in late June or early July 2027, on the clean-up order list after *Rhoney*.
