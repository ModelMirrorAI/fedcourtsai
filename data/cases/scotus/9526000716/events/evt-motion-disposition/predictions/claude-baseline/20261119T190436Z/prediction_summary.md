The Supreme Court will very likely refuse to pause Reinaldo Dennes's execution in Texas; I put the chance of a stay (an order halting the execution while the Court considers his appeal) at about one in seventy. His request rests on a claim that his trial lawyers presented too little evidence in favour of sparing his life, but the Texas court rejected it on a state procedural rule, it was filed a day before the execution, and death-row prisoners' last-minute stay requests succeed only about 3.5% of the time.
