# Forecast: Dennes v. Texas, No. 26A716 (application for stay of execution)

The record shows one entry: the application submitted to Justice Alito on 18 November 2026. No response has been called for, the application has not been referred to the full Court, and no amicus brief is on the docket.

- **Disposition.** The Court will deny the stay, and will deny the linked certiorari petition (No. 26-6187) in the same order. P(relief granted in whole or in part) = 0.015.
- **Referral.** Justice Alito will refer the application to the full Court, which will act on it; the order will read "presented to Justice Alito and by him referred to the Court is denied" (0.93). The residual is a denial by the Circuit Justice alone or a disposition that goes moot.
- **Response.** The Court will not call for a response (0.03). Texas will file its opposition uninvited, as it routinely does in execution-day litigation, and an uninvited filing is not a call.
- **Amicus.** No amicus entry will appear before disposition (0.01).
- **Timing.** The order issues on 19 November 2026, the scheduled execution date, in the hours before the execution. Not scored.
- **Writings.** The order will be unsigned with no noted dissent. If anyone writes or notes a dissent, it will be Justice Sotomayor or Justice Jackson; I put the chance of either writing at a few percent each.
- **Conditional ground, if a stay were granted.** It would rest on doubt that the Texas Court of Criminal Appeals' Article 11.071 § 5 dismissal is an adequate and independent state ground for a Wiggins claim, and would be a stay pending disposition of the petition rather than any merits ruling.
