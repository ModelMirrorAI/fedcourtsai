I expect the Supreme Court to deny Reinaldo Dennes’s request to pause his November 19 execution. I put his chance of a stay at about two in a hundred. Dennes says his trial lawyer failed to show jurors evidence of childhood abuse and cognitive difficulties that could have persuaded one of them to choose life. Texas’s highest criminal court dismissed his repeat challenge under a state procedural rule. That ruling makes it difficult for the Justices to reach the question of what the jury should have heard.
