Code Crim. Proc. art. 11.071, § 5. Both the Fifth Circuit and the Supreme Court consistently treat Art. 11.071, § 5 as an independent and adequate state procedural ground barring federal review unless the TCCA's order depended on an antecedent federal merits ruling.
   - Dennes argues that his *Wiggins v. Smith*, 539 U.S. 510 (2003), trial-counsel mitigation claim was "previously unavailable" under § 5(a)(1) because his initial state habeas counsel (appointed in 1997) failed to investigate childhood trauma and neurodevelopmental impairments, so the TCCA must have reached federal *Strickland* merits. Ryan*, 566 U.S. 1 (2012), and *Trevino v. Thaler*, 569 U.S. 413 (2013), govern equitable cause in federal § 2254 proceedings—not state-court subsequent-writ jurisdiction on direct certiorari.
