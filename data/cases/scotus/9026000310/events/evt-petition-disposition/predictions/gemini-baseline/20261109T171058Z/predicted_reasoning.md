## 3. Question Presented and Merits Scope (Conditional on Grant)

If the Court were to grant review (following a Call for a Response), it would take the **single question presented as written** in the petition:

> Whether employer-mandated training that communicates derogatory racial stereotypes and directs race-conscious workplace conduct may be sufficiently severe to create a racially hostile work environment absent repetition or additional race-based misconduct.

If granted, the Court would address whether and under what circumstances an employer's official mandatory training curriculum can satisfy the "severe" prong of *Harris v. Forklift Systems, Inc.*, 510 U.S. 17, 21 (1993), and *Faragher v. City of Boca Raton*, 524 U.S. 775, 788 (1998), at the Rule 12(b)(6) pleading stage without allegations of recurring workplace harassment or individualized adverse actions.
