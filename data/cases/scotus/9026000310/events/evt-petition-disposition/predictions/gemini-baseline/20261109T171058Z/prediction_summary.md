The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Young v. Colorado Department of Corrections*, with only about a 1.5 percent chance (roughly one in sixty-five) of granting the petition. A former Colorado prison officer claims that a single mandatory online diversity training created a racially hostile work environment under federal employment-discrimination law, even though no supervisor or coworker harassed or disciplined him before he resigned four months later. The state waived its right to file a response, no outside groups filed supporting briefs, and the federal appeals courts agree that a single training session without ongoing workplace mistreatment does not meet the legal standard for a hostile work environment.
