The Supreme Court is likely to decline Joshua Young's challenge to mandatory racial-equity training, with about a one-in-seven chance that it takes the case. The Court has asked Colorado to respond, and two supporting briefs show meaningful interest. But the disagreement may concern how settled workplace-harassment rules apply to these particular allegations, rather than conflicting legal rules that require Supreme Court intervention. Expect another round of scheduling after Colorado responds, followed most likely by a denial without an explanation.
