A Colorado prison employee who is white says a mandatory racial-equity training was so offensive that it created a hostile workplace in violation of federal anti-discrimination law; the lower courts dismissed his case. We think the Supreme Court will most likely decline to hear it, with roughly a one-in-seven chance that it takes the case. The Court asked the State to respond and twenty States support the employee, which raises the odds, but the dispute largely turns on the facts of one training rather than a clear legal disagreement among courts.
