The Supreme Court is likely to decline Joshua Young's challenge to Colorado's workplace diversity training. I give review an 18% chance—roughly one in six. The Court's request for a response and support from a coalition of states show genuine interest. But the appeals court said that one sufficiently serious incident can be unlawful; it found Young's particular allegations inadequate. That makes the dispute look more like disagreement over these facts than a clear conflict between courts. I expect a decision in early 2027.
