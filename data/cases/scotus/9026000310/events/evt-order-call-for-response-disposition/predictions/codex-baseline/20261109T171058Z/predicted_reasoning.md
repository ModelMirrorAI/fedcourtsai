# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely **deny the petition**, after receiving the response it requested. The probability of any grant is **18%**. The usual disposition will be an unexplained denial, not a merits holding approving the training.

The record shows **one distribution**, dated October 21, 2026, for the November 6 conference, followed by the October 22 response request. It shows no prior substantive relist. The petition will almost certainly be distributed again after the opposition is filed or the response period ends: **99.5%** for at least one additional distribution. The modal forecast is one such new distribution followed by denial, not a long series of genuine relists. One or two further conference relists beyond that redistribution are plausible if a grant or a separate writing gathers consideration.

The Court will probably decide the petition in **January–March 2027**, allowing for the November 23 response deadline, briefing, redistribution, and a possible extension. This is a timing forecast, not a scored claim. A spring disposition would become more likely if substantial relisting occurs.

I predict **no invitation for the Solicitor General's views**, with a **4.5%** probability of a new CVSG. Ordinary adversarial briefing is the likelier next source of information.

**If review is granted**, the Court will most likely take the stated question, focused on whether the severe-or-pervasive standard permits a single mandatory training to suffice and whether the dismissal improperly required additional misconduct. It will not need to decide that every diversity program is unlawful or that Young has conclusively proved harassment. The likely ground for intervention would be that official, compulsory conduct must receive an independent severity analysis rather than fail automatically for want of repetition. That is a conditional merits forecast, not an expected opinion accompanying denial.

Conditional on any grant, I assign **20%** to disposition in the cert order itself and **80%** to review opening a merits proceeding. Summary reversal or vacatur for application of the wrong standard is possible, but there is no identified intervening decision making a routine GVR the expected path. The more likely grant route is full briefing and argument on the training-specific question.

Conditional on denial, there is a **12%** probability of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial. Most likely there will be no separate writing. If one appears, Thomas or Alito is the likelier author, with Gorsuch another possibility; Kavanaugh is a less likely candidate given his previously expressed concern about sufficiently severe isolated harassment. The structured order-writer probabilities describe authorship, not votes or joins.
