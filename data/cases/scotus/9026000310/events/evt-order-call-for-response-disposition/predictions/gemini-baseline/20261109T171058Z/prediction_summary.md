The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Young v. Colorado Department of Corrections* (about a seven-in-eight chance of denial, or a 12% chance of a grant). Although the Court asked Colorado to respond to the petition and twenty states filed a supporting friend-of-the-court brief, federal appeals courts agree that a single mandatory workplace diversity training session—without repeated incidents or workplace harassment—does not meet Title VII's high bar for a "severe or pervasive" hostile work environment, and this second lawsuit over the same 2021 training is a poor procedural vehicle.
