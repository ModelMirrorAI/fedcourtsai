# Forecast — No. 26-310

- **State forecast from:** one distribution (for the Nov 6, 2026 conference), with a response requested Oct 22 and due Nov 23. There has been no relist and no CVSG.
- **Further distributions:** Once Colorado's brief in opposition is filed (or its time runs out) and the reply period passes, the petition will be redistributed, likely for a January 2027 conference. Most likely it is denied at that first post-response conference. One relist is possible if a Justice is drafting a writing respecting the denial.
- **CVSG:** Not expected. The United States is not a party, and the Court rarely asks the Solicitor General's views on a fact-bound pleading dismissal.
- **Disposition:** Denied. Colorado will argue the ruling is fact-bound and the split is illusory: the claimed conflict is with single-slur cases, and Chislett involved repeated trainings.
- **If granted:** The Court would take the QP as written, or narrow it to whether a court may require repetition or accompanying harassment before employer-mandated race-based training can satisfy the "severe" prong of Harris. It would most likely set the case for plenary briefing. A summary reversal of the 12(b)(6) dismissal is a minority route (about 12% of grant scenarios). A GVR has no intervening decision to rest on.
- **Separate writing on denial:** Unlikely but possible (about 15%). If one appears, the most likely author is Justice Thomas or Justice Alito (possibly joined by Gorsuch), with a statement noting the importance of how Title VII treats DEI training after Ames.
