## What happened

St. Mary Catholic Parish, St. Bernadette Catholic Parish, the Archdiocese of Denver, and two parents sued Colorado officials over the state's Universal Preschool Program, which offers free preschool through both public and private providers, including religious ones. Participating preschools must sign an agreement not to deny children equal opportunity to enroll based on characteristics including religious affiliation, sexual orientation, gender identity, income, and disability. The petition says the Catholic preschools objected because their faith leads them to decline enrolling children of same-sex parents or gender-nonconforming children, while Colorado allows other providers to prioritize certain groups (such as low-income families or children with disabilities). A federal trial court and the Tenth Circuit Court of Appeals both ruled for Colorado, finding the program's rule neutral and generally applicable, and rejecting the schools' request for a religious exemption.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked to decide what showing is needed to prove that a government rule burdening religion is not evenly applied to everyone, and whether that requires proof that officials have unrestricted discretion to grant exceptions or that identical non-religious conduct is exempted. The Court is also asked whether a different, stricter rule against excluding religious groups from public benefits applies only when a government openly excludes people for being religious, or also when a rule's practical effect does the same thing. Separately, the petitioners ask the Court to reconsider a long-standing precedent that generally applicable rules affecting religious practice do not require the government to justify them with a strong reason.

## Where it stands

The Court granted review limited to two of the three questions and set the case for argument on November 3, 2026; the parties have since filed their merits briefs along with numerous supporting briefs from outside groups, including the federal government supporting the petitioners.
