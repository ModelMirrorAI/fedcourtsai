Given the current conservative supermajority, the Court is highly likely to find that Colorado's program is not generally applicable due to its exemptions or violates the rule of *Carson*, leading to a reversal of the Tenth Circuit.

My main uncertainty is not the direction of the outcome, but the specific doctrinal path the Court takes, as QP3 (overruling *Smith*) was not granted. The Court is likely to use the existing framework of *Smith* and *Fulton* to find a lack of general applicability.
