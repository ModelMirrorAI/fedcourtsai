The Supreme Court is very likely (85% chance) to reverse the Tenth Circuit's ruling and side with the Catholic parish preschools and parents in a 6–3 decision. Although Colorado requires preschools in its state-funded universal preschool program to accept students regardless of religion, sexual orientation, or gender identity, the State allows non-religious exceptions and custom enrollment preferences—such as for low-income families, disabled students, or specialty programs like Montessori and dual-language schools—while denying a religious exemption for Catholic preschools that require families to share their faith's beliefs.
