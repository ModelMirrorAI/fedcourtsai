The Supreme Court is very likely—about a 94 percent chance—to overturn or otherwise undo the lower court's judgment. The most likely result is a 6–3 reversal favoring the Catholic preschools. The majority will probably say Colorado needs an especially strong justification before denying school funding because of religious admissions practices, even when its rule does not explicitly mention religion. The strongest uncertainty is whether the Court adopts that broader principle or instead rules narrowly on Colorado's exceptions, and whether it sends the legal test back for reconsideration.
