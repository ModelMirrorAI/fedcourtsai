We predict the Supreme Court will very likely (about 85%) rule for the Catholic preschools and reverse the
lower court, probably 6–3. That lower court had upheld Colorado's rule keeping them out of the state-funded
preschool program. At argument, most of the conservative Justices questioned why Colorado lets other schools
limit enrollment for many non-religious reasons but makes no room for religious ones. The likely holding is
that the rule is not "generally applicable," meaning it does not apply evenly to everyone. That triggers the
strictest form of court review, which Colorado likely fails.
