## In brief

The Court declined to review a Guantanamo defendant's bid to revive a plea deal sparing him the death penalty after the Secretary of Defense withdrew from it.

## What happened

Walid bin 'Atash is charged before a military commission with participating in the September 11, 2001 attacks. In 2024, a military official approved a pretrial agreement under which bin 'Atash would plead guilty in exchange for the government not seeking the death penalty. Two days later, the Secretary of Defense withdrew from the agreement. A military judge and the Court of Military Commission Review both ruled that bin 'Atash had already begun performing the agreement's promises, so the withdrawal came too late. The United States Court of Appeals for the District of Columbia Circuit disagreed, ruling for the government and blocking the guilty plea from proceeding under the withdrawn agreement.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether it was clear and indisputable that bin 'Atash had not yet begun performing his promises under the pretrial agreement at the time the government withdrew, given that he had signed a detailed confession and taken other steps the government itself said the agreement required.
