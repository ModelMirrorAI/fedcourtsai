The district court dismissed most claims as untimely or otherwise legally deficient, dismissed two defendants for improper service, and ordered Hall's claims against his former employer sent to arbitration, staying that part of the case. When Hall appealed, the Fourth Circuit dismissed the appeal, ruling it lacked jurisdiction because the order was not final while the arbitration stay remained in place.
