## In brief

Timothy Hall asks the Court to review a federal appeals court's dismissal of his employment discrimination lawsuit for lack of jurisdiction.

## What happened

Hall sued his former employer, Eastern Panhandle Anesthesia Associates, and several other defendants, alleging disability discrimination and retaliation for safety complaints, under federal and state law. A federal district court dismissed most claims as untimely or otherwise deficient, dismissed two defendants for improper service, and ordered Hall's claims against his employer sent to arbitration, relying partly on an earlier state-court arbitration order. Hall appealed, but the Fourth Circuit dismissed his appeal, ruling it lacked jurisdiction because the case was not fully finished below. Hall's request for rehearing was denied.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether an appeals court may treat a case as not final, and therefore not yet appealable, when it rests on an order sending part of the case to arbitration, without addressing that a state court had already lifted its own arbitration order and dismissed the earlier case. The Court is also asked whether a company can force arbitration of discrimination and retaliation claims based on that earlier, later-dismissed state court order.

## Where it stands

The petition was filed on October 19, 2026, and three groups of respondents have since filed waivers stating they will not respond unless the Court requests it. The docket shows no action yet on whether the Court will hear the case.

## What each outcome would mean

Hall, the petitioner, is asking the Court to take up his case. If the Court grants review, it would hear the case and decide the jurisdiction and arbitration questions, without yet deciding who wins. If the Court denies review, the Fourth Circuit's dismissal of Hall's appeal stands, leaving the arbitration order and underlying dismissals in place and ending this avenue of appeal.
