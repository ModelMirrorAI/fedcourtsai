# Forecast — No. 26-583, Shaikh v. Fox News Network, LLC

- **Distributions:** none yet (arrival). Expect the respondents to waive a response. The petition will then be distributed once, for a conference in late 2026 or early 2027, and **not relisted**.
- **CVSG:** none. No federal interest is implicated, and the petition is frivolous on its face.
- **Disposition:** **denied** at the first conference, in the ordinary order list.
- **Question presented, if taken:** the only issue with any legal interest is whether a federal court may dismiss for lack of *diversity* jurisdiction claims that are wholly insubstantial under *Bell v. Hood*. The Eleventh Circuit left that question open as forfeited. The Court will not take it on this vehicle.
- **Summary route:** no intervening decision bears on the case, so neither a GVR nor a summary reversal is expected.
- **Separate writing:** none expected from any Justice on the denial.
