The Supreme Court will almost certainly turn down this case (roughly a 1-in-500 chance it agrees to hear it). Mr. Shaikh, representing himself, sued Fox News and other media companies. He claims they used hidden cameras and mind-reading technology to watch him. The lower courts threw the suit out because the claims were too far-fetched to give a federal court authority to hear them (a lack of "jurisdiction"). The only legal question the appeals court noted was one he never argued there. Expect a routine denial at the first conference.
