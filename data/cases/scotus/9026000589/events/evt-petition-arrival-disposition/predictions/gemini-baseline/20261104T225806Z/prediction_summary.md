We predict that the Supreme Court is very likely (85% chance, or about six in seven) to grant the federal government's request to review this case. The dispute involves over $16 billion in terminated Environmental Protection Agency climate grants, and the federal appeals court in Washington, D.C., was sharply divided when it kept in place a lower-court order blocking the terminations despite Congress having repealed the underlying grant statute. Because the Supreme Court has already stepped in twice on an emergency basis in similar cases to rule that federal grant-termination lawsuits generally belong in a specialized federal claims court rather than ordinary district courts, and because federal government petitions are granted at a high rate (about 73% historically), the Court is very likely to take this case to settle the issue nationwide.
