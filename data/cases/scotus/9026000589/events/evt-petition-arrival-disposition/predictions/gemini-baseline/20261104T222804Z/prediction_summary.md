The Supreme Court is very likely (about a four-in-five chance, or 82%) to grant the federal government's petition for review. The Environmental Protection Agency and the Solicitor General are challenging a closely divided D.C. Circuit ruling that kept in place a preliminary injunction blocking the agency from terminating $16 billion in climate-grant agreements. Because the case involves massive federal spending, splits the lower federal appeals courts over whether contract-related grant lawsuits belong in the Court of Federal Claims, and relies on a statute Congress subsequently repealed, the Justices are very likely to hear the appeal.
