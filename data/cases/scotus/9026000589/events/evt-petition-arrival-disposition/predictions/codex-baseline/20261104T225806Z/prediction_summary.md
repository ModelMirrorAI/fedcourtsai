The Supreme Court is very likely to take this case: I estimate an 86% chance of review, including any immediate corrective ruling. The dispute concerns roughly $16 billion in canceled climate grants and whether challenges belong in ordinary federal courts or a specialized court for claims against the government. A sharply divided appeals court and continuing disagreement over grant cancellations make review unusually attractive. Full briefing and argument are more likely than an immediate ruling, although the case's unusual bank-account arrangements could complicate review.
