The Supreme Court is likely to hear this dispute, with about an 82% chance of taking the case or ordering another form of review. The government seeks control over more than $16 billion in climate grants. Its petition raises a broader question: can grant recipients challenge cancellations in ordinary federal courts, or must they use the specialized court for government contract claims? That recurring issue and the financial stakes favor review, although the unusual funding arrangements and Congress's repeal of the program complicate the case.
