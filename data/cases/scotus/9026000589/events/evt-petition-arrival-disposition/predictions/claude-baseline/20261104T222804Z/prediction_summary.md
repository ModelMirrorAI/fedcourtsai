The federal government is asking the Supreme Court to review a lower-court order that blocks the EPA from cancelling about $16 billion in climate grants. We think the Court will very likely agree to hear the case, roughly an 85% chance. The government's own lawyer filed the petition. The Court has already signalled interest in the underlying question: whether such grant disputes belong in a special claims court for contract cases against the government rather than ordinary federal courts. The appeals court's own judges were deeply split.
