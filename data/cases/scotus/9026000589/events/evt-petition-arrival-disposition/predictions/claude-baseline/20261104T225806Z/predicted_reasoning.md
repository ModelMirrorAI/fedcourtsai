# Forecast — No. 26-589

- **Distributions:** none yet (arrival). Expect a first distribution after the BIO, probably December 2026 or January 2027 if respondents seek an extension. Expect at most one or two relists before action; a grant this Term is the likeliest outcome.
- **CVSG:** none. The United States is the petitioner.
- **Disposition:** certiorari granted, most likely on both QPs as written, or on QP 1 with QP 2 added because the SG asked for it to give the Court "flexibility." If Thakur (No. 26-210) is granted first, this petition may instead be held and later GVR'd, or granted and consolidated.
- **Summary route:** unlikely as the first act. A GVR after a hold behind Thakur is the main summary path (about 15% conditional on grant).
- **If denied:** expect a dissent from denial, most likely from Justice Alito and/or Justices Thomas and Gorsuch.
- **If granted, likely ground:** the Tucker Act channels claims seeking to keep grant agreements in force or to restore terminated funds to the Court of Federal Claims, extending *California*/*NIH*. The Court might also hold that the repeal of §60103 leaves no statutory mandate to enforce.
