# Forecast — No. 26-589, Zeldin v. Climate United Fund

- **Distributions:** the docket shows zero. The BIO is due Dec 4, 2026, possibly extended, so I expect a first distribution in December or January. I expect the Court to decide on the first or second conference: most likely one relist at most, then a grant. If the Court holds the case for *Trump v. Thakur* (No. 26-210), it will sit through repeated reschedulings instead.
- **CVSG:** none. The United States is the petitioner.
- **Disposition:** I expect certiorari to be granted, probably limited to QP1 (the Tucker Act question) or granted on both questions as the SG asks. If the Court grants, QP1 is the lead question. QP2 (the effect of the repealed §7434 after the OBBBA) is likely to be included or reframed as whether the repealed statute can support continuing injunctive relief.
- **Route:** plenary review is likelier than a summary disposition. The main summary path is a hold for a lead Tucker Act case, such as Thakur if granted, followed by a GVR. A per curiam reversal on this fractured record is unlikely.
- **If granted, the likely ground:** respondents' challenge to the terminations is in essence contractual, so it belongs in the Court of Federal Claims, following *California* and *NIH*. The injunction would be vacated.
- **If denied (unlikely):** I expect a dissent from denial, most likely from Justice Alito, joined by Justices Thomas and Gorsuch.
