The federal government is asking the Supreme Court to review a court order stopping EPA from canceling about $16 billion in green-energy grants. We think the Court is very likely (about 85%) to agree to hear the case or otherwise send it back. The government wins such requests far more often than other parties do. The money involved is huge, the appeals court was deeply split, and the case raises whether such grant fights belong in a special claims court, a question the Justices have already flagged twice.
