**Why I go well above the anchor:**
- The question is important, and the petition makes a strong case for that: 22 state laws, a program the Court itself called "uniform, nationwide" (*Astra*), and billions of dollars in transactions.
- The United States has filed amicus briefs for the manufacturers in seven circuits. That makes a CVSG, or an SG brief urging a grant, plausible, and the CVSG cut's grant rate (~29%) is far above the base rate.
- The disagreement is deepening. A divided Fourth Circuit panel held a state law likely preempted (now vacated for en banc review), and the Eighth Circuit denied en banc rehearing over a dissent, by one vote according to the petition.
- The counsel are elite, and the case is paid.

**Why I stay well below a coin flip:**
- There is no square split today. The Fourth Circuit panel decisions were vacated, and the D.C. and Third Circuit cases concern federal agency authority, not state preemption. The petition's "split" is a split in reasoning.
- The Court denied this question in *McClain* in 2024.
- The Fourth Circuit en banc is pending, which gives the Court a natural reason to wait or hold.
- The posture is a preliminary injunction appeal, which is a weaker vehicle.
- Mississippi waived. That is common for states that expect a denial, but it also means nothing on the record answers the petition yet.
