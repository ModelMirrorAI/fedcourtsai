The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Novartis's request to hear this case, with about a one-in-seven (14%) chance of granting review. Novartis argues that federal drug-pricing law (the 340B Program) overrides a Mississippi statute requiring drugmakers to deliver discounted medications to outside contract pharmacies. Although the Court asked Mississippi to file a response, every published federal appeals court decision has upheld similar state laws, the Supreme Court recently turned down the same issue from Arkansas, and this appeal comes at an early preliminary-injunction stage before full facts were developed.
