The Supreme Court is more likely to decline Novartis's appeal than hear it; I put the chance of review at about one in four. The dispute asks whether Mississippi can require drugmakers to supply discounted medicines through outside pharmacies under a federal program. The Court's request for a response shows interest, and the issue has national importance. But the alleged disagreement among appeals courts is not clear-cut, and this appeal concerns an early-stage ruling. Further briefing, and possibly federal government advice, will likely come before any decision.
