## Anchor and adjustment

The committed `metrics/statpack.md` uses the same salience version as this context. I pool every rendered Term strictly before 2026, namely 2017–2025, using the baseline band's bracketed reached rate, not its terminal-band rate. The exact corresponding `metrics/statpack.json` segment values give 660 weighted grant-family outcomes over 12,913 weighted resolved petitions: 5.111%. This is the private-petitioner risk set; Mississippi's status as respondent does not make this a state-petitioner cell. I exclude the 2026 row. These are the committed pack's estimates, not a fresh recount of the remote corpus.

I move to P(any grant) = 24%, a substantial but explicitly judgmental adjustment. The response request is an affirmative attention signal after a waiver, but the pack publishes no response-request-conditioned rate. It does not by itself justify treating review as probable. The more important case-specific upward factors are the nationwide federal-program implications, the petition's account of many states enacting similar laws, and its documented citations to United States amicus positions favoring manufacturers in other appellate proceedings. Those government positions are reported in the petition; I have not independently read those briefs, and they are not a Solicitor General recommendation on certiorari here. The petition specifically requests a CVSG, supporting a meaningful consultation probability.

The main downward factors are the lack of a square same-question circuit split and the interlocutory vehicle. The petition contrasts the Third and D.C. Circuits' limits on HHS authority with the Fifth and Eighth Circuits' treatment of state law. Agency inability to require a delivery practice does not automatically entail that states are forbidden to require it. The appendix shows the Fifth Circuit applying its existing AbbVie precedent, distinguishing federal pricing obligations from state delivery regulation, and affirming denial of preliminary relief. That is a real adverse legal holding, but it does not establish that another circuit has invalidated an equivalent state law. The petition itself also acknowledges the Court's earlier refusal to review McClain. That different petition's history is legitimate pre-decision context, not this case's outcome. The asserted later developments and government interest make a renewed attempt more credible without eliminating those obstacles.

The pack's terminal relist cuts are cross-checks, not forward hazards: the zero-relist bucket has 10,203 weighted resolved cases and about 1.8% grant-family outcomes, versus 13.7% among 2,538 cases in the one-relist bucket. A necessary redistribution after a requested response can mechanically place a case in that latter bucket without a genuine post-conference relist. Similarly, the paid CVSG bucket has 164 resolved cases and about 34.8% grant-family outcomes versus about 6.4% over 13,556 without a CVSG; this is a terminal, pooled association, not a rate conditional on receiving an invitation today. The one-amicus bucket has 1,162 resolved cases and about 15.1% grant-family outcomes, but counts briefs at disposition and overlaps the other signals. I do not multiply these correlated marginal rates or replace the strictly-prior band anchor with them.

## Other claims and stakes

The 99% additional-distribution forecast is mostly procedural: one distribution is already recorded, the requested response is due after its scheduled conference, and renewed circulation is expected once briefing is complete. The harness masks this increment at the response-requested moment; it should not be mistaken for a 99% forecast of a substantive relist. I assign a 42% chance of a new CVSG because federal administration is central and the petition expressly seeks one, tempered by the Court's ability to reject the imperfect split without further consultation.

Conditional on a grant, I assign only 8% to summary disposition: there is no identified intervening controlling decision, and a broad contested preemption issue favors plenary treatment. Conditional on denial, I assign 8% to some dissent or statement. The per-Justice order-writing marginals are small, independent probabilities rather than a distribution summing to one; their aggregate scale is consistent with a mostly silent denial and occasional writing on other routes. They are low-confidence judgments without a published case-conditioned writing baseline. I omit individual cert votes because a response request cannot identify a four-vote coalition.

The stakes score is 0.73, independently of the 24% grant probability. As framed by the provisioned filings, a decision could affect contract-pharmacy access, drug-discount transactions, and the division of authority between states and the federal government across many jurisdictions. I do not adopt the petition's contested financial estimates as independently established facts.
