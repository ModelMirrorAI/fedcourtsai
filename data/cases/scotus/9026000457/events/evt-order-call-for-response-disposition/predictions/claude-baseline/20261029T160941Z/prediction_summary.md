Novartis asks the Supreme Court to strike down Mississippi's law forcing drug makers in a federal
discount-drug program (340B) to deliver discounted drugs on terms beyond what federal law requires.
We think the Court will probably turn the case down: about a one-in-six chance it agrees to hear it.
The Court asking Mississippi to respond and the federal government's support for drug makers make
review plausible. But the strongest court disagreement on this issue is being reheard, and the
Court may prefer to wait for that.
