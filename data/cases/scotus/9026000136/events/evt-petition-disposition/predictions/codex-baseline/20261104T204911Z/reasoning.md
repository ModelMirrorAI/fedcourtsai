That materially weakens the strongest apparent grant signal and moves my forecast below the elevated-band anchor. It does not justify ignoring the frozen conditioning or treating the petition as an ordinary undistributed filing.

## Petition versus opposition

The QP asks whether courts may review a PTO denial of inter partes review on allegedly unlawful extra-statutory grounds, specifically expected trial timing in parallel litigation (`questions-presented.txt`; petition pp. i, 11–22). Tesla has a serious institutional argument: statutory limits on an agency are different from disagreement with an individual discretionary decision. Its discussion of Cuozzo and SAS identifies an intelligible boundary question, and its allegations of shifting, consequential institution policies make this more than ordinary patent error correction (petition pp. 23–30; reply pp. 1–8). The snapshot shows two filed amicus briefs and a further amicus leave motion; I do not count that motion as another accepted brief.

But the **actual judgment below is a poor clean vehicle**. The February 27 order is nonprecedential, arises on mandamus, and expressly finds no clear and indisputable right to relief. It also characterizes the agency's action as a resource-allocation judgment and rejects the separate notice-and-comment contention (petition appendix pp. 1a–3a). A court could recognize some reviewability of agency statutory limits without concluding that Tesla satisfies the demanding mandamus standard or that trial timing is statutorily forbidden. Thus, the broad QP somewhat overstates what must be decided to sustain this particular order.

The **federal respondent recommends denial**, not a grant or hold (BIO pp. 10–23). This is the government's ordinary opposition as a respondent, not an invited Solicitor General brief. The BIO emphasizes the absence of a statutory command to institute, the non-dispositive and holistic character of the timing considerations, alternative invalidity litigation and reexamination, and the absence of a square circuit conflict. It also identifies four earlier denials of similar petitions (BIO p. 10). Those earlier outcomes concern different cases and are legitimate historical context, not this petition's outcome. The cross-circuit examples involve different statutes and review mechanisms, making the asserted conflict less compelling (BIO pp. 21–23).

Tesla's reply gives substantial answers: mandamus posture does not prevent deciding a controlling legal issue and remanding, and narrower prior agency policies need not justify continued denial after the alleged recent overhaul (reply pp. 4–5, 9–11). I therefore do not treat either the mandamus posture or earlier cert denials as dispositive. Nevertheless, the opposition and appendix lower the probability relative to the band anchor more than the policy importance raises it.

For external primary context, I retrieved *Mylan Laboratories Ltd. v. Janssen Pharmaceutica, N.V.*, 989 F.3d 1375 (Fed. Cir. 2021), CourtListener opinion 4667257. Its opinion distinguishes jurisdiction to entertain mandamus from entitlement to the writ and rejects a materially similar challenge to using Fintiv factors (opinion pp. 6–13). This supports the vehicle concern; it does not establish that the Supreme Court must endorse that approach. I did not retrieve this case's lower-court docket separately because its operative order is already reproduced in the petition appendix.

## Companion-case pathway and numerical balance

Tesla expressly identifies Google v. VirtaMove, No. 25-1230, and Intel v. Squires, No. 26-73, as overlapping petitions and requests at least a hold (petition pp. 29–30; reply pp. 3–4). I use only the descriptions and then-pending status in the supplied filings; I did not verify their current dockets and assume no grant or decision in either. The empty provisioned `same-question.json` match list is not proof that these expressly identified petitions are unrelated: it reports only that no indexed QP crossed its similarity threshold.

These other vehicles make a derivative GVR plausible if the Court decides to address the common reviewability issue elsewhere. They preserve a meaningful probability above the broad docket's rate, despite this petition's own weaknesses. My 18% total is a judgmental adjustment from the frozen 24.84%, not a newly estimated empirical conditional rate. An illustrative decomposition is about **10.8% summary grant-route** and **7.2% plenary grant**, totaling 18%; hence the conditional summary-route claim is 60%. Most of the summary-route possibility is a later companion-driven GVR, not immediate summary reversal. There is no identified intervening merits decision in the provisioned record that presently compels such relief.

## Other claims, stakes, and limitations

- **Further distribution: 42%.** The modal path is denial without another distribution beyond the two recorded. Another scheduling change, a genuine post-conference relist, or coordination with the expressly identified petitions could nevertheless increment the count. A passive hold does not necessarily create another distribution entry.
- **New CVSG: 0.5%.** The United States is already a respondent and its Solicitor General has supplied a full opposition. An additional formal invitation is possible but unusually redundant; it is not inferred from the extension request or opposition.
- **Separate writing conditional on denial: 10%.** The agency-power issue could attract a statement, but the technical vehicle and previous denials make a silent order much likelier. Gorsuch has the largest individual writing probability as a qualitative fit with the reviewability concern, followed by Alito and Thomas; these are uncertain, unscored judgments, not claims to know any Justice's vote. The individual writing probabilities are unconditional over disposition routes, can overlap through coauthorship, and need not sum to the denial-conditional existence probability.
- **Stakes: 0.62.** A decision could affect access to administrative patent challenges across industries and the availability of judicial control of agency criteria. That significance is separate from the low chance this petition supplies the vehicle.
