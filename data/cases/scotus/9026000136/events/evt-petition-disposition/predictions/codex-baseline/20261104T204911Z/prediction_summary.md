The Supreme Court is likely to turn down Tesla's request, with about an 18% chance of taking some action granting review. Tesla raises an important question about whether judges can check the Patent Office's reasons for refusing patent challenges. But this case reached the Court through an exceptional procedure with a demanding standard, and the federal government opposes review. The apparent second look was only a scheduling change. If related litigation produces a new rule, the Court could instead send Tesla's case back for reconsideration.
