# Predicted Court action

The Court will most likely deny the petition, leaving the Federal Circuit's denial of mandamus in place. The order will most likely be unsigned and unaccompanied by any Justice's separate writing. A denial will not decide whether all of the PTO's challenged policies are lawful.

Two distributions are already recorded, but the second followed a reschedule before the first scheduled conference. My modal forecast is **zero additional distributions**, with denial following the currently scheduled December 4, 2026 conference. I assign 42% to at least one additional distribution. Conditional on an increment, one or two further distributions are the most plausible short path; a longer hold for overlapping litigation is a secondary possibility, not evidence already present in this docket.

The Court will not call for the Solicitor General's views: the federal respondent has already presented the government's opposition. The probability of a new formal invitation is 0.5%.

**If review is granted**, the useful question will be narrower than a general challenge to the PTO's institution policies: whether Section 314(d) permits mandamus review of a claim that the Director used a statutorily impermissible criterion in denying institution, and what clear statutory limit supports such relief. The Court would not need to decide patent validity or create an entitlement to institution whenever threshold requirements are satisfied.

Conditional on a grant, a **summary route is more likely than plenary review here, 60% to 40%**. The leading summary scenario is a hold followed by a grant, vacatur, and remand in light of a decision in an overlapping petition identified by Tesla. That forecast does not assume any companion has already been granted. Immediate summary reversal is unlikely because the availability of review and entitlement to mandamus relief are distinct questions.

If the Court denies review, I assign 10% to a dissent or statement respecting denial. None is the primary forecast. If a Justice does write, Gorsuch is my leading individual candidate to question insulation of agency statutory limits from judicial review, with Alito or Thomas secondary possibilities; the structured order-writer probabilities remain small for every Justice.
