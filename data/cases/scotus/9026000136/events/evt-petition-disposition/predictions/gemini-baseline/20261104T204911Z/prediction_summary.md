We predict that the Supreme Court is very likely to deny Tesla's petition for review, with only a 6% chance (about one in seventeen) of granting it. Tesla asks the Court to order the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office to reconsider its refusal to review three self-driving-vehicle patents that Tesla was sued for infringing. Although outside industry groups filed briefs supporting Tesla, the Supreme Court turned down a nearly identical appeal by Google just two weeks ago, has repeatedly declined similar patent-office challenges in past years, and can leave Tesla to challenge the patents in its ongoing trial-court case and patent-office reexaminations.
