# Forecast — No. 26-136, Tesla v. Granite Vehicle Ventures

**The Court denies certiorari, most likely at or shortly after the December 4, 2026 conference.**

- **Relists.** The docket shows two distributions so far: the original distribution for 11/6 and
  a redistribution for 12/4 after a reschedule. The petition has never actually been conferenced.
  Most likely it is denied at the 12/4 conference with no further distribution. The main
  alternative (≈40%) is another reschedule, or a hold for Intel Corp. v. Squires (No. 26-73)
  followed by redistribution once that petition is resolved.
- **CVSG.** None. The Solicitor General is already the respondent and has opposed.
- **Question presented.** If the Court takes up the reviewability question at all, it will do so
  in Intel v. Squires (an APA vehicle), not here. A plenary grant in this mandamus posture is
  unlikely. If granted, the Court would take the QP as written: whether §314(d) bars review of a
  non-institution decision resting on a ground allegedly contrary to the statute.
- **Summary disposition.** Most of the grant probability is a hold-then-GVR if Intel is granted
  and decided for the challengers. No intervening decision supports an immediate GVR now.
- **Separate writing.** Most likely the denial comes with no noted dissent, consistent with the
  October 2026 denial in Google v. VirtaMove (25-1230). If anyone writes, Justice Gorsuch is the
  likeliest.
