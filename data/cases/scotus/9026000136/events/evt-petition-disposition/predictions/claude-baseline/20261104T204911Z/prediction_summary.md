Tesla asks the Supreme Court to decide whether courts can review the Patent Office's refusal to reconsider (through "inter partes review") a patent Tesla is accused of infringing. We predict the Court will turn the case away; the chance it takes it is about one in eleven. The government opposes, Tesla came via an emergency-style "mandamus" request that the law makes hard to win, and the Court just declined a nearly identical Google case. A related Intel case is the likelier vehicle if the Court acts.
