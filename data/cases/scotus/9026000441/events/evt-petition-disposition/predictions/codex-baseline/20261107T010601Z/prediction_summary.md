The Supreme Court is very likely to decline Christopher Rydberg's appeal: I estimate about a two-in-a-hundred chance of review. He argues that the federal workers' compensation system is not covered by the health-care fraud law used against him. The petition identifies no direct disagreement between appeals courts, and his conviction also involved a private insurer, complicating the case. I expect denial after the December 4 conference, without another round of consideration or a separate written objection from a Justice.
