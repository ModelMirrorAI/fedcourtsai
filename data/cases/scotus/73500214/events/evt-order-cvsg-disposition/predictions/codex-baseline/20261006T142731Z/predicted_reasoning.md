If the Court selects this case for plenary review, it will take the existing question whether the National Bank Act preempts state interest-on-escrow requirements, focusing on application of the significant-interference standard to New York's requirement. If granted, the likely organizing issue will be how to assess practical interference with banking powers, including the significance of the agency developments described in the opposition; this is not an unconditional forecast of a merits holding.

Conditional on a grant, the probability of a disposition in the cert order itself is **0.35**, versus **0.65** for plenary review. A hold followed by a GVR if another escrow case supplies controlling guidance, or reconsideration prompted by the regulatory developments, is the main summary route. An immediate summary reversal is less likely than those routes: the disagreement and agency questions call for more than a simple correction under uncontested law. A grant in another case would not itself guarantee a GVR here; it could instead leave the bank's favorable judgment intact and result in denial here.

If review is denied, I expect a routine order without a separate writing. The conditional probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial is **0.08**. I do not forecast an individual Justice's cert vote or writing.
