Distributed once so far. The Court has just called for the Solicitor General's views (CVSG). Expect the petition to be distributed again once the SG files their brief, but no further relists after that second distribution before a decision is reached. The question presented highlights a deep, acknowledged circuit split between the First and Second Circuits on how to apply the *Cantero* standard regarding National Bank Act preemption of state interest-on-escrow laws. If granted, the Court will likely take the single QP as written to resolve the split definitively. A summary disposition (GVR) is highly unlikely since the Court just remanded this very case to clarify the standard, and plenary review is needed to resolve the ensuing split. Should the Court deny the petition despite the split and the prior history, a dissent from denial is moderately likely from the Justices who previously favored a clear preemption rule.
