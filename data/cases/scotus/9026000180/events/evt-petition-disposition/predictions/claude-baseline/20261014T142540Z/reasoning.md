  establish law.

**Adjustments down.**
- **This is a qualified-immunity vehicle.** The merits question the Justices
  care about sits behind a clearly-established-law defense. The Court has
  repeatedly denied petitions that ask it to rethink QI doctrine (QP 2's
  history test, and QP 1's sources question). Without a BIO I cannot judge the
  split, and I suspect it is overstated. Fact-bound QI reversals are usually
  summary and pro-officer, the wrong direction here.
- **The Court's recent pattern is to deny these cases with separate
  writings.** It did so in *Parents Protecting* and in *Lee*, and took the
  issue up only on an interim application (*Mirabelli*).
- The posture is unusual: a divorce custody order; surgery at a state
  hospital; and whether the procedure was "surgery" contested on the pleadings.
  All of that makes for vehicle problems.
