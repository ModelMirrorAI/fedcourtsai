Distributed once so far, but the Court requested a response after the respondents initially waived their right to file one. The petition will be redistributed once the response is filed; expect at least one further distribution (a relist) after that as the Court considers the case closely. The petition presents a high-profile culture-war issue (parental consent for a minor's gender-related surgery) wrapped in a deep circuit split over qualified immunity doctrine. A CVSG is unlikely since the federal government is not a party and its interests are not directly implicated.

If granted, the Court would likely take both QPs to clarify the qualified immunity standard, though it might focus on QP 1 regarding what sources constitute clearly established law. A summary disposition (GVR) is less likely than plenary review given the 5-5 circuit split, though it is possible if the Court decides to summarily reverse the Ninth Circuit's grant of qualified immunity. Should the Court deny cert, there is a strong possibility of a dissent from denial (e.g., from Justice Thomas or Alito) given the subject matter and the fact that 5 amicus briefs were filed at the cert stage.
