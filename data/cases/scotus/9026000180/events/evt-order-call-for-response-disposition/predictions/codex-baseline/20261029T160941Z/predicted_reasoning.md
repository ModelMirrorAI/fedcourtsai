# Forecast of the Court's disposition

The Court will most likely deny this petition after obtaining the requested response. The forecast assigns 20% to any grant, including a grant accompanied by an immediate disposition, rather than only a grant for argument.

The baseline has one distribution, on September 16 for the October 9, 2026 conference, followed by the September 17 response request. No response or CVSG appears in that baseline. From that state, expect at least one additional distribution after the response deadline or the response and reply process. One additional distribution is the modal path; a second, genuinely deliberative relist is possible but not the central forecast. The 98% distribution-increment probability includes ordinary redistribution following the response request, not just a further conference after substantive consideration. On this moment that claim is masked by the harness.

A CVSG is unlikely, at 1.5%. This is a private damages suit against state and private defendants rather than a controversy requiring the federal government's institutional position. Expect the petition to proceed without an invitation to the Solicitor General. From the September response-request vantage, the likeliest resolution window is late 2026 or early 2027, allowing for response extensions and redistribution; this is a timing forecast, not a report of later docket activity.

If the Court grants plenary review, the likelier question is a narrowed version of question 1: how non-case-law sources, including an individualized custody order and the information known to medical professionals, bear on whether a federal right was clearly established. The Court is less likely to use this case to reconstruct qualified immunity for entire categories of officials under question 2. That question was not raised below, according to the petition itself.

Conditional on any grant, an immediate cert-order disposition has a 30% probability, so briefing and argument remain the likelier grant route. A narrow vacatur and remand for reconsideration of the immunity analysis is more plausible than an immediate ruling establishing damages liability. The parental-rights authority described in the petition could invite another look, but a later decision does not itself establish what officials should have known in 2021. The fact-specific custody order makes a summary merits reversal particularly difficult.

Conditional on denial, expect no noted dissent or statement, with 30% reserved for at least one such writing. Thomas and Alito are the likeliest authors, with Gorsuch a secondary possibility, given the qualified-immunity and parental-rights positions described in the petition. The individual writing probabilities cover all dispositions and authorship, not mere joinder, and are therefore not a distribution that must sum to one.
