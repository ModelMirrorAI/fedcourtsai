We expect the Supreme Court to **turn down** this father's appeal, though not
with certainty: roughly a one-in-eight chance it takes the case. He sued doctors at
a state university hospital over gender-related surgery on his son that he says
needed his consent. The lower court dismissed the suit under "qualified
immunity," a rule that protects officials unless earlier cases clearly
forbade what they did. The Court asked the other side to respond, which raises
the odds somewhat. But it rarely takes cases to rethink that immunity rule.
