The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Hudacko v. Regents of the University of California*, with only an 8.5% chance of granting the petition (about one in twelve). Although the Court requested a response from the university after five outside groups filed briefs supporting the father, the case is a poor candidate for a national ruling. Specifically, the father lost medical custody in divorce court under an order allowing the mother to approve hormone therapy, the lower court's ruling is non-binding, and the father's broader challenge to government-official immunity (qualified immunity) was never raised in the lower courts.
