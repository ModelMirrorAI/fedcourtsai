The Court will deny Eta’s petition. It has been distributed once, for the December 11 conference. I expect zero further distributions and no separate writing on denial. If the Court grants review in Eta’s case for argument, I expect it to narrow the question to whether officers may manually search a traveler’s phone without a warrant solely to gather evidence of an offense unrelated to customs, contraband or admissibility. That would leave forensic searches and the remedy for a violation open. Plenary review is slightly more likely than a summary order conditional on a grant. The plausible summary route is to hold Eta for *Anibowei* and, if that decision changes the governing rule, vacate the judgment and send the case back for reconsideration.
