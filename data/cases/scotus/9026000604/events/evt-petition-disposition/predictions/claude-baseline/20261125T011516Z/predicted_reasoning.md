# Forecast — Eta v. United States, No. 26-604

The docket shows one distribution (Conference of Dec 11, 2026), a government waiver, no call for a response and no CVSG.

- **Further distribution: yes.** The Court will not dispose of this petition at the Dec 11 conference. Most likely a Justice calls for a response from the United States before or at that conference, as happened in *Anibowei v. Blanche*, No. 26-208, on the same question; the response arrives in early 2027 after one extension, and the petition is redistributed once, for a conference in February or March 2027. The alternative path is a silent hold for *Anibowei* and redistribution after that petition is resolved. Expect one or two further distributions in total.
- **No CVSG.** The United States is the respondent.
- **Disposition: denied.** The Court considers this petition together with *Anibowei* and denies both, leaving the border device-search conflict for another day as it did in 2021. If the Court does take the question, it takes *Anibowei* (a civil challenge with no exclusionary-rule complication) as the lead, or both petitions consolidated, and this petition is then either granted with it or held.
- **Question presented, if granted:** the QP as written (whether a warrantless border phone search needs a justification tied to contraband, customs or admissibility), possibly with an added question on the good-faith exception.
- **Summary route:** if this petition is ever granted in any form, a hold followed by a GVR in light of a decision in *Anibowei* is nearly as likely as plenary review. No summary reversal.
- **Separate writing on denial:** unlikely on this docket. If anyone writes, it is Justice Gorsuch or Justice Sotomayor, and more likely on the *Anibowei* order.
- **If decided on the merits:** the Seventh Circuit's alternative good-faith holding means the petitioner's conviction likely stands on remand even if the constitutional rule changes.
