The Supreme Court is almost certain to deny Martin Akerman's emergency application for an injunction and stay (a temporary order halting lower-court proceedings), with less than a one-in-a-hundred chance (0.5%) of granting relief. Akerman is representing himself without a lawyer in an ongoing Freedom of Information Act and federal employment lawsuit in Washington, D.C., where the trial court has not yet issued a final ruling and the appeals court summarily rejected his premature appeal. The Supreme Court grants emergency stays or injunctions only when there is an indisputably clear legal right and a high likelihood that the full Court will review and reverse the decision below, standards this routine procedural dispute does not meet.
