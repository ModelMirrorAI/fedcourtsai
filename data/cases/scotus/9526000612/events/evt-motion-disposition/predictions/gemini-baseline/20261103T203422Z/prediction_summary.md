The Supreme Court grants emergency stays only in exceptional cases of national importance where the lower court clearly erred and irreversible harm would otherwise occur. Here, a self-represented litigant is asking the Court to intervene in the middle of an ongoing federal employment and public-records lawsuit after both the trial court and the appeals court rejected similar emergency requests, and the Supreme Court has already turned down his previous related applications.
