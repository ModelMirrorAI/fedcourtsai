# Forecast — 26A612, Akerman v. MSPB (injunction application)

- **Disposition:** The application will be **denied**. The most likely route is the Chief Justice, as Circuit Justice, denying it in chambers within a few weeks of docketing. If the applicant renews it to another Justice, a referral to the full Court and a denial by the Court will follow.
- **Response requested:** No. The Solicitor General will not be asked to respond.
- **Referral to the full Court:** Probably not, though plausible (about one in four), and only through a renewed application after an in-chambers denial.
- **Amicus briefs:** None will be filed.
- **Timing:** Disposed of by late November or December 2026. Any renewal and referral would add a conference cycle.
- **Separate writings:** No Justice will write on the order.
- The legal standard (an injunction pending review requires indisputably clear rights, plus irreparable harm) is not met on anything the record shows. A self-represented employment dispute against a federal agency rarely presents a question the Court would take.
