# Forecast — 26A612

- **Disposition:** The Chief Justice, acting as Circuit Justice, will deny the application for an injunction, most likely
  without a response and within a few weeks of docketing. Relief is not granted, in whole or in part (≈0.5%).
- **Response requested:** No. The Court will not ask the Solicitor General to respond (≈3%).
- **Referral to the full Court:** Unlikely but plausible (≈25%). That would happen if, after a denial, the applicant renews the
  application to another Justice, who refers it to the Court. A Court denial would follow.
- **Amicus:** No amicus filings (≈1%).
- **Separate writings:** None expected. The order will be a one-line denial.
- **Timing (unscored):** Disposed of by late November 2026. If renewed and referred, a full-Court denial on an
  order list one to two months later.
- *Conditional merits context:* An injunction from the Supreme Court requires an indisputably clear right to relief.
  An individual employee–MSPB dispute coming up from the D.C. Circuit will not meet that standard.
