We predict the Supreme Court will **deny** Martin Akerman's request for an injunction (a court order
directing the Merit Systems Protection Board and other federal respondents to act or stop acting while his case continues).
The chance of any relief is very low, about one in two hundred. He is representing himself in a long-running personal
employment dispute. The Court almost never issues injunctions of this kind, which require a clearly established right to relief.
We expect the Chief Justice to deny it without asking the government to respond.
