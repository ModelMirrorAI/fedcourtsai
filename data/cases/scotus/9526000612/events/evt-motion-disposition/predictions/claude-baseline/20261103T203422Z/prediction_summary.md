Martin Akerman, representing himself, asked the Supreme Court to order (an "injunction") the Merit Systems Protection Board and other federal respondents to act or stop acting while his case continues. We predict the request will be denied, with about a 2% chance of any relief. The Court almost never grants injunctions at this stage, especially to individuals without lawyers in personal employment disputes against the government. The application's text could not be read, so this forecast rests on the docket and on past patterns.
