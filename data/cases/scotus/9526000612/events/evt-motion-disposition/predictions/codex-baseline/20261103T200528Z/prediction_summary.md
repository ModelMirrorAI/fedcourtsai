I expect the Supreme Court to deny Martin Akerman's request for an injunction, an order requiring or stopping action while a dispute continues. I give him about a one-in-twenty chance of receiving at least some of the requested relief, excluding cases ended as moot because relief is no longer needed. This is a cautious estimate: the available record shows only the submission, and the application's text could not be read. A short order without explanation is the most likely result.
