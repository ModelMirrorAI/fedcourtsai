Distributed once so far (prior to the submission of the Brief in Opposition); expect at least one further relist before a decision on the petition, as the Court requested a response and will now consider the full record. The petition presents a qualified immunity challenge to a Ninth Circuit decision involving the "hot pursuit" exception. Given that the Court affirmatively requested the BIO after an initial waiver, there is significant interest in the case, raising the likelihood of intervention. 

If granted, a summary GVR or summary reversal is the most probable route, as the Court frequently corrects the Ninth Circuit's qualified immunity denials without plenary review. The Court would likely address whether an 18-minute delay and a lack of immediate continuous foot chase definitively forecloses the hot pursuit exception under clearly established law. A CVSG is unlikely given that this is a local police matter lacking a direct federal programmatic interest. Should the Court deny the petition, there is a realistic chance of a dissent from denial by one of the more conservative Justices (such as Justice Alito or Thomas), who have historically written separately when the Court leaves intact what they view as an erroneous denial of qualified immunity.
