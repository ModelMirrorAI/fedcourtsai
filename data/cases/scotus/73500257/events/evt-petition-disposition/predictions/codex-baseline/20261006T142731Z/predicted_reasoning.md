# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny certiorari. I assign an 18% probability to any grant, including a summary reversal or GVR, not merely plenary review.

The snapshot records one distribution, on July 15 for the September 28, 2026 conference, followed by a July 21 response request and an extension through October 5. The opposition was submitted on October 5. From that one-distribution baseline, I expect at least one further distribution (90%), most likely one or two additional distributions before disposition. The first additional distribution will most likely bring the completed briefing back before the Court; it need not signify a substantive relist after consideration. I expect disposition in late October through December 2026, with a longer interval possible if a summary opinion is prepared.

There is no recorded CVSG. I expect none to issue (2% probability of a new CVSG): this is primarily a dispute over application of qualified-immunity and hot-pursuit principles to a particular police search, rather than a question requiring the federal government's institutional input.

Conditional on a grant, I assign 75% probability to disposition in the cert order rather than plenary briefing and argument. The leading grant route is a narrow summary reversal on qualified immunity, focused on question two: whether existing precedent sufficiently established that these officers' particular search was unlawful. A GVR based on an intervening decision is not my principal alternative; the supplied record identifies no necessary intervening-case hold.

If plenary review is granted, I expect a narrowed version of question two, potentially paired with question one's treatment of interrupted pursuit. I do not expect the Court to adopt question three's sweeping Supreme-Court-precedent-only rule in this vehicle. Conditional on denial, the probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial is 8%. My modal forecast is no separate writing; I do not forecast an individual Justice's cert vote.
