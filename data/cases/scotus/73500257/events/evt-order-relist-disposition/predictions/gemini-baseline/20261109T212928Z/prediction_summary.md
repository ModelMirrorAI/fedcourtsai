I predict the Supreme Court will **deny** the petition for review, with **about a one-in-four chance (24%)** that it steps in (most likely by summarily reversing the lower court's ruling without full oral argument). North Las Vegas police officers are asking the Court to throw out a lawsuit filed after they climbed into a family's walled backyard without a search warrant and shot two pet dogs while searching the neighborhood for a fleeing domestic-violence suspect. Although eight judges on the federal appeals court dissented and argued the officers should be shielded by qualified immunity (a legal rule protecting police from lawsuits unless they violated clearly established law), key factual disputes about how long the search took and the lack of disagreement among different appeals courts make the Supreme Court likely to turn the case away.
