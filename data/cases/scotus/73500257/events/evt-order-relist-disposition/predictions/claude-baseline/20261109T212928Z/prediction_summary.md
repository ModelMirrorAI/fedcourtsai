Police officers and the City of North Las Vegas ask the Supreme Court to overturn a ruling that
the officers can be sued for entering a backyard (where they shot two attacking dogs) while
tracking a fleeing suspect. We think the Court is slightly less likely than not to take action —
about a 46% chance. The Court asked for a response and has now put the case on a second conference,
and eight appeals judges dissented below, signs that point toward a possible quick reversal without
full argument; but most such cases still end in a denial.
