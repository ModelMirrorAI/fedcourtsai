# Forecast — No. 25-1355, City of North Las Vegas v. Jones

**State:** distributed three times on the docket (9/28 superseded by a response request; 11/6
conference; relisted Nov 9 for 11/13). No CVSG.

- **Further relists:** more likely than not (≈60%) the petition is distributed again after the
  11/13 conference. A per curiam summary reversal typically takes several relists while it is
  drafted and any dissent is written; a denial with a separate writing also needs time. A clean
  denial on 11/13 is the main path to no further relist.
- **CVSG:** no. No federal interest; a § 1983 QI case against city officers (≈2%).
- **Which question:** if the Court acts, it would take QP 2 (clearly established law) and perhaps
  QP 1 (hot pursuit after a temporarily lost trail) — not QP 3, which asks it to rewrite the
  clearly-established standard.
- **Route if granted:** a summary disposition is the likelier route (≈65% given a grant): a per
  curiam reversal holding the officers entitled to qualified immunity because no precedent placed the
  unlawfulness of entering a nearby backyard during a K-9 track beyond debate, in the line of
  Rivas-Villegas / City of Tahlequah / Kisela. Plenary review of the hot-pursuit question is the alternative.
- **Overall:** close to even; slightly more likely denied than granted (P(grant family) 0.46).
- **Writings:** a summary reversal would likely draw a dissent from Justice Sotomayor (possibly joined
  by or with a separate writing from Justice Jackson). If denied, a separate writing is plausible
  (≈30%) — e.g., Justice Thomas or Justice Alito objecting to the denial or to CA9's QI practice.
