# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely grant the petition and summarily reverse the denial of qualified immunity on the warrantless-entry claim, without plenary briefing or argument. P(any grant) is 58%; conditional on a grant, a disposition in the cert order has probability 90%. The expected route is a reasoned summary reversal, not merely a remand to reconsider under an intervening decision.

The docket already contains three distributions, for September 28, November 6, and November 13, 2026. The earlier response extension accounts for a calendar reset; November 9 records the first clear relist following a completed conference. The Court will likely distribute the petition again, with one to three additional distributions before disposition. The probability of at least one further distribution beyond the existing three is 72%.

No CVSG will issue on the expected path. The probability of a new invitation to the Solicitor General is 1%.

If review is granted, the Court will focus on question 2: whether precedent in place in February 2019 clearly prohibited the particular entry following the officers' perimeter and the dog's alert. It will not need to adopt the officers' broader premise that their search was constitutional. The expected rationale is that Johnson's permanently lost trail, without comparable new location information, failed to place the specific conduct here beyond reasonable debate. The Court will leave the broader constitutional boundary of interrupted hot pursuit unresolved and will not decide question 3's categorical proposal that only Supreme Court opinions can clearly establish law.

A summary reversal will likely draw an objection that the Court has minimized factual disputes and weakened the warrant requirement for innocent homeowners. Sotomayor is the likeliest separate author on that path; Kagan and Jackson are more likely to join a writing than author their own. The forecast does not treat joining a dissent as writing it.

If the Court instead denies review, the expected denial is unaccompanied by a separate writing. Conditional on denial, the probability of any noted dissent or statement respecting denial is 12%. A denial-side writing would most plausibly object to the lower court's treatment of the officers, rather than endorse the petition's unpreserved Supreme-Court-only rule.
