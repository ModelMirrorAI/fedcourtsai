The Supreme Court is slightly more likely than not to take this case: I put the chance at 58%. The likeliest result is a short decision reversing the lower court without full argument. Eight appeals-court judges objected to denying the officers qualified immunity, a protection against damages when the legal rule was not sufficiently clear. The Court has also asked for a response and reconsidered the petition. However, disputed facts about the search leave a substantial chance that it declines review.
