We think the Supreme Court will probably turn down this case, but there is about a one-in-four chance it takes it. Police officers searching for a fleeing suspect entered a backyard and shot two dogs. A federal appeals court said the officers can be sued, rejecting their "qualified immunity" (protection from lawsuits unless the law was clearly settled). The Supreme Court asked the other side to respond, which signals interest. It has often reversed this appeals court on such cases without full argument.
