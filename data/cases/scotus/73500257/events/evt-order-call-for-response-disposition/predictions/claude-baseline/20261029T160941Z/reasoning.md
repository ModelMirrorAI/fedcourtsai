# Why P(grant) = 0.24

**Anchor.** `context.json` freezes band `baseline` under sal-v4 with one distribution and no CVSG. The statpack's sal-v4 segment table gives baseline's bracketed `reached` rate for the Terms strictly before this case's Term (2025). It runs 4.5–5.9% for OT2017–OT2024, roughly 5.3% pooled (n ≈ 11,600 weighted). The Ninth Circuit's modern cert row (grant family ≈ 3.2%) is consistent with that.

**Adjustments up from the anchor.**
- **Response requested.** The respondents waived, and a Justice asked for a response anyway. No committed table conditions on this signal. From general knowledge, the grant rate among paid petitions that draw a called-for response is several times the unconditioned rate, roughly the low-to-mid teens. Most such petitions are still denied. This is my judgement, not a published figure.
- **Profile of a QI summary reversal.** The opinion below is published. It reversed a grant of qualified immunity on clearly-established-law grounds that rest on a single 2001 circuit decision. The appendix shows a dissent from denial of rehearing en banc by Judge Collins, joined by seven judges (Callahan, Bennett, R. Nelson, Lee, Bress, Bumatay, Tung). That dissent frames the opinion as defiance of Kisela/Rivas-Villegas. This is the shape the Court has repeatedly corrected by per curiam, which is why I put a large share of the grant mass on the summary route.
- **Amicus support.** Municipal and police-association amici filed at the petition stage. The statpack's amicus `1` bucket (n=1,162 resolved) has a grant family of about 15% against about 4% for `0`. That is a stakes proxy that overlaps the signals above, so I do not count it separately.

**Adjustments down.** The underlying stakes are small and fact-bound (two dogs killed during a K-9 search). The Court denies many QI petitions that come with circuit en banc dissents. QP 3 asks the Court to rewrite QI doctrine, which it has consistently declined to do. The interlocutory posture (remand for trial on the search claim) is a mild vehicle negative.

**Forward retrieval.** The live supremecourt.gov docket (fetched 2026-10-29) shows a 30-day extension, the BIO filed October 5, the reply on October 19, and distribution for the November 6, 2026 conference. It shows no disposition. Under forward mode this is ordinary post-cutoff material, and it does not move my number beyond confirming that the case is pending. I did not read the BIO or the reply text.

**Other claims.** CVSG increment 0.01, because no federal party or interest is involved. Summary route given a grant 0.5, as explained above. Dissent from denial given a denial 0.15: a denial after a called-for response sometimes draws a Thomas or Alito statement, but most such denials carry no writing. Order-writers is consistent with this: Sotomayor and Jackson are elevated through the summary-reversal-dissent path, Thomas and Alito through the denial path.

**Where to discount me.** The called-for-response uplift and the summary-reversal share are judgement calls with no committed table under them. The case summaries under `data/cases/.../summaries/` were not read.
