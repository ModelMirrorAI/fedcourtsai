# Forecast: City of North Las Vegas v. Jones, No. 25-1355

**State forecast from.** The snapshot ends on the call day, July 21, 2026. It shows one distribution (for the September 28, 2026 conference), the respondents' waiver, one amicus brief (City of Henderson and other Nevada municipalities, the Nevada Sheriffs' and Chiefs' Association, and IMLA), and the Court's request for a response. No CVSG has been called for.

**Disposition.** The Court is more likely to deny than to grant, but this petition has a much better chance than most. My forecast is roughly a one-in-four chance of a grant in any form.

**Relists.** The petition will almost certainly be distributed again once the response is in (claim masked for this moment). If the Court is considering a summary reversal, expect at least one relist after the first conference at which it is considered. A per curiam QI reversal is usually preceded by several relists.

**CVSG.** None. No federal interest is implicated in a municipal § 1983 dog-shooting case, and the Court does not call for the Solicitor General's views on QI petitions.

**Route if granted.** I forecast an even split between a summary per curiam reversal on clearly-established-law grounds (QP 2) and plenary review. The Court's line of Ninth Circuit QI reversals runs through Kisela, Emmons, Rivas-Villegas and City of Tahlequah. It fits a published panel opinion that took clearly established law from a single, materially different circuit precedent (United States v. Johnson, 9th Cir. 2001 (en banc)), over an eight-judge dissent from denial of rehearing en banc by Judge Collins. A plenary grant would more likely take QP 1, whether a hot pursuit survives a trail lost for about 18 minutes and recovered by a K-9. That is a real open Fourth Amendment question after Lange v. California. QP 3, which would confine "clearly established" to the Court's own decisions, will not be taken as written.

**Dissent from denial.** If the petition is denied, a separate writing is unlikely. If one comes, the most likely sources are Justice Thomas or Justice Alito, objecting to the Ninth Circuit's QI framing or questioning the doctrine. If the Court reverses summarily, expect a dissent from Justice Sotomayor, possibly joined by Justice Jackson.
