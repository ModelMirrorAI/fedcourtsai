# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition without explanation. The probability of any grant, including a summary reversal or GVR, is 30%; denial remains the modal disposition. The response request will secure adversarial briefing before that decision, not itself establish a decision to intervene.

The baseline contains one distribution, entered July 15, 2026 for the September 28 conference, followed by the July 21 response request and an August 20 response deadline. It contains no completed conference relist and no response. The petition will almost certainly be distributed again after the response arrives or its time expires: P(at least one additional distribution) = 99%. Expect one routine additional distribution as the modal path. A summary-reversal path would more likely involve additional conference consideration, plausibly one to three true relists beyond the ordinary redistribution. The increment claim covers any added distribution, not just a true post-conference relist. No CVSG is expected: P(a new invitation) = 1%.

If the Court grants, it is much likelier to dispose of the case in that order than to schedule full briefing and argument: P(summary disposition | any grant) = 82%. The strongest route is a per curiam summary reversal on question 2, holding that the circuit precedent did not clearly establish that these officers' conduct was unlawful. That route would distinguish an unrecovered trail in an open wooded area from continuous perimeter and canine-search efforts that allegedly recovered a dangerous suspect's trail. It would not require deciding that every such search is constitutional. A GVR is a secondary possibility; the supplied record identifies no controlling intervening decision that makes a mechanical remand the leading route.

For plenary review, question 2 is still the likeliest focus, possibly paired with question 1's narrower inquiry into when a temporarily interrupted pursuit can resume. The Court is less likely to take question 3's categorical proposal that only Supreme Court decisions can establish the law for immunity purposes. A narrow resolution can leave that larger issue open.

Conditional on denial, expect no noted dissent or statement: P(any such writing | denial) = 6.5%. Alito or Thomas is a plausible author if there is a denial-related writing, but no individual cert vote is forecast. Conditional on summary reversal, Sotomayor is the most plausible separate writer, with Jackson another possibility. The JSON writer probabilities are unconditional across disposition paths, so their ordering need not match the denial-only forecast.

The forecast's calendar window is autumn 2026 through early 2027, with preparation of a summary opinion capable of extending consideration beyond a routine denial. The September conference and August deadline are dates recorded in the July baseline, not assertions that a response, conference action, or disposition subsequently occurred.
