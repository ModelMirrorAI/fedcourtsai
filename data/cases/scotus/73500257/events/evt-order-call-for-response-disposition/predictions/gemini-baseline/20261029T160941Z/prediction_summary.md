We predict that the Supreme Court is likely to deny review in *City of North Las Vegas v. Jones*, with about a one-in-four chance (25%) of taking the case or reversing the lower court outright. The case asks whether police officers had "qualified immunity"—legal protection that shields officers from lawsuits unless they violated clearly established rules—when they jumped over a family's backyard wall without a warrant after losing sight of a fleeing domestic-violence suspect for at least eighteen minutes and using a police dog to pick up a scent. Although eight judges on the appeals court argued that the officers should have been protected from the lawsuit, the Supreme Court turns down most appeals, and factual disputes over whether the neighborhood search actually dragged on for hours make the Court likely to leave the lower court's ruling in place.
