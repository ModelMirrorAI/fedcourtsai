The majority ground will rest on textual interpretation of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, emphasizing that equitable considerations cannot override explicit procedural mandates. The breadth of this holding will be categorical across federal civil litigation, establishing a uniform rule that an unpled affirmative defense is waived unless the defendant successfully moves to amend the pleadings. We anticipate a unified Court, likely 9-0, given the non-ideological nature of this procedural issue and the Court's general preference for clear, text-based procedural rules. Justice Gorsuch or Justice Alito might write the majority opinion given their interest in civil procedure and textualism.
