We expect the Supreme Court to rule for Dr. Younge, which we think is very likely (about 4 in 5). We expect it to throw out the appeals court's decision and send the case back, probably by a 7–2 vote with Justices Thomas and Alito dissenting. The district attorney's office won using a defense it never wrote into its formal answer to the lawsuit. At oral argument most Justices suggested the office first has to get permission to amend that answer under the ordinary court rules. Being able to show the plaintiff wasn't harmed is not enough.
