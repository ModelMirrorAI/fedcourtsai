The Supreme Court is very likely—about a nine-in-ten chance—to disturb the ruling against Jasmine Younge. I expect a 7–2 decision setting that ruling aside and sending the case back. Several Justices suggested that the district attorney's office must meet the normal standards for adding a defense late, rather than merely showing that its delay caused no harm. This would give Younge another procedural opportunity, not a victory on her discrimination claim. The office might still win after the lower courts apply the correct standards.
