We predict the Supreme Court will probably **not** take this case, but it is a serious contender: roughly a one-in-four chance (27%) that it agrees to hear it. Bethany Hall lost a Virginia tuition grant when she switched to a religious major. She asks the Court to overrule Locke v. Davey, a 2004 decision allowing states to refuse to fund religious-ministry degrees. Twenty-two states and many groups back her. The Court has narrowed Locke before but has been reluctant to discard it outright.
