The Supreme Court is more likely to turn down Bethany Hall's appeal than hear it, although I put her chance of obtaining review at about two in five. She asks the Court to overturn a ruling allowing states to withhold college aid for training religious ministers. Her case presents that issue directly and has substantial outside support. But the Court has preserved this exception in later decisions. Because the first conference setting was rescheduled, another calendar listing is very likely before a decision.
