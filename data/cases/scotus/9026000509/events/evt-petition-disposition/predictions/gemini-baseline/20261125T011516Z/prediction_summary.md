The Supreme Court is overwhelmingly likely to deny review, with less than a one-in-three-hundred chance (0.3%) of taking the case. Efrain Areizaga, representing himself without a lawyer, asks the Justices to overturn lower-court rulings that dismissed his workplace-retaliation lawsuit against TForce Freight without a trial (summary judgment), where the federal appeals court affirmed without writing an opinion and the company waived filing a response. Because the Supreme Court reserves review for unresolved national legal disagreements rather than case-specific factual or procedural disputes—and even his four-year deadline argument fails because he sued nearly five years after being fired—routine denial is virtually certain.
