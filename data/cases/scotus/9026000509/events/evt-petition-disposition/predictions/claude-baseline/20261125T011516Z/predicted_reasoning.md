# Forecast: No. 26-509, Areizaga v. TForce Freight, Inc.

The docket shows one distribution (for the conference of December 11, 2026). The Court will deny certiorari at that conference, on the order list that follows it, without a relist and without calling for a response from TForce Freight, which has filed nothing (only the Teamsters local waived).

- **Further distributions:** none expected. The only realistic route to another distribution is a call for a response, which I do not expect.
- **CVSG:** none. This is a private employment dispute with no federal interest.
- **Question presented:** the Court would take none of the four. Each asks whether the district court erred on this record (summary judgment on disputed facts, unruled retaliation counts, an extension of time to answer, the limitations period for a section 1981 count). If the Court acted at all, the limitations question (QP 4, 28 U.S.C. § 1658 and *Jones v. R.R. Donnelley*) is the only one with a legal shape.
- **Summary route:** if there were a grant, it would more likely be a summary disposition (a GVR or a per curiam correction) than plenary review, because the petition claims misapplication of settled precedent, not a split.
- **Separate writing:** none on the denial.
