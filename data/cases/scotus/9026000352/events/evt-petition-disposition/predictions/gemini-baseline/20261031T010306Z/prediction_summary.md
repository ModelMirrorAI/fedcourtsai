The Supreme Court is virtually certain to deny Jarin M. Wray's petition for review (`denied`, with a 0.2% chance of a grant, or about one in five hundred). Representing himself without a lawyer, Wray sued his former employer, RTX Corporation, alleging he was fired for reporting alleged business ties to China, but a federal trial court dismissed his lawsuit for failing to meet basic filing requirements—such as completing required federal agency complaint steps before suing—and the appeals court summarily agreed. Because the petition raises no disagreement among lower courts, presents sweeping political arguments rather than a focused legal question, and RTX did not even need to file a response before the case was sent to the Justices' conference, the Court will turn the appeal away without comment.
