The snapshot shows one distribution and no CVSG. I expect zero additional distributions. Any merits analysis is conditional on that unlikely grant.

Conditional on denial, I expect no noted dissent or statement respecting denial; the probability of either is 0.2%. No particular Justice is expected to write, and no majority opinion or author is forecast for a denial.
