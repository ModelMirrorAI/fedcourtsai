The Supreme Court will almost certainly decline to hear this case (about a 3-in-1,000 chance it agrees). A former Collins Aerospace employee, representing himself, sued RTX for firing him after he made whistleblower complaints. The trial court dismissed the case, and the appeals court affirmed in a short unpublished order. The petition asks broad, abstract questions about national security and whistleblowers rather than pointing to a specific legal conflict that the Justices need to resolve, so it is scheduled for a routine conference and will likely be turned away there.
