# Predicted Court Action

- **Relists:** I predict 0 relists. While Long Conference cases are sometimes relisted to clear the backlog or allow a Justice to write a dissent from denial, the vehicle flaws here (multiple rule violations and the use of the judicial robe) make this petition an easy denial without the need for further consideration or a written statement.
- **CVSG:** I predict no CVSG. A response has already been filed, the issue is primarily one of state judicial conduct rules and the First Amendment, and the federal government is not a party and has no unique interest that would prompt a call for the Solicitor General's views.
- **Summary Disposition:** I predict no summary disposition. The case will be denied outright rather than GVR'd or summarily reversed.
- **Dissent from Denial:** I predict no Justice will note a dissent from the denial. The vehicle issues make it an unattractive case for any Justice to highlight.
