# Forecast

The Court will most likely deny certiorari without a separate writing. The probability of any grant, including a GVR or summary reversal, is 25%.

The snapshot records **two distributions**: May 5 for the May 21, 2026 conference and July 29 for the September 28, 2026 conference. A response request intervened on May 12. From this two-distribution starting point, I predict no further distribution as the modal path, with a 38% chance of at least one more. If another distribution occurs, one or two additional conferences are more likely than a prolonged sequence. The next scheduled conference is September 28; denial at the ensuing orders release is the modal timing, not a claim that the earlier conference actually considered a fully briefed petition.

No CVSG is recorded, and I predict none will be called. The probability of a new CVSG is 1%; this constitutional dispute about state judicial discipline does not present an obvious need for the federal government's institutional views.

If review is granted, the Court will most likely address whether content-based restrictions on a sitting judge's noncampaign speech on public issues require strict scrutiny rather than public-employee balancing. That is a narrower formulation of the petition's question than one presupposing that applying strict scrutiny necessarily invalidates Cohen's sanction. Conditional on a grant, plenary briefing and argument are substantially likelier than disposition in the cert order: the summary-route probability is 8%. The forecast identifies no intervening Supreme Court ruling calling for a GVR.

The likely reason for declining review is the combination of disputed comparability among the alleged split cases and the difficulty of separating protected expression from use of judicial prestige across several disciplinary rules. Denial would not resolve which constitutional standard is correct. Conditional on denial, the probability of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial is 16%; no separate writing remains the central prediction, and I do not predict an individual Justice's vote.
