# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny the petition without a separate writing, most likely in the ordinary order list following the December 4, 2026 conference. The docket currently shows one distribution, not a relist. My point forecast is **zero further distributions**; I assign 6% to at least one additional distribution, including a reschedule or distribution after a requested response. If the petition receives further consideration, one additional distribution is more likely than a prolonged series.

The Court will not invite the Solicitor General's views. The Solicitor General already represents the federal respondent, so the relevant intermediate step, if any, would be a call for that respondent's response, not an invitation for an outside government's views. The probability of a new CVSG is 0.1%.

The petition will not receive plenary review merely to correct the handling of this physician's registration proceeding. Its unpublished judgment and absence of a developed circuit conflict will leave the asserted statutory defect below the Court's review threshold. The expected denial will announce no view on whether the agency complied with the statute.

**If review is granted**, the Court will most likely take the single question presented, or narrow it to whether section 824(c)(3) requires the Administrator to address a corrective action plan in the final revocation order after the Division has declined discontinuance or deferral. It will not revive the separate forfeited delay theory or take up the due-process claim that the petition excludes. The conditional merits dispute will concern whether the statutory determination was discharged by the Division's denial, rather than whether the physician should retain his registration as a factual matter.

Within the grant branch, plenary briefing and argument is more likely than disposition in the cert order: **15% conditional probability of a summary route**, against 85% for plenary review. There is no identified intervening merits decision warranting a routine GVR. A grant would be 1% overall, not 15% overall.

Conditional on denial, the probability of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial is 0.6%. I expect none from any Justice. In the unlikely writing branch, Thomas or Gorsuch is a more plausible writer than any other individual Justice; this is not a prediction of their votes or of a majority opinion.
