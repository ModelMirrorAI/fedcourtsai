The Supreme Court is very likely to decline this case; I put the chance of any grant of review at about one in a hundred. A physician argues that the drug-enforcement agency failed to make a required decision about his proposed rehabilitation plan before revoking his registration. That raises a potentially important procedural question, but the lower court issued an unpublished ruling, and the petition identifies no disagreement among appeals courts. I expect a routine denial, without further conference consideration or a separate judicial explanation.
