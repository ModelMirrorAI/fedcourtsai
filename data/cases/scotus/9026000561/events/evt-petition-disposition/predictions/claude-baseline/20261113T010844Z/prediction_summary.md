The Supreme Court will almost certainly decline to hear this case — the chance it takes it is well under one in a hundred. A doctor is challenging the DEA's revocation of his licence to prescribe controlled substances, arguing the agency never properly ruled on his plan to fix the problems. The lower court's ruling was brief and unpublished, no other courts disagree on the issue, and the government did not even file a response. Expect a denial in early December with no Justice writing separately.
