# Forecast

The Court will deny the petition, most likely without a separate writing, following its initial consideration at the November 6, 2026 conference shown in the snapshot. My central timing forecast is an orders release in November 2026, shortly after that conference; this is a forecast, not a verified future orders calendar. P(any grant) is 1.2%.

The provisioned record shows exactly one distribution, entered October 7, and no CVSG. I expect zero additional distributions. P(at least one further distribution) is 8%; if there is additional consideration, one further distribution is more likely than a prolonged series. A request for a response following the respondents' waiver could delay disposition, but is not my central forecast. P(a new CVSG) is 0.3%; I expect none.

The petition is unlikely to secure review because the unpublished appellate decision treats the specifically asserted comparator and officer-testimony arguments as forfeited, and the dispute otherwise concerns application of the retaliatory-arrest exception to an unusual factual record. The alleged conflict with the Eighth Circuit does not present a clean disagreement at the same procedural stage.

If review is granted, I expect the Court to narrow the question to whether the properly preserved arrest-history and officer evidence can establish the Nieves exception after Gonzalez, rather than adopt the petition's broader framing that any objective evidence defeats probable cause. The Fourth Amendment bar-check claim is unlikely to be the focus. Any favorable substantive ruling would concern the retaliation exception despite probable cause, not erase probable cause itself.

Conditional on a grant, I assign 55% to disposition in the cert order and 45% to plenary review. The marginal preference for summary treatment reflects the petition's reliance on an existing Supreme Court instruction rather than a clearly established new legal conflict; summary corrective relief is more plausible than an automatic GVR for an identified intervening decision, since no such intervening decision is established by the materials consulted. The low unconditional grant probability remains controlling.

Conditional on denial, P(any noted dissent or statement respecting denial) is 2.5%. I forecast none and make no per-Justice cert-vote prediction. Denial would not resolve the correctness of the lower courts' substantive analysis.
