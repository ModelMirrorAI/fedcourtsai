# Forecast

The Court is most likely to deny the petition. The arrival record shows zero distributions and no CVSG. I expect the petition to receive one initial conference distribution after the response period and then be denied without a further relist. Because the increment claim begins at zero distributions, I assign a high probability that the distribution count will nevertheless rise at least once from the frozen state.

The Court is unlikely to call for the views of the Solicitor General. The dispute concerns a local retaliatory-arrest claim, does not involve a federal party or program, and principally asks whether the lower courts properly applied existing decisions to a developed factual record.

If the Court intervenes, it is more likely to dispose of the matter in the certiorari order than to set it for plenary review. The likely route would be a GVR or summary correction keyed to *Gonzalez v. Trevino*, rather than a new, broad merits proceeding. If plenary review were granted, the Court would likely reformulate the petition's question to ask what objective evidence can establish the *Nieves* exception at summary judgment, rather than ask whether evidence can "defeat probable cause."

On denial, no Justice is likely to note a dissent or issue a statement respecting the denial.
