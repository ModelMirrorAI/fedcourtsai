Distributed once so far (on the fully briefed record, following an initial waiver distribution and a subsequent request for a response); expect roughly one further relist before a decision on the petition as the Court considers this alongside the related *Hadley* petition (No. 25-1158). No CVSG is expected, as the case concerns local municipal parking enforcement and forfeiture policies, which do not implicate federal statutory or administrative interests. 

If granted, the Court would likely take the first question presented as written to resolve whether the confiscation of surplus property proceeds in a police power context constitutes an unconstitutional taking under the principles of *Tyler v. Hennepin County*. A summary disposition (GVR) is unlikely, as there is no intervening decision that squarely addresses the extension of *Tyler* to police power fines. Should the Court deny the petition, there is a reasonable possibility of a dissent from denial (or a statement respecting the denial) by one or more of the Justices who prioritized the property rights principles articulated in *Tyler*.
