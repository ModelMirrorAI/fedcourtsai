# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition without a separate writing. The
probability of any grant, including a GVR or summary reversal, is **16%**.
Denial would leave the judgment in place, not endorse either concurrence's
reasoning.

The snapshot shows **two distributions**: June 9 for the June 25 conference,
and September 9 for the September 28 conference. A response was requested on
June 17 and the government's opposition arrived on August 26. Thus the second
distribution followed completion of the requested briefing; it is not strong
evidence of a merits-driven relist after full consideration.

I expect **no additional distribution** before disposition, with a **30%**
probability of at least one more distribution beyond the two recorded. In the
additional-distribution branch, one further distribution is the modal increment;
a longer hold for related litigation is also possible. The modal timing is an
order following the September 28 conference, around late September or early
October 2026. A hold could instead carry the petition into 2027. Timing is a
context forecast, not a scored claim.

I expect **no CVSG**: probability **0.5%**. The Solicitor General already
represents the federal respondent and has supplied the requested opposition.
A request for that party's response is not an invitation for the government's
views as amicus.

**Conditional on a grant**, I assign **60%** to disposition in the cert order
itself rather than plenary briefing and argument. The principal summary route
is a hold followed by a GVR in light of the related alternative-remedies issue
in Nielsen v. Watanabe, whose grant is reported in the provisioned opposition.
That is a forecast of a possible future route, not a claim that Nielsen has
already been decided. An immediate summary reversal is considerably less
plausible than that route. The unconditional summary-route probability is
9.6%, not 60%.

If the Court takes this case for plenary review, I expect it to focus on QP 1:
whether the surviving false-arrest claim based on a fabricated confession
falls within the established Fourth Amendment Bivens context. QP 2's role for
alternative remedies is a less clean standalone vehicle because the affirming
judges supplied different rationales. A grant would not itself imply that the
Court will revive the claim.

**Conditional on denial**, I assign **12%** to any noted dissent or statement
respecting denial. The modal forecast is an unexplained denial; I make no
individual Justice vote or writing forecast.
