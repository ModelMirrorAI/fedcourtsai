# Forecast — No. 24-1030, Parker-Hannifin Corp. v. Johnson

The docket shows two distributions (conferences of May 2 and June 26, 2025), a called-for response, and a CVSG issued June 30, 2025 with Justice Alito not participating.

- **Solicitor General's brief.** The SG will file in late 2025 (most likely December, possibly as late as May 2026). The brief will more likely than not recommend granting, taking the position that a complaint resting on comparative underperformance must plead facts showing the comparator is a meaningful benchmark.
- **Further distribution.** The petition will be distributed at least once more after the SG's brief, for a conference in January 2026 (or June 2026 if the brief comes in May). One post-brief distribution, at most one further relist.
- **Disposition.** Slightly better than even that the Court grants certiorari, with Justice Alito again noted as not participating. If the SG recommends denial on vehicle grounds (interlocutory posture; the Sixth Circuit's alternative holding), the Court denies.
- **No second CVSG**; none is possible in practice.
- **Question presented.** If granted, the Court takes the petition's single question as written: whether an ERISA imprudent-investment claim based on comparative returns requires allegations showing the benchmark is a sound basis for comparison.
- **Route.** Plenary review with briefing and argument, not a GVR or summary reversal; no intervening decision bears on the question.
- **Separate writings.** If denied, no dissent from or statement respecting the denial. A grant order carries no writing.
- **Conditional on a grant:** the likelier outcome is a reversal or vacatur holding that Twombly/Iqbal plausibility and Hughes's "range of reasonable judgments" require a like-for-like comparator when underperformance is the basis of the claim, in an eight-Justice Court.
