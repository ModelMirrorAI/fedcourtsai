The Supreme Court is modestly more likely than not to deny Parker-Hannifin's request for review, with about a two-in-five (38%) chance of taking the case. Although the Court asked the Solicitor General (the federal government's top appellate lawyer) for advice on whether retirement-plan lawsuits under ERISA (the federal employee-benefits law) must compare challenged investments to similar benchmarks, Justice Alito's recusal leaves an eight-Justice bench, another claim involving the same funds will proceed below regardless, and the lower court relied partly on case-specific facts.
