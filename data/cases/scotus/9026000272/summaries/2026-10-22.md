## What happened

The Department of War adopted a policy disqualifying from military service people with gender dysphoria, a history of it, or related medical treatment. Several current and prospective servicemembers sued, arguing the policy violates equal protection. A district court issued a nationwide preliminary injunction blocking the policy entirely.
