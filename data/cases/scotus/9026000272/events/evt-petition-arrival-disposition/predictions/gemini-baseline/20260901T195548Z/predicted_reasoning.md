# Predicted Reasoning

This petition is at the arrival stage with zero distributions so far. We expect no distributions before an initial conference, but given the significant constitutional issue, it is highly likely to be granted, potentially after a relist. However, SG petitions on major struck-down federal policies are often granted without extensive relists. I expect 1 further relist before a decision on the petition.

A CVSG will not be called for, because the Solicitor General is already the petitioner representing the United States.

If granted, the Court will take the single question presented as written regarding the Equal Protection challenge to the Department of War policy. A summary GVR is unlikely, as this is a novel constitutional challenge requiring plenary review rather than an application of intervening precedent.

Should the Court unexpectedly deny review, there would almost certainly be a dissent from denial (or a statement respecting denial) given the lower court's invalidation of a major executive branch policy.
