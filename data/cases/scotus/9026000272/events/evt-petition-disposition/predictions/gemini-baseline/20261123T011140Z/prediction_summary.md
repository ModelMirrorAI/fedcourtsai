The Supreme Court is very likely—roughly a four-in-five chance (78%)—to grant certiorari, meaning agree to hear the federal government's appeal, in *United States v. Talbott*. A federal appeals court blocked the military from enforcing its ban on service members with gender dysphoria against roughly thirty active-duty plaintiffs, even though the Supreme Court previously allowed the same policy to take effect in a parallel case. Despite an upcoming January trial, the Court will likely step in now to review this major military policy.
