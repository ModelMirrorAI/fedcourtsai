The Supreme Court is very likely (roughly six chances in seven) to agree to hear the government's appeal in United States v. Talbott, which asks whether the military's policy disqualifying people with gender dysphoria from service is constitutional. The government itself is asking, a lower court blocked part of a major federal policy, and the Justices already let the same policy take effect in a related case. The main reason for doubt is that the ruling is preliminary and protects only 28 servicemembers, with a trial set for January.
