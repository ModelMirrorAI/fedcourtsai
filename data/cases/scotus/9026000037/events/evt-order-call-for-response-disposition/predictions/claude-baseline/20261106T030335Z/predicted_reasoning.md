# Forecast — New Mexico v. Neal, No. 26-37

- **Disposition:** I expect the Court to deny certiorari after the response comes in, most likely at a conference in October or November 2026.
- **Distributions:** The docket shows one distribution (for 9/28/2026), followed by the call for a response. I expect it to be redistributed once the brief in opposition and any reply are filed, and then denied at that conference without a relist. A single relist is possible if a Justice considers a summary disposition.
- **CVSG:** None. This is a state criminal case and no federal interest is directly implicated.
- **If granted:** The Court would take the question as written but narrow it to the multiple-punishment setting: whether Blockburger's same-elements comparison looks only at statutory elements, and whether a state court may rest a federal Double Jeopardy holding on the evidence and arguments at trial. It would likely have to address the adequate-and-independent-state-ground objection first.
- **Summary route:** Unlikely. A per curiam reversal is conceivable, but the New Mexico court's reading of its own legislature's intent and its state-constitution citation make a summary reversal awkward. Plenary review is the likelier route if the Court acts.
- **Separate writing:** If the petition is denied, I expect no dissent from denial and no statement respecting it. If there is one, the likeliest authors are Justice Gorsuch, Justice Thomas or Justice Alito.
