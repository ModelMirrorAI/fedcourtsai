New Mexico asks the Supreme Court to reinstate a man's kidnapping conviction. The state's top court threw it out because convicting him of both rape and kidnapping punished the same act twice, which the Constitution's "double jeopardy" rule forbids. The Court has asked the man's lawyers to respond, which shows some interest. Even so, I think the Court will probably turn the case down, about a one-in-nine chance of review. The main reason is that the state court's ruling rests partly on New Mexico's own law, which the Supreme Court generally cannot override.
