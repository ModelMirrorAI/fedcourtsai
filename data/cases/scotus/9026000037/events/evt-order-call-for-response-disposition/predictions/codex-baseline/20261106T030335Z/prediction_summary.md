I expect the Supreme Court to decline New Mexico's appeal, with about a one-in-three chance that it agrees to review the case. The dispute concerns when two criminal convictions count as punishing someone twice for the same offense. Courts use different methods, and the justices' request for a response shows interest. Still, details of New Mexico's statutes and the trial instructions could make this a poor case for announcing a national rule. A denial would probably come without an explanation.
