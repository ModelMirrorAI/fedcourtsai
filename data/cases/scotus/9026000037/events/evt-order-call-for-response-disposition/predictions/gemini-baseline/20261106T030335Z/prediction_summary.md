We predict that the Supreme Court is very likely to deny New Mexico's petition for review (an 88% chance of denial, or about a one-in-eight chance of a grant). New Mexico asks the Court to clarify how the Constitution's Double Jeopardy Clause—which forbids punishing a person twice for the same crime—applies when a defendant is convicted of overlapping state charges in a single trial. Although the Court asked Samuel Neal's lawyers to file a response, the case is a poor candidate for review because the New Mexico Supreme Court relied on state-law rules for interpreting state statutes and the state constitution, reinstated one of the two disputed convictions, and faulted the prosecution's case-specific jury instructions on the other.
