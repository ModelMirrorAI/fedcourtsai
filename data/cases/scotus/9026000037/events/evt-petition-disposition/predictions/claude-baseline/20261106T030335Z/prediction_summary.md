New Mexico asks the Supreme Court to overturn a state-court ruling that threw out a rape conviction. The state court found that the conviction punished the defendant twice for the same crime along with his kidnapping conviction. We think the Court will probably turn the case away, giving about a one-in-seven chance that it takes it. The Court did ask the defendant to respond, which shows some interest. But the state court also relied on New Mexico's own constitution and laws, which the Supreme Court generally cannot review. That makes the case a weak candidate.
