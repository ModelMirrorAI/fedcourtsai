I adjust this drastically upward because this petition presents an extraordinary vehicle: the en banc Fifth Circuit explicitly declined to follow binding Supreme Court precedent (*Stone v. Graham*, holding Ten Commandments displays in public schools unconstitutional), asserting it was implicitly abrogated by *Kennedy v. Bremerton*. Under the rule of vertical stare decisis (*Rodriguez de Quijas*), lower courts may not declare Supreme Court precedents implicitly overruled; only the Supreme Court can overrule its own decisions. This defiance makes Supreme Court intervention almost inevitable. The Court will likely grant plenary review to either reaffirm *Stone* or, more probably given the current Court's trajectory on the Establishment Clause and reliance on historical practices, formally overrule *Stone v. Graham*. I discount slightly only for the possibility that the Court might find a vehicle flaw not apparent on the face of the petition, but the split and the posture make this a prime candidate for review.
