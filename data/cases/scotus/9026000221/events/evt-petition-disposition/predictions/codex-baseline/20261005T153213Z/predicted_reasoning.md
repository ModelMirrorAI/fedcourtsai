# Forecast

The Court will deny Theodore James Purdy's petition. P(any grant), including a GVR or summary reversal, is **3%**.

The provisioned docket records **one distribution**, on September 16, 2026, for the October 9, 2026 conference; it records no CVSG. I expect **zero additional distributions**, with denial in an order list following that conference, most likely during October 2026. The probability of at least one additional distribution is **14%**; if the petition continues, one additional distribution is more likely than a prolonged series. Timing is an unscored forecast, not a claim that a particular orders date has been verified.

I expect **no CVSG**: the probability of a new invitation is **0.3%**. On the less likely path toward serious consideration, I expect a request for Kansas's response after its waiver rather than an invitation to the Solicitor General.

If review is granted, the likely question is whether an officer's repetition of an absent witness's accusation can remain non-truth background when the prosecution uses it as substantive corroboration. I expect the Court to focus on that use-of-evidence issue rather than announce a categorical rule that all statements by young children to police are testimonial. The testimonial character of the particular statements and the effect on the ineffective-assistance prejudice inquiry would still need resolution. This is a conditional question forecast, not a prediction that the Court will issue a merits opinion following denial.

**Conditional on any grant**, I assign **35%** to disposition in the cert order itself and **65%** to plenary review. A summary correction is plausible if the Court views the decision as disregarding Smith v. Arizona, but the contested statement provenance and alternative prejudice reasoning make a summary route less likely than briefing and argument. I do not predict a hold for any identified pending lead case.

**Conditional on denial**, I assign **6%** to an accompanying noted dissent or statement respecting denial. The modal forecast is a bare denial with no separate writing; I make no individual-Justice prediction. Such a denial would supply no explanation adopting the Kansas court's reasoning.
