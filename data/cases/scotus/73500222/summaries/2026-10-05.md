## In brief

The Court declined to hear civilly committed plaintiffs' challenge to a $366,461.96 cost award imposed against them after their long-running civil rights lawsuit failed.

## What happened

Kevin Scott Karsjens and other patients civilly committed under Minnesota's sex offender treatment program sued state officials in 2011 over conditions of their confinement. After years of litigation, the officials prevailed on all claims. The officials then sought to recover the costs of court-appointed experts used during the case. The district court denied those costs entirely, citing the plaintiffs' indigence and the potential chilling effect on future civil rights litigants. The Eighth Circuit reversed, finding the district court had overlooked an earlier agreement by the parties to split expert costs, and it directed that costs of $366,461.96 be awarded to the officials. The Eighth Circuit denied rehearing.

## What the Court is being asked

The petition raises two issues: first, whether a court deciding how to divide litigation costs may weigh the discouraging effect a cost award could have on future civil rights plaintiffs; second, whether an appeals court may resolve disputed factual questions itself to support a cost award, rather than sending the case back to the trial court to sort out those facts.

## Where it stands

The respondents waived their right to respond to the petition. The petition was distributed for the Court's September 28, 2026 conference, and the Court denied it on October 5, 2026.

## What each outcome would mean

Karsjens and the other plaintiffs asked the Court to take the case and overturn the Eighth Circuit's ruling, which favored the state officials. Because the Court denied the petition, the Eighth Circuit's decision stands: the plaintiffs remain subject to the $366,461.96 cost award in favor of the officials, and the case will not receive further review by the Supreme Court. The denial does not mean the Court agreed with the Eighth Circuit's reasoning; it simply leaves that ruling in place.
