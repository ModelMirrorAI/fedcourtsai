The Supreme Court is very likely (about a 91% chance) to disturb the lower court ruling and reverse the Seventh Circuit's judgment by a 6–3 vote, holding that Cook County's ban on possessing AR-15 platform and similar semiautomatic rifles violates the Second and Fourteenth Amendments. Under the Court's precedents in *Heller*, *Bruen*, and *Wolford v. Lopez* (2026), semiautomatic rifles that fire one round per trigger pull are bearable arms owned by tens of millions of law-abiding Americans for lawful purposes including self-defense, and weapons in common use cannot be banned as "dangerous and unusual." Chief Justice Roberts and Justices Thomas, Alito, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh, and Barrett are expected to form the majority—with Justice Kavanaugh the likeliest author—while Justices Sotomayor, Kagan, and Jackson are expected to dissent.
