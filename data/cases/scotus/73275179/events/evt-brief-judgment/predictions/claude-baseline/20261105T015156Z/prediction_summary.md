The Supreme Court will very likely (about 88% chance) rule against Cook County's ban on AR-15-style rifles and reverse the appeals court decision that upheld it. The likely vote is 6–3, with the six conservative Justices in the majority. The main reasons: the Court waited through 22 conferences to pick this case, the federal government supports the gun owners, and the Court's recent decisions say that guns commonly owned by law-abiding people cannot be banned outright.
