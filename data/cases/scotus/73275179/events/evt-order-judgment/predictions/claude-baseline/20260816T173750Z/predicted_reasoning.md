# Forecast: what the Court will do with the judgment below

**Judgment class: `reversed`.** The Court will disturb the Seventh Circuit's
judgment, most likely by reversing outright and remanding. It granted plenary
review — after twenty-two distributions across seven months, and by
consolidating this case with No. 25-566 (*National Association for Gun Rights
v. Lamont*, the Second Circuit's decision upholding Connecticut's parallel
ban) — to answer the question presented as written: whether the Second and
Fourteenth Amendments guarantee the right to possess AR-15-platform and
similar semiautomatic rifles. Every circuit to reach the question has upheld
these bans, so the grant cannot be split-resolution in petitioners' favor
below; it is the Court stepping in over a uniform body of circuit law, and
this Court does not ordinarily grant a case in that posture to agree with it.
The majority will hold that AR-15-platform rifles are "Arms" in common use for
lawful purposes under *Heller* and *Bruen*, reject the *Bevis*
military/civilian line (the AR-15/M16 equivalence), and hold the ban
unconstitutional. A framework-only vacatur — wrong test, remand for a proper
historical analysis on this record — is the likeliest alternative within the
disturbed mass, and I put roughly a quarter of that mass on `vacated` rather
than `reversed`.

**The one scored claim, `judgment-disturbed`: 0.86.** The undisturbed routes

Barrett to embrace the militaristic-arms carve-out or a

equally divided affirmance (~1%: no recusal is apparent).

**Vote lineup: 6–3.** Roberts, Thomas, Alito, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh, and Barrett
in the majority; Sotomayor, Kagan, and Jackson in dissent. Kavanaugh is the
natural author — his *Heller II* dissent as a D.C. Circuit judge concluded
that AR-15 bans are unconstitutional under a text-and-history method, and he
has publicly flagged the question as one the Court should resolve. Expect a
Thomas concurrence pressing a broader common-use rule, and a principal
dissent by Sotomayor joined by Kagan and Jackson, with a possible separate
Jackson dissent on methodology. (Authorship and writings resolve against no
