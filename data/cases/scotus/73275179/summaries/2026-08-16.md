## In brief

Gun owners ask the Court to decide whether banning AR-15-style rifles violates the right to keep and bear arms; the Court agreed to hear the case.

## What happened

Cutberto Viramontes and other gun owners sued Cook County, Illinois, arguing that its ban on AR-15-style rifles and similar semiautomatic weapons violates the Second Amendment. Seventh Circuit precedent already rejected similar challenges, and while this case was pending, that same appeals court ruled in a related case, Bevis, that Cook County's ban likely survives constitutional review because such rifles resemble military weapons more than tools for self-defense. The trial court ruled for Cook County, and the appeals court affirmed, finding Viramontes had not built a record showing his case differed from Bevis. Viramontes then asked the Supreme Court to review that decision.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether the constitutional right to keep and bear arms covers AR-15-style rifles and similar semiautomatic weapons, meaning bans like Cook County's would be invalid, or whether such weapons can be treated like military-grade arms that fall outside that protection and may be banned.

## Where it stands

The Court granted the petition, consolidating it with a related case, and allotted one hour for oral argument. The parties have since received extensions for filing merits briefs, with Viramontes's brief due in late August 2026 and Cook County's response due in October 2026; the case has not yet been argued or decided.

## What each outcome would mean

Viramontes and the other gun owners asked the Court to take the case. Because the Court granted review, it will decide the constitutional question itself rather than leave the Seventh Circuit's ruling in place. If the Court ultimately rules for Viramontes, Cook County's ban would be found unconstitutional as applied to these rifles. If it rules for Cook County, the ban would stand. If the Court instead vacates the lower ruling, the case would return to the lower courts without a final answer on the question either way.
