# Forecast — 26A634, Guardado v. Comerford (stay of execution)

- **Disposition:** The Court will deny the stay of execution, most likely on the evening of
  Nov 5, 2026 or shortly before, in an order reading that the application "presented to
  Justice Thomas and by him referred to the Court is denied," paired with a denial of cert in
  26-6051.
- **Referral:** Yes — Justice Thomas will refer the application to the full Court rather than
  act alone (~85%).
- **Response:** Florida will file a response in opposition on its own; a formal Court call for
  a response is unlikely (~7%).
- **Amicus:** No amicus briefs expected (~4% of any).
- **Separate writing:** Most likely none. If anyone writes, Justice Sotomayor (dissent focused
  on Florida's execution pace / record-keeping and the compressed discovery) is the likeliest,
  then Justice Jackson. A noted dissent without opinion by one or more of the liberal Justices
  is plausible.
- **Legal standard (context):** The applicant cannot show a significant possibility of success
  on the cert petition: the lower courts' findings that the log problems were record-keeping
  errors and that etomidate was administered are factual findings, and the Eleventh Circuit's
  independent delay ground gives the Court an additional reason not to intervene. If (very
  unlikely) a stay issued, it would be pending disposition of the cert petition only.
