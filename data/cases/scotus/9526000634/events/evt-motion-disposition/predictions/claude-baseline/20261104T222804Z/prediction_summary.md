Jesse Guardado, a Florida prisoner scheduled for execution on November 5, 2026, asked the Supreme Court to pause (stay) his execution. He says Florida's handling of its lethal-injection drugs creates a risk of a painful death. We predict the Court will deny the request, with about a 1% chance it grants one. The lower courts held a hearing and found the drug-record problems were clerical. The Court almost never stops executions on claims like this.
