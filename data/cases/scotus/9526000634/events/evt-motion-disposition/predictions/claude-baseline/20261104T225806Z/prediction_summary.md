Jesse Guardado, scheduled for execution in Florida on November 5, 2026, asked the Supreme Court to pause (stay) the execution while it considers his appeal claiming Florida mishandles its lethal-injection drugs. We predict the Court will say no — the chance of a stay is very small, about 1 to 2 in 100. Lower courts held a hearing and found the drug-log problems were paperwork errors, and the Court almost never halts executions over challenges to execution methods.
