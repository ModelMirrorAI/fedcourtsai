The Supreme Court is extremely unlikely to grant Jesse Guardado's emergency request to halt his November 5, 2026 execution, with only about a 2% chance (roughly one in fifty) of a stay. After holding a seven-hour hearing and privately inspecting unredacted purchase records, a federal trial judge found that apparent errors in Florida's lethal-injection drug logs were clerical mistakes and that the sedative (etomidate) set aside for Guardado does not expire until January 2027. All three judges on the federal appeals court agreed that those factual findings were reasonable, leaving almost no prospect that the Supreme Court will step in to pause the execution.
