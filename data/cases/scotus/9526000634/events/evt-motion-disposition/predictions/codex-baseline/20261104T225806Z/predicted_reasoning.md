# Forecast of the Court's handling

The frozen record shows the November 3, 2026 submission to Justice Thomas, no response request, no referral, and zero amicus entries. From that state:

- **Response request: 80%.** I expect an expedited request for Florida's response, given the execution scheduled for November 5 at 6 p.m. The request would permit adversarial consideration, not signal likely relief.
- **Full-Court referral: 90%.** I expect Justice Thomas to refer this substantive capital application to the Court, rather than dispose of it alone.
- **Amicus increment: 18%.** Zero additional counted amicus entries is most likely; one is the likeliest positive increment. A distinct submitted brief counts even before acceptance, and the submitted-to-accepted lifecycle of the same filer should not be counted twice.
- **Stay granted in whole or part: 8%, conditional on no moot disposition.** I predict denial, probably through a brief unsigned order. A brief administrative hold alone would not be the substantive stay pending certiorari requested here.

I expect disposition by the stated November 5 execution deadline, most likely on November 5, following a very short response schedule. This is a forecast from the frozen record, not a report of later docket activity. Timing is not one of the scored claims.

The modal order gives no extended merits reasoning. If the Court explains a denial, I expect it to emphasize inadequate demonstration of likely success against the lower courts' adverse factual findings rather than hold that execution pain is irrelevant. If it instead grants relief, a temporary opportunity for meaningful review of the disclosure and evidentiary dispute is more plausible than a final nationwide holding about lethal injection. These explanations are conditional, not claims that a reasoned opinion will issue.

No separate writing is the modal outcome. If a Justice writes, an objection focusing on imminent irreversible harm and the compressed discovery process is more likely than a lengthy defense of denial. Independent authorship probabilities are Sotomayor 30%, Jackson 22%, Kagan 5%, Thomas 2%, and Roberts, Alito, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh, and Barrett 1% each. Joining someone else's writing is not authorship. I do not predict a fixed public vote tally.
