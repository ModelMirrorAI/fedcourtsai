Paul Schultz, representing himself, asked the Supreme Court to pause ("stay") a
lower-court ruling in a bankruptcy dispute with a bankruptcy trustee. We predict
the request will be denied — the chance of it being granted is very small, about
one in fifty. Emergency requests from individuals without lawyers almost never
succeed, the dispute appears personal rather than raising a broad legal question,
and the Court has shown no sign of interest (it has not asked the other side to
respond).
