The Supreme Court is almost certain to deny Paul Schultz's emergency application for a stay (1% chance of a grant). Schultz, representing himself in ongoing personal bankruptcy litigation, seeks emergency relief after a federal appeals court refused to remove the trial judge overseeing his bankruptcy appeal. The Supreme Court grants emergency stays only when at least four Justices are likely to take up the case and reverse the lower court's ruling, and routine judicial-disqualification disputes in personal bankruptcy cases almost never meet that bar.
