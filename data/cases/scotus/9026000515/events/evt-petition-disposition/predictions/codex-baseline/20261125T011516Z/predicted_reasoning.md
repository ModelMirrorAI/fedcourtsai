I expect the Court to deny the petition after the December 11 conference. The docket shows one distribution. I expect zero more, no request for a response, and no dissent or statement from any Justice. If a Justice wants briefing, the Court can request a response from the United States as the opposing party. There is no occasion to invite the Solicitor General's views as a friend of the Court.

If the Court grants review, I expect briefing and argument on whether the FBI needed probable cause to suspect the officer himself before it could take custody of his service weapon as evidence of the shooting. That would narrow the first question to this particular seizure. The Court could address that question without deciding what privacy rights any person might acquire by taking possession of evidence. The panel's fact-specific ruling leaves little basis for a summary reversal, and the record identifies no intervening decision calling for a remand.
