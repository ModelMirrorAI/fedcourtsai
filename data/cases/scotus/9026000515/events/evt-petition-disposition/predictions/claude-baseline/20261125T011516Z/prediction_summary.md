The Supreme Court will very likely refuse to hear this case — I put the chance of review at well under one in a hundred. The family of Todd Murray asks the Court to rule that FBI agents could lawfully have taken a police officer's gun at the scene of Murray's death. But the lower-court ruling is unpublished and sets no binding rule, no conflict among appeals courts is shown, and the government did not even file a response, which usually signals a routine denial.
