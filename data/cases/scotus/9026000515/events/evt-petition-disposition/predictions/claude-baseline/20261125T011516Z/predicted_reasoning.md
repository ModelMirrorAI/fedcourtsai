# Forecast — No. 26-515, Jones v. United States

The docket shows one distribution (for the December 11, 2026 conference), with the United States having waived its response. The petition has never been relisted.

- **Disposition.** The Court will deny certiorari on the order list following the December 11 conference (expected December 14, 2026), without calling for a response.
- **Relists.** No further distribution. The only realistic path to another distribution is a call for a response from the Solicitor General, which I do not expect.
- **CVSG.** None; the United States is already the respondent.
- **Question presented.** If the Court took anything, it would be QP 1 (whether investigators at a homicide scene may seize evidence without probable cause as to the person holding it), probably reframed around the plain-view doctrine; QP 2 (the officer's expectation of privacy in his own firearm) would be left behind. The more likely obstacle is that the Fourth Amendment point is embedded in a spoliation-sanctions ruling in a treaty damages suit and may not control the judgment.
- **Summary disposition.** Unlikely. No intervening decision supports a GVR, and the nonprecedential, fact-heavy ruling is not a candidate for summary reversal.
- **Separate writings.** The denial will be unsigned and unaccompanied by any dissent or statement.
