# Forecast

The Court will deny the petition. The central obstacle to review is that correcting the challenged discussion of reasonable mistakes of law would not itself remove the decision's separate immunity grounds. The dispute is more likely to be treated as an application of existing immunity principles to a particular prosecution than as a clean occasion to announce a new national rule.

The provisioned docket has **zero distributions and no CVSG**. I expect one initial distribution after the response stage, followed by denial without a further distribution. The 0.97 probability for `relist-increment` means at least one distribution above the frozen zero count; it is not a 97% prediction of a second conference or a true relist. An additional distribution after the first is possible but is not the modal path. I expect disposition around December 2026 through February 2027, allowing for the November 16, 2026 response deadline and possible briefing extensions. This timing is a forecast, not a docketed conference date.

I expect no invitation to the Solicitor General: P(a new CVSG) = 0.003. The dispute concerns state and local officials' liability, and I do not expect the Court to seek federal institutional advice before disposing of this petition.

If review is granted, the most plausible question is a narrowed version of the first question presented: whether qualified immunity permits dismissal of a malicious-prosecution claim alleging knowing omission of an essential statutory condition, and how that differs from objectively reasonable legal error. The Court would be less likely to take all three questions together. The separate prosecutorial-immunity question would require distinguishing investigative conduct from the preparation of an indictment, not simply equating an unsuccessful prosecution with loss of immunity.

Conditional on any grant, I assign **0.25** to disposition in the cert order itself and **0.75** to plenary review. I do not predict an automatic GVR based on Chiaverini, which predates the decision below and addresses a different categorical bar. A summary correction remains possible if the Court views the pleading treatment as plainly incompatible with governing precedent, but it is not the leading grant route.

Conditional on denial, I assign **0.04** to any noted dissent or statement respecting denial. The modal forecast is an unexplained denial without separate writing. I make no individual-Justice vote or authorship forecast.
