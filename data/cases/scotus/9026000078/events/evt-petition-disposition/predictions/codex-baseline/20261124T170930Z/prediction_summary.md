I expect the Supreme Court to turn down Michael Hunt’s appeal, with about one chance in five that it grants review. His jury voted 10–2 for death. When he committed the crime, that vote would have required a life sentence. Florida later lowered the threshold to eight votes. The change mattered directly to Hunt, but he points to no other state court that has ruled the opposite way on the same sentencing rule. Another pending Florida case also raises the broader question of jury unanimity.
