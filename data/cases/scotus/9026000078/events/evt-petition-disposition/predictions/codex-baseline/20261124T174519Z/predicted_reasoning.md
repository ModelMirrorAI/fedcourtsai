The Court will most likely deny Hunt’s petition after the December 11 conference, with zero further distributions. The docket shows two distributions so far. The first conference date was rescheduled before it arrived. If the Justices need more time to consider Hunt alongside *Jackson v. Florida*, one additional distribution is the likeliest delay. I expect no invitation to the Solicitor General and no separate writing on Hunt’s order. Justice Sotomayor is the likeliest author if a Justice does write respecting denial.

If the Court takes Hunt for argument, I expect it to take his second question as presented, whether Florida can apply the eight-vote death recommendation to a crime committed while unanimity was required. The 10–2 vote squarely presents the effect of the change. Jackson gives the Court a vehicle for the broader Sixth and Eighth Amendment question. Full briefing is likelier than a summary disposition on a grant. The strongest route to a summary order would be to hold Hunt for Jackson, then vacate and return Hunt’s judgment to Florida if the decision in Jackson calls his sentence into question.
