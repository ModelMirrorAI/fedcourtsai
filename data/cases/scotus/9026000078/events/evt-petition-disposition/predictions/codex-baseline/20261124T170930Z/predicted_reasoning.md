I expect the Court to deny Hunt’s petition without a separate writing. The docket shows two distributions, first for December 4 and then, after a rescheduling, for December 11. I expect zero further distributions and no call for the Solicitor General’s views. The more likely reason for another listing would be to consider Hunt alongside *Jackson v. Florida*. If a Justice writes on Hunt’s denial, Justice Sotomayor is the most likely author.

If the Court sets Hunt for argument, I expect it to take his second question as written. May Florida apply its eight-vote rule to a crime committed when all twelve jurors had to agree on death? Jackson already presents the broader Sixth and Eighth Amendment question. Full review is slightly more likely than a summary order conditional on a grant in Hunt. The likeliest summary route is to hold Hunt pending a decision in Jackson and then send his case back to the Florida Supreme Court for reconsideration.
