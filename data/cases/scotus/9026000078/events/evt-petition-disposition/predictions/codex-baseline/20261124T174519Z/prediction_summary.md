The Supreme Court will likely decline to review Michael Hunt’s death sentence. I put the chance it takes his case at about one in five. His jury voted 10–2 for death. When he committed the crime, that vote would have required a life sentence. Florida later lowered the threshold to eight votes. Hunt’s case squarely raises whether Florida could apply that change to him, but courts in other states have yet to disagree directly about that sentencing question. Another pending petition raises the broader question of jury unanimity.
