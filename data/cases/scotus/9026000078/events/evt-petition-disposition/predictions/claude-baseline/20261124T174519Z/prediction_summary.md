The Supreme Court will probably decline to hear Michael Hunt's death-penalty appeal — a grant is about a one-in-six chance. Hunt argues Florida could not apply its 2023 law, which lets a jury recommend death by an 8-4 vote instead of unanimously, to a crime committed in 2019 (the Constitution bars "ex post facto," or retroactive, punishment laws). His lawyers are top-tier and a companion case raises the broader unanimity issue, but the conflict among lower courts is thin and existing precedent favors Florida.
