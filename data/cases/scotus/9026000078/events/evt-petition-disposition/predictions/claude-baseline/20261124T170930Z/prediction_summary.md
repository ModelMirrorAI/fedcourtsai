The Supreme Court will probably decline to hear Michael Hunt's appeal; the chance it takes the case is a little under one in five. Hunt was sentenced to death in Florida after a 10-2 jury vote under a 2023 law, though the law in force when the crime occurred required a unanimous jury. His lawyers are highly regarded and the retroactivity argument is serious, but other courts have not clearly disagreed on it, it affects only Florida, and the Court rarely takes death-penalty appeals from defendants. A written dissent from the refusal is fairly possible.
