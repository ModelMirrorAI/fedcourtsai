Hunt committed murder in 2019 when Florida law required all twelve jurors to vote for death, but he was sentenced after a 2023 law lowered that threshold to eight of twelve jurors and his jury voted 10–2. Although top Supreme Court specialists represent him, past Supreme Court rulings generally treat jury-voting procedures in capital sentencing as outside both the Constitution's jury-unanimity rule and its ban on retroactive ("ex post facto") punishment increases.
