We expect the Supreme Court to deny review—declining to hear Michael Hunt's appeal—with roughly a one-in-six (16%) chance of granting it. Hunt challenges his Florida death sentence after a jury voted 10-2 for death under a 2023 law allowing 8-4 death verdicts, even though Florida required a unanimous 12-0 vote when his 2019 crime occurred. Although top Supreme Court advocates represent Hunt, recent Supreme Court precedent allows nonunanimous death-penalty recommendations once a jury unanimously finds death eligibility, and older precedent permits retroactive changes to capital sentencing procedures.
