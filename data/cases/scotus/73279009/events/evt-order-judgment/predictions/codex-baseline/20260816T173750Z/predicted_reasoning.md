# Forecast of the Court's judgment and reasoning

The Court will vacate the Second Circuit's judgment and remand by a 6-3 vote, with the Chief Justice and Justices Thomas, Alito, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh, and Barrett in the majority and Justices Sotomayor, Kagan, and Jackson dissenting. Vacatur is more likely than an outright reversal in this companion because the judgment below affirmed the denial of preliminary relief and rested in part on separate equitable and public-interest grounds. The lead consolidated case supplies the cleaner summary-judgment vehicle for resolving the constitutional issue.

The majority will reach the protected-arms question and hold that AR-15-style semiautomatic rifles are bearable arms commonly possessed by law-abiding citizens for lawful purposes. It will treat the historical exception for “dangerous and unusual” weapons as unable to sustain a categorical ban on a rifle class that is widespread and ordinarily lawful. Legislative judgments about unusual dangerousness, the availability of other firearms, and analogies to Bowie knives or machine guns will not justify removing a commonly possessed semiautomatic rifle from Second Amendment protection.

The holding will extend beyond Connecticut to categorical possession bans on AR-15s and materially similar semiautomatic rifles. It will remain narrower than a rule protecting every weapon that achieves numerical popularity: select-fire machine guns and regulations that do not ban the protected rifle class will be reserved. At least one separate concurrence is likely to press a broader and more categorical common-use rule, while the dissent will emphasize the disputed self-defense record, mass-shooting lethality, historical restrictions on especially dangerous weapons, and judicial respect for legislative line-drawing.

A dismissal as improvidently granted is unlikely because consolidation with a final-judgment lead case permits the Court to decide the shared issue even if this companion's preliminary-injunction posture prevents direct final relief.
