I move above the 69.7% anchor chiefly because Justice Kavanaugh's statement, quoted in the petition, calls protection for AR-15s a “strong argument” and says the Court should address the issue soon. The petition also reports that three other Justices favored review in *Snope*, but I treat those cert votes as selection evidence rather than as votes to disturb; the merits baseline already conditions on the Court having granted review. The petition's core merits argument is straightforward: AR-15-style rifles are bearable semiautomatic arms, tens of millions are possessed for lawful purposes, “dangerous and unusual” is conjunctive, and the availability of other firearms does not save a ban. Consolidation with No. 25-238, which the opposition identifies as a summary-judgment assault-weapons case, also reduces the risk that this dispute evades a merits decision.

The State's opposition supplies the main downward adjustments. It argues that common ownership does not end the historical inquiry, that the preliminary record did not establish common self-defense use, that AR-15-style rifles have exceptional mass-shooting lethality, and that restrictions on Bowie knives, machine guns, and other especially dangerous weapons are adequate analogues. For this particular companion, it also stresses independent Winter equity and public-interest grounds. Those vehicle points make vacatur and remand more likely than a direct reversal even if the challengers win the shared constitutional issue.
