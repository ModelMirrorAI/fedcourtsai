# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny certiorari. The modal path is a denial following the scheduled October 9, 2026 conference, without a further distribution, a CVSG, or a noted separate writing. The snapshot already records **two distributions**; the further-distribution forecast concerns a third or later distribution, not a restatement of those two. I assign 20% to at least one additional distribution. If there is another, one additional distribution is more likely than a prolonged series.

The Court will not call for the Solicitor General's views (1% probability of a new CVSG). The constitutional and statutory assertions will not overcome the case-specific vehicle problems sufficiently to warrant that intermediate step.

If review is granted, the most plausible question is narrower than the compound QP: whether the Fourteenth Amendment permits conditioning healthcare employment on receipt of an emergency-use vaccine in light of the asserted federal right to refuse it. The Court is less likely to take the full formulation that also assumes an involuntary waiver of judicial remedies. Any merits discussion here is conditional on review, not a prediction that a denial will explain the Court's reasoning.

Conditional on any grant, I assign 80% to plenary consideration and 20% to a disposition in the cert order itself. No identified intervening merits holding makes a particular GVR the leading alternative. A summary procedural correction remains possible, but is not the modal grant route.

Conditional on denial, I assign 8% to some Justice noting a dissent or statement respecting denial. I predict no such writing and no named Justice's publicly recorded objection. An unexplained denial would leave the lower judgment in place without endorsing its reasoning.
