Distributed twice so far (first prior to the requested response, and now for the first time with the full briefing on October 9, 2026). I expect the petition to be denied, potentially after one further relist if a Justice is drafting a dissent from denial. 

The Court will likely deny the petition without comment. There is no circuit split, and the lower court decision is an unpublished memorandum disposition that simply applied settled law regarding state vaccination requirements. 

If granted, the Court would likely take the sole Question Presented as written to address whether the Due Process Clause prohibits states from conditioning licensed employment on taking an "investigational drug" and waiving judicial remedies. A summary disposition (GVR or summary reversal) is unlikely, as there is no intervening authority that the Ninth Circuit failed to consider. 

Should the Court deny review, there is a moderate chance of a dissent from denial (or a statement respecting the denial) by Justice Thomas, Alito, or Gorsuch, who have previously expressed interest in revisiting or clarifying the scope of *Jacobson v. Massachusetts* in the context of modern vaccine mandates.
