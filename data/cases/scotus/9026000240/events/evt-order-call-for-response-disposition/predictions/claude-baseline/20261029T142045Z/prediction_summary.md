Three Virginia students at Liberty University ask the Supreme Court to hear their challenge to state grants that fund some religious majors but not theirs, even before the appeals court rules. We think the Court is somewhat more likely to turn this petition down than to take it (about a 38% chance it is taken or sent back). A very similar case, Hall v. Fleming, is already before the Court. The Court will probably decide whether to hear Hall first and either hold this case until then or set it aside.
