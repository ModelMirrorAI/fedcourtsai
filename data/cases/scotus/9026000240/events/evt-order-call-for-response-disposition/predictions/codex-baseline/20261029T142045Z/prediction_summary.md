The Supreme Court will most likely decline to hear these students' challenge to Virginia's refusal to fund certain religious degrees. I estimate an 18% chance of review, including an order sending the dispute back for another look. The Court's request for a response and seven supporting friend-of-the-court briefs show attention to a significant religious-freedom question. But the students are asking the Court to intervene before the appeals court finishes, and a related case may offer a more straightforward path.
