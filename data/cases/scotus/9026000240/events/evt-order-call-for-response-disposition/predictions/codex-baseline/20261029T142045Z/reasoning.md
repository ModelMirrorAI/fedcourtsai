## Why P(any grant) is 0.18

The response call after waiver is a positive attention signal, but the prompt expressly warns that most called-for-response petitions still fail and the pack publishes no matched conditional rate. Seven amici, including a multistate brief, the concrete contrast between eligible and ineligible religious degrees, and the expressly presented Locke question justify moving substantially above the private baseline. The 0.18 is a judgmental adjustment, not an empirically estimated response-call rate.

The main downward adjustment is vehicle quality. The petition expressly seeks certiorari before judgment under Rule 11, and its own section V recognizes the imperative-public-importance hurdle. The appeal has no merits judgment in the provisioned record. The petition's expectation that an appellate opinion might arrive before consideration is a prediction by counsel, not proof that one exists. Its jurisdictional discussion also mixes preliminary-injunction relief for one student with review of dismissed claims for the other two. I do not conclude that jurisdiction is lacking; I discount a vehicle whose scope has yet to be clarified by a merits appellate decision.

The alleged conflict is also less clean than the petition's framing suggests. Its cited Tenth Circuit decision, Colorado Christian University, addressed exclusion of pervasively sectarian institutions; this case concerns distinctions among degree programs, and the supposedly conflicting merits ruling here is a district-court decision. The filing describes Hall as an existing appellate vehicle on a related Virginia program. On the filing's account alone, that gives the Court an alternative to taking this before-judgment petition. I did not retrieve Hall's docket or assume its disposition. Those considerations keep denial clearly modal despite a serious underlying question.

The petition invokes Trinity Lutheran, Espinoza, Carson, and Catholic Charities to argue that later religious-neutrality cases undermine a broad reading of Locke. I use that as the petition's legal argument, not as independently verified descriptions of each holding. An attempted CourtListener lookup of Carson was throttled, and general official-source web lookups returned no usable content. I therefore did not import any unverified holding, newly reported development, or remembered outcome of this litigation into the forecast.
