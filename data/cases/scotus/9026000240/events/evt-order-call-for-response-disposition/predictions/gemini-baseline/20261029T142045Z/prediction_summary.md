We predict that the Supreme Court is unlikely to grant this petition (about a 15% chance, or roughly one in seven), and will instead deny review. Although the case raises a major religious-liberty challenge to Virginia's exclusion of ministry-related college majors from state tuition grants—attracting seven friend-of-the-court briefs and prompting the Court to ask Virginia for a response—the students filed a "petition for certiorari before judgment," asking the Supreme Court to step in before the federal appeals court has even ruled. Because a companion case from Virginia (*Hall v. Fleming*) already has a final appeals-court ruling on the same core constitutional issue, the Court will likely leave this unfinished appeal to the lower court.
