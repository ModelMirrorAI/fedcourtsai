Thomas Overton, a Florida death-row prisoner scheduled for execution on November 19, 2026, has asked the Supreme Court for a stay, meaning an order pausing the execution while it considers his appeal. We predict the Court will refuse, and think a stay is very unlikely, about one chance in a hundred. His arguments attack Florida's clemency and death-warrant process, claims the Court has not shown interest in, and it has almost always turned down last-minute requests like this one from Florida prisoners.
