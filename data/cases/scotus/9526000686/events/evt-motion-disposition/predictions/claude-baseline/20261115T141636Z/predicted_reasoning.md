# Forecast — Overton v. Florida, No. 26A686 (application for stay of execution)

The Court will deny the stay. Justice Thomas will refer the application to the full Court, and the Court will issue a short unsigned order denying the application for stay of execution, together with a denial of the linked petition for certiorari (26-6154), in the same order.

- **Response:** Florida will file a brief in opposition on its own initiative within a day or two; the Court will not formally call for a response.
- **Referral:** The application will be referred to the full Court; the order will read as presented to Justice Thomas and by him referred to the Court.
- **Amicus:** No amicus briefs will be filed.
- **Timing:** The order issues between November 17 and the afternoon of November 19, 2026, most likely on the 18th or 19th, before the 6:00 p.m. execution time.
- **Writings:** Most likely no noted dissent and no separate writing. If any Justice notes a dissent or writes, it will be Justice Sotomayor, possibly joined by Justice Jackson.
- **Ground (conditional, context only):** The Court will give no reasons. The claims — that Florida's warrant-selection and clemency process violates separation of powers, due process and equal protection — were resolved below in a posture that likely includes independent state-law procedural grounds, and do not present a question four Justices would take up. Were a stay to be granted at all, it would be a brief administrative hold rather than a stay pending certiorari.
