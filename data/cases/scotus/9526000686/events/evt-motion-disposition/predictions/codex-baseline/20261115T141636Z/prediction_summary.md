The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Thomas Overton's request to pause his scheduled November 19 execution. I estimate about a three-in-one-hundred chance of a pause, assuming the request still needs a decision. The filing raises constitutional objections to Florida's clemency and execution-warrant procedures, but provides little detail showing why the Court should intervene. The Court will probably obtain Florida's response and consider the request together, yet those review steps would not themselves signal that it will grant relief.
