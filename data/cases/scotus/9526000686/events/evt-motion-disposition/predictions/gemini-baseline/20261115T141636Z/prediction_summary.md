The Supreme Court is almost certain to deny Thomas Overton's emergency request to halt his November 19, 2026 execution in Florida, with less than a one-in-a-hundred chance of a stay. Condemned prisoners rarely win last-minute stays from the current Court, and Overton's claims — arguing that Florida's clemency process (where the governor decides whether to show mercy) is unfair and overly secret — were unanimously rejected by the Florida Supreme Court as filed years too late and have repeatedly failed in recent Florida execution appeals.
