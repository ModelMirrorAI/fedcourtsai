The Supreme Court will almost certainly refuse to pause Thomas Overton's execution, set for November 19, 2026, in Florida; the chance of a pause is about one in a hundred. His request for a stay (an order delaying the execution while the Court considers his appeal) rests on challenges to the Governor's power over clemency, meaning mercy from a death sentence. The Court has turned away the same arguments from other Florida prisoners in recent weeks, and the request itself is brief and generic. No Justice is expected to write separately.
