The Supreme Court is almost certain to deny Thomas Overton's emergency request to halt his November 19, 2026 execution in Florida, with less than a one-in-one-hundred chance (0.5%) of granting a stay. Overton argues that Florida violated constitutional fairness rules by holding his clemency interview in 2013 and providing no update or formal denial notice before signing his death warrant thirteen years later. However, Florida courts ruled that he waited too long under state procedural rules to raise this objection, and the Supreme Court has rejected identical Florida clemency challenges without dissent in other executions over the past month.
