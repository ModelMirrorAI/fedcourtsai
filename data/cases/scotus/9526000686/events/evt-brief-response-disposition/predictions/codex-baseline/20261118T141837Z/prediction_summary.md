The Supreme Court is very likely to deny Thomas Overton's request to pause his scheduled November 19 execution. I put the chance of a pause at about two in a hundred. His challenge concerns Florida's process for considering mercy and access to its records, where courts generally allow officials broad discretion. His allegation that clemency is categorically unavailable raises a serious concern, but the available papers show a weak path to relief. Expect a brief order before the execution, probably after consideration by the full Court.
