**Inputs.** I read the snapshot `2026-08-21.json`, which carries four entries
through the August 20 "Response Requested", and `record/context.json`
(forward mode, band `baseline` under sal-v4, distribution_count 1, no CVSG,
Term 2026). I also read the provisioned `questions-presented.txt` and
`petition.txt`. The petition is 167 pages and was truncated at the cap; its
body (QP, reasons for granting) was intact. It argues an "acknowledged,
entrenched" split, with the Eighth Circuit on one side and the Seventh and
now the Second Circuit on the other. It says the Ninth Circuit leans toward
the Eighth, the Eleventh has questioned the broad reading, the Third
(*Whittaker*) noted the conflict but avoided it, and a 2026 ABLJ article
criticizes the expansion. It also argues the case is a clean vehicle.

**Anchor.** The petition's band is `baseline` (sal-v4). Its bracketed
`reached` rate, pooled over Terms 2017–2025 (n ≈ 12,900 weighted), is about
5.1%. Each Term's figure sits between 4.0% and 5.9%. I did not anchor on the
2026 row because it contains this case's own Term. The band was frozen on
August 21 and so already reflects the response request.

**Adjustments.**
- **Called-for response (up).** No committed table conditions on a call for
  a response. My judgement, from general knowledge of the Court's practice,
  is that a paid petition with a response requested is granted roughly
  1.5–2.5 times as often as the baseline. The call came the day after the
  first distribution, which I read as the Court acting on its own initiative
  rather than a routine courtesy.
- **Split and counsel (modest up).** The split is real and acknowledged, and
  the Second Circuit opinion is published. The ca2 originating cut shows a
  2.7% grant rate on the whole docket, slightly above the average.
- **Vehicle (down).** The split is about 40 years old and the Court has
  repeatedly passed on it. The underlying Kwok bankruptcy carries extensive
  fraud findings, which makes the case unsympathetic. The trustee can also
  offer alternative grounds (§ 541, estoppel) to argue that the outcome would
  not change.

**Forward retrieval.** I fetched the live docket on supremecourt.gov (forward
mode, unrestricted). It shows activity after my snapshot: the response time
was extended to October 21; a creditors' committee in another bankruptcy
(Vanderbilt Minerals) filed an amicus brief on September 21, apparently
supporting the petitioners; and the trustee's brief in opposition was filed
on October 21. There is no disposition yet, so the cell is not
mis-provisioned. The amicus brief pushes slightly upward: the "1" bucket of
the amicus cut is 10.3% against 2.0% for "0", but that bucket is terminal and
confounded with other signals. I did not read the BIO's text.

**Result.** About 0.10. I discount the CVSG to about 4% and the GVR route to
about 4% conditional on a grant, since nothing intervening calls for one.
Dissent from denial is about 3%, because a technical bankruptcy question
rarely draws one.

**Main uncertainty.** My largest uncertainty is how strongly the call for a
response predicts a grant. That rests on my own prior rather than a committed
table.
