# Predicted course: Guo v. Despins (No. 26-194)

**Where the docket stands.** The petition was distributed once, for the
conference of September 28, 2026, after the trustee waived a response. The
Court then called for a response on August 20. No relist and no CVSG appear
on the record.

**Disposition: denial is likely (about 90%).** Once the trustee's response
arrives, the petition will be distributed again and most likely denied at its
first conference after briefing, in November or early December 2026. The
call for a response shows that someone in the Court read the petition and did
not want to deny it on the waiver alone. That is a low bar. The Court has
tolerated the § 544(a) split between the Eighth Circuit (*Ozark Restaurant*)
and the Seventh Circuit (*Koch Refining*) for decades. This case also comes
out of the unusual Ho Wan Kwok bankruptcy, where the alter-ego findings are
fact-heavy, and the trustee can defend the judgment on other grounds the
Second Circuit did not reach: § 541 standing and collateral estoppel on the
yacht.

**Relists.** The petition will be distributed at least once more, since that
is mechanical once the response is in. I expect one or two distributions
before a decision rather than a run of relists. A grant would most likely
follow one relist.

**CVSG: unlikely (about 4%).** No federal party is involved. The United
States Trustee program has some interest in how § 544 is read, but the Court
rarely invites the Solicitor General's views on a dispute between private
parties in bankruptcy.

**If granted.** The Court would take the single question presented as
written: whether § 544(a) lets a trustee bring a state-law alter-ego claim
just because it belongs to creditors generally. That would put to the test
the Second and Seventh Circuits' "general versus personal claim" line against
the Eighth Circuit's text-based limit, with *Caplin v. Marine Midland* as the
central precedent. A summary disposition is very unlikely, because no
intervening decision calls for a GVR.

**Separate writings.** If the petition is denied, I expect no dissent from
denial or statement respecting it. If anyone writes, Justice Gorsuch or
Justice Thomas, writing on the statutory text, is the likeliest.
