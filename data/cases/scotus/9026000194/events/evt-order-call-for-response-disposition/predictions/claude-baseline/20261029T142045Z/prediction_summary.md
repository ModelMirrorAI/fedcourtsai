We expect the Supreme Court to turn down this appeal, though not with
certainty: we put the chance it agrees to hear the case at about one in ten.
The case asks whether a bankruptcy trustee may sue on claims that belong to a
bankrupt person's creditors as a group. Federal appeals courts disagree on
this, and the Court asked the other side to respond, which shows some
interest. But the disagreement is decades old, and this case's messy facts
make it a weaker candidate for review.
