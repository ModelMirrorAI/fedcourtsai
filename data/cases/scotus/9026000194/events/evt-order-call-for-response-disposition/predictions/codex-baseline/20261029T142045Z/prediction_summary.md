The Supreme Court is more likely to turn down this appeal than hear it, with about a one-in-five chance of review (22%). The dispute asks whether a bankruptcy trustee can bring claims that belong to creditors as a group. The Court's request for a response and an alleged disagreement among appeals courts make review more plausible than usual. But requesting a response is not a promise to hear the case, and an unresolved alternative legal basis could make this a less attractive case to decide.
