# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will most likely deny the petition after obtaining the requested response, without a separate writing. The probability of any grant, including a summary grant, is 22%.

The frozen docket shows one distribution, for the September 28, 2026 conference, followed by the August 20 response request. It does not show a completed conference followed by a relist. Expect at least one further distribution once the response is filed or the response period ends: probability 99%. The modal path is one new distribution followed by denial, with zero or one additional substantive relist. A redistribution needed to accommodate the response should not itself be read as renewed merits interest. This increment is harness-masked for the response-requested moment but is still forecast here.

No CVSG is on the record. Expect none: the chance of a new invitation to the Solicitor General is 4%. If an invitation issues, it will more likely follow review of the opposition in fall 2026 or winter 2027 than precede the response. The statutory issue has nationwide consequences, but the dispute is between private parties and does not obviously require the government's special institutional perspective.

If the Court grants, expect plenary briefing and argument on the petition's single question: whether section 544(a) permits a bankruptcy trustee to assert a state-law alter ego claim because it is shared by creditors generally. The question may be framed more narrowly around the trustee's hypothetical lien-creditor powers and reverse veil piercing. Conditional on a grant, a disposition in the cert order itself has probability 8%; plenary review is substantially more likely. The provisioned record identifies no intervening Supreme Court ruling that calls for a routine grant, vacatur, and remand. Resolving the disputed scope of trustee authority would ordinarily warrant adversarial briefing rather than summary reversal.

Conditional on denial, the probability of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial is 5%. The modal forecast is no writing. Thomas or Gorsuch are the leading low-probability candidates for a writing about statutory limits on bankruptcy powers; neither is individually likely to write. The independent order-writer probabilities in prediction.json are small and do not form a distribution summing to one.

Expect disposition after the response cycle, most likely in fall 2026 or early 2027 unless the Court requests additional briefing. If review is granted, a potential merits rationale would distinguish powers attached to hypothetical creditor status from authority to represent creditors' independent claims. That is a conditional doctrinal possibility, not a prediction that the Court will issue an opinion on the merits.
