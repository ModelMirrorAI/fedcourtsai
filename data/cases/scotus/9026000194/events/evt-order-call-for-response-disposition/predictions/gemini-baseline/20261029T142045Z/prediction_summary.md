The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Mei Guo v. Despins*, with about a one-in-seven chance (14%) of granting the petition. Although the case raises a genuine disagreement among federal appeals courts over whether the federal Bankruptcy Code allows a bankruptcy trustee (the court-appointed official managing a debtor's estate) to bring "alter ego" claims (lawsuits alleging that a shell company is merely a front for the debtor) on behalf of creditors generally, the case has serious procedural flaws, including alternative legal grounds supporting the lower court's ruling and an ongoing bankruptcy proceeding below.
