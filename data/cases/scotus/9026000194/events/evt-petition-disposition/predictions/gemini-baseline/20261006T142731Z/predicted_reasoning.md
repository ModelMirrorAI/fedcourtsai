The presence of an amicus brief from the unsecured creditors committee highlights the importance of the issue in bankruptcy practice. While this increases the chances of review, the base rate remains low. Expect a denial, but with a non-trivial chance of a dissent from denial given the mature split. No CVSG is expected, as the issue primarily concerns private creditors and trustees, lacking a strong federal sovereign interest. A summary GVR is unlikely because there is no intervening authority to apply. If granted, the Court would likely take the question as presented.
