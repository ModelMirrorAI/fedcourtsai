## In brief

Mei Guo and a company she owns ask the Court to overturn a ruling that let a bankruptcy trustee seize their yacht and other assets as her father's alter ego.

## What happened

Ho Wan Kwok filed for bankruptcy after a New York court fined him $134 million for hiding assets, including a yacht called the Lady May, which was legally owned by HK USA, a company owned by his daughter, Mei Guo. The bankruptcy trustee, Luc Despins, argued that HK USA was really Kwok's alter ego, so its assets belonged to Kwok's bankruptcy estate. The bankruptcy court and district court agreed, and the Second Circuit Court of Appeals affirmed, holding that a bankruptcy law provision let the trustee bring this claim.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether the law giving bankruptcy trustees the same powers as a hypothetical creditor with a court-ordered claim on the debtor's property also lets a trustee pursue a legal claim that treats a company as merely a front for its owner, when that claim belongs to creditors generally rather than to the debtor personally.
