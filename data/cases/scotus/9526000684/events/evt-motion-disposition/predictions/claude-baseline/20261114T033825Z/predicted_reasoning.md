# Forecast — 26A684, Schultz v. BOKF, N.A.

The record shows an application for a stay submitted to the Chief Justice on November 10, 2026, with no
response requested, no referral to the full Court, and no amicus filings.

- **Disposition.** The Chief Justice will deny the application himself, in a one-line docket entry,
  without entering the temporary administrative stay it asks for. No relief, in whole or in part.
- **Response.** No response will be called for from BOKF, N.A.
- **Referral.** The Chief Justice will not refer it to the Court. The only realistic path to a referral
  is a renewed application addressed to a different Justice after the denial; if that happens (about one
  chance in eight), that Justice will refer it and the Court will deny it on an order list without
  comment.
- **Amicus.** None will be filed.
- **Timing (unscored).** Denial by the Chief Justice within about a week of submission, i.e. by roughly
  November 17–20, 2026 — well ahead of the December 17 foreclosure sale. A renewed application, if any,
  would be denied in December or early January.
- **Writings.** No Justice will write or note a dissent.
- **Standard, conditionally.** Were anyone to engage with it, the application fails at the first step:
  a Fourth Circuit unpublished denial of mandamus over recusal and bankruptcy stay-relief rulings
  presents no reasonable probability of certiorari, and rehearing is still pending below.
