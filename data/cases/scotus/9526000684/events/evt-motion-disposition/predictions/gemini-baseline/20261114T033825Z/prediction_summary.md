Chief Justice Roberts will almost certainly deny this emergency request, with only about a two-in-a-thousand (0.2%) chance of granting any stay. Paul Schultz, representing himself without a lawyer, asks the Supreme Court to pause a bankruptcy appeal and block a December 17, 2026 home foreclosure while he seeks rehearing of a refusal to disqualify—remove from the case—the federal district judge assigned below. Because lower-court requests remain pending, routine appeal routes exist, and the Chief Justice just denied two similar emergency requests by Schultz, solo denial without full-Court action is overwhelming.
