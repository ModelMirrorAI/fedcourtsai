# Evaluation

This is an `interim` cell, evaluating an emergency stay application in an election-related case.

## Accuracy
The predictor incorrectly forecast a denial (15% probability of grant), while the actual outcome was a grant.

## Baseline
Because this is an interim cell, `segment_base_rate` and `brier_skill_score` are computed and stamped by the harness. The committed statpack provides a sufficient pool of resolved substantive applications from strictly prior Terms (OT2024 and OT2025) to support the baseline calculation.

## Reasoning Quality
The reasoning correctly identified the baseline rate for substantive interim applications and carefully justified its upward adjustment (from ~10.5% to 15%) based on the Chief Justice's request for a response and the national salience of the election-law dispute. Although the prediction ultimately proved incorrect, the rationale was sound, well-anchored in the available data, and logically structured for this highly uncertain shadow-docket cell.
