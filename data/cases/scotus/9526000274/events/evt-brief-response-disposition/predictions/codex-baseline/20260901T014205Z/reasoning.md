# Rationale

I assign a 0.74 probability to an unqualified grant. The committed statpack provides a valid baseline for this OT2026 interim cell: OT2024 and OT2025, the strictly prior application Terms with resolved substantive cases, contain 31 grants among 296 resolutions, or about 10.5%. That clears the 50-case floor. I adjusted far upward because this application has already reached the response-requested rung, was submitted to the Chief Justice in an exceptionally time-sensitive nationwide election dispute, and is likely to be referred to the full Court.

The provisioned snapshot shows a stay application filed August 28, a response request on August 31 with a September 3 deadline, and a United States/FCC response filed the same day. It shows zero amicus briefs and no referral as of the frozen context. The response's content was not provisioned, so I did not assume what position it takes; the fast filing and the government's role in defending the FCC policy below nevertheless confirm that the matter is institutionally serious.

CourtListener supplied the Fourth Circuit's August 25 published opinion. The panel granted review 2-1, set aside the FCC Media Bureau notice, and treated Commission inaction as a constructive denial permitting judicial review. Judge Wilkinson's dissent presented a substantial contrary jurisdictional reading and argued that the majority's merits analysis conflicts with the Supreme Court's 2026 decision in *National Republican Senatorial Committee v. FEC*. The combination of a divided lower court, a fresh Supreme Court analogue, a consequential First Amendment setting, and a rule scheduled to take effect September 4 makes both certiorari and interim relief materially more plausible than in the unconditional application pool.

I set referral at 0.96 because a requested response in a nationwide, noncapital regulatory dispute ordinarily precedes full-Court consideration. I set the amicus increment at 0.65 because the issue directly affects national party committees, candidates, broadcasters, and campaign-finance groups, although the few-day schedule limits filings. The response-request increment is 0.02 because that rung had already fired and the claim is vacuous for this cell; a second request is possible but unlikely.

The main uncertainty is the unavailable application and response text. I could not assess the exact relief requested, the parties' treatment of the stay factors, or whether the government supports the application. The Fourth Circuit majority also offered a detailed textual account, and the Court could regard monetary advertising costs or disruption of the lower-court judgment as insufficient for emergency relief. The corpus query was used only as a small check on the escalation ladder, not as a conditioned rate: its recent results were dominated by unlike extension matters, though it did show another recent substantive application with a requested response, referral, amici, and a grant.
