P(grant) 0.82. The pooled base rate for a substantive interim application (from Term 2024 and 2025 data in the statpack) is roughly 10.5% (31 grants out of 296 resolved). However, I adjusted this probability sharply upward based on the specific facts of this case. The United States Solicitor General filed a response supporting the applicants (the NRCC and NRSC), arguing that the Fourth Circuit lacked jurisdiction to vacate the FCC's Media Bureau guidance regarding the "lowest unit charge" for political advertising. When the federal government supports an emergency stay to preserve its agency's guidance against a lower court vacatur, the Court is highly likely to grant it. The imminent opening of the 60-day pre-election window on September 4 adds urgency that favors a stay to maintain the status quo. I set P(referral) to 0.90 because cases with such high partisan and national stakes are almost invariably referred to the full Court by the Circuit Justice. The amicus increment probability is set low (0.20) because the extremely short timeline before the September 4 window leaves almost no time for amicus briefs to be prepared and filed. Since a response was already requested and filed, the response-requested increment is 0.05.
