# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny certiorari. The probability of any grant, including a summary grant route, is **28%**. The contested nature of the asserted circuit split and the still-interlocutory, overlapping litigation posture will weigh against taking the case now, despite the importance of independent remedies for individual Indian trust landowners.

The baseline contains **zero distributions and no CVSG**. The petition will receive its initial distribution after response-stage briefing; I assign **99%** to at least one distribution beyond the present zero. My central procedural forecast is an initial distribution followed by roughly one further distribution before disposition. The structured distribution-increment probability covers the first distribution, not the separate question whether the petition is genuinely relisted after first consideration.

No CVSG is the more likely outcome, but I assign **40%** to an invitation because the United States holds title in trust and has taken a position on the remedy below. If an invitation issues, expect it after initial conference consideration, plausibly during winter 2026–27, with disposition delayed for the government's response. I do not forecast that the government has already recommended review.

If review is granted, the Court will take the single question substantially as framed: whether an individual Indian allottee with a current federally conferred right of possession can sue for trespass under federal common law. It will not use that grant to decide pipeline damages, the precise injunction, or all issues in the parallel administrative litigation. Conditional on a grant, plenary review is the expected route: **12%** goes to disposition in the cert order itself and **88%** to a merits proceeding. There is no identified intervening decision supplying the ordinary basis for a GVR.

If granted and reached on the merits, the likely ground is that an existing federal possessory remedy extends to individual trust allottees, rather than that the Court should create a new implied statutory action under the right-of-way legislation. That merits statement is conditional only; the primary forecast is denial without a merits opinion.

Conditional on denial, expect no separate writing. I assign **15%** to some noted dissent or statement respecting denial, without predicting an individual Justice's vote or authorship.
