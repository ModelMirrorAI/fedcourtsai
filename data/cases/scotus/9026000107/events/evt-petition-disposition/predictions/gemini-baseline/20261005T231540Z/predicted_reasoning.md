Distributed once so far, but the Court requested a response on the same day as the distribution. Now that the Brief in Opposition has been filed, expect at least one further distribution (relist) to consider the fully briefed petition.

If granted, the Court would likely take up the first question presented regarding whether a property owner must exhaust legislative remedies to ripen an as-applied regulatory takings claim under the Fifth Amendment, as it presents a clean circuit split.

A summary GVR is unlikely because the petition asks the Court to resolve a split and establish a new rule, rather than apply an intervening decision.

No CVSG is expected, as the case involves a county and private property owner, not a federal agency or federal statutory scheme.

Should the Court deny review, a dissent from denial is plausible given the high level of amicus interest (six cert-stage amicus briefs) and the significance of the property rights issues raised.
