# Forecast: petition disposition

The Court will deny certiorari. The probability of any grant, including a grant accompanied by summary relief, is **0.22**. The principal obstacle to review is the disputed finality of the County's position in this particular permitting process, despite a substantial question about requiring legislative change before a takings suit can proceed. This forecasts certiorari, not whether Walls ultimately deserves compensation.

## Further docket movement

The claim nevertheless counts that additional distribution. The September conference listing alone does not establish that the Court considered the completed papers then.

If an invitation issues, expect it during the next round or two of consideration, not as the ordinary next step.
- **Timing:** expect disposition in late October or November 2026 following redistribution, with December plausible if the Court gives the petition another conference. A longer hold or CVSG would push disposition beyond that window. This is an unscored timing forecast, not a report of a scheduled conference.

## Conditional grant and denial routes

**If granted**, the most likely focus is Question 1, narrowed to whether the possibility of a discretionary legislative water-and-sewer category amendment prevents finality when the administering agency has communicated that existing law does not permit the requested use. The Court would be less likely to use this record to abolish prudential ripeness across the full range of cases encompassed by Question 2. On the grant branch, expect a finality-versus-exhaustion holding rather than a determination that a compensable taking has already occurred. This conditional framing follows the dispute in the petition, pp. 9–20, and opposition, pp. 1–11.

**Conditional on any grant, the probability of disposition in the cert order itself is 0.25.** Plenary briefing and argument are more likely. A summary vacatur applying the distinction in *Pakdel v. City and County of San Francisco*, 594 U.S. 474 (2021), is a credible alternative if the Court reads the County's position as definitive. No identified intervening decision supplies an automatic GVR rationale; the disputed local procedures make summary relief less likely than plenary review on the grant branch.

**Conditional on denial, the probability of a noted dissent or statement respecting denial is 0.20.** The modal forecast is an unexplained denial without a separate writing. If a writing appears, expect criticism of procedural obstacles to takings litigation rather than an assessment of compensation. No individual Justice's participation or vote is forecast.
