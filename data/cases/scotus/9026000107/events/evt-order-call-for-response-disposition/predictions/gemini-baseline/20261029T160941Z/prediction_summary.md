The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Garnell Walls v. Prince George's County*, with about a one-in-seventeen chance (6%) of granting the petition. Property owner Garnell Walls argues that the County unconstitutionally took his land without compensation—a "regulatory taking"—by refusing to let him install a temporary well and septic system, and that lower courts wrongly dismissed his lawsuit as premature ("unripe") because he did not petition the County Council to amend its water-and-sewer plan. Although six supporting "friend of the court" briefs prompted the Justices to request a response from the County, the lower-court ruling is an unpublished decision and the record shows Walls may never have properly completed the initial administrative waiver application, making this a poor test case.
