We expect the Supreme Court to turn down this case, though not with
certainty: we put the chance it takes the case, or sends it back to the lower
court, at about one in eight. A Maryland landowner says county rules that
block a well and septic system took his property without payment. The lower
court said he had to ask the county council to change the rules before he
could sue. The Court asked the county to respond, a sign of some interest. But
the lower-court ruling was short and unpublished, and it turns on this
case's particular facts, which makes the case a weak vehicle.
