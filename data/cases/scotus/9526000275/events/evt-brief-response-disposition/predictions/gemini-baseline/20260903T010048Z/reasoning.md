P(grant) 0.02. This is an application for an emergency injunction pending appeal to place a citizen voting measure on the Michigan ballot. The pooled base rate for substantive interim applications (from strictly-prior terms OT2024 and OT2025) is approximately 10.5%. However, this application asks the U.S. Supreme Court to intervene in a state election dispute while a parallel mandamus action is still pending in the Michigan Supreme Court (which the applicants characterize as "constructive denial"). The Court is historically very reluctant to grant extraordinary relief like an injunction when lower state courts have not yet ruled, especially under the *Purcell* principle regarding last-minute election changes. While a response was requested (indicating the Circuit Justice is taking it seriously enough to want the state's view), the bar for an original injunction from the Supreme Court is exceptionally high. I am therefore adjusting the probability significantly downward from the base rate.
