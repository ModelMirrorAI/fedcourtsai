The Court will deny the application for an injunction pending appeal. The application asks for extraordinary relief to compel state officials to certify a ballot initiative, despite the Michigan Supreme Court not having formally ruled on the pending mandamus petition. A response has already been requested by the Circuit Justice and filed. I expect the application will be referred to the full Court, as is typical for high-stakes election cases shortly before printing deadlines. I do not expect the Court to call for further briefing or amicus participation at this very late stage. The Court will likely issue a brief order denying the application, perhaps with a note that the denial is without prejudice to any ongoing state court proceedings.
