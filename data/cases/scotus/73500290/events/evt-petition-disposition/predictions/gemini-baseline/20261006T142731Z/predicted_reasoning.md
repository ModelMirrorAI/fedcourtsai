Distributed once so far, and the Court called for the views of the Solicitor General on October 5, 2026. Expect the petition to be distributed at least once more after the Solicitor General files their brief. A further CVSG is not expected since one has already been issued.

If granted, the Court would take the single question presented as written to resolve the circuit split on whether the *Illinois Brick* indirect-purchaser rule applies to the intended and actual targets of a group boycott. A summary disposition (GVR or summary reversal) is unlikely, as resolving a deep circuit split on antitrust standing requires plenary review. Should the Court deny the petition despite the CVSG, expect a separate writing dissenting from the denial, likely from a Justice interested in strict statutory interpretation of antitrust standing, such as Justice Thomas or Justice Gorsuch.
