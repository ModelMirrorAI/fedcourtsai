# Forecast

The Court will most likely deny certiorari after receiving the Solicitor General's views. The probability of any grant, including a GVR or other summary grant, is 42%. The invitation for government views signals substantial interest, but the disputed scope of the circuit conflict and the separate proximate-cause objection make denial the modal outcome.

The snapshot records **one distribution**, on September 2, 2026, for the September 28 conference, and an **October 5, 2026 CVSG**. From that state, I expect the petition to await the government's brief and then return to conference. I assign 97% to at least one additional recorded distribution and expect roughly one or two additional distributions before disposition. This is a forecast of additional distribution entries, not a claim that every such entry represents a substantive relist rather than a rescheduling. The decision is more likely to arrive in 2027 than immediately after this invitation.

No new CVSG is forecast: the invitation has already occurred. The required `cvsg-increment` probability is 0, and the claim is vacuous for this moment; it is not a prediction that the existing invitation disappears or that the government will not respond.

If the Court grants, I expect plenary briefing and argument rather than summary disposition. The conditional probability of a summary route is 15%. A later related ruling could supply a remand route, but the provisioned record identifies no intervening controlling decision that already warrants one. The disagreement over the nature of UAS's injury also makes immediate summary reversal less likely than plenary review.

If granted, the Court will likely take the petition's central Illinois Brick question, perhaps reformulated to ask whether its indirect-purchaser limitation categorically bars nonduplicative lost-profit damages claimed by the intended target of an exclusionary boycott conducted through that target's customers. I do not expect a grant framed as reconsideration of Illinois Brick altogether. Conditional on reaching that issue, a plausible merits ground would distinguish the categorical bar on passed-on price injuries from ordinary proximate-cause limitations; that would not itself establish UAS's entitlement to damages.

Conditional on denial, I expect no separate writing. I assign 12% to any noted dissent from denial or statement respecting denial, without predicting an individual Justice's private cert vote.
