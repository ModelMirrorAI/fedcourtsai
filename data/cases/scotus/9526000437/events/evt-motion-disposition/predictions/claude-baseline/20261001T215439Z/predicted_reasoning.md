# Forecast: In re Nikolay M. Valov, No. 26A437 (application for an injunction pending appeal)

**Disposition.** The application will be **denied**, and denied outright rather than granted in part. The record shows a self-represented applicant seeking an injunction pending appeal from a Virginia county circuit court civil matter (Mecklenburg County, CL25-2218), submitted to the Chief Justice as Circuit Justice for the Fourth Circuit. No order of the Supreme Court of Virginia or the Court of Appeals of Virginia is identified, so the application does not present a judgment the Court could ever review under its state-court jurisdiction in the form the record discloses, and an injunction (as against a stay) is the relief the Court reserves for cases where the applicant's right is indisputably clear. Nothing on this record approaches that bar.

**Who acts and when.** The Chief Justice will deny the application **alone, without referring it to the full Court** — the ordinary handling for a pro se civil application that presents no question the Court would want to speak to collectively. Expect the disposing entry within roughly one to two weeks of docketing, so by about mid-October 2026, in the form "Application (26A437) denied by The Chief Justice." A referral is the one procedural step with any real chance of happening, and it is a small one.

**Response.** No response will be called for. The application does not present the kind of question a Circuit Justice wants the opposing side's view on before acting, and the respondent (whoever it is in a caption styled *In re*) will not file one uninvited in the days before a denial.

**Amici.** None. The docket shows zero amicus entries and no one files in support of a pro se injunction application arising from a county civil case.

**Conditional merits content.** If, against this forecast, the Court were to act on the merits, it would be to deny for want of a clear right to relief rather than to grant any injunction; there is no plausible path on this record to the Court restraining a Virginia state proceeding pending appeal. No separate writing is expected to accompany the denial.
