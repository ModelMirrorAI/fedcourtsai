A Virginia homeowner facing eviction under a civil-contempt order asked the Chief Justice to block enforcement while state courts reviewed his case; the application was denied.

## What happened

Nikolay Valov, a co-owner of a house in Alexandria, Virginia, was held in civil contempt by a state circuit court for interfering with a court-ordered sale of the property. A court officer later said Valov missed a payment deadline and filed a notice to vacate. Valov pursued emergency relief in the Virginia Court of Appeals and Supreme Court of Virginia, without success in getting possession-protecting orders, while an eviction writ was sought in circuit court.

## What the Court is being asked

Valov asked the Chief Justice, acting as the circuit justice for the region covering Virginia, to temporarily block any enforcement action that would remove him from the home while state court proceedings continued, so that his claims about inadequate legal process and improper government interference with his home could still be reviewed.

## Where it stands
