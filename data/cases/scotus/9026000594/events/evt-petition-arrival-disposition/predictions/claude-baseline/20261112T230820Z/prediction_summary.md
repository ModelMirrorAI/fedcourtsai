Citibank is asking the Supreme Court to lift a court order that forces it to release frozen climate-grant money, and it filed alongside the federal government's own request for review in the same dispute. We think the Court is somewhat more likely than not, about three chances in five, to take up Citibank's request or send it back for reconsideration. The main reason is that the government's companion request, involving about $16 billion, is very likely to be heard, and Citibank's fate follows it.
