# Forecast — No. 26-594, Citibank, N.A. v. Climate United Fund

The docket shows zero distributions. This petition will travel with No. 26-589 (*Zeldin v. Climate United Fund*), the Solicitor General's petition from the same en banc D.C. Circuit judgment, and will be distributed for the same conference, most likely in January 2027 after the responses now due 15 December 2026 and the replies.

- **Disposition.** The Court will grant the government's petition, and will more likely than not grant Citibank's alongside it and consolidate the two for argument this Term. The next most likely course is that the Court holds this petition pending the decision in 26-589 and then grants, vacates and remands it once the government prevails on the Tucker Act question.
- **Relists.** The petition will be distributed at least once. Expect one or two distributions before the Court acts, with any relist mirroring 26-589's.
- **CVSG.** None; the United States is already a party.
- **Question presented.** If granted, the Court takes Citibank's question as written, or simply consolidates without reformulating, treating it as derivative of the Tucker Act question in 26-589. The Court will not reach Citibank's independent defences (derivative immunity, contract terms) in any detail.
- **Summary disposition.** A plenary grant with consolidation is likelier than a cert-order disposition; a GVR arises only on the hold path, after the decision in 26-589.
- **Separate writings.** None expected on the order disposing of this petition. If the Court denies, it does so without noted dissent unless it also denies 26-589, in which case a dissent from one or more of Justices Thomas, Alito, Gorsuch or Kavanaugh is possible.
- **If granted, likely ground.** The injunction against Citibank falls with the injunction against EPA: if the claims against the government belong in the Court of Federal Claims, the derivative relief against the financial agent is vacated.
