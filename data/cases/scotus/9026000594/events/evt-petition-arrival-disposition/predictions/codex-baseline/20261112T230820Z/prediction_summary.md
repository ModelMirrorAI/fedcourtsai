I give Citibank about a two-in-three chance of obtaining Supreme Court action on its petition. The most likely single outcome is that the Court sends the case back for another look after resolving the government's closely linked challenge. Citibank argues that it should not have to release climate-grant money while the government directs it to keep that money frozen. The link to the government's case substantially improves its chances, but the bank's narrow, contract-related issues may not warrant separate review.
