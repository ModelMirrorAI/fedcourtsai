## 3. Structural Adjustments and Joint Probability Decomposition

Because Citibank takes no position on the underlying EPA–grantee merits (`Pet. 1, 12`) and presents no standalone circuit split on Financial Agency Agreements, Citibank's petition would face steep odds (<5%) if filed alone. Its true grant probability is overwhelmingly driven by the joint disposition tree with the Solicitor General's `Vide` petition in **No. 26-589 (*Zeldin v. Climate United Fund*)**:

### Step A: Likelihood of a positive disposition in No. 26-589 (*Zeldin*) — ~69% (`0.56` plenary grant, `0.13` GVR/summary vacatur, `0.31` denial/dismissal)
Several extraordinary features push No. 26-589 toward the upper end of SG cert petitions, balanced against real procedural/vehicle headwinds:
1. **Stakes and stay-of-mandate cliff (`Pet.App.117a`):** At stake is **$16B–$20B** in Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund awards (which Congress repealed as to unobligated balances in the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, Pub. L. No. 119-21, § 60002). Crucially, on August 7, 2026 (`Pet.App.117a`), the en banc D.C. Circuit withheld its mandate and extended its April 16, 2025 administrative stay freezing the funds **only pending the Supreme Court's final disposition of certiorari**. If the Supreme Court simply denied certiorari in No. 26-589, the administrative stay would expire and the preliminary injunction requiring multi-billion-dollar disbursements would immediately take effect.
2. **Acknowledged circuit split and five-Justice emergency-docket alignment:** Twice in 2025 — *Department of Education v. California*, 604 U.S. 650 (2025), and *NIH v. American Public Health Ass'n*, 145 S. Ct. 2658 (2025) — a five-Justice majority (Thomas, Alito, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh, Barrett) stayed grant-termination injunctions under the Tucker Act, 28 U.S.C. § 1491(a). Following Judge Rao's vacated panel opinion here (`154 F.4th 809`), the Fourth Circuit in *Sustainability Institute v. Trump*, 165 F.4th 817 (4th Cir. 2026), and *Solutions in Hometown Connections v. Noem*, 165 F.4th 835 (4th Cir. 2026), held that the Tucker Act bars district-court APA grant-termination injunctions, whereas the en banc D.C. Circuit affirmed the injunction here (6–4 as to EPA; 5–5 equally divided as to Citibank, Judge Henderson recused) and the Ninth Circuit (*Thakur v. Trump*) has permitted subsets of grant injunctions. Corpus queries of the 60 most recent SCOTUS cert grants through November 9, 2026 confirm that **no merits-docket Tucker Act grant-termination vehicle has yet been granted**.
3. **Countervailing vehicle risks (~31% denial/dismissal on No. 26-589):** The en banc D.C. Circuit deliberately issued **no merits opinion** (`decision-below.txt`), resting on case-management grounds because the district court had already adjudicated summary judgment on an expanded record in October 2025 (while staying entry of final judgment pending the PI appeal). Moreover, respondents have elite representation and a colorable factual distinction from *California* and *NIH*: here the $20B had already left the Treasury into accounts in respondents' names at Citibank under Account Control Agreements. Because *California* and *NIH* were 5–4 (with Chief Justice Roberts dissenting on grant terminations), hesitation by even two conservative Justices over the interlocutory, unreasoned posture could produce a denial (~31%). Conversely, that same unreasoned affirmance (or a hold behind another Tucker Act petition) supports a ~13% chance of summary vacatur / GVR in No. 26-589. Note also that because the D.C. Circuit withheld its mandate and barred any party action regarding the awards/funds (`Pet.App.117a, 119a`), final summary judgment in the district court is unlikely to moot the PI before conference.

### Step B: Conditional fate of No. 26-594 (*Citibank*) across No. 26-589's branches
1. **If No. 26-589 is denied/dismissed (`0.31`):** No. 26-594 is almost certainly denied as well (~99% -> `0.306` denied, `0.004` positive).
2. **If No. 26-589 receives GVR / summary vacatur (`0.13`):** Because Citibank prosecuted a separate consolidated appeal (`No. 25-5123`) affirmed 5–5 in the same judgment, the Court will almost always GVR No. 26-594 alongside No. 26-589 (~88% -> `0.114` GVR, `0.016` denied).
3. **If No. 26-589 receives plenary review (`0.56`):** The Court has three options for No. 26-594:
   - **Consolidate for plenary review (~40%–42% of `0.56` ≈ `0.23`):** As in *FDA v. Alliance for Hippocratic Medicine*, 602 U.S. 367 (2024), and *Little Sisters of the Poor v. Pennsylvania*, 591 U.S. 657 (2020), the Court often grants and consolidates a private co-party's `Vide` petition from the same PI judgment — particularly here, where grantees argued below (`Pet. 1, 12–13`) that district-court claims and the 5–5 injunction against Citibank survive *even if* the Tucker Act bars jurisdiction over EPA.
   - **Hold pending No. 26-589 (~48% of `0.56` ≈ `0.27`):** As Citibank itself explicitly invites in Footnote 3 (`Pet. 11 n.3`), holding No. 26-594 avoids cluttering the landmark Tucker Act merits briefing with New York Account Control Agreement and Rule 19 sub-issues while preserving the Court's ability to vacate the 5–5 affirmance in Citibank's appeal (`25-5123`). Conditional on a hold, if the government prevails in No. 26-589 (~75% given the 5–4 stays in *California* and *NIH* and the 70.5% overall merits disturbed rate in `statpack.md`), standard Supreme Court practice is to **GVR** the held companion petition from the same judgment (~90% -> `~0.18` unconditional GVR; ~`0.02` denied/dismissed), whereas if grantees prevail (~25%), No. 26-594 is mostly denied (~`0.06` denied, ~`0.01` GVR).
   - **Immediate denial at conference while granting No. 26-589 (~10% of `0.56` ≈ `0.06`):** Small but real risk that the Court deems Citibank's Rule 12.6 party status inside No. 26-589 sufficient and denies No. 26-594 outright rather than holding it.

Summing across all branches yields **`P(granted plenary) ≈ 0.23`**, **`P(gvr) ≈ 0.30`**, **`P(summary-reversal) ≈ 0.01`** -> **`probability = 0.54`** (`granted = 1`, `predicted_disposition = "gvr"`), vs. **`P(denied/dismissed) = 0.46`**.
