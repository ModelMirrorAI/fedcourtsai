# Predicted Court Action — *Citibank, N.A. v. Climate United Fund, et al.* (No. 26-594)

## Current Docket State and Conference Trajectory
At the November 6, 2026 arrival snapshot, this petition has **zero** conference distributions (`distribution_count = 0`), with responses due December 7, 2026, and travels alongside the Solicitor General's companion `Vide` petition from the same August 4, 2026 en banc D.C. Circuit judgment, *Zeldin v. Climate United Fund* (No. 26-589, response due December 4, 2026).

- **Distributions and relists (`relist-increment` = 0.98):** From the zero-distribution arrival baseline, the petition is virtually certain to be distributed for conference at least once after briefing closes in December 2026 or January 2027 (absent an improbable pre-distribution Rule 46 dismissal). Past that initial conference distribution, expect **one to two further conference relists** (two to three distributions total) as the Court coordinates action on No. 26-589 and No. 26-594, followed most likely by a **hold** of No. 26-594 pending resolution of No. 26-589 (or, as the secondary grant route, immediate consolidation of both petitions for argument).
- **CVSG (`cvsg-increment` = 0.005):** The Court will **not** call for the views of the Solicitor General. The United States is already a party on this docket and the Solicitor General has already petitioned for certiorari in companion No. 26-589.

## Disposition Route (`disposition` = 0.54; `summary-disposition-route` = 0.57)
A positive disposition (`granted` or `gvr`) is slightly likelier than denial, and conditional on a grant, **summary disposition via grant, vacatur, and remand (`gvr`) is likelier than plenary review** of Citibank's petition:

1. **Primary positive route — Hold pending No. 26-589 followed by GVR (or simultaneous GVR):** The Court will likely grant plenary review in the Solicitor General's lead petition (No. 26-589) to resolve Tucker Act jurisdiction and APA reviewability over the $16B–$20B Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund grant terminations while **holding** Citibank's derivative financial-agent petition (No. 26-594). Because Citibank's separate appeal below (No. 25-5123, affirmed 5–5 by an equally divided en banc court) rests on an ancillary injunction ordering Citibank to disburse frozen grant funds, if the Court vacates or reverses the preliminary injunction in No. 26-589, it will dispose of No. 26-594 by a **GVR order** remanding for further consideration in light of *Zeldin v. Climate United Fund*. Alternatively, if the Court summarily vacates the unreasoned en banc judgment in No. 26-589, it will GVR No. 26-594 alongside it.
2. **Secondary positive route — Plenary grant and consolidation:** If four Justices prefer to foreclose respondents' contention that district-court injunctive relief against Citibank survives independently of Tucker Act jurisdiction over EPA, the Court will grant No. 26-594 and consolidate it with No. 26-589 for one hour of oral argument.

## Question Presented and Conditional Merits Ground
If granted for plenary review alongside No. 26-589, the Court will take Citibank's single Question Presented as written — whether the preliminary injunction against Citibank for following the federal government's instructions can remain in place. Conditionally on reaching that question on plenary review, the Court would likely vacate the injunction against Citibank on the ground that plaintiffs cannot circumvent federal sovereign immunity and Tucker Act channeling by enjoining a neutral federal financial agent designated under 12 U.S.C. § 90, or independently because the courts below failed to make individualized *Winter*/*Starbucks* preliminary-injunction findings as to Citibank.

## Separate Writings on the Order (`dissent-from-denial` = 0.16)
If the Court denies certiorari — most plausibly if it declines interlocutory review in both No. 26-589 and No. 26-594 because the en banc D.C. Circuit issued no merits opinion and summary judgment has already been adjudicated in the district court — there is a modest (~16%) chance of a separate writing attached to the order, most likely a dissent from denial by Justice Alito, Justice Thomas, or Justice Gorsuch, or a procedural statement respecting denial by Justice Kavanaugh.
