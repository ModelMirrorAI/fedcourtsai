The Supreme Court has a 54% chance—slightly better than a coin flip—of granting Citibank's petition, most likely through a "grant, vacate, and remand" order, which sets aside the lower court's ruling and sends the case back for a fresh look without full oral argument. Citibank was ordered by a lower court to release billions of dollars in frozen federal climate grants despite instructions from the Environmental Protection Agency to pause payments. Because the federal government simultaneously filed its own Supreme Court appeal challenging the same $20 billion ruling, the Justices are likely to take up the government's lead case while holding Citibank's companion petition and later sending Citibank's case back once the government's authority is decided.
