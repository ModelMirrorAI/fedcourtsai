## In brief

Citibank asks the Supreme Court to lift an injunction ordering it to disburse frozen climate-grant funds despite the government's instructions to withhold them.

## What happened

The EPA awarded billions in grants to nonprofits under the Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund, with Citibank holding the money as the government's financial agent. After a change in administration, EPA froze and then terminated the grants, citing fraud and oversight concerns, and directed Citibank not to release funds. The grantees sued Citibank and EPA. A district court issued a preliminary injunction against both, and a sharply divided en banc D.C. Circuit affirmed, with no majority opinion explaining the result as to Citibank specifically. Citibank now asks the Supreme Court to review that outcome. A related petition by the government concerning the same underlying dispute is noted as separately filed.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether Citibank, which says it merely followed the government's instructions as its financial agent, can be held under a court order to pay out the grant funds, when neither lower court explained why an injunction against Citibank itself was justified or applied the usual factors courts use before ordering such relief.

## Where it stands

The petition was filed November 2, 2026, with a response originally due December 7, 2026. After motions and replies about the briefing schedule, the Court on November 12, 2026 extended the response deadline to December 15, 2026, for all respondents. The petition itself has not yet been acted on.

## What each outcome would mean

Citibank, which lost below, is asking the Court to take up its case and set aside the injunction. If the Court grants review, it would decide the merits of whether Citibank can be ordered to disburse the funds; a grant would not itself decide who wins. If the Court denies review, the injunction requiring Citibank to disburse funds, as affirmed by the en banc appeals court, stays in place. The case also involves a related petition from the federal government concerning the broader dispute over the grant terminations.
