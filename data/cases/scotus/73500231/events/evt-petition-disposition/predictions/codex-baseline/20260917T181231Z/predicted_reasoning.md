# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny the petition for certiorari without a substantive explanation. The probability of any grant, including a GVR or summary reversal, is **0.4%**.

The snapshot records **one distribution**, on June 24, 2026, for the September 28, 2026 conference. That is not an already completed conference or an existing relist. I expect **zero further distributions**, followed by denial in the first orders following that conference, approximately late September or early October 2026. P(at least one additional distribution) is **2.5%**; if an additional distribution occurs, one is more likely than a prolonged sequence. The timing is a forecast, not a verified future release date.

No CVSG is recorded. I expect no invitation for the Solicitor General's views: **P(new CVSG) = 0.1%**. The United States is already a respondent and has waived its response. A request for a respondent's brief would not itself be a CVSG.

If the Court nevertheless grants review, I expect a narrowed version of the first four questions: whether a dismissal with prejudice imposed as a sanction precludes this subsequent action, including its asserted newly discovered grounds. The Court would be less likely to take the petition's entire eight-question package, which also contests MSPB jurisdiction and seeks renewed consideration of employment-discrimination and reinstatement claims. This conditional question-selection forecast does not imply that the alleged precedent conflict is established.

Conditional on a grant, I assign **25%** to disposition in the cert order itself and **75%** to plenary review. No intervening decision justifying a GVR is identified in the material consulted. If the Court found a review-worthy, recurring legal question in this record, plenary consideration would therefore be the more likely grant route.

Conditional on denial, **P(a noted dissent or statement respecting denial) = 0.3%**. I expect none and do not forecast an individual Justice's position. A denial would leave the judgment below undisturbed without endorsing its reasoning or adjudicating the petitioner's factual allegations.
