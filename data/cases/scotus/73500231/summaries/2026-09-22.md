## What happened

Tatyana Drevaleva, a former VA employee, lost her job in 2017 after taking leave to travel to Russia; she says the trip was for fertility treatment and to get medication unavailable in the United States, and that her supervisor approved it. Her discrimination lawsuits over the firing were dismissed by federal courts in California and New Mexico, and those dismissals were upheld on appeal. She later filed a new case in federal court in Washington, D.C., challenging a Merit Systems Protection Board ruling on the same firing. The district court dismissed that case too, holding it was barred because of the earlier rulings, and the D.C. Circuit affirmed. Drevaleva now asks the Supreme Court to review that outcome.

## What the Court is being asked

The case raises questions about when an earlier lawsuit's dismissal should block a person from bringing related claims again later. Drevaleva argues that her earlier case was never fully decided on its facts, so it should not count as a final ruling that closes the door on her new claims, especially since she says she later found new evidence and legal arguments. She also argues the lower courts wrongly decided that the Merit Systems Protection Board lacked authority over her case, and that a federal law required the district court to fully re-examine the facts of her firing.
