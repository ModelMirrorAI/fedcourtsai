## In brief

A former crime lab analyst asks the Court to hear her appeal from a ruling that denied her immunity from a wrongly convicted man's civil rights lawsuit.

## What happened

Donna Birks, a latent print examiner for the Seminole County Sheriff's Office, testified at Clemente Aguirre-Jarquin's 2006 murder trial that his palm print matched one found on the murder weapon. Aguirre was convicted and sentenced to death. Years later, an investigation raised doubts about Birks's print work, DNA testing implicated another person, and Florida's high court ordered a new trial; prosecutors then dropped the charges. Aguirre sued Birks and others under federal civil rights law, claiming she fabricated evidence in violation of his right to due process. The district court and the Eleventh Circuit Court of Appeals both refused to grant Birks qualified immunity, a legal protection that shields officials from suit unless they violated clearly established law.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether Aguirre's own trial testimony that he handled the murder weapon means Birks's disputed print testimony cannot be blamed for his conviction and imprisonment, whether a due process violation requires the analyst to have known her conclusion was wrong, and whether it was already clearly established that her actions were unconstitutional at the time.

## Where it stands

The petition has been distributed for the Court's conference of October 9, 2026, after the respondent waived and then was asked to file a response, and after both sides filed briefs. No decision on whether to hear the case has been recorded yet.

## What each outcome would mean

Birks, the analyst, is asking the Court to take up the case. If the Court grants review, it would hear full arguments and decide the legal questions about causation and immunity, deciding nothing yet about who wins. If it denies review, the Eleventh Circuit's ruling stands, and Aguirre's lawsuit against Birks continues in the lower courts without her having qualified immunity on this claim.
