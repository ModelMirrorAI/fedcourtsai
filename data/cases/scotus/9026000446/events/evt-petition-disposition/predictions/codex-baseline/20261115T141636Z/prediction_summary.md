The Supreme Court is very likely to decline this case; I put the chance of taking it up in some form at about six in one hundred. Tarquinii asks whether workers alleging discrimination must expressly name a particular way of proving causation before a judge rules without a trial. The petition identifies disagreement among appeals courts, but the unpublished ruling and disputes over the evidence make this an imperfect test case. The government waived its response, and the Court has not requested one.
