The Supreme Court will very likely decline to hear this case — I put the chance of review at about three in a hundred. A former Navy civilian employee asks whether a worker suing for job discrimination must spell out a "motivating factor" theory (that bias was one reason, not the only reason, for a firing) to keep it alive. The government did not even file a response, the lower-court ruling was a short unpublished one, and the case has procedural wrinkles, although skilled counsel and a claimed disagreement among appeals courts leave a small chance.
