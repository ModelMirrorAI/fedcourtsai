The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Tarquinii v. Cao*, with only a 2% (one in fifty) chance of taking the case. Former Navy employee Jawan Tarquinii asks the Court to decide whether a worker suing for job discrimination must specifically mention a "motivating factor" legal test—showing bias was at least one reason for her firing—at summary judgment, when a judge decides whether a case has enough evidence for trial. Although prominent Supreme Court lawyers filed her appeal, the government waived its response, no outside groups filed supporting briefs, the lower court's ruling is unpublished, Tarquinii made no legal arguments at all when representing herself in the trial court, and federal employees are governed by a different statutory provision than the private-sector law her petition relies on.
