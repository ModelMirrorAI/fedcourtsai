# Forecast of the Court's action

The Court will deny certiorari. The likely path is an order following the September 28, 2026 conference, in late September or October, without a written explanation. The petition's two RLUIPA questions will not be resolved on the merits in this case. The competing accounts of an outcome-determinative split, the invited-error objection, and the thin comparator record will make this an unattractive vehicle despite its substantial outside support. A silent denial will not itself establish which concern carried weight or endorse the decision below.

**Further conferences.** The baseline contains two distributions: May 12 and September 2. The first preceded a requested response; the second followed the opposition and reply. I forecast no additional distribution as the single likeliest path, with **45% probability of at least one further distribution** beyond that count of two. If another distribution occurs, one additional conference is likelier than a prolonged sequence.

**Solicitor General.** The Court will not request the Solicitor General's views. The probability of a new CVSG is **10%**. If it does request views instead of disposing of the petition, I expect that invitation during the fall conference sequence, which would materially extend the timetable.

**Questions if review is granted.** The more likely lead question is QP1, narrowed to whether feasible alternative religious facilities and foreknowledge of a zoning restriction can defeat a substantial-burden claim concerning the proposed religious structure. If the Court also takes QP2, it will focus on the appropriate comparison between religious uses and the ordinance's exempt secular uses, rather than announce that every difference in listed uses automatically violates equal terms. These are conditional forecasts, not predictions that the Court will reach either question after denying review.

**Route if granted.** Plenary briefing and argument are likelier than disposition in the cert order: **12% probability of a summary route conditional on any grant**. If plenary review occurs, I expect the central dispute to be how RLUIPA's statutory protections interact with the lower courts' alternative-site and comparator requirements. I do not forecast an unconditional merits holding or identify an intervening case as a GVR trigger.

**Writing if denied.** I expect no dissent or statement respecting denial. The aggregate probability of at least one such writing is **20% conditional on denial**. No individual Justice's vote or writing is forecast.
