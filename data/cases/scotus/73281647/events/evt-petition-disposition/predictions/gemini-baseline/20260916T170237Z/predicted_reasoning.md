Distributed once so far; expect at least one further relist before a decision on the petition. The case presents a developed circuit split on whether the government is exempt from liability under the Takings Clause when law enforcement intentionally destroys property. Given that Justices Sotomayor and Gorsuch previously called for further percolation on this exact issue, the Court is likely to discuss it at conference.

No CVSG is expected, as the dispute is between a private citizen and a municipality, and does not directly implicate federal interests or programs. 

If granted, the Court would likely take QP 1 as written to resolve the split over the law-enforcement exemption to the Takings Clause, and would likely also consider QP 2 regarding the "public necessity" doctrine as it is intertwined. A summary disposition (GVR or reversal) is unlikely, as the Court would want to establish a substantive precedent on this constitutional question through merits briefing and argument. 

Should the Court deny review, expect a dissent from denial or a statement respecting the denial from Justice Sotomayor or Justice Gorsuch, given their prior stated interest in the issue.
