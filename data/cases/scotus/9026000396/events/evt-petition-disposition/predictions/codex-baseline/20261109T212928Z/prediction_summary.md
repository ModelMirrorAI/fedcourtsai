The Supreme Court is slightly more likely to turn this case away than hear it: I estimate about a two-in-five chance of review. Rivera-Perez argues that credits earned through prison programs should shorten supervision after release, and federal appeals courts disagree. That conflict and the Court's request for a government response make review a serious possibility. Still, the Court recently declined a similar case. I expect another conference listing after the government responds, followed most likely by a denial without explanation.
