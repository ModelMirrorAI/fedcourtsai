We expect the Supreme Court to most likely turn down this case, but the chance it takes it is unusually high, about three in ten. The case asks whether federal prisoners can use "time credits" earned through rehabilitation programs to shorten their post-prison supervision. Federal appeals courts disagree, which the government has admitted, and the Court has asked the government to respond. Against that, the Court declined a similar case in April 2026, and the government is expected to oppose review.
