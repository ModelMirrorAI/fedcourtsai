# Forecast — No. 26-396, Rivera-Perez v. Flowers

- **Distributions.** One so far, for 11/6/2026, overtaken by the Court's 10/13 call for a response. The SG's response is due 12/14/2026. Expect the petition to be distributed again for a January 2027 conference. If the Court holds it for Maxwell v. Dinis, expect it to sit without further distributions until Maxwell is decided, then be distributed once more. Otherwise expect a decision after one or two further conferences. A grant would likely come after one relist.
- **CVSG.** None. The United States is the respondent.
- **Most likely outcome: denial**, with roughly a 30% chance of review, including a possible GVR.
- **Question presented, if granted.** The QP as written: whether First Step Act time credits under 18 U.S.C. 3632(d)(4)(C) can be applied to reduce a term of supervised release. The Court might add or flag the mootness/remedy posture, because the 2nd Circuit directed dismissal as moot on the strength of its merits ruling.
- **Summary disposition.** It is unlikely relative to plenary review, but the realistic summary path is a hold pending Maxwell v. Dinis (§ 2241 cognizability of First Step Act credit claims), followed by a GVR in light of it.
- **If denied.** Most likely no separate writing. If anyone writes, the likeliest are Justice Jackson, Justice Sotomayor or Justice Gorsuch, noting the acknowledged three-circuit conflict.
- **If granted, likely ground.** Text-first reading of "applied toward time in … supervised release". A ruling for the petitioner on ordinary meaning is plausible, given the Court's recent First Step Act textualism (Hewitt, Pulsifer).
