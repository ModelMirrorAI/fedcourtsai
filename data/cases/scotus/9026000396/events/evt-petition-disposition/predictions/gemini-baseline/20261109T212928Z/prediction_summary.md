The Supreme Court is very likely to deny review in *Rivera-Perez v. Flowers*, with only a 1% chance of granting the petition. Petitioner asks whether time credits earned by federal inmates under the First Step Act of 2018 can shorten post-prison supervised release (court-monitored supervision in the community after incarceration). All eight federal appeals courts to consider this question agree that those credits cannot reduce supervised release, the Supreme Court recently rejected the same issue in another case, and the federal government waived its response without the Court requesting one.
