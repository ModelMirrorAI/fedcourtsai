The Supreme Court is more likely to decline this case than hear it, but review has a meaningful chance—about 36%, or a little more than one in three. The case asks whether credits earned through prison programs can shorten supervision after release. Federal appeals courts disagree, and this prisoner appears to present the issue more cleanly than an earlier rejected case. Still, asking the government to respond does not mean the Court will take the case. Another scheduling step and then an unexplained denial are the likeliest path.
