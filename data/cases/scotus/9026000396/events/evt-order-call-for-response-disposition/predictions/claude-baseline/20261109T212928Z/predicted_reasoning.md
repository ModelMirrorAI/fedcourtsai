# Forecast — No. 26-396, Rivera-Perez v. Flowers

- **State:** distributed once (for 11/6/2026, overtaken by the Oct 13 response request); no relist; the respondent is the United States, so no CVSG.
- **Relist:** the petition will be redistributed once the SG's brief in opposition is filed (likely after one 30-day extension, so a December 2026 or January 2027 conference). Expect it to be relisted at least once before any grant; a denial would most likely come at the first post-response conference.
- **CVSG:** none — the SG is already the respondent.
- **Disposition:** more likely denied than granted (about one in three for a grant). The SG will probably oppose, again acknowledging the conflict but arguing vehicle or limited practical importance; the Court denied *Hargrove* (No. 25-818) on this question in April 2026.
- **If granted:** the Court takes the QP as written — whether §3632(d)(4)(C) time credits can be applied to shorten a supervised-release term — on plenary review, not summarily.
- **Summary disposition:** unlikely as a primary route; the realistic summary path is a hold for *Maxwell v. Dinis* (No. 25-5930) followed by a GVR if *Maxwell* reshapes how FSA credit claims proceed under §2241.
- **Separate writing on denial:** probably none; if any, a statement respecting denial from Justice Jackson or Justice Sotomayor (or Justice Gorsuch, who has written for defendants on FSA questions) noting the acknowledged split.
