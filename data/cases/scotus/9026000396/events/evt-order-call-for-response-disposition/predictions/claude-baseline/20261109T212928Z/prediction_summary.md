We expect the Supreme Court will most likely turn down Raul Rivera-Perez's appeal, but we put the chance it agrees to hear it at about one in three, which is far higher than usual. The case asks whether prison "time credits" earned under the First Step Act can shorten the period of supervision after release. Federal appeals courts disagree, and the Court asked the government to respond. However, the Court refused a similar case earlier this year, and the government will probably oppose review again.
