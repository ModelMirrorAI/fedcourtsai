The Horsemen want the Supreme Court to also decide whether the horseracing law unconstitutionally lets a private company write binding rules. I predict the Court will probably say no to this request: about a one-in-five chance it takes the question. The Court is very likely to hear the government's related appeal about the law's enforcement powers. This rulemaking question has no disagreement among lower courts, so the Court will most likely set it aside until that case is decided and then decline it.
