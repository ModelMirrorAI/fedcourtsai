The Supreme Court is very likely to deny this conditional cross-petition for certiorari (grant probability of 12%, or about one in eight). While the Court is expected to grant the companion petitions filed by the Federal Trade Commission and the Horseracing Integrity and Safety Authority to resolve a disagreement among federal appeals courts over the Authority's enforcement powers, the federal appeals courts unanimously agree that Congress's 2022 statutory amendment gave the Federal Trade Commission sufficient control over the Authority's rulemaking power to satisfy the Constitution's limits on delegating government power to private entities. Both the federal government and the Authority oppose adding this separate rulemaking question to the case.
