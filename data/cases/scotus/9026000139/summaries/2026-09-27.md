## What happened

Mike Dennis sued Monsanto Company in California state court, claiming the company failed to warn consumers that its weedkiller Roundup could cause cancer. He developed non-Hodgkin's lymphoma after using Roundup on his property for about 20 years. A jury ruled for Dennis, awarding damages and a large punitive damages amount later reduced by the trial court. The California Court of Appeal affirmed, rejecting Monsanto's argument that federal pesticide law overrides, or preempts, this kind of state-law claim. California's supreme court declined to review that decision. Monsanto then asked the U.S. Supreme Court to take the case, pointing to its own recent decision in a different case, Monsanto Co. v. Durnell, which held that federal law does preempt these cancer-warning claims when the Environmental Protection Agency has not required such a warning.

## What the Court is being asked

The Court is asked whether, given its ruling in the Durnell case, it should set aside the California Court of Appeal's decision in this case and send the case back for the lower court to reconsider it in light of that new ruling. Dennis's response says he does not oppose this outcome and agrees the case should go back to the California court to apply the new rule.

## Where it stands
